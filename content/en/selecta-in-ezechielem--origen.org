FROM THE SELECTIONS OF ORIGEN ON EZEKIEL =13.768= CHAP. Αʹ. And it came to
pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth of the month,
and I was in the midst of the captivity by the river Chebar; and the heavens
were opened. Ezekiel bears a type of Christ in many things, and you will begin
to see this in the proems of the prophecy. It came to pass in the thirtieth
year, in the fourth month, =13.769= on the fifth of the month, and I, he says,
was in the midst of captivity by the river Chebar; and the heavens were
opened. These things have also been written concerning Christ, when he was in
this world, in this captivity, at the Jordan, when Jesus was beginning about
thirty years of age, and when the heavens were opened to him. But the thirty
years in the prophecy according to the sensible meaning is the time of the
life of the prophet; and of the captivity, the fifth year from when they went
down. But according to the name Ezekiel also has a symbol of Christ, who is
interpreted might of God. And the, son of man, toward anagogy of the one who
was named Son of man. I was in the midst of the captivity. The mind which the
wording has is this; but it is not simply so: as if he says, I, who have
nothing of captivity, was in the midst of captivity through economy. Thus also
Christ, not having been taken captive, was in the place of the captives, but
for the sake of their redemption; as also the prophet was not in the midst of
the captivity through sins, but of healing. And I saw visions of God. God
painted the invisible and intelligible nature in the visible and sensible
nature, in order that those under sense might be trained in it toward
contemplation of the intelligible. And the word of the Lord came to Ezekiel.
Word who was in the beginning, Word of God. I said: You are gods and sons of
the Most High, all of you. The Spirit calls gods those to whom the Word of God
came, the God Word; for this one is deifying. In the land of the Chaldeans.
Chaldean is interpreted all toil. And these are astrologers who speak of fate,
and who are wholly nailed to the sensibles, and who toil in them, making them
gods; the land of the Chaldeans is the place and the worst habit. Indeed the
Chaldeans also have a symbol of those arrogant in impiety. And behold a
lifting spirit. A lifting spirit is God; for God is spirit, and lifting evil.
And a great cloud. This cloud is righteousness, the good that extends from God
to us, from which we are enlightened with the knowledge of the truth. And
brightness, for also around it. A likeness as the appearance of a man. We are
a likeness of the four living creatures. And seek what the three are. The
charioteer of these four living creatures was not wholly fire, but from the
loins toward the things below, and from the loins even upward electrum. For
the word does not have only instruments of punishment, but also has those
through which it gives rest. And it punishes through the lower powers. For he
did not see fire about the head, nor from the loins toward the things above
was the charioteer fire, but from the loins toward the things below there was
fire, in order to show that those who happen to be in generation, these need
fire; for the loin is a symbol of generation. And not only those under
generation did the, from =13.772= the loins even downward, show, but also all
who are below and who are attached to matter. For generation and corruption
are in matter. And electrum is the fusion from gold and silver, as these most
precious things of matter are symbols of the precious and upper ranks of the
holy powers. CHAP. Βʹ. Son of man, stand upon your feet. As far as we
remember, most continuously and through the whole prophecy the, Son of man, is
said to Ezekiel, but more rarely to Daniel, of whom each in the captivity
being a type of him who toward us captives


has gone forth, Jesus the Savior God. If then they hear, or are dismayed. Like
this hesitating expression is that in Jeremiah: Perhaps they will hear, and
they will repent. And we were saying also when interpreting those things, that
these are said for the exhortation of those who hear, in order that they may
not seem, the worse or better things among us having been already taken in
advance by the knowledge of God, that it is not possible for the opposite of
the things foreknown to come about, as they hear the things said. For thus
equally it is in our power to hear, and not to hear, as if God had not
foreknown, neither becoming less in our power because of his having foreknown,
nor more, if he had not foreknown. Of the same. It would be said more securely
instead of they are dismayed, they will cease. And the, they are dismayed, has
been arranged here of those who are moved by divine oracles and who travel
from being dismayed in the things said also toward fearing God. And one must
think something else also to be signified here from they are dismayed besides
what is said by the Greeks, who define dismay as a passion of a human soul.
And in it a roll of a book. The divine words, because they are in part and
least in comparison with the whole Word and all wisdom, have been called not a
book, but a certain roll of a book. And in the Psalms also the: In a roll of a
book it has been written concerning me, shows the like. But no longer is that
upon the right hand of the one seated upon the throne called a roll of a book,
but a book written within and behind. Except: No one was able, says John,
neither in heaven, nor upon the earth, nor under the earth, to open that book,
nor to look at it, except only the lion who has conquered from the tribe of
Judah, the root of David. And you will find also another roll of a book
written in the Apocalypse, which John eats; for human nature does not have
room to eat more than a roll. And there had been written in it lamentation and
a song. A song for the holy, and lamentation for those not despaired of among
those who have sinned; but woe for those who will be punished. =13.773= CHAP.
Γʹ. And he fed me the roll. From here it is especially clear that human nature
does not have room to eat more than the roll, and this even when it is fed by
the hand of God. Yet the one who eats this roll, as the stretched out hand
unrolls it toward the one who eats, will eat it; in order that through the
unrolling of the roll he may take hold of each one of the things written in
it, and, having chewed the roll and the unrolling of it, he may take all the
things written into his intelligible belly, not only in front, but also
behind. And I think that through the things in front of the roll are shown the
leading doctrines, and the things concerning the holy, and the things
concerning the promises, and in a word the things concerning the blessed and
the conduct of their living; and through the things behind, the things
concerning those punished and the manner of their punishment and of the things
that followed in the creation upon those that had come about leadingly. And
the leading things and the things in front are shown through the song, and the
remaining things through the lamentation and the woe. Those then who have come
to be in sins and have wholly fallen away from the doctrines of salvation I
suppose to be lamented; and the song to be announced to the blessed; and the
woe to those who have perfectly fallen away from blessedness. It is possible
also thus for even the one who has tasted in knowledge of the living bread
that has come down from heaven to taste, when, having been lamented, he
becomes worthy of blessedness. And Theodotion accordingly with the Seventy has

mourning song; and Aquila says, it was written upon it creation and a casting
opposite; and it shall be. And perhaps through this it is shown according to

him; which you will seek if it has been said anywhere. Blessed is the glory of

descent of our Savior is prophesied, as he goes forth from his place and has
come down from his leading condition with the Father. Yet a place of God can
also be called that which is named by Paul unapproachable light in the: Who
alone has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light. And what is this? The
deepest knowledge of God, to which it is not possible for anyone to approach,
nor to have room for the grasp of it. I have given you as a watchman to the
