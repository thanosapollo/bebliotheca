through whom are all things, and one Holy Spirit, in whom are all things; of
the one from whom, and through whom, and in whom, not dividing natures (for
neither would the prepositions, or the orders of the names, have shifted), but
characterizing the properties of one and unconfused nature. And this is clear
from the fact that they are gathered again into one, if that saying is not
read carelessly by anyone in the same apostle: From him, and through him, and
unto him are all things; to him be the glory unto the ages. Amen. The Father
is the Father, and without beginning; for he is not from anyone. The Son is
the Son, and not without beginning; for he is from the Father. But if you take
beginning from time, he too is without beginning; for he is maker of times,
not under time. The Holy Spirit is truly the Spirit, proceeding from the
Father, yet not filially, nor indeed generatively, but processively; if one
must even coin something about the names for the sake of clarity. Neither has
the Father departed from unbegottenness because he has begotten; nor the Son
from generation, because he is from the unbegotten. For how? nor has the
Spirit shifted either into Father or into Son, because he has proceeded, and
because he is God, even if it does not seem so to the godless; for the
property is unmoved. Or how would a property remain, if it is moved and
shifts? But those who set unbegottenness and generation as natures of gods of
the same name would perhaps also alienate Adam and Seth from one another
according to nature (because the one is not from flesh, for he is a formation;
and the other is from Adam and Eve). One God then in three, and the three one,
as we have said.
13. And since these things, or this, are so, it was necessary that the worship
not be bounded by those above only, but that there be some worshippers below
as well, that all things might be filled with the glory of God, since they are
also of God; and for this reason man is created, honored with the hand of God
and with the image. But to overlook this man, pitiably severed from his Maker
by the devil's envy and the bitter taste of sin, was not of God. What happens?
And what is the great mystery concerning us? Natures are made new, and God
becomes man; and he who has mounted upon the heaven of heaven toward the east
of his own =36.349= glory and brightness is glorified toward the west of our
meanness and lowliness, and the Son of God accepts both to become and to be
called son of man; not changing what he was (for he is unchangeable), but
taking on what he was not (for he is a lover of man), that the uncontainable
might be contained, having conversed with us through intervening flesh, as
through a veil; since to bear his pure Godhead was not of the nature that is
in generation and corruption. For this reason the unmixed things are mixed:
not God with generation only, nor mind with flesh, nor the timeless with time,
nor the uncircumscribed with measure; but also birth with virginity, and
dishonor with him who is higher even than all honor, and the impassible with
suffering, and the immortal with the corruptible. For since the sophist of
wickedness thought himself invincible, having baited us with the hope of
godhead, he is baited by the screen of flesh; that as he attacked Adam, he
might fall upon God, and thus the new Adam might save the old, and the
condemnation of the flesh be undone, death having been put to death by flesh.
14. So then we have already kept the fitting feast of the birth, both I the
leader of the feast, and you, and all that is in the world and above the
world. With the star we ran, and with the Magi we worshipped, with the
shepherds we were shone about, and with the angels we glorified, with Symeon
we took him in our arms, and with Anna we gave thanks, the aged and sober
woman; and thanks be to him who came to his own in a foreign way, because he
glorified the stranger. But now there is another act of Christ, and another
mystery. I cannot contain the joy, I become inspired, and I almost, like John,
proclaim the good news, even if I am not a forerunner, yet from the
wilderness. Christ is illumined, let us shine with him; Christ is baptized,
let us go down with him, that we may also come up with him. Jesus is baptized;
this only? Or is it necessary also to observe the other things carefully?
Being who? and by whom? and at what time? The pure one, and by John, and at
the beginning of the signs? That we may learn what, and be taught what? To be
purified beforehand, and to be humble-minded, and to preach in fullness, both
of the spiritual and of the bodily age. That is toward those who improvise
baptism, and do not prepare beforehand, nor grant security to the redemption
=36.352= through the habit toward the good. For even if the gift has remission
of the things past (for it is a gift), yet then it is more worthy of
reverence, lest we return to the same vomit. This is toward those who lift
themselves up against the stewards of the mystery, if they excel in some
dignity. The third toward those who are confident in youth, and think every
time is a time for teaching, or for a presidency. Jesus is cleansed; and you
despise the cleansing? By John; and you rise up against your herald? Being
thirty years old; and you before the beard teach the old men, or you are
entrusted with teaching, having the venerable perhaps neither from age nor
from manner? Then Daniel is here, and so-and-so, young judges, and the
examples are on the tongue. For everyone who does injustice is ready for a
defense. But the rare is not a law of the Church; if indeed neither one
swallow makes spring, nor one line the geometer, nor one voyage the seaman.
15. Yet John baptizes, Jesus approaches; that he may sanctify perhaps the
baptist as well; but what is clear, the whole old Adam, that he may bury him
in the water; and before these and for their sake, the Jordan; as he was
spirit and flesh, so perfecting with Spirit and water. The Baptist does not
accept; Jesus contends: I have need to be baptized by you, the lamp says to
the Sun, the voice to the Word, the friend to the Bridegroom, he who is above
all among those born of women, to the Firstborn of all creation, he who leaped
from the womb, to him who was worshipped in the womb, he who ran before and
will run before, to him who has appeared and will appear. I have need to be
baptized by you; add also, on your behalf. For he knew that he would be
baptized with the martyrdom; or, like Peter, that he would be cleansed not in
the feet only. And do you come to me? This too is prophetic. For he knew that
as after Herod, Pilate would be mad, so Christ would follow him who had gone
before. And what does Jesus say? Suffer it now; for this is the economy. For
he knew that after a little he himself would baptize the Baptist. And what is
the fan? The cleansing. And what is the fire? The consuming of the light
stuff, and the heat of the Spirit. And what is the axe? The cutting out of the
incurable soul, even after the dung. And what is the sword? The cutting of the
Word, which divides the worse from the better, and splits the faithful and the
unfaithful, and raises the son, and the daughter, and the bride, against the
father, and the =36.353= mother, and the mother-in-law, the new and recent to
the old and shadowy. And what is the latchet of the sandal, which you who
baptize Jesus do not loose, you of the wilderness and without food, the new
Elias, more than a prophet, inasmuch as you also saw the one prophesied, the
mediator of Old and New? What is this? Perhaps the word of the sojourn, and of
the flesh, of which not even the outermost is easily undone, not only for
those still fleshly, and infants in Christ, but not even for those according
to John in the Spirit.

for us formed, and baptized, and crucified, come, let us philosophize a little
about the difference of baptisms, that we may go away from here purified.
Moses baptized, but in water; and before this, in the cloud and in the sea.
And this was typically, as it seems also to Paul: the sea, of the water; the
cloud, of the Spirit; the manna, of the bread of life; the drink, of the
divine drink. John also baptized, no longer indeed in the Jewish way; for not
in water only, but also unto repentance; yet not yet wholly spiritually; for
he does not add, in the Spirit. Jesus also baptizes, but in the Spirit. This
is the perfection. And how is he not God, that I may be a little bold, from
whom you also =36.356= become God? I know also a fourth baptism, that through
martyrdom and blood, with which Christ himself also was baptized, and much
more venerable than the others, inasmuch as it is not stained with second
filths. I know still a fifth also, that of tears; but more laborious, as he
who washes his bed every night and his pallet with tears, whose sores also
stank of wickedness; who goes mourning and downcast, and who imitates the
turning of Manasseh, and the pitied humility of the Ninevites; who utters the
words of the publican in the temple, and is justified rather than the boastful
Pharisee; who bends down like the Canaanite woman, and seeks loving-kindness,
and crumbs, the food of a dog very famished.
18. I then (for I confess that I am a man, a changeable living thing and of a
flowing nature), both receive this eagerly, and worship him who has given it,
and impart it to the others, and I bring mercy first as an offering for mercy.
For I know that I myself also am clothed with weakness, and that as I measure,
I shall be measured. But what do you say, what do you legislate, O new
Pharisee, and clean in the name, not in the purpose, and blowing the things of
Novatus at us with the same weakness? You do not receive repentance? you do
not give room to lamentations? you do not weep a tear? May you not meet with
such a judge. Do you not reverence the loving-kindness of Jesus, who took our
weaknesses, and bore our diseases, who came not for the righteous, but for
sinners unto repentance, who wants mercy rather than sacrifice, who forgives
the sins seventy times seven? How blessed is your height, if it were purity,
and not conceit, legislating above man, and undoing the amendment by despair.
For it is equally an evil, both undisciplined relaxation, and unforgiving
condemnation; the one letting out the whole rein, the other choking with
severity. Show me the purity, and I accept your boldness. But now I fear that,
teeming with sores, you introduce the incurable. Do you not even receive David
repenting, for whom repentance also preserved the prophetic gift? Nor Peter
the great, who suffered something human concerning the saving passion? But
Jesus received him, and by the threefold of the question and of the confession
he healed the threefold of the denial. Or do you not even receive him who was
perfected through blood? For this too is of your =36.357= madness. Nor him who
sinned in Corinth? But Paul even confirmed love, when he saw the correction;
and the reason: lest such a one be swallowed up with the more abundant sorrow,
weighed down by the excess of the rebuke. Nor do you give the young widows in
marriage, because of the vulnerability of the age? But Paul dared this, whose
teacher you clearly are, as having reached a fourth heaven, and another
paradise, and having heard more unspeakable words, and having taken in a
greater circuit for the Gospel.
19. But these things were not after baptism. What is the proof? Either show
it, or do not condemn. And if it is doubtful, let the loving-kindness prevail.
But Novatus, he says, did not receive those who fell in the persecution. What
is this? If they did not repent, justly; I too do not receive those who either
are not bent, or not worthily, nor balance the correction against the evil;
and when I do receive them, I assign them the place that belongs to them. But
if those melted away with tears, I will not imitate him. And what law is the
misanthropy of Novatus to me, who did not punish greed, the second idolatry;
but so bitterly condemned fornication, as if fleshless and bodiless? What do
you say? Do we persuade you with these words? Come, stand with us who are men.
Let us magnify the Lord together. Let none of you dare to say, not even if he
has trusted himself too much: Do not touch me, for I am clean, and who is such
as I? Share the brightness with us too. But we do not persuade? We will weep
for you also. These then, if they are willing, let them go our way and
Christ's, and if not, their own. Perhaps there they will be baptized with the
fire, the last baptism, the more laborious and longer, which eats the matter
as grass, and consumes the lightness of all wickedness.
20. But let us honor the baptism of Christ today, and keep the feast well, not
luxuriating with the belly, but rejoicing spiritually. And how shall we
luxuriate? Wash, become clean. If you are purple in sin, and less bloody, be
whitened as snow; and if scarlet and complete men of blood, reach even to the
whiteness of wool. But in every way be purified and keep being purified. As
=36.360= God rejoices in nothing so much as in the correction and salvation of
a man, for whose sake is every word and every mystery; that you may become as
lights in the world, a living power to the other men, that standing as perfect
lights beside the great Light, you may also be initiated into the illumination
there, being enlightened by the Trinity more purely and more clearly, whose
one ray from one Godhead you have now moderately received, in Christ Jesus our
Lord, to whom be the glory and the power unto the ages of ages. Amen.
