Again he consoles them for the persecutions which the Thessalonians were
enduring, recalling from the very preface the just and great recompense of
Christ, which punishes the wicked and crowns the saints. Then, since there was
much inquiry concerning the second coming of Christ, when he will come, he
also corrects the opinions about this, so that they may not be forestalled or
carried away by foreign teachings. He also sets forth both the manner and the
sequence of the coming of Christ which is to take place after the deception of
the antichrist. 2 Thess 1:3 Love with righteousness is not only to love but
also to be loved in return. 2 Thess 1:6-8 "If indeed it is just" is not of
doubt, but of emphasis and confession; for it is said in a manner of speaking,
as we often say when it is day: "if indeed it is day." Giving vengeance to
those who do not know God, that is, bringing punishment upon the impious. With
angels of his power in a flame of fire. He presents the terror of the glorious
coming both through these words and through what he adds, encouraging those
who have been wronged; for it is a great consolation not only the reward for
the sufferings but also the punishment that comes upon those who have wronged
them. 2 Thess 1:10 Because our testimony was believed on that day; for the
faith here prepares beforehand the reward on that day. On which? On the day in
which Christ will come. 2 Thess 2:1-4 He exhorts them not to be disturbed
untimely before the appearing of the Lord, neither if prophets should claim to
be such in whom there is a spirit of error, nor if words and rumors about the
Lord should be sown abroad, nor if some, having forged a letter, should bring
it as ours. that unless the apostasy come first and the man of sin be
revealed, the son of perdition, the one who opposes and 333 exalts himself
above every so-called god or object of worship. Do not expect, he says, the
savior before the deceiver who must come first, as I have said. exalting
himself above every god or object of worship, that is, above all idolatry; for
as he himself established it, so he himself will also overthrow it when he
comes, and every heresy which he set against the word of truth, that men may
also be condemned in this respect, that they themselves did not take down the
idols by which they were deceived, but the one who deceived them. And these
deceptions the devil established from the beginning, drawing men away from God
little by little and quietly introducing his own worship, so that when he
comes afterward, finding them deceived and outside the help of God, he may
easily subject them to himself. And concerning the coming deception of men the
savior himself also foretold that it would be hard, when he comes, to find the
faith upon the earth. the man of sin, that is the antichrist; for he will come
to Jerusalem as if to rebuild the temple, and he will stand in the holy place
proclaiming himself God. the one who opposes and exalts himself above every
so-called god, that is, above all idolatry; and above every object of worship,
that is, every 334 heresy which he set against the word of truth. As the
scaffolding of an arch, so the heresies among those called both Jews and
Greeks are a preparation for the devil. And as when an arch has been tightened
and has received its proper construction the scaffolding is taken down, and
the arch alone remains firm, so the devil laid the many heresies and the paths
of deception underneath as scaffolding, having fitted his own worship as a
kind of arch. 333, col 2 Through this he shows that the idolatry which the
devil established, he himself when he comes overthrows. And every heresy which
he set against the word of truth, he himself when he comes dissolves, that in
this too men may have the judgment, that they themselves did not take down the
things by which they were deceived, but the one who deceives them. For these
deceptions which the devil pieced together part by part, drawing men away from
God and quietly introducing his own worship, so that when he comes afterward,
having found things prepared, and men deceived and outside the help of God, he
may easily seem to himself to accomplish what he aims at, that is, to be
worshiped by all men. 334, col 2 For as the scaffolding of an arch, so are the
heresies both among the Greeks and among the Christians to the devil. And as
when an arch has been tightened and has received its proper construction the
scaffolding is taken away, and the arch alone remains firm, so the devil laid
the many heresies and the paths of deception underneath as scaffolding,
preparing beforehand his own worship as a greatest arch, so that when he
comes, doing away with all the heresies, he himself may also abolish them,
introducing his own worship naked among men. 2 Thess 2:4 In imitation of the
savior Satan comes in a complete man. 2 Thess 2:6-8a The Holy Spirit restrains
and hinders the coming of the lawless one. Whenever, then, he says, this
through the wickedness of men is taken out of the way and withdraws, then the
lawless one will have room to be revealed and to rise up, with no one
hindering. And he said this, that the mystery of lawlessness has already taken
a beginning, since in the apostolic times both schisms and heresies have come
to be, and from then the devil was working the unbelief of men. For the
mystery of impiety is already at work. For the Manichaeans have already said
that the devil is the god of the law, and Marcion said that the god of the law
is not the Father of Christ, and some say they worship the one greater 335
than all. And Manes also says that he himself is Christ, and that he himself
is the Paraclete. only what now restrains until it is taken out of the way,
and then the lawless one will be revealed. And the Holy Spirit both restrains
and hinders the coming of the lawless one, as he has already said. And now you
know what restrains. 334, col 2. What restrains, he says, is the grace of the
Holy Spirit. 335 2 Thess 2:8b-9 This Isaiah also said beforehand; for having
mentioned the one from the root of Jesse he added: and he shall strike the
earth with the word of his mouth, and with the spirit through his lips he
shall slay the ungodly. And that he would strike the earth he also foretold
through the last of the prophets, having said: the sun of righteousness shall
rise upon you who fear, and healing in his wings. And a little later: behold I
will send you Elijah the Tishbite, and he added: lest coming I strike the
earth utterly. And he will abolish him by the appearing of his coming. Our
life is Christ; when therefore Christ is manifested, our life, of necessity he
also abolishes the author of death, the devil. And in imitation of the savior
Satan comes in a complete man, whose com336ing is according to the working of
Satan with all power, signs, and the rest. in imitation of the bodily sojourn
of Christ, of the economy for our sake, Satan comes and works in a man, as I
have already said. 335,col2 This is the "and with the spirit through his lips
he shall slay the ungodly," which Isaiah says; for having mentioned the one
from the root of Jesse he added: and he shall strike the earth with the word
of his mouth, and with the spirit through his lips he shall slay the ungodly.
And the striking of the earth he also foretold through the last of the
prophets; for having said: the sun of righteousness shall rise upon you who
fear me, and a little later: behold I send you Elijah the Tishbite, last he
added the word: lest coming I strike the earth utterly. And he will abolish
him by the appearing of his coming. The apostle called Christ our life, and
added: when your life is manifested. Life therefore being manifested from
heaven, it is impossible that the author of death not be abolished, whose
coming is according to the working of Satan. 336,ξολ2 this ought to have
persuaded the Apollinarians and the Arians that the God Word assumed a whole
man. For in imitation of the righteousness and of the economy for our sake
Satan comes and will work in a man.
