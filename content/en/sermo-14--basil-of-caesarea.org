CONCERNING FAITH
By the grace of the good God, to the one who comes, by the healthy words of our
Lord Jesus Christ and by the teaching according to piety, the faith of
Christians shines as light, which I will set beside you through brief things;
that you the church of God, as a certain unstormed ship having as helmsman our
Lord Jesus Christ, may be moored into his holy and calm harbor, lest sometime,
baptized by some triple-wave, the teaching of the heretics, you shipwreck
concerning the faith.
And I set beside you the Lord's own faith through brief things as I said; for
the things through a shorter setting-out are naturally to remain more easily
with those who hear. Our Lord Jesus Christ, by compassion concerning his own
fashioning, having come upon earth to seek and to save the lost, and having been
incarnate through a holy virgin, and having worked all those wonders which we
have learned through the gospels as God, and having benefited the race of men
through all his goodness, and having suffered every incurable thing because of
our salvation from those who were benefited, at last he was crucified, and
having been crucified he died, and having died he was raised on the third day.
And after his being raised, and having filled all the loving economy for us,
when he was about to be taken up whence he came down, he handed down the faith
to his holy disciples: going, he says, make disciples of all the nations,
baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy
Spirit.
We then according to the tradition of the Lord Jesus Christ, and according to
the faith of the holy fathers three hundred eighteen gathered in Nicaea, as we
received so also we baptize, into the name of the Father and of the Son and of
the Holy Spirit; and as we baptize, so also we believe, into Father and Son and
Holy Spirit; and as we believe, so also we glorify, Father and Son and Holy
Spirit. Let us keep this faith I entreat, until the appearing of him who handed
it down to us, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, by the grace of God and our
Father, to whom is the glory and the power, with his only-begotten Son and the
holy and good Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages, amen.
