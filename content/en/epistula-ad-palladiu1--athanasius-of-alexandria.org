Of our father among the saints ATHANASIUS, archbishop of Alexandria, a letter
to PALLADIUS the presbyter.
To the beloved son Palladius the presbyter, Athanasius the bishop, greeting in
the Lord. And having received the letter written from you alone, I rejoiced,
especially that you breathed orthodoxy as usual. And the cause for which you
live with our beloved Innocent, not now first, but having learned from of old, I
accepted your piety. Thus then acting, write and make known to us how the
brothers there, and how the enemies of the truth think concerning us. And since
you also made clear concerning the monks in Caesarea; and I learned from our
beloved Dianus that they are grieving and standing against our beloved Basil the
bishop; I accepted you for making it clear, and I have made clear to them the
fitting things; that as children they may obey a father, and not contradict what
he himself approves. For if he were suspected concerning the truth, they would
fight well; but if they have taken courage, and we all have taken courage, that
he is a boast of the Church, contending rather for the truth, and teaching those
who need; one must not fight toward such a one, but rather accept his good
conscience. For from the things the beloved Dianus related, they appear to
grieve in vain. For he himself, as I have taken courage, becomes weak to the
weak, that he may gain the weak; and let our beloved, looking to the aim of his
truth, and the economy, glorify the Lord, who has given Cappadocia such a
bishop as each country also prays to have. And you then, beloved, be willing to
make it clear to them, that, as I wrote, they may be persuaded. For this also
establishes them as well-minded toward a father; this also will keep =26.1169=
the peace for the Churches. I pray that you be well in the Lord, beloved son.
