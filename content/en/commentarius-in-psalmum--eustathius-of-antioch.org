From the interpretation of the 15th psalm:
But indeed the [soul] of Jesus had experience of both. For it also came to be
in the place of human souls, and having come to be outside the flesh it lives
and subsists. It is therefore rational, and of the same substance as the souls
of men, just as the flesh also happens to be of the same substance as the
flesh of men, having come forth from Mary.

Of Eustathius of Antioch from interpretation of the 15th psalm:
God sent his son, become of a woman; not "become" in the plural, but "become";
for the person is unique; I did not say a unique nature; far be it, may it not
be; nor the same substance of flesh and Godhead, but I said one Lord Jesus
Christ, through whom are all things, recognized in the difference of the
natures according to all.
