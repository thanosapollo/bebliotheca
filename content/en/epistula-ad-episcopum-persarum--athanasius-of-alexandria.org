* Letter to the Bishop of the Persians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

=28.1565= To the beloved papa, and to the clergy under him who dwell in the
land of the Persians, Athanasius greeting in the Lord.
The holy God-inspired Scripture recounts to us the deeds of those of old,
guiding us from them to the useful things of what is to be done, as we abhor
every form of wickedness and impiety. In the Books of Kings, then, it says
that Israel has committed lawlessness, and worships the creation rather than
the Creator. And you know how great an evil it is, having left the Creator
and the glory due to him, to attach it to idols and creatures. When those
men did this, God withdraws his help from them, and they are delivered into
slavery to your kings. And having become captives, they are sentenced by
Salamanasar king of the Assyrians to a dwelling instead of their own land,
Palestine I mean, that of the Assyrians in Alae and in Amor, by the rivers
Gizan, and the mountains of the Medes. He brings in as a dwelling in their
place Samaria, Babylonians from Chuthan, and from Aian, and from Emath, and
Sepharvaim, and they were settled in the cities of Samaria in place of the
sons of Israel. And it came to pass at the beginning of their sitting, they
did not fear the Lord, and he sent the lions among them, and they were
killing them. And they said to the king of the Assyrians, saying: The
nations whom you removed, and settled instead (sic) in the cities of
Samaria, have not known the judg ment (sic) of the God of the land, and he
sent the lions among them, and behold they are putting them to death,
because they do not know the judgment of the God of the land. And the king
of the Assyrians commanded, saying: Lead away from there, and let them go,
and let them dwell there, and they shall enlighten them in the judgment of
the God of the land. And they brought one of the priests whom they had
removed from Samaria, and he sat in Bethel. And the priest was enlightening
them, that they might fear the Lord. And the transplanted, having been
taught the law given to the sons of Israel, did not keep it to the end; but
they were serving both the law in part and the customs. These things have
been used by those cast out by God, because they have not a pure but a mixed
worship, serving neither the law of Moses nor the ancestral =28.1568=
customs to the end. Knowing these things, beloved, let us offer to God a
pure worship, having no mixed form of impiety, but spotless and pious, in
which God is pleased. For he accepts no one, but rather condemns him who
serves two lords. Turn away from your ancestral idolatry; do not join
yourselves to heretics. For it is better for such not to have known the
truth, than having known it to turn back. For it must be said to them: A dog
that has returned to its own vomit, and a sow that has washed to a rolling
in the mire. But we are not so; for we have one God, from whom are all
things; a perfect Trinity, consubstantial, equal in power, equal in glory;
Father the fountain of all good things, from whom the Son was begotten, from
whom the Holy Spirit proceeds according to what is written; one Godhead
known in three hypostases. But in the last times, having had mercy on the
race of men that had slipped into impiety, that which was created according
to the image and likeness of God, turned to the worship of the works of
their hands, the only-begotten Word of God lowered himself into a voluntary
emptying, who is consubstantial with the Father according to the Godhead,
and the same consubstantial with us according to the humanity; and he is
incarnate from the holy Virgin and Theotokos; and he orders our life, having
taught how one must live through the teaching of the evangelical
proclamations; and he suffers in his own flesh, and having spoiled Hades, he
lived again on the third day, and has gone up into the heavens, and sat at
the right hand of the Majesty on high. Him we expect to come in the glory of
the Father judging living and dead; rendering to each according to his
works, to the pious those things which eye has not seen, and ear has not
heard, and which have not arisen upon the heart of man; but sending the
impious into the eternal fire, where the unending worm is, and the weeping
and the gnashing of teeth.
