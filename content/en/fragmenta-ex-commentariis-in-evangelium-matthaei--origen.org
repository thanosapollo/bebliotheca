OF THE COMMENTARIES ON MATTHEW
TOME 1
Matt. Inscr.
Euseb. h. e. VI,25, 4-6 Having learned as in tradition concerning the four
gospels, which also alone are undisputed in the church of God under heaven,
that first was written that according to Matthew, once a tax-collector, later
an apostle of Jesus Christ, who published it for those who believed from
Judaism, composed in Hebrew letters; and second that according to Mark, who
made it as Peter related to him, whom he also acknowledged as a son in the
catholic epistle through these words, saying: "She who is co-elect in Babylon
greets you, and Mark my son"; and third that according to Luke, the gospel
praised by Paul, which he made for those from the nations; after all that
according to John.
