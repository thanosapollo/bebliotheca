* On an Image of the Blessed Virgin
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Verses of the logothete of the genikon, kyr George Acropolites, made for an
image of the all-holy Theotokos that has the twelve dominical feasts.
Having saved common nature by a God-bearing birth, I ask you to steer my life
toward what is needful, and after life grant salvation, yes, pure mother of
the compassionate Word of God, whom you conceived from the words of an
archangel, whom, having borne, you offer in swaddling-clothes to the temple,
who also cleanses the nature of mortals, having been baptized, and shows
himself light-formed to the disciples, gives life to Lazarus, goes forth with
palms, and dying on the wood is covered with a stone, but having risen and
going up to the pole sends the Spirit from the Father to his friends, and
translates you too and sets you beside the throne, wishing the pious to have
you as protectress; and I, presenting Kaloeidas by surname, and Nicholas by
name, may I find a great lot even if I ask for Eden after life.
