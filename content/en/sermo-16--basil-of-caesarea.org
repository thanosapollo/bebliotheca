* Sermon 16
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Brother, if in your absence it happens that you are reviled by someone, and
another comes and reports to you that such-and-such a brother reviled you,
you, as a prudent man, recognize whose plot it is. And do not grow bitter
against your brother. And say also to the one who came and reported it to you
thus: even if my brother happens to have insulted me, nevertheless he did not
do this of himself; the evil one prepared this, that he might cast a fight
between us; but the Lord will bring the evil one to nothing, and will have
mercy on my brother, and may he not abandon us.
