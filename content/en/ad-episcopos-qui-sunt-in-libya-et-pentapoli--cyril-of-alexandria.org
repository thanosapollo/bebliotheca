OF THE SAME TO THE BISHOPS IN LIBYA AND PENTAPOLIS D
[Preface]
One must make a care of every useful and necessary thing toward a building of
the peoples, and also completing unto a reputation of the holy churches; for it
is written, that you make the sons of Israel pious. Fathers then of monasteries
of those in the Thebaid province, pious men and 282 having a way of life not
unwonderful, having come in Alexandria, and being asked by me the state of the
monasteries there, taught that many are scandalized because of such a cause.
(a) Concerning not ordaining unexamined. Some newly married and as having come
down from the very bridal chambers snatch some of the most God-beloved bishops,
and with perhaps no one announcing the things concerning them, they are ordained
clerics, or at any rate presbyters.
(b) Concerning those going out from monasteries and being ordained. And certain
others thrown out from the monasteries themselves as disorderly, again run under
the ordinations, and having become clerics they also go in even into the
monasteries themselves, whence they have been thrown out, and they wish to offer
and to fill as many things as it is custom for clerics; and to do these things,
so that some of those who know them also decline 283 both the synaxes and do not
endure to communicate when those are liturgizing.
(c) Concerning how and when one must ordain and whom one must examine. Since
then toward a building, as I said, of the peoples all things must be done by us,
let your piety watch these things; and if someone is about to be ordained a
cleric, let him search around his life, and whether sometime he has a wife or
not, and how or when he took her, and if he has kept away; and if he is not one
of those thrown out either by another most pious bishop or from monasteries; and
then let him ordain, having been found unslandered.
For thus we will keep both our own conscience clean and the holy and venerable
liturgy unslandered. E Concerning the catechumens and those repenting, if they
would draw near the end. And if some having been rebuked because of faults
undergo a separation, 284 then are about to die, being catechumens, let them be
baptized, and let them not depart from the human things unpartaking of the
grace, or at any rate uncommunicated; for this also seems to attend to the
ordinances of the church. Greet the brotherhood with you; that with us greets
you in the Lord.
