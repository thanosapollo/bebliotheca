* Exhortatory Sermon
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

For God requires three things from every Christian: right faith, truth from
the tongue, and chastity from the body. And likewise keep the other things:
first do not speak, but when you hear someone slandering, flee, withdraw, so
that you too may not become like the slanderer, and go away into eternal
punishment, to fiery dragons eating the tongue. 10 For God said through the
prophet that the eyes of those who wink are dug out by unmerciful angels.
Then those who laugh will weep bitterly, then those who babble will groan
piteously, being condemned into the fiery river, into the dynon Hades, into
the sleepless worm, into the bitter weeping, into the gnashing of teeth. 11
Fast on Wednesday and Friday, but on the other days you have authority: if
you wish, fast; if you wish, do not fast. As God made man to breathe
according to nature, so he made it fitting also for the women that they
should always fast. 12 When you rise in the morning make the type of the
precious cross upon all your members. And walking and sleeping and always
say: Lord Jesus Christ, help us. Evening and morning go away to the church,
and confess to God the sins which you have done, and see, O man, that you
swear neither by heaven nor by earth, for heaven is the throne of the Master,
and the earth is the footstool of his feet, but if force come upon you from
someone to swear, say to him: I speak truth and I do not lie. With men and
babbling women, never sit together. 13 And see, O man, that you not become
sharp-tempered, because the devil brings on irascibility, that you may become
sharp-tempered and lose your soul; but God would give meekness, that through
meekness and almsgiving you may be saved. And see, O man, that you not love
the adornment of garments, nor desire sloth, but rather wear humble and cheap
things for the sake of God, so that from there our God may clothe you with a
robe of glory, a garment of incorruption, a crown of beauty, a diadem of
kingship, so that then your soul may have boldness toward God saying: Lord,
in this vain world I was much in want that I may enjoy your kingdom, Master;
and then our Master Christ, lover of mankind and merciful, will say: where
you have labored little, I have first prepared for you, but come, enter into
the bridechamber of my glory, and enjoy what eye has not seen, and ear has
not heard, and what has not arisen upon the heart of man, as many things as I
have prepared for those who love my name. 14 For in eighty years the time of
man is fulfilled; even if we live in luxury and are rich fifty years and a
hundred, and after these things what is set forth? Blessed are those who are
eager to attain the unending kingdom. Blessed is that man who through his own
wealth may ransom his own soul from the punishment, for a man's own wealth
ransoms him, and again: Blessed is he who understands upon the poor and
needy; in an evil day the Lord will deliver him. What evil day does the
prophet speak of? He was narrating to us concerning the day of judgment; for
what is worse than punishment? Truly nothing. For what is impossible for God,
to bring down rains of gold upon the earth so that all may have wealth? With
God all things are possible. So that the poor man may become forgiveness of
the sins of the rich, and the rich of the poor, because to God belongs honor
and glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
