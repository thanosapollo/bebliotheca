* Fragments of a Prayer and Exorcism
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

We call upon you, God Almighty, Lord Jesus Christ, heavenly King; we call upon
you who made heaven and earth. I adjure you, unclean spirit, by the Word of
God who made heaven and earth. Fear him who wraps himself in light as in a
garment; who alone has immortality and unapproachable light; to whom be glory
and power unto the ages. Amen.
