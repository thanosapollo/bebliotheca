* Letter to the Emperor Jovian
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of bishop Athanasius to Jovian Augustus concerning orthodox faith
Our Lord Jesus Christ, legislating at the baptism, made the exact and turned
bound of the faith, saying to the holy apostles: "going, make disciples of all
the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of
the Holy Spirit". Therefore the perfection in faith is into Father and Son and
Holy Spirit. The holy Trinity then not admitting addition, altogether also,
the Only-begotten his Word, having of the Father's good pleasure voluntarily
partaken of the things in us from the Holy Spirit and Mary the virgin mother,
is again one Son also with the taking-on by the economy of the union. For at
once flesh, at once flesh of the Word of God and at once ensouled rational, at
once ensouled rational flesh of the Word of God; for in him it also had the
hypostasis. Whence justly, rather even truly, the things taken on will be
confessed as the Lord's own and not of some other. For first the Lord with her
in a divine manner and then from her the same one economically a child,
wherefore also the holy Mary is virgin-mother as Theotokos. One therefore he
seems rather to say and to confess the nature and hypostasis of the Word,
incarnate and having perfectly become man, and he who does not say this is a
fighter against God and an enemy of the holy fathers.
