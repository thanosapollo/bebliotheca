The book of the generation of men, on the day in which God made Adam according
to his image and likeness. Thus then the first man came to be from God, whose
name was Adam, and his wife Eve; male and female God made them. And Adam begot
from his wife three sons, Cain, Abel, and Seth, and two daughters, Azoura and
Asouam. And Adam according to the command of God set names on all the
four-footed creatures and birds and amphibians and reptiles and fishes and on
his own children; but his own name and that of his wife an angel of the Lord
told them. And Cain took to wife his first sister Azoura, and Seth the second,
Asouam; and Abel was both virgin and righteous and a shepherd of sheep, from
which having brought sacrifices to God and been accepted he is slain, envied
by his brother Cain.

=4= And Cain being a farmer, and after the condemnation having lived worse,
first invented measures and weights and boundaries of land; then having
founded in the land of Naid a city opposite Eden he named it after the name of
his son Enos, and having compelled his own household to come together into one
he occupied himself with wars. And during these things, his house having
fallen upon him, he died, as some say, but others that Lamech killed him. But
Cain begot Enos, and Enos Gaidad, and Gaidad Maleleel, and Maleleel
Mathousala, and Mathousala Lamech, who also taking two wives, Eldan and Selan,
begot Jobel and Joubal and Thobel. And Jobel showed forth cattle-rearing, and
Joubal showed forth the psaltery and the cithara, and Thobel the hammer-work
of bronze and iron. And the one chose to dwell in tents and to raise cattle
and to farm, and the other invented cithara-playing and tragedy in addition to
the demonic practices, and the other contrived to furnish swords and arms for
wars. The race of Cain then, having been deemed worthy of mention as far as
this, is separated from the number of the fathers, that it may neither be
ranked with the first nor be the narrator of those that follow. The saying
then "everyone who kills Cain will loose seven vengeances" has this solution:
each man will die by his own sin, but you =5= who began the murder and became
a guide of the sin to the others will loose seven vengeances, that is, you
will be punished sevenfold and very greatly. For it is customary for Scripture
to use this number as sevenfold, and it is significant of an undefined
multitude, as "render to our neighbors sevenfold" and "the barren has borne
seven" and "I do not say to you until seven times, but until seventy times 7."
For among the things dared by Cain the first sin is envy, 2nd guile, 3rd
murder, 4th that it was of a brother, 5th that it was the first murder, 6th
that he made mourning for the parents, 7th that he lied to God; these then are
the 7 sins of Cain. And the punishments brought upon him for his chastisement
by the divine justice are these. 1st cursed is the earth from you, 2nd you
shall work the earth, 3rd it will not add to give you its strength, 4th
groaning and trembling, after the 5th together, you shall be upon the earth,
6th which Cain himself disclosed, saying, If you cast me out now from you I
shall also be hidden from your face, 7th that the punishment is not even
hidden, but is made known to all by a manifest sign; for the Lord God, it
says, set a sign on Cain that everyone who found him should not kill him.

And Adam was 230 years old when he begot Seth. This Seth first discovered
Hebrew letters and wisdom and the signs =6= of heaven and the turnings of the
years and the months and the weeks, and he set names on the stars and on the
five planets, that they might be recognized by men and that only, and the 1st
planet-star he called Kronos, the 2nd Zeus, the 3rd Ares, the 4th Aphrodite,
the 5th Hermes; for the two luminaries, sun and moon, God named. And Seth
being 205 years old begot the other Enos, who hoped to call upon the Lord.
After this his descendants and Seth, having been forewarned from above, wrote
the things about to be concerning the flood on tablets of stone and of clay,
signifying that the whole was about to be consumed and destroyed either by
fire or by water, and if by fire, the writings would be saved on clay tablets,
but if by water, on stone ones. And when the flood came and destroyed all,
Noah alone was saved and his sons and the daughters toward a second renewal of
the world. And after the flood Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, composed
astronomy, having found the naming of Seth and of his children, as has been
said, of the stars written on a stone tablet; which stele after the flood
remained on the mountain of Siris, and it is until now, as Josephus says. Adam
then according to the most wise and divine interpreter =7= Moses, having
become 230 years old, begot Seth, and he dies according to the day of the
transgression; for the day of God having the interval of a thousand years,
having lived only 930 years he comes to his end. This man is said to have been
buried in the land of Jerusalem, as Josephus says. And Seth having become 205
years old begot Enos, and after this he lived 707 years, and he died having
lived in all 912 years. Concerning whom it has been said "the sons of God went
in to the daughters of men," that is, those from Cain. For the men of that
time addressed him as God because he discovered the Jewish letters, the
namings of the stars, and in addition to these also having marveled at his
great piety, so that he was also first called and named God, as the Lord also
says to Moses, "I have given you as a god to Pharaoh," and concerning the
virtuous and spiritual and the judges he said, "You shall not speak ill of
gods and you shall not speak evil of the rulers of your people." Fittingly the
children of Seth and of Enos and Enoch are to be understood as sons of God and
sons of the gods according to Symmachus, who having been caught by wantonness
went in to the daughters of Cain, from whom from the corresponding mixed =8=
marriage the giants come to be, through the righteous Seth strong and very
great, but through the unjust and profane Cain wicked and most evil. And Enos
having become 140 years old begot Cainan, and after this he lived 740 years,
and he begot Maleleel, and he died having lived in all 910 years. And Maleleel
having become 165 years old begot Jared, and after this he lived 730 years,
and he died having lived in all 811 years. And Jared having become 162 years
old begot Enoch, and after this he lived 800 years, and he died, concerning
whom the translation has been said, having lived in all 962 years. And Enoch
having become 165 years old begot Mathousala, and after this he lived 802
years, and he died having lived in all 969 years. And Lamech having become 188
begot Noah, and after this he lived 565 years, and he died having lived in all
753 years. Two =9= Lamechs then Scripture mentions, one the one from Cain, and
the other the father of Noah; for Noah has been begotten through Lamech not
from Cain who had also murdered a man and a young man, but from a later and
homonymous one from Seth; for that one says "I have killed a man for a wound
to me and a young man for a bruise to me." And as having committed two murders
and having taken the wives of the two, Eldan and Selan, he blames himself as
being worthy of punishment seventy times seven, because from Cain, he says,
vengeance is taken seven times, but from Lamech seventy times seven, for this
reason he is punished more greatly, even as Lamech also has suffered thus; for
he killed the brothers of the righteous Enoch, who having prayed in faith
through these not to see such a death, and having been heard, was translated.
And Enoch was from the righteous Seth, from whom Christ is descended, being
genealogized according to the reverse reckoning of the divine and pious Luke
as far as Seth and Adam and God. And Noah having become 500 years old begot
three sons, Shem, Ham, Japheth. And after 100 years from his own sons being
born he went into the ark, being 600 years old, and thus the flood comes to
be. And in the 601st year of his life he went out from the ark, and after the
flood he lived =10= 350 years, and he died having lived in all 950 years. And
since at some time the flood ceased, the fear was now yielding, but the things
of despondency were stretching very much upon him, and he suffered the
prophetic thing which Amos says concerning the day of the Lord, "as if a man
should flee from the face of a lion and a bear should fall upon him and he
should go into his house and lean his hands upon the wall and a serpent should
bite him," so it was happening then. For having looked out from the ark and
been delivered from that foul-smelling and constricted prison, another storm
succeeded him no less than the former, when he had considered the want and
destruction of the race.

And Shem after two years of the flood, having become 102 years old, begot
Arphaxad, and after this he lived 500 years, and he died having lived in all
602 years. And Arphaxad having become 135 begot Cainan, and after this he
lived 403 years, and he died having lived in all 538 years. And Cainan having
become 109 years old begot Sala, and after =11= this he lived 330 years, and
he died having lived in all 367 years. And as for Sala, his father when he had
grown educated him in letters; and indeed at some time Sala having gone to
survey a colony, coming upon the land of the Chaldaeans encountered letters
engraved upon certain rocks; and these were in fact the tradition of the
Watchers; and Sala having written these down both himself sinned in them and
taught the others the same absurdity. And Sala having become 130 years old
begot Heber, and after this he lived 403 years, and he died having lived in
all 533 years. And Heber having become 134 begot Jektan and Phalek. And in the
time of Heber men think to construct a heaven-high tower toward escape from
the flood, as though God were plotting against them; for they were all of one
voice. And Nebrod the giant, son of Chous the Ethiopian, was giving orders and
hunting animals he furnished for food. And Heber, the son of Sala, had been
entrusted to press the building of the tower and of Babylon, which is
confusion. And when all had rushed upon the building of the tower and of the
city they had 72 leaders, but Heber alone did not consent to their irrational
work; wherefore also the tongues of all were confused, those who had consented
being divided into 72 tongues according to the number of their rulers; to this
Heber alone =12= God did not withhold the ancient speech. Whence those who
remained in the former tongue named it Hebrew according to the name of him who
possessed it, Heber, into the race of the Hebrews, who are called Hebrews both
patronymically and by derived name. Wherefore also those from Adam used the
tongue of the Hebrews, and those descended around Abraham and Noah and the
second Adam, I mean Christ, it is clear used the speech of the Hebrews
according to the first of all, Adam, using it, as ephatha and talitha koum and
lima sabachthani and the like. And indeed also in addition to these the names
of the ancients are a clear proof that this is the speech before the
confusion, and by this the thing sought is interpreted, in cases where
according to no tongue is it possible to resolve the meaning, as the
appellations of Adam and of the rest are interpreted through no other, except
through the Hebrew alone. For this tongue they also call Syrian, as the very
learned Origen thus testifies, interpreting from the Syrian book and next
having called the dialect of the Hebrews Syrian, since the ancients named
Judaea Syria and the Palestinians Syrians. The tower then was built in the
time of Heber in forty years, and it is demolished, its =13= traces alone
being preserved in the midst of Assour and Babylon. And it was 5433.

And Phalek having become 130 years old begot Ragau, and after this he lived
209 years, and he died having lived in all 339 years. There are then from Adam
until the flood 2262 years, and from the flood until the confusion of the
tower-building and the end of Phalek, which is division, 536 years. And thus
there comes to be a distribution, that is a dispersion, of the sons of Noah
and of those begotten from them; wherefore they were also called meropes both
from the divided speech and from the division of the earth. Shem however begot
Elam and Assour and Arphaxad and Loud, and Ham Chous and Mesraim and Phoud and
Chanan, and Japheth Gamer and Magog and Theran and Jouan and Jektan and Thobel
and Mosoch and Madai, from whom the Medes having come to be and having
mastered the Babylonians, the country was named Media properly and by derived
name.

After the confusion then and the dissolution of the tower the three sons of
Noah send for all those who had come from them, and they give them a written
allotment of the places, =14= which they had received from their father Noah,
and they assign to each and to each one's tribes and families a place and
climates and countries and islands and rivers according to the exposition
lying below, and they are allotted to the firstborn son of Noah, Shem, from
Persia and Bactria as far as India and Rhinocorura the parts toward the east,
and to Ham from Rhinocorura as far as Gadeira the parts toward the south, and
to Japheth from Media as far as Gadeira the parts toward the north. And the
countries that fell by lot to Shem are these: Persia, Bactriane, Hyrcania,
Babylonia, Cordyaea, Assyria, Mesopotamia, Arabia the ancient, Elymais, India,
Arabia the blessed, Coele Syria, Commagene and all Phoenicia and the river
Euphrates; and to Ham Egypt, Ethiopia that looks toward the Indians, another
Ethiopia, whence the river of the Ethiopians goes forth, the Red Sea looking
toward the east, Thebais, Libya that stretches as far as Cyrene, Marmaris,
Syrtis, another Libya, Numidia, Massyris, Mauretania opposite Gadeira; and in
the parts toward the north the things beside the sea he has Cilicia, =15=
Pamphylia, Pisidia, Mysia, Lycaonia, Phrygia, Camalia, Lycia, Caria, Lydia,
another Mysia, Troas, Aeolis, Bithynia, ancient Phrygia, and islands likewise
Sardinia, Crete, Cyprus, and the river Geon which is also called Nile; and to
Japheth Media, Albania, Armenia both small and great, Cappadocia, Paphlagonia,
Galatia, Colchis, Bosporus, Maeotis, Derbe, Sarmatis, Taurianis, Bastarnis,
Scythia, Thrace, Macedonia, Dalmatia, Molosse, Thessaly, Locris, Boeotia,
Aetolia, Attica, Achaea, Pellene, that called Peloponnesus, Arcadia, Epirotis,
Illyris, Lychnitis, Adriake, from which the Adriatic sea; and he has also
islands Britain, Sicily, Euboea, Rhodes, Chios, Lesbos, Cythera, Zacynthus,
Cephallenia, Ithaca, Corcyra, and a certain part of Asia, that called Ionia,
and the river Tigris that marks the boundary between Media and Babylonia.

=16= Of these then that had been allotted, the son of Ham, Chanaan, having
seen the land toward Lebanon as both good and fertile and differing greatly
from his own land, tyrannically seized it, and drove out those of the lots of
Shem, and thus all the land of the promise has been named of Chanaan. From
this point then the just judge afterward gave it back to the sons of Shem,
Israel, through Jesus the son of Nave, as he had also promised beforehand to
Abraham. And Ragau having become 132 years old begot Serouch, and after this
he lived 207 years, and he died having lived in all 339 years. And Serouch
having become 130 years old begot Nachor, and after this he lived 6 years, and
he died having lived also in all 330. And Nachor, the son of Serouch, having
become 79 years old begot Tharra, and after this he lived 129 years, and he
died having lived in all 208 years. And Tharra having become 70 years old
begot Abraham and Nachor and Aran, the father of Lot, who also died before his
father Tharra, and after this Tharra lived 135 years, and he died having lived
in all 205 years.

And in the times previously spoken of there came to be a certain giant, by
name =17= Nebrod, son of Chous the Ethiopian, from the tribe of Ham, who
having founded the city Babylon and first having shown forth hunting and the
magic of the Persians held the first place, having taught them astronomy and
astrology, all the things concerning those being born supposedly being
signified by the heavenly motion; from which the Hellenes having learned
genethlialogy began to refer those born to the motion of the stars. Therefore
both astronomy and astrology and magic began from the Magousaeans, that is the
Persians; for the Persians are addressed as Magog by the natives, who say that
Nebrod was deified and came to be among the stars of heaven, whom they call
Orion, concerning whom Moses says that the beginning of his kingdom was
Babylon and Chalane in the land of Senaar, whence Philistiim went out from the
tribe of Shem of Assour, from whom the Assyrians. And having mastered Syria
and Persia and the remaining parts of the east there appeared also another, a
son of a certain one called Ouranos and of Aphrodite, his wife, a gigantic
man, named Kronos according to the name of the planet-star; and this one too
having become very powerful and having subjected many and having become lord
first showed forth the reigning and mastering of the other men. Who also
having first reigned over Syria 56 years subjected all =18= Persia, having
begun from Syria. And having a wife Semiramis, who is also called Rhea among
the Assyrians, he had also two sons and one daughter, and the one he addressed
as Zeus after the name of the planet-star, and the other he named Ninos, and
the daughter Hera, whom Picus who is also Zeus took to wife, his own sister.
And after Kronos Ninos his son reigned 52 years. Who having taken his own
mother Semiramis to wife, it became a law for the Persians to take their own
mothers and sisters because Zeus also, who is also called Picus, took his own
sister Hera. And Ninos having become master of Syria, and having founded a
city exceedingly great, of a journey of three days' road, called it after his
own name Nineve, and first reigns in it. From whom also came to be Zoroastros
the renowned astronomer of the Persians; who being about to die prayed to be
consumed by heavenly fire, saying to the Persians, If the fire burns me, take
from my burning bones and keep them, and the kingdom will not fail from your
country, so long as my bones are kept. And having prayed to Orion he was
consumed by aerial fire, and the Persians having taken from his ashen bones
keep them until now.

=19= And after Ninos there reigned over the Assyrians a certain one by name
Thouras 30 years, whom they also renamed Ares after the name of the
planet-star. To which Ares the Assyrians first having set up a stele and until
now worshipping him as a god call in Persian Baal god, that is Ares the
warlike. Of whom Daniel also makes mention as being worshipped and served
among the Persians. And after Ares Lames reigned 20 years. And after Lames
Sardanapallos the great reigned 35 years, and he was slain by Perseus. And the
flatterers and imitators of that man's love of flesh and gluttony and raging
lust inscribed as if from him on his tomb such things as these. I have so much
as I wantoned and ate and drank and lived with pleasant love. And having
suffered many things all the prosperous things have been left; for even now I
am ash, having reigned over great Ninos. And the brother of Ninos, Picus who
is also Zeus, continued reigning over Italy. And in the same times there was
neither city nor =20= any administration in the west, but simply all that land
was inhabited from those who had migrated there from the tribe of Japheth, son
of Noah. And Picus who is also Zeus lived 120 years, mastering the west and
reigning over it. And he had many sons and daughters from the comely women.
And Picus who is also Zeus himself had a son named Faunus, whom he also called
Hermes after the name of the planet-star. And after the death of this Picus
who is also Zeus his son Faunus who is also Hermes reigned over Italy
thirty-five years, who was a cunning and mathematical man; who first
discovered the mine of gold in the west and the smelting. And knowing that his
brothers were jealous of him, those from the women whom Picus who is also
Zeus, his father, had; for they wished to murder him, being many, around
seventy; for mixing with many women Zeus begot children; having pocketed much
gold in excess he withdrew, and he goes away into Egypt to the tribe of Ham,
son of Noah, who received him in honor. And he spent time there being arrogant
over all, and wearing the golden robe he practiced philosophy among the
Egyptians, telling them divinations of things to come; for he was by nature
exceedingly logical; and they worshipped him, saying god Hermes, as telling
the things to come and ministering to them from the =21= god the answer of the
things to come and furnishing them money. Whom they also called giver of
wealth, naming him as the gold god. When therefore this Hermes came into
Egypt, there reigned then over the Egyptians from the race of Ham Mestraim.
When this man had died the Egyptians made Hermes king. And he reigned over the
Egyptians 39 years in arrogance. And after him Hephaestus reigned over the
Egyptians 1680 days, so as to be 4 years, 7 months, 3 days; for the Egyptians
did not then know how to measure years, but they called the circuit of the day
years. And this same Hephaestus they called a god; for he was a warrior and a
mystic. Who having come into war fell together with his horse, and having been
struck remained lame. And this same Hephaestus set a law that the Egyptian
women should be married to one man and live temperately, and that those found
in adultery should be punished. And the Egyptians gave thanks to him, because
they received this as the first law of temperance. And this same Hephaestus
from a certain mystic prayer received the tongs out of the air for the
fashioning of arms from iron; whence he was also found master of iron in the
wars; they therefore dei =22= fied him, as having legislated temperance and
having found provision for men through the fashioning of arms and having made
power and salvation in the wars; for before him they used to war with clubs
and stones.
