Encomium on Holy Mary the Theotokos.

Our word is bright, and filled with grace, since the synod of the holy Fathers
is also splendid. For while I was in great grief because of the lawless
blasphemy of Nestorius, they stirred up this much-resounding and manifold and
angelic and heavenly theatre. These are the teachers of piety, the pillars and
roof-beams of the faith, the unshaken towers, the calm-bearing harbors, the
faithful and prudent stewards, the wise master-builders, those who live the
ethereal course and an angel-equal life upon the earth, tent-fellows of the
prophets and throne-fellows of the apostles, and protectors of the holy
Churches, avengers of the foul blasphemy, wise champions of our poverty, or
rather of our praise, those who hold the fair-speaking and God-formed throne
of the high priesthood, those who pour forth honey-flowing streams, spiritual
orators of the wisdom of God, who have walked the four-cornered inhabited
world with unwearied journeying, whom neither burning heat, nor seas,
=77.1032= nor the savage wrath of raging waves, nor stormy triple-surges cast
into hesitation; but leaping with faith-bearing steps they have come here,
leaving all hesitation behind through longing, or rather through fear of the
Lord, and taking up the cross as wise avengers of Mary the Theotokos, they
have arrived here. Therefore, taking courage also from their holy prayers, let
us first greet this city with a few words, and thus I will stretch out my
speech toward the encomia of the Theotokos.

Hail, O city of the Ephesians, or rather sea-spectacle, because instead of
earthly harbors, angelic and heavenly harbors have come to you. Hail, boast of
the Asian diocese, because as with precious pearls you have been built around
with the temples of the saints, and now, being trodden by the footsteps of
many holy Fathers and patriarchs, you are sanctified. For truly the gates also
are blessed, and the streets, and the harbors, by the sojourn of the holy
Fathers. For where many shepherds drive their flocks, through them the
assembly of holiness becomes great; and especially these God-loving and
faith-loving and angel-equal men drive out every satanic working, and Hellenic
idol-madness, of Porphyrians and Sabellians, of Phrygians and Apollinarians,
of Photinians, and ....... and simply every foul heresy is put to shame, while
the boast of the orthodox faith is glorified. Hail also yourself,
thrice-blessed John, apostle and evangelist, boast of virginity, teacher of
chastity, destroyer of the deceit of the demons, overthrower of the temple of
Artemis, harbor and champion of the Ephesians, nourisher of the poor, refuge
of the afflicted, refreshment and rest of those near and those far. Hail,
undefiled vessel filled with prudence.

For to you also our Lord Jesus Christ, being taken up upon the cross, handed
over the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin, as to a virgin. Hail also yourself, Mary
Theotokos, Virgin-Mother, Light-bearer, undefiled vessel. Hail, Virgin Mary,
mother and handmaid: Virgin, because of him who was born from you the Virgin;
mother, because of him who was borne in your arms and nourished with your
milk; handmaid, because of him who took the form of a servant. For the king
entered into your city, or rather into your womb, and again he went out as he
himself willed, and your gate was shut. For you conceived without seed, and
you gave birth in a manner befitting God. Hail, Mary, the lodging temple, or
rather the holy one, as the prophet David cries, saying: "Holy is your temple,
wondrous in righteousness." Hail, Mary, the treasure of the inhabited world;
hail, Mary, the undefiled dove. Hail, Mary, the unquenchable lamp. For from
you the Sun of righteousness has been born. Hail, Mary, the place of the
Uncontainable, who contained the Only-begotten God the Word, who brought forth
the unfading ear of grain without plough and seed. Hail, Mary =77.1033=
Theotokos, through whom prophets sing, through whom shepherds give glory,
saying with angels that fearful hymn: Glory to God in the highest, and on
earth peace, among men good will.

Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom angels dance, archangels leap, sending up
most awesome hymns. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom magi worship, led by a
torch-bearing star. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom the ten-rayed beauty of
the apostles has been chosen. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom John, while
still in his mother's womb, leaped, and the lamp worshipped the unsleeping
light. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom the unspeakable grace came forth,
concerning which the Apostle cried and said: The saving grace of God has
appeared to all men. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom the true light came
forth, our Lord Jesus Christ, who says in the Gospels: I am the light of the
world. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom light shone upon those sitting in
darkness and the shadow of death. For the people, he says, who sat in darkness
have seen a great light. What light, then, if not our Lord Jesus Christ, the
true light, which enlightens every man coming into the world? Hail, Mary
Theotokos, through whom it is proclaimed in the Gospels: Blessed is he who
comes in the name of the Lord; through whom also in cities and in villages and
in islands Churches of the orthodox have been founded.

Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom the conqueror of death and destroyer of
Hades came forth. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom the fashioner of the
first-formed man came forth, and the corrector of his transgression, and the
guide of the kingdom in heaven. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom the beauty
of the resurrection blossomed and shone forth. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through
whom baptism of holiness, fearful and Jordanian, flowed.

Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom John and Jordan are sanctified, and the
devil is dishonored. Hail, Mary Theotokos, through whom every believing breath
is saved. Hail, Mary Theotokos; for through you the waves of the sea also,
growing calm and being still, bore our fellow-servants and fellow-ministers
with joy and gentleness. For the earth also, once trodden by robbers, has been
changed into peace by the sojourn of the holy Fathers.

For it is written: How beautiful are the feet of those who preach good news of
peace! What peace? Our Lord Jesus Christ, the herald of peace, who says in the
holy Gospels: My peace I give to you. What peace? Which Nestorius, having
blasphemed, did not receive, saying that our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son Word
of God, was not born of the Virgin Mary, neither knowing the childbirth of
virginity as unwounded, nor believing the archangelic voice that says: Hail,
full of grace, the Lord is with you. =77.1036= For likening himself to the
Persian kingdom, and trying to set aside the only-begotten Son of God, he
mythologized these things unto the destruction of many and the blasphemy of
souls. But since in the former discourse I sang much concerning this badly
blaspheming rooster, when I introduced to you the partridge, which Jeremiah
the prophet proclaimed, saying, The partridge cried, he gathered what he did
not bring forth; and again this foul and blasphemous man, seeing the counts
shielding him, took courage in them and flapped his wings, and thinking to
strike us with fear, rather stirred us up; come, with groanings and tears let
us say as is our custom: It is good to trust in the Lord, rather than to trust
in rulers. For this man, this accursed one, having emptied his golden quiver,
stirred up against us consuls and counts, and a choir of magistri and of
empresses; but let us say that prophetic word: These in chariots, and these in
horses, but we in the name of the Lord our God. For if I had to speak
something even toward the authorities of the world, or toward the most pious
emperor himself, I will not be ashamed, nor will I play the coward, having the
prophet David continually advising me and saying: I spoke before kings and was
not ashamed.

Who has heard such fearful and terrible things? Christ and God the Word,
proclaimed to us by prophets and apostles, now being transformed into a man,
and the Theotokos being called a man-bearer! Let no one henceforth speak to me
of the abrupt daring of the thankless and foul Jews, which those ungrateful
men then dared against Christ. For who has now given boldness? He who
blasphemed the oracle of God, saying, You crucified a man. And still also the
deceit of the Hellenes, and lawless burnt-smells and unlawful sacrifices
offered to stones, and this too is a practice of ingratitude; nor yet the
bitter disease of Arius, and the most foul blasphemy of the Manichees, and the
pestilential practice of Sabellius and Porphyrius and Photinus the ill-minded,
into whom this man also, having peered, blasphemed; woe is me! the wickedness
of these has surpassed all, and there has been fulfilled in him that chapter
of that proud man who says: I will pull down my storehouses, and I will build
greater; to whom it was said: Fool, this night the angels require your soul
from you; and the things you have prepared, whose will they be? For as far as
you were raised on high, being a pollution and base-born, so far again you
were brought down, =77.1037= having fallen into a pit of blasphemy, whom Paul
the apostle did not persuade, the vessel of election, the ecclesiastical
cicada, the evangelical and heavenly incense, the myrrh-bearing spring, the
letter-bearer and letter-writer, who was blinded on the road and looked up
wholly toward the height of heaven by the knowledge of God, whom a vision of
Christ threw upon his face, and knowledge of God again led toward faith, who
established the inhabited world to believe in a consubstantial Trinity, in one
Lord, one faith, one baptism, one Father, one Son, one Holy Spirit, an
undivided essence, a Godhead not to be pried into, incomprehensible, God from
God the Lord, light from light, radiance of glory, born of the Virgin Mary,
according to the voice of the archangel who says: Hail, full of grace, the
Lord is with you. And again the same: The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and
the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore also the holy one
who is born shall be called Son of the living God. And not only through the
archangel Gabriel, but also through the blessed David we hear him singing
every day in the churches and saying: The Lord said to me: You are my Son;
today I have begotten you.

And high-minded Isaiah, son of Amos the prophet, prophet from a prophet,
proclaimed saying: Behold, the Virgin shall have in the womb, and shall bear a
son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which is interpreted, God with us.
But you do not believe prophets and apostles, and evangelists and the
archangel Gabriel? Imitate at least your fellow-demons, who shuddered at his
power and cried: What have we to do with you, Jesus, Son of God? Have you come
here before the time to torment us? For if even at that time the truth was
spoken by the demon as "before the time," yet now it has reached even you. The
Antichrist had to come; but quickly, in his place, you have arrived. You did
not even obey the devil who deceives you, who says: If you are the Son of God,
speak, that these stones may become bread. O fearful matter, and one not even
full of astonishment! The demons, with their father the devil, call him who
was born of Mary Son of God, and this man transfers the Son of God into a man,
and having found some overturning and an evil madness, he sought to unseat the
most pious emperor, who is a friend and servant of the orthodox faith, and
also a choir of holy empresses shining in faith; but he was not able to
proceed to the end. For giving the things of the poor to the gold-lovers, he
thought through them to conquer the truth, and through all-fair love of money
he fell into a most terrible shipwreck; dwelling in harbor, he was
shipwrecked, not holding the tillers of the faith. Therefore he has also gone
under the waves in his evils, and become a theatre of tragedy =77.1040= for
the whole inhabited world. For he thought through his all-evil and most bitter
mind to persuade the laborers of piety, not taking it into his darkened mind
to consider the teaching of Fathers and apostles and evangelists, and the
voices of archangels, having found some ruthlessness and an evil madness, and
having clothed himself in atheism, he wished to decree a pestilence and a
godless religion upon the inhabited world.

But he did not find us unprepared against his lawless and ill-speaking device.
For we do not take courage in military weapons, nor in golden-looking and
all-fair punishment, but through the holy and God-inspired Scriptures we make
our answers toward him. For our weapons are not fleshly, nor earthly, but
spiritual and heavenly, as the blessed Paul proclaimed. But let no one suppose
that in saying these things we rejoice over your fall, most wretched man; for
when you had fallen upon the wrestling-ground of this ill-speaking, we
stretched out a hand to you through letters, and you disobeyed our mediocrity;
for this reason you inherited the danger of disobedience. And that these
things are truly so, we furnish a trustworthy witness, the most holy
archbishop of the whole inhabited world, Father and patriarch Celestine of the
great city of Rome, who himself also advised you through letters, telling you
to stand away from the most mad ill-speaking. And you disobeyed him also, and
you boasted in the vanity of your madness; having become a razor of the whole
inhabited world, you troubled the four-cornered inhabited world, and you drove
all the saints together into this place with much toil and fear. For you wrote
wickedness, and not goodness at all.

For this reason God will pull you down completely from the priesthood, and you
shall be cut off from the wisdom of the Fathers, and having first been driven
from the imperial city, then also from the throne of the high priesthood, of
which you were unworthy. For this reason the righteous shall see and fear, and
they shall laugh at you and say: Behold a man who did not set God as his
helper, but hoped in the abundance of his wealth, and was strengthened in his
vanity.

But we all who have followed the truth of the Gospel shall be, according to
that prophetic word, as a fruitful olive tree in the house of God, glorifying
God the Father almighty, and his only-begotten Son who was born of Mary, and
the Holy Spirit, who gives life and grants life to all, being subject to most
faithful emperors, honoring also the holy empresses who shine in virginity;
and for her who is under marriage we shall pray to see a son born from them,
and that the God-loving and faith-loving and orthodox empire may remain in
Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom be glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
