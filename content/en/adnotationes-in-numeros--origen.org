ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON THE NUMBERS
=17.21= CH. 11. Verse 7. And the manna, as seed of coriander. And the as seed
of coriander, the rest bdellium; another says, from honey; Aquila and
Symmachus, oil of a breast, of a breast into fat. CH. 12. Verse 1, 2, 10, 15.
Miriam, having reviled Moses, was leprous, having sat outside the camp seven
days. And this happened toward fear of the people. For if he did not spare the
sister, nor another. CH. 14. Verse 36. And the men. Those blaming the land
showed God, according to them piously, a liar, praising Palestine. CH. 22.
Verse 2. And Balak seeing. Nations heard and were angered. Among which also
the: Rulers of Moabites, trembling took them. Verse 4. Because as a seer they
brought the wage of this to lead him. CH. 23. Verse 7. And taking up the
parable. The divine Scripture in certain places prefers and puts forward the
symbolic being of the historical narrative in its own hypothesis. CH. 24.
Verse 7. And he will lord over many nations. And there are many lords, and
many gods. And blessed are those who have our Lord Jesus Christ as lord; for
they will lord over many lordships. Verse 17. And a man will rise from Israel.
They say Balaam has pupils in the art, who were disciples to him in the magic;
and having an opinion concerning =17.24= him, to write down his prophecies,
and leave them, in which also the: A star will rise. Or also the Magi, having
received this from paternal tradition and succession, came to Bethlehem.
