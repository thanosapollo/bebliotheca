* Prologue 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Since our Lord Jesus Christ commands: What I say to you in the darkness, speak
in the light; and what you heard in the ear, proclaim upon the housetops; and
since the apostle shows the fearful judgment of silence, through the things he
says to the elders of the Ephesians: I testify to you this day that I am clean
from the blood of all; for I did not shrink from announcing to you the whole
counsel of God; and since he also commands us to do the same, in the things he
writes: Remind them of these things, testifying before God; because I am not
able to testify face to face to all concerning the commandments of the Lord, I
thought it necessary at least to write to the love of you all in Christ, both
to free myself from the judgment, and to give you full assurance concerning
the things that fall to you, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ; so that
neither you, from ignorance, should somewhere fall upon the sting of death,
which is sin, or pass by some commandment of God, concerning which it is
written that his commandment is eternal life, and fall away from this, the
Lord saying: He who disobeys the Son shall not see life, but the wrath of God
remains upon him; nor we be found guilty of the blood of those put to death by
sin through silence.
The judgment will set forth for me: first, the cause and the danger of so
great a disagreement and division of the churches of God and of each toward
the other; second, the proofs from the God-inspired Scripture that every
transgression of a commandment of God is punished vehemently and fearfully, so
that even if someone seems to have accomplished the greater part, yet if he
neglects a few, or even any one commandment whatsoever, or is indifferently
silent toward the one who has erred, and does not show a good zeal according
to the judgment of God, he pays the penalty for this alone; and even if he
suffer something of this kind from ignorance, not even so does he escape
unpunished; after these things it will contain the pious confession of God the
Father, and of his only-begotten Son and God, and of the Holy Spirit; then the
knowledge of the things distinguished for us by Scripture, of how many it is
necessary to abstain, and about which those who desire eternal life and the
kingdom of the heavens must be especially zealous; and likewise it will set
forth separately the things proper to each rank or order; upon these the
discourse will try to give us from the holy Scriptures a kind of summary and
brief character of a Christian, holding the form of a definition, and again a
similar character of those who preside over the word of the Lord's teaching;
in which both the exact and extremely purified quality of the way of life will
be made manifest, and the, that I may so name it, dignity of those who excel
in this will shine forth. Following these there will be joined for us all the
things that I answered, when asked by the brethren, toward the common training
of the life according to God. Copies of all of which I have sent to your love
in Christ, that there might be fulfilled in you or through us what was said by
the apostle, that you should commit these things to faithful men, who will be
able also to teach others.
