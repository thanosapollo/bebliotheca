* Spiritual Sayings Arranged Alphabetically

1 The beginning of salvation is the condemnation of oneself. 2 It is
better to cast a stone at random than a word. 3 Be to all as you wish
all to be. 4 Practice righteousness more in deed than in word. 5 Pious
is not the one who pities many, but the one who wrongs no one. 6 It is
better to live lying on a pallet and to be of good courage than to be
troubled while having a golden bed. 7 That one among friends is sweet
who nourishes the soul. 8 Revere God with works, hymn him with word, and
honor him in thought. 9 Prudence and life and nobility make a priest.
10 It is worst to be a slave to shameful passions. 11 Teach virtue with
word, and proclaim it with deed. 12 Do not think that having virtue is
the good, but having it as one ought. 13 Honoring the law you will live
according to the law. 14 That one is a stranger whose things of the
world are foreign. 15 The one who envies harms himself; for the one who
speaks ill against another is already suffering ill from himself. 16
Neither flatter nor provoke a rich man. 17 Wine strengthens the body,
but the word of God the soul. 18 Use foods not the pleasant ones, but
the beneficial ones. 19 Trying to please the crowds you will be like the
crowds. 20 Above all, make full use of the body. 21 That one is a lover
of labor for whom time is abundant. 22 Do not accept the outward form of
a Christian man, but the mind of the soul. 23 To blame the blameless is
a sin against God. 24 Great is the danger of ears and tongue.

Other sayings
25 It is absurd, while pursuing honors, to flee the labors through which
the honors come. 26 If you wish to know God, first know yourself. 27
Genuine among friends is the one who shares the circumstances. 28 It is
a terrible thing to think great things while doing small ones. 29 In
everything, the supposing hinders the knowing. 30 A demon is the leader
of irrational life. 31 The nobility of the soul is revealed from its
vigor. 32 That one is God-fearing who does not speak against himself.
33 A blameless man is a temple of God. 34 An unapproved life is the
worst possession. 35 Speak what one ought and when one ought, and you
will not hear what one ought not. 36 An unfailing friend is the greatest
possession. 37 A vainglorious mind is a disease of the soul. 38 A
stranger and a poor man are God's eye-salve - 39 The one who receives
him will quickly see again. 40 It is good to do good to all, but more so
to those who cannot repay. 41 An orator in righteousness is a cup of
cold water. 42 Temperance and meekness of soul are Cherubim. 43 To be
honored in old age is a proof of having been a lover of labor before old
age; earnestness in a young man is a pledge that he will be honored in
old age. 44 True contemplation is the upper room of the soul. 45 Loving
what one ought not, you will not love what one ought. 46 It is better to
possess counterfeit gold than a friend. 47 A pure soul after God is a
god. 48 O, away from self-love that hates all things!

Other sayings
49 The beginning of love is a conceiving of glory. 50 It is good to be
rich in meekness and love. 51 A proud thought is a winnower of virtue.
52 A smooth road comes about by almsgiving. 53 An unjust judge is a
polluted conscience. 54 It is a terrible thing to be a slave to the
passions of the flesh. 55 If you wish to be without grief, strive to
please God. 56 The one who cares well for himself is cared for by God.
57 A temperate heart is a harbor of contemplations. 58 When you wish to
know yourself, who you are, do not compare who you were, but what you
have become from the beginning. 59 A vainglorious soul is a waterless
pit. 60 A proud soul is an inn of robbers, and it hates the sound of
knowledge. 61 The evil wandering of a true man is not to know the
Scriptures. 62 If you fear God, know it from the tears. 63 Humility of
soul is an unbreakable weapon. 64 A man who loves virtue is a tree of
paradise. 65 Jesus Christ is the tree of life. Use him as one ought and
you will never die unto the age. 66 Do good to those who are truly poor
and you will eat Christ. 67 True strength is to eat the body of Christ.
68 If you love Christ, you will not forget to keep his commandments; for
from there a benefactor after God is revealed. 69 The friend according
to God flows with milk and honey in the true words, but a sluggish soul
will not have such a friend. 70 A pleasure-loving thought is a cruel
master. 71 To keep silent the truth is to bury gold. 72 The one who
fears God does all things according to God.
