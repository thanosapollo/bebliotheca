OF THE SAME, A DISCOURSE AGAINST THE MANICHAEANS
Of twelve syllogisms, that the evil is corruptible and generated and
non-existent, and the father of this, the devil, is not unbegotten.
If anything is evil, it is to be punished; and nothing punished is
incorruptible. Therefore nothing evil is incorruptible. Nothing corruptible is
unbegotten; and the evil is corruptible. Therefore the evil is not unbegotten.
Nothing is evil according to essence; because the evil is a quality. And
nothing qualitative is essence. Therefore the evil is not essence. All things
contrary of one another are corruptible; and the unbegotten beings are not
corruptible. Therefore the contraries are not incorruptible. Nothing
unbegotten is changeable; and the good is changed, having been mastered by the
evil. Therefore the good is not unbegotten. The unbegotten is not desirous of
some destruction, nor even destructive; and the evil is desirous of
destruction. Therefore the evil is not unbegotten. The divine Scriptures say
that not only the other evil ones, but also the devil himself, are given over
to punishments; and everything given over to punishment is changeable; and
nothing changeable is unbegotten. Therefore nothing of the evils is
unbegotten. Of things opposing through the whole, there is nothing common. So
that all necessity the good exists, the evil therefore not being. Everyone
reasonably desiring something has the desire attained, in that he reasonably
desires; therefore vice is of the corruptible things; being of the corruptible
things it will be made to vanish. And being made to vanish, it is not
unbegotten. If the evil is unbegotten, it belongs to it by nature to be evil.
And no one acting according to nature sins; therefore the unbegotten does not
sin. That which does not sin is not liable; and Satan is liable. Therefore not
unbegotten.
