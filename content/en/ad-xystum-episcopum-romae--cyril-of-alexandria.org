From Cyril, from the letter to Justus [corr. Xystus], bishop of Rome.
For I am neither convicted of having ever thought anything other than what
seems to the truth, nor have I ever said that the divine nature of the Word is
passible. And a little later: And I know that the nature of God is impassible
and =77.288= unchangeable and unalterable, even in the nature of humanity, and
that Christ is one in both and from both.
