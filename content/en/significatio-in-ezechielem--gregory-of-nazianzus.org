Signification on Ezekiel.
We think the man to be the rational; the lion, the irascible; the calf,
the concupiscible; the eagle, the conscience lying upon the rest, which
is the spirit of the man spoken of by Paul; the one seated upon them,
the Father; and the spirit, the Holy Spirit; and the cloud, the Son; and
from the north, from the introductory, going toward the better things.
Brightness, because of being enlightened; and fire, because of the
discipline; the flashing, because the chastisements come at intervals;
and the purified of the soul is called electrum; it is to be noted that
the likeness of the man goes first. And all rational nature is winged
with spiritual wings. And they bind one another together, because of
fellowship and concord. And a man's hand underneath, because of good
order. And the firmament, because of the firm. And a rainbow, because of
the peace and covenant toward us from God. Sapphire, the ineffable
things; crystal, the pure and unadulterated. The feet are straight,
because of sure footing and permanence. Fire whirling in the midst, not
slack and flowing apart. And brightness, because of the end of rest. And
the sole of the feet, as Symmachus, of a calf, because of the labor of
the earth; but as Aquila, round, because it is the most beautiful of
shapes. And the appearance of the wheels, as Symmachus, hyacinthine.
=36.668= And they turned back, as the appearance of bezeq, as if going a
hard way, not having the whole of the light, but as the appearance of
bezeq, which is of the lightning. If he completes the likeness of the
man, he rules over the other men; but if not, the contrary happens. The
lion has something, the proud, the vigorous, that we may use it well.
And the calf, that we may desire as we ought. Isaiah says: Thou shalt
sanctify him in burning fire. From the loins and above, electrum; for
the things above do not need chastisement. From the loins and below,
fire, the things of generation; and these need chastisement. The Spirit
first lifts up, purifying; then those not purified by this, the fire
purifies. And the brightness, a good end. For such are the
chastisements. One definition of a craftsman is to make the underlying
matter as he wishes, and as is possible to the art; just as God is good
and wise, but not as we are, so also is he craftsman; since he is simple
and uncompounded; for we, being compounded, are not thus. Wisdom was
justified from her children, calling the wise the children. And again
the Savior himself says to them in the saying: The sons of this age are
wiser than the sons of light, in their generation; calling sons of light
those enlightened by knowledge. And, We were by nature children of
wrath, not speaking of that according to nature, but that in truth those
who sin are liable to wrath. And concerning Judas it is written that
Son of perdition; and he called him son of perdition because he had done
the things of perdition. Isaiah went about for three years naked and
most unshod. They say that Moses, after hearing the oracles of God, no
longer touched his wife, nor begot children. The abomination of
desolation standing in a holy place, they say that the temple in
Jerusalem will be built later, as the Antichrist will be believed by
Jews to be Christ, and will sit, and will seem to be king of the whole
inhabited world. And he will come upon the desolation of the world; for
he is the abomination of desolation. =36.669= The three children, those
in Babylon, and those of the furnace, are said to be children of
Hezekiah, and Ezekiel to have been formerly a servant of Jeremiah.
