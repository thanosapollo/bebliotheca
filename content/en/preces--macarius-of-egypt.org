A prayer of Saint Macarius.
O God, be propitious to me the sinner, because I have done nothing good before
you. Deliver me from the Evil One, and deem me worthy uncondemnedly to open my
=34.448= unworthy mouth, and to hymn your all-holy name of the Father and of
the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Forgive me, Lord, every unfitting impulse of my
heart, you who search hearts and kidneys. For you know, Master of all, that they
are unwilling of me. Forgive me coming near to you unworthily, because I have
longed for you and I long. Forgive me the sinner, the wicked, the liar, the
impatient, the small-souled, the unstable, the slothful, the careless of your
holy commands, who has completed every and every kind of sin and lawlessness,
fornications, adulteries, softness on earth, in sea and in every place of your
mastery. Lord, before your unerring eyes I have not ceased to work all the
wicked and shameful things. And still the enemy does not cease to entangle me
with gluttonies and pleasures and wicked desires, both deceits and vainglories
and blasphemies. But you, as being only good and merciful, help me and save me
as the prodigal, as the publican, as the harlot, as the robber. Yes, Master,
lover of men, super-good, do not abominate me your sinful and useless household
servant, by the intercessions of our all-undefiled Lady Theotokos, and of all
your saints. A prayer to the holy angel who has been ordered from God to cover
and keep us.
Holy angel, who stands over my wretched soul and my miserable life, do not
abandon me the sinner, nor stand away from me because of my uncleanness, do not
give place to the wicked demon, to lord it over me by the overpowering of this
mortal body. Hold my wretched and slackened hand, and lead me into a way of
salvation. Yes, holy angel of God, who stands over my wretched soul and body,
forgive me all things, as many as I have distressed you all the days of my life,
and if I have sinned anything this present day. Cover me in the present night
and keep me from every spite and plot of the opposing one, that I may not in
some sin anger God. And intercede for me toward the Lord to establish me in his
fear and to show me a worthy slave of his goodness. Amen.
