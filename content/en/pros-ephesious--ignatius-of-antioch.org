* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also called Theophorus, to her who is blessed in greatness by
the fullness of God the Father, who was foreordained before the ages to be
always unto a glory abiding and unchanging, united and elect in true
suffering, by the will of the Father and of Jesus Christ our God, to the
church worthy of blessing, which is in Ephesus of Asia, abundant greeting in
Jesus Christ and in blameless joy.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
Having welcomed in God your much-loved name, which you have acquired by a just
nature according to faith and love in Christ Jesus our Savior; being imitators
of God, having rekindled in the blood of God the kindred work, you have
accomplished it perfectly;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
for having heard that I was bound from Syria for the common name and hope,
hoping by your prayer to attain in Rome to fighting with beasts, that by
attaining it I might be able to be a disciple, you hastened to see me;

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h3
:END:
since then I have received your multitude in the name of God in Onesimus, who
is beyond description in love, and is your bishop, whom I pray that you love
according to Jesus Christ, and that you all be like him. For blessed is he who
granted you, being worthy, to obtain such a bishop.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
Concerning my fellow-servant Burrhus, your deacon according to God, blessed in
all things, I pray that he may remain to your honor and that of the bishop.
And Crocus also, worthy of God and of you, whom I received as an exemplar of
the love from you, has refreshed me in all things, as the Father of Jesus
Christ shall also refresh him, together with Onesimus and Burrhus and Euplus
and Fronto, through whom I have seen you all in love.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h2
:END:
May I have joy of you always, if I am worthy. It is fitting then in every way
to glorify Jesus Christ who has glorified you, that being perfectly joined in
one submission, subject to the bishop and the presbytery, you may be
sanctified in all things.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
I do not command you as though I were somebody. For even if I am bound in the
Name, I have not yet been perfected in Jesus Christ. For now I am beginning to
be a disciple, and I speak to you as my fellow-learners. For I needed to be
anointed by you with faith, admonition, endurance, long-suffering.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
But since love does not let me be silent concerning you, I have therefore
taken the lead in exhorting you to run together with the mind of God. For
Jesus Christ, our inseparable life, is the mind of the Father, just as the
bishops appointed throughout the ends of the earth are in the mind of Jesus
Christ.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
Whence it is fitting for you to run together with the mind of the bishop,
which you also do. For your presbytery, worthy of its name, worthy of God, is
so joined to the bishop as strings to a lyre. Therefore in your concord and
harmonious love Jesus Christ is sung.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h2
:END:
And you individually also become a choir, that being harmonious in concord,
taking the key of God in unity, you may sing with one voice through Jesus
Christ to the Father, that he may both hear you and recognize you, by the
things you do well, as members of his Son. It is profitable then for you to be
in blameless unity, that you may always partake of God also.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
For if I in a short time had such fellowship with your bishop, not human but
spiritual, how much more do I count you blessed who are so mingled, as the
church with Jesus Christ, and as Jesus Christ with the Father, that all things
may be harmonious in unity?

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
Let no one be deceived: if anyone is not within the altar, he lacks the bread
of God. For if the prayer of one and of a second has such power, how much more
that of the bishop and of the whole church?

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h3
:END:
He therefore who does not come to the same place already acts proudly and has
separated himself. For it is written: God resists the proud. Let us then be
eager not to resist the bishop, that we may be subject to God.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
And the more anyone sees the bishop silent, let him fear him the more. For
everyone whom the master of the house sends for his own stewardship, we must
receive as the one who sent him. Therefore it is clear that we must look upon
the bishop as the Lord himself.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
Onesimus himself then praises exceedingly your good order in God, that you all
live according to truth, and that no heresy dwells among you. But you do not
even listen to anyone more than to one speaking concerning Jesus Christ in
truth.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
For some are accustomed to carry about the Name with wicked deceit, while
practicing other things unworthy of God; these you must shun as wild beasts.
For they are mad dogs, biting in secret; you must guard against them, for they
are hard to heal.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
There is one physician, both fleshly and spiritual, begotten and unbegotten,
God in man, true life in death, both from Mary and from God, first passible
and then impassible, Jesus Christ our Lord.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
Let no one then deceive you, as indeed you are not deceived, being wholly of
God. For when no strife has been fixed in you that can torment you, then you
live according to God. I am your offscouring, and I am consecrated for you
Ephesians, the church renowned through the ages.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
Those who are fleshly cannot do the things of the Spirit, nor those who are
spiritual the things of the flesh, just as faith cannot do the things of
unbelief, nor unbelief the things of faith. But even the things you do
according to the flesh are spiritual, for you do all things in Jesus Christ.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
But I have learned that certain persons passed through from there, having an
evil teaching; whom you did not allow to sow among you, stopping your ears, so
as not to receive what was sown by them, as being stones of the Father's
temple, prepared for the building of God the Father, being drawn up to the
heights by the engine of Jesus Christ, which is the cross, using as a rope the
Holy Spirit; and your faith is your windlass, and love the way that leads up
to God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
You are then all also fellow-travelers, God-bearers and temple-bearers,
Christ-bearers, bearers of holy things, adorned in all things with the
commandments of Jesus Christ; in whom I also, rejoicing, was counted worthy
through what I write to converse with you and to rejoice with you, that
according to the life of men you love nothing except God alone.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
And pray also without ceasing for the other men, for there is in them hope of
repentance, that they may attain to God. Permit them then at least to be
discipled by your works.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h2
:END:
Toward their wraths you be meek, toward their boastings you be humble-minded,
toward their blasphemies you offer the prayers, toward their error you be firm
in the faith, toward their wildness you be gentle, not being eager to imitate
them in return.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h3
:END:
Let us be found their brothers in gentleness; and let us be eager to be
imitators of the Lord, striving who may be more wronged, who deprived, who set
aside; that no plant of the devil may be found among you, but that you may
remain in all purity and sobriety in Jesus Christ, in flesh and in spirit.

* Chapter 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h1
:END:
These are the last times. For the rest let us be ashamed, let us fear the
long-suffering of God, lest it become judgment for us. For either let us fear
the coming wrath, or let us love the present grace, one of the two; only to be
found in Christ Jesus unto the true life.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h2
:END:
Apart from him let nothing be fitting for you, in whom I carry about my bonds,
the spiritual pearls, in which may it be granted me to rise by your prayer, of
which may I always be a partaker, that I may be found in the lot of the
Ephesian Christians, who also always agreed with the apostles in the power of
Jesus Christ.

* Chapter 12
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h1
:END:
I know who I am and to whom I write. I am a condemned man, you have obtained
mercy; I am under danger, you are established;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h2
:END:
you are the highway of those who are being slain unto God, fellow-initiates of
Paul, who was sanctified, who was attested, worthy of blessing, under whose
footsteps may I be found when I attain to God; Paul who in every letter
remembers you in Christ Jesus.

* Chapter 13
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h1
:END:
Be eager then to come together more frequently for thanksgiving to God and for
glory. For when you come together frequently to the same place, the powers of
Satan are destroyed, and his destruction is dissolved in the concord of your
faith.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h2
:END:
Nothing is better than peace, in which all war of heavenly and earthly things
is brought to nothing.

* Chapter 14
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15-h1
:END:
None of these things is hidden from you, if you have perfectly toward Jesus
Christ the faith and the love, which is the beginning of life and the end; the
beginning is faith, and the end is love. And the two becoming in unity are
God, and all the rest follow unto nobility of life.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15-h2
:END:
No one professing faith sins, nor does one who has obtained love hate. The
tree is manifest from its fruit. So those who profess to belong to Christ will
be seen by the things they do. For the work is not now a matter of profession,
but if one is found in the power of faith unto the end.

* Chapter 15
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16-h1
:END:
It is better to be silent and to be, than to speak and not to be. It is good
to teach, if the one who speaks does. There is then one teacher, who spoke and
it came to be; and even the things he has done in silence are worthy of the
Father.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16-h2
:END:
He who has truly obtained the word of Jesus is able also to hear his silence,
that he may be perfect, that he may act through what he speaks and be known
through the things in which he is silent.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16-h3
:END:
Nothing is hidden from the Lord, but even our secrets are near to him. Let us
then do all things as he dwells in us, that we may be his temples and he
himself in us our God; which also he is and will appear before our face, from
the things by which we justly love him.

* Chapter 16
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h17
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h17-h1
:END:
Do not be deceived, my brothers; those who corrupt households will not inherit
the kingdom of God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h17-h2
:END:
If then those who do these things according to the flesh died, how much more
if one corrupts the faith of God by evil teaching, for which Jesus Christ was
crucified? Such a one, having become filthy, will go into the unquenchable
fire, and likewise the one who hears him.

* Chapter 17
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h18
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h18-h1
:END:
For this reason the Lord received ointment upon his head, that he might
breathe incorruption upon the church. Do not be anointed with the stench of
the teaching of the ruler of this age, lest he take you captive from the life
that is set before you.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h18-h2
:END:
Why then do we not all become prudent, having received knowledge of God, which
is Jesus Christ? Why do we perish foolishly, being ignorant of the gift which
the Lord has truly sent?

* Chapter 18
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h19
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h19-h1
:END:
My spirit is an offscouring of the cross, which is a stumbling-block to those
who do not believe, but to us salvation and eternal life. Where is the wise?
Where the disputant? Where the boasting of those called intelligent?

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h19-h2
:END:
For our God, Jesus the Christ, was carried in the womb by Mary according to
the economy of God, of the seed of David and of the Holy Spirit; who was born
and was baptized, that by his suffering he might purify the water.

* Chapter 19
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h20
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h20-h1
:END:
And the virginity of Mary and her childbearing escaped the notice of the ruler
of this age, and likewise the death of the Lord: three mysteries of a cry,
which were wrought in the silence of God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h20-h2
:END:
How then was it manifested to the ages? A star shone in heaven beyond all the
stars, and its light was unspeakable, and its newness caused astonishment, and
all the rest of the stars together with sun and moon became a chorus for the
star, and it was exceeding its light beyond all; and there was confusion,
whence the newness unlike them.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h20-h3
:END:
Whence all magic was being dissolved and every bond of wickedness was
vanishing; ignorance was being destroyed, the old kingdom was being corrupted,
God being manifested humanly unto newness of eternal life; and that which had
been perfected with God was receiving a beginning. From there all things were
being stirred together because the dissolution of death was being planned.

* Chapter 20
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h21
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h21-h1
:END:
If Jesus Christ counts me worthy by your prayer and it is his will, in the
second booklet which I am about to write to you I will further make known to
you the economy which I have begun, unto the new man Jesus Christ, in his
faith and in his love, in his suffering and resurrection;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h21-h2
:END:
especially if the Lord reveals to me that you all, each by name, come together
in common in grace in one faith and in Jesus Christ, who according to the
flesh is of the race of David, the Son of man and Son of God, so that you obey
the bishop and the presbytery with an undistracted mind, breaking one bread,
which is the medicine of immortality, the antidote that we may not die but
live in Jesus Christ always.

* Chapter 21
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h22
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h22-h1
:END:
I am your ransom, and of those whom you sent to the honor of God to Smyrna,
from where I also write to you, giving thanks to the Lord, loving Polycarp as
I do you also. Remember me, as Jesus Christ also remembers you.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h22-h2
:END:
Pray for the church in Syria, from where I am being led bound to Rome, being
the last of the faithful there, as I was counted worthy to be found unto the
honor of God. Farewell in God the Father and in Jesus Christ, our common hope.
