Preface of the life of the holy fathers
In this book has been written down a virtuous ascesis and a wondrous manner of
life of the blessed and holy fathers, monks and anchorites in the desert,
toward zeal and imitation of those who wish to accomplish the heavenly way of
life and who want to travel the road that leads to the kingdom of the heavens;
and memorials of aged women and of renowned God-inspired mothers, who with a
manly and perfect mind accomplished the contests of virtuous ascesis, toward a
pattern and desire of those who wish to bind on the crown of continence and
chastity; because of † a certain most mighty man and in judgment much-learned
and in character peaceful and in heart pious and in mind God-loving, and
sharing toward those in need of the necessities, and by the very summit of
dignities through goodness of manners preferred above many chosen men, and
wholly guarded by the power of the divine Spirit, who commanded us, or rather,
if one must speak the truth, who woke our sluggish mind toward the
contemplation of the better things, toward imitation and rivalry of the
virtues of 4 the ascesis of our holy and immortal spiritual fathers and of
those who have lived in the good-pleasure of God in much hardship of the body;
so that we, having written down the lives of the unconquered athletes, might
send them to this man, proclaiming the manifest virtues of each one of the
great men. And the lover of this divine and spiritual longing is the best of
men, Lausus, who after the inclination of God has been appointed guardian of
the God-inspired and pious empire. I therefore, both uneducated in tongue and
having somehow tasted spiritual knowledge only at the surface, and unworthy of
the catalogue of the spiritual life of the holy fathers, fearing the
immeasurable greatness of the command beyond me, was hard put to bear the
command, which needed much both of the wisdom from without and of spiritual
understanding; yet being first put to shame by the earnestness of the virtue
of him who woke us to this command, and considering also the benefit of those
who come upon it, and fearing also the danger in a well-reasoned disobedience,
having first committed the noble command to Providence and using much
attention, winged by the intercession of the holy fathers, I entered into the
contests of the wrestling-ground, and as in an epitome having written down of
the noble athletes and great men only the topmost contests and signs, not only
of renowned men who accomplished a most excellent way of life but also of
blessed and seemly women who practiced an extreme way of life. And of some I
was counted worthy to see the sacred faces by face-to-face sight, and of those
already perfected in the wrestling-ground of piety I learned their heavenly
way of life from God-bearing athletes of Christ. And having gone about many
cities and most villages, and every cave and all the tents of the monks of the
desert, by a journey on foot, with the aim of piety with all accuracy, some
things I myself having inquired wrote down, and some I heard from the holy
fathers, marking in this book the contests of great men and of women more
manly than nature because of the hope in Christ, I have sent them to your
hearing that is a friend of the divine oracles, ornament of the best and
God-loving men and splendor of the most faithful and God-loving empire,
genuine and Christ-loving servant of God 5 Lausus, having engraved according
to the brevity that is mine both the renowned name of each of the athletes of
Christ, males and females, and having related from the many and very great
contests of each a few and exceedingly brief ones, having added of most also
the race and the city and the place of the monastery. And we have mentioned
men and women who had accomplished virtue to the extreme, but through the
mother of pride, that called vainglory, were brought down into the last pit
and bottom of Hades, and the beloved and fought-over accomplishments of
ascesis gained by them from long times and many labors flowed away in one turn
of a moment under conceit and self-opinion; but by the grace of our Savior and
the providence of the holy fathers and the compassion of spiritual bowels they
were snatched out of the nets of the devil and by the prayers of the saints
recovered the former virtuous life.
