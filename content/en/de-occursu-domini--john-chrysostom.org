* On the Meeting of the Lord
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

A discourse on the Meeting of our Lord Jesus Christ, and on the Theotokos, and
on Simeon.
=50.807=
Our Lord Jesus Christ not only wears flesh, but is also circumcised according
to the law, that he may take away the pretext of Jewish unbelief. For he comes
to the law on behalf of the law, that he may free the disciples from the law
through faith; and he takes flesh, and is circumcised with the Jews. He shared
in flesh, he shared also in circumcision; he confirmed the kinship, that they
might not deny the Christ who comes from the seed of David, whom they also
expected; he showed the proof of the kinship. For even when he was being
circumcised they said: We do not know where he is from; if he had not been
circumcised according to the flesh, their denial would have had a reasonable
pretext. When therefore the eight days were fulfilled for circumcising the
child, Jesus was awaiting the growth of the member.
When the eight days were fulfilled: for the law commands to circumcise on the
eighth, and if the eighth arrives, the physician comes forward into the midst
and takes the iron in hand and works his craft; and the Sabbath is idle
because of the circumcision. Let us then ask the Jews: The Sabbath is rest;
the day is complete idleness; for what reason does the eighth cast out the
seventh? why does the eighth come to be above the seventh? But the Jews do not
know the things of the Jews; the Church of Christ knows Christ and the Jewish
teachings. For the child is circumcised =50.808= on the eighth, because on the
eighth the resurrection was to be, that is, the Lord's day was to be the
circumcision of the whole world. For why did Moses not command to circumcise
on the sixth? why not on the ninth or the tenth? Plain, then, is the eighth,
on which the Lord rises. If anyone therefore does not believe in the
resurrection, he is uncircumcised in heart, being alienated from God through
unbelief. And circumcision is true knowledge and perception of the faith. For
this reason, beloved, circumcision is delivered to the faithful by the word of
holy baptism; and holy baptism is a type of the resurrection of Christ.
Pass then from flesh to the spirit, and from the bodily to the spiritual
ascent, and you will find there a fleshly circumcision, but here a spiritual
one, and a cleansing of sins. And the circumcision is an eighth; and the
eighth is also the resurrection; and baptism is a type of the resurrection;
from the small things to the greater, from the bodily to the more spiritual.
Let the Jews therefore come and advance; for one must advance from the fleshly
things, and not remain in them. Our Lord Jesus Christ, then, who came not to
destroy the law but to fulfill it, was circumcised with the Jews. The
evangelist therefore says: Eight days were fulfilled for him for the
circumcising, and his name was called Jesus, the name called by the angel
before he was conceived in the womb. For we are named after being born, but
Jesus is named before being born; for he was before being conceived. And he
was called Jesus, because he had the work =50.809= of a savior. And when, he
says, the days of their purification were fulfilled, according to the law of
Moses.
Of whose purification? Of Mary and of Joseph; for the law commanded the newly
delivered woman to be purified, and to keep the days, and to be without going
out. When therefore the days of the purification were fulfilled according to
the law of Moses (although the Virgin had no necessity, yet the law was being
fulfilled), they brought him up to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord, as
it is written in the law of the Lord. Where there is a bodily purification,
According to the law, he says, of Moses; where there is a presentation of the
holy one, As it is written, he says, in the law of the Lord; not that the law
of Moses also was not a law of the Lord; for whatever a prophet speaks
conversing by the Holy Spirit, he himself does not speak but the Lord suggests
it; yet because the purification had a bodily type, he says, the law of Moses;
but because the firstborn was being presented, he says, according to the law
of the Lord, honoring the one being born; As it is written in the law of the
Lord: Every male opening a womb shall be called holy to the Lord. This saying
then, and the whole law, and the cause of the law, were laid down because of
the one who was going to open a womb. For all the firstborn of animals and of
men never opened a womb, but were firstborn only; but he whom a Virgin bore,
he alone opened a womb. See then for me the patterning of the whole law
because of the one who was going to be born from a virgin alone.
And from where was it possible for Jews to understand this? for being carnal,
they are as far as can be from understanding the thoughts of the spiritual
sequence. Then they go up to give a sacrifice, According to what is said in
the law of the Lord, a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons. And these
things too came to pass typically according to the law, that nothing might be
lacking for the fulfillment of the law. These are legal enigmas; but let us
come to the evangelical interpretation: And behold, a man in Jerusalem, whose
name was Simeon; and that man was righteous and devout; and the Spirit of God
was upon him. This man had been informed by the Holy Spirit that he would not
first complete his life, until he should see the Christ of the Lord. Simeon
was an old man, and he was awaiting the good tidings; he remained in the
temple, saying that within himself: Wherever he is born, he will certainly
meet here. This man came in the Spirit into the temple at that hour, when the
parents were bringing the child. For he came often, but for a reason of his
own; then, however, being led by the hand by the Holy Spirit, he comes at the
right time toward the fulfillment of the promise. This man received Jesus in
his arms, and blessed God, and said: Now you release your servant, Master,
according to your word, in peace. From where do you release? From the
wrestling-ground of this life; for the affairs are grievous, life is a prison.
He then wished to be released; but if anyone counts the departure from here a
loss, this one is not yet perfect in the faith. Yet he was saying: Now you
release your servant, Master, according to your word, in peace. For the one
who was going to make peace for the world had arrived; the peacemaker has
come, he who joins heaven to earth, and makes the earth heaven through the
evangelical teaching, has arrived. Simeon cries: Now you release your servant,
Master, according to your word, in peace; because my eyes have seen your
salvation, he says. What is it that he says?
For formerly I believed with the mind, and I knew by reasoning; but now my
eyes also have seen; and what I expected in the heart, behold my eyes have
seen fulfilled. And what? I have seen, he says, your salvation. Which? Which
you prepared before the face of all the peoples, not of the one people, nor of
Israel only, but before the face of all the peoples; for the one born is a
teacher universally. A light for revelation of the Gentiles, and glory of your
people Israel. Why a light? Because the Gentiles were in darkness; because the
darkened Gentiles were being illumined =50.810=. A light for revelation of the
Gentiles, and glory of your people Israel. Here glory, and there revelation;
there the beginning of teaching, but here the consequent learning. Glory of
your people Israel. But someone will surely say: And where are the Israelites?
You have Peter, you have Paul, you have John, you have the three thousand, you
have the five thousand, you have the Church of Jerusalem, you have those of
the Jews who have believed; for the nation was in those who have believed. For
if the Lord of Sabaoth had not left us a seed, we would have become as Sodom,
and we would have been made like Gomorrah. For God also says: Then I left for
myself seven thousand men, who did not bend a knee to Baal. Thus in the people
the seed of the faith was being kept, and the whole people did not perish; God
forbid! nor were all the Jews corrupted; since even now in this blessed state
and calling many are called, but few chosen. For Christ called the whole
inhabited world, and prepared the holy banquet of the Gospel. But when he
comes at the second coming, he enters and makes a selection, and examines
those reclining; and if he finds one of those reclining not having a wedding
garment, he says to him: Friend, how did you come in here, not having a
wedding garment? And he will cast him out, as we have heard in the Gospels. So
that there also a selection took place, and here a selection will take place.
For it is not, because we have been called, that we ought from this already to
grow insolent, as if we had already taken hold of perfection; let the fall of
the first ones become our security. Thus, beloved, the people also did not all
perish, nor was it wholly corrupted, nor did it wholly disbelieve, nor did it
wholly persecute the apostles, but through the apostles three thousand
believed at once, besides women and children; and there came to be in
Jerusalem a church of ten thousand men, the temple not yet having been
destroyed, the Jews not yet having been cast out, Jerusalem not yet having
been brought down. A Church was built, and the words of John became a manifest
truth: He must increase, but I must decrease. Simeon then, being a prophet,
says: Glory of your people Israel. For the meeting of the expected one was the
glory of those who were expecting; and both Joseph and Mary glorify the things
said; an angel announced the good tidings, magi recognized, shepherds heard,
armies of angels danced, a star made it known from above, Simeon prophesies,
Anna daughter of Phanuel prophesies, the earth rejoiced, heaven spoke through
a star, magi denied the tyrant, the shepherds worshiped the chief shepherd,
all things recognized, the mother knew, Joseph heard, they trembled at the
events, and they also understood the outcome of the events. And Simeon blessed
them, and said to Mary his mother: Behold, this one is set for the fall and
rising of many in Israel, and for a sign spoken against. For the fall of whom?
Clearly of those who disbelieve, of those who contradict, of those who
crucify. For the rising of whom? Of those who recognize and confess with a
good mind. And for a sign spoken against. What sign spoken against? The sign
of the cross, which the Church knows as the salvation of the world, which the
Jews treat as an enemy, which heaven also has often proclaimed. And the sign
is spoken against, that the truth may conquer; for without contradiction a
complete victory does not come about. It was necessary then that the
contradiction be introduced, that the judge may by long-suffering bring the
verdict through the whole age. Therefore he says: And for a sign spoken
against; and those who disbelieve contradict. And you, he says, seem to be a
mother; will you then be untried, because you confessed to be a mother,
because you bore him, because you seemed to lend him your womb? for your belly
became a vessel of the operation of the Spirit.
Will you then be untried =50.811=, because you have become Theotokos, because
being unwed you conceived, because you were made mother of your Creator? will
you be untried? Not even you will be untried, but A sword shall pass through
your own soul also. Why, O Lord? for what have I sinned? You have sinned
nothing; but when you see him hanging on a cross, when you see him suffering
for the world, when you see his hands stretched out on the cross, and nailed
on the wood, then you begin to doubt and to say: Is this the one concerning
whom an angel spoke with me? is this the one upon whom the wonder of my
conception took place? I was a virgin and I have borne and I remained a
virgin. Why is this one crucified? And a sword shall pass through your own
soul also. So that no one has been untried, according to the prophecy of the
righteous Simeon. Peter the chief of the disciples denied three times; the
other disciples, leaving him, fled. For the shepherd had no need of the
protection of sheep, driving away wolves; nor did the combatant have need of
those who help, but all withdrew; and Christ alone was hanging as a ram. A
sword therefore passed through her soul also: the trial, and the doubt; And a
sword shall pass through your own soul also, so that thoughts out of many
hearts may be revealed. So that Jesus suffers for the conviction of unbelief,
for the confirmation of the good confession of those who believe. And the sign
is spoken against, that those who contradict through wickedness may be
convicted. For if the truth were in every way indisputable among men, piety
too would have been untested; but the permission of contradiction makes a
proving for the choice of the truth. The sign is spoken against; for how would
martyrs have been tested in persecutions? how would they have contended, being
shown forth as victors through endurance? See how much the contradiction
benefited, that it made not simply faithful, as one might say, but also
martyrs who went forward as far as tortures and =50.812= death, and who
furnished a proof of the grace of Christ through their endurance.
When therefore Simeon says: This one is set for the fall and rising of many in
Israel, and for a sign spoken against, it must be understood that he neither
works the fall, nor brings the rising by force; but he is set for a fall for
those who stumble against the stone of stumbling; and for a rising for those
who believe from choice; for he is set; as one might say: The light rises,
that those who are healthy may see; but those whose eyes are in pain may all
the more flee the rays of the brightness. And how would some have fallen,
being worthy to be convicted, and others have risen, having good hopes from
choice, if the sign were not spoken against? How does Simeon say, And for a
sign spoken against? So that the contradiction may not surprise those who
believe. And that the truth according to God is also spoken against is clear,
because God permits it; for no one would have been able to contradict, if God
did not permit; and the permission is necessary, that those who are approved
may become manifest. For there will be a time when there is no longer
contradiction; for when the sign shines forth from the heavens, then every
knee will bow of those in heaven and on earth and under the earth, and every
tongue will confess that Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. For
as long as the sign itself alone is faintly shown, and it is a sign only, and
the thing signified is nowhere seen, the sign is spoken against; but when the
one signified himself is revealed at his second coming, no one any longer
dares to contradict the sign, the one signified having arrived with clear
godhead against those who contradicted.
Then those who received the sign will be glorified by the one signified; and
those who contradicted the sign will be condemned by the one made known; and
then there will be the end of contradiction, the end of error, the end of
doubt, the end of unbelief, but the beginning of prizes and crowns; of which
may we all obtain in Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom be the glory and the power
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
