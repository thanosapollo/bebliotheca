would have anything difficult, as the desire of money does; a pure mind draws
near to wisdom, since it knows the good; but the impure will be far from it.
After the transgression Adam is forbidden to partake of this tree; if indeed
from fruits of righteousness a tree of life is planted; and the tree of life
is the wisdom of God; and justly every lawbreaker has been forbidden to touch
this tree. God founded the earth by wisdom; and through his perception the
abysses were broken open, and the clouds flowed dews; the earth, then, which
he named here, holy Paul named breadth; and the heavens named here, that one
in the letter to the Ephesians calls height; and the things tropically called
abysses he names depth; the dewed clouds he calls length; and all these are
symbols of rational natures, divided by worlds and by body according to the
analogy of the constitution; this fits even now the Son of God; but the
faithful soul also is earth bearing fruit a hundredfold; and this, from the
fatness which is wisdom, is wise, God founding it upon it; for he settles it,
the synagogue of the teaching, and making a fixing from wisdom; and as it
always advances, he works it into heaven, a most pure creature, a dwelling of
angels and of the intellectual essences, having also received perception
=17.169= of what is, and considering the exactness of the things, as it were
the much-speaking of the judgments of God. Against wisdom alone the demons are
powerless, not being allowed to cast reasonings into the heart of the one made
wise; for the mind that claims the contemplations of wisdom becomes incapable
of unclean reasonings. A pure mind draws near to wisdom, since it knows the
good; but the impure will be far from it. A stumbling-block of rational nature
is an unclean reasoning, or false knowledge; but he who is recast by the will
of God does not stumble in these. Neither an approach, he says, of evil
reasonings will frighten the one who has become wise, nor will the impious
attacking from without pull down his power, wishing to make desolate the
things good by nature; for no one will be able to take the unassailable; and
yet, what is more arrogant than the impious? But even of this beast of every
kind, he says, you will get the better, being meek and reasonable, the Lord
being with you; and from this we learn that mercy takes away fearful
imaginings that happen to us by night, or also contemplatively; and not only
this, but it also takes away from those who think thus the evil accusations in
the departure, and the harmful punishments in the judgment; and meekness and
freedom from anger and long-suffering do the same, and as many things as are
naturally fitted to settle the passion when it is disturbed; if indeed from
the disturbance of the passion the fearful phantasms are accustomed to come to
be. Do not refrain from doing well in need not only of foods, but also of
knowledge of God and of wisdom and of commandments; and whatever is dear to
God and makes one intimate with him, do not refrain from doing well to the one
who is in need of you. This enmity toward the neighbor works a man evil, not
only bodily but also of the soul. Ignorance of the Lord is in houses of the
impious, but knowledge of God in souls of the just; dwellings of the just are
blessed, when Christ assigns the prizes to each according to their works; but
to the impious a curse and not a blessing. Extreme vice, raised even to the
end, is called pride; and the Lord arrays himself against the unjust as
righteousness; against liars as truth; and against the proud as humility;
wherefore he is also a friend to those who think humble things; for this is,
He gives grace to the humble; but against the proud he arrays himself. Those
who have both learned and taught the inspired wisdom will inherit heavenly
glory; but the impious have raised up dishonor for themselves before angels
and men from Christ. Instruction he calls ethical virtue; thought, the divine
knowledge; and a son of virtues is the one who has been qualified =17.172=
according to them, who works the things according to virtue not for the sake
of another, but for the sake of the virtue. A good gift I give you, the
heavenly kingdom, if you do not abandon my law; and it must be noted

here that he names the mind a good gift, because of the one who takes gifts in
the bosom unjustly and is not prospered; for those unjust gifts he calls the
affairs of the evil one, which the mind, receiving, is not prospered in its
ways. A crown of graces are the commandments of God, which furnish in return
the crown of the kingdom to those who do them; and a crown of luxury is the
knowledge of God, which, covering us, thrusts away every passionate reasoning
and every false knowledge. These many ways lead into one way, the one that
said: I am the way; and he has called many ways the virtues that bear toward
the knowledge of God. And the years of life of the one who hears through the
prophets the heavenly Father, and receives through the only-begotten Son his
words, will be multiplied, so as to be made straight in the ways of the
righteousness of life, that he may be saved through into the ever-living life.
The place of this army is vice and the knowledge falsely so called, and
whatever is unacceptable to God; for the impious reasonings are enemies,
killing souls. In his beloved the Lord gives sleep; but the impious are not
his beloved; therefore to the impious the Lord does not give sleep; and from
this it is possible also persuasively to show that demons are not of a nature
to sleep. The virtues go before us, and give us light in the present life,
which, toward the coming one that is day, is called night; until the day shall
succeed in which the sun of righteousness rises. Or instead of, until they set
us in safety, they do not stand off; for this is proper to day, to dissolve
the darkness; which indeed Christ also said: He who walks in the day does not
stumble; and the opposite the ways of the impious; since then they did not
know nor understand, they go through in darkness; they stumble, but they do
not have pardon as stumbling from ignorance; for they themselves worked the
darkness for themselves; and see how virtue even in darkness made light; but
vice even in light darkness. Nor do the impious know even the manner how they
sin; nor do they know the cause, from where they stumble; but they do not even
know this very thing, that they transgress; which is a mark of the last vice.
He calls fountains the virtues, from which is generated the living water of
the divine words, which is the knowledge of Christ. And David: For the
fountains of the waters, he says, were seen, and the foundations of the
inhabited world were uncovered, at the sojourn, clearly, of our Savior Jesus
Christ. Let your eyes, he said, look straight things, that =17.173= is, the
things dear to God alone, or the things made clear to us by the prophets
concerning Christ alone and his disciples; and as many things as might be
spoken by anyone making one intimate with Christ and that only. With these
agrees, Unless the Lord build a house, and guard a city; that is, unless the
Lord give us strength to make straight the steps toward him, we labor in vain,
who do not call upon him in encouragement, but attempt in arrogance. For both
the openly prostitutes, and the teachings of the heterodox, chatter, deceiving
through fair speech the hearts of the innocent; greatest safety then is not to
be caught at the beginning; for once caught, it is hard to escape. As far as

uncleanness is born; whose offspring is vice and ignorance; than which nothing
more bitter is to be found among the things that have come to be in the day of
judgment. From this we know that the passionate part prevails in the demons;
For unmerciful, he says, is wrath, and sharp their anger; for they hunt to

and drinks my blood, has eternal life, and I will raise him in the last day;

drinks judgment to himself. There was a time when evil was not, and there will

through it to set right the virtues of soul and of body, both to think the

signifies the knowledge of God; and this was given to us from youth; which
Solomon above calls teaching of youth; For son, he says, let not an evil
counsel overtake you (meaning the devil, as having counseled badly); she who
has left teaching of youth, and has forgotten a divine covenant; and
forgetfulness and leaving are second to possession of knowledge; as also
disease is last of health, and death second of life; and together with these
also, that the same knowledge is called both mother and wife and sister;
mother, since he who taught me has begotten me through it, as Paul the
Galatians through the Gospel; and wife, because dwelling with me it bears the
virtues and right doctrines; if indeed wisdom bears prudence to a man; sister,
because both I and she have come to be from the one God and Father; For I
said, he says, that wisdom is your sister. Grace and friendship set free; and
virtue and knowledge set free a rational soul; for grace and friendship are
virtue and knowledge; and if a deer is from the friendship, the foal is
generated from the graces; the deer then is a symbol of contemplation; and the
foal, of passionlessness; for the one is of a nature to come to be from
virtues; and the other, from knowledge. The deer is a symbol of contemplation,
of bodies and of bodiless things and of knowledge of God; and friendship, of
the love toward God and the neighbor; and the foal, of passionlessness; and
the contemplation of the knowledge toward God is accustomed to come to be from
virtues of the soul; and love is of a nature to come to be from wisdom; and
passionlessness, from the virtues of the body; and it exhorts us always to
converse with such graces; and he who lingers in these will not only converse
with God, but will also become a housemate of him. Proper to rational nature
is virtue, which is called friendship, and it makes those who follow it
friends with God through the knowledge of God. For in the friendship of virtue
and that of knowledge, the one who goes about with it is one of very many of
those benefited. Some think this is said about the outside wisdom, with which
it is not good to spend time because of the deceits in it; and some interpret
it about vice and not being long toward it thus: that it is impossible
altogether for a man to keep off from evil reasonings; yet not to linger in
them is possible; which he also calls embraces. Everyone who pledges the
friend, Christ, as righteousness and truth, delivers his own soul to the
enemies, who are accustomed to war against men, because of the friendship
toward the Savior; for spiritual friendship is knowledge of God, according to
which the saints also are styled friends of God, as John the Baptist was a
friend of the bridegroom, and Moses and the apostles; For no longer, he says,
do I call you slaves, but friends. Do you see contest and sleeplessness? Sleep
of a soul =17.177= is the sin according to act; and drowsiness, the first
unclean thought constituted in the soul; and further-drowsiness the
inclination toward the worse; the law then hinders the sleep; and the Gospel
the drowsiness; if indeed the one cuts around the sin according to act; and
the other, the vice first constituted according to thought; For it was said,
he says, to the ancients, You shall not murder; but I say

to you, You shall not be angry. It must be noted here that he calls the
natural well-fitted movement of the ant wisdom; for even the wiser than a wise
man is called wiser; and how is it also not under a master, if indeed all
things together are slaves of God? or perhaps God is called master in two
ways, as creator, and as being known? Wherefore Paul also writes: But now
having been set free from sin, and having been enslaved to God (clearly
according to virtue and knowledge) you have your fruit unto sanctification,
and the end eternal life; and if the ant is without share of such an end and
of the knowledge, being irrational, it is clear that it is also free of such
slavery; well then it is said that the ant is not under a master, according to
this mastership; and again it is said to be under mastership, under God, as
under a creator. Through the ant Solomon seems to sketch for us the practical
way; and through the bee he signifies the contemplation of the things that
have come to be and of him who made them, which both pure and impure, and wise
and mindless, bring forward toward the health of the soul; and the wax seems
to me to hold the account of the things themselves; and the honey stored in it
to be a symbol of the contemplation of them; and the wax will pass away; For
the heaven, he says, and the earth will pass away; but the honey will not pass
away; for neither will the words of our Savior Jesus Christ pass away; about
which Solomon says: Honeycombs of honey, good words; and their sweetness,
healing of a soul; and David, How sweeter, he says, to my throat are your
oracles than honey to my mouth; and the result of the toil of the virtues and
of the knowledge he named toil; and kings and private men, he does not call
those who have the diadem, or the poor on a dunghill, but those who have
toward God the mind made wise and strong in the virtues, he calls kings. This
sleep is of a nature to happen only to a rational soul; for here it signifies
ignorance and lack of knowledge; of which the sleeplessness makes one, as a
sparrow solitary upon a housetop. One must attend here lest perhaps through
such movements of the body the opposing ones know their own reasonings turning
in us; since we have believed that God alone is knower of hearts. Everyone who
eats the fleshes of Christ and drinks his blood acquires just blood; of which
a rational soul being deprived =17.180= is said to die; For a soul, he says,
that sins, this will die; and if there is just blood, there is clearly also
unjust blood; which those gather in themselves who eat down the food given to
peoples, to the Ethiopians, and are fed with the bread of the lie; and those
who sacrifice to foreign gods consume their own just blood; and those who
sacrifice to themselves to the only God destroy the unjust blood; and it will
altogether follow the destruction of the unjust blood; but this is altogether
of a nature to come to be now, and from the beginning it was not so; for it is
not according to destruction of disease that there is health of the children,
who are born from the beginning with the health. Casting the lie against one
another, he brings them into distinctions and lawsuits; or brothers are those
who have the gift of adoption, and are under the same father Christ; whom the
witness of injustice attempts to separate, casting disturbances and judgments
into them; and "it kindles," as I think, is added because of the passionate
reasonings, lighting passion toward anger and hate, and desire toward shameful
works; these reasonings holy Paul also named fiery darts of the evil one,
wounding the soul, and working death. He commands to bring the mother, who is
the wisdom that begets, the one according to God; and yet he ought to have
said the father; for this one is more suitable toward being with the son; but
since it is not possible for him to know God before the contemplation of the
things that have come to be, for this reason he commands to bring the mother
and not the father; in order that through the mother the son may see the
father; for if she does not beget, he does not see the light, which is
knowledge of God himself. That the commandment of the law is a lamp and light;
and perhaps a lamp is the Old Testament;

for that was the lamp that was burning and shining; light, the New Testament;
For I, he says, am the light of the world; a refutation of the evils, and
instruction of the goods; for through law is knowledge of sin. For so great is
the pleasure of vice, as even of one loaf. If he is caught by true knowledge,
he will put away all false knowledge, he who formerly stole, through want of
knowledge, from the knowledge and wisdom made foolish by our Savior; and he is
said to pay sevenfold, the praising God seven times in the day; and having
given all the senses that belong to him to God, he will rescue himself from
the punishments prepared for the devil and his angels. Everyone who has shared
in vice is an adulterer of the devil who first married vice, and from the
beginning has become a man-killer; and with him he procures destruction for
his own soul. Wisdom is our sister, because the Father who made the =17.181=
bodiless nature has also made this; and wisdom here he does not call the Son
of God, but the contemplation of bodies and of bodiless things, and the things
lying in the judgment and providence in it. He names the flesh of the man a
window now; for through this the evil one works the deceits for men, for those
who wish to travel the broad and spacious way, leading away to destruction;
and here one must attend to what Solomon says about vice; that it itself does
not at the beginnings lead the man onto the broad way; nor does it compel him
to go through passages of its houses, or to draw near to a corner, or to speak
in evening darkness; but if it sees someone giving himself over to the
pleasures, at once it meets him having a prostituting form, or it makes the
hearts of young men fly out. Those who roam in the broad places receive
reasonings of adultery and fornication and theft; and those who roam outside
these pursue the pleasures against nature, seeking to lie with males, and
taking imaginings of certain other forbidden things; and see lest this be also
an accusation of holy men; and someone not being still but roaming will share
in the accusations of the dishonored woman. Of the reasonings, some are pure,
and some unclean; and of the lines some are called straight; and some broken;
and a corner is a broken straight line; or a corner intellectually is the
unclean reasoning; the lying in wait then of vice at every corner makes clear
that through all the unclean reasonings it deceives the soul. Through the
fillets and the crocus and the rest, he signifies the deceitful
contemplations, or the various passions. See vice, not having a beginning from
itself, but from the things that preface virtue; for to take a partner of a
sacrifice is a good. And when it has wrestled them down, then it sends in the
poison, listing the things desired by those who love, which are all sufficient
to bewitch, not about its own nature, but about the folly of those who hear;
and according to thought vice seeks to shame our face, through the fillets and
the bed, and the carpets, and the crocus, and the cinnamon; which evils and
various passions of pleasures he signifies coming to be among the inventors of
the evils. That after many days he will return to his house, Paul having gazed
very spiritually at the economy, says that the last enemy is to be abolished,
death; whom Solomon also sketched beforehand to be a husband of wickedness;
who took as a bag of silver in his hand the humanity, even if again in the
sinning he is not present to foresee the fear of God; because he cares,
bearing with the race of men; since if he did not foresee the fear of God, he
would have destroyed all with a rush. Of the herons there are three kinds,
both the cemphos, and the =17.184= white, and the one called asteriskos; of
these the cemphos, being unrestrained about mixings, beds and mounts with
difficulty, and cries, and in mounting lets blood go from the eyes, and bears
badly and painfully; and the scripture seems, from the history of this animal,
those who obey wickedness to be cemphos-like and made like the birds through
incontinence; and there is also another kind

of the cemphos in the sea flying in flocks here and there; which the sailors,
baiting a many-hooked cord, catch; and not only does it not flee seeing those
hunted, but also one runs before another to be caught; thus those who converse
with vice, hastening one to outdo another through the pleasures, pierce
themselves round with a destructive death. Heart here he has called the virtue
that happens upon the heart, or the spiritual one created by God. Wisdom here
he took as genus of the instruction and of the knowledge; for having set forth
instruction and knowledge, he adds: For wisdom is better than costly stones,
as the instruction and the knowledge being contained in the wisdom, which is
given through the Spirit, a word of wisdom; and this same thing is also called
a spirit of adoption. It must be noted that injustice and insolence and pride
he calls the unjust insolent and proud man, naming him from the worst habit.
That is, from me is the well-counselled and the safe; for where this is, there
that also; do you see everywhere the bodiless things set before the bodily and
prevailing? Then having said that even the being king itself comes to be
through it, he added also what is much greater; for this is to be king, the
being able to use the rule justly, better; and some call kings the saints and
grandees; and tyrants, those still warring the flesh, and tyrannizing the
passions. He introduces love as greater than friendship here, when it is not
arranged against the same; indeed when the Savior says to Peter, Do you love
me? knowing the one conversing, he gives himself the lesser, the, That I am
fond of you; and having heard the third, Simon son of Jonah, are you fond of
me? he was grieved that he assigned him the lesser a third time. That only the
wise man is rich and well-famed; for there is in him possession of many things
and offspring of wisdom; which also sowing into the spirit, he reaps life; and
he seems through saying the wealth of herself to say some such thing, that You
have no need of expense, nor of innovation, toward the love toward me, but it
is enough only to be fond, and you will be loved in return. But indeed, also
what is wonderful, the being righteousness in wealth, this too is with me, and
all the other goods. =17.185= Being substance, the wisdom of God has been
begotten before ages, and before creation it was eternal; and when it received
a relation toward the generated things, then it became a beginning of the ways
of God, the making and providential ones; the beginning then is yoked to the
creatures of which it became a beginning, that is the relation toward the
generated things; but the wisdom is eternal, existing substantially before
ages with God. Impiety is the sin toward God; and he also is impious toward
his own soul who heaps punishment on his own soul, the one prepared for the
impious; and it follows also that those who hate wisdom love death, if indeed
wisdom is life. Wisdom here one must take in two ways, I mean that given from
the Holy Spirit in words and writing to the godly-minded, through which also
the knowledge of God was made known to the world; and likewise also the
hypostatic Son and Word of God, who brought all things together from not being
into being by his wisdom. Sacrifices here he says are as many as are prophets
sealed by the exhortation of Christ and being slaughtered, because they mix in
the mixing-bowl of knowledge the knowledge of him as sweet wine for the
sensible; saying, Do not fear those who kill the body, but are not able to
kill the soul; for he himself also slaughtered them, and slaughters, because
he himself exhorts those who believe in him to die for him, as he dies for
you. If Paul the apostle is a slave of Christ, and Christ is wisdom, Paul the
apostle is a slave of wisdom; and likewise also upon the remaining apostles
and prophets we will gather the account, having shown them slaves of his
wisdom. One must not refute the bad when they sin, but remind them of divine
fear, which will stand them off from vice; or bad those turning from sin, and
wishing to come to repentance; still being bad and blinded, bear with them;
and do not refute according to one fall, lest they come into hate from

the beginnings; and when they become wise, and are made firm, refute. And
Christ did not refute the disciples while they were still young, because the
old wineskins are not well-fitted toward new wine. Or he calls bad the
mindless and foolish; or those altogether foreign to the divine teaching, whom
one must not refute, but admonish and teach; and if Ahab and Herod were also
refuted, yet they already had a share of the divine law, even if they
transgressed; wherefore one must refute these also. Or these things seem to be
said toward those then, the more infantile; since for what reason did John and
Paul refute? One must not refute the bad when they sin; for the refutation to
the face makes the sinner more shameless; but we must do this upon wise and
just men; between the one refuted and the one refuting, and not upon many.
=17.188= He who is carried about by every wind of teaching, having suffered
shipwreck concerning the faith, as having shepherded both himself and others
under a wind, has destroyed; for he left the ways of the commandments, of the
one who said, I am the vine, you the branches, and my Father the farmer. He
who does not gather with the virtues the divine knowledge, this one does not
gather the deathless good fruit of the eternal kingdom; and fruitlessness is
vice and ignorance. Gladly to take hold of the divine words as living loaves,
and of the divine thoughts in the hidden contemplation of the mind. Loaves of
the soul are the virtues, which also were commanded by our Savior to come to
be with us; that is fasting, self-control, prayer, and the like; and in
secret, toward their not being seen by men. A man, the one who has become poor
of the word; and hands, the acts of wisdom. The intelligent man, not being
burned down by the afflictions and the disasters, bears offspring of
righteousness; but the lawbreaker is destroyed even by the better things.
Unrefuted instruction he names the evil way of life of the soul, which also
becomes a procurer of destruction and of wandering for it; and the way of life
not only from the eternal, but also from every hardship of vice of life,
guards the innocent; and he calls innocent those who endure badly from
someone, and practice innocence in love. Or thus. Unrefuted, that which has a
pretence of piety, but is unexercised and unexamined; for he who is not
refuted, and does not make clear to others his thoughts and his reasonings,
wanders by conceit. The, they cover, instead of, they do not let lift up. Or
he calls enmity the vice, through which we become enemies of God; and the
enmity against God and men that comes to be in us in the heart from the evil
one, this the just shake off, and with the lips they hymn God, when the evil
one thus suggests to them; For if being enemies, Paul says, we were reconciled
to God through the death of his Son; and if the enmity is vice, the friendship
is virtue and knowledge of God, through which we become friends of him and of
the holy powers; and upon this friendship, those who are his friends are also
friends of one another. Lord, all my desire is before you, says David; that
is, as many things as are pleasing to God, and fit just men, one must desire,
in order that our prayer may become acceptable before God; but the impious is
carried about in eternal destruction in desiring and doing the things not dear
to God. If fear of the Lord adds days, and a beginning of wisdom is fear of
the Lord, these days are parts of the wisdom coming to be under the sun of
righteousness; for advancing he adds knowledges and illuminations; and for
such days David also prays saying: Do not =17.189= lead me up in the half of
my days; and these =17.189= days Abraham also having fulfilled died an older
man and full of days; and the Lord adds the divine visions as days to those
