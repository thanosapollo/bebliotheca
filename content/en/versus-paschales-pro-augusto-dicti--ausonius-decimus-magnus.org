* Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

The holy solemnities of saving Christ return
and the pious initiates celebrate devoted fasts.
But we, holding eternal cult in the breast,
continue the force of unstained honors.
Annual is the care for the rites, continual reverence for us.

Great father of things, whom earth and sea and air
and Tartarus and the milky region of the painted sky serve,
whom the guilty crowd of the people's crimes trembles and again the kindly
crowd of pious souls celebrates with vows:
you grant this course of brief age and the swift end of a falling
soul with the gift of eternal life.
you impart to the human race the mild warnings of laws and the holy prophets
and you save descendants,
having pitied deceived Adam, whom Eve, captured by poisons,
entangled as a partner in bland errors.
you, kindly father, grant your word to the earth, both son and God,
wholly like and equal,
true from the true and living from the living origin,
he, taught by your warnings, added this one thing,
that the spirit which swam over the watery waves
might quicken sluggish limbs with an immortal washing,
threefold faith from one author, certain hope of salvation

[lost]
grant pardon and furnish the gifts of hoped-for life

to him who embraces this number with joined virtues.
Such a pattern also is seen in earthly shores
Augustus the father, sower of twin Augusti,
who embracing both brother and son with pious
numen shares the kingdom and does not divide the one,
alone having all things and having lavished all things.
These therefore, thriving for us with threefold piety,

peaceful rulers of earth and ministers of heaven,
Christ, as one to be appeased, claim before the eternal Father.
