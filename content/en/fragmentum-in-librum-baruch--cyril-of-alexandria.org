* Fragment on the Book of Baruch
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

That you sit unto the age. For always and in every time the created nature is
corruptible, and of generated things the measure is in these. But Wisdom, the
artificer and creator of all, sits, that is, has unshaken continuance in
identity. And again: For our sitting in these signifies the unshaken and
established character of existence, and of the continuance of the substance
above all; but that we perish the age [signifies] being corruptible at every
time, as far as it pertains to the account of having been made; if indeed it
is true that everything in becoming, and brought over from not-being into
being, is altogether and in every way subject to corruption.
