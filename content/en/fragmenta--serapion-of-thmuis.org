AND THAT you may also know this, that the energy of this false order of the
so-called new prophecy (namely the heresy of the Montanists) is abominated by
all the brotherhood in the world, I have sent you also letters of Claudius
Apollinarius, who became most blessed bishop in Hierapolis of Asia. Eusebius
brought these in book v. Hist. c. 19; where immediately after he goes on to
write, And in this letter of Serapion there are also carried subscriptions of
various bishops; of whom one subscribed somehow thus: Aurelius Cyrenius a
martyr, I pray that you be well; and another in this way: Aelius Publius Julius
from Debeltus 452 a colony of Thrace, a bishop; God lives who is in the heavens,
that the blessed Sotas who is in Anchialus wished to cast out the demon of
Priscilla, and the hypocrites did not let him. And autograph notes of still more
other bishops agreeing with these are carried in the shown letters.
FROM THE BOOK ON THE GOSPEL WHICH WAS CARRIED UNDER THE NAME OF PETER.
For we, brothers, accept both Peter and the other apostles as Christ; and the
things falsely inscribed with their names, as experienced, we refuse; knowing
that we did not receive such things. For I, having been with you, supposed that
all were bringing themselves with a right faith. And not having gone through the
gospel brought forward by them with the name of Peter, I said: that if this
alone is what seems to furnish you a smallness of soul, let it be read; but now
having learned that their mind 453 nested in a certain heresy from the things
said to me, I will be eager again to come to you. So that, brothers, expect me
in haste. And we, brothers, having grasped of what sort of heresy Marcianus was,
and who also opposed himself not understanding what he spoke, which you will
learn from the things written to you. For we were able from others who had
practiced this same gospel, that is from the successors of those who began it,
whom we call Docetists (for the more thoughts are of their teaching) having used
from them to go through; and to find the more of the right word of the Savior;
and some things additionally distinguished, which we also have subjoined for
you. Eusebius brought this in book vi. Hist. cap. 12.
