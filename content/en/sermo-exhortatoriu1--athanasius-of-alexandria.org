* Exhortatory Sermon
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

For God requires three things from every Christian: right faith, truth from
the tongue, and chastity from the body. And likewise keep the rest; first do
not speak, but when you hear someone slandering, flee, withdraw, lest you also
become like the slanderer, and go away into eternal punishment, to fiery
dragons eating the tongue. 10 For God said through the prophet that the eyes
of those who wink are dug out by merciless angels. Then those who laugh will
weep bitterly, then those who babble will groan pitiably, being condemned into
the fiery river, into dunon Hades, into the sleepless worm, into the bitter
weeping, into the gnashing of teeth. 11 Fast on Wednesday and Friday, but on
the other days you have authority: if you wish, fast; if you wish, do not
fast. As God made man according to nature to breathe, so he made it fitting
also for women that they always fast. 12 When you rise in the morning, make
the type of the precious cross upon all your members. And walking and sleeping
and always say: Lord Jesus Christ, help us. Evening and morning go to the
church, and confess to God the sins which you have done, and see, O man, that
you do not swear either by heaven or by earth, for heaven is the throne of the
Master, and the earth is the footstool of his feet, but if force comes upon
you from someone to swear, say to him: I speak truth and I do not lie. With
babbling men and women never sit together. 13 And see, O man, that you not
become sharp-tempered, because the devil brings on irascibility, that you may
become sharp-tempered and lose your soul; but may God grant meekness, that
through meekness and almsgiving you may be saved. And see, O man, that you not
love the adornment of garments, nor desire sloth, but rather wear humble and
cheap things for God's sake, that from there our God may clothe you with a
robe of glory, a garment of incorruption, a crown of beauty, a diadem of
kingship, that then your soul may have boldness toward God, saying: Lord, in
this vain world I went without many things that I may enjoy your kingdom,
Master, and then our Master Christ, lover of mankind and merciful, will say:
where you labored little, I first prepared for you, but come, enter into the
bridal chamber of my glory, and enjoy what eye has not seen, and ear has not
heard, and has not arisen upon the heart of man, as many things as I prepared
for those who love my name. 14 For in eighty years the time of man is
fulfilled; even if we live in luxury and are rich fifty years and a hundred,
and after these things what is set before us? Blessed are those who strive to
attain the unending kingdom. Blessed is that man who through his own wealth
ransoms his own soul from the punishment, for a man's own wealth ransoms him,
and again: Blessed is he who considers the poor and needy; in an evil day the
Lord will deliver him. What evil day does the prophet mean? He was relating to
us concerning the day of judgment; for what is worse than punishment? Truly
nothing. For what is impossible for God, to bring down showers of gold upon
the earth so that all may have wealth? With God all things are possible. That
the poor man may become remission of the sins of the rich, and the rich of the
poor, because to God belongs honor and glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
