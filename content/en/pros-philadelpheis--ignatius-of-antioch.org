* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also called Theophorus, to the church of God the Father and
of the Lord Jesus Christ which is in Philadelphia of Asia, shown mercy and
established in the concord of God, and rejoicing without wavering in the
passion of our Lord, and fully assured of his resurrection in all mercy;
which I greet in the blood of Jesus Christ, which is joy eternal and
abiding, especially if they are one with the bishop and with the
presbyters and deacons who are with him, appointed in the mind of Jesus
Christ, whom according to his own will he has established in firmness by
his Holy Spirit.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
This bishop I have known to possess the ministry that belongs to the common
good not from himself, nor through men, nor out of vainglory, but in the
love of God the Father and of the Lord Jesus Christ. I am struck by his
gentleness, who, being silent, can do more than those who speak empty
things.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
For he is tuned to the commandments as a lyre to its strings. Therefore my
soul blesses his mind toward God, having recognized it as virtuous and
perfect, his steadfastness and his freedom from anger, in all the
gentleness of the living God.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
Children of the light of truth, then, flee division and evil teachings.
Where the shepherd is, there follow as sheep.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h2
:END:
For many wolves, seeming trustworthy, take captive by evil pleasure those
who run toward God. But in your unity they will have no place.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
Keep away from the evil plants, which Jesus Christ does not cultivate,
because they are not the Father's planting. Not that I found a division
among you, but a sifting.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
For as many as are of God and of Jesus Christ, these are with the bishop.
And as many as repent and come to the unity of the church, these too shall
be of God, that they may live according to Jesus Christ.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h3
:END:
Do not be deceived, my brothers. If anyone follows one who makes a schism,
he does not inherit the kingdom of God. If anyone walks in an alien mind,
he does not consent to the passion.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
Be eager, then, to use one Eucharist. For there is one flesh of our Lord
Jesus Christ, and one cup for union with his blood, one altar, as there is
one bishop together with the presbytery and the deacons, my fellow
servants, so that whatever you do, you may do according to God.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
My brothers, I am poured out in loving you, and overflowing with joy I make
you secure. Yet it is not I, but Jesus Christ, in whom, being bound, I fear
the more, as one still unfinished. But your prayer to God will finish me,
that I may attain the lot in which I was shown mercy, taking refuge in the
gospel as in the flesh of Jesus, and in the apostles as the presbytery of
the church.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
And let us love the prophets also, because they too proclaimed the gospel,
and hoped in him, and waited for him; in whom, having believed, they were
also saved, being in the unity of Jesus Christ, saints worthy of love and
of wonder, attested by Jesus Christ and numbered together in the gospel of
the common hope.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
But if anyone interprets Judaism to you, do not listen to him. For it is
better to hear Christianity from a man who has circumcision than Judaism
from one who is uncircumcised. But if neither speaks of Jesus Christ, they
are to me monuments and tombs of the dead, on which only the names of men
are written.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
Flee, then, the evil arts and ambushes of the ruler of this age, lest,
pressed by his mind, you grow weak in love. But all of you come together
in an undivided heart.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h3
:END:
I thank my God that I have a good conscience among you, and that no one can
boast, either in secret or in the open, that I was a burden to anyone in
small things or in great. And for all among whom I spoke I pray that they
may not take it as a witness against them.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
For even if some wished to deceive me according to the flesh, yet the
Spirit is not deceived, being from God. For it knows whence it comes and
whither it goes, and it exposes what is hidden. I cried out while I was
among you; I spoke with a loud voice, with the voice of God: Give heed to
the bishop and to the presbytery and to the deacons.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
But some suspected that I said these things because I already knew of the
division of certain people. Yet he in whom I am bound is my witness that I
did not learn it from human flesh. But the Spirit was proclaiming, saying
this: Do nothing apart from the bishop; keep your flesh as a temple of
God; love union; flee divisions; become imitators of Jesus Christ, as he
also is of his Father.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
I, then, was doing my part as a man made ready for union. But where there
is division and anger, God does not dwell. The Lord forgives all who
repent, if they repent into the unity of God and the council of the
bishop. I trust in the grace of Jesus Christ, who will loose from you
every bond.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
I exhort you to do nothing from faction, but according to the teaching of
Christ. For I heard some saying, If I do not find it in the archives, I do
not believe it in the gospel. And when I said to them, It is written, they
answered me, That is still in question. But for me the archives are Jesus
Christ; the inviolable archives are his cross and his death and his
resurrection and the faith that is through him, in which I want to be
justified by your prayer.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
The priests also are good, but better is the high priest who has been
entrusted with the holy of holies, who alone has been entrusted with the
hidden things of God. He himself is the door of the Father, through which
Abraham and Isaac and Jacob enter, and the prophets and apostles and the
church. All these things are unto the unity of God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
But the gospel has something exceptional: the coming of the Savior, our
Lord Jesus Christ, his passion, and the resurrection. For the beloved
prophets proclaimed him; but the gospel is the completion of immortality.
All things together are good, if you believe in love.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
Since, according to your prayer and according to the compassion you have
in Christ Jesus, it has been reported to me that the church in Antioch of
Syria is at peace, it is fitting for you, as a church of God, to appoint a
deacon to go there on God's embassy, to rejoice with them when they have
come together and to glorify the name.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h2
:END:
Blessed in Jesus Christ is he who shall be counted worthy of such a
ministry, and you too shall be glorified. If you are willing, it is not
impossible for you, for the name of God, as the nearest churches also have
sent bishops, and others presbyters and deacons.

* Chapter 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h1
:END:
Now concerning Philo, the deacon from Cilicia, a man well attested, who
even now serves me in the word of God together with Rheus Agathopus, a
chosen man who follows me from Syria, having said farewell to this life:
they also bear witness to you, and I give thanks to God for you, that you
received them, as the Lord also receives you. But may those who dishonored
them be redeemed in the grace of Jesus Christ.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h2
:END:
The love of the brothers in Troas greets you; from there I write to you
through Burrus, who was sent with me by the Ephesians and Smyrnaeans as a
mark of honor. The Lord Jesus Christ, in whom they hope in flesh, soul,
spirit, faith, love, and concord, will honor them. Farewell in Christ
Jesus, our common hope.
