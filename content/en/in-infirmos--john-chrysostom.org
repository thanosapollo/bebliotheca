* On the Sick
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

O Master, Lord, physician of the sick and God of all consolation, who does
not will that man be sickly or in pain, but cheerful and wholly healthy:
yourself also raise up your servant Maria, who has fallen into bodily
weakness and come into all despondency; have pity, do not overlook her,
quench the fever, soothe the pain, drive away the listlessness. You who
raised Peter's mother-in-law when she was feverish and lying grievously
ill, and by your word raised her also to minister to you: yourself, O King,
be present even now over your servant Maria, and soothe the pain of the
soul and the sickness of the body. You who led the chief of the apostles
out of the bonds of the prison through an angel, you who delivered Paul
the apostle, the herald of the Church, from the punishment of the emperor
Nero: yourself also ransom your servant from the pain that holds him. You
who delivered your bosom friend and beloved, John the Theologian, from
Patmos and from Artemis, you who saved all-prudent Noah from the dreadful
and all-destroying flood: yourself even now stretch out your invisible and
spiritual right hand and raise up your creature from this going astray.
You who delivered Abraham, the hospitable and most righteous, from every
affliction and pain, you who raised Job of countless contests from the
dunghill: yourself raise up also your servant Maria from the bodily
weakness that surrounds her. You who called the lawgiver and most
righteous Moses out of Egypt, you who saved your beloved child Israel from
the most evil and wicked slavery of the most bitter Pharaoh: yourself also
help your servant Maria, quench the fever, soothe the pain, drive away
from her every wicked sickness, and raise your servant from a bed of pain
(=ne=) and from a pallet of affliction. You who saved Jacob from the hand
of Esau, you who gave eyes to Tobit 324 and granted him the light, you who
saved all-fair Joseph, sold wickedly by brother-killers and wicked men,
you who saved all-wise David, the wondrous prophet, from the hand of Saul:
yourself also have mercy on your servant who lies in sickness and is in
need of your mercy; have pity, raise her up, keep her safe, set her apart
from every evil. You who saved the most righteous Daniel the prophet from
the fearful snatching of the lions, you who snatched Jeremiah from the
gloomy and dark pit, you who saved the three youths, Ananias, Azarias, and
Misael, from the seven-times-heated furnace and turned the fire into dew:
yourself also deliver your servant from the pain that holds her. You who
enlightened the eyes of the blind, who cleansed the flesh of the lepers,
who raised the dead: yourself raise up, yourself give life, and do not
repay us according to our sins. You who bound up the paralytic by a word,
who healed the woman with the issue of blood, who healed the legion:
yourself, O Master, be entreated, I beseech; be prevailed upon, I urge;
have compassion, I implore, as one long-suffering; do not bring me down
living into Hades, but yourself raise me by your invisible and spiritual
right hand. Holy three youths who quenched the furnace, holy seven youths
in Ephesus who rose from the dead, help; holy nine martyrs who work
wonders in Cyzicus, holy ten martyrs who were perfected in Crete, holy
forty martyrs who work wonders in Sebasteia, holy thirty-three martyrs
from Rome, holy three hundred and eighteen God-bearing fathers who work
wonders in the city of Nicaea, holy ten thousand men who were perfected in
the Theologian (sic). Holy Therapon from Smyrna, holy Parthenius from
Lampsacus, holy Julian from Caesarea, holy Leontius from Tripolis, holy
Demetrius from Thessalonica, holy George from Cappadocia, holy
Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian and Panteleimon, Cyrus and


John, Samson and Diomedes (sic), Florus and Laurus and Hermolaus. Holy
Andronicus with the holy Athanasia, holy confessors from the city of
Edessa, Gurias, Samonas, and Abibus, who snatched the girl from the tomb
and led her back to her homeland. Holy Acindynus, come swiftly; holy
Aphthonius, overshadow; holy Elpidephorus, raise up; holy Anempodistus, do
not overlook; holy 325 Unmercenaries, come swiftly; holy Panteleimon,
chief physician, overshadow; holy Cyrus and John, come swiftly; holy
Agapetus from Synao, holy John the Theologian, holy Peter from Rome, holy
Paul from Damascus, holy Euthymius from Madytos, holy Nicholas from Myra,
holy Basil from Caesarea, holy Epiphanius from Cyprus, holy John
Chrysostom, holy Thyrsus, Leucius and Philemon, holy Alypius and
Procopius, holy Lady Theotokos from Athens, holy Anastasia the deliverer
from potions, holy Barbara from Patras, holy Marina who trampled the
demon, holy proto-martyr Theophano from Constantinople, holy Catherine
from Alexandria, holy John, prophet and Forerunner and Baptist of Christ,
holy prophets, apostles, martyrs, venerable and righteous, all the
heavenly powers of the holy angels and archangels, principalities,
authorities, and the many-eyed cherubim and the six-winged seraphim, the
incomprehensible power of the precious and life-giving Cross: fall down,
prevail upon the only all-good God, that you may grant the servant of God
Maria health and salvation and release from the evils that hold her, and
drive this grievous sickness from the servant of God Maria: fever, inward
fever, of a strong chill, of a short chill, of a wasting chill, harmful
disease, grievous disease, a terrible disease coming upon her, sent
against her and wrought. I adjure you, the seventy-two sicknesses that
man has, to withdraw from the servant of God Maria, whether the sickness
came down from heaven or from the stars or from the sun or the moon,
whether from the gloom of a cloud, whether from freezing air, whether it
came down from thunder or from lightning, whether from earthquake or a
crash and collapses, whether from mountains, valleys, or a ravine or
slopes, whether from a hill or a plain or fields, whether from a stone or
water or a spring or a road or a river or a country or a field or a
garden-enclosure, whether in a garden or in a park, whether in passages
or at crossroads, whether in entrances or in exits, whether in the court
of a bath or of an oven or of a mill-wheel, whether in a door or in a
window or of upper rooms or of lower rooms or of a courtyard or of a
threshing-floor, and you came to try the servant of God. Lest some
enchanter worked it by envy or procured it, or from a drug or envy or
jealousy, whether from heavy 326 shameful eyes, whether from the evil eye
and every other calamity that comes upon her, whether a blasting one or a
bubonic one or a sent-upon one or a head one or of the hands, heart, and
feet, or a demonic, spectral one, or a kalis of the mountains, whether of
Onoskelis or of neraids, whether of those that fly in the gloomy airs,
and you came to wrong the servant of God. Whether headache or eye-pain or
tooth-pain or chest-pain or mind-pain or hand-pain. Whether of dysuria or
of triple urination or inward swelling or outward swelling or womb or
colic or joint-pain or a smooth tumor or sleepless or much-sleeping. I
adjure you, whatever it is that is wronging the servant of God: withdraw
and go away into the wild mountains, wherever you may lie and wrong the
servant of God. Withdraw and go away into the wild mountains, whether in
the hairs of the head, whether in the marrow of the head, whether in the
skull or the half-skull, whether in the foreheads or temples or eyelids
or midriff, whether in the nostrils or ears, whether in the mouths or
molars or lips or teeth, whether in the necks or the shoulder-blades of
the hands, whether in the breasts or inflammations or the heart and every
moisture, whether in the backs, the napes, or the navels and the lower
belly, whether in the knees of the feet or the ankles or the fingers and
nails, wherever you may lie and wrong her and nest in her, withdraw and
go away upon the heads of those who made it and sent it and of those who
knew of it, whether a stranger made it or one of the household or a
betrayer or a slave or a free man, whether


drug-enchanter, whether a gello or a strix or a striga or an envious man.
I adjure you, all unclean sufferings, by the great name of our Lord Jesus
Christ and by his undefiled head and by his mighty right hand, withdraw
from the servant of God. I adjure you by him who hung the earth upon the
waters, I adjure you by him who founded the heavens in untrodden coils, I
adjure you by the unshaken throne of God, I adjure you by the blessed
powers and immaterial ranks, I adjure you by the four archangels Michael,
Gabriel and Uriel and Raphael, I adjure you by the twenty-four elders who
minister to the throne of God, I adjure you by the twel 327 ve apostles:
come out and withdraw from the servant of God Maria, and grant her, O
Master, Lord, health, life, peace, length of days, that your all-holy name
also may be glorified by her, of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy
Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages, amen.
