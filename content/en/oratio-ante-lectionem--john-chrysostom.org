PRAYER OF JOHN CHRYSOSTOM When about to read, or to listen to another reading.
=63.923=
Lord Jesus Christ our God, open the ears and the eyes of my heart, that I may
hear your words, and understand and do your will, Lord, for I am a sojourner
on the earth. Do not =63.924= hide your commandments from me, but uncover my
eyes, and I shall behold the wonders from your law. For in you I hope, my God,
that you may enlighten my heart.
