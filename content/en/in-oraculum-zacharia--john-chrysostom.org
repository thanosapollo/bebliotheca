Homily on the oracle given to Zechariah, and on the conception of
Elizabeth.
=50.785=
Nothing is unsubmissive to the Master's decrees; for all things yield and
serve the divine command. For he set a command, it says, and it shall not
pass away; for he who at the beginning established the universe by a word
can also again recast his creatures by a word. He once told the waterless
rock to become a stream; and it immediately gushes forth rivers of water:
For he struck a rock, and waters flowed, and rivers flooded; and the rock
that was formerly dry and without taste of water became a fountain of
rivers and a mother of waters; for nothing is barren to which God commands
offspring. He, then, who then changed the barren rock into a pool, now
also commanded barren Elizabeth to be shown forth as a child-bearing
mother, and he showed the fruitless fruitful, and her who was weighed down
with old age as a newly-wed maiden.
When she was hastening toward the tomb, then she was being made ready for
childbirth; when old age had withered the body, then God renewed the womb;
and what she had lost through time, she received back through the Maker;
and what youth had lost, old age was required to restore; when she had
become a neighbor of death, then she was named a mother of a child; and
passing beyond the years of a grandmother, she fell into a mother's care,
and she carried an infant on her arm, she who because of old age needed a
staff to walk; and the old woman handled a first-born child, and a barren
woman gave birth contrary to expectation, that a virgin giving birth
contrary to nature might be believed. Both are new and contrary to
expectation; for a barren woman and a virgin are unexpected mothers. Of
this unexpected birth the messenger to the old =50.786= man Zechariah was
the archangel Gabriel, who was sent; and standing at the right of the
altar he appeared to him. And Zechariah was troubled, it says, when he
saw, and fear fell upon him. For he saw what he had never seen: a speaking
fire, a bodiless man, an ungraspable form, an incomparable sight, an
unmatched face, a runner walking on air, a winged being in human form; and
Seeing, he was troubled. And seeing him troubled, the angel drives out the
fear with good promises: Do not fear, Zechariah, for your petition has
been heard; and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall
call his name John; and there will be joy for you and gladness; and many
will rejoice at his birth. Do not fear, Zechariah; I have not come
brandishing fear, but I have arrived as a messenger of joy to you; of what
joy? Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall call his name
John; this one will shine upon the world as the morning star, and will
drive away the darkness of ignorance, calling upon the sun of
righteousness. For he who comes after me has come before me; whose sandal
strap I am not worthy to loose. And he will go before in the spirit and
power of Elijah. The spirit of Elijah dwelt also in John; for the spirits
of prophets are subject to prophets; and in almost all things we find John
like Elijah.
That one lived in the mountains, and this one spent his time in the
wilderness; ravens fed =50.787= that one, and this one ate locusts of
plants; that one was wrapped in a sheepskin, and this one in camel's hair;
that one was austere and reproving, and this one likewise; that one
reproved Ahab the king of the Jews, and Jezebel his wife: You are, he
says, the one who perverts Israel, and the house of your father; and John
reproved Herod and Herodias: It is not lawful for you to have your
brother's



wife; and he will go before in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the
hearts of fathers to children. What is, To turn the hearts of fathers to
children? The fathers of the Jews, Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, and the
twelve patriarchs, and all the righteous, seeing the people fond of sin,
and readily committing fornication after strange gods, turned away from
them as being such. But from the time John preached the baptism of
repentance, many turned back to God. So when their fathers saw them
turning back to God, they themselves also turned their hearts toward them;
and this is the, To turn the hearts of fathers to children, to prepare for
the Lord a people made ready, a peculiar people; of whom David the prophet
proclaimed beforehand, saying: This is the generation of those who seek
the face of the God of Jacob.
Still the unexpected conception of the child astounds the old man
Zechariah, still he is distressed at the birth beyond due season, not
reasoning wisely that the potter of the womb can renew the aged womb.
Therefore he also rolled down the precipice of unbelief. For when the
angel said, Behold, your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall
call his name John, Zechariah contends against the word, and says: By what
shall I know this? for I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her
days. From where, he says, will certainty come to me? what token of the
promise can you set before me? Show me even now a rod that has blossomed,
as in the case of Aaron the high priest, and then I will believe you that
even old age can bear a child as fruit.
For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her days. Your words are
good; but the promise is hard to believe. You procure a child for me, a
hope after hopes; the time of my prime has passed; I have spent the life
of youth =50.788= without fruit. When I have become like a dry tree, then
you compel me to bear fruit; I go about the marketplace walking bent, I no
longer have strength to look up to heaven; old age drags me down toward
kindred earth; for I am earth, and to earth I shall go; I scarcely shift
the step of my feet; my two feet are not enough for walking; instead of a
third foot I carry a staff; until now I have not been furnished with a
child to lead me by the hand; I am a moving tomb, and a walking dead man.
How then from now on will a child name me father? For I am an old man, and
my wife is advanced in her days. When old age is leading me to the tomb,
then shall I mount a bridal bed, and plant a child when the sun of my
youth has set? I myself am faded through time, and Elizabeth's womb has
been deadened.
By what then shall I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is
advanced in her days. And the angel answering said to him: I am Gabriel,
who stands in the presence of God, and I was sent to speak to you, and to
bring you these glad tidings. And behold, you shall be silent, and not
able to speak, until the day these things come to pass, because you did
not believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their time. And behold,
you shall be silent and not able to speak. I wish to do something, and how
do you ask? I have chosen to work wonders, and you seek the manner? You
altogether pry into the beginning of my works. Is every word impossible
with God? Tell me, from where was Isaac born? did not Abraham the old man
beget him, and call barren Sarah a mother? Having fallen into the same
expectation, why did you not receive my words as that one did? Therefore
you shall be silent, and not able to speak, because you did not believe my
words. I will bind your tongue that was moved toward a disorderly answer;
in which you begot the sin



organ, in that same be disciplined; For by the things with which one
sins, by these he is also punished.
Therefore You shall be silent, and not able to speak, because you did not
believe my words; speaking you did not believe my utterances; in silence
await the words, Which will be fulfilled in their time; for the things
impossible with men are possible with God. To him be the glory unto the
ages. Amen.
