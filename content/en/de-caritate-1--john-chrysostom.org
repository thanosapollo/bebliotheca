On charity.
=62.769=
The Lord spoke well when he said, My burden is light. For what weight, or what
labor, is it to let go a brother's faults, which are light and of no account,
=62.770= and to have one's own forgiven, and to be justified at once? He did
not say, Bring me money, or calves, or goats, or a fast, or a vigil, lest you
should say, =62.771= I have none, I cannot; but what is light and easy and
swift, this he commanded, saying: Forgive your brother his faults, and I
forgive yours.

You forgive small offenses, perhaps a few obols, or even a hundred denarii;
but I remit the ten thousand talents. And you only forgive, without granting
anything further; but I both give the remission and the healing, and I bestow
the kingdom. And I receive your gift then, when you are reconciled with your
enemy, when you hold no enmity against anyone, when the sun does not go down
upon your anger, when you have peace and charity toward all. Then your prayer
too is acceptable, and your house is blessed, and you are blessed. But if you
will not be reconciled with your brother, how do you seek forgiveness from me?
You trample my words, and you demand pardon? I, the Master, command, and you
do not heed. And you, the slave, how do you dare to offer prayer to God, or a
sacrifice, or firstfruits, while you hold enmity against anyone? For as you
turn your face away from your brother, so God also turns his eyes away from
your prayer and from your gift; because God is charity, and he takes no
pleasure in the things done without charity. How then will God receive a
murderer's prayer, or gift, or firstfruits, or offerings, unless he first
repents as he ought? But you will surely say to me, I am not a murderer.

I will show that you are a murderer; or rather John the Theologian convicts
you, saying: He who hates his brother is a murderer. Therefore, my beloved
brethren, let us prefer nothing, nor strive to acquire anything, except
charity. Let no one hold anything against anyone; let no one repay evil for
evil. Let not the sun go down upon your anger. But let us forgive our debtors
all their faults. For what is the profit, children, if someone has everything,
yet does not have the charity that saves? It is as if someone should make a
great banquet in order to invite a king and rulers, and should prepare
everything richly so that nothing is lacking to him, yet has no salt: can that
banquet be eaten? Not at all; rather he has lost the expense, and wasted his
labors, and even brought insult upon himself from those invited by him. So it
is here as well: what is the profit of laboring into the winds? Without
charity every work and every deed is unclean, even if someone should possess
virginity, even if he fasts, even if he keeps vigil, whether he prays, or
makes a feast for the poor, even if he seems to offer gifts, or firstfruits,
or offerings, even if he builds a church, or does anything else: without
charity all those things will be counted as nothing with God. For the Lord
takes no pleasure in them. Hear the Apostle saying: For he who holds enmity
against his brother, and seems to offer something to God, it is as one who
sacrifices a dog, and they will be counted as a harlot's hire.

Do not then choose to do anything at all without charity, because charity
covers a multitude of sins. O of what a good we make light! O of how many
goods we are deprived, and of how much joy, when we have not acquired charity!
Judas, because he would not acquire it, went out from the midst of the choir
of the apostles, leaving the true light; having hated his own teacher and his
own brothers, he went in the darkness. Therefore the chief Peter also said:
Judas went aside to go to his own place. =62.772= And again John the
Theologian says: He who hates his brother is in the darkness, and walks in the
darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has
blinded his eyes. But if you say, Even if I do not love my brother, still I
love God, John convicts you, saying: If anyone says, I love God, and hates his
brother, he is a liar. For he who does not love his brother, whom he has seen,
how can he love God, whom he has not seen? He therefore who has charity with
all the brethren, who holds no enmity against anyone, who fulfills the
Apostle's word, Let not the sun go down upon your anger, this one loves God;
this one is his disciple, who said: By this all will know that you are my
disciples, if you have charity among yourselves.

It is plain, then, that the disciples of Christ are known from this, from true
charity. But he who has hatred against his brother, even if he seems to love
Christ, is a liar and deceives himself. For the apostle John says: This
commandment we have from him, that he who loves God should love his brother
also. And again the Lord: You shall love the Lord your God with all your
heart, and your neighbor as yourself. And he added, wishing to show the power
of charity: On these two commandments hang the whole law and the prophets. O
extraordinary wonder, that he who has unfeigned charity fulfills the whole
law. For charity is the fulfillment of the law, as the Apostle says. O
incomparable power of charity! O immeasurable power of charity! Nothing is
more precious than charity, neither in heaven nor on earth. For this reason
Paul the Apostle, having learned that nothing is a match for charity, wrote
and sent to the ends of the inhabited world, saying: Brethren, owe nothing,
except to love one another, and to lay down your lives for one another. For
this charity is the head of all the virtues, the salt of the virtues; charity
is the fulfillment of the law; charity is the unerring salvation. This from
the beginning conquered in the heart of Abel; this became Noah's helmsman;
this assisted the patriarchs; this saved Moses; this made David a
dwelling-place of the Holy Spirit; this pitched its tent among the prophets;
this strengthened Job. And why do I not speak of the greater things? This
brought the Son of God down from the heavens to us. Through charity the
fleshless is made flesh, he who is without beginning takes a beginning, the
Son of God becomes son of man. Through charity all things that belong to
salvation are dispensed: death has been abolished, the devil has been thrown
down, Adam has been called back, Eve has been set free. Through charity one
flock has come to be of angels and men; through charity the curse has been
loosed, paradise has been opened, life has been made manifest, the kingdom of
the heavens has been promised.

This, having netted the fishers of fish, made them fishers of men; this
contended alongside the martyrs and strengthened them; this peopled the
deserts with citizens; this filled the mountains and the caves with psalmody;
this made angels and men one, and men and women to walk the narrow and hard
way. But how long ** to pursue the incomprehensible.
