Prayer of Saint John Chrysostom
The good and man-loving God, have mercy on us. O you who are rich in
mercy and good in compassions, have mercy on us. O perfect and immortal
mind, have mercy on us. O unsleeping and incomprehensible eye, have mercy
on us. O Lord and God begotten before the morning star, have mercy on us.
O creator and fashioner of all that is, Jesus Christ, have mercy on us. O
you who laid down the soul and the body by the will of the Father, have
mercy on us. O you who shaped the well-distinguished matter and
established the world, have mercy on us. O you who stretched out the
heaven and adorned it with the stars, have mercy on us. O you who founded
the earth and adorned it with green and well-ordered flowers, have mercy
on us. O you who shut up the abysses and walled the sea by your command,
have mercy on us. O you who made four-footed animals, creeping things,
and winged birds, have mercy on us. O you who made the sea-monsters and
commanded the fishes, have mercy on us. O you who founded rocks in the
waters and grasped the mountains and the hills in the heights, have mercy
on us. O you who founded the sun and the moon, the only maker of all
things and Lord, have mercy on us. O you who formed man with your own
hands and commanded him to rule over all these, have mercy on us. O you
who set seasons and times and months and the measures of days and nights,
have mercy on us. O all-saving cause and charioteer of all, have mercy on
us. O you who rebuke the winds and shake the earth with a nod, have mercy
on us. O you who stored up fire and ice, have mercy on us. O you who rain
upon the earth and make springs gush forth and order the rivers, have
mercy on us. O you who wear away mountains and hills, who calm the raging
sea, have mercy on us. O you who weighed all things in a moment with a
balance, have mercy on us. O you who fitted the things that are from the
things that are not by the will of the Father, only Creator, have mercy
on us. O you who crushed the devil and delivered him with chains to the
glooms of Tartarus, have mercy on us. O you who, on account of his pride,
struck him off like a spark from the heavenly shields and utterly
destroyed him, have mercy on us. O you who subdued the craftiness of the
serpent, have mercy on us. O you who had compassion on Adam according to
your unspeakable love for mankind, have mercy on us. O you who contended
with Abel and steered Noah, have mercy on us. O you who guided Abraham
and through him granted us patriarchs, have mercy on us. O you who were
bound with Isaac and sojourned with Jacob, have mercy on us. O you who
were sold with Joseph and campaigned with Moses, have mercy on us. O you
who gave the law to Israel and allotted him an inheritance, have mercy on
us. O you who were incarnate of a virgin as you yourself were well
pleased, have mercy on us. O you who were born as man in Bethlehem of the
virgin and after childbirth kept the virgin undefiled, have mercy on us.
O you who received swaddling-clothes in a manger and were laid down and
were recognized by the unreasoning beasts as Lord and God, have mercy on
us. O you who were hymned by angels and glorified upon earth and
recognized by shepherds, have mercy on us. O you who were announced by a
star and worshiped by magi, have mercy on us. O you who fled from Herod
into Egypt and shook the handmade things of Egypt by your sojourn, have
mercy on us. O you who dwelt in Nazareth and paid taxes, because you
willed to be subject, have mercy on us. O you who were proclaimed
beforehand by John and were baptized by him in the Jordan, have mercy on
us. O you who were witnessed to by the undefiled Father, who sent the
Holy Spirit upon you, have mercy on us. O you who made known to us the
mystery of the holy and consubstantial Trinity, have mercy on us. O you
who were tempted in the wilderness and were found Lord and God and
rebuked the devil, have mercy on us. O you who were presented in the
temple and taken into the arms of Simeon, have mercy on us. O you who
were seen in the temple and were disbelieved by the people, have mercy on
us. O you who healed the lame and cleansed the lepers, have mercy on us.
O you who brought light to the blind and raised the dead, have mercy on
us. O you who endured toil and healed the paralytic by a word, have mercy
on us. O you who endured thirst and changed the water into wine, have
mercy on us. O you who wept for Lazarus and raised him four days dead,
have mercy on us. O you who gathered the apostles and showed them heirs
of the kingdom of heaven, have mercy on us. O you who were seized by Jews
and delivered up by high priests, have mercy on us. O you who were
brought before Herod and examined by Pilate, have mercy on us. O you who
were nailed in the flesh upon a cross 7r and were hanged upon his wood,
have mercy on us. O you who preached the good news to those in Hades and
by your three-day resurrection rose from the dead, have mercy on us. O
you who revealed to the women the mystery of your resurrection and gave
joy to the disciples, have mercy on us. O you who fully assured the
apostles through the marks of the nails and of the side, have mercy on
us. O you who lodged with the disciples for forty days and were taken up
into the heavens, have mercy on us. O you who sit at the right hand of
the Father and are coming again to judge the living and the dead, have
mercy on us. O charioteer of the cherubim and you who ride upon the
seraphim, have mercy on us. O commander of the angels and king of the
ages, have mercy on us. O resurrection of those put to death and
salvation of those who are perishing, have mercy on us. O sun of
righteousness and life of men, have mercy on us. Guide of those who have
gone astray and refuge of those who have despaired, have mercy on us. O
good shepherd of the sheep and bridegroom of the church, have mercy on
us. God from God, true God from true God, only-begotten Son of the
Father, Lord Jesus Christ, the Savior who was crucified in the flesh for
us, have mercy on us.
