* On the Resurrection
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Concerning Lazarus and the rich man it can be puzzled; the simpler thinking
these things have been said as both receiving with the bodies the things
worthy of what has been done in life; and the more exact, since after the
resurrection no one is left in life, thinking these things have not come to be
in the resurrection; for the rich man says that "I have five brothers, and
that they may not come into this place of torment, send Lazarus announcing to
them the things here"; and through these seeking the tongue, and the finger,
and the bosoms of Abraham, and the reclining. And perhaps the form of the soul
together with the release, being of like kind with the thick and earthy body,
can thus be taken. If at any rate someone of those who have fallen asleep has
been related appearing, he has been seen like the form when he had the flesh.
Then: But also Samuel appearing, as is clear, being visible, presents that a
body was laid around him; especially if we are forced by the proofs to declare
the essence of the soul to be bodiless even this. But also the punished rich
man, and the poor man resting in the bosoms of Abraham, before the presence of
the Savior and before the completion of the age, and for this reason said
before the resurrection, the one to be punished in Hades, and the other to
rest in the bosoms of Abraham, teach that even now at the release the soul
uses a body. From the things of Methodius. Having said (Origen) that the Lord
declared that "those who will obtain the resurrection will then be as angels,"
where the Sadducees try him, he added: "And the angels being outside of flesh
in a height of blessedness, for this reason they also are of glory; and we
then, being about to be equaled to angels, it is necessary that we likewise
must be naked of fleshes as those."
