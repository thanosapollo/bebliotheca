PROEM OF THE RULES IN EPITOME.
=31.1080=.31 The loving God, who teaches man knowledge, commands those
entrusted with the teaching gift through the Apostle to remain in the
teaching, and to those who need the building from the divine teachings he
exhorts through Moses saying: Ask your father, and he will announce to you;
your elders, and they will tell you. Therefore it is necessary for us, who
have been entrusted with the ministry of the word, to be eager at every time
toward the fitting of souls; and some things to witness in common to the whole
Church; and others privately to furnish ourselves to each of those who come,
with authority to ask, both the things toward health of the faith and truth of
the way of life according to the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ, from both of
which the man of God is firmly perfected; and for you to pass over nothing
fruitless, nothing idle for yourselves, but besides what you learn in common,
also privately to ask concerning the things that profit, and to dispose all
leisure of life toward the useful. If therefore God has gathered us for this,
and there is much quiet from the tumults outside, let us neither turn to some
other work, nor again give over the bodies to sleep, but in the care and
examination of the necessary things let us carry through the remaining part of
the night, fulfilling what was said by blessed David, that in the law of the
Lord he will meditate day and night.
