* Fragment on the Epistle to Philemon
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Phm 1:1 In many epistles he places a foreign word as preparatory to the
argument about to be introduced, and now, since he was about to make a request
on behalf of Onesimus by his own dignity (and his dignity is that he has been
bound), he shows the credibility of the appeal.
