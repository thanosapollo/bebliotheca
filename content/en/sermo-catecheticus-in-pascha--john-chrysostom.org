A catechetical on the holy Pascha.
=59.721=
If anyone is pious and a lover of God, let him enjoy this fair festival; if
anyone is a well-minded slave, let him go in rejoicing into the joy of his Lord;
if anyone has toiled fasting, let him now receive the denarius; if anyone has
worked from the first hour, let him receive today the just debt; if anyone came
after the third, let him feast giving thanks; if anyone arrived after the sixth,
let him doubt nothing; for he is not harmed at all; if anyone was late unto the
ninth, let him come near nothing hesitating; if anyone arrived unto the eleventh
only, let him not fear the slowness.
For the Master being munificent receives the last, just as also the first; he
rests the one of the eleventh, =59.722= as the one who worked from the first;
and he has mercy on the later, and he serves the first; and to that one he
gives, and to this one he grants. And he honors the deed, and he praises the
purpose. Therefore let all go in into the joy of our Lord, both first and second
receive the wage, rich and poor dance with one another, continent and slothful
honor the day, those who have fasted and those who have not fasted be glad
today. The table is full, let all luxuriate; the calf is much, let no one go out
hungry. Let all enjoy the wealth of the goodness.
Let no one lament poverty; for the common kingdom has appeared; let no one wail
the faults; =59.723= for forgiveness has risen from the tomb; let no one fear
death; for the death of the Savior has freed us; he quenched it being held by
it; he punished Hades having come down into Hades; he embittered it having
tasted of his flesh.
And Isaiah having taken this beforehand cried: Hades, he says, was embittered.
Having met you below it was embittered; for it was also taken down; it was
embittered; for it was also mocked. It took a body, and it met with God; it took
earth, and it met heaven; it took what it saw, =59.724= and it has fallen whence
it did not see. Where is your sting, death? where is your victory, Hades? Christ
has risen, and you have been thrown down; Christ has risen, and demons have
fallen; Christ has risen, and angels rejoice; Christ has risen, and no dead is
upon a monument. For Christ having been raised from the dead became a firstfruit
of those who have fallen asleep; to him the glory and the power unto the ages of
ages. Amen.
