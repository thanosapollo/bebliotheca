On the same treatise, 5.
=59.731=
1. Among the Jews are the types, but among us the truth. For the law, he says,
had a shadow of the good things to come; and, The law, he says, was given
through Moses; grace =59.732= and truth came through Jesus Christ. For the
things of the Jews are not alien to Christ, but they had a typical and shadowy
kinship toward Christ; and the perfection was reserved =59.733= for his
presence face to face, and the clear vision of the things themselves, when
there is also the vision of the king himself. For one who paints an image
first sketches in shadow, then completes the painting with colors; and the
image is other than the shadow according to what appears, but the same
according to the character that underlies the colors. Consider with me, then,
through the shadows the true things, and behold through types what is
signified, and go forward from the small toward the great; and do not, I pray,
remain upon the earth, performing memorials of the liberation of one nation
from a tyrant, and of the temporary salvation of children, and of a migration
from land to land. For you will not find the festivals of the Greeks standing
very far off from such memorials. For they too celebrate escapes from
diseases, and deliverances from tyrannies, colonizations and foundings of
cities, and victories in wars, and such things, taking whatever is earthly and
temporary as the subject of festivals. Already the great things must be
recalled, the divine gifts and those that are truly of God, and we celebrate
the great eternal feast: a liberation from opposing demons that make war
against the good, dragging away from the kingdom of God; a salvation not
toward a brief life, but toward an eternal abiding; a migration from earth to
heaven according to the present way of life, and into an eternal possession
according to the resurrection that is to come. Egypt oppressed the Jews, who
were freeborn from their fathers, subjecting them to a yoke of slavery,
compelling them to make bricks and to build cities. This is a symbol of
worldly affliction, in which the human race was worn down, having fallen away
from the delight and freedom that is with God, toiling about the working of
earthly things, and constructing tents that perish; because nothing among men
is lasting or secure, but all the works of human hands are perishing, and
wastes consumed by time. God wishes to free his own work from this slavery,
having pitied it as it was worn down, just as he saw the affliction of the
sons of Israel, and he frees them by taking them as his own. For those who
leapt away willingly must return to God willingly. Servants are sent carrying
a law, calling through the law toward righteousness, leading through
righteousness toward God, still bringing small chastisements upon the opposing
powers, not yet bringing complete destruction even in part, not yet wiping out
the ungodly altogether. For this was reserved for the presence of the Master,
who comes after the servants. And types and symbols of these things are Moses,
sent to the Jews with Aaron his brother, calling them to the worship of God,
scourging Egypt with partial plagues on behalf of the enslaved and afflicted
people. But even as these things were happening, Israel was still in Egypt,
and the king of the Egyptians had room to fight against God. Nine plagues, and
still no end of the matter. The last must be brought on, the destruction of
the firstborn, and together with this Israel must go out.
Here a sheep is sacrificed, and the entrances of the Hebrew houses are
anointed with the blood, and the firstborn of the Jews escape the plague of
death through the symbol of the blood. The Jews eat the sheep with unleavened
bread and bitter herbs. They go out from Egypt; Pharaoh pursues, and those
left of the Egyptians; they come to the Red Sea. This receives the army of the
Hebrews, being divided by the power of God and the rod of Moses; but it sinks
the Egyptian force that entered in after them. Christ is a sheep for you at
the exodus from the world, and he translates you with him as he ascends into
heaven; he also raises you up unto the eternal life. Translating the man who
came from David, he translates with him those who follow, the obedient and
tractable, who no longer fear Pharaoh, nor remain in the worldly slaveries and
earthly hardships, because of the small and pungent pleasure, of which Israel
had the type in the eating of flesh and onions and garlic in Egypt. For such
is pleasure according to the world, having a most pungent city of cares woven
together with it, and a fleshly and beastlike fullness. But for you as you go
out from the worldly life, a beginning of true life comes to be; just as for
them the typical Pascha was a beginning of months.
For now is a beginning of living, when you renounce the worldly and earthly
life; now you find what the true living is, that which is in the Spirit.
Christ is sacrificed, so that you know his sacrifice that came to be for you
=59.734=. Then is fulfilled for you, He was led as a sheep to the slaughter,
and as a lamb silent before the one who shears him. And the gentleness in him
preserves the original type of the sheep; but the ascent into height, and not
being held among the things below, preserves the kid's journey upon the
heights. For goats leap and climb upon the higher and hard-to-reach places.
And heaven belonged to high and hard-to-reach men, from the day he fell,
having slipped from the divine commandment, having served the flesh and been
separated from the Holy Spirit. The divine victim sacrificed for you both
ascends and raises you up. For he was dying with you, that you might live in
him. And the blood of the lamb is saving for you, if you approach the blood in
faith, if you anoint, as it were, with the faith of the blood your own senses
and thoughts, through which death enters sending sin in besides, just as
Israel anointed the entrances with the blood. For if desire does not enter in
upon you through the eyes, through hearing, through smell, through taste,
through touch, and after these through the reasonings, death does not master
you, but bearing in the body the dying of Jesus, you also have the life made
manifest in your body, and thus the death from the first-formed Adam will pass
over you, as the destruction of the firstborn passed over the houses of the
Hebrews. Wherefore also the name of the feast upon these things, Pascha,
signifies a passing-over. The sheep is unblemished for you; for Jesus is
unblemished for you; Who, he says, did no sin, nor was deceit found in his
mouth. A perfect male. For the perfection of righteousness is with the only
true king. And the yearling sheep is a type of the eternal Christ. For a year
is a symbol of an age, the same cycling often in every age, and having all the
turnings in itself, a type of the new man, in whom the old of our humanity has
been changed into newness; For put on, he says, the new man. Having put on the
spotlessness and perfection of the sheep you will be a sheep of God, yourself
also presenting your own body a living sacrifice, holy, well-pleasing to God,
according to the Apostle, and you will be sent on toward the eternal life.
2. Do you see that your Pascha does not even admit comparison with the Jewish
one, but that one had only a shadowy likeness of it? And Israel was a child at
the sacrifice of the sheep, thinking this was what God was eager for, and
supposing that through this he had received salvation, and not seeing it as a
type of the sacrifice that truly saves. Wherefore it is said by God toward
them through Jeremiah, that I did not command you concerning sacrifices. And
there is no falsehood with God, that having commanded he should say he had not
commanded. But the force of the matter is made clear, that the sacrifice was
performed prophetically and as a fore-announcement unto Christ. And God,
commanding them to sacrifice a sheep, was showing Christ who would be
sacrificed, and all the things about the sheep prefigured Christ; of which you
have already considered some, and consider the rest in order according to your
power. Upon every house of the Hebrews one complete sheep, and no part of the
flesh is carried out from the house in which it is sacrificed. For upon every
Church and soul Christ is whole everywhere, and nowhere partial. Wherefore
there the sheep are many, according to each house; but one Christ, the same,
according to every soul. For five days the sheep is kept upon the house, from
the tenth unto the fourteenth; then it is thus sacrificed on the fourteenth
toward evening by the synagogue of the Jews. Five intervals of time are
signified by these five days from Adam until the consummation: one, that until
Noah; a second, that from there until Abraham; a third, that unto Moses; a
fourth, that from Moses unto the presence of Christ; a fifth, the present time
of the presence. And it is sacrificed toward evening, because at the end of
the age Israel, by the whole synagogue, agreed together against Christ.
The sacrifice of Christ was rehearsed from the beginning in the ever-recurring
sufferings of the righteous from Abel the just, and Christ was the one
suffering in them all; but it was completed when the divine lamb, present
through himself, suffered. And indeed he suffered on the fourteenth of the
same month, and he gave over the spirit toward evening, and according to this
type this was the taking of the sheep from the tenth for the sacrifice,
=59.735= and the sacrificing on the fourteenth toward evening, because from
the beginning there was the rehearsal of the passion, but at the end the
passion. Then consider with me concerning the eating of the sheep a new and
paradoxical command of God, not to eat the flesh of the sheep raw. For who
would eat it raw? Let it be said, Nor boiled, that they may not delay; for the
time was pressing. What then is the command, not raw, but roasted with fire?
For no one forbids what does not come to be. But through the types, as I said,
the things themselves are signified. He offers the holy food raw who has idle
faith; boiled, the lover of pleasure, watering down in the slackness of life
the firm work of faith; [but roasted, the one] who accomplishes it in a spirit
warm and boiling. They are commanded to eat the head with the feet and the
inward parts. Know Christ also from the head, that being God before the ages
he is head of the invisible and spiritual Church; and from the feet, that he
is also man, having set foot upon the earth at the last of the ages; and from
the inward parts, that having a hidden power and righteousness in the flesh,
he is beneficial and saving to those who partake not only according to the
outside and what is seen, but also according to the inward and according to
the hidden. And the flesh is eaten in the night, and is not left over into the
day.
For there is a remembrance of the passion of Christ in the time of the worldly
night, after which day will shine forth, and the life of Christ will appear.
According to, And the day has drawn near. And no one breaks a bone. For
neither did the executioners break the legs of Christ, having broken [those]
of the ones crucified with him, as the evangelist bore witness. The bitter
herbs eaten with the sheep signified the bitterness of afflictions; the
unleavened bread, simplicity and sincerity, as Paul says, that is, the new
virtue unmixed with the old wickedness. It remains for you both to consider
spiritually the figure of those eating the Pascha, and to imitate it; for in
the spiritual the bodily figure will be. It is not an empty figure, but a
saving work: gird your loin, not that of the body with a strap, but that of
the soul in readiness. Likewise put sandals on the feet in the preparation of
the Gospel, according to the Apostle. Have a staff in the hands, not wood, but
of supporting faith. That whole figure being of travel, as of those hastening
toward the exodus from Egypt, imitate it still better, as one hastening toward
the exodus from the world and from corruption. And let there be haste, and
slackness nowhere in the spiritual eating of the Pascha =59.736=. And the
eagerness is good here, not displaying the gluttony of those eating the flesh
in haste, but the love of God of those hastening from the world toward God.
And for seven days the unleavened food, that is, sincerity through the whole
of life. For through the seven days the whole of time is multiplied. And the
one who has leaven is utterly destroyed. For the wickedness of the old man is
destructive and in no way able to be mixed with the righteousness and holiness
of the new man. And you see that the destruction because of such leaven is
just; but because of the common leaven of bread God would not have seemed
justly to be severe. For justice does not appear in the types, but upon the
truth it both shines forth and is known; just as also the very matter of the
anointing of the blood, seeming to be unseemly for God, is taken away, when
the type is understood according to the truth. For it is unseemly that God
should seem to need a sign of blood toward the recognition of the Hebrew
houses; but most fitting that we should be known to God through the faith of
the blood of Christ and be saved by God. Is it not then also unseemly simply
to hear the matter of circumcision thus, as of God commanding in haste that
the one about to eat of the Pascha, if he should not be circumcised, be
circumcised, and so come to the eating? But if you understand the force of
circumcision spiritually, you will see a mystery and will glorify God.
Circumcision was of the generative member. From birth you had, O man, a
covering, which you needed to have stripped away, that with unveiled face you
might behold truth, and not, covered with the body and an earthly covering,
look up toward the truth.
For when you become naked of the fleshly body, you approach Christ as spirit,
and being joined to the Lord you will become one spirit, sharing in the holy
and saving communion, which the eating of the flesh made known. Be circumcised
then and strip off the body following Christ, according to Paul who says, that
You were circumcised with a circumcision not made with hands in the stripping
off of the body of the flesh in the circumcision of Christ; and thus being
circumcised of all love of money, love of glory, love of pleasure, be filled
with the food toward God that leads up into heaven, that frees from wicked
rulers, that looses from hard toils, as he grants you a glorious and blessed
return unto God, in Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom be the glory unto the ages
of ages. Amen.
