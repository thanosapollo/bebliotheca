ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON THE NUMBERS.
=12.576=
Nothing except toward the manna are our eyes. Man was named from the
Hebrews first seeing a strange sight and saying to one another in the
ancestral tongue, "Man," that is, "What is this?" which they would not
have said, if it were customary, as some of the Gentiles say, for the
nature of the places from of old to bear such things. And now too, if it
comes down a little for the sake of memory. And they added no more.
There is lacking, "to prophesy." They prophesied no more, he says. For
they prophesy once, that they may not be made equal to Moses; but so
that it may be known that they have partaken, and all prophesy together.
For the Spirit is one in Christ, and the operation through all is one.
And the people did not set out, until Miriam was cleansed. And the
tabernacle remained seven days upon the place in honor of Miriam, that
she, being such, might not follow. Send men for yourself, and what
follows. He commands certain ones to be sent, showing that they are
drawing near to Palestine, having presented what of Egypt was more ready
for the Hebrews; and having convicted the unbelief, he assigns the
wilderness as a tomb to those whose mind remained hard; for the death of
the ungodly is more to be chosen than the wandering in life. And Moses
named Ause the son of Nave, Joshua. It is to be noted that the son of
Nave became a type of Christ, who, having freed the most pitiable people
that was wandering, led them up into heaven; not into a land that eats
those who dwell in it. Surely they shall not see the land which I swore
to their fathers. It is to be observed that of those who came out of the
land of Egypt some entered into the land of promise; for God did not
forbid this; but he destroyed in the wilderness only those twenty years
old and above of those who came out of the land of Egypt who made the
calf; sparing a kindred youthfulness in them whose time of life was not
yet twenty years, and much more the infants, and children, until the age
of youth. =12.577= Of the same. This is a type of the truth. "For unless
you turn and become as the children, you shall not enter into the
kingdom." Take up the bronze censers. As the bronze serpent was a sign
of their unbelief, so also the censers of those around Korah and Dathan
and Abiram, being an overlay for the altar. And to the sons of Levi,
behold I have given every tithe in Israel. Reasonably the firstfruits
have been assigned to the priests, since the tribe of Levi does not
inherit, nor receive a lot as the rest. This is the procurer of
possessionlessness for the priests, the lot of sharing with the altar.
The people gave tithes to the Levites, and the Levites to the priests.
Shall we bring water for you out of this rock? Moses, not having
glorified God before the people, was hindered from passing through with
them. For that they might not honor him whom they honored while living,
in the worships of God after he had gone, he made the place [perhaps:
tomb] unseen. Now this congregation will lick up all those around us. He
took this as an example: as the calf has the green things in the mouth,
so also the holy people, warring with the lips, have the weapons in the
mouths through the prayers. And Balak, knowing this as clearly happening
in the former wars, wants himself also to wage war with the weapons
through the lips; and he calls Balaam who has the weapons of the
opposing power, the curses upon the lips. For the holy angels work
together with the just toward the things of salvation and good works,
and the wicked demons with the ungodly sinful men toward the wicked
works and those harmful to the salvation of men. For the holy wage war
with words of prayers, but the ungodly and sinners with words of magics.
Of the same. And the history of the things according to sense is filled
with great benefit. And from the history at hand we learn many things,
both that it is possible to wage war with words, and that great affairs
are managed and set right through words; and the holy wage war with
words of prayers; but the ungodly with words of magics. Of the same. The
opposing power never does a good work, but works all things most evil;
and whatever evil it works, it cannot restore into the better, for it
has no rank of the better. But to the better power all things are
possible. For our God makes to suffer, and again restores, but he has
not to work evil; for he is good not according to habit only, but also
according to essence. Of the same. Among human things there are also
certain completed oracles, and sacrifices, and certain gifts offered to
the demons; so that through these being offered those who wish may
divine. And an oracle is each affair. For it is not a true prediction.
And the Lord came to him. In truth =12.580= a divine power came to
Balaam, not called by him. It came, then, that it might make an aversion
of the demons called by Balaam, and might manage the divine predictions.
And the angel of God rose up to oppose him on the way. The angel
opposing Balaam on the way, is this not perhaps the one concerning whom
God said to Moses that "My angel goes before you, guarding you on the
way"? Go with these men. God did not permit Balaam to go away to Balak;
but since he wished to go away, he concedes. And he charges him to say
only the things revealed; and the angel also, having appeared and
frightened him, confirms this. Build me seven altars. And there are also
works done through another body, both among the better and among the
worse. As for example the things done in Egypt by Moses and Aaron and
the enchanters. But the divine power, being compared with the opposing
one before the face of those in doubt, this divine power conquers, as
the rod of Moses swallowed the rods of the enchanters. Of the same.
Balaam was accustomed, serving with the same sacrifices, to call the
demons. For such sacrifices were performed in the world to the demons.
For this reason then God requires sacrifices of the first people, that
through the offering to God, the offering to the demons might be
abolished, even if they were especially symbols. Of the same. The Savior
therefore, having become man, sent his disciples that they might loose
the donkey, that is from the bonds with which the opposing power, the
intelligible Balaam, bound it; and having loosed it they led it to
Jesus, and having laid the garments upon the donkey, that is the virtues
of the Gospel; and the Savior, having sat upon it, entered into the holy
city. And Balak took Balaam upon the peak of Peor. Balak supposed the
art of Balaam measured in the places. Wherefore he took him to the peak
of Peor, thinking the magician had power from there to curse the people
of God, Balaam. And Peor is interpreted seasoning, or skin of mouth,
that is, the extremity of love of pleasure, and deadness of word. From
which Balaam tried to curse the people of God. Of the same. There are
two peaks, that is of the most extreme virtue and of vice; and Moses has
gone up upon the height of virtue, but Balaam upon the height of vice.
He did not go according to his custom to meet the omens. This Balaam is
a symbol of the people from the nations, who formerly followed the
omens, but knowing that it is not pleasing to the Lord, he added no
more; but indeed he is also interpreted vain =12.581= people, and son of
Beor is interpreted leathern. The vain was according to the former of
the nations, and Beor of him who was in vanity and in deadness according
to the former, clearly. A man truly seeing. I, the once Balaam, the vain
people, having opened the inner eyes of my mind, and having seen the
spiritual Israel of old, and the economy according to him, am counted
worthy of the Holy Spirit. "Says the man who truly sees," and what
follows, does not fit the once Balaam, but fits the now Balaam. How fair
are your houses, Jacob, your tents, Israel. The name of Jacob is not of
the one dwelling, but of the house; but the name of Israel is of the one
dwelling; for Jacob belongs to the bodily affair. As shady glens.
Intelligible glens are those shading that in which there are rational
fruit-bearing things and a covering of practical virtue. Paradises upon
a river are intelligible, a place in which rational things have been
planted, watered either by the contemplation of the things that have
come to be, or by the contemplation of the Holy Trinity. Of the same.
Those dwelling in tents are of those who journey, and there is outside
this world a great way and many tents. Those dwelling are not of those
who journey, but of those who stand. As cedars beside waters.
Intelligible cedars grown beside waters are rational souls watered by
the knowledge of the truth. And he will lord over many nations. A lord
is the one who commands with authority, but a slave is the one who obeys
slavishly. A lord is the one who buys with his own labors and, qualified
by lordship, is free of passion. A slave is the one who is passionate
and in need of the better, and who needs the oversight of his own lord.
And there are many lords, but blessed are those who have our Lord Jesus
Christ as lord. And Gog, his kingdom, shall be exalted. Kingdom of Gog
is interpreted gifts. According to one interpretation gifts are the
gifts of the Holy Spirit; but according to another, "Let him who is upon
the housetop not come down into his house." There is then a kingdom of
such a habit. God led him out of Egypt. Both the Savior according to,
"Out of Egypt I called my son," and Israel. The one he casts out, the
other he calls. Of the same. The oracle, that the Lord would come into
Egypt, indicated the conveyance of our Savior Jesus Christ that would
take place together with his parents. And this world also is compared to
Egypt. As the glory of a unicorn to him. A unicorn is =12.584=
intelligibly Christ according to the accident; from whom there are many
unicorns. For from one Jesus Christ many have partaken to be christs.
Some say that the deer also is a unicorn. He shall eat the nations of
his enemies. Concerning Christ: we were once nations of his enemies.
Christ therefore shall eat us, the essential virtue feeding upon us, as
he says: "My food is to do the will of him who sent me." And he shall
suck out their fatness. Then, he says, when he transforms the fleshly
condition into a spiritual one through the practical. Those who bless
you have been blessed. Two persons are signified upon these; and they
are of the faithful and unfaithful upon Christ. And a little after: In
these things then both the undertaking of those opposing Israel has been
overturned, and the evil craft of magic. And the people was profaned to
commit fornication. And the counsel for the Hebrews to commit
fornication and to worship Baal-peor was of the seer. Wherefore Moses
says: "These were to the sons of Israel, according to the word of
Balaam, to make them revolt and to overlook the word of the Lord for the
sake of Baal-peor." And the side toward the southwest shall be yours.
From the river of Egypt to the river Euphrates he promised to Abraham.
Why then did he not give it to them? For Numbers signifies a lesser one
being given. And I think that he would have given it, if they had been
faithful according to him who received the promises. And this
reasonably. For often, having threatened evils, he did not bring them
on, for they repented, as in the case of the Ninevites. Nevertheless the
women shall be from the people of their father. God therefore commands,
except for Judah and Levi, that it not be permitted to join from tribe
to tribe, that what comes from above concerning the Savior may not be
shown as foreign, the, "King and priest according to the order of
Melchizedek."
