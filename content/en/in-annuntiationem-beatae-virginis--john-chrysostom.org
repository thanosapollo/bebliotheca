On the Annunciation of our all-glorious Lady, the Theotokos.
=50.791=
Again glad tidings of joy, again messages of freedom, again a recalling,
again a return, again a =50.792= voice of gladness, again deliverance from
slavery; an angel speaks with a virgin, since the serpent also spoke
=50.793= with a woman. In the sixth month, it says, the angel Gabriel was
sent by God to a virgin betrothed to a man. Gabriel was sent to announce
the salvation of the whole world; Gabriel was sent, bearing to Adam the
writ of his recalling; Gabriel was sent to a virgin, that he might turn
the dishonor of the female into honor; Gabriel was sent to make ready a
worthy chamber for the pure Bridegroom; Gabriel was sent to betroth the
creature to the Creator; Gabriel was sent to the living palace of the
King of the angels; Gabriel was sent to a virgin betrothed to Joseph, yet
kept for the Son; a bodiless servant was sent to an undefiled virgin; he
who is free of sin was sent to her who cannot admit corruption; a lamp
was sent to announce the Sun of righteousness; dawn was sent, running
before the light of day; Gabriel was sent, declaring him who is in the
bosom of the Father and in the arms of the mother; Gabriel was sent,
showing him who is on a throne and in a cave; a soldier was sent, crying
the mystery of the King; a mystery made known by faith, not searched out
by curiosity; a mystery worshipped, not weighed in the balance; a mystery
confessed in theology, not pried into; a mystery confessed, not measured.
In the sixth month Gabriel was sent to a virgin.
What sixth month does he mean? Which? From the time Elizabeth received
the glad tidings, from the time she conceived John. How is this clear?
Let the archangel himself interpret, saying to the virgin: Behold, he
says, Elizabeth your kinswoman has also conceived a son; and this is the
sixth month for her who was called barren. In the sixth month. The sixth
month of the conception of John. For the soldier had to go first, the
bodyguard had to take the lead, the herald of the Master's coming had to
make the way. In the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent to a virgin
betrothed to a man, not joined to him; betrothed, yet kept. Why
betrothed? So that the Robber might not learn the mystery too soon. For
that the King would come through a virgin, the Evil One knew; for he too
had heard the words of Isaiah, Behold, the virgin shall conceive; and he
reasonably watched every utterance of the virgin, so that, whenever he
should perceive that this mystery was being fulfilled, he might prepare
the slander. For this reason the Master came through one who was
betrothed; for she who was betrothed was already pledged. In the sixth
month the angel Gabriel was sent to a virgin betrothed to a man whose
name was Joseph.
Hear what the prophet says of this man and of the virgin. The sealed book
shall be given to a man who knows letters. What is the sealed book, if
not, in every way, the undefiled virgin? By whom shall it be given? By
the priests, clearly. To whom? To Joseph the carpenter. This Joseph,
having first lived in honorable marriage and begotten sons, had put away
his wife, and thereafter lived in chastity, bringing up the children in
the discipline and admonition of the Lord. And indeed when the apostle
Paul came into the preaching and found one of this man's sons, he writes
saying: But I saw none other of the apostles except James the Lord's
brother; not as born of the virgin Mary, but of Joseph. Since then the
priests, as to a chaste man, betrothed Mary to Joseph and entrusted her
to him, waiting for the time of marriage; and he was going to receive her
and keep the virgin in incorruption. This the prophet proclaimed from
afar: The sealed book shall be given, he says, to a man who knows
letters, that is, who has known marriage, and he shall say: I cannot
read. Why can you not, O Joseph? I cannot read, he says; for the book is
sealed. Kept for whom? Guarded as a dwelling for the Maker of all. But
let us return to the matter at hand. In the sixth month Gabriel =50.794=
was sent to a virgin, having received, no doubt, some such commands as
these: Come, he says, O angel, become a minister of a fearful mystery;
serve a hidden wonder. I am eager, by my compassions, to come down in
search of the Adam who went astray. Sin has made old him who was formed
in my image, has wasted the work of my hands, has darkened the beauty I
fashioned. The wolf is devouring my nursling; the dwelling of paradise is
desolate. The tree of life is guarded by the flaming sword, and the place
of delight is shut. I pity him who was warred against, and I wish to
seize the enemy. I wish to escape the notice of all the powers in heaven;
to you alone I trust the mystery. Go to the virgin Mary; go to the living
gate of which the prophet said: Glorious things are spoken of you, city
of God.
Go to my rational paradise; go to the eastern city; go to the dwelling
worthy of me, the Word; go to the second heaven upon earth; go to the
light cloud; announce to her the rain of my coming; go to the sanctuary
prepared for me; go to the bridal chamber of my becoming man; go to the
pure bride-chamber of my birth according to the flesh; speak into the
ears of the rational ark: prepare for me the entrances of hearing; but do
not alarm her, do not trouble the soul of the virgin; appear gently to
the sanctuary; cry to her first a voice of joy; you say to Mary, Hail,
full of grace, that I may have mercy on Eve who was made wretched. The
angel heard these things, and reasoned with himself, as was likely:
Strange is the matter, beyond thought what is spoken. He who is fearful
to the cherubim, unseen by the seraphim, incomprehensible to all the
angelic powers: what a meeting he promises! He announces to a maiden a
presence in his own person, or rather he promises her an entrance through
hearing! And he who condemned Eve is so eager to glorify her daughter,
and he says: Prepare for me the entrances of hearing! And can a womb
contain the uncontainable? Truly fearful is the mystery. While the angel
was pondering such things, the Master says to him: Why are you troubled
and astonished, O Gabriel? Were you not sent by me a little while ago to
Zechariah the priest? Did you not give him the glad tidings of the birth
of John? Did you not impose the penalty of silence on the priest when he
disbelieved? Did you not condemn the old man to speechlessness? Was it
not you who pronounced, and I who confirmed? Did not the deed follow upon
your announcement? Did not the barren woman conceive? Did not the womb
kindle? Did not the disease of fruitlessness depart? Did not nature's
idleness flee? Is she not now bearing an ear of grain who formerly bore
no fruit?
Is any word impossible with me, the Creator? How then have you been
wrapped in doubt? What then does the angel say again? Master, to heal the
faults of nature, to dissolve the winter of the passions, to recall the
deadness of members to vital power, to command nature to the work of
childbearing, to loose barrenness in members past their bound, to recast
an aged stalk into a green-bearing form, to show the barren field all at
once a mother of sheaves, this is a work of your power, accomplished
according to custom; and Sarah bears witness, and after her Rebecca, and
again Anna, who served the hard passion of barrenness, then received
freedom from you. But for a virgin to bear who has not met with a man is
above the laws of nature. And you even promise the maiden your own
presence. The bounds of heaven and earth do not contain you; and how
shall a virgin womb contain you? And the Master says: How did the tent of
Abraham contain me? The angel says: Because it had the sea of
hospitality. Then consider, Master: there you appeared to the patriarch
in the wayside tent, and you who fill all things passed by. How shall
Mary bear the fire of the Godhead? Your throne burns, compassed with
brightness, and can the virgin bear you without being struck down?
=50.795= Yes indeed: if the fire in the desert harmed the bush, my
presence will surely harm Mary also; but if that fire, which sketched in
shadow the coming of the divine fire from heaven, watered the bush and
did not burn it, what would you say of the truth, which comes down not in
a fire of flame but in the form of rain? Then at last the angel fulfilled
what was commanded him; and going to the virgin, and entering, he cried
to her, saying: Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you. No longer is
the devil against you; for where the enemy first wounded, there the
physician lays the plaster; from where death had the beginning of its
going out, from there life fashioned the entrance of life.
Through a woman the evils well forth, through a woman the better things
spring. Hail, full of grace; do not be ashamed as the cause of
condemnation; for you become mother of him who condemned =50.796=. Hail,
full of grace; hail, undefiled bearer of the Bridegroom for the world
that was left a widow; hail, full of grace, who in the womb sank the
death of the mother; hail, living temple of God; hail, dwelling equally
balanced with heaven and earth; hail, spacious place of the uncontainable
nature.
But things being so, the physician stood over the sick, to those sitting
in darkness the sun of righteousness appeared, to those storm-tossed the
anchor and the waveless harbor, to the slaves hated without
reconciliation the ambassador was born, and the bond of peace, to the
captives the Redeemer sojourned, to those warred against the mighty
shelter of unspeakable joy and love. For he himself is our peace, as the
divine Apostle says, of which may we always have enjoyment, by the grace
of Christ himself our God; to whom be glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
