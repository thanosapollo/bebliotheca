FROM THE INTERPRETATION OF SAINT CYRIL ON THE PROVERBS
The Lord created me. {Of Cyril.} "He created me," if you take it of the
Godhead, is instead of "He appointed me"; as in, "Create in me a clean heart,
O God." And, "That he might create the two in himself." For it is not
substantially, that he might create those already come into being, but that he
might appoint. He therefore appointed me as beginning of his providential and
creative works. For he also says to David: I was appointed king by him.
