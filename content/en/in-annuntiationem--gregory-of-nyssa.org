* On the Annunciation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Encomium of Gregory, bishop of Nyssa, on the Annunciation of the all-holy
Theotokos. Always when I remember the disobedience of Eve I weep; and when I
see again and consider the fruit of the Theotokos Mary I am renewed. Come,
peoples, let us all hymn him who was born of a virgin: the glory and imprint
of the Godhead existing before the ages became like us in poverty; the
magnificent authority, being in the form of God, took the form of a servant;
he who upon the cherubim is hymned by myriads of angels dwells as a citizen in
the world; he who is before and who saves the whole world was born of a holy
virgin, that he might again give life to the first-formed man. Come then we
also, beloved, having rushed toward the harbor of divine grace of our Savior,
saying with Gabriel "Hail, full of grace," let us hymn the holy virgin. For
from her came forth the true light, which enlightens us by his goodness; for
she was a consecrated sanctuary and an unmixed temple of God, the golden altar
of the incense, because of her unsurpassed purity, the divine incense of the
compound and the holy oil of use, the precious alabaster of the pure nard, the
priestly diadem, announcing the ineffable will of God, she who alone is holy
in body and spirit, the gate that looks toward the east and through the
Master's entrance and exit has illumined the world, the fruitful olive, from
which the Holy Spirit, having taken the mystical twig of the Lord, ordered the
storm-tossed race of men. She is the boast of virgins and the gladness of
mothers, receiving the archangelic message and divine gospel: "Hail, full of
grace, the Lord is with you and again from you"; for the Lord, having been
well pleased to be conceived from the holy virgin, sent forth the archangel
announcing the good news. And he, coming from the unutterable powers above to
Mary, first proclaimed to her "Hail, full of grace," and immediately added
again: "The Lord is with you, blessed are you among women, and blessed is the
fruit of your womb." And she was troubled and reasoned of what sort this
greeting might be. Fittingly, then, has grace chosen the holy virgin Mary, for
the holy one was wise in all things among women from all generations; no one
like her has ever come to be. Not as the first virgin Eve, being alone in
paradise, received the word from the serpent with slack mind, and so the
thought of the heart was corrupted, and through her the whole contest of the
saints came about; but in the holy virgin alone has that one's fault been
restored. Not as Sarah, when given the good news concerning the conception of
the son, laughed with rashness; not as Rebecca of her own accord received the
ornaments and eagerly watered the camels of the suitor; not as every woman
received the gracious greeting without inquiry; but with a stout mind, with
bright thought, she was at a loss in herself and said: "Of what sort might
this greeting be? From where does the gift possess its ground? From where has
this one brought us the blessing? From where has the pearl of the word been
sent to us from the treasures? I wish to know what the gift is, and who is the
bearer, and who the sender. You have come from heaven, you show a human form,
I behold a man, and you flash lamps of light." These things she was at a loss
about in herself and said; and with such words the archangel resolved Mary's
perplexity: "The Holy Spirit will come upon you and the power of the Most High
will overshadow you; therefore you will conceive and bear a son; and you shall
call his name Jesus." The holy virgin says to the angel: "How shall this be to
me, since I do not wish to know an earthly man, since I have allotted myself
to the heavenly bridegroom? I wish to remain a virgin; I do not wish to betray
the dignity of virginity." And with such words the angel strengthened the holy
one: "Fear not, Mary, for I have not come to make you afraid, but to drive
away the ground of fear; fear not, Mary, for you have found grace with God; do
not examine the grace, for grace does not endure to be subject to the laws of
nature. And the Holy Spirit will come upon you, therefore also the holy thing
born of you shall be called Son of God, of like form, of one essence,
coeternal with the Father. In whom the Father possesses the whole
manifestation of the Godhead, the imprint in the person, and through the
radiance the glory shines forth, that he may enlighten those sitting in
darkness. You have learned, O blessed one, things that until now were hidden
even from the angels; you have known, Mary, the things that escaped the
prophets and patriarchs; you have heard, Mary, things of which the choir of
the God-bearers has not yet been counted worthy. David and Isaiah and the rest
of the God-speakers proclaimed beforehand concerning the Master's becoming
man, but they did not know the how. But you alone, holy virgin, receive and
learn the mysteries unknown to these; and do not reckon 'How shall this be to
me?' For he who formed man from virgin earth, he also now does whatever he
wills, that he may save his own formation." Now it is fitting for me, as far
as is possible, to wonder at the manner of the holy virgin. This one the angel
first again greeted "Hail, full of grace," since with her the whole treasure
of grace dwells. From all generations she alone has been counted worthy to
bear God; she alone bears him who bears all things by the word. And it is
possible to wonder not only at the beauty of the Theotokos, but also at the
virtuousness of the soul, wherefore also the angel proclaimed to her
beforehand "Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you," for the holy one,
having prepared her soul as hospitality for God, became a receptacle of
guest-gifts and of divine mysteries. Come then we also, beloved, let us pay
the debt according to our power; following the voice of the angel let us cry:
"Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you," for the rejoicing is truly yours,
since the Lord of all has tabernacled with you, as he knows. "Hail, full of
grace, the Lord is with you," not an earthly bridegroom, but the Lord himself,
the Father of purity, the guardian of virginity and Lord of sanctification,
the creator of incorruption and giver of freedom, the caretaker of salvation
and dispenser of the treasury of true peace; the Lord himself is with you,
since in you the divine grace has rested. "Hail, full of grace, the Lord is
with you": Adam no longer fears the deceitful serpent, for he who is born of
you has abolished all the power of the enemy; the race of men no longer fears
the error and deceit of the serpent, for the Lord crushed the heads of the
dragon in the water of baptism; I no longer fear to hear "earth you are, and
to earth you shall go," for the Lord in holy baptism has washed away from me
the filth of sin; I no longer weep, I no longer mourn, I no longer say "I was
turned to misery when a thorn was stuck in me," for the Lord, having uprooted
the thorn of our sins, crowned his own head. My sin has been loosed, my
ancient curse has been loosed, that which says "thorns and thistles the earth
shall bring forth for you"; the thorn has been uprooted, the thistle has dried
up, the tree of life and of grace has blossomed from the holy virgin. Eve no
longer fears the reproach, nor the pangs of childbirth, for in the holy virgin
that one's fault has been restored. For the holy virgin has become for us a
fountain of life, a fountain of light for those who believe in Christ, being
the rising of the noetic light. "Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you,"
and from you, he who is perfect in Godhead, in whom dwells all the fullness of
the Godhead. "Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you," with the servant the
King, with the undefiled one he who sanctifies the universe, with the
beautiful one he who is fair in beauty beyond the sons of men, that he might
save the man whom he made according to the image, in whose divine faith and
grace we rejoice and are illumined with the thrice-sunned light. Roses and
lilies and ointments of noetic fragrance; our unutterable spring, Christ, has
come; the delightful and most beautiful paradise of the churches has filled
the three parts of our soul from the divine spices and heavenly gifts. Let us
therefore approach with a pure heart and we shall find the gold-gleaming one
and fruits of immortality giving fragrance upon her. For from the incorrupt
Mary has sprouted for us the ever-living tree of grace, for the blessed one
was filled in all things with spiritual wisdom from all generations .... In
the undefiled ever-virgin Mary alone has the plant of life blossomed for us,
for she alone has become pure in soul and body and with bold thought conversed
with the angel: "From where is this greeting? and I wish to learn how this
shall be to me; how does the magnificent authority become like us in poverty?
how does he who has the authority of the power of the heavens take on the
image of our lowliness, and he who is God before the ages is about to become a
child, and he who wraps himself in light as a garment is to be incarnate?" And
the angel says to her: "Yes, he was well pleased that these things be so, that
he might save his own formation." Such a mystery the holy virgin sought, the
incomprehensible, and she became a receptacle of the divine mysteries, the
Holy Spirit having overshadowed. And the truth of the body was assumed, and
Wisdom built a house; the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us, that is,
in that very body which he took from a human, and he animated it with a spirit
of a rational soul. And the unchangeable God received the form of the servant,
that to the unbelieving he might be thought a man, but to the faithful be
manifested as God; for being Son of God and God before the ages, he deigned to
become son of a holy woman, and the invisible is seen, and the rich becomes
poor for us, and the impassible and immortal suffers as man. He was united to
a body and was not altered in spirit; the uncontained has entered into a
mortal body, that he might make it immortal, making it immortal together with
the Godhead. Light has come into the world, Christ our God, enlightening all
things with everlasting light, the voice of the invisible Father bearing
witness: "This is my Son and Word, existing before the ages." And Mary was
walled about by the word of the angel, and she reasoned about the birth of the
Master, meeting with human reasonings too unequal: at one time being raised to
the height of the Godhead, at another considering the lowliness of the
humanity. And as this scale of her mind was carried toward both sides, then
she is counted worthy of the exact tipping of God's weight. For he who kept
the pure workshop of virginity unharmed has also made the discerning faculty
of the heart unswerving. So the holy one was filled within with joy, because
of him who took flesh from her. Grace has come to us, Christ the Son of God,
born unutterably from the virgin Mary; by grace he who created all things
saves us. All creation dances, Christ having been born in the world, and we
then, still being in the body, shall be eager to propitiate God with angelic
hymnodies, and with an examined pattern let us faithfully sow in the mind the
truth of the divine doctrines. For the mystery is incomprehensible and
invisible, unchangeable and uncircumscribed; worshipped in silence and struck
with awe in the mind. For the holy one also wondered at the manner, how the
radiance of the light becomes the offspring of a woman; she clasped the
treasure of life and reasoned about the greeting of the angel, until she
brought to fullness the fruit of salvation. Here then the fair-formed flowers
of the teaching of Christ offer the fruits of blessing; here all purity and
fragrance bring the incense of prayer to God with a pure conscience; here
virginity and temperance, dancing, have borne the life-bearing cluster; here
the prizes of the victory-bearing power make fragrant for us the treasures of
love; here the mystery of the Holy Trinity has been manifested by the
archangel to the virgin according to the gospel, "The Holy Spirit will come
upon you and the power of the Most High will overshadow you, wherefore also
the holy thing born of you shall be called Son of God." To him be the glory
and the power and worship unto the ages. Amen.
