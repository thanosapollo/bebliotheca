If the flood is by water, the stone pillar will remain and the things carved
on it; but if by fire, the brick one will be saved. From the Magusaeans, that
is the Persians, astrology and magic began, they having taken their beginnings
from Nimrod who founded Babylon. After Ninus there reigned over the Assyrians
a certain man named Thyras, 30 years, whom they also renamed Ares after the
name of the wandering star; to this Ares the Assyrians set up the first
pillar, and worshipping him as a god even until now they call him in Persian
Baal god, which is Ares god of wars, of whom Daniel also makes mention. In the
times of the reign of Phoenix there was Heracles the philosopher, the Tyrian,
and the discoverer of the murex. For being carried up toward the seashore of
Tyre he saw a shepherd's dog eating the so-called murex, and when the shepherd
thought the dog was bleeding, having taken a lock of wool from the sheep and
wiped off what was dripping from the dog's mouth, and when the lock was dyed,
Heracles perceived that it was not blood but a source of dye; and marvelling
at this as a great gift he brought it to king Phoenix, who, being still more
astonished, 5 commanded wool to be dyed from the murex and a mantle to be made
for him, and so he first wore a garment of purple. After Nebuchadnezzar and
Belshazzar his son, Cyrus the Persian reigned over the Persians, 32 years,
who, being about to join battle with Croesus king of the Lydians and being
afraid, sent for the prophet Daniel from the land of India, who when asked
said to Cyrus, "Take courage, you are conquering your enemy. For concerning
you the prophet Isaiah said. Thus says the Lord to Cyrus, whose right hand I
have held. Nations shall hearken before him, and I will break the strength of
a king. He shall build my city, and he shall turn back the captivity of my
people. Said the Lord of Sabaoth." And Cyrus, hearing this, said, falling at
the feet of Daniel, "The Lord your God lives. I will release Israel from my
land, that they may serve their God in Jerusalem." So having drawn up against
Croesus, having defeated him utterly and having lost 400 thousands, and having
taken the rest captive together with him, and having completely overthrown the
kingdom of the Lydians, he at once released all the sons of Israel with
Zerubbabel to Jerusalem. After Cyrus, Darius his son, who is also Cambyses,
reigned 8 years. After Cambyses, Artaxerxes Mnemon, 20 years. 6 And it is to
be known that Romus who reigned over Rome invented the so-called Brumalia for
a cause


of this kind. For they say that he and his brother, having been born of
fornication, were cast out in a wooded place and, being found by a certain
rustic woman, were reared. And it was a reproach among the Romans to be fed
from others and to be called eaters of what is another's. So Romus, wishing to
wipe out his own reproach, invented the Brumalia, saying it was necessary that
the emperor feed his own senate in the time of winter as men of honor, when
they rest from war, and that the senate in turn feed the army. And he called
the name of the meal from that Brumalium, which is in Latin to be fed from
others. After Romus, when Rome was administered by consuls and generals, of
whom one was also Malius, Februarius, being a senator and envying Malius,
arranged that the senators drive him from the city. Then when the Gauls came
on the twentieth of the month Sextilis by night and took the city and worked
much slaughter, the senators fled and call Malius back again, who coming with
an army and taking the Gauls killed them all, and having recovered Rome again
he shortened the days of the month Sextilis as having been unlucky for Rome
because its capture happened in it. And stripping Februarius of his
possessions and throwing him out of the city dishonorably 7 he killed him,
calling the month Sextilis February as worthy of the name. After the said
Artaxerxes, Choos his son reigned, who also took Egypt and destroyed it,
Nectanebo then reigning over it, who fleeing and foretelling the future
through certain Egyptian illusions and magics becomes known to Philip and
Olympias his wife, from whom he begot Alexander. And Philip reigned over
Macedonia 20 years, who having subjected Thessaly founded the city
Thessalonica. After Philip, Alexander reigned over the Macedonians, when also
Darius son of Arsames, reigning after Choos, had become lord of all, whom
Alexander overcame and took all his kingdom and all the land of the Persians
and Medes and Parthians and Babylonians and all the Indian parts. And having
founded great Alexandria in his own name he returned to Babylon, in which he
also dies, being 32 years old, having reigned 12 years. It is to be known that
Scripture mentions two Lamechs, one from Cain, who also murdered a man and a
young man (for having slain the two brothers of righteous Enoch he also took
the two men's wives), and another the father of Noah, who is also later and
descended from Seth. 8 Terah begot Abraham, Nachor, and Aram the father of
Lot, who also died before his father Terah; for when Abraham made a fire so as
to burn his father's idols, Aram going in to rescue them died in the house
before his father. From the race of Sidos son of Egyptus, king of the
Egyptians, was descended Melchi the father of Sedek, who becoming priest of
God and king of the Canaanites was called Melchizedek patronymically, and
having founded a city on mount Zion named it Salem, which is city of peace, in
which having also reigned 13 years he died righteous and a virgin, as Josephus
says. And the great Cyril also says. Melchizedek is called without genealogy
because he is not at all from the seed of Abraham, nor was he genealogized by
Moses, but is a Canaanite by race and sprung from the accursed seed. Hence he
was not even thought worthy of a genealogy; for it was not at all fitting that
one who had laid hold of the height of righteousness be woven together with
the race of the height of injustice. And Jerusalem was named from the addition
of the syllable of the temple with Salem by conjunction, bearing the name
Jerusalem, in one way because of the priestly service of Melchizedek, in
another because of the construction of the holy temple; for it was formerly
called Jebus. Lot, nephew of Abraham, from his two daughters begot two sons,
Moab and Ammon, from whom Moabites and Ammonites. 9 And Abraham from


his Egyptian maidservant Hagar begot Ishmael, and from Sarah Isaac. And Isaac
begot Esau and Jacob from Rebecca. And from Esau was born Edom, a son of the
same name as the father, and Amalek, from whom Idumaeans and Amalekites, and
from Jacob who is also IsraJudah, from whom also all the Jews and those from
the rest were named, and Israelites from the father Jacob, and Jews from their
more notable brother. And from Ishmael rulers and nations of them six,
Ishmaelites, those now called Hagarenes and Saracens. And the divine Abraham
begot from his last wife Keturah five sons, of whom is Midian, from whom
Midianites, and Apher, from whom Africa. And Isaac died having lived in all
180 years. And Jacob having lived 154 years died in Egypt, having spent 17
years there. Who when he was 75 years old, fleeing Esau his brother, came to
Haran of Mesopotamia to Laban the brother of his mother Rebecca, whose
shepherd of sheep he became for 20 years and taking his two daughters Leah and
Rachel as wives he had sons and daughters, one from both the free women and
from the maidservants Bilhah and Zilpah. And after the 20th year of the return
from Mesopotamia to the land of Canaan he saw a vision at Bethel, and having
tithed all that he possessed he puts the children last, and beginning from
Benjamin backwards and coming to Levi 10 he offered this one to God. So Isaac
and Rebecca after Jacob's return, having summoned both Jacob and Esau, and
having exhorted them and also put them under oath to keep peace with one
another and to embrace brotherly kinship, die, Rebecca first, then also Isaac,
leaving to Jacob the prizes of the birthright. And after this Esau, provoked
by Amalek his son against Jacob and gathering nations, rushed against him, as
Josephus says. And he, having shut the gates of the city into which he had
fled, from the wall exhorted Esau to remember the parental commandments. And
when he would not bear it, but rather even insulted and threatened to destroy
him utterly, Jacob drawing the bow and striking him in the right breast
brought Esau down. And thus was fulfilled Isaac's prediction saying to Esau,
"If you take down the yoke of your brother from your neck, you will commit a
transgression of death." And Levi begot Kohath, and Kohath begot Abram, and
Abram begot Aaron and Moses. And in the 80th year of Moses the sons of Israel
went out from Egypt, carrying also the bones of Joseph. And they spent 40
years in the wilderness. After Moses, Joshua son of Nun succeeded to the rule,
and having spent 32 years in the land of promise he died, having lived 110
years. 11 After Joshua God saved the people through the judges, and first
Judah judged the people 7 years, having also overpowered Adonibezek king of
the foreigners, and having cut off his hands and feet; who also said, "The
kings, the extremities of their hands and feet cut off, were gathering what
was under my table. As I did, so I also received from God." After Judah,
Othniel became judge 50 years, after Othniel Ehud 80 years, after Ehud Shamgar
7 years, after Shamgar Barak, with whom also Deborah the prophetess, and Jael
killed Sisera with a peg, the wife of Heber the Kenite. In whose time
Prometheus and Orpheus the Thracian, the wisest among the Greeks, and
Asclepius the physician and Lycurgus the Spartan and lawgiver of the Greeks
were known. After Barak, Gideon 40 years, who struck Midian with the 300 who
shone in the drinking, and Oreb and Zeeb and Zebah and Zalmunna their rulers.
After Gideon, Abimelech his son 3 years, having also struck his brothers, 70
men, upon one stone, wherefore he later also perished by a woman from a tower
through a throw of a stone that crushed his skull. Who also said to the one
carrying his 12 armor,


"Draw your sword and kill me, lest they ever say that a woman killed him." And
so the boy slew him. After Abimelech, Tola judged 23 years, after Tola
Jephthah years ʹ, after Jephthah Ibzan 7 years, after Ibzan Elon 10 years,
after Elon Samson 20 years, in whose time also Heracles the famous among the
Greeks was known. After Samson there was anarchy 40 years. After the judges,
Eli the high priest judged the people 20 years, and by the drunkenness and
disorder of his sons Hophni and Phinehas at the sacrifices the divine justice
came upon them; for they were terribly slain in the war, and he falling
backward from the seat and having his back broken died, years ʹ. After Eli,
Samuel the prophet and priest 30 years. And the people gather to him at Ramah
asking for a king. And to him being greatly grieved God said, "Hearken to the
voice of the people, for they have not rejected you but me, that I should not
reign over them." And he gave them Saul of the tribe of Benjamin, having
anointed him as king. After Samuel then Saul reigned 40 years, who having
dared first to burn incense to God, then having transgressed the commandment
of God concerning the Amalekites, having kept Agag their king alive, 13 heard
from the prophet, "Because it has been made vain for you, because you did not
keep my commandment which the Lord commanded you," and what follows. After
Saul, David of the tribe of Judah reigned 40 years, who also did all the will
of the Lord. And having struck Melcho king of the Syrians, and having taken
the great golden crown upon his head, having precious stones, he wore it, and
it was upon his head a most seemly ornament, which making clear he said,
"Lord, in your power the king shall rejoice," and the rest of the psalm. So
having become old and full of days, and having made his son Solomon king, he
said to him, "And now, Solomon, know the God of your fathers, and serve him
with a perfect heart and a willing soul, for the Lord searches all hearts and
knows every thought. And if you seek him, he will be found by you, and if you
forsake him, he will forsake you to the end." And he gave him the pattern of
the whole temple in writing, saying, "Be strong and of good courage, for the
Lord my God is with you." And after this he died in a good old age, in wealth
and glory and piety, 70 years old. And David was 30 years old when he began to
reign, and he also reigned 40 years. But of the royal anointing he was first
thought worthy through Samuel; for the history named the proclamations of the
people various anointings. So having died he is buried in the city Jerusalem
in the greatest tomb, which his most wise son Solomon prepared, in which he
also buried with his father many myriads of talents of gold, as Josephus says.
14 And this is clear from what happened later; for the high priest Hyrcanus,
when the city was being besieged by Antiochus son of Demetrius, having opened
the tomb carried out from there 3 talents. And the talent has 125 nomismata.
After whom Herod, learning that Hyrcanus had done this, himself also opened
the tomb, and money he did not find, but he took away adornment and very many
golden treasures. And when he tried to go further in, where the bodies of
David and Solomon were laid, fire came out and destroyed two of his
bodyguards. After David then Solomon his son reigned 40 years, who having
received the all-wondrous and God-given and superhuman wisdom built both the
city and the temple of the Lord in seven years, length 60 cubits, breadth 20
cubits and height 120 cubits; for it was of two stories, as Josephus says. And
Solomon's daily provision was 30 cors of fine flour, 60 cors of beaten meal,
10 choice calves, 20 pasture-fed oxen and 100 sheep, besides deer and gazelles
and choice and fattened birds. And the cor made 30 modii. Together becoming
2700 modii. And Solomon died 73 years old, and was buried with his father. In
whose time were Homer and Hesiod. 15 After Solomon, Rehoboam his son reigned
17 years, who did what is evil


before the Lord as his father, and died in his sins. In whose time Shishak
king of Egypt, having taken Jerusalem, took away the money in the temple and
the palaces and the golden spears and the golden shields, in place of which
bronze ones were made. After Rehoboam, Abijah his son reigned, and he did what
is evil before the Lord, and died having reigned years ʹ. After Abijah, Asa
his son reigned, and he did what is evil before the Lord, and died having
reigned years 10. After Asa, Jehoshaphat his son reigned, and he did what is
right before the Lord, and died having reigned 25 years. After Jehoshaphat,
Jehoram his son reigned, and he did what is evil before the Lord, and died of
dysentery, having reigned 8 years. After Jehoram, Ahaziah his son reigned 1
year, and he did what is evil, and was slain by Jehu. After Ahaziah, Athaliah
his mother reigned years ʹ, and she killed all the sons of Ahaziah except one
infant saved miraculously by the wife of Jehoiada the priest; who was also
suddenly proclaimed king by Jehoiada the priest, being 7 years old. And
Athaliah was killed in the house of the king. 16 After Athaliah then Joash son
of Ahaziah, having reigned from the age of seven 40 years, did what is right
before the Lord, then having turned aside was slaughtered in his house. Who
also killed Zechariah the prophet, the son of his teacher. In whose time the
Sibyl was known. After Joash, Amaziah his son reigned 29 years, and he did
what is right before the Lord, but was slaughtered in the war. After Amaziah,
Uzziah, who is also Azariah, his son reigned 52 years, and he did what is
right before the Lord, and prospering he was lifted up, and having wished to
burn incense to God of himself and having burned incense he was at once made
leprous, and so he died. After Uzziah, Jotham his son reigned years 10, and he
did what is right before the Lord, and died. After Jotham, Ahaz his son
reigned years 10, and having done what is evil before the Lord he died. After
Ahaz, Hezekiah his son reigned 29 years, and he did what is right before the
Lord, and died. In whose time the Mosaic bronze serpent was still being
worshipped by the Jews, which Hezekiah destroyed, as also the books of
Solomon. In which times Romus and Remus were reigning in Rome, and the
Erythraean Sibyl was known. In whose time also Sennacherib, having gone up
against Jerusalem 17, having lost 185 thousands in one night through a divine
angel, returned with shame. After Hezekiah, Manasseh his son reigned 55 years,
who also sawed the great Isaiah and filled Jerusalem with innocent blood and
did what is evil before the Lord. But later after his capture and captivity,
having corrected himself by repentance, he died. After Manasseh, Amon his son
reigned 2 years, and he did what is evil before the Lord, and was slaughtered
in his house. After Amon, Josiah his son reigned 31 years, being 8 years old,
and he did what is right before the Lord, and died having been shot in the
war. In which times also Byzantium was founded by Byzas king of Thrace. After
Josiah, Jehoahaz his son reigned 3 months, and he did what is evil before the
Lord, and being taken by Necho king of Egypt was led away to Egypt, in which
he also died. After Jehoahaz, Jehoiakim, who is also Eliakim, his brother,
reigned 11 years, and he did what is evil before the Lord. And Nebuchadnezzar
coming made all Judaea tributary. And when Jehoiakim set aside the tribute,
Nebuchadnezzar came again to Jerusalem, and having overcome him and slain him
he ordered him to be thrown from the wall, and left him unburied for a long
time. 18 After Jehoiakim, Jeconiah his son, who is also Jehoiakim, reigned
three months by command of Nebuchadnezzar, and he did what is evil before the
Lord. And when he was dishonest about the tributes, Nebuchadnezzar came again
to Jerusalem, and having set up Zedekiah in his place


he deported Jeconiah and his mother and his rulers to Babylon. And he brought
out the treasures of the Lord and of the king and all the golden vessels which
Solomon made in the temple of the Lord. After Jeconiah, Zedekiah his uncle
reigned 3 years, and he did what is evil before the Lord, and when he likewise
broke faith about the tribute, Nebuchadnezzar came again, and having taken the
whole city he burned it, and having overcome Zedekiah he slew his wife and
children before his eyes, and having blinded him and shut him in bonds he led
him captive to Babylon. After the division of the ten sceptres, Jeroboam son
of Nebat of the tribe of Ephraim, the servant of Solomon and apostate, reigned
42 years, and he did what is evil before the Lord; wherefore God also struck
him, and he died in his sins. After Jeroboam, Nadab his son reigned 2 years,
and he did what is evil before the Lord, and was slaughtered by Baasha. After
Nadab, Baasha reigned 24 years, and he did what is evil, and was slaughtered
in war. 19 After Baasha, Elah his son reigned 2 years, and he did what is
evil, and was slaughtered by Zimri. After Elah, Zimri reigned 12 years, and he
did what is evil, and was burned by the enemies, having fled to a house. After
Zimri, Ahab his son reigned 22 years, and he did what is evil, and was
slaughtered, and died in his sins. After Ahab, Ahaziah his son reigned 2
years, and he did what is evil, and falling from the upper room of his house
he died. After Ahaziah, Jehoram his brother reigned 2 years, and he did what
is evil, and was slaughtered by Jehu. In whose time the great Elijah was taken
up, and Elisha healed the water of Jericho, being bitter by nature and hard to
drink. After Jehoram, Jehu son of Nimshi reigned 28 years, and entering the
city of Samaria he saw Jezebel leaning out through the window, adorned, and he
ordered her eunuchs to throw her down, who was trampled by the horses and
eaten by the dogs. And he also struck the sons of Ahab, and having gathered
with cunning all the priests and prophets of Baal he ordered them all to be
slain, and he burned Baal and all that was his. Yet he did not keep to the end
walking in the law of the Lord, and he was slaughtered in the war. 20 After
Jehu, Jehoahaz his son reigned 27 years, and he did what is evil before the
Lord, and died. After Jehoahaz, Joash his son reigned 14 years, and he did
what is evil, and died. In whose time Elisha died, having prophesied his
former victory against the Syrians through symbols. And when the prophet was
being buried in the tomb, a certain dead man, having drawn near in the same
grave to his blessed bones, rose. After Joash, Jeroboam his son reigned 34
years, and he did what is evil, and died. After Jeroboam, Azariah his son
reigned months ʹ, and he did what is evil, and was slaughtered by Shallum.
After Azariah, Shallum reigned 1 month, and he did what is evil, and was
slaughtered by Menahem. After Shallum, Menahem reigned 3 years, and he did
what is evil, and was slaughtered by Pekahiah. After Menahem, Pekahiah his son
reigned 2 years, and he did what is evil, and was slaughtered by Pekah. After
Pekahiah, Pekah reigned 20 years, and he did what is evil, and was slaughtered
by Hoshea. After Pekah, Hoshea reigned 9 years, and doing what is evil he
died. After the said kings of the Assyrians there reigned, 21 as has been
said, Nebuchadnezzar 27 years. And because he was lifted up, by divine wrath
being driven from among men and dwelling with beasts and eating grass, later
having recognized the God of all he returned again to his own kingdom and
became more splendid. After Nebuchadnezzar, Evil-merodach his son reigned 5
years. After Evil-merodach, Belshazzar his brother reigned 3 years, who having
defiled the holy vessels of God at his banquet, which


his father Nebuchadnezzar had brought from Jerusalem, was slain by divine
wrath, and Darius took the kingdom. After Belshazzar, Darius the Mede, who is
also Astyages and Artaxerxes, reigned 17 years. In whose time also the visions
through the angel were shown to Daniel, and the time of Christ's coming was
made known; who being envied by the grandees because he was greatly honored by
the king was thrown into the den of lions. After Darius, Cyrus the Persian
reigned 30 years, who having slain Darius held all the land of the Chaldaeans
and Assyrians and Persians and Palestine and all Asia, with which he subjected
the kingdom of the Lydians, having slain Croesus, as has been said before. In
whose time Daniel, being still more distinguished and having shattered Bel and
killed the dragon, the objects of Persian worship, was thrown into the den of
lions and was there six days. 22 After Cyrus, Cambyses his son, who was also
surnamed Nebuchadnezzar, reigned 18 years. In whose time also Judith, by the
help of God and her own wisdom, having deceived and beheaded Holofernes, who
was sacking the city Bethulia, procured a great victory for the sons of
Israel, and she died a widow 105 years old. After Cambyses, Smerdis the magus
reigned 7 months. After Smerdis, Darius son of Hystaspes reigned 20 years. In
whose time again Zerubbabel and Jesus, having been commissioned, completed the
building of the temple, when Zerubbabel having won in a riddle, and having
been commissioned to ask what he wished, asked that the captivity be released
and Jerusalem be built; which also happened, and then the years of the
captivity and of the wrath, 70, were fulfilled to the end. And the high priest
and scribe Ezra was teaching the law of God and interpreting as the most
gifted of all, who also restored the sacred books that had been destroyed by
the enemies; for the Chaldaeans after the capture of Jerusalem burned them
all. Wherefore God also inspired this man so as to set them forth. After
Darius, Artabanus reigned 7 months. After Artabanus, Artaxerxes Longimanus
reigned 41 years. In whose time Sophocles, Heraclitus and Anaxagoras and
Pythagoras and Thucydides and Euripides and Herodotus and 23 Empedocles and
Diogenes and Zeno, Pherecydes and Aristarchus and Hippocrates, Parmenides,
Plato and Aristotle and Demosthenes and Socrates were known. After Artaxerxes,
another Artaxerxes reigned and those in succession until Darius, whom
Alexander slew, whose kingdom of the Persians having been overthrown and who
having held all the earth and having died in Babylon, his children divided the
kingdom, as has been said before, thus. Over Egypt Ptolemy son of Lagus
reigned and the 13 Ptolemies in succession until Cleopatra daughter of
Dionysius, and over all Asia Antigonus, and over Syria and Babylonia and
Palestine Seleucus Nicator, who also having founded two cities in the parts of
Cilicia and another in Syria called the one Seleucia and the other Antioch and
Laodicea after his own name and his son's and his daughter Laodice's. In whose
time the Rhodians, having become masters of the sea, set up in the city, that
is on the island there, a bronze statue of the sun, which because of its size
they called Colossus, and they themselves were named Colossians. After
Seleucus, Antiochus his son reigned and 5 others. Then Antiochus Epiphanes
reigned, son of Seleucus Philopator, in whose time the second capture of
Jerusalem happened and the things against the Maccabees were done and the
temple was defiled according to Daniel's prediction. Who also for the many
evils he did ended his life by a most shameful death in a foreign land. 24
After Antiochus, his son Eupator reigned. After these things Julius Caesar
reigned, who was not born; for when his mother died in the ninth month, they
cut her open and brought him out. Hence he is also called Caesar; from whom
the kings of the Romans were named Caesars, which is cutting-open in the
language of the Romans. Who first and alone held the Roman sceptres with


much arrogance, hence he was also called dictator, which is monarch. And
having held all with rashness and tyranny for 18 years he was slain in the
senate. Who gave laws to the Romans, and invented the bissextile, and renamed
the month July, formerly called Quintilis. In whose time was also Judas the
Galilean who is mentioned in the Acts of the Apostles, and Antipater the
father of Herod was set as king of Judaea. After Julius Caesar, Augustus
Caesar son of Octavius reigned, who is also Augustus, years 50; from whom the
kings of the Romans were named Sebastoi and Augusti, and the month was called
August, formerly named Sextilis. In the 42nd year of his reign our Lord Jesus
Christ was born. And in the 41st year of his reign, in year 5500 from the
creation of the world, Herod also from the nations was already proclaimed king
of Judaea. And when our Lord Jesus Christ was born according to the flesh and
Herod had ended his life badly, there was fulfilled "A ruler shall not fail
from Judah, until he come for whom it is reserved," 25 who is the Christ. And
Herod had three sons of the same name and of two names, Herod Antipas who also
beheaded the Forerunner, in whose time also the Lord was crucified, Herod
Philip, to whom first Herodias was joined and bore a daughter Salome who
danced, and Herod Antipater who also slew James of Zebedee, who while speaking
publicly, when the people cried "the voice of a god and not of a man," paid
the penalty at once; for an angel of the Lord immediately struck him suddenly,
and he leapt up from the seat as his belly was terribly torn. And the accuser
of the divine James, seeing him being led on the road to death and having
repented, fell at the apostle's feet saying, "Forgive me, man of God, for I
have repented of what I spoke against you." And the blessed one having kissed
him said to him, "Peace to you, child, peace to you, and forgiveness of the
fault." And he at once with a loud voice proclaimed himself a Christian before
all, and so being numbered with the one he had accused he also carried off
with him the prize of martyrdom. And wretched Herod, being eaten and vomiting
worms for days, ended his life pitiably, as also his more impious father.
After Augustus, Tiberius his son reigned 23 years, who having founded a city
in Judaea called it Tiberias after his own name. In the 15th year of whose
reign our Lord, having been baptized, began the gospel preaching. And in the
18th 26 having been crucified he rose on the 25th of the month Phamenoth, that
is March, the first-created month called Nisan among the Hebrews, as Sunday
was dawning. And why is the Lord's resurrection named the eighth? Because we
find a resurrection of the dead seven times in the divine scripture and after
the 7 the Lord rose from the dead as eighth. For before Christ's resurrection
in the old, first was that of the widow's son, whom Elijah raised at
