* On the Nativity of Christ

Of the same holy John, archbishop of Constantinople, Chrysostom, On the
Nativity of Christ.
This present day is full of fear and shuddering, and of much astonishment
and boundless joy. For the Master comes forth from a virgin bride, having
found the maiden a paradoxical bridal chamber. For he who above [...] was
begotten without corruption, this one below [...] is born without seed. And
he who above lodges in the bosom [of the] Father, this one is carried [in]
the arms of a virgin mother. If anyone 20then20 will consider the present
day, [...] he is contracted with trembling; for it is full of fear and
trembling, as has already been said. Today [the] refutation of the unbelief
of the Jews and of all [...] the firstfruits and advance. Those girded with
leaves and wearing skins, these, in his being worn, wear Christ.
Today then is a day of joy and of tumult; [for there is] war of the demons;
[and] uproar of the devil; [And] destruction of the idols; death of death,
and from this the beginning of freedom for the whole world. But you, look
at the holy virgin for me (fol. 49ῃ) [...], and consider the matter, how it
is boundless. For how is she not torn apart bearing the Lord; how [for]
even her womb is not wholly dissolved, But she bears in the womb the
Creator [...] [...] of the womb, as he himself knows. And if Egypt did not
endure [to bear] the finger of God, how did a virgin bear the right hand of
God? [And] if a man did not bear a small weight of gold, how did the maiden
bear the treasure of the Father? And if it were possible also to come into
converse with Christ and say: "When you were in the maiden's womb, Did you
not have immeasurable nature and invincible strength? did you not say that
heaven is your throne? And being thus altogether uncontainable to all, you
have the virgin's belly as a dwelling. And how is he who is in [the] bosom
of your undefiled Father in the womb of a maiden who has not known
marriage?" "[Yes, he says], Adam was corrupted; the world was flooded;
Sodom was burned; the law was trampled; Prophets were insulted; I [I] God
was not heard; the dragon was master; the demons held sway; The world was
perishing; an angel had no strength to be incarnate and save [...].
Therefore [...]; I take up the form of the slave [...] [...]."
O the wonder [...]; I am astonished and I shudder, my mind being tossed by
so many wonders. That alone I find myself able to say: O 20the depth of
the riches and of the wisdom and of the knowledge20 of God." For this too
is extraordinary and exceedingly paradoxical: the maiden is found both
mother and virgin. She is not sown and she begets; she is not ploughed and
she brings forth; she does not wait for passion; she does not receive
corruption. Not the corruptible [...] [...] receiving she is loosened in
her members, Because after bearing the Lord without seed so much as of the
Lord's unchambered child ... Truly 20how were your works magnified, O
Lord; all things in wisdom20 [...] [...] 20you made20". Consider [then] for
me [also] Adam dancing as he is clothed and much reproaching the beast
[...] [And] saying something in joy: "All-wicked one, what did you gain by
despoiling me? What did you traffic by despoiling me?"
......... See: he who formerly wore fig-leaves about the loins and was
clothed in <the> tunics of a dead man, See now the life-giver-bearer being
worn; whence, he says, has such boldness been given? Whence, "20The Lord
said to me: You are my Son?20 [...] For that you may reckon the nativity to
the [holy] Virgin and think her a true mother of the flesh, I now signify
my Lord [...]; for he himself is also of [blessed] Mary
the Creator. [To him the glory and the power now and always and unto the
ages of ages; amen].
