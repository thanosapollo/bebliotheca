OF GREGORY BISHOP OF NYSSA ON THE GOAL ACCORDING TO GOD AND
ON TRUE ASCETICISM

If someone, having withdrawn the mind a little from the body and having come
outside both the slavery of the passions and folly, should behold his own soul
with undeceitful and sincere reasoning, he will see clearly in its nature both
God's love toward us and the will of that one's creation. For examining in
this way he will find both the impulse of desire toward the fair and best
coessential and grown together with the human being, and, of that intelligible
and blessed image of which the human being is an imitation, the passionless
and blessed love joined to the nature. But a certain error of these visible
and ever-flowing things, through irrational passion and bitter pleasure,
deceiving and bewitching the careless and unguarded soul through slackness,
8,=1.41= draws it toward a terrible vice, which is both born from the
pleasures of life and begets death for its own lovers. For this reason the
grace of our Savior granted the knowledge of the truth, the saving medicine
for souls, to those who receive it with longing; by which the error that
bewitches the human being is loosed, and the dishonorable mind of the flesh is
quenched, the soul that has received the knowledge being led by the light of
the truth toward the divine and toward its own salvation.
Since then you, receiving this knowledge worthily and directing the divine
love according to the nature given to the soul, have come together eagerly,
jointly fulfilling the apostolic character in deeds and longing to receive
from us some guide and leader of the journey of life, a word that will lead
onto the straight way, showing accurately what the goal of this life is for
those who pursue it, and what the good and well-pleasing and perfect will of
God is, and of what sort the road toward this goal is, and how those traveling
it ought to be with one another, and how those who preside must direct the
chorus of philosophy, and with what labors those who are going to ascend to
the summit of virtue must be employed and prepare their own souls as worthy
for the reception of the Spirit; since then you demand this word from us, not
8,=1.42= simply from the mouth but shaped in letters, so that having it stored
in these as from a treasury of memory you may † receive this for the needs
according to the season, we shall attempt according to the grace of the Spirit
that supplies us to speak upon the present eagerness, knowing accurately that
the canon of piety in you has been fixed by the right dogma of the faith,
having one Godhead of the blessed and eternal Trinity nowhere in no way
altered but conceived and worshiped in one essence, one glory, and the same
will according to the three hypostases, as we previously set down the
confession under many witnesses to the Spirit who washed us in the fountain of
the mystery; knowing this pious and unerring confession of the faith to be
securely established in you in the depth of the soul, and your impulse and
ascent upward toward the good and blessed through the course of deeds, we
write you the brief seeds of the teaching, selecting from the writings †
granted beforehand to us by the Spirit 8,=1.43=, and also placing in many
places the very words of Scripture according to need for the faith of the
things said and for the manifestation of our judgment concerning it, so that
we may not seem, having left the grace from above, ourselves to beget bastard
things by some base and lowly mind and, fashioning types of piety by outside
reasonings, to introduce these ignorantly into the Scriptures, puffed up with


vain mind. It is necessary then that the one who is going to bring soul and
body to God according to the law of piety and to render to him the bloodless
and pure worship, making the pious faith leader [and] of the life, which the
voices of the saints cry out to us through all Scripture, thus yield the soul
obedient and well-reined to the courses of virtue, loosing himself purely from
the shackles of this life and being freed from all slavery of the lowly and
vain things, and becoming wholly of God alone in faith and in life, knowing
clearly that where there is faith of piety and a blameless life, there the
power of Christ is also present, and where the power of Christ is present,
there is flight of every vice and of the death that plunders our life; for the
wicked things do not have in themselves so great a power as to stand against
the Master's power, but they are accustomed to supervene upon disobedience of
the commandments 8,=1.44=; which the first-formed one suffered of old, and now
all who imitate this one's disobedience by self-chosen purpose; but those who
approach the Spirit with undeceitful judgment and have faith in all full
assurance, no stain being present in the conscience, this power of the Spirit
cleanses according to the one who said that our gospel toward you did not come
to be in word only, but also in power and in Holy Spirit and in much full
assurance, as you know, and again May your spirit and soul and body be kept
whole blamelessly in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, who through the bath
granted to the worthy the pledge of immortality, so that the talent entrusted
to each may by the working of those entrusted bring the invisible wealth. For
great, brothers, great toward the acquisition of the intelligible things is
holy baptism for those who receive it with fear; for the rich and ungrudging
Spirit that always flows for those who receive the grace, of which the holy
apostles, having been filled, displayed to the churches of Christ the fruits
of the fullness, this remains as fellow-worker and housemate for those who
have received the gift sincerely according to the measure of the faith of each
of those who have partaken, building the good in each toward the soul's zeal
in the works of faith according to the Lord's voice that says that the one who
received that mina† upon working of what was given, that is, for the progress
and increase of the receiver, to each is given the grace of the Holy Spirit;
for the soul regenerated by the power of God must be nourished 8,=1.45= until
the measure of the intelligible stature in the Spirit, being watered
continually with both the sweat of virtue and the supply of grace; for just as
the nature of the body, when the infant has just been born, does not remain in
the tender stage of age, but being watered by the nourishments of the body
according to the law of nature presses on toward the measure given, so it is
fitting that the newly-born soul also, whose participation of the Spirit,
destroying the disease that entered through disobedience, renews the ancient
beauty for the nature, not remain always an infant nor be idle and at leisure,
unmoved, sleeping in the standing of the birth, but water itself with its own
nourishments and rear it through all virtue and labors toward the greatness
that nature demands, so that it may make itself impossible for the invisible
plunderer who casts many unseen devices against the souls, by the power of the
Spirit through its own virtue.
It is necessary then always to advance oneself into a perfect man according to
the apostle Until we all arrive at the unity of the faith and of the knowledge
of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the
fullness of Christ, that we may no longer be infants, tossed and carried about
by every wind of teaching in craftiness toward the method of error, but
speaking truth we may grow into him in all things, who is the head, Christ;
and 8,=1.46= elsewhere the same Do not be conformed to this age, but be
transformed by the renewal of your mind, that you may prove what the will of
God is, the good and


well-pleasing and perfect, calling a perfect will of God that a soul be formed
by piety, which the grace of the Spirit brings to flower into extreme beauty,
joining with the labors of the one being formed. The advance of the body
toward growing up, then, is nothing in our power; for nature does not measure
the greatness by the human being's judgment and pleasure, but by its own
impulse and necessity; but the measure and beauty of the soul in the renewal
of the birth, which the grace of the Spirit furnishes through the zeal of the
one who receives, hangs upon our judgment; for as far as you stretch the
contests on behalf of piety, so far also the greatness of the soul is
stretched together, both through contests and through labors, on behalf of
which our Lord also commands saying Struggle to enter through the narrow door;
and again Use force; for the forceful seize the kingdom of the heavens; and He
who has endured to the end, this one will be saved; and In your endurance gain
your souls; and the apostle also Through endurance we run the contest set
before us; and Thus, he says, run that you may seize; and again As ministers
of God in much endurance and the rest. For this reason he exhorts to run and
commands to hold the contests intensely, since the gift of grace is measured
by the labors of the one who receives; for eternal life and 8,=1.47= the
unspeakable gladness in the heavens the grace of the Spirit grants, but the
worthiness to receive the gifts and to enjoy the grace the love concerning the
labors through faith furnishes. And when a work of righteousness and grace of
Spirit have come together into the same, they have filled with one another the
soul into which they came together with blessed life; but having been divorced
from one another they furnish no gain to the soul; for neither does the grace
of God have a nature to visit souls that flee salvation, nor does the power of
human virtue suffice by itself to raise up to the form of life souls that have
no share of grace.
For if, he says, the Lord does not build a house and guard a city, the one who
guards has kept watch in vain and the one who builds toils; and again For not
by their sword did they inherit land, and their arm did not save them,
although they used swords and arms toward the contests, but your right hand
and your arm and the illumination of your face. Saying what is this? The
alliance from above for the workers of the Lord, and at once that those who
think the whole crown lies in their own labors must not think highly of human
zeals, but must carry up the hopes concerning the end upon the will of God. It
is necessary then to know what the will of God is, looking toward which <it is
necessary> that the one who longs for the blessed life be zealous and who
desires to dispose his own life toward that. 8,=1.48= The perfect will of God,
then, is to cleanse the soul through grace of every defilement, having made it
higher than the pleasures of the body, and to bring it pure to God, longing
and able to see that intelligible and unutterable light. Such ones the Lord
also blesses saying Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God; and
in other places he commands Therefore be you perfect as your heavenly Father
is perfect. Toward this perfection the apostle also exhorts to run saying That
I may present every human being perfect in Christ, unto which I also toil
struggling; and David speaking in the Spirit teaches those who wish to
philosophize rightly the road of true philosophy, through which one must walk
toward the perfect goal, asking from the one who gives the things that the
Spirit teaches through David Let, he says, my heart become blameless in your
justifications, that I may not be put to shame. He commands to fear the shame
and to strip off, as a filthy and dishonorable garment, those who have put
that on from vice; for again he says Then I shall not be put to shame when I
look upon all your commandments. You see how in the fulfillment of the
commandments the Spirit 8,=1.49= places the boldness; and again


Create a clean heart in me, O God, and renew a right spirit in my inward parts
and support me with a leading spirit; and elsewhere he asks Who shall go up
onto the mountain of the Lord? then he answers Innocent in hands and clean in
heart. This one who is clean through all things he leads up onto the mountain
of God, who neither in thought nor in knowledge nor in the deeds defiled the
soul to the end by remaining in the evils, and whoever through works and
<thoughts> good, having received the leading Spirit, created his own heart
again, which had been corrupted by vice. And the holy apostle, speaking about
virginity, sketches for those who have chosen to live with it of what sort the
life of such a one must be; The virgin, he says, is anxious about the things
of the Lord, that she may be holy in body and in spirit, saying to be clean in
soul and in flesh; and he commands to flee every sin as far as possible, both
the manifest and as much as is secret, that is <to abstain wholly from sins
both> practical and those accomplished in reasoning; for the goal for the soul
that honors virginity is to draw near to God and to become a bride of Christ.
8,=1.50= It is necessary then that the one who desires to be made intimate
with someone take that one's manner by imitation, with whom he is made
intimate; therefore it is necessary also that the one who longs to become a
bride of Christ be likened to the beauty of Christ through virtue according to
power; for it is not possible ever to be joined to light without having shone
toward that light; and I heard the apostle John saying For everyone, he says,
who has this hope purifies himself as that one is pure; and the apostle Paul
also Become imitators of me as I also of Christ; for the soul that is going to
fly up toward the divine and be glued to Christ must drive out every sin from
itself, as much as is accomplished openly in the deeds, I mean theft and
plunder and adultery and greed and fornication and disease of tongue and every
manifest kind of sins, and as much as sitting secretly in the souls and
escaping those outside eats the human being bitterly with the worst teeth; and
this is envy unbelief malice deceit, appetite for things that must not be,
hatred boastfulness vainglory and all the underhanded swarm of vice, which
equally with the manifest kind of sins Scripture hates and abominates, being
kindred to one another and growing from the same vice. And whose bones did the
Lord scatter? not of the men-pleas 8,=1.51= ers? Whom does the Lord abominate
as the accursed and bloodstained? not the underhanded and deceitful man? For a
man of bloods and a deceitful man the Lord abominates. Does not David openly
curse those who speak peace with their neighbors, but evils in their hearts,
crying to God Give them according to their works? and In heart, he says, you
work lawlessnesses in the earth. The movement of sin in the secret God called
a work; for this reason he commands neither to pursue the good-repute from
human beings nor to be ashamed of the dishonors from them.
For those who show mercy ostentatiously to the poor and who triumph the
sharing upon earth Scripture deprives of the rewards in the heavens; for if
you seek to please human beings and for this reason you give, that you may be
praised, your reward of well-doing has been fulfilled by the praises of human
beings, for whose sake you were displaying the mercy. Do not then seek a
reward in the heavens, having deposited the works below, nor expect honor from
God, for from human beings you have received this. Do you long for immortal
glory? Show the life in secret to the one able to furnish what you long for.
Do you fear eternal shame? Fear the one who uncovers this on the day of the
judgment. How then does the Lord say Let your light shine before human beings,
that they may see your good works and glorify 8,=1.52= your Father who is in
the heavens? Because he commands the one who follows the commandments of God
to do whatever he may do looking toward that one and to please this one only,
hunting no glory from human beings; but to flee the praises from these and the
display, and through the life and the works


to be known to all, that having become spectators of these, he did not say
"1they may marvel at the one displaying"2, but "1they may glorify your Father
who is in the heavens."2 For there he commands all glory to be carried up and
every deed to be completed toward that one's will, with whom also the reward
of the works of virtue is stored. But you he commands to flee and turn away
from the good-repute from tongues and from the earth; and the one who seeks
this and draws the life toward this is not only deprived of the eternal glory,
but let him already expect punishment also; For woe, he says, when all human
beings speak well of you. Flee then every human honor, whose end is shame and
eternal dishonor, and reach for the praises above, saying the word of David
From you is my praise; and In the Lord my soul shall be praised; and the
blessed apostle does not even command the one who eats to partake slackly of
the food set forth, but first to render the glory to the one who has given the
resources of living; thus unto every point it is charged to dishonor the
glories from human beings, and to seek the one from the only God. For the one
who is thus is named faithful by the Lord; but the one who longs for the honor
here 8,=1.53= he ranks among the unfaithful; For how can you, he says,
believe, receiving glory from one another, and you do not seek the glory from
the only God? And of what sort hatred is, hear John saying The one who hates
his brother is a human-killer, and you know that every human-killer does not
have eternal life. He casts out of life the one who hates the brother as the
man-slayer, or rather he names hatred murder outright; for the one who has
destroyed and corrupted the love toward the neighbor and has become an enemy
instead of a friend might reasonably † be numbered together, keeping hidden
toward the neighbor that enmity of those against the ones plotted against; and
that there is no difference of the evils hidden within toward the manifest and
seen, the apostle clearly shows through these, gathering them and enumerating
them as of equal honor; As they did not approve, he says, to have God in
knowledge, he gave them over to an unapproved mind, to do the things not
fitting, filled with all injustice fornication wickedness greed vice; full of
envy murder strife deceit malice; whisperers slanderers God-haters insolent
arrogant boastful inventors of evils, disobedient to parents, without
understanding covenant-breakers without affection implacable unmerciful, who
having known the justification of God, that those who practice such things are
worthy of death 8,=1.54=, <not only do them but also consent with those who
practice them>.
You see how he wove together malice and arrogance and deceit and the rest of
the hidden things with murder and greed and all such things? And what does the
Lord himself cry saying What is high among human beings is an abomination with
God, and The one who exalts himself will be humbled and the one who humbles
himself will be exalted; and Wisdom says Unclean before the Lord is every
high-hearted one; and one would find many things also in the other Scriptures
toward accusation of the passions hidden in the souls. And so wicked are these
and hard to heal and possessing the strength in the depth of the soul, that it
is not possible by human zeal and virtue alone to wipe them out and destroy
them, unless one, having taken the power of the Spirit as ally through prayer,
should prevail over the vice tyrannizing within, as the Spirit itself teaches
using the voice of David From my hidden things cleanse me and from aliens
spare your servant. For there being two [human beings], from which the one
human being has been fitted, both soul and body, and the one containing from
outside 8,=1.55=, and the other remaining within through the life, one must
keep watch over the one as a temple of God, guarding lest some of the manifest
sins falling upon it shake it down and destroy it; for concerning this the
apostle also threatens saying The one who destroys the temple of God, God will
destroy this one; but the one within one must guard through all guarding, lest
some ambush


of vice, having risen from somewhere from the depth and having destroyed the
reasoning of piety, may enslave the soul, having filled it with the passions
that drag it secretly. It is necessary then to guard securely, turning
frequently toward the soul as the general shouting and commanding: human
being, With all guarding keep your heart; for from these are the exits of
life; and a guard of soul is a pious reasoning fortified by fear of God and
grace of Spirit and works of virtue; for the one who has armed his own soul
with these easily turns the assaults of the tyrant, I mean deceit and desire
and conceit and anger and envy and as many wicked movements of vice as there
are within. And the farmer of virtue must be some simple and firm one, knowing
to farm only the fruits of piety, and neither ever to turn the life aside
toward the roads of vice nor 8,=1.56= to drag the reasoning of piety away from
the faith, but to be some single-mannered and straight one and inexperienced
in the passions that lie outside his own road; for it is not possible that the
one who lives with the only husband and who prostitutes the marriage expect
the same reward of the life.
You shall not yoke, says the blessed Moses, in your threshing-floor animals of
another kind upon the same, as an ox and an ass, but having yoked the
same-kind you shall thresh your harvest; nor shall you weave linen with a
woolen garment nor again wool with linen; you shall not farm in the country of
your land two fruits upon the same nor a second of the year; you shall not put
an animal of another kind upon another for generation, but you shall yoke the
same-kind with the same-kind. What do these riddles will for the holy one?
That one must not plant vice and virtue together with one another in the same
soul nor, dividing the life for the opposites, farm both thorns and wheat
together from the same soul, nor that the bride of Christ commit adultery with
the enemies of Christ and on the one hand conceive light, on the other bear
gloom; for these have not a nature to come together with one another, just as
neither the parts of virtue 8,=1.57= with the parts of vice. For what
friendship of temperance toward incontinence? And what concord of
righteousness toward injustice? And what communion of light toward darkness?
Does not the one always withdraw before the other and refuse to remain toward
what fights it? It is necessary then that the wise farmer send forth the
streams of the life pure as from a drinkable and good spring and unmixed with
any mire, knowing only the farmings of God and laboring at these through life
and remaining, so that even if some foreign reasoning of the fruits of virtue
should grow under in secret, the one who sees all things, having seen your
labors, may quickly by his own power cut out that deceitful and underhanded
root of the reasonings before the sprout. For to the one who perseveres in the
labors of virtue the grace of the Spirit follows quickly, destroying the seeds
of vice, and it is not possible to miss the hope nor for the one who always
sits beside God to be overlooked unavenged. Did you see the widow in the
gospel, who was bringing the greatness of the injustice not to a humane judge,
but the much leisure and endurance about the petition, having conquered the
manner of the judge, calls him forth toward punishment of the one who did
injustice. Do not then you either, praying, give up; for if that woman's
shamelessness about the petition bent the judgment of a pitiless ruler, how
must we despair of the zeal concerning God, whose mercy knows often to
anticipate those who ask? 8,=1.58= But the Lord himself also, accepting our
persistence about the prayers and urging to use the zeal See, he says, what
the unjust judge says; how much more will our heavenly Father do the vengeance
of those who cry to him night and day; yes I say to you he will do the
vengeance quickly. And the apostle also, having much zeal and contest that the
disciples of piety come to perfect progress, and at once making the goal of
the truth manifest to all, says Admonishing every human being and teaching in
all wisdom, that we may present every human being perfect in Christ, unto
which I also toil struggling. And again for those who of the seal of the
Spirit through baptism have been


†deemed worthy he prays that they receive increase of the intelligible stature
through the supply of the Spirit saying For this reason having heard the faith
according to you and the love which you have toward all the saints I do not
cease praying for you and asking, that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the
Father of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and of revelation in
knowledge of him, the eyes of your heart being enlightened, that you may know
what is the hope of his calling and what the wealth of the glory of his
inheritance in the saints and what the surpassing greatness of his power
toward us who believe; then he says concerning the manner of the partaking of
the Spirit According to the energy of his power, which he energized in Christ,
having raised 8,=1.59= him from the dead. He speaks purely concerning the
participation of the Spirit and that one's energy toward those who partake, so
that, he says, you also may receive in the same manner that one's full
assurance; then having descended a little of the letter he prays something
greater for them, seeking that a more perfect power of the Spirit come upon
them;
For this reason I bend my knees toward the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,
from whom every family in heaven and upon earth is named; that he may give you
according to the wealth of his glory to be strengthened in power through his
Spirit unto the inner human being, that Christ dwell through the faith in your
hearts, being rooted and founded in love; that you may be able to grasp with
all the saints what is the breadth and length and depth and height, and to
know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled unto
all the fullness of God. And already also in another letter he discourses to
the disciples about the same things, uncovering the treasure of the Spirit to
them and exhorting them to partake; For be zealous, he says, for the spiritual
gifts; and still I show you a way according to excess. If I speak with the
tongues of human beings and of angels, but do not have love, I have become
sounding bronze or a clashing cymbal. And if I have prophecy and know the
mysteries and all the knowledge, and if I have all the faith so as to move
mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing; and if I feed out all my
belongings and if I give over my body that I may be burned, but love 8,=1.60=
I do not have, I am not benefited. And what the gain of love is and of what
sort its sprouts are, from what things it withdraws the one who has it and
what things it furnishes, he shows clearly in these; Love does not envy, does
not vaunt, is not puffed up, does not behave unseemly, does not seek its own,
<is not provoked, does not reckon the evil,> does not rejoice upon the
injustice, but rejoices with the truth, covers all things, believes all
things, hopes all things, endures all things; love never falls. Very wisely
and accurately Never, he says, does love fall. And what is this? Even if one
should happen upon the other gifts which the Spirit furnishes (I mean tongues
of angels and prophecy and knowledge and gifts of healings), but should not
yet be purely redeemed from the passions that trouble within by the love of
the Spirit nor receive in the soul the perfect medicine of salvation, he still
stands in fear of the fall, not having the love that supports and confirms in
the standing of virtue. Do not then, having thought because of the rich and
ungrudging grace of the Spirit that you still need nothing toward the
perfecting, remain in that one's gifts; but when the wealth of that gift comes
toward 8,=1.61= you, then become poor in the mind, always crouching and
expecting love as some foundation of the treasure of grace for the soul and
struggling against every passion, until <you> arrive at the summit of the goal
of piety, at which the apostle himself both arrived and leads up the disciples
through prayer and teaching, showing to those who love the Lord the change
toward the better and the grace by love saying For neither is circumcision
anything nor uncircumcision, but a new creation; and as many as


walk by this canon, peace upon them and mercy and upon the Israel of God. And
again If anyone is in Christ, a new creation; the ancient things have passed.
The new creation is an apostolic canon; and this is what he himself in another
part clearly showed saying That he may present the church to himself glorious,
not having spot or wrinkle or any of such things, but that she may be holy and
blameless. He called a new creation the indwelling of the Holy Spirit in a
pure and blameless soul and one freed from all vice and wickedness and shame.
For when the soul hates the sinning, and makes itself intimate with God
according to power by the constitution of virtue and, having been transformed
in the life, receives the grace of the Spirit into itself, 8,=1.62= it has
become wholly new and has been created again. And Cleanse out the old leaven,
that you may be a new lump, shows this, and Let us keep the feast, not in old
leaven, but in unleavened things of sincerity and truth. For since the tempter
sets many snares for the soul, casting forth his own vice against it from
every side, and the human power by itself is lesser than the victory against
that one, the apostle commands us to arm the members with the heavenly
weapons, commanding to put on the breastplate of righteousness and to shoe the
feet in preparation of the peace and to gird the loin in truth, and upon all
having taken up the shield of faith, through which he says You will be able to
quench all the fiery darts of the wicked one; and fiery darts are the unchaste
passions; and he charges to receive the helmet of salvation and the holy sword
of the Spirit; and he calls a holy sword the word in the power of God, with
which one must, having armed the right hand of the soul, beat off the devices
of the enemy.
How then are the weapons to be taken by us, learn 8,=1.63= from the apostle
himself saying Through all prayer and petition praying in every season in
spirit and watching unto it in all perseverance and petition; whence he also
prays over all thus The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ and the love of God and
the communion of the Holy Spirit with all of you. And again May your spirit
and soul and body be kept whole blamelessly in the day of our Lord Jesus
Christ. You see how many manners of salvation he showed you, stretching toward
one road and one goal of being a perfect Christian? For this is the end at
which the lovers of the truth must arrive through strong faith and firm hope,
walking in pleasure with contest and eagerness. By these the course of the
life toward the summit of the commandments is also easily fulfilled, from
which
every prophet together with the law has been hung. What commandments do I
mean? You shall love the Lord your God from all your heart and from all your
soul and from all your mind and your neighbor as yourself. Such then is the
goal of piety, which both the Lord himself and the apostles who received the
knowing from that one 8,=1.64= handed down to us; and if demonstrating through
longer things we have led the word forward into length, caring more to present
the truth than to cut short the things said, let no one blame. For those who
