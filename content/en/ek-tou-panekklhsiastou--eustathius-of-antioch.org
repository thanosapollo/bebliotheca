From the Panecclesiastes (fragment)
Of Saint Eustathius, bishop of Antioch, from the Panecclesiastes:
They underwent a manifest portion of shame when, having armed their murderous
hands, they poured forth the undefiled blood. And immediately the veil of the
temple was divided and was cut in two because of the subjects of the
many-faced dramas. And they were frightened, struck in their own way by the
terrors and beholding the concourses of all kinds of thunderbolts. And they
were clearly captured when, having killed the divine Word, they nailed him to
a cross.
