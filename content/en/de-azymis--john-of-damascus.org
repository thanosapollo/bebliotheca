OF SAINT JOHN OF DAMASCUS ON THE UNLEAVENED
=95.388=
It must be known that those who offer the unleavened offer a dead flesh, and not
a living. For the leaven becomes instead of a soul to the dough, and the salt
instead of the mind; for the Lord, having taken from the Ever-Virgin an ensouled
and mind-bearing body, reasonably handed down the mystery of the New Covenant
through a perfect bread; having blessed it, and broken it, and said: "Take, eat;
this is my body." And Luke in his holy Gospel, "And having taken bread, he says,
having given thanks he broke." And behold he himself also said that Christ took
bread, and not unleavened. For it was not yet, it being then the fifth day; for
it was the thirteenth of the moon then, and there was not unleavened because it
had not yet come to be.
For on the 15th of the moon it had been legislated to eat these. And on the 14th
they ate the lamb and only; wherefore also the law names the fourteenth of the
same month of the moon Pascha; and the 15th itself it also names a Sabbath. If
then Christ was sacrificed according to the fourteenth of the moon, on a Friday;
and the Sabbath was a feast of the unleavened, when they ate the unleavened,
when did he hand these down to the apostles, that they say they received these
from the apostles? For Christ did not even eat the lawful Pascha according to
the time. For also after having supped, he handed down through the breaking of
the bread the mystery of the New Covenant according to the fifth day. And when
it was a feast of the Unleavened on the Sabbath according to the 15th day of the
month, the Lord lay in the tomb. Unleavened then not being before the feast of
the Unleavened in the whole year, where were the unleavened found then, when
Christ ate the supper with his disciples? Cease, you who fight against the
truth.
And the, "And it was Preparation of the Pascha," is thus understood. For they
were preparing, as also they were making ready the things of the feast of the
Pascha; and the, "They themselves did not go into the praetorium, that they
might not be defiled, but that they might eat the Pascha," how will you
understand, if on the fifth was the Pascha? how on the Friday did they not go
into the praetorium, but they were cautious being somehow pre-purified because
of the partaking of the Pascha?
