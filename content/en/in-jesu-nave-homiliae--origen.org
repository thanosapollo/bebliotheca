290 "Moses has died," the law has died, Moses being so named, according to:
"they have Moses and the prophets." 291 When therefore Moses was leading
through the sea, of salt water, there was no order such as we now see when
Jesus was general and the Lord was fulfilling the symbol. For the one about to
cross the Jordan there is need of provision ... For the way of God demands the
working of virtue. 293 But Moses did not say: "Let the sun stand," he did not
look up into heaven, he did not show a heavenly sign. But Jesus says: "Let the
sun stand." And Scripture testifies that "never did God so hearken to men." My
Jesus therefore also made the sun stand, and at his presence he has made the
day greater for me against the enemies, that being marshaled under Jesus I may
put the adversaries to flight. 298 The Father subjected "the wilderness" to
Christ. Concerning which it has been said: "rejoice, thirsty wilderness" and:
"many are the children of the desolate more than of her who has the husband."
And 299 it was not Lebanon that he subjected to Jesus, Israel, so called in
the prophets, who is also called "a cultivated olive tree"; but the nations,
"Antilebanon," "grafted" into Israel, and become in its place. 300 (these
received their lot through Moses, since many were perfected through the law
before grace. And since Israel is named firstborn), through Moses the
firstborn received the lots, Reuben and Gad and Manasseh. Yet they too cross
well-girt before the others. For those who have believed from the Jews led the
salvation of the nations. 301 And they themselves guide the people "having
girded their loin in truth." For even after life they contend together with
the faithful, as many as have become men "having put away the things of the
child." 302 And why not 3 and 9 tribes, but "two and a half tribes" are
allotted by Moses and "nine and a half" by Jesus? ... and perhaps, they say,
the former also touched the Trinity, yet not purely. For the [matter] of the
economy was not yet added, even if they proclaimed Christ beforehand. 305
being truly a type of the Church ****, who formerly was mixed with the
fornication of the demons according to: "for we too were once disobedient";
and she received the spies of Christ, the Apostles, "preparing for the Lord a
people made ready." "And she brought them up upon the housetop"; for no spy of
Jesus can be on the ground nor remain below. 308 And just as all creation
makes war on the sinner, as in the case of the Egyptians, so all things become
passable for the righteous who is going through the Red Sea and the
wilderness, the great [wilderness], being supplied the food from heaven; and
the air too is passable for him according to: "We shall be caught up in clouds
into the air." For in general he has heard not to fear: "for if you pass
through fire, a flame shall not burn you." 310 For of those who perish after
baptism the water of the Jordan "goes away into the sea of salts"; but the
other water is of the righteous. 312 And they cross the Jordan on the tenth of
the first month, in which also the Passover. 394 "And Jesus was older,
advanced in days." Not simply are the long-lived called "elders" by Scripture,
but those who have a mind worthy of the honor of the heavenly presbytery.
Therefore it called neither <Adam> nor Methuselah nor Noah "elder," whom
rather it ought, but the more short-lived Abraham it first called "elder." And
to Moses the Lord says: "choose for yourself from all the people elders, whom
you yourself know that they are elders." And if somewhere Scripture should say
this concerning a sinner, it says it with a qualification 395, as: "the Lord
himself will come into judgment with the elders and with the rulers of the
people." And likewise also "to have advanced in days" it says of saints. For
no one "turning back to the things behind" "has advanced," but if "someone
stretches forward to the things before, forgetting the things behind." 396
"And the land


has been left remaining very much." And yet it said before: "and the land
ceased from being warred upon" and that "Jesus took all the land." Understand
therefore a first holding of the earth by the Lord Jesus, when he sowed his
word into all the inhabited world. For by having held perhaps the demons in
each 397 nation and having driven these out he sowed the word into the
Churches. This is one holding of Jesus. See for me also upon that a second,
when he will hold all, many portions of the earth now being left remaining.
Wherefore to the former is added "being warred upon," and to the second "very
much." Yet blessed are all who have been held in the first coming; for it is
grace to them that they are subjected while many are still making war. For
those at the second are held by necessity, as also "the last enemy, death, is
to be abolished." And first "all the nations," as it seems to the prophets,
"from the ends as far as the ends 398 of the inhabited world shall be
subjected." And when many of the subjected nations have been named, of the
Sidonians alone it says that: "I will utterly destroy them from the face of
the sons of Israel," either taking this as common against all, or because
Sidonians are interpreted hunters. For every power that hunts souls of men "he
will utterly destroy." And then, when there is no one hunting, "each shall
rest under his vine and under his fig tree." 406 Moses therefore allots, but
"beyond the Jordan"; and "Jesus allots with the chiefs of the tribes of
Israel." Thus Jesus the Christ, giving the true allotment, takes along "the
rulers" of the spiritual "Israel," the Apostles, to whom he said that: "you
shall sit upon 12 thrones judging the 12 tribes of Israel." And "Eleazar" also
is taken along, being with Jesus, each typing the priest and ruler, the Son of
God. 407 And Caleb reasonably obtained a lot first. And he is interpreted as
heart; and this is the one who attends in all things to the thoughts, the one
who besides all the members to which he has bid farewell is named as heart and
wholly reconstituted into the ruling [faculty]. And his father is Jephunneh;
and he is interpreted <turning; Caleb therefore> son of turning. And he is a
lesson of Moses and second of Jesus. And what does he say? "You know the word
which the Lord spoke to Moses." For who knows the law as Jesus the Christ, who
also accurately unfolded it for us? "And the Lord," he says, "spoke to Moses
concerning me and you." For Moses prophesies concerning both Jesus and the one
[who is] as heart. Who also says 408 "today to be strong as when he was strong
then." For in both words "the saint is strong," in both old and new, under
Moses and Jesus. And he "asks a mountain," wanting nothing that creeps on the
ground, in which are "great and fortified cities," as knowing how to war
against "the Anakim"; for "a wise man went up upon fortified cities and went
up upon the fortress, upon which the ungodly had trusted." For Solomon says:
"a wise man fortified cities and 409 takes down the fortresses," that is, the
words that with plausibility are able to deceive. Which he says are in a
"mountain," since "they are heights lifting themselves up against the
knowledge of God." These Caleb promises "to be sufficient" to take down;
wherefore "Jesus blessed him." And Hebron is interpreted yoked; for the bones
of the fathers are stored there according to 410 pairing, Abraham and Sarah,
Isaac and Rebekah, Jacob and Leah. He therefore gives the honor of the fathers
to Caleb who wants to take down the aliens. "And the land abated from the
war." And how could anyone inherit from Jesus without having dissolved the war
in himself? 411 And there being four generic regions at "the borders of
Judah," speaking the two, "south" and "east," he has kept silent the rest. For
after "the Libyan [parts]," he says, "the borders of Judah" succeed. Which are
near "Idumaea," which is interpreted earthly things, after which "the borders
of Judah." And he also says "from the wilderness of Zin"; and it is
interpreted temptations. After which is the inheritance of "Judah." And at
these borders of the tribe toward the "south" no other region has been named
except "south" or "west" or "the brook of Egypt," that one may pass through
these and 412 the temptations

414 world. For the things glorious according to God belong to the allotment of
Judah, both a royal tribe and in a holy land. 415 (Toward not growing weary in
the reading of the divine scripture for the one who does not understand the
obscure [character] of the riddles and parables in it. From the 20th homily on
Jesus Nave): The highest benefit from such readings comes to the one able to
understand the true allotment partitioned by Jesus to the sons of Israel, and
to the one who has become able to go up upon "the holy land," the true one,
"the" truly "good," and to fit from the naming of the things said to the
difference of those inheriting the things said according to the places. But
since it is difficult to find the one thus benefited, we wish to console the
hearers not to grow weary at the readings. What then is the consolation
=416.20= for the hearer of such readings not to grow weary, must be said. Just
as therefore incantations have a certain natural power and the one being
charmed, even without understanding, receives something from the incantation
according to the nature of the sounds of the incantation, whether unto harm or
unto healing of body or of his own soul; so understand for me that the naming
of the names in the divine scriptures is more powerful than every incantation.
For there are certain powers in us, of which the better, through these as it
were incantations, are nourished, being akin to them, and though we do not
understand, 417 those powers, understanding the things said, become more
powerful in us toward cooperating with our life. For that there are certain
invisible things in us, and many of them, the Psalm that said will make clear:
"Bless the Lord, O my soul, and all that is within me, his holy name." There
is therefore a certain multitude of powers in us that have been allotted our
souls and bodies; which, if they are holy, when scripture is read are
benefited and become stronger, even if "our mind is unfruitful," as it has
been written concerning "the one who speaks in a tongue," that: "My spirit
prays, but my mind is unfruitful." Understand 418 therefore for me that
sometimes "when the mind in us is unfruitful" the powers that cooperate with
the soul and with the mind and with us all are nourished with the rational
food from the sacred letters and from these names and, being nourished, become
more powerful toward cooperating with us. And just as the better powers are as
it were charmed and benefited and become more powerful from such letters and
names, so the opposing powers of those in us are as it were dragged down and
conquered by the incantations of God and, being conquered, are put to sleep.
And if ever one of you has seen an asp being put to sleep by incantations or
some other of these venomous things, let him take that example for the
scripture; when it is read and not understood, sometimes the hearer is 419
listless and grows weary. And let him believe that the asps in him and the
vipers in him become weaker from the drugs that drug them, as from wise Moses,
from wise Jesus,


from the wise holy prophets. Let us not therefore grow weary hearing
scriptures which we do not understand, but "let it be to us according to our
faith," which we also believe, that "every scripture, being God-breathed, is
profitable." For you must accept one of the two concerning these scriptures,
either that they are not "God-breathed," since they are not "profitable," as
the unbeliever would suppose; or, as a believer, to accept that, since they
are "God-breathed," they are "profitable." It is to be known, however, that
the benefit also often comes to us unperceived, as often we are ordered to eat
a certain food that is good for sharp sight and we do not of course perceive
in the eating that we are benefited in the eye, but when a second day has
passed and 420 a third the digestion of the food that benefits the eye makes
us believe by experience that we have been benefited in the eye. But this is
also to be seen in the case of other foods that benefit certain members of the
body. So therefore believe also concerning the divine scripture, that your
soul is benefited, even if "the mind does not receive the fruit" of the
benefit from the letters, from the bare reading alone. For the things in us
are charmed and the better are nourished, and the worse are abolished. "And
the sons of Judah came near to Jesus." Before the portion which the tribe
received, Caleb without lot 421 receives a part through a command of God. And
what city ought the only one saved with Jesus besides the 600 thousands to
receive except "the mother-city of Anak, Hebron"? which having received "he
went up against those dwelling in Debir, which formerly was called city of
Letters." And he proclaims "to the one who takes it to give Achsah his
daughter," which "Othniel son of Kenaz, younger brother of Caleb, took"; 422
clearly as from another father at least according to the account of the
letter. And Achsah receives according to request from the father "Gonethla the
upper and Gonethla the lower of the inheritance of Judah." But what is the
"mother-city"? There being therefore many cities in 423 the holy allotment and
differing ranks, there must also be a rank of ranks, both comprehensive and
ruling and as it were a "mother-city," which those who excel receive, and
another others. For Scripture says many mother-cities, as in Isaiah: "Faithful
mother-city Zion." Of which Caleb receives the first, being of the "Anakim,"
which is interpreted empty humiliation or empty answer. He therefore receives
a "mother-city" of empty humiliation. For there are two humiliations: the one
empty, and the other full. The one praiseworthy, concerning which the Savior
says: "Learn from me, that I am meek and humble in heart" and "everyone who
humbles himself shall be exalted"; and Paul: "Humble yourselves therefore
under the mighty hand of God." And the blameworthy, with which the sinner is
humbled, and concerning the one who unlawfully comes together with a woman it
has been said in Scripture: "he humbled her." This humiliation "Caleb
abolished," the one called "Anak," whose children are "Susi," who is
interpreted outside me (=424.20= for the son of the empty humiliation is
outside God and the holy one); and "Ahiman," which is brother of mine from
counsel; for all are brothers, but the sinner is a brother without counsel,
cast out of counsel; and "Thalamin," who is interpreted hanging, someone aloft
and not established. Whom "Caleb having slain" "goes up against those dwelling
in Debir, which was city of Letters" and is interpreted speech. And this is to
be understood to be the old Scripture, which being called "of Letters" becomes
speech when it obtains an intelligent narration. 425 And he promises "his own
daughter to the one who takes the Scriptures; and Othniel takes the city," who
is interpreted answer of God. When God answers me, I have become an answer of
God. And "I cut off the letters being a younger brother of Caleb," that is,
the one from the 426 Church. For I am no longer circumcised according to the
letter, I do not keep Passover, not unleavened bread, I do not sabbatize. And
I receive the offspring of my elder brother, having become "son of Kenaz," who
is interpreted cheapening. Of which sort we from the nations have become of

Jerusalem in the present life, seeing only peace and sinning nothing, seeing

toward their casting out, calling upon God who alone is able. And thirdly
Scripture says "the Canaanite" is with the sons of Ephraim, not a bare

"to utterly destroy the Canaanite," so that "going up into the forest" they
may prepare a place for themselves. Therefore first the Canaanite is with us,
being able nothing so great against us, being "tributary," but not subject nor
"slave." For these things come to be the second time. For when the bodily
things =434.20= are subjected to the soul, he is not only with us, but also "a
slave." And the third, if we are perfected, he "shall be utterly destroyed"

should come," when also "Gezer" the place of the Canaanite "is given as a
