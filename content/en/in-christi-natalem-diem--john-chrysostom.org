On the Nativity of Christ.
=61.737=
The sun of righteousness has risen today, and has covered the one that rose
before; I have been redeemed from darkness, and I cannot bear the rays; the
light has been born for me again, and I am dimmed by fear; I rejoice at the
birth, and I am troubled at the manner; I see a new spring coming forth, and
an ancient wound fleeing; I saw an infant being born, and heaven bowing down
for his worship; a mother giving birth, and a womb not opening; a child
sealing his own conception; a parent without a husband, and a son without a
father; a savior being born, and a star being created; an infant being
swaddled, and bearing the infinite; a manger being formed into a heavenly
throne, and cattle imaging the cherubic standing; a luminary appearing, and
the wonder speaking; angels announcing beforehand, and shepherds prophesying;
magi declaring God, and priests fighting against God; Herod falling, and death
being mourned; Adam being loosed, Eve rejoicing, and the serpent mourning;
captives coming into release, and tyrants into punishment; an infant being
nursed, and nourishing her who nurses; being carried in hands, and bearing her
who bears; creation recognizing, and nature trembling. Therefore I too am
beside myself at the wonder, trembling at the mystery. I will take courage
from Gabriel, and I drive my fear away.
The words to Mary, interpreting wonders. What are these? And he says: Hail,
full of grace, unharvested field of the heavenly ear of grain; hail, full of
grace, true virgin mother of the true vine; hail, full of grace, unfailing net
of the unchanging Godhead; hail, full of grace, spacious place of the
uncontainable nature; hail, full of grace, bridegroom of the widowed world,
undefiled birth; hail, full of grace, who wove for creation a crown not
plaited by hands; hail, full of grace, holy dwelling of fire; hail, full of
grace, the return of the fugitive inhabited world; hail, full of grace,
inexhaustible storehouse of starving creation; hail, full of grace, the
boundless grace of the holy virgin; hail, full of grace, lamp-bearer adorned
with many virtues, the unquenchable light brighter than the sun; hail, full of
grace, the bait of the intelligible hook; hail, full of grace, intelligible
ark of glory; hail, full of grace, the golden jar that holds the heavenly
manna; hail, full of grace, who filled the thirsty with the sweet drink of the
ever-flowing spring; hail, full of grace, the intelligible sea, holding the
heavenly pearl, Christ; hail, full of grace, the bright heaven, who holds in
herself the God uncontainable in the heavens, uncontainable and unconfined;
hail, full of grace, pillar-like cloud, holding the God who led Israel in the
wilderness.
What shall I say and what shall I speak? How shall I bless the wholly rooted
glory? Because, apart from God alone, you are higher than all. But she was
troubled at the word, and fleeing with her gaze, she stands with her body,
shaping the sight in a cruciform figure, and she stood before the angel in two
minds, neither daring to flee nor taking courage to stand. Seeing the bodiless
one standing as a man, she was ashamed to remain, honoring her virginity; she
did not dare to question the one unlike herself. And the virgin's face had
come forth as a mirror of fear, saying in herself, What sort of greeting might
this be? or who is the one who greeted me? He outshines the sun, and converses
with clay; he has run down from heaven, and greets a maiden; he is not clothed
with a body, and he stands beside a woman, walking on the earth with wings as
with feet. What does he seek from her who is of earth? An angel appears to me,
and he speaks as a man; a voice is put forth to me from bodiless holy ones;
his face is adorned brighter than a luminary; he has put on a tunic; I see a
young man not wearing flesh, treading the ground, and not tracing a footprint.
=61.738=
not possessing a tongue, and crying Hail to me; lips not moving, and
prophesying joy to me. I do not know how I should reckon the shuddering
vision. If I do not understand the one sent, how shall I know the one who sent
him? If the announcer is fearful, the one announced is more fearful; if one
flashing thus has darkened my mind, in what body shall I hide the sun of
righteousness? If the creature startles me, how shall I bear the Creator? If I
am terrified at the servant, how shall I bear the Master? And the angel said
to her: Do not fear, Mary.
You are not tripped as Eve was; from her, death, and from you, immortal life;
from her, fruit of death, and from you, life-giving fruit; from her, deceit,
and from you, love; from her, the separation of God from men, and from you, an
inexplicable intertwining of God and flesh; from her, the dark sleep of Hades,
from you, the bright lamp of the world; from her, curse, and from you,
blessing; from her, the sentence, and from you, the pardon; from her,
affliction, and from you, faith; from her, tears, and from you, rivers of
living water; from her, the sweats, and from you, rest; from her, the ancient
wound, and from you, the life of the Trinity; from her, hatred of brothers,
and from you, love of mankind; from her, a flood, and from you, a bath of
immortality; from her, the murderers, and from you, regeneration; from her,
slaughters of the living, and from you, the rising of the dead; from her, a
hated twelve-tribe people, and from you, twelve apostles united in love; from
her, death running through the world, but having stumbled against you he will
fall; from her, the fall, and from you, the resurrection. Do not fear, Mary;
for you have found favor with God. And behold, you will conceive in the womb
him who is in the Father and in flesh; and you will bear a son older than
Adam, you will bear a son more ancient than I, you will bear a son higher than
heaven, you will bear a son above the Cherubim, you will bear a son, the
Creator of the ages, you will bear a son co-unoriginate with the Father, you
will bear a son co-eternal with the Spirit, you will bear a son whose all
things are, you will bear a son whom we do not behold, you will bear a son
whose form is hidden from me, you will bear a son before whom I stand
trembling. And the days were fulfilled for her to give birth, and she bore her
firstborn son, whom a man did not plant; she bore her son, whom a father did
not carry; she bore the ear of grain, which a body did not sow; she bore the
Creator become man; she gave birth in a cave, and he appeared in Persia. For
when Christ was born in Bethlehem of Judea, behold, magi from the east came to
Jerusalem saying.
Where is he who has been born king of the Jews? Where is he who was not sown
and was born? he who sent his own star before himself? Where is he who through
a strange ray draws us toward a light unknown? Where is he who walls the
inhabited world with an immeasurable palm? Where is he who appears everywhere,
and is hidden in flesh? Where is he who loosed us from error, and leads toward
grace? Where is he who appeared under the oak as a triad of angels? Where is
he who did not burn the bush, and did not corrupt the mother? Where is he who
from the Father is not seen, and is held by a mother? Where is he who dried
the Red Sea, and is given milk to drink? Where is he who struck Pharaoh, and
is hidden from Herod? Where is he who sank the dragon, and lies in swaddling
clothes? Where is he whom archangels did not see, and men behold? And behold,
the star which they saw in the east went before them, until it came and stood
over where the child was; and having worshipped, they offered him gifts, gold
and frankincense and myrrh. And being warned in a dream not to return to
Herod, they withdrew to their own country by another way. And to the Christ
who has been born, our God, let us hymn and glorify, together with his Father
with the Holy Spirit, now and always, unto the ages. Amen.
