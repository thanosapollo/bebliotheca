79 He names the soul spirit; for choice is a certain movement of mind; and
David: into your hands I commit my spirit; Lord Jesus, receive my spirit; and
in the Books of Kings: David, he says, did not grieve the spirit of Amnon his
son.
80 That one end of all such things is vanity; of which there is found no
surplus into what follows; for when the activity has ceased, the enjoyment
also has been wiped out, and nothing is stored up into what follows. Nor is
any trace of gladness left to those who take pleasure, or remnant, once the
activity according to pleasure has passed.
