161 It pleased me also to speak with the prophet Isaiah: that I waited as "she
who gives birth" until I "dry them up" and destroy them. If then you know
yourself, that the fountain of the Holy Spirit gushes in you, this is the sign
that they have been dried up and have perished, as our Savior said, that the
kingdom of heaven is not here or there, but is within you. Again the apostle
says: "the kingdom of God" is not "in word," "but in power." For there are
some who speak the things of the kingdom but do not work them, and there are
some who work the things of the kingdom but do not speak in watchfulness nor
in knowledge. But those in whom the word of the Savior has come to pass, "the
kingdom is within you," are few and rare and hard to find; upon these the Holy
"Spirit" of God has come, and the word of the evangelist John has been
fulfilled: that those who believed in his name "he gave them authority to
become children of God," "who were born not of blood nor of the will" "of man,
but of God." These are they who have been delivered from the bitter sentence
that went forth against Adam: that "cursed is the earth in your works," these
are they who have laid hold of the joy which Mary laid hold of: that "the
Spirit of God will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will
overshadow you." For as grief came down upon Eve and upon her seed until now,
so again joy laid hold of Mary and her seed until now. 163 As then we were
once sons of Eve and recognized her curse upon us through our dishonorable
thoughts, so again we ought to recognize ourselves, that we have been born of
God through the thoughts of the Holy Spirit and through the sufferings of
Christ, if they truly are in our body. For it is written in the apostle: that
"examine yourselves," whether "Christ is in you," or do you not know "that
Christ Jesus is in you," "unless" perhaps "you are reprobate." When therefore
we wore "the image of the earthy," we knew that we are his sons through the
dishonorable matter of his thoughts that dwell in us, which are the passions.
But those who have worn "the image of the heavenly" recognize themselves, that
they are sons of the heavenly one "through" his Holy "Spirit" that "dwells in"
them. For Isaiah cries out saying: "from your fear, Lord, we conceived in the
womb and were in labor and brought forth a spirit of salvation
† which we conceived† upon the earth." It is written again in
Ecclesiastes: that "as bones in the womb of her who is with child" so "is
the way of the spirit." For just as this holy virgin conceived him in the
flesh, so also those who have received the grace of the Holy Spirit conceive
him in their heart according to the word of the apostle saying: that "Christ
may dwell (in the inner man) through faith in your hearts," "rooted and
grounded in love," "that" you may be able to present yourselves "with all
the saints," and again: "but we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that
the exceeding greatness of the power may be of God and not from us." If then
you have arrived at this word: that "we are being transformed into the same
image from glory to glory," and if the word of the apostle has been
fulfilled in you that says: "and let the peace of God rule in your hearts"
and in your mind (which is Christ Jesus), if you have arrived at this word:
that "from darkness light" "has shone in your hearts" "of the 165 knowledge
of the glory of God," if the word has been fulfilled in you: that "let your
loins be girded and your lamps burning," and you will be as servants waiting
for their own master, "when he returns" from the wedding, so that your mouth
may not be stopped, having no defense among the saints, if you know that
your vessel has oil, as the prudent virgins, so that you may not be left
outside the door, but that you may enter into the bridechamber with the
bridegroom, if you have perceived that your spirit and your soul and your
body have been united blamelessly and rise on the day of our Lord Jesus
Christ unspotted, if you have become unreproved and uncondemned from your
conscience and if you have become a little child according to the word of
the Savior that says: "do not hinder" "the children" from coming "to me; for
of such is the kingdom of heaven." If you have truly become a bride to him
and his Holy Spirit has inherited you while you are still in the body,
expect a kingdom, but if not, expect grief and groaning, because shame and
reproach go before you in the presence of the saints. And know this, that as
a virgin of this world, rising in the morning each day, does not take up
another care before she adorns herself for her bridegroom, looking often in
the mirror, lest there be a blemish on her face. For if she has a blemish
she will not please her bridegroom. Thus there is much care for the saints
by night and by day to search their own thoughts and to learn whether they
are under the yoke of the Holy Spirit or not. Therefore strive, brother,
carefully in toil of heart and mouth, in knowledge, to acquire that eternal
joy, because rare are those who have been deemed worthy of it, who acquired
"the sword of the Spirit" and brought their soul to progress and freed all
its senses from every defilement of the passions, as the apostle said: that
we may furnish their increase in us in all things, "which is the head,
Christ." And it is possible for his holy name to help us in our weakness,
that we may be deemed worthy to arrive with all his saints. Amen.
