TO THE ROMANS.
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to the church shown mercy in the majesty of
the most high God the Father and of Jesus Christ his only-begotten Son,
sanctified and enlightened in the will of God who made all things that are,
according to the faith and love of Jesus Christ, our God and Saviour, which
also presides in the place of the region of the Romans, worthy of God, worthy
of honor, worthy of blessing, worthy of praise, worthy of being granted its
request, worthy of holiness and presiding in love, named from Christ, named
from the Father, Spirit-bearing, which I also greet in the name of God
Almighty and of Jesus Christ his Son; to those united in flesh and spirit to
every commandment of his, filled with all the grace of God without distinction
and filtered from every alien color, very much in God and the Father and our
Lord Jesus Christ blamelessly to rejoice. Since having prayed to God I have
obtained to see your faces worthy of God, as I was asking even more to
receive; for being bound in Christ Jesus I hope to greet you, if it be the
will that I be counted worthy to be unto the end. For the beginning is well
ordered, if I obtain grace unto receiving my lot unto the end without
hindrance. For I fear your love, lest it do me injustice; for it is easy for
you to do what you wish, but it is hard for me to attain to God, unless you
spare me under pretext of fleshly friendship. For I do not wish you to please
men, but to please God, as indeed you do please him; for neither shall I ever
have such a time so as to attain to God, nor you, if you are silent, have a
better work to have written down to you. For if you are silent from me, I
shall become God's; but if you are in love with my flesh, I shall again be a
runner. Rather grant me to be poured out as a libation to God, while the altar
is still ready, that becoming a choir in love you may sing to the Father in
Christ Jesus, that God has counted the bishop of Syria worthy to be found unto
the west, having sent him from the east as a witness of his own sufferings. It
is good to be dissolved from the world toward God, that I may rise unto him.
You never envied anyone; you taught others. And I wish that those things also
may be firm which you command when you make disciples. Only ask for me power
both from within and from without, that I may not only speak but also will,
that I may not only be called a Christian but also be found one; for if I am
found, I can also be called one, and then be faithful, when I no longer appear
to the world. Nothing that appears is eternal; for the things that are seen
are temporal, but the things that are not seen are eternal. Christianity is
not a work of persuasion, but of greatness, when it is hated by the world. It
is loved by God. For if you were of this world, he says, the world would love
its own; but now you are not of the world, but I chose you; remain with me. I
write to all the churches and I charge all, that I die willingly for God,
unless you hinder me. I exhort you, do not become an untimely kindness to me.
Let me be food of beasts, through which it is possible to attain to God. I am
wheat of God and I am ground by the teeth of beasts, that I may be found pure
bread of God. Rather flatter the beasts, that they may become a tomb for me
and leave nothing of my body, that I may not, having fallen asleep, a burden
to anyone

be found; and then I shall be a true disciple of Jesus Christ, when the world
will not see even my body. Entreat the Lord for me, that through these
instruments I may be found a sacrifice to God. I do not command you as Peter
and Paul; they were apostles of Jesus Christ, but I am least; they were free
as servants of God, but I am a servant until now. But if I suffer, I shall
become a freedman of Jesus Christ and I shall rise in him free. Now I learn,
being bound in him, to desire nothing worldly or vain. From Syria as far as
Rome I fight with beasts through land and sea, night and day, being bound to
ten leopards, which is a company of soldiers, who also become worse when they
are treated well. But in their wrongs I am rather being made a disciple, yet I
am not on this account justified. May I have joy of the beasts that have been
prepared for me, which I also pray may be found ready for me; which I will
also flatter to devour me quickly, not as some whom they did not touch, being
cowardly. And even if they themselves are unwilling, I will force them. Grant
me pardon; what is profitable for me, I know. Now I begin to be a disciple.
Let nothing of things visible and of things invisible envy me, that I may
attain to Jesus Christ. Fire and cross and packs of beasts, cuttings,
divisions, scatterings of bones, choppings of members, grindings of the whole
body, and the punishment of the devil, let them come upon me, only that I may
attain to Jesus Christ. The ends of the world will profit me nothing, nor the
kingdoms of this age. It is good for me to die through Christ Jesus, rather
than to reign over the ends of the earth. For what is a man profited, if he
gain the whole world, but lose his soul? I long for the Lord, the Son of the
true God and Father, Jesus the Christ. I seek him who died for us and rose.
Grant me pardon, brothers; do not hinder me from arriving at life, for Jesus
is the life of the believers; do not wish me to die, for life without Christ
is death. Do not grant to the world him who wishes to be God's. Let me receive
pure light; when I have come there I shall be a man of God. Permit me to be an
imitator of the passion of Christ my God. If anyone has him in himself, let
him understand what I wish, and let him sympathize with me, knowing the things
that constrain me. The ruler of this age wishes to snatch me and to corrupt my
mind toward my God. Let none then of you who are present help him; rather
become mine, that is, God's. Do not speak Jesus Christ and prefer the world.
Let envy not dwell in you. Not even if I myself, being present, exhort you, be
persuaded; rather believe these things which I write to you. For I write to
you living, longing to die through Christ. My eros has been crucified, and
there is not in me fire that loves matter; but living water, leaping in me,
says to me from within: Come to the Father. I take no pleasure in food of
corruption nor in the pleasures of this life. I wish the bread of God,
heavenly bread, bread of life, which is the flesh of Jesus Christ, the Son of
God, who was born in the last times of the seed of David and Abraham; and I
wish drink, his blood, which is incorruptible love and ever-flowing life. I no
longer wish to live according to men. And this will be, if you wish. I have
been crucified with Christ; and I no longer live, since Christ lives in me.
Through a few letters I ask you; do not refuse me; believe me, that I love
Jesus who was delivered up for me. What shall I render to the Lord for all
that he has rendered to me? And God himself and the Father and the Lord Jesus
the Christ will make these things plain to you, that I speak truly. And you
also pray with me, that I may hit the mark in the Holy Spirit. I wrote to you
not according to flesh, but according to the mind of God. If I suffer, you
have loved; if I am rejected, you have hated. Remember in your prayer the
church in Syria, which in my place has as shepherd the Lord who said: I am the
good shepherd; and he alone will oversee it, and your love toward him. And I

am even ashamed to be called from them; for I am not worthy, being the last of
them and an abortion. But I have been shown mercy to be someone, if I attain
to God. My spirit greets you, and the love of the churches that received me in
the name of Jesus Christ, not as one passing through; for even those not on my
way according to city brought me forward. And I write these things to you from
Smyrna through the Ephesians who are worthy of blessing. And there is with me
together with many others also Crocus, the name beloved. Concerning those who
have gone before from Syria to Rome unto the glory of God I believe you have
come to know; to whom you will also make known that I am near; for they are
all worthy of God and of you; whom it is fitting for you to refresh in every
way. And I wrote these things to you on the day before the ninth of the
kalends of September. Farewell unto the end in the endurance of Jesus Christ.

TO THE PHILADELPHIANS.
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to the church of God the Father and of the
Lord Jesus Christ, which is in Philadelphia, shown mercy in love and
established in the concord of God and rejoicing in the passion of our Lord
Jesus without wavering and fully assured in his resurrection in all mercy,
which I greet in the blood of Jesus Christ, which is joy eternal and abiding;
especially if they are in one with the bishop and the presbyters and deacons
appointed in the will of God the Father through the Lord Jesus Christ, who
according to his own counsel firmly established his church upon the rock, a
spiritual building, not made with hands, which the winds and the rivers,
dashing against it, were not able to overturn, and may the spiritual things of
wickedness never be able, but may they be weakened by the power of Jesus
Christ our Lord. Having seen your bishop I knew that he was counted worthy to
be entrusted with the ministry that belongs to the common good not from
himself nor through men nor according to vainglory, but in the love of Jesus
Christ and of God the Father, who raised him from the dead; whose gentleness I
have been struck by, who being silent is able to do more than those who speak
more. For he is fitted to the commandments of the Lord and to the ordinances,
as strings to the lyre, and he is blameless no less than Zacharias the priest.
Therefore my soul blesses his mind toward God, having come to know it as
virtuous and perfect, his unmovedness and his freedom from anger in all the
gentleness of the living God. As children then of the light of truth flee the
division of the unity and the evil teachings of the heretics, from which a
pollution has gone out into all the earth. And where the shepherd is, there
follow as sheep; for many wolves clothed in fleeces with evil pleasure take
captive the God-runners; but in your unity they will have no place. Abstain
therefore from the evil plants, which Jesus Christ does not cultivate, but the
man-killing beast, because they are not a planting of the Father, but seed of
the evil one. Not that I found a division among you, do I write these things,
but I am securing you beforehand as children of God. For as many as are
Christ's, these are with the bishop; but as many as turn aside from him and
greet communion with the accursed, these will be cut off with them; for they
are not a tilled field of Christ, but a sowing of the enemy; from whom may you
always be delivered by the prayers of the shepherd who sits before you, the
most faithful and most meek. I exhort you therefore in the Lord: as many as
having repented come to the unity of the church, receive them with all
meekness, that through kindness and forbearance, having recovered from the
snare of the devil, having become worthy of Jesus Christ, they may obtain
eternal salvation in the kingdom of Christ. Brothers, do not be deceived; if
anyone follows one who splits from the truth, he will not inherit the kingdom
of God; and if anyone does not stand away from the false-speaking preacher,
unto Gehenna

he will be condemned; for one must neither stand away from the pious, nor must
one be joined with the impious. If anyone walks in an alien mind, this one is
not of Christ nor a partner of his passion, but he is a fox, a corrupter of
the vineyard of Christ. Do not mix with such a one, that you may not perish
with him, even if he be a father or a son or a brother or a household member.
For your eye shall not spare him, he says. Those who hate God you also must
hate and waste away over his enemies; yet not also strike them or persecute
them, as the nations that do not know the Lord and God, but consider them
enemies and separate from them, yet admonish them and exhort them to
repentance, if perhaps they hear, if perhaps they yield. For our God is a
lover of men, and he wishes all men to be saved and to come to knowledge of
truth. Therefore he makes his sun rise upon evil and good and rains the rain
upon righteous and unrighteous. Wishing us also to be imitators of his
kindness the Lord says: Become perfect, as also your heavenly Father is
perfect. I have trusted in you in the Lord, that you will think nothing else.
Therefore also taking courage I write to your love worthy of God, exhorting
you to use one faith and one preaching and one eucharist; for one is the flesh
of the Lord Jesus and one his blood poured out for us (for one bread was
broken for all and one cup was distributed to the whole), one altar for all
the church and one bishop together with the presbytery and the deacons, my
fellow-servants. Since also there is one unbegotten, God and Father, and one
only-begotten Son, God the Word and man, and one the Paraclete, the Spirit of
truth, and one also the preaching and the faith one and the baptism one and
one the church, which the holy apostles established from ends unto ends in the
blood of Christ with their own sweats and toils. You too then must as a people
for his own possession and a holy nation accomplish all things in concord in
Christ. Wives, be subject to your husbands in fear of God; virgins to Christ
in incorruption, not abhorring marriage, but aiming at the better, not for
slander of union, but for the sake of the study of the laws. Children, obey
your parents and cherish them as fellow-workers of God unto your birth.
Slaves, be subject to your masters in God, that you may become freedmen of
Christ. Husbands, love your wives as fellow-servants to God, as your own body,
as partners of life and fellow-workers of childbearing. Virgins, have only
Christ before your eyes and his Father in your souls, being enlightened by the
Spirit. May I have joy of your holiness as of Elijah, as of Jesus the son of
Nun, as of Melchizedek, as of Elisha, as of Jeremiah, as of John the Baptist,
as of the beloved disciple, as of Timothy, as of Titus, as of Evodius, as of
Clement, who departed this life in purity. Not blaming the remaining blessed
ones, because they associated with marriages, have I mentioned these just now
(for I pray that, having been found worthy of God, I may be found at their
footsteps in the kingdom as Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, as Joseph and Isaiah
and the other prophets, as Peter and Paul and the other apostles who
associated with marriages), but from eagerness concerning the matter I myself
had those in mind. Fathers, bring up your own children in the discipline and
admonition of the Lord, and teach them the sacred letters and crafts so that
they may not rejoice in idleness. And well, he says, a righteous father brings
up, over a prudent son his heart will be glad. Masters, attend kindly to the
household servants, as the holy Job taught; for one is <the> nature and one
the race of humanity; for in Christ there is neither slave nor free. Rulers,
let them obey Caesar, the soldiers the rulers; the deacons the presbyters as
priests, the presbyters and the deacons and the remaining clergy together with
all the people and the soldiers and the rulers and Caesar to the bishop, the

bishop to Christ, as Christ to the Father; and thus the unity through all is
preserved. And let the widows also not be gadabouts, not greedy, not running
about, but as Judith the most solemn, as Anna the most temperate. These things
I do not command as an apostle (for who am I, or who is the house of my
father, that I should say myself equal in honor to those?), but as your
fellow-soldier holding the rank of a prompter. My brothers, I have been poured
out exceedingly, loving you, and overjoyed I secure you; yet not I, but
through me the Lord Jesus, in whom being bound I rather fear; for I am still
unfinished; but your prayer will finish me unto God, that I may attain that in
which I was called, having fled to the gospel as to the flesh of Jesus Christ
and to the apostles as to the presbytery of the church. And the prophets also
I love as having announced Christ, as having shared in the same Spirit, of
which the apostles also. For as the false prophets and the false apostles drew
one and the same evil and deceitful and people-leading spirit, so also the
prophets and the apostles received one and the same holy Spirit, good and
ruling, true and teaching, from God through Jesus Christ, a right spirit. For
one is the God of old and new covenant; one the mediator of God and men unto
both the creation of intelligible and sensible things and a fitting and
suitable providence; and one also the Paraclete, who worked in Moses and
prophets and apostles. All the saints then were saved in Christ, having hoped
in him and waited for him, and through him they obtained salvation, being
saints worthy of love and worthy of wonder, attested by Jesus Christ in the
gospel of the common hope. If anyone preaches one God of law and prophets, but
denies Christ to be Son of God, he is a liar, as also his father the devil,
and such a one is a false Jew of the circumcision below. If anyone confesses
Christ Jesus Lord, but denies the God of the law and of the prophets, saying
that the maker of heaven and earth is not the Father of Christ, such a one has
not stood in the truth, as also his father the devil, and such a one is a
disciple of Simon the magician, but not of the Holy Spirit. If anyone says
there is one God, and also confesses Christ Jesus, but thinks the Lord to be a
mere man, not only-begotten God and wisdom and Word of God, but thinks him to
be of soul and body, such a one is a serpent, preaching deceit and error unto
destruction of men, and such a one is poor in understanding as by surname
Ebion. If anyone confesses these things, but calls the lawful mixing and the
birth of children corruption and pollution, or some of the foods abominable,
such a one has as indweller the dragon the apostate. If anyone confesses
Father and Son and Holy Spirit and praises the creation, but says the
embodiment is seeming and is ashamed of the passion, such a one has denied the
faith no less than the Christ-killing Jews. If anyone confesses these things,
and that God the Word dwelt in a human body, being in himself the Word, as a
soul in a body, because God is indwelling, but not a human soul, and says the
lawless mixings are something good and sets pleasure as the end of happiness,
such as the falsely named Nicolaitan, this one can be neither a lover of God
nor a lover of Christ, but a corrupter of his own flesh and on this account
empty of the Holy Spirit and alien to Christ. All such are monuments and tombs
of the dead, upon which only names of dead men have been written. Flee
therefore the evil arts and ambushes of the spirit that now works in the sons
of this age, lest those who have been afflicted grow weak in love; but all
become together in an undivided heart and a willing soul, of one soul,
thinking the one thing, always holding the same opinions about the same
things, both in ease and in dangers and in griefs and in joys. I give thanks
to God through Jesus

Christ, that I am of good conscience among you and no one has to boast either
secretly or openly, that I burdened anyone either in a small thing or in a
great. And for all in whom I have spoken, I pray that they may not acquire it
unto a testimony. For if some wished to deceive me according to flesh, yet my
spirit is not deceived; for I have received it from God. For it knows whence
it comes and where it goes and it convicts the hidden things. For I cried out
being in the midst, I was speaking with a great voice (the word is not mine,
but God's): Attend to the bishop and to the presbytery and to the deacons. But
if you suspect me of saying these things as having learned beforehand the
division of some, witness is he for whom I am bound, that I did not know it
from the mouth of a man. But the Spirit preached to me saying these things: Do
nothing without the bishop; keep your flesh as a temple of God, love the
union, flee the divisions, become imitators of Paul and of the other apostles,
as also they of Christ. I then was doing my own as a man fitted unto union,
saying this also, that where there is a standing apart of mind and anger and
hatred, there God does not dwell. To all then who repent God forgives, if they
run together unto the unity of Christ and the session of the bishop. I trust
the grace of Jesus Christ, that he will loose from us every bond of injustice.
I exhort you therefore: do nothing according to factiousness, but according to
Christ-learning; for I heard some saying that unless I find it in the archives
of the gospel, I do not believe. And to such I say that my archives are Jesus
the Christ, to disobey whom is obvious ruin. Untouchable archive for me is his
cross and his death and his resurrection and the faith concerning these
things; in which I wish to be justified in your prayer. He who disbelieves the
gospel disbelieves all together; for the archives of the Spirit are not set
forth. It is hard to kick against the goads, hard to disbelieve Christ, hard
to set aside the preaching of the apostles. Good are the priests and the
ministers of the word, but better is the high priest who has been entrusted
with the holy of holies, who alone has been entrusted with the hidden things
of God. Good are the liturgical powers of God. Good also is the Holy Spirit,
which is above all the holy things most holy and a minister of the Word. And
above all the saints the most holy is the high priest, who is angel and
minister of the Father and ruler of the legions of the army of heaven, through
whom the Father has made all things and provides for the whole; this one is
the way leading to the Father, the rock, the fence, the key, the shepherd, the
victim, the door of knowledge, through which Abraham and Isaac and Jacob
entered, Moses and the whole choir of the prophets and the pillars of the
world, the apostles, and the bride of Christ, for whom as a dowry by a word he
poured out his own blood, that he might redeem her. All these things unto the
unity of the one and only true God. And the gospel has something exceptional,
the presence of our Saviour Jesus Christ, the passion, the resurrection
itself. For the things which the prophets announced saying: Until he come, for
whom it is stored up, and he is the expectation of nations, these have been
fulfilled in the gospel: Going, make disciples of all the nations, baptizing
them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. All
things then together are good, the law, the prophets, the apostles, the whole
assembly that

TO THE SMYRNAEANS.
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to the church of God the Father most high
and of his beloved Son Jesus Christ, shown mercy in every gift, filled in
faith and love, being lacking in no gift, most God-befitting and holy-bearing,
which is in Smyrna of Asia, in blameless spirit and word of God very much to
rejoice. I glorify God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who through him
has thus made you wise; for I perceived you fitted in unmoved faith, as if
nailed to the cross of the Lord Jesus Christ in flesh and in spirit and
established in love in the blood of Christ; fully assured as truly unto our
Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, the firstborn of all creation, the God
Word, the only-begotten Son, and being of the race of David according to
flesh, of Mary the virgin, baptized by John, that all righteousness might be
fulfilled by him; having lived holily without sin, and under Pontius Pilate
and Herod the tetrarch nailed for us in flesh truly, from which we also are,
from his God-blessed passion, that he might raise a sign unto the ages through
the resurrection unto his saints and faithful, whether in Jews or in nations,
in one body of his church. For all these things he suffered for us, and he
truly suffered and not in seeming, as also he truly rose; but not as some of
the unbelievers, being ashamed of the fashioning of the man and of the cross
and of the death itself, say that in seeming and not in truth he took up the
body from the virgin and in appearance suffered, having forgotten him who
said: The Word became flesh, and: Destroy this temple, and through three days
I will raise it, and: If I am lifted up from the earth, I will draw all to
myself. Therefore the Word dwelt in flesh; for Wisdom built herself a house.
The Word raised his own temple, destroyed by the Christ-fighting Jews, on the
third day. The Word, when his flesh was lifted up according to the bronze
serpent in the wilderness, drew all to himself unto eternal salvation. And I
know him to have come to be in a body not only in being born and being
crucified, but also after the resurrection I know and believe him to be in
flesh. And when he came to those about Peter, he said to them: Take, handle me
and see, that I am not a bodiless demon; for a spirit does not have flesh and
bones, as you behold me having. And to Thomas he says: Bring your finger into
the print of the nails, and bring your hand and put it into my side. And
immediately they believed that he himself was the Christ. Therefore also
Thomas says to him: My Lord and my God. For on this account they also despised
death, for it is a small thing to say, insults and

TO THE ANTIOCHENES.
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to a church shown mercy by God, chosen by
Christ, sojourning in Syria and first having received the name of Christ, that
in Antioch, in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ to rejoice. The Lord
has made my bonds light and easy, having learned that you are at peace and
live in all concord both fleshly and spiritual. I exhort you therefore, I the
prisoner in the Lord, to walk worthily of the calling with which you were
called, guarding against the heresies of the evil one that have revelled in
unto deceit and destruction of those persuaded by him, and to attend to the
teaching of the apostles and to believe law and prophets, to throw off all
Jewish and Hellenic error and neither to bring in a multitude of gods nor to
deny Christ on pretext of the one God. For Moses the faithful servant of God,
having said: The Lord your God is one Lord, and having preached the one and
only God, confessed immediately also our Lord saying: The Lord rained upon
Sodom and Gomorrah from the Lord fire and brimstone. And again: And God said:
let us make man according to our image; and God made man, according to the
image of God he made him, and next: In the image of God I made man. And that a
man will come to be, he says: The Lord will raise up for you a prophet from
your brothers as me. And the prophets, having said as from the person of God:
I am God first and I after these things and besides me there is not a God,
speak concerning the Father of all. And concerning our Lord Jesus Christ: A
son, he says, has been given to us, whose beginning is from above, and his
name is called messenger of great counsel, wonderful, counsellor, strong God,
one in authority. And concerning his becoming man: Behold the virgin will take
in womb and will bear a son, and they will call his name Emmanuel. And
concerning the passion: As a sheep he was led upon slaughter and as a lamb
before him who shears him voiceless, and: I as a harmless lamb being led to be
sacrificed. And the evangelists, having said the one Father only true God, did
not omit the things according to our Lord, but wrote: In the beginning was the
Word, and the Word was toward God and God was the Word; this one was in the
beginning toward God; all things came to be through him, and without him came
to be not even one thing. And concerning the becoming man: The Word, he says,
became flesh and tabernacled in us, and: Book of genesis of Jesus Christ son
of David, son of Abraham. And the apostles, having said that God is one, said
themselves also that there is one also mediator of God and men, and they were
not ashamed of the embodiment and the passion; for what does he say? Man Jesus
Christ, who gave himself for the life of the world. Everyone then who
announces one God unto abolition of the divinity of Christ is a son of the
devil and an enemy of all righteousness; and he who confesses Christ not Son
of him who made the world, but of some other unknown, besides him whom the law
and the prophets preached, this one is an instrument of the devil himself; and
he who declines the becoming man and is ashamed of the cross, for which I am
bound, this one is antichrist; and he who says Christ is a mere man is
accursed according to the prophet, not having trusted upon God, but upon a
man; therefore he is also fruitless similarly to the wild tamarisk. I write
these things to you, O youth of Christ, not being conscious of such a mind in
you, but guarding you beforehand, as a father his own children. See then the
malicious workers, the enemies of the cross of Christ, whose end is
destruction, whose god is the belly, whose glory is in the shame

of them; see the dumb dogs, the serpents that crawl, the scaly little dragons,
the asps, the basilisks, the scorpions; for these are fox-like jackals,
man-mimicking apes. You have become disciples of Paul and Peter; do not lose
the deposit. Remember Evodius your shepherd worthy of blessing, who first was
entrusted by the apostles with your patronage. Let us not put the father to
shame; let us become genuine children, and not bastards. You know how I
conducted myself with you. What I was saying to you being present, these
things I also write being absent: If anyone does not love the Lord Jesus, let
him be anathema. Become imitators of me. May I become a ransom for you, when I
attain to Jesus. Remember my bonds. Presbyters, shepherd the flock among you,
until God shows him who is about to rule you; for I am already being poured
out, that I may gain Christ. Let the deacons know of what dignity they are,
and let them be zealous to be blameless, that they may be imitators of Christ.
Let the people be subject to the presbyters and to the deacons. Let the
virgins know to whom they have consecrated themselves. Husbands, let them
cherish their yoke-fellows, remembering that one was given to one, not many to
one in the creation. Wives, let them honor the husbands as their own flesh,
and let them not dare to call them even by name; and let them be temperate,
thinking only the yoke-fellows to be husbands, to whom they were also united
according to mind of God. Parents, educate the children with sacred education.
Children, honor the parents, that it may be well for you. Masters, do not
attend proudly to the slaves, imitating the enduring Job who said: And if I
also set at nought the judgment of my servant or of my maidservant, when they
were being judged toward me; for what shall I do, if the Lord makes an
examination of me? and you know the things next. Slaves, do not provoke the
masters in anything, that you may not become causes of incurable evils for
yourselves. Let no one eat idle, that he may not become a gadabout and a
fornicator. Drunkenness, anger, envy, reviling, crying, blasphemy, let them
not even be named among you. Let the widows not live in luxury, that they may
not wax wanton against the word. Be subject to Caesar, in the things in which
the subjection is without danger. Do not provoke the rulers unto inflammation,
that you may not give a pretext to those who seek against you. And concerning
sorcery or boy-love or murder it is superfluous to write, seeing that these
things are forbidden even for the nations to do. These things I do not command
as an apostle, but as your fellow-servant I remind you. I greet the holy
presbytery. I greet the sacred deacons and the name beloved to me, whom may I
see in my place in Holy Spirit, when I attain to Christ; of whom may I become
a ransom. I greet subdeacons, readers, singers, doorkeepers, those who toil,
exorcists, confessors. I greet the keepers of the holy gates, the deacons in
Christ. I greet the Christ-taken virgins, of whom may I have joy in the Lord
Jesus. I greet the most solemn widows. I greet the people of the Lord from
small unto great and all my sisters in the Lord. I greet Cassian and his
yoke-fellow and his dearest children. Polycarp the bishop worthy of honor
greets you, who also cares concerning you, to whom I also have commended you
in the Lord; and all the church of the Smyrnaeans also remembers you in the
prayers in the Lord. Onesimus the shepherd of the Ephesians greets you. Damas
the bishop of Magnesia greets you. Polybius of the Trallians greets you.
Philon and Agathopus the deacons, my fellow-followers, greet you. Greet one
another in a holy kiss. I write these things to you from Philippi. May he who
alone is unbegotten, through him who was begotten before the ages, keep you
faring well in spirit and flesh, and may I see you in the kingdom of Christ. I
greet him who is about to rule you in my place, of whom may I also have joy in
Christ. Farewell in God and Christ, enlightened by the Holy Spirit.

TO HERON DEACON OF ANTIOCH.
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to the God-honored and most longed-for, most
solemn, Christ-bearing, Spirit-bearing, genuine child in faith and love, Heron
deacon of Christ, servant of God, grace, mercy and peace from the Almighty God
and Christ Jesus our Lord, his only-begotten Son, who gave himself for our
sins, that he might take us out of the present evil age, and save us into his
heavenly kingdom. I exhort you in God to add to your course and to avenge your
dignity. Take thought for the symphony toward the saints; bear the weaker,
that you may fulfill the law of Christ. Be at leisure for fasts and petitions,
but not without measure, that you may not throw yourself down. Do not abstain
from wine and meats entirely; for they are not abominable; For the goods of
the earth, he says, you shall eat, and: You shall eat meats as vegetables,
and: Wine gladdens a heart of a man and oil makes cheerful and bread
strengthens; but measuredly and in good order, as God supplying. For who will
eat or who will drink besides him? because if anything is beautiful, it is
his, and if anything is good, it is his. Attend to the reading, that not only
you yourself may know the laws, but also may explain them to others. Be sober
as an athlete of God. No one serving as a soldier is entangled in the affairs
of life, that he may please him who enlisted him; and if anyone also contends,
he is not crowned, unless he contends lawfully. I the prisoner am a ransom for
you. Everyone who speaks besides the things ordered, even if he be
trustworthy, even if he fasts, even if he lives as a virgin, even if he does
signs, even if he prophesies, let him appear to you a wolf in a sheep's skin,
working corruption of sheep. If anyone denies the cross and is ashamed of the
passion, let him be to you as the adversary himself; even if he feeds the poor
with his belongings, even if he moves mountains, even if he delivers the body
unto burning, let him be abominable to you. If anyone makes cheap the law or
the prophets, which Christ being present fulfilled, let him be to you as the
antichrist. If anyone says the Lord is a mere man, he is a Jew a
Christ-killer. Honor widows, the true widows. Stand before orphans; for God is
a father of orphans and a judge of widows. Do nothing without the bishops; for
they are priests, and you are a deacon of the priests. Those baptize, perform
sacred rites, appoint, lay on hands; and you minister to them, as the holy
Stephen in Jerusalem to James and the presbyters. Do not neglect the
gatherings; seek all by name. Let no one despise your youth, but become a type
of the faithful in word and conduct. Do not be ashamed of household servants;
for the nature is common to us and to them. Do not abhor women; for they have
borne you and brought you up. One must therefore love the causes of the birth,
only in the Lord; and without a woman a man will not beget children; one must
therefore honor the fellow-workers of the birth. Neither is a man without a
woman nor a woman without a man, except in the case of the first-formed; for
the body of Adam was of the four elements, and that of Eve of the side of
Adam; and the paradoxical birth of the Lord was of the virgin alone, not the
lawful mixing being abominable, but the birth being God-befitting; for it was
fitting for the Demiurge not to make use of the customary birth, but of the
paradoxical and strange, as Demiurge. Flee pride; for the Lord arrays himself
against the proud. Abhor false-speaking; for you will destroy all who speak
the lie. Guard against envy; for its originator is the devil and its successor
Cain, who envied a brother and from envy worked murder. Advise my sisters to
love God and to be content only with their own husbands; likewise also advise
my brothers to be content with their yoke-fellows. Virgins

guard as treasures of Christ. Be long-suffering, that you may be much in
prudence. Do not neglect the poor, in whatever you may have plenty; for by
alms and faiths sins are cleansed. Keep yourself pure as a dwelling of God;
you are a temple of Christ, you are an instrument of the Spirit. You know how
I brought you up; even if I am least, become a zealot of me; imitate my
conduct. I do not boast in the world, but in the Lord. I advise Heron my
child: And let him who boasts boast in the Lord. May I have joy of you,
longed-for child, of whom may the only unbegotten God become a guard, and the
Lord Jesus Christ. Do not believe all, do not take courage in all, not even if
someone talks you down with endearments; for many are servants of Satan, and
he who trusts quickly is light in the heart. Remember God, and you will never
sin. Do not become double-souled in your prayer; for blessed is he who has not
doubted. For I believe in the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ and in his
only-begotten Son, that God will show me Heron upon my throne; add therefore
upon the course. I charge you upon the God of all and upon Christ, the Holy
Spirit also being present and the liturgical orders: guard my deposit, which I
and Christ have commended to you, and do not judge yourself unworthy of the
things that have seemed good concerning you to God. I commend to you the
church of the Antiochenes. To Polycarp I have commended you in the Lord Jesus
Christ. The bishops Onesimus, Bitus, Damas, Polybius greet you and all those
from Philippi in Christ, from where I also sent to you. Greet the
God-befitting presbytery, greet your holy fellow-deacons, of whom may I have
joy in Christ in flesh and in spirit. Greet the people of the Lord from small
unto great by name, whom I commend to you, as Moses to Jesus the general after
him. And let the thing said not seem heavy to you; for even if we are not such
as those, yet at least we pray to become so, since we are also children of
Abraham. Be strong then, O Heron, heroically and manfully; for you from now
will lead in and lead out the people of the Lord in Antioch, and the
congregation of the Lord will not be as sheep, for whom there is no shepherd.
Greet Cassian my host and his most solemn yoke-fellow and their dearest
children; to whom God will grant to find mercy from the Lord in that day for
the ministry toward us; whom I also commend to you in Christ. Greet all the
faithful in Laodicea by name in Christ. Do not neglect those in Tarsus, but
look upon them more continually, establishing them with the gospel. I greet
Maris the bishop in Neapolis by Zarbus in the Lord. And address also the most
solemn Mary my daughter the most learned and the church according to her
house, of whom may I become a ransom, the exemplar of pious women. May the
Father of Christ through the only-begotten himself keep you in health and
well-approved in all things for the longest time of life unto benefit of the
church of God. Farewell in the Lord and pray, that I may be perfected.

TO THE PHILIPPIANS.
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to a church of God shown mercy in faith and
endurance and unhypocritical love, which is in Philippi, mercy, peace from God
the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, who is Saviour of all men, especially of
believers. Remembering your love and your zeal in Christ, which you displayed
toward us, we have thought it fitting to write toward your brother-loving
psychic love according to God, to remind you of your course in Christ, that
you may all say the same thing, of one soul, thinking the one thing, walking
by the same canon of the faith, as also Paul was admonishing you. For if there
is one God of all, the Father of Christ, from whom

are all things, and one also our Lord Jesus Christ, the only-begotten Son of
God, the Lord of all, through whom are all things, and one also Holy Spirit
who worked in Moses and prophets and apostles, and one also the baptism given
into the death of the Lord, and one also the chosen church; one ought to be
also the faith according to Christ. For one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one
God and Father of all and through all and in all. One then God and Father, and
not two nor three; one who is, and there is not besides him, the only true.
For the Lord, he says, your God, the Lord is one, and again: Did not one God
create us, did not one Father of us all? And one also Son, Word God. For the
only-begotten, he says, who is into the bosom of the Father, and again: One
Lord Jesus Christ, and in another: What is his name or what is the name of the
Son, that we may know? And one also the Paraclete. For one, he says, also
Spirit, since we were called in one hope of our calling, and again: We were
given to drink one Spirit and the things next. And all these things, the gifts
clearly, one and the same Spirit works. Neither then three Fathers nor three
Sons nor three Paracletes, but one Father and one Son and one Paraclete.
Therefore also the Lord, sending the apostles to make disciples of all the
nations, commanded them to baptize into the name of the Father and of the Son
and of the Holy Spirit, neither into one three-named nor into three who became
man, but into three of equal honor. For one is he who became man, neither the
Father nor the Paraclete, but only the Son, not in seeming, not in fantasy but
in truth; for the Word became flesh, for Wisdom built herself a house. And God
the Word was born as a man with a body of the virgin without converse of a
man; for the virgin will take in womb and will bear a son. Truly then he was
born, truly he grew, truly he ate and drank, truly he was crucified and died
and rose. He who has believed these things, as they are, as they have come to
be, is blessed; he who has not believed these things is accursed, no less than
those who crucified the Lord. For the ruler of this world rejoices, whenever
anyone denies the cross; for he knows the confession of the cross to be his
own ruin. For this is the trophy against his power, which seeing he shudders
and hearing he fears. And before the cross came to be, he was eager for this
to come to be, and he worked in the sons of disobedience, he worked in Judas,
in Pharisees, in Sadducees, in old men, in young, in priests. But as it is
about to come to be he is thrown into tumult and casts regret into the
betrayer and shows him a noose and teaches hanging; and he also frightens the
little woman, troubling her in dreams, and tries to stop the things according
to the cross, he who moves every rope unto its construction; not repenting
over so great an evil (for if so, he would not have been all evil), but he
perceived his own destruction; for a beginning of condemnation for him is the
cross of Christ, a beginning of death, a beginning of destruction. Therefore
he also works in some to deny the cross, to be ashamed of the passion, to call
the death seeming, to cut away the birth from a virgin, to slander the nature
itself as abominable. He allies with Jews unto denial of the cross, with
Hellenes unto a slander of magic, with heretics unto fantasy. For the general
of wickedness is many-colored, mind-stealing, unstable, contrary to himself
and putting forward some things, but showing others; for he is wise to do
evil, but what the good is he does not know, he has been filled with ignorance
through willing madness. For how is he not such, who does not even see his own
word before his feet? For if the Lord is a mere man of soul and body, why do
you cut away the birth of the common nature of men? And why as something
paradoxical that has come to be upon a man do you call the passion seeming and
think the death of the mortal a glory? But if God and man, why do you call the
Lord of glory lawless, him who is by nature unchangeable? Why do you say the
lawgiver is lawless, him who does not have a human soul

having? The Word became flesh, the Word a man, not having dwelt in a man. And
how is this one also a magician, who of old constructed every sensible and
intelligible nature by mind of the Father, and in the becoming man healed
every disease and every infirmity? And how is this one not God, who raises
dead, sends lame away sound, cleanses lepers, gives eyes to the blind, either
increasing or changing the things that are, as the five loaves and the two
fishes and the water into wine, and putting your army to flight by a word
only? And why do you speak evil of the nature of the virgin and call the parts
shameful, of old parading these things and bidding males to be stripped unto
sight of females, and females unto unbridled desire of males? Now these things
have been thought shameful to you, and you pretend to be solemn, you the
spirit of fornication, not knowing that something becomes shameful then,
whenever it is defiled by lawlessness, and when sin is absent none of the
things that have come to be is shameful, nothing cheap, but all things very
good; and you, not seeing, speak evil of them? And how again does Christ no
longer seem to you to be of the virgin, but the God over all, he who is, the
Almighty? Who then is he who sent this one, say; who is he who is lord of this
one, and by mind of whom did this one obey, and of what laws has he become a
fulfiller, he who yields neither to mind of anyone nor to authority? And
taking Christ out of the birth, you legislate that the unbegotten has been
born and that the beginningless has been nailed to a cross; with whom having
conceded, I cannot say. But you have not escaped me of the shifting one, nor
do I not know that you walk crooked and twin; but you do not know who is he
who was born, you who pretend to know everything. For many things escape you;
the virginity of Mary, the paradoxical birth, who is he in the body, the
leading star of those in the east, of the magi bringing the gifts, greeting of
an archangel toward a virgin, paradoxical conception of a betrothed virgin,
heralding of a forerunner child upon him of the virgin and leaping in the womb
from him who was seen beforehand, hymns of angels upon the one born, good
tidings of shepherds, fear of Herod upon taking away of a kingdom, command of
infant-killing, migration into Egypt, return from there unto the things here,
childhood swaddling-clothes, human registration, milk-feeding, name of a
father who did not sow, a manger because there was no place, no human
preparation, progress of growth, human words, hunger, thirst, journeying,
toil, offerings of sacrifices, since also circumcision, baptism, voice of God
upon the one being baptized, who and from where, testimony of Spirit and of
God from above, voice of John a prophet signifying passion through the
appellation of the lamb, workings of various signs, varied healings, a
master's rebuke commanding sea and winds, evil spirits being put to flight,
you yourself being racked, being tortured from the power of him who appears,
not having what you may do. Seeing these things you grow dizzy, and that the
one who bore was a virgin, you did not know; but the hymnology of the angels
was striking you, the worship of the magi, the rising of the star. You run
back into ignorance because of the cheap things; for swaddling-clothes,
manger, circumcision, milk-feeding seem small to you; these things appear to
you unworthy according to God. Again you saw a man remaining forty days and
nights without tasting of human food, angels ministering, at whom you also
shuddered, having first seen him being baptized as a common man and not
knowing the cause; and after the fast, when he was hungry, you took courage
again and were tempting as a common man, not knowing who he was. For you were
saying: If you are Son of God, say, that these stones may become bread. For
the if you are Son of God is of ignorance; for if you truly knew, you were
trusting that for a Demiurge both to make what is not and to change what is is
equally possible. And through a belly you tempt him who feeds all who need
food, and you tempt the Lord of glory, having forgotten from ill-mind that a
man will not live upon bread only, but upon every word going out through a
mouth of God. For if you had known that

he was Son of God, you would have known that he who in forty days and equal
nights made the corruptible body without need was able also to do this unto
the continual. Why then does he hunger? that he may show that in truth he took
up a body of like passions with men. Through the first he showed that he is
God, and through the second, that he is also man. You then, who fell from the
highest glory as lightning, dare to say to the Lord: Throw yourself from here
down, to whom the things that are have been reckoned as not being, and to
challenge unto vainglory him who is not showing off? And you pretend to read
the scripture concerning him: That he will command his angels concerning you,
and they will lift you upon hands, of not striking your foot toward a stone?
And the remaining things you pretend not to know, stealing what he prophesied
concerning you and your attendants: Upon asp and basilisk you will step, and
you will trample lion and dragon? If then you are a trampling of the feet of
the Lord, how do you tempt the untemptable, having forgotten the lawgiver
commanding that you shall not tempt out the Lord your God, but you also dare,
most accursed, to appropriate the works of God and to say that the rule of
these things has been delivered to you; and you hold out your fall to the Lord
and you promise to give him his own things, if having fallen upon the earth he
worships you? And how did you not shudder to send forth such a voice against
the Master, O spirit more evil from ill-mind than all evil spirits? Through a
belly you were defeated and through vainglory you were dishonored, through
love of money and love of rule you are drawn into impiety. You Belial, the
dragon, the apostate, the crooked serpent, who stood away from God, who was
separated from Christ, who was made alien from the Holy Spirit, who was driven
out of the choir of the angels, the insulter of the laws of God, the enemy of
the lawful things, who rose against the first-formed and moved from the
commandment those who did you no injustice, who set the man-killing Cain upon
Abel, who made war upon Job, you say to the Lord: If having fallen you worship
me? O the daring, O the madness; a runaway slave, a slave for the whip, having
shaken off the good Master, to so great a Master, God of all intelligible and
sensible things, you say: If having fallen you worship me? But the Lord is
long-suffering and does not take away into not-being him who from ignorance is
so bold, but answers meekly: Go, Satan. He did not say: Go behind me (for he
is not able to turn back), but: Go, Satan, in the things you chose; go, in the
things you were provoked from ill-mind; for I know who I am and by whom I have
been sent and I understand whom one must worship. For the Lord, he says, your
God you shall worship and him only you shall serve. I know the one, I
understand the only, of whom you have become an apostate. I am not an
anti-god, I confess the superiority, and I do not decline to worship that one,
whom I understand as cause of my birth and Lord and guard of subsistence; for
I live through the Father. These things, brothers, from a disposition toward
you I was compelled to send unto glory of God, advising, not being something,
but as a brother. Be subject to the bishop, to the presbyters, to the deacons;
love one another in the Lord as images of God. See, you husbands, cherish the
wives as your own members; wives, as being one by the union, cherish your own
husbands. If anyone is pure or continent, let him not lift himself up, that he
may not lose the reward. Do not dishonor the feasts; do not set at nought the
forty days (for it contains an imitation of the manner of life of the Lord)
and do not overlook the week of the passion; fast the fourth and the
preparation, supplying the abundance to the poor. If anyone fasts a Lord's day
or a sabbath except one sabbath, this one is a Christ-killer. May your prayers
be stretched out unto the church of Antioch, from where I also am being led
bound unto Rome. I greet the holy bishop Polycarp, I greet the holy bishop
Vitalius and the sacred presbytery and my fellow-servants the deacons; of whom
may I become a ransom. I still take leave of the

bishop and the presbyters in the Lord; if anyone accomplishes the pascha with
Jews or receives the symbols of their feast, he is a partner of those who
killed the Lord and his apostles. Philon and Agathopus the deacons greet you.
I greet the company of the virgins, the order of the widows; of whom may I
also have joy. I greet the people of the Lord from small unto great. I have
sent you these my letters through Euphanius the reader, a man God-honored and
most faithful, having met about Rhegium, putting to sea in a ship. Remember my
bonds, that I may be perfected in Christ. Farewell in flesh, soul, spirit,
thinking perfect things, turning away the workers of lawlessness and
corrupters of the word of truth, being empowered in the grace of our Lord
Jesus Christ.
