* Scholia on the Apocalypse
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Since, with the report running about concerning our training, there came to
me, devoted to the word, sometimes heretics, sometimes those from Greek
studies and especially those in philosophy, it seemed good to examine both the
doctrines of the heretics and the things promised by the philosophers
concerning truth. And we have done this imitating both Pantaenus who before us
benefited many, who had no little preparation in those things, and Heraclas
now sitting in the presbytery of the Alexandrians, whom I found with the
teacher of philosophical studies, already having persisted with him five years
before I began to hear those words; because of whom, formerly using common
clothing, having stripped it off and taken up a philosopher's habit he keeps
it until now and does not cease, as far as he can, studying the books of the
Greeks.
