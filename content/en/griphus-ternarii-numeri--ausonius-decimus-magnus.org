There lay hidden among my trifles an undistinguished little book; and
would that it had stayed hidden and not perished by its own evidence
like a shrew-mouse. This I, when like Euclio's cock I had dug it out of
the mould of papery dust, shook out and reread, and like a greedy
money-lender I preferred to put a bad coin into circulation rather than
to store it away; then thinking with myself, not that Catullan thing, /
to whom do I give a charming new little book,/ but ἀμουσότερον and
truer: / to whom do I give an uncharming, rude little book,/ I did not
seek long, for you came to meet me, whom I, if the power of choosing
from all were mine, would always have chosen as the one. I have sent
you therefore these frivolities, vainer than Sicilian nonsense, that
when you are doing nothing you may read them and, lest you do nothing,
you may defend them. Therefore this trifling little book, already long
torn by a reading secret indeed, but of the crowd, will at last come
into your hands. Which you will either, like Aesculapius, restore to
life, or like Plato with Vulcan helping free from infamy, if it ought
not to come to fame. Now the matter of this little ineptitude was this:
on campaign, a time which, as you know, is of military licence, over my
table an invitation was made, not that from Rubrius's banquet, that one
should drink in the Greek manner,1 but that from Flaccus's eclogue, in
which because of /midnight/ and /the new moon/ and /Murena's
augurate/ /the astonished bard demands thrice three cups./ This
passage on the number three at once that poetic itch of ours began to
work out: and since the contagion of this disease is easy, would that
the itch may migrate to you too, and with the dye of your correction
added you may clap on the sponge which may finish the imperfect work of
a badly foaming horse. And lest you not know me as a boaster, the
little verses begun amid lunching I finished before the time of dinner,
that is, while I drink and a little before I was drinking. Let the
weighing therefore be according to the matter and the time, but you too
read this very thing a little merrier and more diluted; for it is
unjust for an abstemious reader to judge of a badly sober poet.

Nor does it escape me that there will be someone who with sharp
nostrils and wrinkled brow will condemn this jest of ours and deny that
I have touched all things which pertain to the numbers three and nine.
Whom I will confess to speak truly, justly, I will deny, for if he is
good, let him judge that what I omitted was not forgotten by me but
passed over; then of whatever sort he is, let him think with himself
how many of these he would not have found if he himself had sought, let
him know also that I have neither used all things dug out nor abused
certain that were offered. For how many things about the ternary I
knowingly nec-lego! tenses and persons, genders and degrees, the nine
natural metres with trimeters, the whole of grammar and music and the
books of medicine, thrice-greatest Hermes and the first lover of
philosophy and Varro's numbers and whatever the profane crowd does not
know. Last, which is easy, when he himself has found many things, let
him compare himself and me, the busy with the idle, the one who has
lunched with the abstemious, my jest and play, his diligence and
cavil. For one can find more than another: no one all.

But if I shall seem obscure to anyone as well, you will defend me
before him thus: first that epyllia of this sort, unless they are even
obscure, will be nothing; then that the nature of numbers is not a
rush, so that they should be without a knot: last, if I shall have been
obscure even to you, to whom nothing has been either unread or not
understood, then truly I, blessed, will attain what I have aimed at,
that you seek me, that you miss me, that you think of me. Farewell.

** Riddle of the Number Three
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:

Drink thrice or so many times threes: so the mystic law is,
either for one drinking three or multiplying thrice three,
with odd nines of threes to weave a coebum .
The same right is to three as to thrice three: all things are in these;
the form of man begun and the completion of full birth
and he who nine times nine holds the last end of fate,
three brothers begotten of Ops, three born in order,

Vesta, Ceres and Juno, of female sex, sisters.
thence are Jove's three-forked thunderbolts, Cerberus thence,
thence the trident and Helen's triple egg with her brothers.
thrice new purple has filled Nestor's spindles
and so many times the crow more long-lived. than a trine age.
whom nine times three, gathering the courses of ages,
bronze-footed stags three times a team of Nestors surpass,
whose three ages the Phoebian singer-bird surpasses,
whom nine times older the Gangetic bird precedes,
a bird rayed at the temples from a cinnamon nest.
Hecate is threefold, three are the faces of the virgin Diana;
three Graces, three Fates, a triple voice, three elements,
three Siredones in Trinacria; all things threefold:
three birds, three half-goddesses, three half-girls,
bid to contend for the palm with thrice three Camenae,
singing with mouth, hand, breath, boxwood, lyre, voice,
three parts of wisdom, three Punic wars, three-monthly
turns of years and of heaven and through the shadow of night
threefold watchmen, thrice the clear signs of approaching Dawn
the late attendant sings, Mars having been caught.

and he who, conceived on the eve of a tripled night,
fixed the commanded rich spoils on four times three trophies.
And the lyric bards are in the number of the Mnemosynae,
which once the right hand of Phoebus held as three only:
but Cithaeron so many times three from bronze consecrated
by the religion of the fathers, who feared to have spurned six,
the triple three-nights celebrated in the Tarentine game,
such as the trieteric rites at Thebes for twice-born Bacchus.
the three first Thracian fights in three wars in order
the Iuniadae sent as offerings to their father's tomb,
she also seeking marriage-chambers through three riddles,
who would be two-footed and four-footed and three-footed, all things alone,
terrified Aonia, a bird, a lion, a threefold maiden
sphinx, a bird in feathers, a beast in feet, a girl in brow.
Triple partnerships gleam in the Tarpeian temple,
three kinds of arts make human dwellings:
he who places stones in walls, he who [places] beams on the roof,
and he who combs the plaster with the last finish.
hence Bromius's quadrantal and hence the Sicanian medimna:
this for three, this for twin threes the use of doing unfolds.

in physics three first things, god, world, given form:
three kinds of all-born, begetter, begetter-mother, begotten.
Through three species the rule of triangles runs,
equilateral or with equal leg or unequal in all.
the perfect number,1 comes together into three parts, so that the same
by a gathered thrice-team through thrice three is dissolved.
three first has even, odd, and a middle: but itself,
as three, so five and seven also, one divides;
and placed in the whole number under the point of the centre
it distinguishes solid thirds with the coebus going on 2,
separating equal parts from an odd team:
and for the evens a triple middle, when four and six
and twice of fours the same navel is separated.
A triple law, which thrice four tables sanctioned:
sacred, private, and the people's common which is anywhere.

a threefold kind of interdicts: whence I shall have been driven
by force or where it shall have been or of whose goods,
triple liberty and a triple lessening of the caput.
there is a threefold kind of speaking: sublime, modest
and of thin thread. a triple form also of healing,
to which logos or methodos and to which experience is the name,
and medicine is triple: to preserve, to beware, to heal,
three cultivations of orators; him whom Rhodes ruled by the Colossus,
whom you, Actaean Athens, loved
and whom from the stages grim to the benches
Asia's prose dragged, imitating in causes the measures of choruses.
hence Orpheus's tripods, because there are three, earth, water, flame.
a triple placing for the stars, distance, form.
and measure and the mother of measures, music is triple,
mixed in books, secret in stars, published in theatres.
Martial Rome is triple: by cavalry, plebs, senate,
by this number the tribes and the tribunes from the sacred mountain.

three troops of knights, three names of the more noble.
there are three names for strings, there are three names for a month.
triple Geryons, triple joining of the Chimaera:
triple Scylla, joined from three: dog, maiden, fish.
Gorgons and Harpalycae and Erinyes in a team-line,
and three fate-speaking Sibyls, a common name,
whose threefold books are fatal songs,
which the care of thrice five men keeps.
Drink thrice. three is a number above all, three is one god.
lest this play also run through with a sluggish number,
let it have thrice ten times three and ten times nine.
