But on this it is good to seal the word of our holy father the bishop. For who
is so foolish and altogether shameless of piety (sic, pour apeschummenos), as
not to bear throughout in heart-bearing memory the shadow-bearing man of our
father among the saints, etc., fol. 61 v. 2 Well the "was found"; for she is a
worthy myrrh-vessel of grace, having received the rank of heaven, found an
incorruptible ark, she who contained the uncontainable Word by an uncomposed
word, and received the tablets of the Gospels, fol. 60 r.
