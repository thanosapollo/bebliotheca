* Fragments on the Second Epistle to the Corinthians

2 Cor 1:9 Or thus: of ourselves, he says, we despaired altogether, that we
might have the hope upon God alone; for he who beforehand despairs of himself
how would he wish to be saved by his own counsel or power or defense, so that
as a consequence he gives himself wholly over to God. 2 Cor 2:14 For we are a
censer, he says, of this myrrh and heavenly incense, carrying about its
fragrance everywhere. 2 Cor 3:5 He spoke concerning the same. 2 Cor 3:13-14
And why, as if someone said: you preach unveiledly and do not speak in a
shadowed
way after the manner of a veil. Why? First, being confident in the purity of
the preaching. Then because you also are able to see the beauty that is in the
preaching; for you do not see dimly equally with the Jews. For by how much the
new is greater, by so much also is the difference of those being discipled. 2
Cor 5:1 He calls the body an earthly house of a tent. 2 Cor 5:17 What ancient
things? Either the sins, he says, and the impieties, or all the Jewish things.
2 Cor 5:21 Others interpreted thus: sin is called the sacrifice for sins, as
also the prophet: they shall eat the sins of my people, that is the sacrifices
for sins. The Father therefore made the Son a sacrifice for sins. 445 2 Cor
7:12 And he does not say this, that I did not write on account of those, but
that I did not write primarily because of those, but both because of you and
because of those. 2 Cor 8:9 If then that one, he says, became poor for you, do
you not even give money for him? 2 Cor 8:18 These things can also be
understood of Luke. 2 Cor 8:24 Unto the face, he says, of the churches, that
is also unto the honor of the churches that sent them; for if you honor these,
you honored
the ones that sent them. And he does not say "of men" but of churches, making
the word at once more solemn and inescapable. 2 Cor 11:9 Or also thus: and
these things, he says, being in want I did not grow numb, nor was I negligent,
or become more slothful toward the preaching, but from me whatever was needed
came about for your salvation; and I spent my time in want. 2 Cor 11:25 You
see that not all things which Paul suffered did Luke write for us in the
Acts. Many then of the things said here he omitted; for the things of the
writing were
not labored by him toward ambition. 2 Cor 12:4 Where are they who composed the
falsely-inscribed apocalypse? 2 Cor 12:20 That is, punishing those who are
incurably sick. Such as wranglings. 446 2 Cor 12:21 Where are the Novatians? 2
Cor 13:2 To those who have sinned, that they may be corrected, and to all the
rest, that they may bear witness that, he says, it is nothing on my part, if I
punish those who have not repented. 2 Cor 13:4 Understand this likewise also
with the above: but he lives from the power of God. And the "unto you" as "we
shall live for you", that is unto your benefit, or because of you, since, he
says, we converted you, or we shall live with you then.
