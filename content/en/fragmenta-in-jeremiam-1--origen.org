1.1 {And as all the gifts of God are exceedingly greater than the mortal
subsistence, so also the exact word of the wisdom concerning all these things,
being with God who also arranged these things to be written, if the Father of
the Word wishes, would come to be in the soul purified to the extreme with all
munificence and a co-perception of the human weakness concerning the grasping of
wisdom. And if someone should give himself over more rashly, not having seen
together the unspeakable of the wisdom of God and of the Word "in the beginning"
"toward God", himself also being God, and that according to the Word and God,
and according to the wisdom with him, these things are both to be sought and to
be found, such a one, falling out into mythologies and babblings and
fabrications, of necessity subjects himself to the danger concerning impiety.
Wherefore one must also remember the command concerning such things in Solomon
in Ecclesiastes, saying: "do not hasten to bring out a word before the face of
God; because God is in the heaven above, and you upon the earth below; for this
reason let your words be few."} And it is fitting to believe that the holy
letters have no tittle empty of the wisdom of God; for he who commanded me the
man and says: "you shall not appear before me empty", much more himself will say
nothing empty. For taking "from his fullness" the prophets speak; wherefore all
things of them breathe from fullness; and there is nothing in prophecy or law or
gospel or apostle which is not from fullness. For this reason since it is from
fullness, it breathes of the fullness to those who have eyes seeing the things
of the fullness, and ears hearing the things from fullness, and a sense-organ of
the fragrance of those breathing from fullness. And if ever reading the
scripture you strike upon a thought being a fair stone of striking and a rock of
scandal, blame yourself. For do not despair that this stone of striking and the
rock of scandal have thoughts so as to become according to what is said: "and
the one who believes will not be put to shame". Believe first, and you will find
under the supposed scandal much holy benefit.
