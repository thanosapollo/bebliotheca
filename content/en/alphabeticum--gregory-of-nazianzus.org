* Alphabet
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Wing your mind upward toward your God.
Desire a life from there made splendid.
Walking the earth, do not think the things of earth.
Give a hand to the poor man lying below.
Desire the best work, made splendid.
Seek the delights of the hopes from there.
The tongue of the worthless does not even speak the false.
Do not become a hunt of the dragons of the demons.
Do not travel at all the tracks of the profane.
Weep, groan; wash filths with tears.
Honor the words of the wise and become wise.
Love not the praises, but the blames.
Make yourself a sanctified temple.
Entertain strangers, and feed them in a manner fit for guests.
Abhor oaths, highwaymen of the soul.
Hate wealth and the delights of life.
Think the flowing world as like a spider's web.
Melt the mind of the flesh with godly toils.
Wear the armor of light instead of purple.
See that, lifting yourself, you do not fall greatly.
Bend your neck also in the fear of God.
Go rejoicing to the dwelling above.
Wish to be blamed by another rather than to blame.
O child, keep these things and you will be great.
