* Fragment on Saint Paul's First Epistle to the Corinthians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of the same from the 7th of the exegetical tomes of the former epistle to the
Corinthians:
For as far as it pertains to its own nature, the human body has been brought
under death and corruption. But it is overshadowed with immortality, God
accomplishing the matter in whatever way he would know; thus we say the first
man was made in the beginning. But when he became accursed, having neglected
the commandment defined for him, he stripped off incorruption and the wrapping
of divine glory; for they knew thus that they were naked. And at the time of
the resurrection there will be a taking-up-again of the goods in the
beginning; for the flesh will live again, clothed with immortality and
enriching itself with the wrapping of divine glory.
