PRAYERS, THAT IS, EXORCISMS OF BASIL THE GREAT FOR THOSE
SUFFERING UNDER DEMONS, AND FOR EVERY ILLNESS.
God of gods, and Lord of lords, the creator of the fiery ranks
and the craftsman of the immaterial powers, the artisan of the heavenly and of the
earthly, "6whom no one among men has seen, nor =31.1680= is able to see,"6 whom
all creation fears and trembles before, who once tore down to the earth the
commander who had grown stiff-necked and who by disobedience had rejected his
ministry, and who tartarized into the darkness of the abyss the angels that
apostatized with him and became demons, grant that this exorcism, performed upon
your dread name, may take place in fear for him who is the leader of wickedness,
and for all his phalanxes that fell out together with him from the light-bearing
realm above, and turn him to flight, and command him and his demons to withdraw
completely, so that he may work nothing harmful against the sealed image; but let
these who have been sealed receive strength of might to tread upon serpents and
scorpions and upon all the power of the enemy. For yours is hymned, and
magnified, and by every breath is glorified in fear the all-holy name of the
Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages
of ages. Amen. {1Another prayer of the same.}1 Let us beseech the Lord. I
exorcize you, the first-evil one of blasphemy, the leader of rebellion, and the
author of wickedness. I exorcize you who were cast out from the light-bearing
realm above, and brought down into the darkness of the abyss because of your
pride. I exorcize you and all the fallen power that follows your choice. I adjure
you, unclean spirit, by the God of Sabaoth, and all the host of the angels of
God, Adonai, Eloi, God Almighty: come out, and withdraw from this servant of
God. I exorcize you by the God who created all things by a word, and by our Lord
Jesus Christ, his only-begotten Son, who before the ages was begotten of him
ineffably and without passion, who created the invisible and visible creation,
who formed man according to his own image, who first schooled these things by
natural law, and guarded them by the oversight of angels, who flooded sin from
above with water, and opened the abysses under heaven, and destroyed the giants
who acted impiously, and shook down the tower of the profane, and reduced the
land of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes with divine fire, of which an unresting smoke
is exhaled as witness, who split the sea with the rod, and caused the people to
pass through with unwetted feet, and destroyed forever in the waves Pharaoh the
tyrant and the God-fighting army, the war of impiety, who in the last times was
incarnate from a pure Virgin unspeakably and kept the seals of purity intact, who
was well-pleased to wash by baptism the ancient stain which we had drawn upon
ourselves through transgression.
I exorcize you by him who was baptized in the Jordan, and who by grace grants us
in water a type of incorruption, at whom angels and all the powers of the heavens
were astonished, seeing the incarnate God having condescended, when the
beginningless Father revealed the beginningless generation =31.1681= of the Son,
and the descent of the Holy Spirit bore witness to the unity of the Trinity. I
adjure you by him who rebuked the wind, and calmed the surge of the sea, who
drove out the troop of demons, and fitted clay so that those lacking the pupils
of the eyes from the womb, being blind, might look up, and who renewed the
ancient fashioning of our race, and restored speech to the speechless, who wiped
away the marks of leprosy, and raised the dead from the tombs, who consorted with
men even unto burial, and by his rising plundered Hades, and made all humanity
invulnerable to death. I adjure you by God Almighty, who inspired men with the
God-breathed voice, and co-operated with the apostles, and filled all the
inhabited world with piety. Be afraid, flee, run away, withdraw, unclean and
unholy demon, subterranean, of the abyss, deceitful, formless, visible through
shamelessness, invisible through hypocrisy, wherever you may be, or wherever you
go; whether you yourself are Beelzebub, or the shaker, or dragon-like, or
beast-faced, or as vapor, or as a bird, or night-speaking, or deaf, or mute, or
terrifying by assault, or tearing, or plotting, or in heavy sleep, or in disease,
or in infirmity, or rambling in laughter, or producing pleasure-loving tears, or
lustful, or foul-smelling, or desirous, or pleasure-seeking, or fond of sorcery,
or love-mad, or of astral magic, or dwelling in houses, or shameless, or
contentious, or unstable, or changing with the moon, or turning with some season,
or of the morning, or of midday, or of midnight, or of some untimely hour, or of
dawn, or you met of your own accord, or you were sent by someone, or you
approached suddenly, or in the sea, or in a river, or under the earth, or of a
well, or a cliff, or from a pit, or a lake, or a reed-bed, or a wood, or from the
land, or from something unclean, or a grove, or a thicket, or a tree, or a bird,
or thunder, or from the roof of a bath, or in a pool of waters, or from an
idolatrous tomb, or from where we know, and do not know, known, or unknown, and
from an unforeseen place: be divided and be gone, be ashamed before the image
fashioned and formed by the hand of God. Fear the likeness of the incarnate God,
and do not hide yourself in this servant of God, but an iron rod and a furnace of
fire, and Tartarus, and the gnashing of teeth, the penalty of disobedience, await
you.
Be afraid, be muzzled, flee, do not turn back, do not hide yourself together with
other wickedness of unclean spirits; but go away into a waterless land, desert,
uncultivated, which man does not inhabit, which God alone oversees, who binds all
who envy and plot against his image, and who tartarized with chains of gloom into
a long night and day you who have been tried in all evils, and inventor, devil.
For great is the fear of God, and great is the glory of the Father, and of the
Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen. {1
Another prayer of the same.}1 God of the heavens, God of the lights, God of the
angels who are under your strength, God of the archangels who are under your
might, God of the =31.1684= glorious dominions, God of the saints, the Father of
our Lord Jesus Christ, who loosed the souls bound by death, who through your
only-begotten Son enlightened the man nailed to the darkness, who loosed our
pangs, and scattered every burden, who separated from us every assault of the
enemy, Son and Word of God, who immortalized us by your death, and glorified us
with your glory, who by your rising granted that we pass from men to God, who
bore every bond of our sins through your cross, who took up our bruise, and
healed it, Lord, who made a way for us into the heavens, and transformed
corruption into incorruption: hear me who cry out to you with longing and fear,
you before whose fear the mountains melt together with the firmament under
heaven, at whose power the speechless souls of the elements shudder to keep their
own bounds, through whom the fire of vengeance will not overstep the bounds
appointed to it,
but waits groaning for your counsel; through whom all creation travails, groaning
with unutterable groanings, having been commanded to remain until the times, from
whom every opposing nature has fled, and the army of the enemy has been subdued,
and the devil has fallen, and the serpent has been trampled, and the dragon has
been slain; through whom the nations that confessed you were enlightened, and
were strengthened in you, Lord; through whom life has been manifested, hope has
been established, the faith has prevailed, the Gospel has been preached; through
whom man from earth has been refashioned, having believed in you.
For who is as you, God Almighty? Therefore we beseech you, God of the fathers,
and Lord of mercy, pre-eternal, and beyond being: receive him who has come to you
upon your holy name, and that of your beloved Child Jesus Christ, and of your
holy and all-powerful and life-giving Spirit; drive away from his soul every
infirmity, every unbelief, every unclean spirit, tearing, subterranean, fiery,
foul-smelling, desirous, gold-loving, money-loving, Typhonic, fornicating, every
unclean demon, dark, formless, shameless. Yes, O God, drive away from your
servant, so-and-so, every operation of the devil, every magic, every sorcery,
idolatry, astromancy, astrology, necromancy, bird-augury, sensuality, eros, love
of money, drunkenness, fornication, adultery, licentiousness, shamelessness,
anger, contention, instability, and every evil suspicion. Yes, Lord our God,
breathe into him your peaceful Spirit; so that being guarded by it he may produce
the fruit of faith, virtue, wisdom, purity, self-control, love, kindness, hope,
meekness, long-suffering, patience, temperance, understanding; because he has
been called your servant upon the name of Jesus Christ, believing in the
consubstantial Trinity, with angels, archangels, glorious dominions, and all the
heavenly host bearing witness together. Together with this, guard our hearts; for
you are mighty, Lord, and to you we send up the glory, to the Father, and to the
Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
