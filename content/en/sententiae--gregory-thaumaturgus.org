Piety must be cared for above all, which they call mother of the virtues,
speaking rightly; for this is beginning and end of the virtues. 2 To know God
and the cohesive and productive cause of all things, both sight is a teacher
and the natural law; the one striking upon things seen and established, the
other through the very things seen and ordered reasoning the leader of these.
3 Better is open-air faith than costly piety, and three gathered in the name
of the Lord than many denying godhead. 4 Nothing is so magnificent to God as a
purified word and a soul perfected in the doctrines of truth. 5 The only
unchangeable and remaining good is piety. 6 For the unattainable is not
somehow credible because of envy; for to profane souls nothing of the
beautiful is trustworthy.
