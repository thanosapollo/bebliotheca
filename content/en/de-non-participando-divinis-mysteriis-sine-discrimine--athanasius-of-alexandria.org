OF SAINT ATHANASIUS CONCERNING NOT COMMUNICATING UNEXAMINED OF THE DIVINE
MYSTERIES THOSE WHO MIX WITH THEIR OWN WIVES
5 Concerning not communicating unexamined of the divine mysteries those who mix
with their own wives.
Since some after having come together with their own wives on that same day
unobservantly partake of the fearful mysteries of the undefiled body and of the
life-giving blood of Christ our God, let them hear what is laid in the divine
scripture concerning this, and then let them do what seems to them. "And Moses
said to the people: Become ready; three days do not come to a woman"; and these
things the prophet Moses commanded by a command of God. And if for the sake of
only hearing a voice of the Lord he commanded a purification of so many days,
how much more now is it fitting for someone to keep, when he is about to receive
the undefiled body of God himself? And again similarly from the 1st book. "And
the priest answered David and said: There are not profane breads under my hand,
but they are holy; if the little children have been kept from a woman, they will
also eat. And David answered the priest and said to him. We have kept from a
woman yesterday and the third day; in my going out into a way all the little
children have become purified. And Abimelech gave him the breads of the
setting-forth". And if upon the "shadow", that is the old, there was such a
declining, so that someone coming together with his own wife not eat from the
breads of the setting-forth, which are far and incomparably distant from the
life-giving mysteries of the undefiled body and blood of Christ the true God,
how much more now in the "grace", where we are demanded a much greater exactness
of way of life, ought we to purify ourselves, being about to partake of such
fearful mysteries?
