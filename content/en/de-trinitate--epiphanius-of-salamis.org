* On the Trinity
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

From Epiphanius of Cyprus, from the discourse on the Trinity.
There is one doxology of Father and Son and Holy Spirit, and one worship; for
when the seraphim glorify God, "holy, holy, holy," they glorify Father and Son
and Holy Spirit.
