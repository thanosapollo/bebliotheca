* Scholia on the Song of Songs
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Upon flocks of your companions as one wandering. He himself toward her: Know
yourself first, that you may also be able to know me. If you do not know
yourself; if you have not known; if you do not know, go out you on heels
according to tracks, and shepherd your kids; and you will nourish sins instead
of virtues. Upon tents of the shepherds; in the desires of the contrary powers
that shepherd you. I called him, and he did not obey me. I will arise and I will
go around in the city. Of Athanasius. I will read the law, I will read the
prophets, I will search the Gospels. And these things she says, having been
pricked by the divine longing: Upon my bed 27.1349 in nights. She names a bed
the more perfect partaking of the good, and she calls night the time of the bed.
And through the name of the night she indicates the contemplation of the unseen,
according to a likeness of Moses, who came to be in the gloom in which God was.
Of the same. For I did not know who it was who wounded me. The Word sowed
knowledge of God in me sleeping. Having quickly arisen, I seek the one who
wounded, and I entreat him to appear to me, and I go around seeking my brother's
son. Go out and see, daughters of Zion, in the king Solomon. She puts Jews to
shame saying, Go out, instead of, Until when do you sit beside the shadow? for
behold from Judah the Lord has risen, a love from daughters of Jerusalem. The
solid of the reasonings, or the inner holy stones. In the crown with which his
mother crowned him. For you have set, he says, upon his head a crown from a
precious stone. Let my brother's son come down into his garden. Breathe upon me,
O Word, and teach me your oracles, and write your commands upon the breadth of
my heart. I have become ready henceforth; I am able, helped by your grace, and
steered by your unseen and almighty hand, both to hear divine words, and to
become a partaker of mysteries.
