THE DISCOURSE ON THE INSCRIPTIONS OF THE STEPS
33 The Father does not receive an acquired glory, being perfect, infinite,
incomprehensible, in need of no beauty and of every kind of seemliness. Nor
does his Word, being God who was begotten from him, through whom angels and
heavens and the infinite magnitudes of earth came to be and all the materials
and constitutions of generated things together; but the man of Christ, being
raised from the dead, is exalted and glorified, the adversaries having taken
up shame openly. 34 And those who took up hatred against him by envy and were
fenced with a hostile array are scattered, when the Word and God had raised
his own temple worthily.
