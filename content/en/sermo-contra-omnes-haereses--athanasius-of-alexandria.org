OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS ATHANASIOS AGAINST ALL THE HERESIES.
The thing set before us is from the Gospel according to John: "In the
beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and God was the Word. This
was in =28.504= the beginning with God. All things came to be through him,
and without him came to be not one thing that has come to be." Each of the
holy prophets and evangelists, being farsighted, and being moved by the Holy
Spirit, narrates the whole holy and great salvation, the one about to come to
pass for the race of men. But someone will say: How? Explain to us. Hear,
beloved. The prophets indeed, being farsighted, as they foretold, signify the
things about to be before so many generations, pointingly showing the things
to come as present. Likewise also the great evangelists, at the sojourn of
the Word, each confirms in the Gospels the signification of the things
proclaimed beforehand through the prophets. For instance: the great prophet
Isaiah says: "Like a sheep he was led to the slaughter, and like a lamb
before the one shearing him silent, so he opens not his mouth. And in his
humiliation his judgment was taken away. And who shall declare his
generation? By his bruise we all were healed." And when the one signified
beforehand had sojourned to us, and the great Baptist John had seen him
passing by, fulfilling the thing signified beforehand through Isaiah the
prophet, he says: "Behold the Lamb of God, the one taking away the sin of the
world." Behold, the great Baptist confirms the prophesied voice. Likewise
also Moses the hierophant, concerning this bruise, through which the whole
race of men was about to become partaker of salvation, what did he say?
"Cursed is everyone that hangs on a tree;" and again he says: "You shall see
your life hanging on a tree." He did not say first the life, then the curse;
but he said first the curse, then the life. For it was fitting that first the
handwriting be nailed to the cross, and the side of the Word be pierced with
the spear, that the bruise, in the piercing, become a cleansing to the
inhabited world, the blood and the water to the race of men. Wretches, the
ungrateful Jews! such and so great signs and benefactions having come to pass
among them, instead of glorifying the one who sojourned among them and
blessing him for the things being done, from the things contrary they said:
"If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross." But the sun drew in
its rays, not bearing to see the Lord hanging on the gibbet. And two robbers
were crucified =28.505= with him; and of the one cursing, the other robber
said: "Why do you curse the righteous one? we suffer according to our own
sins; what is it to you and the righteous one?" To Jesus he said: "Remember
me, Lord, whenever you come in your kingdom." And Jesus to him: "Amen, amen I
say to you, today with me you shall be in paradise." O the great thing! the
curse nailed, he opened paradise to the robber in the promise. O this blessed
robber! he plundered in the world robbing, and he plundered also the kingdom
of the heavens in the confession. Concerning this the great prophet said:
"And a scarab out of wood shall cry aloud." You know, brothers, that the
scarab lingers about unclean things, being unclean; so also the robber once
lingered in his robberies; but when he was on the cross, he confessed him, as
I said before, and the prophesied thing is fulfilled in him: "And a scarab
out of wood cries aloud." Having cried aloud, then, he was counted worthy


to seize beforehand the kingdom of the heavens. And again the great prophet
Isaiah says, pointingly showing as present: "Behold, the Virgin shall have in
the womb, and shall bear a Son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel," which
is, being interpreted, "With us God." This prophetic signification then being
about to be fulfilled, the great archangel Gabriel is sent, the one standing
at the right of the majesty, to signify to the spotless Theotokos and ever-
unmarried Mary, and he says to her: "Hail, full of grace; the Lord is with
you." And saying Lord, he signified the common Master, confessing the fellow-
servitude. "The Holy Spirit shall come upon you, and the power of the Highest
shall overshadow you; wherefore also the thing begotten from you shall be
called Holy." Truly, beloved, holy is called the temple of the one about to
sanctify the whole inhabited world. And again the same prophet says: "A child
is born to us, and a son is given to us, whose government is upon his
shoulder. And his name is called Angel of Great Counsel." These things then
having been fulfilled, the great evangelist John the son of thunder says: "In
the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and God was the Word.
This was in the beginning with =28.508= God. All things through him came to
be, and without him came to be not one." If through him all things came to
be, then he is outside the things generated, and it is evident that he was in
the Father always. Wretches, the new Jews, the Arianomaniacs! not reading
rightly, nor searching exactly, having the eyes of the mind blind, they think
the maker and craftsman of all to be a creature. Hear the great evangelist's
"In the beginning." O Christ-fighting Arian, if he had said, In the beginning
he came to be, you would have had occasion to say: Behold, the evangelist
said: In the beginning he came to be. But since he said: "In the beginning
was," search exactly, and you will find the truth; then question the great
evangelist, saying to him: O evangelist, tell us the transaction of the
beginning, and of the God Word that is; for Christ-fighting Arians think the
maker of all to be a creature; straightway the great evangelist will tell
you: Ascend to the Genesis of the world-making, speaking thus: "In the
beginning God made the heaven and the earth." Let then the things that are
not come into being, into the things about to be. The God Word was with the
Father, through whom all things came to be. Not then rightly does the
evangelist say: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God,
and God was the Word." Behold, threefold the "was," nowhere the "was not."
And the great archangel Gabriel, when he said to the spotless and ever-
unmarried Theotokos: "The Holy Spirit shall come upon you, and the power of
the Highest shall overshadow you," he did not say that a Spirit will come to
be upon you, or that a power will be made upon you, but, "The Holy Spirit
shall come upon you, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow you." This
also the blessed Apostle made clear, saying: "Christ the power of God, and
the wisdom of God." But to these things the Arianomaniac will say, that Why
does the Preacher say that "The Lord created me the beginning of his ways for
his works?" O most impious one, did you not hear the archangel saying to the
Virgin: "The thing begotten from you shall be called Holy?" You see that he
spoke concerning the transaction of the incarnation. But if you wish to hear
concerning the indivisible and one Godhead, hear Paul saying: "Who being the
radiance of the glory and the character of


his hypostasis;" and again he says: "Who, subsisting in the form of God, did
not consider the being equal with God a thing seized, but emptied himself,
taking the form of a servant." You see that such voices are concerning the
incarnation. And the Lord himself said with authoritative voice: "I in the
Father, and the Father in me;" and again he says to Philip: "I and the Father
are one." And he says through the great prophet: "Behold me, that I am, and I
am not changed." And again he says: "I came forth from the Father, and am
come." And through Jeremiah the Father says: "Me they forsook, a fountain of
living water, and they dug for themselves broken cisterns, which cannot
gather to themselves water." Not yesterday the fountain, and today the water;
may it not be! Always fountain, always water. Wretches, the ungrateful
Arianomaniacs! better than these was found Nebuchadnezzar, who, thinking
himself to be God, and setting up an image of himself in Babylon, and casting
into the furnace of fire Ananias, and Azarias, and Misael; and the word of
cooling coming to be in the midst of them, and driving out the fire, and they
hymning; and Nebuchadnezzar coming, and seeing them hymning, said to his
servants: "Did we not cast three men into the furnace?" And they said:
"Truly, O king." And he said to them: "Behold, I see four men, and the fourth
like a Son of God." O Nebuchadnezzar, you made yourself God, how do you know
that he is like a Son of God? straightway he said: He himself made sign to me
to say. Better than these impious ones was found Nebuchadnezzar, and they
neither saying the Son like, nor consubstantial. Say to the impious not to
blaspheme, but, reverencing, to worship with good words the God Word existing
in spirit, if perchance the impiety of their blasphemy may be forgiven. These
things then, having been ostracized and cast out of the divine choir, let us
speak also to the brothers of these, the Pneumatomachi, the Tropici, the ones
saying the Spirit to be created. O Tropican, =28.512= did you not hear David
saying: "By the Word of the Lord the heavens were established, and by the
Spirit of his mouth all the power of them?" Behold the indivisible Triad. And
again, narrating the world-making, the great servant Moses: "In the beginning
God made the heaven and the earth; but the earth was invisible and unformed,
and darkness upon the abyss, and the Spirit of God was borne upon the water."
Likewise also the great Paul says: "God sent forth the Spirit of his Son into
our hearts, crying: Abba, Father." And the Father himself says through the
great prophet: "I will pour out from my Spirit upon all flesh, and they shall
prophesy." And the Lord himself, having come to save our race of men, said
with authoritative voice: "I in the Father, and the Father in me;" and, "I am
the truth;" and, "If I go away to my Father, I will not leave you orphans,
but I will send to you the Paraclete, the Spirit of truth." Behold, the
Spirit in the truth, the truth in the Father. Behold, the indivisible Triad,
the triple-stranded rope, in which binding the draughts we offer to the
Father. And these then perished with the Arianomaniacs. Let us speak also to
the most wretched Paul of Samosata, the one saying that, from the time he
came forth from the ever-unmarried Virgin, from then he was called Son.
Senseless one, did you not hear the Lord himself saying to the Jews: "Your
father Abraham rejoiced to see my day, and he saw and was glad." And of the
ungrateful Jews saying to the Lord: "You have not yet forty years, and you
say you have seen Abraham?" He answered and said: "Amen, amen I say to you,
before Abraham came to be, I am." If, before Abraham came to be, he is, how
do you say that after his bodily presence he was called Son? Did you not hear
in the Preacher,


Wisdom herself, how she said: "I was, wherein he rejoiced; daily I rejoiced
in his face," when he made the inhabited world, I was alongside him? And
again the great Preacher says: "God founded the earth in wisdom." Behold, he
is always in the Father; and the great Apostle: "Christ the power of God and
the wisdom of God." What does the foolish Samosatene say to these things? And
he also, having diluted this divine wine, and drinking it, and having been
beaten, perished with the Arianomaniacs. =28.513= Let us speak also to the
most impious Manichaeans, the lees of the evils. O most impious Manichaeans,
do you say that there are two hypostases, of the good and of the evil? And
you say the evil one wishing to fight with the good one, that he may receive
some part; and the good one, he says, being good and not wishing to fight,
gave way, yielding some part to the evil one, and there became to the evil
one one half part, and to the good one a half part. And, he says, having
repented, the good one came to prepare for war, and fashioned himself in the
shape of the evil one. But, O most impious Manichaean, when the good was
whole, you say, It did not wish to fight, and when it became half, as you
say, it came to war. For this reason the most impious Manichaean says that "I
came to seek that which was lost." O the great foolishness! Where then is
still the mark of the good? Especially that he assumed even the shape of the
evil one against you. Just as, if a painter should paint a householder's wife
unseemly at once in an image, and the householder passing by should see his
own wife as an image, would he not, being enraged and becoming full of wrath,
kill the one who dared to do this? How much more the one who says that he
assumed the shape of the evil one? Stronger then is he whose shape he used.
These things are the daring deeds of the foolish and impious. O most impious
lees of the evils Manichaean, did you not hear Matthew saying concerning the
incarnation: "Book of the generation of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of
Abraham?" But he will say, that I do not receive the Old. And I will say to
him: Why do you not receive the Old? Did you not hear the Lord himself saying
to the most impious and ungrateful Jews: "If you believed Moses, you would
believe me, for Moses spoke of me." What did he speak, O Lord? "A prophet to
you the Lord shall raise up from your brothers, like me." This prophet the
blessed Samaritan woman confessed, saying: "Lord, I perceive that you are a
prophet." If the blessed Samaritan woman confessed both man and Lord and
prophet, behold, altogether also the law and the prophets, the great and the
holy ones, confess. How can a grape-cluster be without a vine? How can every
transaction be confirmed, and the salvation about to come to pass in the
world? The signifiers will confess, the great prophets and the law;
especially the Lord himself saying, that =28.516= "The law and the prophets
are until John;" and again: "I came not to destroy the law nor the prophets,
but to fulfill." O lees of all heresies! Their name alone is sufficient to
their faith. Concerning these the great psalmist David said: "The fool said
in his heart: There is no God. They are corrupted and abominable in their
devices," and they themselves not confessing the bodily presence, but a
phantasm, the Lord saying to the Jews: "Why do you seek to kill a man, who
has spoken the truth to you?" And again he saying to the servant striking
him: "If I spoke evil, bear witness concerning the evil; but if well, why do
you strike me?" Can a phantasm be struck, or nailed to the cross? Was it not
a body? And how does he say that it is a phantasm? And let these also be
blotted out together, thinking the divine wine to be unmixed, and having come
into ecstasy, they perished. Let us speak also to Marcion and Valentinus and
Basilides, the one saying that the Lord, having passed through as water
through a channel, through the


spotless and ever-unmarried Mary. O most impious ones! did you not hear the
great archangel Gabriel saying to the spotless Virgin Mary: "Hail, full of
grace, the Lord is with you. The Holy Spirit shall come upon you, and the
power of the Highest shall overshadow you. For the thing from you shall be
called Holy." If then he had said, Through you, they would have had their
shamelessness plausible; but in saying, "from you," he shows that from her
the God Word took the temple, the man. And let these be cast out together
with the now Christ-fighting Jews the Arianomaniacs. Likewise let us speak
also to the foolish and most wretched Hieracas, the one thinking to exalt
virginity and to annul marriage. O Hieracas, let us say something juridical:
The God Word of the Father in paradise led Eve to Adam. But what do you say,
that I do not receive the Old? let it be; in the Gospel did you not hear: On
the third day the Lord reclined at Cana of Galilee at the marriage, and wine
failing, he made the water wine? And again the Jews saying to him: "Moses
gave us a book of divorcement," what did the Lord say to =28.517= them:
"Moses gave you a book of divorcement for your hardheartedness;" for it is
written: "What God joined together, let not man separate." Then how can a
branch subsist, a root not underlying? How can a virgin be, a father and a
mother not existing? And let these perish with their companions. Let us speak
also to Novatus, the one calling himself pure, and thinking to abolish
repentance. Being full of mire and stench, did you not hear the good and
philanthropic God of all saying through the great prophet: "I do not will the
death of the sinner, but the repentance?" And again the great Job: "No one is
clean from filth, not even if his life were one day upon the earth." And
again the disciples saying to the Lord: "How often shall we forgive the
sinning one? until seven times?" And the Lord said to them: "Not so, but
until seventy times seven." The promise being renewed, repentance is made
everlasting; not that, hearing, we should continue sinning; may it not be!
but that we may show the goodness of the good and philanthropic God of all;
and again through the great Isaiah proclaiming to Jerusalem, and him saying:
"Hear, O heaven, and hearken, O earth: Sons I begot and exalted; but they set
me at naught," and the things following. You know, brothers. And after his
such threat came to be, he says: "Wash, become clean, take away the evils
from your souls." O the great goodness! And again Peter the great having said
to him, when he asked the disciples saying: "Whom do men say me to be?" Some
said that Elijah; others said a prophet. And he saying to them: "But you,
whom do you say me to be?" And Peter answering said: "You are the Christ, the
Son of the living God," the one coming into the world. Straightway the Lord
said to him: "Blessed are you, Simon bar Jonah; for flesh and blood revealed
not to you, but my Father in the heavens. You are Peter, and upon this rock I
will build my Church. To you I will give the keys of the kingdom of the
heavens; and all things whatsoever you bind on the earth shall be bound in
the heavens." And =28.520= having received such promises, he said to the
Lord: "I will follow you, wherever you go, and I will lay my soul for you."
Straightway the Lord said to him: "Amen I say to you: today before the cock
crows, thrice you will deny me." And the Lord being led into the house of
Caiaphas, and all the sanhedrin with Caiaphas, and Peter standing in the
gateway, the servant seeing him said to him: Are you not with him? And Peter
said to him: I am not. And again the doorkeeper saying to him: Are you also
of the Galileans? He answered and said: I am not. And the servant questioning


him, he denied, and straightway a cock crew. And the Lord of all, having
turned, looked upon Peter; and Peter wept, and through the tears washed away
the denial in the very repentance. And again the holy prophet said: "But the
stars are not clean before him." If the stars are not clean, how do you call
the clean and tender-hearted and philanthropic God hard and unphilanthropic?
And let this one be cast out together with the other heresiarchs. Let us
speak also to the wretched Phrygian, the one saying that after the sojourn of
the Savior Montanus and Priscilla sojourned. These things are, perhaps, the
spittings of old women, as of those grown doting. How can a prophet again
sojourn after the sojourn of the Savior? Did you not hear that the law and
the prophets are until John? Hear you, O people of the Lord. Just as a king
is about to arrive in a certain city, and sends signifiers saying: Behold, in
what manner I am about to come. But if he sojourns, and they recognize the
signified sojourn, worshipping him, there is no longer need of the signifier;
for with their own eyes they saw the signified one. But if someone say in
that very city that the king is about to come, would not those in the city
spit upon him, saying: Him whom we saw and have with ourselves, how do you
say that another is about to exist? And these then perished with the other
heresies. Let us speak also to the new heresy, the one come from Syria into
Egypt, the Apollinarians, Apollinaris having brought forward a new dogma,
(Christ) taking only a body, God from the spotless and ever-virgin Mary, and
the God Word himself being instead of a soul. O most imperfect and soulless
one! If the perfect =28.521= man transgressing the commandment, and being
cast out of paradise, and having fallen into death, (he) saved the man
partially, and how was the soul about to be saved? Did you not hear the great
prophet Isaiah proclaiming against your godlessness, that "A child is born to
us, and a son is given to us, whose government is upon his shoulder, and they
call his name Angel of Great Counsel?" Did he say that Flesh, a child and a
son, was born? Know, unintelligent one, that, saying child, he shows a
perfect man. The perfect one then having been shown, the great evangelist
John the son of thunder says with the simple voice: "In the beginning was the
Word, and the Word was with God, and God was the Word. This was in the
beginning with God. All things through him came to be, and without him came
to be nothing." And the Lord himself says: "Now my soul is troubled." Unto
whom does this voice reach? unto the God Word? Away! The untroubled God Word,
having come to stop the trouble, himself spoke the voice? But do you wish
rather to say the body, that it itself said? You err, for the body by itself
is immovable, unless the soul were in it. Did you not hear Moses saying:
"Show me yourself?" and straightway he hears: "No one shall see my face and
live." But after the body was placed in the tomb with the bodies, how did the
Godhead go down into Hades to save the souls naked? Rather it went down to
destroy them than to save them. For no one was able to see the Godhead naked
and be saved. Therefore then there is need of the soul; that in the soul the
souls may be able to be saved. And again the Lord himself says: "The good
shepherd lays his soul for the sheep. I am the good shepherd." And again he
says: "I have authority to lay my soul, and again to take it." And again the
angel commands Joseph in Egypt, and says to him: "Rising, take the child and
his mother, for behold those seeking the soul of the child have died."
Behold, altogether it is witnessed in the divine Scriptures concerning the
sojourn of the good and philanthropic God Word, that he wore a perfect
temple, a man, having become man, that the whole race of us men might be
saved. But what will you say, that through Isaiah he said to the Jews:


"Your new moons =28.524= and your sabbaths my soul hates?" Most senseless.
Just as through this great prophet: "Behold, the Virgin shall have in the
womb and bear a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel." And again: "A
child is born;" and again, that "He is a man," and, "Who shall know him?" and
concerning this the Lord himself signified through himself, that in the
sojourn, he being about to do such and so great signs and wonders on
sabbaths, and instead of blessing him, being instigated under the devil, they
insulted him. For this reason, as I said before, this also was signified
beforehand. Then to these things what do you say, O soulless one? And you
perish with the other heresies. But the catholic Church is this, having read
rightly and searched exactly the divine Scriptures, she built herself upon
the rock, the perfect dove, the one holding the rudder of the right and pious
faith in the apostolic ship. The waves dashing upon the unmoved rock, and
foaming, resolving into themselves they vanish. All the heresies are storms.
But we, I beseech, with this triple-stranded and indivisible rope let us bind
the draughts, doing the commandments, I say, that we may arrive there into
the saving port in the kingdom of the heavens, and may the God Word, the Son,
the wisdom of the Father say: "Behold I and the children, which God gave me,"
and may he deign to say also to us: Well, faithful and good servants; you
were faithful over few things, I will set you over many; enter into the
kingdom prepared for you. Be it! Amen.
