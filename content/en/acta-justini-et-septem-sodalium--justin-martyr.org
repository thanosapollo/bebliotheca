Martyrdom of the Holy Justin, Chariton, Charito, Euelpistus, Hierax,
Paeon, Liberianus, and of their company
In the time of the lawless ordinances of idolatry the aforementioned
saints, having been arrested, were brought before Rusticus, the
prefect of Rome.
When they had been brought in, the prefect said to Justin: What life
do you live?
Justin said: One blameless and not to be condemned by all men.
Rusticus the prefect said: What discourses do you handle? Justin said:
I have tried to learn all discourses; but I have given my assent to
the true discourses of the Christians, even if they do not please
those who hold false opinions.
Rusticus the prefect said: Then those discourses please you? Justin
said: Yes, since I follow them with a doctrine.
Rusticus the prefect said: What is the doctrine? Justin said: That by
which we worship the God of the Christians, whom we consider one of
these from the beginning as creator of the making of the whole world,
and the child of God, Jesus Christ, who was also proclaimed beforehand
by the prophets as about to come to the race of men, a herald of
salvation and a teacher of good lessons.
And I think I am saying little toward his divinity, confessing a
certain prophetic power, that it has been proclaimed beforehand
concerning this one whom I just now said is the son of God. For know
that from of old the prophets foretold concerning his presence that
came to be among men.
Rusticus the prefect said: Where do you assemble? Justin said:
Wherever each has preference and ability. For do you really think it
possible that we all assemble in the same place?
Rusticus the prefect said: Tell, where do you assemble, or in what
place?
Justin said: I dwell above the bath of Myrtinus for the whole time
that I have been sojourning for the second time in the city of the
Romans; and I know of no other assembly except the one there. And if
anyone wished to come to me, I used to share with him the words of the
truth.
Rusticus said: Then you are a Christian? Justin answered: Yes, I am a
Christian.
Rusticus the prefect said to Chariton: Chariton, are you also a
Christian? Chariton said: I am a Christian by the command of God.
Rusticus the prefect said to Charito: And what do you say, Charito?
Charito said: I am a Christian by the gift of God.
Rusticus the prefect said to Euelpistus: And who are you? Euelpistus
said: I too am a Christian and sharing in the same hope.

Rusticus the prefect said to Hierax: Are you a Christian? Hierax said:
Yes, I am a Christian, worshipping the same God.
Rusticus the prefect said: Did Justin make you Christians? Hierax
said: I have been a Christian from of old.
Paeon standing said: I too am a Christian. Rusticus said: Who taught
you? Paeon said: We have received it from our parents.
Euelpistus said: I used to hear Justin's word with pleasure, but from
my parents I received being a Christian. Rusticus said: Where are your
parents? Euelpistus said: In Cappadocia.
Rusticus the prefect says to Hierax: Where are your parents? Hierax
said: They have died. And I was torn away from Phrygia a considerable
time ago.
Rusticus the prefect said to Liberianus: Are you too a Christian?
Liberianus said: I too am a Christian, pious.
The prefect says to Justin: If you are scourged and beheaded, are you
persuaded that you are about to go up into heaven?
Justin said: I hope from the endurance if I endure; and I know that
also for those who have lived rightly the divine gift remains until
the conflagration.
Rusticus the prefect said: Then do you suppose this, that you will go
up? Justin said: I do not suppose, but I am precisely persuaded.
Rusticus the prefect said: If you do not obey, you will be punished.
Justin said: We have it as a prayer that, having been punished, we be
saved.
Rusticus the prefect pronounced: Let those who have not been willing
to sacrifice to the gods, having been scourged, be led away according
to the sequence of the laws.
And the holy martyrs, glorifying God, going out to the accustomed
place, completed the martyrdom in the confession of our Savior, to
whom be the glory and the power with the Father and the Holy Spirit
now and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
