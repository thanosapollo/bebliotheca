Of the same, a letter to Antiochus
Your piety ought not to turn aside its mind by the words of those who have
spoken, but in the multitude of falsehood to track the truth with much
consideration. For if all the things being rumored are true, all would be in
danger. And if hidden things are tracked by judgment and laws, I for my part
would have sought a tribunal from you, if someone will not again blow other
weapons of slander upon me. For I fear, I fear henceforth the shadows and the
phantoms, since you yourselves have judged thus. Friends have denied
friendship, those nearby have stood far off and being far off they shoot the
arrows of slander, and you have made me, being in the midst of the harbor, to
undergo shipwreck. But even if I am cast out of the city and barred from the
church, I am persuaded to strip for every punishment; for it has been judged
by me to philosophize and to bear the hard things nobly. For I know, I know
clearly, that the desert is more lawful than the city and the beasts of the
field tamer than friends.
