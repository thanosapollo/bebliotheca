OF THE DAMASCENE.
The creator and sustainer of the heavens, and Craftsman of every cosmic
and supercosmic creation, of whom there is no place among all things; if
indeed that which contains is defined as the place of the things within:
in you, from himself, a child shall be formed without seed, and he shall
show you a spacious treasury of his all-filling and solely
uncircumscribed divinity. Whole without passion contracting himself in
you, and remaining wholly outside; and having himself as a place
unencompassed! from you the sun of righteousness shall rise, two natures
even if the Acephali rave; one hypostasis, even if Nestorius bursts
asunder. For as a kind of divine spiritual seed the Father will send
forth his own Son, and the Word shall be born of you as flesh without
change. And the Father, having without passion begotten the Son first
according to nature, will without passion beget the same also the second
time according to economy. For like some shell from heaven, from the
lightning of the divinity, you shall have in the womb, and you shall
bear the most precious pearl, Christ, the only-begotten light, which
does not beget, but is always being begotten, whose being-begotten is a
hypostatic property alone. In you also the laws of nature shall be
innovated, and the unmixable things shall come together. For there shall
be virginity giving birth. A mixing of divinity and humanity, of passion
and impassibility, of life and death, so that in all things the worse
may be overcome by the better. For you shall not be a vessel of an
energy of God, but essentially of the hypostasis of God; and the Creator
shall take a portion from you, the firstfruits of the human dough,
having been incarnate from the Holy Spirit and from your bloods. Prayer
of John of Damascus.}1 Master Lord Jesus Christ, our God, who alone has
authority to forgive sins for men, as good and lover of mankind,
overlook all my faults in knowledge and in ignorance, and count me
worthy without condemnation to partake of your divine and glorious and
undefiled and life-giving mysteries, not unto punishment, not unto
addition of sins, but unto cleansing and sanctification and a pledge of
the life and kingdom to come, unto a wall and help and overturning of
the adversaries, unto blotting out of my many transgressions. For you
are a God of mercy and compassions and love of mankind; and to you we
send up the glory =96.817= with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and
always and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
Another prayer of the same John of Damascus.
I have stood before the doors of your temple, and I do not depart from
the dreadful thoughts. But you, Christ God, who justified the publican,
and had mercy on the Canaanite woman, and opened the gates of paradise
to the thief, open to me the bowels of your love of mankind, and receive
me as I come forward and touch you, as the harlot and the woman with the
issue of blood. For the one, having touched your hem, easily received
the healing; and the other, having grasped your undefiled feet, obtained
the release of her sins. But I, the pitiable one, to receive your whole
body of many, may I not be burned up, but receive me as you did those
women, and enlighten the senses of my soul, burning up the charges of my
sin. By the intercessions of her who bore you without seed, and of the
heavenly powers; for

you are blessed unto the ages of ages. Amen. Another prayer of the same.
I am wounded in heart; your zeal has melted me, your love has altered
me, Master; I am bound by your love. May I be filled with your flesh;
may I be sated with your life-giving and deifying blood; may I enjoy the
good things; may I take delight in your divinity; may I become worthy,
when you come with glory, to meet you, caught up in the air in clouds
with all your elect, that I may hymn and worship and glorify you
thankfully in confession, with your unoriginate Father and your
all-holy and good and life-giving Spirit, now and always and unto the
ages of ages. Amen.
