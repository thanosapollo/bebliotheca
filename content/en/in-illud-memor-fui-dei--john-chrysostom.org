On the saying, "I remembered God and was glad."
=61.689=
"I remembered God, and I was glad," the melodist David bore witness. He did
not bear witness from his own nature, but he sang from the Holy Spirit of God;
David did not simply remember, but he also made supplication wisely; he did
not remember from the lips only, but the heart also taught; David did not
forget by night or by day,
but even while asking these things he remembered at every hour. And that you
may learn that we are setting down truth and not a lie, let us harvest the
melodies of the Holy Spirit from the written history itself. "I remembered
God, and I was glad," David said. And if you wish to know truly that David
remembered God both by night and by day, and remembering was glad, attend with
much love of hearing to what is said: "I remembered your name in the night, O
Lord"; and, "I will remember your name in every generation and generation";
"At midnight I rose to give thanks to you, O Lord"; "In the mornings I
meditated on you." "Evening and morning and at noon I will declare and
announce"; "O God, my God, toward you I rise at dawn." And again, "To declare
your mercy in the morning, and your truth by night." Do you see that there is
no idle hour in which he forgot? not the morning hour, nor the night. If
indeed you wish to learn truly that David did not simply remember God, but
with a constraining of heart, hear: "Out of the depths I cried to you, O
Lord"; "O Lord, I will give thanks to you in uprightness of heart." And that
David may set a bound to all the hours, he then says: "How I have loved your
law, O Lord! it is my meditation all the day." See, as he meditated all the
day on the oracles of the Lord, and remembered God at each thing, of what rank
of grace did he partake? Did he then gather the rewards into his bosom upon
earth? I think him both shining and reigning on earth: here subjecting the
enemies, and there commanding the angels; here wearing the purple of the
earthly kingdom, and there flashing the glory of the kingdom in heaven; here
making the voices of the Holy Spirit's fair-sounding shine, and there
illuminating the gleamings of all-virtuous wisdom. And these things we sketch
in part. Yet of all these he received the better as his lot. For when the Holy
Spirit saw God being hymned continually by David upon earth, the Holy Spirit
then went into the heavens for intercession, saying: "Lord, at every hour
David remembers you, and remembering he is glad; and do you yourself remember
him, Lord, our God," and it says: "Remember, O Lord, David and all his
meekness." Then the man-loving God answers: "Since David remembers me
continually and with understanding, I too will proclaim before all my
disposition and love toward him; above saints and angels and powers I will
magnify him upon the earth." Saying what? =61.690= "I have found David my
servant, a man after my heart"; and, "I will set him firstborn, high among the
kings of the earth"; and, "His throne as the days of heaven." And having said
this, David exhibited himself. Do you see that great gain comes to the human
being from remembering God? "I remembered God," David said, "and my heart was
glad." For the other gladnesses enter through the mouth, and are conducted out
through the privy; but the gladness of God, as a fountain, gladdens the heart
with honey-flowing words, so that such a one says: "How sweet are your oracles
to my throat? more than honey to my mouth." For the one who remembers God has
all the goods of gladness


lurking in the heart; there a fountain gushing goods, there honey and
honeycomb and a lamp being kindled, there a hidden treasure of the Holy
Trinity. Then the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit come, and take
lodging with him, and then he is no longer a catechumen human being, but an
angel borne by God. Since then the human being has been counted worthy of such
grace, and David, having tasted such gladness, was glad, let us too, tasting
the gladness of God, remember God continually, that we may taste of his goods.
Come then, let us mine the virtues of the Savior's presence, and let us hymn
his earthly presence as an all-virtuous pillar; since I heard from the tongue
of the lawless Jews tokens of blasphemy, and since they disbelieve the
Scriptures, which they read unworthily, and they do not know the mystery. For
that the Lord was born of a virgin, in this, he says, the wretched and wholly
wicked take offense. O the folly! O those lawless contrary to nature! what
scripture, or what prophet, does not trumpet these graces of the Lord? First
Solomon blessed the Virgin, the charioteer of the great wisdom, saying:
"Blessed is the barren that is undefiled, who has not known the bed in
transgression." For he calls her barren before the Holy Spirit tabernacled,
and before the sun blossomed. "She has not known the bed in transgression,"
because she did not converse with a man. And when the honored Isaiah heard
concerning the undefiled barren one, at once he swaddled the saying of the
wise man, and sang to the same: "You," he says, "called her an undefiled
barren one; and I say: Rejoice, barren one who does not bear." For until now
you have not borne; from now on rejoice, because you have been counted worthy
to receive him whom the heavens do not contain. And as the two witnesses were
proclaiming these things, they sought to mix yet another into the mystery,
that upon three witnesses the thing sought might stand secure. And as they
were considering these things, both found David, the orator of melody, and
they remind him of the things to come of the economy. And when he heard the
mystery, he ans =61.691= wered with a trumpet's voice: "See, both of you have
spoken about the barren one; why have you not spoken about the glorious
conception?" Isaiah at once, perceiving the prudent point, sets word against
word: "Behold, the virgin shall conceive in the womb, and shall bear a son."
And Solomon seconded: "She shall have fruit in the visitation of souls."
David, having heard these things, said to them: "See, you have said that she
shall bear; in what place? in a city, or in a wilderness, or in a country?"
They say to him: "You interpret concerning the place and the city; for you
have been counted worthy of more abundant grace." Then David, the Spirit
having told him the working, with a loud cry set up the mystery as a pillar:
"You," he says, "murmur in secret; I with a high voice will hymn the
conception: Behold, we heard of it in Ephratha, we found it in the fields of
the wood." And having understood upon these doctrines that the riddle is deep,
he painted it: "Behold, we heard of it in Ephratha, we found it in the fields
of the wood"; that is, in Bethlehem; for it is named Ephratha. "We heard of it
in Ephratha," that she is with child; for there the hearing went out, that the
Virgin is with child. Then "we found her in the fields of the wood," in the
cave, where the light was manifested. Here we heard of her with child, here we
received a hearing concerning a man; there we have found the mother of the
Lord. See, from your own we have set three witnesses before you, proclaiming
the glory of the Virgin; not that we have found these only; for all the
prophets, having seen him, hope; but that the just thing may stand upon three
witnesses. But I do not treat upon the three; but give me one most faithful
witness saying that the mystery is a lie, or that the Virgin brought forth
from a man; or prove this, and having turned, worship her as Theotokos. Either
show the Savior held in a tomb and a coffin, or I show you him seated at the
right hand of the Father. For not


from foreigners, but from your own race I bring the witness saying: "The Lord
said to my Lord: Sit at my right hand, until I set your enemies as a footstool
of your feet." He speaks concerning the Jews; for there is no other enemy,
except the one who denies the cross. But I, leaving the God-fighting
synagogue, join myself to the God-inspired Church. If a Jew says to you, "Tell
how the virgin gave birth without a man," say to him also: "How did Adam bring
forth Eve without a woman? How did the virgin give birth without seed? how did
the earth blossom without seed? How did the virgin give birth? how did the
unhewn virgin gush the water? How did the virgin give birth? tell how Aaron's
rod sprouted? How did the virgin give birth? tell how the manna from heaven?
how was the jar multiplied? how did the flower sprout from the root of Jesse?
how did Elisha's oil gush into the jars? How did the virgin give birth? tell,
how upon Gideon did the fleece let go dew? tell, how upon Samson did the bone
gush the water? tell, how did the furnace keep the children? tell, how upon
Manoah was the rock kindled without matter? tell, how was the bush burning,
and was not consumed? tell, how was Elijah not burned up in the fiery chariot?
tell, how did Moses enter into the gloom, and come out again? How did the
virgin give birth? how did Isaiah behold the Lord seated upon a throne high
and lifted up? tell, how did Daniel make known the hidden dream to
Nebuchadnezzar? how did Ezekiel see the Invisible seated upon a chariot of
Cherubim? How did the virgin give birth? how was Jeremiah sanctified a prophet
from the womb? tell, how did the dead bones of the prophet then raise the
dead? tell, how was the Red Sea struck with a rod? tell, how did the Jordan
turn back the stream? tell, how do the trees conceive the fruits? tell, how
does the vine bear the cluster? tell, how did the night give birth to the day?
How did the virgin give birth? tell, how does the luminary of the sun warm the
world, tell, how does the heaven bring forth the stars?" And upon these there
are mysteries unspeakable, hidden and incomprehensible in the heavens. If you,
examiner of the Jews, interpret these things for me, I know that you also
grasp the conception of the Virgin. But you are a stranger both to those
mysteries and to these; but we both take these things kindly, and believe
those. Interpret the prophets, and look at the orators of the presence. Let it
be cro =61.692= wned, the preaching. As many things as he did and suffered,
these the ones who went before painted so many generations earlier. First
David entreats: "Lord, bow the heavens, and come down." And having obtained
the request, he evangelizes and says: "He bowed the heavens, and came down."
And since he was to appear as a child, Isaiah says: "A child has been born to
us." And that his mother was going to take him up and flee into Egypt,
"Behold, the Lord sits upon a light cloud, and will come into Egypt, and the
handmade things of Egypt shall be shaken." The word reasonably called the
Virgin a light cloud, because she did not receive the weight of man-making
seed. And that he was going to be baptized in the Jordan, Jeremiah says: "How
will you do in the swelling of the Jordan?" And why do we number them by kind?
Come then, gathering all the prophecies together into one luminary. Concerning
the interpretation of Gabriel he says: "Behold, I send my angel before your
face, who shall prepare your way before you." And concerning the meeting of
Christ and the Baptist David says: "Mercy and truth met"; and concerning the
greeting of Elizabeth toward the holy and pure Theotokos, "Righteousness and
peace kissed." And again concerning John and concerning the Lord he says:
"Truth sprang up from the earth, and righteousness looked down from heaven."
And concerning the apostles he says: "I said: You are gods." And concerning
the testimony from above David says: "You are my Son, today I have begotten
you." And concerning the sick he says: "He sent his word and healed them"; and
concerning the


sinners he says: "He himself bears our sins"; and concerning the raised dead,
"The dead shall arise"; and concerning the souls that were held, "And those
sitting in darkness, a light has sprung up for you." And concerning Caiaphas
and Herod he says: "Rulers of peoples were gathered together with Abraham";
and concerning Judas, "He who eats my bread magnified a heel against me"; and
concerning the synagogue of the Jews and high priests, "The Lord will come
into judgment with the elders of the people"; and concerning the cross: "Come
and let us cast wood into his bread"; and concerning the robber on the right,
"The right hand of the Lord has done power"; and concerning the tomb: "They
set me in the lowest pit"; and concerning the crown of thorns, "My vine
instead of clusters sprouted thorns"; and concerning being tempted by the Evil
One, "An enemy reproached the Lord"; and concerning being called Son of man,
"And upon a Son whom you strengthened for yourself"; and concerning his
burial, "And I will give the wicked for his burial"; and concerning the
resurrection of the Lord, "Arise, why do you sleep, O Lord?"; and concerning
his ascension: "God went up with a shout"; and concerning the seat above, "Sit
at my right hand." And why do I say many things? For not even the orators
announce all the prophecies that are sung concerning the grace of Christ. Do
you wish to know the security of a good history? that the Old painted
concerning the New Covenant, and the things being economically ordered here,
there being sketched in shadow? See him there arriving at the tent of Abraham
with two angels, and here entering with twelve disciples into the house of
Zacchaeus. See him there calling two brothers into priesthood, and here
choosing two brothers into apostleship: Moses and Aaron among his priests, and
Peter and Andrew among his apostles. See him there raising the son of the
widow through Elijah, and here raising the only-begotten of the widow through
himself; there Naaman being cleansed through Elisha, and here washing the
leper through himself; there bringing in Miriam sister of the holy prophets,
and here naming the same appellation of a sister of the holy apostles; there
magnifying four from the prophets, and here honoring four in number,
evangelists. There he typed twelve other prophets, and here he called apostles
twelve in number; there he appointed ministers to the great prophets, and here
seventy to the apostles. Thaddaeus then it was who healed Abgar. There Joshua
the son of Nun chastened the kings of Palestine, here Jesus the true God
glorified the kings of the inhabited world; there twelve =61.693= fountains
gush in Eden, here twelve disciples from the sun of righteousness cause
baptism to spring; there seventy stems of palms were shown, here seventy
disciples of the apostles sprouted; there a tree sweetened the water that had
been made bitter, and here the wood of the cross changed the most bitter of
sins into a sweet fountain of gifts; there the tent of the testimony was
sanctified, here the tent of the bridal chamber was glorified; there David
sang the hymns, and here Paul sounded the teachings; there Elijah from the
prophets was shown a virgin, and here likewise John from the apostles a
virgin; there Elijah sat in the fiery chariot, and here John leaned upon the
breast of the Lord; for our God is a consuming fire. There three great ones
were shown, having the name of the Savior, making clear the character of the
glorious Trinity, the three holy mighty ones. For they were not simply of the
chance ones, nor of the ignoble, Jesus the son of Josedek the great among
priests; then the three trustworthy witnesses honor the outline of the Holy
Trinity. And truly my mind is drawn to leap out also into other things, into
saving faith. For I do not stand off from the prophet saying:


"I remembered God, and I was glad"; whom the all-virtuous Wisdom proclaims in
the songs: "Awake, north wind, and come, south, blow through my garden, and
let spices flow." Who then is the north, or the south? or who is the garden?
or what are the plants in the garden? or what are the spices that flow? I
think this to be, as the Scripture interprets, that the north is the holy
Virgin, the south the Savior of the world, plants the holy martyrs and
apostles, a fountain watering the garden the baptism of the Savior, a guard of
the garden the seal of the cross, spices to be the healings and the gifts of
the Savior. This then is it: "Awake, north," that is, the Virgin from Galilee;
"and come, south": come, Christ, from heaven from the south, and breathe into
the holy Virgin; blow through into the world, and raise up the holy martyrs
and apostles, and let the spices flow upon the sick and the led astray. These
spices are fixed into various graces, and become for the sick a token of
health, for the led astray a proof of turning, for sinners an advocate of
repentance, for the righteous a help of vigor. How then shall we hymn worthily
the healings of the healings, the life-giving hope, the earthly life, the
heavenly salvation? How was the earth myrrhed, when these spices flowed? and
how again did the earth bear its own incense? and offering to the Master with
fear it said: "You," it says, "Master, furnish the heavenly spices, and I
offer the earthly things of the incenses to your divinity; you, Master, myrrh
my back with your holy and all-undefiled tracks, and I leaping offer myrrhs to
you." Then the earth, being gladdened upon both of these toward all, cried to
all the fellow-servant creation: "Come, be gathered, all my offspring, and see
upon earth the one who is glorified in the heavens; come, see borne in a
woman's arms the one who is seated upon the Cherubim; come, see fed with milk,
the one who channels milk to the females; come, see contained in a Virgin's
womb, the one who is not contained in the heavens; come, see receiving food
from a mother, the one who feeds the ends; come, see being handed over to
teachers, the one who trains teachers; come, see being tempted by human
beings, the one who is glorified by angels; come, see entering into the house
of Zacchaeus, the one who is seated above the heavens; come, see conversing
with the Canaanite woman, the one who is hymned by the Cherubic liturgy; come,
see associating with a sinful woman, the one who is seated at the right of the
Father; come, see the one touched at the hems by the sick woman, the one
worshipped by the bodiless nature of the powers; come, see born as a human
being, the one who exists as maker of the ages; come, see being an infant, the
one who exists before the foundation of the world; come, see being tempted by
high priests, the one who is escorted by angels; come, all the creatures
seeing the one who created; and as the life is =61.694= upon earth, all of you
live; come, all be God-borne; come, small with the great come together, the
poor with the rich, the slaves with the masters, the sinners with the
righteous, the creeping things and the four-footed with the winged, and see
the one who granted the life. Seasons of winter and of autumn, be changed, and
bear fruit contrary to nature, and bring your increase to the one who made
your fruits conceive; do all things, and myrrh the divinity; either bring
forward your own incenses, or harvest the spices from him." And as in all
these things the earth was overlooked by the offspring, behold all things, as
it said, ran together, and the desert places sprouted, and showed much
fragrance; that they might fulfill the prophecy of Isaiah


that sang: "Let the desert rejoice, and let it blossom as a lily, and the
deserts of the Jordan shall be wood-glad and shall give fragrance." These
things being done well thus, then the sea itself also desired to enjoy the
fragrance, and began to make itself public, and it set word against the earth
saying: "Yield the Lord to me as well now; for the Master is not yours only,
because you received the grace of the whole of his divinity; entreat him to
come to me, lest I, coming to him, wipe out the whole of your face. For if I
had not recognized him, and feared the one who sealed me and bound me with
unlooseable bonds, I would long ago have come and bent your ends. Or did you
not know that I have been more honored and older than you? for before you
appeared, I was dancing in the abysses. Give me back my own Master, as the
prophet said: 'For the sea is his, and he made it'; and then he added: 'And
the dry land his hands fashioned.' You then were fashioned later. Dried-up
one, mother of dust, insatiable, and tomb of the dead, house of bloods, of
deceits and of sins, why do you tyrannize your old woman, and hold the Lord
alone? Give him back to me now, that what was said may be fulfilled, 'In the
sea are your ways'; give me back my own fashioner, that he may fill my bosoms
also in grace." And the earth, thus being pressed by the sea at his nod,
puffing at the world paraded saying: "For I am not worse than you, you who are
tossed, and swollen, and unstable, and hissing, you who roar in the flood and
of bile, charioteer of seaweed, and useless to life, salt-streamed and
ill-channeled, untraversable by journeys, kin of the squall, four-waved by the
hurricanes; if you know, as you said, that you are more honored, the Master
would first have tabernacled in you; but now this dignity of mine is apparent,
that I am superior to you, inasmuch as in me first he let loose the rays of
the divinity. I have been set so much more honored than you, as I am mother of
human beings, and you of creeping things; I am mother of the holy Virgin, who
blossomed the Master; but you of the evil dragon, who mocks your living
things; I am mother of prophets and apostles and holy men; but you mother of
wild and evil creeping things, and destruction of those who sail ill; I a
paradise and fruit-bearing, having flowers and spices, but you winds and
harbors and disorders. If it were in me to hold the Lord, I would not have
yielded him to come to you." And as, the elements then acting against the
nods, the Lord who fills all things and economically orders all things comes
to the sea itself, wishing by the holy tracks to glorify and honor the
continent of the waters, then the sea, spreading its bosoms, received the Lord
with much joy saying: "Come, all the living things, the small with the great,
come together you in the depths, swim up to the one who from the beginning
enthroned you, and granted you to live; all the contrary spirits of the storm,
be muzzled, and shudder at the divinity of the one present; all the squalls,
hurricanes, be quiet before the greatness of the sovereignty; all the winds
and winter storms, be drawn in, and all the waves be still and grow calm, and
receive the Lord of life and of death with joy. Who will announce to me today,
that I have the Lord as a sea-walking rider? Let me be magnified from today,
that I have become a Cherubic seat, and have taken up Seraphic couches; that
the one seated there I have here walking; the one flying upon wings of winds I
have being dispat =61.695= ched among the islands; the one being lifted above
so many high and lofty firmaments, I have this one going on foot upon my
splendid country. Now I am no longer addressed as sea, but a heavenly font;
now I have become a lamp, flashing exceedingly, heavenly; now I have become a
fountain gushing healings; now I have become a sun, shining rays; now I have
become


a heaven casting gleamings, not submitting the things of nature, but gushing
streams of grace." And as all creation was exulting, together also earth and
the things under heaven were brightened with grace. For the led astray were
turning, the sinners were repenting, the impious were being pious, the sick
were partaking of health, the dead unto a workshop of life, the living unto
the face of divinity. And as the demons were being driven out, and the disease
was wasting, and death was being put to shame, then also those held in Hades
by the enemy, having received a hearing concerning him, were eager to see the
glory, and were entreating to be loosed from the bonds. And when the
forerunner of destruction and ruler of the darkness saw him having set these
things right here, and ready to sack the kingdoms of Hades, the Evil One went
over to the demons guarding there, saying: that "He who brought the things
under heaven to their senses is coming under the earth; he is coming to strip
the ends, and to take captive our unseen storehouses." And answering, the
accursed say to him: "And who is stronger than you? were you not saying, There
is none more powerful than I who arrays himself against me? Have you now grown
cowardly, being worsted by that one? For the present try to make war; take
courage, and you will find him worsted in all things." But he: "I have tried,"
he says, "and I have tested, and from every side he mastered my sovereignties;
I warred against him in the wilderness, and he showed me a wilderness from
every side. Nor did I have strength against his disciples; for he did not let
them lack the same glory." And they to him: "For the present go in within, and
shut the bronze gates, and let us bar the iron bars, and let us make ready our
arrows." As the evil ones were crying these and such things, behold then also
the Lord arrives with fire-bearing armies. "Open gates to me, you rulers of
the demons; for the king of glory shall enter." But those being within dared,
and answering: "Who is this king of glory, the Lord strong and mighty in war?"
But the general of the demons was saying to them from within: "Did I not tell
you, that he is strong and mighty in war, and powerful in strength?" And the
angels were pressing: "Lift up gates, before we shatter them, and break the
bars; open with peace, before we enter with wrath. Lift up gates, you rulers
of Hades, and the king of glory shall enter." And they, being held by fear as
they were tossing words: "Who is this king of glory?" they themselves
answered: "He is Lord of the powers, Master of the things above and of the
things below." And as they were in faction, not opening, being trained by
trembling, at a nod of the divinity the gates were at once shattered, and the
bars were broken, and the proclamation of the prophecy that had been trumpeted
so many generations before was fulfilled, that "He shattered bronze gates, and
crushed iron bars." Then the workers of insult, being at a loss, the lawless
said: "Having the arrows and the swords, let us rush upon him." But he who had
broken the gates shattered them also, and fulfilled the two prophesying
proclamations: "The Lord crushing wars, Lord is his name." There he shattered
the strengths of the bows, shield and sword and war. And when the shut-in ones
saw the light of the eternal life, they cried out saying: "You light
wonderfully from everlasting mountains." They called mountains the firmaments
of Hades. "All the senseless in heart were troubled"; that is, the diabolic
rulers were beside themselves; "They slept their sleep, and found nothing."
For they were put to death by the Lord, and what they had in their hands they
lost, and they found nothing. "From your rebuke, O God of Jacob, the ones
mounted on the horses slumbered." He calls horses the souls of those held, and
riders the evil demons, demons who train the souls of human beings without
mercy. They slumbered and were put to death. Then when they saw the salvation
of the souls, and their own destruction, their =61.696= freedom, and their own
slavery, those being released, and


themselves being held with unlooseable bonds, they cried out with a strong
cry: "You are fearful, and who shall stand against you face to face? You who
were scourging souls, from heaven you hurled a just judgment upon us; for we
were not judging justly; therefore we are judged, therefore we do not obtain
sparing. For creation is without pity to the one who has not done mercy." He
shut them into the storehouses of Hades, being punished under the smell of
dragons, having bound them with unlooseable bonds. And taking all the souls
the Lord gathered them, and fulfilled the prophecy of Jeremiah. "A partridge
gathered what it did not bear." For those whom the enemy led down into Hades,
these the Lord gathered; those whom the devil bore there, or rather scattered,
these Christ gathered. "Arise," the Lord said, "and let us go up into heaven."
And this whole thing has come to be fulfilling the riddle of the prophecy,
"Arise, and let us go up to the mountain"; that is, into the heavenly
Jerusalem. The gates below were opened, or rather were shattered; it is
necessary then that the upper gates of the heavens also be opened; for the
gatekeepers above also, not knowing, were hindering. For when he came down,
they did not know; therefore as he was going up they did not recognize him,
and for the present they were attempting to treat of it (for the Lord had with
himself the souls which he had redeemed, and the robber, or rather disciple),
saying: "Who is the one coming with so great an array?" But the angels going
before were signaling the dignity of the divinity. But they were not hurrying,
being worn by ignorance: "And if it is the Lord going up, how did we not know
him coming down? for did he not also come down at other times, and order the
ark upon Noah? upon Abraham he arrived at the tent, upon Jacob on the ladder,
upon Job he spoke through whirlwind and cloud, upon Moses on Mount Sinai, upon
Joshua at Jericho, upon Elijah into the cave. And there is no generation where
he did not come down, and it did not escape us. And now, as it is said, if the
Lord is present going up, how did we not know him coming down?" Those going
before answered: "He is the one who was then coming down; that he might not be
recognized going up, he wore a servant's image, that you might not know the
Master's magnificence. And learn a Masterly mystery, and a Lordly device, and
do not hinder a purpose of loving-kindness. For if you had known him coming
down in much humiliation for the sake of the creature, you would have hindered

"Unless we hear his divine voice, we would not open the doors. For no one
unclean passes through here. For this," it says, "is the gate of the Lord; the
righteous shall enter in it." Then the Lord spoke with an honored voice: "I am
the one sought, open to me gates of righteousness; having entered in them I
will give thanks to the Lord"; that is, "What I did and suffered, as many good
things as I did and as many evil things as I received. I will announce the
tokens of the righteous, the gifts of the healings, the successes of the
deeds. Open to me gates of righteousness; having entered in them I will give
thanks to the Lord." Then those holding the gates, knowing the Master's
undefiled voice, came out with fear into worship: "We did not know you,
Master, coming down, we worship you going up." Then he himself passing through
beyond contradiction, and the followers. But they were hindering those
entering from behind saying: "These are the gates of the Lord; the righteous
shall enter in them; not also the unjust? here adulterers and tax-collectors
and plunderers? why is this being done by your holiness? You ratified these
gates, you set us as gatekeepers, you dogmatized a law for us, that These are
the gates of the Lord; the righteous shall enter in them. Has it not been said
concerning you, that He set a command, and it shall not pass? For you said,
Only the righteous shall enter; not also the unjust? here adulterers and
tax-collectors are being led,


here magi and robbers and plunderers are entering? At least first lead in the
righteous, and afterward hand-lead the sinners. There are times when you lead
all these in; it is clear that they repented; but this robber, why do you lead
him in before all into the kingdoms above? where did he change his mind, where
did he repent? not one day in his life did he =61.697= accomplish, nor has he
ever done any good thing; but neither did he have room for even one hour unto
repentance. See, we hold his sins in writing, which he did not wipe out in
repentance, and you bring him in as forerunner into paradise of all the
righteous?" But the Savior: "Do you seek," he says, "his sin, which I before
all wiped out? Let the written record of his sins be idle, because his faith
wiped it out. Let the contract of his wrongs be loosed by the good confession.
He did not repent before the time, but he understood unto the time; before
death he was naked without faith, in the death his faith conquered the sea of
the floodings; in a time of ease he did not know, but in a time of necessity
he recognized the just thing. When my disciples fled from me, this one fled to
me; when Peter was saying, I do not know the man, then this one confessed a
king of heavens; when priests were putting me to death, then this one called a
Lord of life. The work of repentance has been completed for me, who see the
purpose and the eagerness; the just claim of endurance has been done for me,
who bent the heart unto the good. Through the side Adam shut paradise, this
one in the confession opens the gate guarded by the flaming sword. Do not seek
his sin, which you will not find. For this David prophesied so many
generations before: His sin shall be sought, and it shall not be found." Then
the Savior went up, having conquered every authority and power, and all the
armies of the angels met him, and worshipped him as Master, and every Cherubic
order and Seraphic liturgy worshipped him and the Father who begot him. And as
many things as he received also for the Father, he sat of the seat at the
right, he who never stripped the paternal bosom. For even if he came down upon
earth through loving-kindness, yet he was in no way left behind of the throne;
even if he became a perfect human being economically, he in no way underwent a
standing-out from his own natural divinity. For Christ did not become God from
a human being's advance, may it not be, but being God he became a human being,
that he might save the human being. Therefore we do not proclaim a human being
deified, but we confess God who became human; as the divine Paul said:
Therefore every principality and authority were subjected to him, as being
before all creation, or rather maker of the creation, and co-mastering and
co-reigning with his undefiled Father, and with the life-giving Spirit unto
the ag =61.698= es. Amen. For he himself is the one who is going to judge the
inhabited world, and all that is under heaven, and to render to each according
to what he has done. Who then will speak the mercy of the loving-kindness, or
the greatness of his sovereignty? who will express the understanding of his
goodness, or the magnificence of the power? who will narrate the compassions
of his pity, or track out the dignity of the excess? that not leaving the
things above, he came down upon earth for us; that escaping the principalities
and the authorities, he went into an undefiled Virgin's womb; he escaped the
choirs of angels and archangels, and put on a servant's form, that he might
save the human being according to the image from corruption and death; and he
died in the flesh, that he might make us partakers of his immortal nature. And
what shall we give back to him concerning all that he gave back to us? what
thanksgivings, or supplications? Not even if all our members take up voices,
and our hairs bring out speeches, and our whole instrument be filled with
melody, would we be able worthily to hymn the pity and magnificence of his
condescension


that has come to be toward us. But as much as we have room, we hymn the only
all-holy one, fulfilling a prediction, the, "I remembered God, and I was
glad." For through this period I have gone through the great and paradoxical
wonders of the Savior, that at each I may remember and be glad; if indeed we
have worthily had room in the memory, as was fitting. But whether in pretext
or in truth God is announced; therefore let us say with the prophet: "I
remembered God, and I was glad." And may the God of the powers, who has shown
forth loving-kindness and much goodness, receiving our weakness and the
prayers by grace, and having led us into a better good providence, himself
keep our soul unstained, keep the body spotless, the spirit unsoiled, the life
unassailed, the faith unplundered, the brotherly love uninsulted, the
virginity blameless, the friendship not made a byword, the love
unhypocritical, the virtue unstormed, the righteousness unhindered, the faith
bearing fruit. May he spread open the gates of paradise for us, and quench the
flaming sword, and open the door of the kingdom, and prepare the undefiled
enjoyment, and set us at the right, and crown us with the right hand by the
loving-kindness of his goodness; because to him belongs glory, to the Father
and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of the
ages. Amen.
