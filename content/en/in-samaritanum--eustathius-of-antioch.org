DISCOURSE ON THE SAMARITAN WOMAN
We therefore, with unveiled face, behold as in a mirror the all-holy flesh of
the Lord through the form in the image. Having then the eye of the soul
unclouded, looking toward the form of the prototype [that has been formed],
let us glorify the archetype of the image.
