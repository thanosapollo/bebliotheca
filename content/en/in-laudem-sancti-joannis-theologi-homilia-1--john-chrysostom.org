Encomium on Saint John the Theologian.
=61.719=
He has called us together, beloved, to the same place, the most holy John the
Theologian and friend of Christ, not because he has caused calyces of
sweet-scented roses to rise for us, but because he has made the virtues of
spiritual fathers bloom for us. The fragrance of these is the pious
confession, and the salvation of our souls. What rose, tell me, has been so
adorned with its petals, as the most holy John has been glorified with the
God-befitting crown of the saints? For the rose flashes from within its
gold-gleaming fragrance; but the most holy John, the fragrant way of life of
our all-wise shepherd and teacher, shines forth in power from the very summit
of the throne. O John, remembered through the year by those sojourning, but
dwelling as a fellow-citizen with the Ephesians day by day; O John, as a lofty
president you shine forth upon this holy mountain, not granting money to your
citizens, but granting healing to those who ask you; O John, you dashed down
the single-combat she-dragon Artemis with the trophy of the cross, and you
powerfully raised up the once-married bride of Christ, the Church, by the
power of the cross.
O John, you left your father Zebedee upon the boat, and you loved your Master
the Nazarene upon the breast. As two mountains you beheld the two breasts,
between which, having rested sweetly, you theologized him who is known in the
midst of two living creatures: In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was
with God, and the Word was God. And consider with me the paradox of the
apostle. He who was not buried into earth gushes forth earth, and what gushes
is healing manna, and what is emptied is not emptied by time. Then what the
cause is, what the operation, no one would find to say; for the gifts of our
Savior Christ are unchangeable, and the workings of the saints are unfailing.
O John, the weapon of the pious, the truly pious confession, and for all of
them an escape, the refuge toward God.
For what a harbor is to those tossed by storm, this God is to those worn down.
Today for us the most holy John shines forth the beauty of his own virtue; for
he himself has also been read aloud to us, shining forth his own piety as from
a royal image of the ancient book. John, the fair beginning of the faith, the
flourishing root, the unfailing spring, he who among =61.720= all nations is
not a law of that time only, but unto forever the one entrusted with the
august profession of piety; the first firstfruits of the faith, he who spread
the traces of piety for all, he who taught us to emulate the fairest gifts of
love. For we see the bridal chamber of virginity, and we shall embrace the
spring of healings. But let us also unfold the word concerning the light.
Light is the Father, light is the Son, light is the Holy Spirit. Light is the
Father, since he is true God; light is the Son, since he shone forth from the
Father; light is the Holy Spirit, since he proceeds from the Father. And for
this reason true God. The apostle will bear witness for me, saying that God is
light. And, John came, that he might bear witness concerning the light; and,
He was the true light, which enlightens every man coming into the world, that
is, coming into the truth. And the Son, I am the true light; and, I in the
Father and the Father in me. And that the Son is light and sun of
righteousness, the prophet also cries: Shine, shine, Jerusalem. For your light
has come, and the glory of the Lord has risen upon you.
And concerning the Holy Spirit it is sung in the psalms, that, With you is the
fountain of life. In your light we shall see light, that is, in the Son we
shall see the Holy Spirit; light through which we were enlightened, and until
today we are enlightened in our hearts. Light is the Father, since God is
true; light is the Son, since he is the radiance of that light; light is the
Holy Spirit who proceeds from the Father; For no one knows the things of God,
except the Spirit that is in him. Light then is the Holy Spirit, the true,
which is contemplated and understood in the true light. Let there remain then
for us, brothers, the faith unbreakable, an inseparable Trinity. For the
blessed Solomon also says that the threefold cord will not be broken. For God
sanctifies, enlightens, makes alive, sets free, and the things more than
these. And the Son likewise; for all things, as many as the Father does, and
the Holy Spirit likewise sanctifies, enlightens, makes alive, sets free. For
we were set free and we were sanctified and we were enlightened and we lived
again, having been baptized into Father and Son and Holy Spirit, to whom is
the glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
