* 20s

THE Faliscans, having revolted, were on the sixth day subdued and permitted to
surrender. a colony was plantedat Spoletium. then for the
first time an army marched against the Ligurians. The Sardinians
and Corsicans having revolted were reduced to subjection. tuccia , a
Vestal Virgin, was convicted of unchastity. War was declared
against the Illyrians on account of the murder of one of the envoys who had been
dispatched to them, and they were subdued and received in surrender.
The number of praetors was enlarged to four.
transalpine Gauls who had made an incursion into Italy were cut
to pieces. The author states that in that war the
Roman People had under arms 800,000 men, of their own and of the Latin name.
then for the first time Roman armies crossed the Po, and
defeating the Insubrian Gauls in several battles, received their submission.
The consul Marcus Claudius Marcellus having slain the chief of
the Insubrian Gauls, Vertomarus, brought back the spoils of honour.
The Histrians were subjugated. The Illyrians having
gone to war a second time were defeated and their submission was received.
The census was thrice taken by the censors. in the
first census there were registered 270,212 citizens. . . The freedmen were
assigned to four tribes, whereas before they had been dispersed through them all
—the four being the Esquilina, the Palatina, the Suburana, the Collina.
The censor Gaius Flaminius built 'the Flaminian Way and the
Flaminian Circus. colonies were established in the territory taken
from the Gauls, at Placentia and Cremona.
