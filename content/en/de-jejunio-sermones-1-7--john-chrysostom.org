On a proclamation of fasting.
=60.711= Discourse 1.
Today God calls the race to healing. For through the prophet he appointed
fasting as a medical treatment, saying, Sanctify a fast, proclaim a healing.
For since God saw Adam cast out of paradise covered with many wounds, he
assigned fasting to him as healing; well. For what does fasting not heal? And
what passion of the mind does it not make vanish? Driving away the satiety of
incontinence, it renews the heart; emptying out the moisture of life, it calms
the inflammations of anger, plasters the hardness of envy, surgically cuts the
swellings of desires, makes vanish the fevers of pleasures, drives out the
fantasies of vainglory, drives away the sleepless watches of care, cleanses
the rheum of the eyes, thoroughly cleanses the senses of body and soul; for
she is a craftswoman who received the art of healing from above from the
Creator. Let the lover of health then readily give himself to fasting; for
having healed she does not demand medical fees, but even gives a wage besides.
What wage then does she give besides? for it is good to learn this too. She
gives a wage, not obols, but sober thoughts; not gold, but a good character;
not silver, but purity of members; not precious stones, but devout thoughts;
not splendid garments, but the senses of the flesh unstumbling. She gives to
harlots the wage of dignity, to drunkards sobriety, to the stingy generosity,
to lovers of money love of the poor, to haters of brothers brotherly love and
hospitality, to the small-souled long-suffering; and what is greater, having
made alive she joins to God. For it is not, it is not otherwise possible to
converse with God, unless we have first consorted with the medicine of
fasting. For thus from of old some have been shown enemies and others friends
of God, incontinence and self-control having worked each. For Adam having
eaten fled God as an enemy; Cain not yielding the first things to God groaned
and trembled, as unloved of God; those in the time of Noah having lived in
luxury raised against themselves the judge of the judgment; those in Sodom
having fed their pleasures, as enemies of God, received the thunderbolts from
above; food did not allow Esau to be called beloved and firstborn; food and
drink made an enemy of Israel the one who had led them in signs and wonders;
through wine and strong drink the prophet accused the people; wine and
drunkenness deprived =60.712=.20 Herod of the friendship of John and of
Christ. You have known those who through incontinence became enemies of God;
learn also those who through fasting were established as friends of God. For
Abel, not by tasting, but by offering to God, appeared as one who had shown
kindness toward God; Moses having fasted was called a renowned and faithful
friend; Elijah having fasted was taken up as a friend; prophets having fasted
knew the mysteries of God, as friends; Isaiah having fasted described the
six-winged Seraphim;
Ezekiel having become without food contemplated the many-eyed Cherubim; those
in Babylon who had fasted God visited as friends, sending to those in the
furnace the angel, and to the one in the pit Habakkuk; the Ninevites became
friends from enemies through fasting; David likewise a friend from an enemy.
For when he desired the wife of Uriah, he was at enmity with God; but when he
humbled his soul in fasting, he heard, I have found David the son of Jesse, a
man after my heart. John the son of Zacharias, friend of the bridegroom: why?
Because he remained in the wilderness collecting locusts, and gathering wild
honey. Anna: why? Because serving in fasts and prayers, night

and day she ministered to God. Peter and John friends: why? Because without
food they were going up at the ninth hour of prayer toward the temple. Paul a
friend: why? Because by pressing he was bringing the flesh into slavery.
Timothy a friend: why? Because by drinking water he quenched the fire of
pleasures. Cornelius a friend: why? Because without food he was awaiting the
salvation through Peter. The martyrs of Christ friends: why? Because they
despised flesh and temporary life. Do you see how God through fasting calls
the race not only to healing, but also to divine friendship, hastening to make
Adam holy and healthy, sober, a friend, self-controlled and sociable,
dignified and akin? For what is it to be a friend of God, if not to become
akin to God? Become then through fasting a friend of God, become through
fasting akin to God. Do not remain incontinent, do not remain an enemy, do not
remain sick, do not remain a wounded man, do not remain paralyzed. For behold,
today also Christ has stood over you in this fair church, the new Jerusalem;
behold, the Forty Days has stood over you, showing you a spiritual pool, not
healing one sick man in a yearly cycle, but a whole people. Behold Jerusalem,
behold Christ, behold the Forty Days, a spiritual pool. Step in rejoicing,
enjoy her streams glad, wash flesh, wash heart, scour soul, a clean mind
within and without from the filth =60.713= of sin; wash Adam, having heard
Christ saying, Rise, take up your bed, and walk. Does not the one who falls
rise? Stand, having evangelical feet, God-loving thoughts, generous hands,
unbending knees, thighs without share of pleasures, kidneys free of desires,
loin and belly secured, study of the law of God, breast, a clean heart, neck,
an obedient manner, soul, a devout mind, a dignified mouth, a pupil that does
not turn aside, a steady hearing, lips bringing forth the things dear to
Christ.
Become such a one of the Forty Days, as having taken experience of her as a
spiritual pool. For through this Jesus wishes to wash you still more from your
lawlessness; through this the one who takes away the sin of the world wishes
to cleanse you from sin; through this to heal, through this to renew, through
this to purify, through this to bring you again to the measure of stature. And
that I may also tell you something hidden, but let this not hold you, because
of the generosity of the judge, in drowsiness; shall I tell something hidden?
Through fasting Christ wishes to bring you who were once born again by water
and Spirit and divine fire back to the ancient nobility. Behold, I have
revealed to you the mystery of the physician and judge. Therefore I entreat
you and beg, do not, as one who has the medicine ready, remain =60.714=
cutting often; do not, as one who has repentance ready as a mediator, remain
doing the things not dear to God, rolling in the world, and putting off
friendship toward God; do not, as one who has the cleansing, put it off. For
the future is unclear, tomorrow is unclear, today is unclear, the present is
unclear; we are wayfarers in the world; not masters, but strangers, and we are
called sojourners. We have no strength to call the judge out, when we stand
present with the soul; when we are demanded, we do not find a time and a turn
of a moment to borrow. Let them not then, in the way the rich man was of good
cheer, find us, those who demand the soul from us, in a night of incontinence,
in a gloom of vice, in a darkness of greed; but in a day of fasting, in a day
of dignity, in a day of brotherly love, in a light of piety, in a dawn of
faith, of almsgiving and of prayer; that having found us sons of day they may
lead us to the Sun of righteousness, not having left standing the storehouses
of vice, but having emptied and demolished them, and having renewed ourselves
through repentance and fasting, by the grace of Christ, to whom be the glory
and the power unto the ages. Amen.


On fasting. Discourse 2.
Again the holy fast calls us toward enjoyment of goods, again it prepares
contestants, again it wishes us to be courageous, that it may show us victors
of the passions, and display the beauty of the soul more splendid, and through
the toils weave crowns for our heads. For what is fasting, if not a crown of
contests, and a procurer of wages, and a way of salvation? For fasting
transfers from slavery into freedom, leads back from captivity into the
fatherland; fasting heals the wounds of the soul, fasting renews a soul
decayed by sins, sinews the soul, makes the mind courageous, brings in the
fear of God, sacks the passions, calms the thoughts. In days of fasts
pleasures are quenched, and virtues bloom, and the beauty of self-control is
shown most pure, and body passes over into soul, having become spiritual
beyond nature. Rejoice in fasting, and do not be gloomy, as the hypocrites;
they disfigure their faces toward human viewing, destroying the wage of the
fast. For the word is the Savior's: In fasting anoint your head, wash the face
with well-doing, shine with the virtues, that you may appear fasting to me
alone, who look upon the hidden things. Do not be displeased, beloved, toward
fasting, the mother of the virtues, the root of the goods, the fountain of
self-control, the guardian of piety, the foster-companion of the saints, the
tent-mate of the angels, enmity of the devil, friend of the Spirit; through
which pleasures flee us and demons withdraw, and anger grows faint, and desire
is put to death, and virtues live and shine in us; through which the passions
are still, and the tumult of pleasures is calmed, and the mind sails as in a
calm, having well completed the triple waves of the winter of wickedness
through this self-control, and having moored the merchant-ship at the harbor
of virtue. You have then the gain unplundered, the cargo unshipwrecked, when
you trade with the commandments of the Spirit, when having fixed the thought
in the fear of God you also beat off the present things, hearing the divine
Scripture saying: Do not love the world, nor the things in the world. Let us
not love the luxury of this world, which as a heavy cargo becoming to the boat
of the soul works it under water. Or do you not know that it stands only as
far as the throat, and after the throat all luxury is idle; but the punishment
born from it has an endless torment? Luxury darkens those who are not sober,
dims the beauty of the soul, works a breathing corpse; it prepares one to do
as many things as are not lawful, it stirs the organs of pleasure, it
increases the war, =60.714= it wakes the desires; you are then inactive toward
virtue.
For when the members have been loosened, and you have been robbed of your mind
by much wine, you no longer have strength to contend in return, and you become
easy for the enemies to attack, like a ship driven by a wind, not having a
steersman able to fight against the assaults of the waves, which is thrown
this way and that, and nothing else remains for it except to expect shipwreck.
Do not then, I entreat, after the fast let us have leisure for luxury, nor let
us quench the many toils by carelessness; but joining toils to toils, so let
us expect the recompense. For when someone joins wage to wage, he has much
gain stored up. Let us not quench the flower of the fast, nor let us yield the
mind to shipwreck by luxury toward pleasures, lest with incurable evils the
soul


we store up. And if after the fast you wish to tend the body as being weak,
tend it with due measure; that, as a merchant-ship carrying a light load, the
soul may be higher than the waves of wickedness; and if the trials of the
desires are stirred, it may be able to be sober toward counter-contest, toward
steering of thoughts, that we may keep the boat unshipwrecked. For we have a
sister of the fast as an ally toward help, I mean prayer; whether it be death,
or trial, or some other of the evils, it is dissolved by prayer and fasting.
And what do I say? Fasting puts demons to flight, and dissolves the tyranny of
the devil, especially if it has prayer working with it; fasting and prayer led
Elijah up into the heavens, delivered the Ninevites from death, fasting kept
Daniel unharmed from the lions, fasting preserved the three children unhurt in
the midst of the furnace, fasting counted Moses worthy of the unseen glory,
fasting made Elisha a ruler of the prophets. And what do I still say? If it
were not a medicine of salvation and a procurer of life, God would not from
the beginning have commanded to fast. For if Adam had fasted, death would not
have drawn near him, he would not have fallen from the dignity given him by
God, and been cast out of the paradise of luxury, and been condemned to this
toilsome life; but since he obeyed a law of the belly, for this reason he came
down into death. Fasting is a diet of angels; and what is greater, the Savior
fasted, not himself needing the medicine of fasting, but that he might sow in
us patterns of fasting toward salvation. Take then fasting as a
fellow-traveler of the whole of life, that it may keep you, that it may
deliver you from death, that it may save you from trials, and lead you into
the harbor of the kingdom of the heavens, by the grace of our Lord and God
Jesus Christ, with whom to the Father be the glory with the Holy Spirit, unto
the ages of the ages. Amen. =60.715= On fasting Discourse 3. A little while
ago, from what you have heard, you have learned how generous is the prelude of
fasting, brethren. For going without food, we were banqueted on the elements
of the world as on relishes, not slightly, but rather vehemently having seen
God with the eye of the soul stretching out the heaven, adorning the
firmament, bridling the sea, nourishing from earth. And consider with me the
paradox: for women bear an infant, and then offer food; so that if
an infant does not come forth, the teat is barren; but earth, before Adam came
to be, brought forth food, and before birth a table was set out for the child,
before birth a table was set out for the first-formed. For Let it sprout herb,
says God, and then he formed the man, dust; not idly, but that coming forth
from the earth, and walking upon the earth, and calling this mother, and
looking toward this, he may not be lifted up, but may offer to God that
Abrahamic voice saying: And I am earth and ashes. For this confession the
hospitable man once offered to God as an embassy; this it is good for us too
to offer. For this voice is a most secure bridle of nature; this does not
allow one to walk unordered, if anyone should bear to be in love with earth,
being himself earth. For if he loves earth, let him possess as property his
own body, let him show himself fruit-bearing, let him render the virtues as
tributes to the farmer of the creation. My Father, he says, is the farmer. And
when will someone desire gold, who knows himself as earth? and when would he
fall in love with a splendid garment, who calls himself dust? when will he be
eager to come into a longing for dignity, having confessed himself earth, For
that the one who has practiced this voice will not speak ill to the neighbor,
will not move tongue or right hand against the one nearby, is surely clear to
everyone. So wise and divine is the voice of the hospitable man: For I, he was
saying, he says, am, not your formed thing, which was true, not your image,
which was unlying, not a divine inbreathing, which was exact, not a rational
statue, which was secure; but having silenced all these things through
reverence and given them over to forgetfulness, earth upon the


judge he calls himself, and ashes. Let someone who has reckoned this voice
practice gentleness, let him emulate humility, let him yield the first things
to the neighbor, in dignity let him not be lifted up, in power let him not be
inflamed, in a belt let him not raise his brows, in wealth let him not hope,
in health let him not kick, in fluency let him not boast. For he who today is

=60.716= who have hoped upon the uncertainty of the present surge. For the

successive waves each day raising the storm of the soul. For one it puffs up
upon honor, another upon a belt, another it throws over the neck toward power,
another it leads over a cliff by wealth or beauty; and all, so to speak, in
desires of a wind it has pushed into the depth of the passions. But you,
faithful one, according to the Apostle, man of God, flee the waves of desire,
turn aside from the surges of life; and having become higher than the
passions, as than waves, sail the sea of life, having entrusted the little
boat of the soul to the saving wood. Let the cross become a mast for you,
faith an anchor, hope a rope, prayer an oar, right thoughts tillers, Christ a
sail, the Holy Spirit a fair-sailing wind, the Father of all the steersman.
If you sail thus, the conclusion of life is not unclear to you. For having
sailed thus Noah crossed unharmed that world-wide deep; having sailed thus
Abraham was yielding the first things to Lot, and was naming himself earth,

sailed thus Jacob has remained guileless and unstormed, asking from God only
bread and a garment, not wealth; for those hastening to be rich fall into
trial. Having sailed thus Joseph escaped the storm of the plot and of
pleasure; having sailed thus Moses, and Joshua too and the rest of the choir
of prophets and of the righteous, as into an unwavering anchorage, in the
right hand of the Creator, they set themselves as a boat; For the souls of the
righteous, he says, are in the hand of God, together with sailing well having
the divine right hand as the anchorage, those who converse with God, and call
themselves earth and ashes. But O the madness, O the standing-out of the wits;
for Abraham converses with God, has been appointed father of nations, the
sojourner an heir of the promise, was offering the firstborn as a sheep, and
was calling himself earth and ashes; but those now, not masters of a very
little earth, but having become stewards, of a temporary or daily dignity

On fasting. Discourse 5.
Today, beloved, let us worship the physician. For behold the fast stands over
us, granting salvation of souls and of bodies, as supplying a paradoxical
resurrection, as oil for us to be anointed toward spiritual contests. Prayer
and fasting are the work of the angels, fasting a gate of paradise, fasting a
cleansing of sinners, fasting boldness toward God, fasting purity of heart,
fasting the chief point of all the goods given us by God, fasting a
sanctification of soul, fasting health of body, fasting security of a house, a
pedagogue of the young, a guardian of infants, a chastener of the old, good
order and settledness of women. But when it is present together with prayer
and with fasting and almsgiving, then the worker of piety is found rich
according to God. For he who supplies to the poor lends to God. Have you seen
God become a surety on behalf of men? For this reason also the prophet,
reproaching the Jews as not completing the fast from all their power, was
saying: Not this fast have I chosen, says the Lord, the humbling of a man of
his soul. For what profit is it to fast with the mouth, and to murder with the
works? what is the profit of abstaining from foods, and being defiled by
fornication? You do not eat meat, but you devour the flesh of your brother.
What gain is it not to be gladdened with wine, and to be drunk with money?
what is the profit of not eating bread, and being drunk with anger; what gain
is it to waste away with fasting, and to revile the neighbor? what is the
benefit of being continent of foods, and seizing what belongs to others? what
necessity is there to dry the body, and not to feed the hungry? what is the
benefit of crushing the members, and not showing mercy to widows and orphans?
what advantage is it to live in cares and anxieties, and storm-tossed orphans


not to rescue?
There is no profit for men to bear only a name of Christians, but not to have
acquired good works; for dignities are not honored with God, but works are
sought. Since Judas too was among the apostles, and enjoyed the equal honor
with the others; but having loved love of money he received a noose; as even
now many wear garments of sheep, but within they are rapacious wolves. If you
wish to fast, hate =60.718= love of money; love of money is the great evil.
You fast? Flee adultery, flee fornication, flee slander, falsehood, reviling,
enmity, blasphemy and every meddlesomeness. You fast? Flee greed, plunder,
strife and soul-destroying envy. You fast? Flee anger, fornication, jealousy,
perjury and every injustice. You fast? Flee gluttony the mother of every vice,
which separates us even from God himself; gluttony which brings us down as far
as the abysses of destruction. If you fast for God, flee every thing which God
hates, and he receives your repentance. For the present time is of repentance,
and not of relaxation; repentance raises the fallen soul, establishes as a
friend to God the one who had become alienated; repentance wakes the shattered
soul, sets upright the one that limps, heals the crushed, makes healthy the
wounded. Let us run then toward repentance, brethren, let us run. For it is a
time to repent, and not to live in luxury and to lie down; a time to lament
and to weep, and not to be titillated and to be lifted; a time of confession
and of mourning and of groanings, and not of carelessness and sloth. For
repentance is a procurer of the kingdom of heavens, and an entrance of
paradise, and enjoyment of eternal luxury; repentance a loosing of sins, a
cleanser of stains; repentance, a mother of salvation, a tripping of the
devil, a vanishing of passions; repentance dissolves the bonds of sins, saves
the souls that have been despaired of, leads the darkened by light, turns
those who have been made far toward God, calls back the wandered; repentance
is a guiding and a consolation of all the given-up; repentance is a clinic
and a saving medicine of every weakness of soul and of body. But let us return
to the matter at hand. You fast? Do not overlook a poor man. You fast? Look at
the tears of the widow. You fast? Wrap the naked. You fast? Look at orphans
groaning.
You fast? If you have, show mercy; but if =60.719= you do not have, at least
do not seize what belongs to others. You fast? Stop your tongue from evil, and
your lips from speaking deceit, and it is enough for you toward salvation. You
fast? Do not disfigure your face, as the hypocrites, do not be sullen, but
anoint your head, and wash your face, that you may not appear to men fasting,
but to the one who searches hearts and kidneys. If you fast, fast thus as
Christ commanded, lest you both endure the toil, and lose the wage. For if you
fast, see that you do not lend your silver at interest. You fast? Tear the
handwriting of violent contracts; forgive, that it may be forgiven you; let go
the grief to your brother, that you may be let go; show mercy, that you may be
shown mercy; let go the fault to the neighbor. You fast? Have mercy on him
whom you wronged, never envy a brother, nor again hate anyone. You fast? Do
not be vainglorious. You fast? Flee fornication, which defiles our soul and
the body; fornication, which separates us from God and from the saints;
fornication, the procurer of the eternal and unquenchable fire. You fast? Do
not slander a poor man, lest you anger the one who made him. You fast? Feed
the hungry, give drink to the thir =60.720= sty, clothe the naked, console
strangers, visit the sick, of those in prison do not


forget, have mercy on the oppressed, take care of the afflicted and those who
lament, become compassionate, meek, good, gentle, a peacemaker; become
long-suffering, merciful, not remembering injuries, not quarrelsome, devout,
that God may accept your fasting, and grant you the gain of repentance with
much addition. For nothing, a brother, can help us on that fearful day of
judgment, as repentance and almsgiving. These things I exhort, brethren, that
we may keep them securely, and receive a wage from God; that we may hear that
blessed voice which says: Come, you blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom
prepared for you from the foundation of the world; for I hungered, and you
gave me to eat; I thirsted, and you gave me drink; I was a stranger, and sick
and naked and in prison, and you ministered to me. Of which voice may we all
be counted worthy to obtain, by the grace and love of mankind of our Lord
Jesus Christ, to whom belongs glory, honor and worship, now and always, and
unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

On fasting. Discourse 6.
Again a time of fasting, again an occasion of luxury, again a view of
contests, again the trumpet of Christ proclaiming the good contest has
assembled the contestants upon the wrestling-ground; again the yearly presence
of Christ is proclaimed. Let us all cleanse ourselves, that we may receive
him. For if the presence of a mortal king being proclaimed forces those about
to receive him to prepare the houses with fear and trembling, that they may
make the dwellings worthy of the one about to dwell; how much more ought we
who are about to receive the true king to prepare ourselves worthy of the one
about to dwell in us?
Let Jeremiah the prophet come forward to us today, who was sent from the Lord,
having heard: Behold I have set you today a prophet to the nations, to root
out and to plant, to pull down and to rebuild. Let us obey this man; he is the
one who prepares the temples of the great king. Let us root out pleasures, and
plant self-control; let us pull down vice, and rebuild virtues; let us drive
away falsehood, and harvest truth; let us throw off pride, let us take up
repentance; let us cut out anger, and receive meekness. For Christ says: Upon
whom shall I rest? Upon the meek and quiet and the one who trembles at my
words. Do not prepare a house from stones; For the Most High does not dwell in
things made with hands. Prepare a rational temple; adorn it with virtues; the
present time contributes to you. About this Paul says: Behold the time is
shortened; that even those who have wives may be as not having; and those who
buy, as not holding, and those who use this world, as not using it up; for the
fashion of this world is passing. He does not need gold nor silver, he does
not need costly stones nor great buildings, but he needs a soul made bright,
an unadulterated repentance. The forerunners of the earthly king who announce
this one's presence carry nothing heavenly, but all earthly things; one a
shield, another a breastplate, another golden dragons, another garments shot
with gold; but the king himself bears the authority of all in himself, the
purple and the diadem. But the forerunners of the heavenly king carry nothing
earthly, but all things worthy of the king; Moses announcing the royal law,
Isaiah fasting, Jeremiah prayer, John a baptism of repentance; but the king
himself bears


the purple and the diadem, through which he sets all free; For he has a
purple; the body is a purple, and the diadem is the word. John having seen
this cries and says: We all in part.
Moses legislates for Israel, Isaiah prophesies to Israel, Jeremiah like
=60.720= wise, but I a baptism of repentance to the world; but no one sets
free. He has come who has the authority of all; he is the one proclaimed by
us, he is the king of glory; he does not grant grace to one nation, but
becomes salvation for the whole world. The law had a lamb; but it was
slaughtered on behalf of one nation, but it did not let go sins; for it was a
type. After this lamb Isaiah proclaims: As a sheep he was led upon slaughter,
and as a lamb voiceless before the one shearing it, so he does not open his
mouth. Who will recount his generation? his judgment was taken from him. This
is the true lamb, the one proclaimed through words, and now appearing through
works; this is the one who gave himself up on behalf of the whole world; this
is the lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. The prophets through
letters prophesied the things about to be, they did not show the deeds; if the
deeds come to be, they make the letters true. David prophesied concerning the
things about to come to be toward Jesus; but the deeds not yet being present,
the letters had a shadow, God having ordered this. For if the Jews had known
the deed appearing through letters, they would have wiped out the letters,
fearing the conviction from deeds. For David was saying in the second psalm:
Why did nations snort, and peoples study empty things? the madness of Herod
and of Caiaphas; The kings of the earth stood together and the rulers upon the
same; he speaks the plot against the Master Against the Lord, and against his
Christ. Let us break their bonds, and throw off from us their yoke. And
speaking the things of the cross, in the twenty-first he was saying: Many dogs
surrounded me, fat bulls held me. They dug my hands and my feet, they numbered
all my bones. And they themselves considered and looked upon me; they divided
my garments to themselves, and upon my clothing they cast a lot.
And the letters had a shadow; but when the deeds came, then the shadow was
withdrawing, and the Jews see the hands and the feet nailed from their own
house, their conviction containing these things in their own books; and they
can no longer wipe out the letters. For both have become evident; the letters
agreed with the deeds. Isaiah was saying As a sheep he was led upon slaughter,
and as a lamb voiceless before the one shearing it, so he does not open his
mouth. His judgment was taken. Who will recount his generation? Jews were
reading the letters, and were not thinking the deeds; For the law had a shadow
of the goods to come, not itself =60.721= the image. But when the time came
for the deeds to become manifest, and the true lamb was being led upon
slaughter, and the true sheep was being sacrificed on behalf of the whole
world, and the true cleansing of men was coming to be, and the true blood was
being poured out on behalf of the salvation of the whole world, and the things
by Jews were being staged; and as the prophecy of Isaiah was being fulfilled
the Jews were put to shame. For if the Jews should wish to deny, I read David
to them, I read Isaiah, I bring forward their conviction from their own house.
Moses too comes advocating with me concerning the one crucified; who wishing
to proclaim to them the one about to be crucified, proclaimed through letters.
For this reason he said: Even if I am silent, the works cry. What works? The
Jews do not know; for the shadow of the


law lies upon them. But the time comes, when the deeds become manifest, and
the shadow withdraws, and the deeds establish letters. Serpents once came out
in the wilderness, and were biting the men; and Moses made a bronze serpent
and crucified it, and ordered everyone being bitten to come forward and obtain
healing; God having arranged this beforehand in a type, that, when the truth
comes, the shadow may withdraw and type may show the truth. And that what was
coming to be was a type of the truth, Moses was saying thus: This is not
truth; it is a type; the truth is about to be. For you will see your life
hanging before your eyes night and day, and you will not believe upon your
life; calling Christ the life hanging before the Jews, and Jews not =60.722=
believing that he himself is the life. And why did Moses hang a serpent upon
the cross? As a type of Christ, since through a serpent and wood sin came to
be; and it was necessary that Christ through the wood dissolve the sin through
the wood, and having drawn up into himself the curse through the serpent make
it vanish; that having become a curse on our behalf, he may grant us
righteousness of God. For he did not know sin, and he was crucified on our
behalf, that we may become righteousness of God. But the serpent hanging upon
the cross was not truth; it was a type; and the shadow was hiding the truth.
The truth came, Christ; he went up upon the wood. Then I read to Jews the
thing of Moses, and I stop their mouths: And you will see your life hanging
before your eyes night and day, and you will not believe upon your life. And
how was the blessed David saying to you concerning him? He went up into
height, he captivated captivity, he gave gifts among men. He went up into
height upon the cross; he captivated the devil, he loosed death, he nailed
sin, he threw the curse and the serpent from the wood. When the truth has
come, the types are thrown; he himself sets a trophy against the devil, and he
gives also to the subjects under him the authority against the devil, saying
to them: For you under a curse, for you under law, for you under witness; for
you war, for you victory. Behold, I have given you authority to tread upon
serpents and scorpions, and upon all the power of the Enemy. To him be the
glory and the power unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

On fasting. Discourse 7.
Jeremiah the prophet has come today too, the one who prepares the temples of
the great king, and he converses with us saying: Fallow for yourselves
fallows, and do not sow in thorns. Now he converses without distinction with
all, having been trained by the Master. For of old, not wishing to speak
toward a disobedient people, he suffered those things which he himself
related. For seeing the race of men mixed in sins, he was not willing to
speak, saying: So then murder and theft and adultery have been poured upon the
earth, and for this reason I hold back the word, until they cleanse
themselves. As a faithful servant entrusted with the precious myrrh does not
throw this down in a vessel that has been mired, lest the myrrh lose the
fragrance; in the same way the prophet too, having the word more precious than
myrrh, seeing the men mired, holds back from sowing the word. For whom this
comes to be as a fire burning in his inwards, and he was being let go from
every side, and he was not able to bear it. For God was saying to him: You
were sent to proclaim; why are you silent? you were sent to sow; why do you
hold back the seed? Sow without distinction what I entrusted to you, sow also
upon the thorns, sow also upon the


rocky places, sow also upon the good earth. You are a debtor of the sowing,
and the earth of the fruit-bearing. If thorns come up, and choke the seed, you
will not be the cause; if the good earth bears fruit, it renders also the seed
of the good earth. You are a servant; I entrusted the silver to you; give it
to the bankers, and I when I come will demand it from the bankers. If they
offer me double what I entrusted to them, I will set them from small things
upon the great, from the earthly upon the spiritual. But if having received
the seed they do not prepare themselves worthy of bearing fruit, but receive
the cares of the world, which is thorns, they will become worthy of fire. And
if also into the rocky places a depth of earth is not found; that is, if a
hard-hearted one is found, and does not have faith, but dries up the seed;
that one will be demanded a penalty of his hard-heartedness. In the same way
also those entrusted with the silver, as many as bring double, will be
entrusted with the greater things; but whoever offers only the talent which he
was entrusted, will be condemned to punishment. But you proclaim without
distinction; for you were sent as a physician to the weak. For the farmer too
farms the unfarmed earth, and sows the unsown. For the =60.722= farmed and
sown earth does not have need of being farmed, or of being sown in a second
time, nor does the healthy man have need of the physician; so neither does the
righteous man of the teaching. For it is not the one who sees who wishes to
look up, but the blind. For so the Master says in the Gospels: I did not come
to call righteous, but sinners to repentance.
In sequence Paul too was saying: Christ Jesus came into the world to save
sinners, of whom I am first. So Peter too was saying in the Acts: Men
brethren, let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of flesh and of
spirit. And do not say: We have Abraham as father. For I say to you, that God
is able from these stones to raise children to Abraham. For this reason the
prophet, having been trained to proclaim without distinction, was crying:
Fallow for yourselves fallows, and do not sow in thorns. For as the unfarmed
earth is fallowed in wood and iron of the oxen drawing, and so the farmer puts
down the seed; so also upon men let us too take the wood and the iron and the
oxen drawing. Let the wood be for us the cross; the iron, the nails; the oxen,
the law and the prophets, drawing us by the teaching toward the one hanged
upon the cross; that having seen us prepared, he may send down the Holy Spirit
unto sanctification of the waters, and unto regeneration of those fallowed
through the teaching, according to the blessed David saying: You will send
forth your Spirit, and they will be created, and you will renew the face of
the earth. Properly the man was named, because He breathed into his face, and
the man became a living soul. And David also says: Come toward him and be
enlightened, and your faces shall not be put to shame.
And again in the Gospels Christ having called the disciples, breathed into
their face: Receive Holy Spirit. Let us fallow for ourselves fallows, put away
the old vice, root out the thorns of disobedience; for when Adam disobeyed, he
was cast out of paradise. This he received from the Master: Cursed is the
earth in your works; thorns and thistles it will spring up for you. The
prophet knowing this was proclaiming: Fallow for yourselves fallows, and do
not sow upon thorns; showing through the word, that we should renew ourselves
=60.723= by repentance, and root out the penalty of the disobedience, the
thorns. And thorns in the man are strife, jealousy, anger, divisions,
uncleannesses, greeds, adulteries, murders. Immediately


Eve conceiving bears Cain, who has become a leader of jealousy, an inventor of
murders, a father of hypocrisy, a craftsman of falsehood. For being asked by
the Master: Where is Abel your brother? he is ready and begets falsehood
saying: Am I a keeper of my brother? But God wishing to persuade him from the
deeds, that he knows all things, said: A voice of blood of your brother cries
toward me. The blood does not cry with a word; for it does not share in word;
but the deed through the cruelty triumphs the brother-killer. For this reason
the prophet cries: Fallow for yourselves fallows, and do not sow upon thorns.
You heard in the Acts of the apostles Simon the Magus, how not having fallowed
nor having rooted out the thorns, he came forward to the apostles to receive
the baptism. And such as he went down, such he also came up, not because the
baptism was without power, but because this man disobeyed the one crucified,
the one nailed. He did not believe the prophets, who as oxen draw the symbols
of the cross, the iron and the wood, and renew by the teaching the race of
men. For this reason he is not loosed from the curse of Adam, but again he
receives a second curse, and lawlessness is added upon his lawlessness, that
he may not enter in righteousness of God; as the baptism benefited him
nothing. He came forward to the apostles, saying: Take gold, and give me this
authority, that on whomsoever I lay the hands, he may receive Holy Spirit. But
Peter said to him: You have no part, nor lot in this part, because you wished
to acquire the gift of God through money. This man did not obey the prophets,
he did not fallow his rational land; this man did not even obtain the Holy
Spirit proclaimed by the prophets. Fear the example, and fallow for yourselves
fallows, and do not sow upon thorns. If you cut out the thorns, you will
receive the seed, you bear fruit, and you become a sharer of the Holy Spirit.
Thus Cornelius having obeyed the =60.724= prophet fallowed himself, cut out
the thorns, and was bearing fruit to God. For an angel appeared to him saying:
Cornelius, your almsgivings have gone up before God. This good farmer rooted
out the thorns, fallowed his own man, his fruit went up before God. Do you see
a fruit of repentance? From earth it went up into heaven, and has stood before
God with boldness. For as the good works advocate with boldness, so also those
that are otherwise accuse with eagerness. You heard the good works advocating
on behalf of Cornelius; hear also the murder of Cain accusing with eagerness;
for so God says toward Cain: A voice of blood of your brother cries toward me;
and the penalty being defined, that Groaning and trembling you shall be upon
the earth. Hear also the wage of almsgiving and of prayer, what the angel
says: Send to Joppa, and send for Peter, and this man will teach you what you
must do. Peter came, and as he began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell upon him,
just as also upon the apostles. Do you see, the wage of fasting and of prayer
and of almsgiving the Holy Spirit being given? This is the time of fasting and
of almsgiving; this time Elijah having seen beforehand, fasted forty days; for
this reason he is taken up with a fiery chariot, and until today he is in a
body. This time Moses prophesied, having fasted forty days; for this reason he
received a law toward correction of those of the same race. This time Christ
taught us, having fasted forty days; that he may train us to receive readily
this cause, through which the rational land is fallowed, through which thorns
are plucked out, through which desires are quenched, through which the works
of the devil are dissolved, through which the waters of the Jordan have been
sanctified, through which a man is born again, through which the Holy Spirit
comes down, through which life and resurrection. This the


prophets prophesied, this Christ taught; let us love this, let us receive
this; let us add almsgiving and prayer, that we may obtain the Holy Spirit, as
Cornelius also; and let us glorify Father and Son and Holy Spirit. To him be
the glory unto the ages. Amen.
