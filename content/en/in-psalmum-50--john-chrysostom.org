BY THE SAME. On David the king and Paul the apostle, concerning
repentance; and on various sayings of the Psalter that have their end in
Christ; and that we should not despair of our own salvation.
=55.527=
Just now you heard the hymn-writer David crying out: Have mercy on me, O
God, according to your great mercy, and according to the multitude of
your compassions blot out my transgression. And again, Create in me a
clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within my inward parts,
David cries. And for what reason does the prophet say, Have mercy on me,
O God, according to your great mercy? Because where there is mercy,
salvation is unexamined; where there is mercy, a tribunal does not sit;
where there is mercy, accounts are not demanded. Once to be shown mercy
=55.528= I seek: set me free from the sickness. And why does David cry,
Create in me a clean heart, O God?
He who after a multitude of achievements, he who after so many trophies,
he who killed Goliath and strangled the lion, he who also had such great
boldness of the Holy Spirit, says: Have mercy on me, O God, according to
your great mercy, and according to the multitude of your compassions
blot out my transgression. Me, a man who was a farmer, you made a king;
when I had become king, you appointed me a prophet; in the war you made
me =55.529= victorious, who overthrew Goliath not by nature of body but
by power of faith; you clothed me with purple; you cast out another and
brought me in; you have given me wealth. That you may be justified in
your words, and prevail when you are judged. What sin made old, grace
has made new. And what will you give, O prophet? I will teach the
lawless your ways, and the ungodly shall turn back to you. Leaving aside
to speak of the kingship, he speaks of the prophecy. Thus you honored
me: you revealed your Son to me and made him known to me.
I learned that you have a Son born as man. I came to know that you have
one who shares your throne. I proclaimed as gospel to the inhabited
world the cross, the tomb, the descent, the resurrection. I spoke of his
tribunal; I spoke of the salvation of the nations; I spoke of the
election of the apostles, I spoke of the casting out of the Jews, I
spoke of the calling of the Church; I spoke of the choir of virgins, I
spoke of the sitting at the right hand. For behold, the hidden and
secret things of your wisdom you have made known to me. And where did he
make these things known? Hear the prophet himself speaking of his
descent from heaven: And he shall come down as rain upon a fleece.
Because when the Son of God arrived he did not come shaking the
inhabited world, nor casting lightnings, nor sending forth thunderbolts,
nor with the Godhead naked. For if he had presented the Godhead naked,
the mountains would not have borne it; the sun would have been quenched.
If he had shown the essence naked, the moon would have vanished, the
earth would have perished, the nature of us all would have withered. For
this reason, coming gently and without a sound, with no one knowing, he
came gently, and dwelt in a virgin womb. O new and paradoxical things!
The stone is melted, and a womb is not melted. The mountains are
destroyed, and the womb bore God.
How shall I interpret it? How shall I set it forth? O madness of the
blasphemer! How did the handmaid bear God? You who examine the things
above, speak of the things below. He who is present everywhere, how was
he in a womb, and was in heaven? But I, that he both sat upon the throne
and dwelt in the womb of the virgin, I know; but the how, I do not know.
I receive it by faith, I do not over-inquire with reasoning; but that he
appeared, but that he is about to come, not disturbing,


nor throwing into tumult, not destroying, nor being dissolved. And he
shall come down as rain upon a fleece.
You spoke of the kingship, you spoke also of his betrayal: He who eats
my bread has magnified a heel against me. Speak also of his tribunal:
Why did the nations rage, and the peoples imagine vain things? Speak
also of the cross: They pierced my hands and my feet, they numbered all
my bones. Speak also of his gall: They gave me gall for my food, and for
my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. Speak also of his anointing:
You have anointed my head with oil. Speak also of his baptism: Beside
the water of rest he has nourished me. Speak also of his mysteries: You
have prepared a table before me in the presence of those who afflict me.
Speak also of his cup: Your cup that inebriates me, as most excellent.
Speak also of the casting out of the Jews: I will not accept calves from
your house, nor he-goats from your flocks. Speak also of the taunts from
the Jews: He hoped in the Lord; let him deliver him, let him save him,
for he delights in him. Speak also of the salvation of the nations: Ask
=55.530= of me, and I will give you the nations as your inheritance.
Speak also of his tomb: For you will not abandon my soul to Hades, nor
will you give your holy one to see corruption. Speak also of his
ascension into the heavens: God has gone up with a shout, the Lord with
the voice of a trumpet. Speak also of the election of the apostles: In
place of your fathers, sons have been born to you. Speak also of the
choir of virgins: Virgins shall be brought to the king after him. Speak
also of the calling of the Church: The queen stood at your right hand,
clothed in a gold-woven garment, embroidered. Speak also of his sitting
at the right hand: The Lord said to my Lord: Sit at my right hand, until
I make your enemies a footstool for your feet. O David, you have
announced the whole prophecy, and why do you cry: Create in me a clean
heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within my inward parts? You are a
king, you are girt with a diadem, you were clothed with purple. But he
says: These are leaves, and night, and a dream. That beauty I seek.
Give me the Holy Spirit, that you may speak to me again, and I may speak
to you. For the Spirit has departed from me, as a dove seeing mire. I
want her to return. Then I will come and appear before your face. I
cannot bear it, since I have lost my boldness. You see David confessing
to God. See how great an evil sin is. And what sort of sin? Adultery and
murder and transgression of the law, setting aside the words of God. And
in saying these things I do not accuse the prophet, but I announce his
swift repentance. For it was fitting for the prophet to fulfill what is
written: Speak you first your sins, that you may be justified; for this
also justified him. Saying, I have sinned against the Lord. But the
prophet Nathan came, and said: And the Lord has taken away your sin.
Brief was the sin, briefer the confession.
For since he sinned with the wife of Uriah, he was worsted by the sin,
and having written a psalm, he said: Have mercy on me, O God, according
to your great mercy. And through the whole confession he received
release from all sin. But how was Paul also shown mercy? Saul, Saul, why
do you persecute me? O loving-kindness of the Master! To a man he says:
Why do you persecute me? He imitates his own Father. For that one also
addresses the Hebrew people: My people, what have I done to you, or in
what have I wearied you? Answer me. And his Son was saying: Saul, Saul,
why do you persecute me? What then did Paul say? Who are you, Lord, who
speak to me? O well-disposed servant! At once he confessed the lordship,
saying, Who are you, Lord? And the Lord said to him: I am Jesus the
Nazarene, whom you persecute; but rise, enter into Damascus, and there
it will be told you what you must do. And he who was once a wolf is now
a sheep; he who was once a persecutor is now an evangelist; he who was
once a weed is now wheat; he who was once a pirate and a sinker of ships
is now a helmsman of a people; he who was once laying waste the


Church is now entrusted with it; he who was once cutting down the vines
has now become a planter; he who was once lead has now become gold.
And you cry out, beloved, that the tumult of the sins is great, and at
once you hear: But great is the Master's loving-kindness. For God became
man, and entered into a virgin womb, and dwelt there. And whom did he
call? Magi. After those, whom? A =55.531= harlot. After her, whom? A robber.
After him, whom? A blasphemer. O new and paradoxical things! The
first-fruits of the calling are the tyranny of sin. Because the affairs
of the inhabited world were in a bad state, men were held by a sleep of
sin, and by Judaism, and by much despair. For this reason, then, having
come, from the outset he called the first-fruits of the Church, so that
no one after these things might despair of his own salvation. Are you
ungodly? Think of the robber. Are you unclean? Think of the harlot. Are
you a blasphemer? Think of Paul, the blasphemer. Are you an unbeliever?
Think of the magi. I too know of what sort is the despair of sin. The
devil stands by, sharpening a sword, and saying these words to you:
You have spent your whole life dwelling with harlots; after that you
have sworn falsely, you have committed adultery throughout all the days
of the present life. These are the devil's words. But you dare the
opposite. Have you fallen? But rise. Have you fornicated? Repent. Have
you committed adultery? Change your mind. Your repentance is small; but
the Master's loving-kindness is great. As long as you breathe, even if
you lie on the very bed, =55.532= repent; the straitness of the time
brings on the loving-kindness of God. Smear on these medicines, and you
would allay the inflammation. For I know, beloved, that the wounds of
the soul are many, but each wound has its own fitting medicine prepared.
You have a Church being brought to completion, a presence of the Spirit.
You have an assembly of martyrs, and many other things that can call you
back from sin to righteousness.
You have heard the mean sins of the righteous; see the swiftness of the
repentance through words and tears. For David himself also cries saying:
With my tears I will wet my couch. And Paul used to say: But I was shown
mercy, because I persecuted Christ in ignorance. Therefore let us also
not despair of our own salvation, having the memory of those who sinned
before and were counted worthy of the kingdom of the heavens, by the
grace and loving-kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ, for to him belongs
the glory and the power, together with the Father and the Holy Spirit,
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
