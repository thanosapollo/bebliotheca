* On the Macedonian Months
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of John of Damascus, on the months of the Macedonians, from the
ecclesiastical tradition.
For the Greeks say that there are twelve signs from stars in the
heaven, having a contrary motion to both the sun, and to the moon,
and to the other five planets, and that through twelve signs the
seven pass. And the names of the seven planets are these: Sun, Moon,
Zeus, Hermes, Ares, Aphrodite, Kronos. And they call them planets
because they are moved with a motion contrary to the heaven. And that
there is according to each zone of the heaven one of the seven
planets; one in the first and higher, Kronos; and in the second,
Zeus; in the third, Ares; and in the fourth, the Sun; and in the
fifth, Aphrodite; and in the sixth, Hermes; and in the seventh and
lower, the Moon. The Sun then according to each sign completes one
month, and through the twelve months he passes through the twelve
signs. And the names of the twelve signs are these, and the months of
these: αʹ, Ram; in the month of March on the καʹ it receives the sun;
βʹ, Bull; in the month of April on the κγʹ; γʹ, Twin, in May on the
κδʹ; δʹ, Crab, in June on the κδʹ; εʹ, Lion, in July on the κεʹ; ϛʹ,
Virgin, in August on the κεʹ. ζʹ, Balance, in September on the κεʹ;
ηʹ, Scorpion, in October. on the κεʹ; θʹ, Archer, in November on the
κεʹ; ιʹ, Goat-horn, in December on the κεʹ; ιαʹ, Water-pourer, in
January on the κʹ; ιβʹ, Fishes, in February on the κʹ. And the Moon
according to each month passes through the ιβʹ signs, being also
lower, and traversing these more swiftly; as if you should make a
sphere within another sphere, the inner sphere will be found smaller.
Thus also the course of the Moon, being lower, is less, and is
accomplished more quickly. On various months. Months according to the
Romans. αʹ. March λαʹ; Eat sweets, drink sweets. βʹ. April λʹ; Do not
eat radish. γʹ. May λαʹ; Do not eat foot-heads. δʹ. June λαʹ; Drink
little water. εʹ. July λαʹ; Abstain from things of Aphrodite. ϛʹ.
August λαʹ; Do not eat raw vegetables. ζʹ. September λʹ; Do not eat
milk. ηʹ. October λαʹ; Abstain from eating of sour things. θʹ.
November λʹ; Abstain from much bathing. ιʹ. December λαʹ; Do not eat
cabbage. ιαʹ. January λαʹ; At the ιβʹ hour drink a little unmixed
wine. ιβʹ. February κηʹ; Do not eat beet. =95.237= Months according to
the Jews. αʹ. Nisan. βʹ. Iar; γʹ. Siouan. δʹ. Thammouz. εʹ. Ab. ϛʹ.
Aloul. ζʹ. Thesre. ηʹ. Marsouan. θʹ. Chasleu. ιʹ. Tibeth. ιαʹ. Sabat.
ιβʹ. Adar. Months according to the Macedonians. αʹ. Bull. βʹ. Twin;
γʹ. Crab. δʹ. Lion. εʹ. Virgin. ϛʹ Balance. ζʹ. Scorpion. ηʹ. Archer.
θʹ. Goat-horn. ιʹ. Water-pourer. ιαʹ. Fishes. ιβʹ. Ram. Months
according to the Greeks. αʹ. Xanthikos. βʹ. Artemisios. γʹ. Daisios.
δʹ. Panemos. εʹ. Loos. ϛʹ. Gorpiaios. ζʹ. Hyperberetaios. ηʹ. Dios.
θʹ. Apellaios. ιʹ. Audynaios. ιαʹ. Peritios. ιβʹ. Dystros. Months
according to the Egyptians. αʹ. Pharmouthi. βʹ. Pachon. γʹ. Pauni. δʹ.
Epiphi. εʹ. Mesori. ϛʹ. Thoth. ζʹ. Phaophi. ηʹ. Athyr. θʹ. Choiak. ιʹ.
Tybi. ιαʹ. Mecheir. ιβʹ. Phamenoth.
