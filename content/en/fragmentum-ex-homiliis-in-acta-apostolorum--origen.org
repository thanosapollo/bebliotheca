FROM ORIGEN'S ON THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES.
From the 4th homily on the Acts.
=14.829= The Scripture had to be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit spoke
beforehand through the mouth of David concerning Judas; =14.832= in which
psalm the things concerning Judas have been written. Someone might say that it
is not the Holy Spirit that speaks; for clearly the words are of the Savior
saying: O God, do not pass over my praise in silence; because the mouth of a
sinner, and the mouth of a deceitful one, has been opened against me; and the
rest until: And let another take his oversight. How then, if it is the Savior
who says these things, does Peter say: The Scripture had to be fulfilled,
which the Holy Spirit spoke beforehand through the mouth of David? Perhaps
then what we are taught here is such. The Holy Spirit personifies in the
prophets; and if it personifies God, it is not God who is speaking, but the
Holy Spirit speaks from the person of God; and if it personifies Christ, it is
not Christ who is speaking, but the Holy Spirit speaks from the person of
Christ. Thus, even if it personifies the prophet, or that people, or this
people, or whatever it personifies, it is the Holy Spirit that personifies all
things.
