* On the Failure of the Synagogue
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of Saint Cyril, from the discourse whose title is: A proof through the divine
oracles that came to be from the beginning to the inspired patriarchs concerning
the exchange and transfer of the Jews and of the nations.
The renaming of Abraham has been grasped as signifying the triadic number of
the God-ruling hypostases; for since the inspired patriarch was named Abram, he
was renamed Abraham from God. If someone should choose to interpret this, taking
the first letter of the name, the a, with that joined and neighboring it, I mean
the b, he will altogether find, by a transposition of the a after the r,
signifying Father; for aba is interpreted father, of course.
And if again he will wish to make clear separately the second letter in
position, I mean the b, with the r joined and neighboring it, he will again find
likewise by a transposition of the same a, signifying Son; for bar is interpreted
son, of course. And if he should wish to read additionally and make clear the
final letter, I mean the m, with that joined and neighboring it, =76.1424= he
will again find conversely by a taking-together of the same r, signifying
highest; for mar is clearly interpreted highest; which hints at the hypostasis
of the all-holy Spirit; the word teaching us periphrastically that he is by
nature and is God; for the highest is nothing other, but altogether God according
to nature. This renaming then having been given by God to the patriarch, there
has been found clearly prewritten and pre-shown also the most unspeakable and
most mystical doctrine of the holy Trinity, the appellation of Abraham clearly
from a name showing and signifying and mystagoguing Father and Son and Holy
Spirit, according to the most evident naming and numbering and tradition of
Christ and our Savior upon theology.
