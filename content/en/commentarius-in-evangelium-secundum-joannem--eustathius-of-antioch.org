DISCOURSE ACCORDING TO JOHN
Christ subordinates and rather confirms the will of the Father above his own,
in various places; not because there was in him a will needing to be
abolished, neither a divine one nor that of his becoming-man (for as he
accomplished the nature, so also the things of the nature); but speaking to
Jews who thought him opposed to the Father, he said these things economically,
saying: I do not seek my own will but the will of the Father who sent me.
