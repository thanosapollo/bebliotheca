* On the Betrayal of Judas
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

On the betrayal of Judas, and on the passion of our Lord Jesus Christ. On
Holy Thursday.
=61.687=
I see the Church gloomy, a downcast form signifying the betrayal of her
own son. For the thing plotted is terrible, and the thing prepared is
murder, and the one who suffers these things is the heavenly Judge of
the dead and the living. And what is more terrible than all, suddenly an
enemy and a plotter is he who a little before was a disciple and
obedient, all at once a beast the sheep, a deserter of light the apostle,
a seller of the Master the discordant slave, of the eleven disciples the
twelfth.
And why do I not speak the name, that I may not bring the insult upon
all the apostles, hiding the one responsible? Going, he says, One of the
twelve. Who? The one called Judas. And another thing, lest the guiltless
hide the guilty (for we find among the apostles also another called by
the same name), Judas the Iscariot, he says, going, one of the twelve.
No longer, then, one; for he had with himself the devil as fellow-worker.
This one going to the chief priests said: What will you give me, and I
will deliver him to you? Tell me here, Judas, putting up for sale the
Master of the world and your own teacher, in what honor do you leave the
worth of the disciple? And what forces you to campaign as a traitor of
your own king? What preference did you see of the remaining fellow
disciples being made against you, that you meditated this lawless
counsel? You who know the Master crying out toward you and the eleven
apostles: If anyone among you wishes to be first, let him be last of
all, and servant of all =61.688=.
Did he not foretell these things, checking your counsel? And he urged
you to serve the first, that you might not, as last, moved by unjust
toil, construct the deceit? If you had been without money, granting the
betrayal to the Jews as a gift, a person would have supposed that, having
suffered something unjust, you went toward vengeance on the one who
wronged you; but your saying openly: What will you give me, and I will
deliver him to you? makes plain your craft. And they weighed out for him
thirty pieces of silver. And from then he sought an opportunity, that he
might deliver him. What are you doing, Judas, having agreed thirty pieces
of silver for the sale of the priceless pearl? Count first the stars
which he created by the word alone, and then by a word study to deliver
the Word. He sought an opportunity, that he might deliver him. He himself
made the times and the ages, and against him he sought a time fitting
for his own betrayal.
And when evening had come, he reclined with the twelve disciples, and as
they were eating he said: Amen, amen I say to you, one of you will
deliver me; the one who has entered against me and against you and
learned what he was not able to keep. And being very sorrowful they
began to say to him, each of them, Is it I, Lord? The thing said moved
all to a precise searching of conscience, those whose hidden things of
the heart are pure. Jesus answered saying: Why do you all slander
yourselves with the undertaking of the betrayer? He who dipped with me
in the dish the =61.689= hand, this one will deliver me. Unwillingly he
points himself out, before the drama he speaks even if not willing; the
name is held for now by me, until he says before you the things being
meditated by him against me. And answering, he says, Judas who was
delivering him said: Is it I, Rabbi? He says to him: You have said. You
have freed the eleven, making these innocent of the things being done
by you.

Receive also another condemnation of the things you are about to do
through love of money. And as they were eating, he says, Jesus taking
the bread, and having given thanks broke it, and gave it to his
disciples, and said: Take, eat, this is my body. And taking the cup,
having given thanks he gave it saying: Take, drink of it all; for this
is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many for the
forgiveness of sins. Take of it all. And you too, he says, the betrayer,
share in the eternal life; and if you remain in it, the agreements
toward the Jews are forgiven you; but if you do not overturn your own
impulse, know well what kind of Master you are selling, a lover of
mankind.
But he, not enduring the Master's compassion, running to the Jews was
pressing to bring the thing plotted into deed. And behold, =61.690= he
says, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great crowd with
swords and clubs from the chief priests and elders of the people. And
the one delivering him gave them a sign saying: Whom I shall kiss, he
is the one; seize him. Attend to my lips; for otherwise the word cannot
be betrayed. And immediately coming to Jesus, he said to him: Hail,
Rabbi, and he kissed him. O kiss, destruction of the peace of the world,
or rather, to speak truth-lovingly, the dissolution of the worldly war,
even if Judas did not dare the betrayal following this purpose. And
Jesus said to him: Friend, for what are you here? You have given me the
kiss; toward those standing by fulfill the agreements. Then coming they
laid hands on Jesus, and seized him.
The betrayer went away alien to the apostles, and the pioneer of life
was brought to the Jews unto death; a cross was prepared, and a tomb is
built unto life; the dead return, and Judas goes into Hades; the Savior
is crucified with robbers, and he calls all toward paradise. To him be
the glory and the power unto the ages of ages.
