HOMILY OF THE SAME BEFORE THE EXILE. =52.427=
αʹ. Many are the waves and hard is the swell; but we are not afraid of being
drowned; for we stand upon the rock. Let the sea rage, it cannot dissolve the
rock; let the waves rise, they have no power to sink the ship of Jesus. What
are we afraid of, tell me? Death? For me to live is Christ, and to die is
gain. But exile, tell me? The earth is the Lord's, and the fullness thereof.
But confiscation of goods? We brought nothing into the world, and it is clear
that we can carry nothing out; and the fearful things of the world are easy
for me to despise, and the pleasant things are laughable. I do not fear
poverty, I do not desire wealth; I do not fear death, I do not pray to live,
except for your progress. Therefore I also now remind you, and I exhort your
love to take =52.428= heart. For no one will be able to tear us apart; for
what God has yoked together, man cannot separate. For if he says concerning a
woman and a man: For this cause a man shall leave his father and his mother,
and shall be joined to his wife, and the two shall be one flesh; what
therefore God has yoked, let not man separate. If you cannot tear a marriage
apart, how much more are you unable to dissolve the Church of God? But you war
against her, not being able to harm the one who is warred against. But you
make me more illustrious, and you dissolve your own strength by the fight
against me; for it is hard for you to kick against sharp goads. You do not
blunt the goads, but you bloody your =52.429= feet; since the waves also do
not dissolve the rock, but they themselves are dissolved into foam. Nothing is
stronger than the Church, O man. Cease the war, lest it dissolve your power;
do not bring war into heaven. If you war against a man, you either conquer or
are conquered. But if you war against the Church, it is impossible for you to
conquer; for God is stronger than all. Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are
we stronger than he? God has fixed it, who attempts to shake it? You do not
know his power. He looks upon the earth, and makes it tremble; he commands,
and the things that were shaken were made firm. If he set the city that was
being shaken, much more can he set the Church. The Church is stronger than
heaven: Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.
What words? You are Peter, and upon this rock I will build my Church, and the
gates of Hades shall not prevail against it.
βʹ. If you disbelieve the word, believe the facts. How many tyrants wished to
get the better of the Church? How many frying-pans? How many furnaces, teeth
of beasts, sharpened swords? And they did not get the better. Where are those
who warred? They have been silenced and given over to oblivion. And where is
the Church? She shines above the sun. Their things have been quenched, hers
are immortal. If when they were few they were not conquered, when the
inhabited world has been filled with piety, how can you conquer? Heaven and
earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away. And very reasonably;
for the Church is more desired by God than heaven. He did not take a body of
heaven, but he took flesh of the Church; heaven is for the Church, not the
Church for heaven. Let none of the things that have happened trouble you.
Grant me this, an unshaken faith. Did you not see Peter walking upon the
waters, and doubting a little, and about to be drowned, not because of the
disordered rush of the waters, but because of the weakness of the faith? For
did we come here by human votes? Did a man bring us, that a man might dissolve
us? I say these things not being out of my mind, God forbid, nor boasting, but
wishing to make firm what has been shaken in you. Since the city has stood,
the devil wished to shake the Church. O polluted and all-polluted devil, you
did not get the better of walls, and you expect to shake the Church? Is the
Church in walls? The Church is in the multitude of the faithful. See how many
steadfast pillars, not bound with iron, but bound tight with faith. I do not
say that so great a multitude is fiercer than fire; but not even if there were
one, would you have gotten the better. And you know what wounds the martyrs
gave you. Often a tender maiden unacquainted with marriage came in; she was
softer than wax, and she became firmer than rock.
You scraped her sides, and you did not take her faith. The nature of the flesh
grew faint, and the power of the faith was not given up; the body was being
spent, the mind grew young; the substance was being consumed, and the piety
remained. You did not get the better of one woman, and you expect to get the
better of so great a people? Do you not hear the Lord saying, Where two or
three are gathered in my name, there I am in the midst of them? Where so great
a people is bound tight with love, is he not present? I have his pledge; for
do I take heart in my own power? I hold his bond. That is my staff, that is my
security, that is my harbor without waves =52.430=. Even if the inhabited
world is troubled, I hold the bond; I read it (f. it); those letters are a
wall to me and security. What letters? I am with you all the days until the
close of the age. Christ is with me, and whom shall I fear? Even if waves are
raised against me, even if seas, even if the angers of rulers; all these
things are cheaper than a spider's web to me. And if it were not for your
love, I would not even today have declined to go away. For I always say, Lord,
let your will be done; not what so-and-so and so-and-so, but whatever you
will. This is my tower, this is my unmoved rock; this is my unshaken staff. If
God wills this to happen, let it happen. If he wills me to be here, I give
thanks. Wherever he wills, I give thanks.
γʹ. Let no one trouble you; attend to the prayers. The devil did these things
in order to cut off the zeal concerning the litanies. But it does not succeed
for him; but we have found you more zealous and more fervent. Tomorrow I will
go out to the litany with you. Or where I am, you also are there; where you
are, there I also am; we are one body; a body is not separated from the head,
nor a head from a body. We are parted by place, but we are united by love; not
even death will be able to cut us apart. For even if my body dies, the soul
lives, and remembers the people; you are fathers to me; how can I forget you?
You are fathers to me, you are life to me, you are my good repute. If you
progress, I am well spoken of; so that for me the wealth of life lies in your
treasury. I am ready to be slain ten thousand times for you (and I grant no
favor, but I even pay a debt; for the good shepherd lays down his life for the
sheep), both to be slain ten thousand times, and to have ten thousand heads
cut off.
This death is for me a ground of immortality, these plots are for me an
occasion of security. For am I plotted against for money, that I should be
grieved? Am I plotted against for sins, that I should feel pain? For the love
concerning you; since I do all things, so that you may remain in security, so
that no one may slip into the flock, so that the flock may remain unharmed.
The ground of the contests is enough for me unto a crown. For what would I
suffer for you? You are citizens to me, you are fathers to me, you are
brothers to me, you are children to me, you are members to me, you are a body
to me, you are light to me, or rather even sweeter than this light. For what
such thing does the ray furnish me as your love? The ray benefits me in the
present life, but your love weaves a crown for me in the life to come.
And I say these things into ears that hear; and what is more ready to hear
than your ears? For so many days you kept vigil, and nothing bent you, neither
length of time made you softer, nor fears, nor threats; toward all things you
became noble. And why do I say, you became? This which I always desired, you
despised the affairs of this life, you renounced the earth, you transferred to
heaven; you were set free from the bonds of the body, you contend toward that
blessed philosophy. These things are crowns to me, these consolation, these
comfort, these are ointment to me, these life, these a ground of immortality.
δʹ. But I see some who are persuading me to persist in my own dogmas. For many
of the fortunate things bring one to the contrary; because I have fallen in
with the wickedness of those among whom I seemed to be a zealot; some who
depose according to the manner win the contest by the difference of the
affairs; they were not threatening, but they were standing by. For it is time
now to speak the things concerning my affliction. There is a law, but the
lawgiver is conquered. Children, by your love, I see a conspiracy warring, and
God being insulted; I see the contest falling, and the judge of the games
being grieved; I see the persuasiveness of the truth withering, and the
conspiracy blooming. They say to me, You ate and you baptized. If I did this,
let me be anathema; may I not be numbered in the root of bishops; may I not be
with angels; may I not please God; and if I both ate and baptized, I did
nothing untimely of the affairs. Attend to me with accuracy what I say, and I
will not cease saying it. For me to speak is not irksome, and for you it is
safe. But let us return to the matter at hand.
They say that I ate and baptized. Let them then depose Paul, because after he
had dined he granted baptism to the jailer. I dare to say, let them depose
Christ himself also, because after the supper he granted communion to the
disciples. But these things are reasonable and great for us; these are the
bright things of peace; these are the praises of the people. Mine is the
crown, yours is the fruit. But you know, beloved, why they wish to depose me.
Because I did not spread carpets, nor did I put on silk garments, and because
I did not soothe their gluttony. For the offspring of the asp have blossomed,
the seed of Jezebel is still left; and still the grace also contends together
with Elijah. But bring me into the midst the marvelous and rich herald of
life, I mean John, the poor man, who did not even possess a lamp; for he had
the lamp of Christ; whose head was desired by the fellow-minister of Eve, she
who became a hindrance of the saints, she who persecuted the prophets, she who
proclaimed the fast in deceit, she who knew a dance of equal honor with the
name of the viper, having danced at the unfinished banquet. She did not desire
life, she did not desire a mass of money, not the dignity of a kingdom, not
any other abundance. But tell me, O man, what did she desire? The head of a
man. And what do I say? Not only of a man, but of an evangelist. But she did
not conquer by taking the head. For she cut the head having asked for it,
receiving the lawless desire eagerly upon a platter. See, and marvel at the
power of God. The blameless man was convicting, he was beheaded; but =52.432=
the one beheaded is at the right hand of Christ, and she awaits inescapable
punishment. Again her seed, the thorny seed, seeks and hastens. But Herodias,
seeking the head of John again, seeks a dance, not the one we play with the
feet, but the dance of Mary. Again John cries out and says: It is not lawful
for you to have your brother's wife. εʹ. But what shall I say? The present
time is of tears; for all things run out into dishonor, and time judges all
things. Gold glorifies the whole. But bring me the holy David who says and
cries out: If gold flow, do not set your heart upon it. But tell me, who was
this man who sent forth this voice? Did he not have that of the kingdom laid
upon his head? Did he not give orders in the royal authority? But he was not
looking toward plunder, his mind was not toward the deposition of piety, nor a
care of treasures, but of the gathering of the camps; not a woman's consent.
Flee therefore, women, the gatherings of others; do not counsel your husbands
with an evil counsel; but be made secure by the things that have been said.
Have we quenched your flame? Has your heart been softened? But I know that the
daughters of Mary will be benefited; but others, filled without wine and drunk
with love of money, as the blessed Paul cries out and proclaims saying: The
love of money is the root of all evils. So also the senseless women stop up
their own ears, and instead of good seed they bear thorns. But I exhort, let
us not cast our seed as upon a rock. We are Christ's field, from whom may we
hear: Well done, good servant, enter into my house; let it not be said instead
of this voice, Well done, evil servant.
But I exhort, let your manner of life shine before men, let us not make our
salt foolish, but let us glorify, let us give thanks, the rich to the rich
one, the poor to the loving and lover of the poor, Christ, the powerful to his
mighty hand. And these things concerning you. But perhaps God allows me to
suffer these things which they plan against me, that in misfortunes he may
prove the things concerning me; because in the toils victory is wholly laid
up, and in the contests a crown has been prepared. For the divine Paul also
was saying: I have finished the course, I have kept the faith; henceforth
there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness; of which crown the Master
of all will count you worthy unto the ages. Amen.
