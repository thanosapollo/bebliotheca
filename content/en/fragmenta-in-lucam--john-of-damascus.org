Of the Damascene.
=95.233= And the Spirit comes down upon the apostles. For he is God; and God
is a consuming fire. For this reason Christ is said to baptize with fire,
since in the form of fiery tongues he poured out the grace of Christ upon his
holy disciples, or because of the punishing of the fire to come. For that too
is a last baptism, not saving, but destroying wickedness; for wickedness and
sin no longer have their citizenship; yet punishing without end.
