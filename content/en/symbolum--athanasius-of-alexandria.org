CREED OF SAINT ATHANASIUS.
If anyone wishes to be saved, before all things it is necessary for him to
hold the catholic faith; which unless one keep sound and unblemished, without
any doubt he shall perish forever. And the catholic faith is this, that we
worship one God in Trinity, and Trinity in unity, neither confusing the
persons, nor dividing the essence. For other is the hypostasis of the Father,
other of the Son, and other of the Holy Spirit; but of Father, and Son, and
Holy Spirit, there is one Godhead, equal glory, coeternal majesty. Such as the
Father is, such also is the Son, such also is the Holy Spirit. Uncreated is
the Father, uncreated the Son, uncreated also the Holy Spirit. Immeasurable is
the Father, immeasurable the Son, immeasurable also the Holy Spirit. Eternal
is the Father, eternal the Son, eternal also the Holy Spirit. And yet there
are not three eternals, but one eternal; even as there are not three
uncreated, nor three immeasurables, but one uncreated, and one immeasurable.
Likewise almighty is the Father, almighty the Son, almighty the Holy Spirit;
and yet there are not three almighties, but one almighty. So God is the
Father, God the Son, God the Holy Spirit; but yet there are not three gods,
but one God. Likewise Lord is the Father, Lord the Son, Lord the Holy Spirit;
and yet there are not three lords, but there is one Lord. Because, even as we
are compelled by Christian truth to confess each hypostasis singly as God and
Lord, so we are forbidden by catholic piety to say three gods or three lords.
The Father is made by none, neither created, nor begotten. The Son is from the
Father alone =28.1584= not made, nor created, but begotten. The Holy Spirit is
from the Father not made, nor created, nor begotten, but proceeding. There is
then one Father, not three fathers; one Son, not three sons; one Holy Spirit,
not three holy spirits. And in this Trinity nothing is first or later, nothing
greater or less, but all the three hypostases are coeternal with one another
and equal. Thus, that through all things, as has already been said before,
both Trinity in unity, and unity in Trinity may be worshipped. Let him then
who wishes to be saved think thus concerning the Holy Trinity. But it is also
necessary to eternal salvation, that he also firmly believe the incarnation of
our Lord Jesus Christ. The right faith then is, that we believe and confess
that our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, is God and man. He is God from the
essence of the Father, begotten before the ages; and he is man from the
essence of the mother, begotten in time. Perfect God, and perfect man,
existing from a rational soul and human flesh; equal to the Father according
to the Godhead, less than the Father according to the manhood. Who, although
he is God and man, yet is not two, but one Christ; and one not by conversion
of Godhead into flesh, but by assumption of manhood into Godhead. One
altogether not by confusion of natures, but by union of hypostases; for as a
rational soul and flesh is one man, so God and man is one Christ; who suffered
for our salvation, and descended into Hades, and on the third day rose from
the dead, and ascended into the heavens, and sits at the right hand of God and
Father almighty, whence he shall come to judge the living and the dead. At
whose coming all men shall rise with their own bodies, rendering account
concerning their own works; and those who have done good shall enter into
eternal life, and those who have done evil into eternal fire. This is the
catholic faith, which unless one believe faithfully and firmly, he shall not
be able to be saved.
=28.1585= Confession of the catholic faith of our father among the saints
Athanasius the Great, which he gave to Julius, pope of Rome. For him who
wishes to be saved, before all things it is necessary to hold the catholic
faith; which unless one keep entire and unbroken, without doubt he shall
perish forever. And this is the catholic faith, that we worship one God in
Trinity, and Trinity in unity, neither confusing the persons, nor dividing the
essence. For other is the person of the Father, other that of the Son, other
that of the Holy Spirit. But of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy
Spirit there is one Godhead, equal glory, coeternal majesty. Such as the
Father is, such is the Son, such is the Holy Spirit. Uncreated is the Father,
uncreated the Son, uncreated the Holy Spirit. Infinite is the Father, infinite
the Son, infinite the Holy Spirit. Everlasting is the Father, everlasting the
Son, everlasting the Holy Spirit. Not indeed three everlastings, but one
everlasting. So neither three uncreated, nor three infinites, but one
uncreated, one infinite. Likewise all-powerful is the Father, all-powerful the
Son, all-powerful the Holy Spirit; not indeed three all-powerful, but one
all-powerful. So also God is the Father, God the Son, God the Holy Spirit; not
indeed three gods, but one God. So Lord is the Father, Lord the Son, Lord the
Holy Spirit; not indeed three lords, but one Lord; since even as we are
compelled by Christian truth to confess each of the persons as God or Lord, so
also the catholic religion forbids us to say three gods or lords. The Father
then is by none either made, or created, or begotten. The Son alone is from
the Father alone, not made, not created, but begotten. The Holy Spirit is from
Father and Son, not made, not created, not begotten, but proceeding. There is
then one Father, not three fathers; one Son, not three sons; one Holy Spirit,
not three =28.1586= holy spirits. But in this Trinity nothing is before or
after, nothing greater or less; but three persons are coeternal with one
another, and equal through all things. Thus, so that according to all things,
as we have already said, we worship both the Trinity in the unity, and the
unity in the Trinity. Let him then who wishes to be saved think thus
concerning the Trinity. And it is necessary to eternal salvation also to hold
faithfully the incarnation of our Lord Jesus Christ. The firm faith then is,
that we believe and confess our Lord Jesus Christ the Son of God to be God and
man; God from the essence of God, begotten before the ages; perfect God and
perfect man, existing from a rational soul and human flesh; equal to the
Father according to the Godhead, less than the Father according to the
manhood. Who, although he is God and man, is not two, but one Christ; and one,
not by the Godhead being changed into flesh, but by the manhood being assumed
by God; one entirely, not by mixture of essence, but by the unity of the
person. For as the rational soul and the flesh is one man, so also God and man
is one Christ; having suffered for our salvation, having descended into Hades,
having risen on the third day from the dead, and having ascended into the
heavens, and sitting at the right hand of the all-powerful God and Father,
from there to come to judge the living and the dead; at whose presence all men
shall rise with their own bodies, rendering account concerning their works.
Those then who have done good shall go away into eternal life, and those who
have done worthless things into the eternal fire. This is the catholic faith,
which unless one believe faithfully and firmly, he shall not be able to be
saved. =28.1585=

If anyone would wish to be saved, before all things he has need to hold the
orthodox faith; which if one do not keep unstained and incorrupt, he shall
find eternal destruction. The orthodox faith then is this, that we worship one
God in Trinity, and Trinity in unity, neither confusing the hypostases, nor
dividing the essence. =28.1589= For other is the hypostasis of the Father,
other of the Son, other of the Holy Spirit. But of the Father, and of the Son,
and of the Holy Spirit, one is the Godhead, one the power, one authority, one
kingdom, equal the glory, equal and eternal the greatness. Such as the Father
is, such is the Son, such is the Holy Spirit. Uncreated is the Father,
uncreated the Son, uncreated the Holy Spirit; and not three uncreated, but one
uncreated. Almighty is the Father, almighty the Son, almighty the Holy Spirit;
and not three almighties, but one almighty. Eternal is the Father, eternal the
Son, eternal the Holy Spirit; and not three eternals, but one eternal.
All-powerful is the Father, all-powerful the Son, all-powerful the Holy
Spirit; and not three all-powerful, but one all-powerful. So God is the
Father, God the Son, God the Holy Spirit; and not three gods, but there is one
God. Lord is the Father, Lord the Son, Lord the Holy Spirit; and not three
lords, but there is one Lord. And even as we advocate by Christian truth to
confess each hypostasis singly as God and Lord, so we do not consent to say
three gods or three lords, but entirely forbid it. For the Father is made by
none, not created, but unbegotten. The Son is from the Father not made, not
created, but begotten. The Holy Spirit is from the Father not made, not
created, not begotten, but proceeding. There is then one Father, not three
fathers; one Son, not three sons; one Holy Spirit, not three holy spirits. And
in this Trinity no one is first or last, no one great or small; but the three
hypostases are both eternal and equal in all things, as has been said before.
Let every Christian then piously worship unity in Trinity, and Trinity in
unity; and thus let him glorify concerning the holy and life-giving and
consubstantial Trinity. And it is also necessary to believe rightly the
incarnation of the Son of God and our Savior Jesus Christ. The right faith
then is, that we believe and confess that our Lord Jesus Christ the Son of God
is God and man. He is God from the essence of the Father [al. he is uncreated
and essence of the Father], he is man from the essence of the Mother. For
since Adam having sinned subjected the race of men to death, and made our
nature liable to the debt, the Son then of the invisible Father and God, not
departing from the heavens, came down to us; and entering through hearing into
the womb of the holy Virgin, he wrought the conception mystically, putting on
our nature according to extreme union; coming forth God together with man,
thus he fulfilled the economy concerning us through unspeakable mercy. And
being perfect God, he became perfect man, not converted, not altered as to the
beyond-essence and unspeakable essence of his Godhead, being substantiated
from a rational soul and human flesh; himself creating for himself together
with his connatural all-holy Spirit the all-holy assumption of the flesh, a
perfect human essence enhypostatic in him and indivisible from him, without
=28.1592= share in sin; the body being compacted from the virginal pure bloods
of the Mother of God, without any fleshly will or thought; and the soul being
created immaterially, intelligent, rational, volitive, operative, wholly
deified together with the flesh, from the very beginning, from first
existence, because of the extreme union toward the God Word himself,
unconfused according to hypostasis. He is then equal to the Father according
to the Godhead, and less than the Father according to the manhood. And he is
truly true God and man; yet not two, but one is the Christ; and one, the flesh
not converted, but taken up into God; one wholly not by confusion of essences,
but by union according to hypostasis. For as a rational soul and flesh is one
man, so also God and man is one Christ. Who suffered in the flesh for our
salvation, the Godhead remaining impassible; and having been buried, and
having risen, and having been taken up into the heavens, he sat at the right
hand of God and the Father. And from there coming again he will judge the
living and the dead. At whose presence all men who have fallen asleep from of
old shall rise, to render account concerning their deeds. And those who have
done good shall go at his command into eternal life; and those who have done
worthless things shall go into eternal punishments. This then is the orthodox
faith, which he who has not kept unblemished cannot be saved.
