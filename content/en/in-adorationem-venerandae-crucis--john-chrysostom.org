Nor again commit fornication; for fornication has become the cause of the
world's overthrow, being a harmful and soul-uncovering sin. We who wish to
touch the cross, and to reap the benefit from it, let us not cast ourselves
into the dark pit of fornication, and into the gloomy well of adultery; for
the sword-bearing portals of lawless fornication, and the fiery darts of
adultery, beget the sleepless worm, and the unquenchable fire; fornication and
adultery bring forth a luxury-temper-kindled wrath, and a root-melting-heart
and a nerve-calamity. Those therefore who approach the cross contemptuously
and without fear shall become thunder-shattered, grim-roaring,
lightning-shuddering, =62.752= dart-depth-amaze-quake-slain.
You who wish to come to the cross, hate slander, abhor remembrance of wrongs,
do not cease to have mercy on the poor, do not hesitate to visit the sick, do
not desire love of money, do not cease visiting those in prison. Gathering in
the churches, let us come to the cross with fear and trembling and joy, and
let us not cease honoring the priests. Let us consider, beloved, the power of
the cross. For when the cross was planted, and Christ was stretched upon it,
and he bowed his undefiled head upon it, and his side was pierced with the
spear, and all that is under heaven was fluttering with fear, and preparing
for the vengeance of him who was hanging in flesh on the cross, but being
restrained by him who was crucified in the flesh, the devil, seeing the
elements thrown into confusion, the hoof of the lawless and their station, the
sound of thunders and their booming, the brightness of lightnings and their
course, the standing of darts and their lightness, the boldness of earthquake
and its power, the multitude of angels and their ranks, the gleam of fire and
its flame, the tempest of wrath and its anger, the armies of the high-equipped
and their encampments, the powers of holy living beings and their strength,
the subjection of clouds and their sending forth: seeing these things the
devil hastened down to Hades, and ordered gates and bars to be secured. And
looking intently he saw the essences of torch-bearing lightnings coming down
toward him, and the heavy-sounding noise of thunders terrified him, gloom
encircled him, a trembling of the elements threw him into confusion, a rank of
well-girt ones arrived first, the earth was shaken, the rocks were split, bars
were crushed, the elements were quivering, death was being tossed, lamps of
noetic fire went before; the Tartarean deep was being made to tremble, the sea
was being stirred into mire, the underworld things of the abyss were being
levered up, the voices of the trumpet were being strengthened. The sun was
being darkened, and one could see the heaven and the appearing firmament, the
enclosure of the world, the roof of creation, the tiling of the common house,
the unaging roof, the water-compacted vault, the crystalline bowl, the even
under-papering, the gold-lettered book, the one silver-bought with the stars,
the dark-blue-dyed chamber, the torch-bearing bridal chamber, in which sun and
moon sit as bridegroom and bride. And the heaven was being clothed with
darkness instead of brightness, and rivers and springs and lakes were
hastening to draw in their stream, but by the nod of the crucified Christ they
were commanded to put forth their currents; and a gloomy phalanx and array of
demons was being put to flight.
And every rank of angels and archangels and of the ministering powers was
being shaken with fear and trembling, beholding the timeless and incorruptible
and immortal God not leaving the heavens, and going down under the earth to
Adam. And Hades was in travail, and Belial, stupefied with fear, had been made
dead, and death, smoked and thunderstruck by the lightning of the Godhead,
vomited forth all the righteous whom he had. While these things were happening
thus, behold a voice was coming forth from the mouth of the holy and undefiled
and immortal and invisible one, giving life, and not bearing death, but making
joy and trembling: joy for the righteous, but trembling for the rulers of the
darkness: To you I say who are in bonds, Come out, and to those in the
darkness, Be uncovered. Hearing these things the souls of the righteous
rejoiced, and the choir of the prophets, being glad, were singing: Our God
will come manifestly, our God, and will not keep silence. Fire shall go before
him, and round about him a mighty tempest. And the souls of the holy ones,
loosed from the bonds, were going out together with Adam, the prophets
striking the devil, and the righteous slapping him, and the holy ones,
spitting on him, were laughing him to scorn; and the phalanx of the demons,
putting their heads to their knees, were sounding with the lyres and harps in
the hands of the prophets, and with songs of drums, singing the
victory-bearer. Who then will not marvel at the power of the precious and
life-giving cross? O name of the cross, the hidden mystery; o grace
unspeakable, and power unsearchable. But it is time now to come to the fearful
table, and to approach the unbloody sacrifice.
For this reason I too, with the fitting sobriety and watchfulness, having
added again some small =62.753= encomia to the life-giving cross, will bring
the discourse to rest. Hail, all-fearful and life-giving cross, sweet light,
and ever-appearing sun; hail, precious and venerable cross, who turn the souls
of those who are beguiled, and destroy the souls of the lawless; hail, cross
worthy of wonder, who show the ways of the righteous, and burn the paths of
the ungodly; hail, thrice-blessed cross, dispenser of good things, and helper
of those who hope in you; hail, all-blessed cross, wall and salvation of those
who flee to you; hail, all-shining cross, deliverer of those in danger, and
physician of the weak; hail, all-greatest cross, helper of those who suffer,
and avenger of those who are worn down; hail, all-illumining cross, helper of
the unattended, guide of the wandering, helper of the afflicted; hail,
victory-making cross, giver to those who ask, and hearer of those who cry out;
hail, unconquered cross, undeceiving protector, holder-together and president
of the world; hail, life-torching cross, benefactor of the inhabited world,
recompenser of those who do good; hail, height-making cross, and victory-maker
of your servants; hail, life-bearing cross, benefactor of friends, winnower of
Jews, chastiser of the ungodly, rewarder of the righteous; hail, cross worthy
of wonder, adorner of priests, boast of monastics, seeker of the persecuted,
opener of the humbled; hail, life-bestowing cross, exalter of the poor,
raising of the fallen, long-suffering toward sinners, receiver of those who
repent; hail, all-holy cross, propitiation of those who confess, pitier of
those who turn back, chooser of your holy ones, destroyer of your enemies,
kinsman of Christians, and builder of the Churches, and worshiped by all
creation.
This precious and venerable wood, honored by all, is worshiped: Greeks and
barbarians, Macedonians and Thessalians, Paeonians and Illyrians, Athenians
and Argives and Laconians, Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and those who
dwell in Mesopotamia, both Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia, Egypt and
the parts of Libya toward Cyrene, Cretans and Arabs, Indians and Ethiopians,
and Homerites, and all the rest of the nations, as many as the sun looks upon,
having left their own deceit, being signed with the cross they worship,
fleeing the many-tangled cords of the devil. For through the cross Christ tore
open the insatiable belly of Belial, and loosed his flesh-eating teeth; having
shattered him with his firm cross he paralyzed his sinews, and crushed his
bones; he rent his robe round about, and plucked out his hair, he cast upon
him a dark and =62.754= endless lamentation and mourning; he quenched his
shameless eyes, he put to shame his dishonorable face, he speared him with
sharp javelins, he pierced him with a three-edged sword, he scattered his
hopes, he searched out his hidden nests, and he winnowed his stores to every
wind. He dashed to the ground the pitiless children and offspring of his
demons, he quenched with unquenchable fire his handmade idols, he sank his bed
into a pit, he levered up his dark storehouses, he razed his cities, he cast
down the walls of lawlessness, he flung open the gates of Hades, he crushed
the adamantine bars, and having loosed all who believed in him, and

taking them with himself into the heavens to the Father, he ran up as a giant,
as the prophet says: He shall rejoice, as a giant, to run his course. By this
sign enemies are put to flight, by this sign walls of adversaries fall, demons
are destroyed, all things are subjected to the wood of the cross through the
power of him who was crucified on it.
And not only now does the type of the cross conquer the adversaries, but even
before this it was conquering and prevailing. And know the power of the type.
The rod of Moses bore the type of the cross; and the rod was the cross. But
see for me the type of the cross, how many wonders and signs it worked, evils
for the adversaries, but good things for the faithful. And Moses through the
rod turned the rivers of the Egyptians into blood, and through that same rod
he brought on locust and palmerworm and hail, a darkness that could be felt
and a storm, and a heap of the firstborn; for wherever the rod nodded, there a
trophy also was set up. Through the rod God called back the race of the
Hebrews from bitter slavery, and a type of the cross was that which was saving
them. You have known, beloved, the strength and the power of the type of the
cross; worship the truth of Christ, having heard the manifestation of the
prefiguration; reverence the appearing of the truth.
Great then is the word of the cross, and unspeakable is its power. Those who
worship the cross with longing and faith have known the strength in it, and
they put the devil to flight. For the all-powerful cross, benefiting all, is
worshiped by all. For the Lord is the true God and savior of our souls, having
been nailed to it, and having saved the world; to whom from us all honor is
due and owed, glory and thanksgiving with the unoriginate Father, together
with the all-holy and life-giving Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages of
ages. Amen.
