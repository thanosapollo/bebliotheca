A DISCOURSE OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS BASIL CONCERNING THE STATE OF
PRIESTS.
Attend to yourself, O priest, and see the service which you have received, that
you may fill it with fear of God. See henceforth; for you have not been handed
an earthly service but a heavenly, not a human but an angelic; be eager to
present yourself a workman =31.1688= unashamed, cutting right the word of truth;
attend, lest you stand into a synaxis having enmity with someone, lest you put
the Paraclete to flight. On a day of synaxis do not judge, nor contend at all;
but hiding yourself pray, reading until this hour; and thus stand in
compunction at the holy altar; nor looking around here and there; do not in
haste cut the prayers short; but entreating, do not take a face of anyone, but
see the king set forth, and the powers standing around in a circle; and do not
hypocrite, nor give a share of the divine body where one must not. Make yourself
worthy of the sacred canons, and liturgize together as the canons are set. See
then how you stand at the holy table, and how you priest, and of whom you give a
share, and how you arrange; see then, lest you forget the master's commands and
the traditions of his holy disciples; for he says: "Do not give the holy things
to the dogs, nor throw my pearls before the swine."
See then you also, lest you be led by a human fear, and hand over the Son of God
into hands of unworthy ones; but do not be shamed by one of the glorious of the
earth, nor even of those wearing the diadem be frightened in that hour when you
would priest. See those wishing to partake of the gifts in the houses; you will
see, that they are also granted by women, but also of unworthy. See then, as the
divine canons and the synods of the holy Fathers have confirmed; so act, and do
not give a share to unworthy or to laymen, except they turn themselves to the
orthodox faith. And woe to those who give a share to them! See then lest flies
fall into the holy cup, or from your carelessness it be moistened, or rust, or
be dusty, or be handled by heretics; but see how you arrange after the filling
of the divine liturgy, not hastening lest a pearl fall away, or remain in the
cup; or the holy cup having a stream, and be dusty; but having arranged both, go
in peace. If you keep this tradition, O priest, you will save yourself, and those
you are about to teach; praying also for me the wretched toward the Lord, to
whom glory is fitting unto the ages. Amen.
