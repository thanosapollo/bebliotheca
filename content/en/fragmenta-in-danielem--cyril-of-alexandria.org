* Fragments on Daniel
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

And this was nothing other than to boast against the glory of God, and to
exalt oneself over the one who stood over those from Israel, as having been
able in no way to benefit his own people. Books were opened. For when the
Only-begotten came to be in the form that is according to us, then also the
Father opened books; and he released those liable for sins from being judged,
and he granted to those who play the man to be written and numbered henceforth
with the choirs above and to lie in memory. And what is, He came as far as the
Ancient of Days? Is it locally? Then how is this not ignorant? because the
Divine is not in a place; for he fills all things. What then is, He came as
far as the Ancient of Days? Clearly the Son came into the glory of the Father.
And from where is this to be seen, he said again next: To him was given the
honor and a kingdom. For he heard the Father saying: Sit at my right hand
until I set your enemies a footstool of your feet.
