On the beginning of the indiction.
=59.673=
Marvelous are the festivals of the Orthodox, bright the memorials of the
martyrs, unashamed the feastings of the pious. We do not feast a time of
things that move unstably; we do not reverence a year, the expenditure of
human life; we do not worship a course that draws us into corruption; we do
not serve the creation; for the things that have come to be are
fellow-servants; we do not tend matter; for it came into being from things
that are not; for as these had a beginning of generation, they are also
subject to an end of corruption. We do not reverence heaven; for it was
made firm as smoke; we do not reverence the firmament; it was crystallized;
we do not reverence the light; for it was created by a word; we do not
reverence the moon; for it was adorned with form; we do not reverence the
earth; for it was drawn up from water; we do not reverence fire; for the
element is material; we do not worship the water; for the nature is
flowing; we do not serve time; for it does not stand still, but withdraws;
we do not reverence the day; for it is slave to an ordinance.
All things grow old with time, and time grows old after all things; but God
alone is above old age; and the day of the consummation shows it. Greeks,
then, and Jews, brothers of lawlessness, the spoils of the devil, the
offspring of sin, the nurslings of error, the hardening of impiety, the
naval recruiters of Gehenna, the serpents of ungodliness, the sons of the
curse. Let these feast impiously, since they think unholy things. Let the
ones honor error, let the others trample the law, and let both be schooled
in evils; for they have exchanged. Jews learned idolatry from Greeks, and
Greeks learned prophet-killing from Jews; for the Jews displayed every
Greek error under the law. But you are indignant, O Jew, hearing these
things? Consider deeds, then, and do not be harsh toward the reproaches.
Where did you not practice idolatry, tell me? In the wilderness you forged
a calf, bearing trophies you were initiated to Beelphegor, in the land of
Moab you worshipped Thamatz, in Palestine you sacrificed to Dagon, in
Phoenicia you served Astarte, in captivity you worshipped Chemosh. Place
failed, abomination did not fail. For this reason the Master also hates
your feasts. Hear the prophet saying, I have hated, I have rejected your
feasts, and I will not be glad in your assemblies.
For every feast of the Jews brought on murders. Fasting, you stoned
Naboth; feasting, you sawed Isaiah; holding festival, you threw Jeremiah
into a pit. Into judg =59.674= ments and fights you fasted impiously;
reveling you delivered the ark in the time of Eli, you beheaded John. If
you feast such things, mourning is of use to you. But we feast divine and
wondrous things, the contests of the martyrs, the victories of those
buried, the nets of those not seen, the healing of those dissolved; first
of all the wonders of Christ and my God, which he accomplished having
become man for my salvation; then thus the sufferings of his servants; the
stone-set crown of Stephen, the trophies against death of glorious
Laurence, the unshipwrecked virginity of Anna. O triad, herald of the
Trinity! O relics, the medicines of those in pain! O dust, a fountain of
healings! O house imitating the beauty of heaven! But even the soulless
element, the sea, cries out to you without speech. You have refuted the
fellow-servants; I too will proclaim the Master. The footprint of the
Fashioner does not weigh me down; the feet of Christ sanctify me. Moses
split me, but this one sanctified me; I swallowed Jonah, but I shudder at
this one; I tossed Noah with waves, but I do not dare to look upon him who
fashioned man with his hands. With his feet he sanctified the sea, with
the throne he glorified heaven, with the manger he made the earth bright,
through the tomb he illumined Hades. I am not ungrateful and soulless; as
an element I recognize the one who made me; I do not say with the Jews,
This man is not from God; but piously I cry, This is my God, and I will
glorify him.
I will show the things of my nature, I will refute the minds of the
bloodstained, I will publish the power of him who walked upon me. These
are fellow-servants, that one is Master, by whose fear I trouble these.
The Master walked on the sea without getting wet, but those sailing were
in uproar; and the fear prepared those in danger to cry out, but the
lover of mankind commanded them to take heart, saying: Take heart, it is
I, do not be afraid. Take heart, having the anchor of the faith; for a
reef of idolatry did not sink you; take heart, a Jewish tempest did not
drown you; take heart, a heretical surge does not toss you.
It is I, the Word co-unoriginate with the Father, the branch of the
uncreated beginning, the lamb of the world's sin, the helmsman of the
shipwreck of the cities, the treasure of the orthodox faith, the
prize-giver of the martyrs' contests. It is I, do not be afraid. For
where God is present, danger is driven away by the grace of God; to him
be the glory and the power unto the ages of ages. Amen.
