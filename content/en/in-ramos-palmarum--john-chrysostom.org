On the Palms.
=59.703=
αʹ. From wonders to wonders of the Lord let us go, brethren, and let us
arrive, as from power to power. For just as in a golden chain joined with
interlocking rings one is held by one, and of those joined each is fastened,
the one to the other, and is passed along: so also the wonders of the holy
Gospels lead the feast-loving Church of God from one into another, and they
gladden not with the food that perishes, but with that which remains unto
eternal life. Come then today we too, beloved, in readiness of heart and with
ears ready to hear, let us hear what the Lord God will speak to us both in
prophets and in Gospels concerning this most divine feast. He will surely
speak peace upon his people, and upon his holy ones, and upon those who turn
their heart toward him. Today prophetic trumpets have set the inhabited world
on wing, and have made bright and crowned the Churches of the Lord everywhere,
and having taken the faithful from the wrestling-pit of the holy fasts and
from the palaestra against the passions, they have taught them to sing to
Christ the victory-maker the triumphal hymn and the new spectacle of peace.
Come then all, let us rejoice in the Lord; come all the nations, let us clap
our hands, and let us shout to God our Savior with a voice of rejoicing. Let
no one be without a share of the joy, let no one fall short of the calling,
because the seed of the disobedient has become for destruction; let no one be
ungrateful toward the meeting of the king, lest he be shut out from the
bridechamber; let no one sullen or downcast be found among us toward the
reception, lest he be condemned together with the wicked citizens. And wicked
citizens I call those who do not wish to receive the Lord as king over
themselves. Let us all run together brightly, let us all =59.704= eagerly
receive him, and let us keep festival in a manner above the world. Instead of
garments let us pour out our hearts before him, and in psalms and hymns and
thanksgivings let us stretch them up to him, and the, Blessed is he who comes
in the name of the Lord, let us cry unceasingly. For those who bless him have
been blessed; and those who curse him have been cursed. Again I will speak,
and exhorting toward the good I will not cease: Come, beloved, let us bless
the Blessed, that we may be blessed by him. Every age and rank the word has
called together to the same toward the praise of the Lord, Kings of the earth
and all the peoples, rulers and all judges of earth, young men and virgins.
And the novel thing of the wonder, that to the new and inexperienced-in-evil
age of infant children and sucklings he granted the first honors of leading
off for the Lord in confession the God-taught song, as once Moses to the
people out of Egypt, the, Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.
Today the blessed David rejoices with joy, having had his lyre plundered by
infants, with whom also dancing in the spirit and feasting together, as
formerly before the face of the ark of God, he makes music together and
sweetly lisps along, the, Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord. To
whom we shall say: Tell us, O prophet, who is this who comes in the name of
the Lord? Not mine, he says, is the school today; for it has been dedicated to
infants by him who prepared praise out of the mouth of infants and sucklings,
to destroy enemy and avenger; That through this wonder also hearts of fathers
may be turned


upon children, and the disobedient in the prudence of the righteous. Tell, O
children, from where is this fair and gracious contest and song of yours? who
taught? who made wise? who gathered you together? where are the tablets? who
=59.705= are the pedagogues? Share with us, they say, the feast and the
melody, and you will learn the things Moses and the rest of the prophets had
in desires. Since then we have been invited, and the children have given us
right hands of fellowship, come, beloved, let us too emulate the God-befitting
dance, and with the apostles let us make a way for him who has mounted upon
the heaven of heaven toward the east, and who, as he was well pleased, is
borne upon a beast of burden upon the earth. With infants let us take up the
branches, and with the branches let us clap, that the Holy Spirit may breathe
upon us also, and that we may send up in good order the God-taught song,
Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord; Hosanna in the highest. Today
the patriarch Jacob keeps festival in the spirit, seeing his prophecy brought
to work, and him who binds his colt to the vine, mounted upon a young colt;
and with the faithful he worships in the spirit. Today a colt is prepared, the
formerly irrational exemplar of the unreasoning nations, for a showing of the
subjection of the new people from the nations; and infants declare their
infancy toward the knowledge of God. Today the king of glory is glorified
prophetically upon earth, and he shows the earth-born as partakers of the
heavenly feast; that he may show that he himself is Lord of both, as being
hymned from both sides in concord with Father and Spirit. For this reason
those above, declaring the salvation upon the earth, sang, Holy, holy, holy,
Lord of Sabaoth; the whole earth is full of his glory; but those below,
keeping festival together with the heavenly gladness, cried out, Hosanna in
the highest, Hosanna to the son of David. And those offered doxology, Blessed
is the glory of the Lord from his place; but these theologized, Blessed is he
who comes in the name of the Lord. βʹ. And while these things were being
accomplished thus, and the disciples were dancing concerning all the mighty
works they had seen, and saying, Blessed is he who comes in the name of the
Lord; glory in the highest, and on earth peace; the city was shaken, saying:
Who is this? stirring hardened envy against the Master's glory. And when you
hear city, recognize the old disorderly multitude of the synagogue. The
ungrateful ask, Who is this? as not yet having seen the benefactor and one
famed by God-befitting wonders. For the shadow did not overtake the
unapproachable light that shone forth in it. Whence pointedly Isaiah cried out
beforehand to them saying: Hear, you deaf, and look, you blind, that you may
see. And who is blind, but my children? and deaf, but those who lord it over
them? And the servants of God were blinded. You have seen many times, and you
have not kept watch; the ears have been opened, and you have not heard. Do you
see, beloved, the exactness of the words? how the foreseeing divine Spirit
through his saints proclaimed beforehand the things to come as present? For
the ungrateful saw, and through the wonders they handled the wonder-working
God, and they remained in unbelief; they saw the man blind from birth
proclaiming the Lord who had given him light; they saw a paralytic
contemporaneous with and yoked to the infirmity, parted by a command from the
misery; they beheld Lazarus become a fugitive of death; they heard the walking
on the sea, the uncultivated wine-giving, the toilless bread-feeding, the
driving-out of the demons, the vigor of the sick; their streets proclaimed the
powers, their roads announced the healings to the wayfarers; all Judaea was
filled with the benefactions, and now they inquire, Who is this? O the folly
of the falsely-named teachers! O the disobedient fathers! O the senseless
elders! O seed of Canaan the


shameless, and not of Judah the pious! The children recognized the creator,
and the disobedient fathers said, Who is this? The new age hymned God, and
those grown old in days of evils inquired. Who is this? The sucklings
theologize, and the elders blaspheme, children piously minister praise as a
sacred rite, and unholy priests impiously are indignant? You disobedient in
the prudence of the righteous, turn your hearts upon your children, learn the
mysteries of God. The very thing being accomplished bears witness, that he is
God who is thus hymned by untaught tongues. Search the Scriptures, as you have
heard from the Master, that they are they that bear witness concerning him,
and you are not ignorant of the wonder. Hear, you ungrateful, what the prophet
Zechariah =59.706= proclaims as good news to you. For he says: Rejoice
greatly, daughter of Zion; behold your king comes to you, righteous and
mounted upon a colt of an ass. Why do you shake off the joy? why, while the
sun is shining, do you love the darkness? why toward the peace that cannot be
warred against do you study war? If then you are sons of Zion, dance with your
children; let the piety of the children be for you an occasion of joy. Learn
from them, who taught, who gathered, from where the teachings, what is the new
theology, and the old prophecy. And if no one of men taught any of these
things, but of their own accord they strike up the praise, know that the work
is of God, as it is written in the law: Out of the mouth of infants and
sucklings you have prepared praise. Double then the joy, because you have
become fathers of such, who even the things unknown to the elders have praised
as God-taught. Turn then your hearts upon your children, and do not shut your
eyes toward the truth. But if you yourselves are they, and hearing you do not
hear, and seeing you do not see, and you are vainly at variance with the
infants, they themselves will be your judges according to the word of the
Savior. Well then also this among the other things the prophet Isaiah
proclaimed beforehand concerning you saying: Jacob shall not now be ashamed,
nor shall he now change his face; but when they see their children, my works,
they shall sanctify the Holy One of Jacob because of me, and they shall fear
the God of Israel; and they who err in spirit shall know understanding, and
the murmurers shall learn to obey, and the stammering tongues shall learn to
speak peace. Do you see, O foolish Jew, how from the opening of the word the
prophet announces shame beforehand to you because of your disobedience? Learn
at least from him, how he proclaims the hymnody of your children as
God-taught, as also the blessed David proclaimed beforehand saying: Out of the
mouth of infants and sucklings you have prepared praise. Either then
appropriate the holiness of the children, as is due, or together with the
piety grant us the children also. Let us dance with these and keep festival
with them, and with the new tongue let us sing together the God-taught song.
For once Simeon the elder met the Savior, and in his arms received the Master
of the ages as an infant, and proclaimed him Lord and God; but now instead of
ungrateful elders the children met the Savior as Simeon, and spread the
branches instead of arms, and blessed as Lord and God him who sat upon a colt
as upon Cherubim, Hosanna to the son of David, blessed is he who comes in the
name of the Lord. With whom let us also cry, Blessed is the God from God, the
king of glory, who became poor in his own things without poverty for our sake,
that he might enrich us by his goodness; blessed is he who came in humility,
and comes again in glory; first, meek upon a colt and praised by infants, that
the written word might be fulfilled, Your goings were seen, O God, the goings
of my God, the king who is in the holy place; second, fearful upon the clouds,
and escorted by angels and powers. O the honey-flowing tongue of the children!
O the


unadulterated teaching of those well-pleasing to God! David having prophesied
hid the meaning under the letter; the children, having opened the treasure,
brought the wealth upon the tongue, and with a graced word they called all
together piercing-loud unto the enjoyment. Let us then draw with them also the
wealth that cannot be taken away, laying up the gifts in insatiable bosoms and
storehouses; let us cry without end, Blessed is he who comes in the name of
the Lord, true God, almighty from almighty, Son in the name of the Father,
true king, from a true king, having the kingdom co-eternal and before the
ages, as he who begot him. For the kingdom is common, and the Word does not
allot its honor to the Son from without, neither one that has begun or is
added or is diminished, far be it, but natural and truly his own. For one is
the kingdom of Father and Son and Holy Spirit, just as also one essence and
one lordship. Whence also with one worship let us serve one tri-hypostatic
Godhead, without beginning, uncreated, unbounded, and without successor. For
neither will the Father ever cease from being Father, nor the Son from being
Son and =59.707= king; so neither the Holy Spirit from being that which it is
in hypostasis. For nothing of the Trinity will be lessened, or will ever cease
either from the eternity, or from the community and kingdom. For it is not
because the Son of God became man for us, and conquered the tyrant against us,
having worked this victory in the flesh against the enemy hostile to it, that
he was named king, but because he is always Lord and God, therefore he is
king, as he who begot him, and now, both with the former, and unto the
perpetual. Do not blaspheme the kingdom of Christ, O heretic, lest you
dishonor him who begot him. If you are a believer, come to Christ our true
God, and not as having liberty as a covering of evil. If you are a slave, in
trembling be subject to the Master. For the word-fighter is not well-disposed,
but a manifest enemy. For it is written: He who does not honor the Son does
not honor the Father who sent him. γʹ. But we, beloved, let us return from
where we went out, crying with the word, Blessed is he who comes in the name
of the Lord, the shepherd the fair and good, to lay down his soul willingly
for his sheep; that just as wolf-hunters catch with a sheep the wolves that
feed on sheep, so the chief shepherd, having set himself forth as a man to the
intelligible and soul-destroying wolves, may make prey of the hunters by Adam
who was long ago hunted by them. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the
Lord, God against the devil, not openly through the power that cannot be
looked upon, but through the weakness of the flesh, to bind the strong one
against us. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, the king against
the tyrant, not through the almighty power and wisdom, but through the
supposed foolishness of the cross, to plunder the spoils of the serpent wise
in evil. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, the true against the
liar, the Savior against the destroyer, the prince of peace against the
war-maker, the lover of man against the hater of man. Blessed is he who comes
in the name of the Lord, Lord to save the man who had gone astray, to dissolve
the error, to give light to those in darkness, to bring to nothing the deceit
of idols, to bring in instead a saving knowledge of God, to sanctify the
world, to drive out the defilement and pollution of the service of vain
things. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, one for many, to
deliver a poor race of men from the hand of those stronger than it, and a poor
and needy one from those who plunder him. Blessed is he who comes in the name
of the Lord, to pour wine and oil upon him who fell among the robbers, and was
overlooked. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, to save us
through himself, as the prophet says: Not an ambassador, nor an angel, but the
Lord himself saved them.


For this reason we too bless you, Lord, who with Father and Holy Spirit before
the ages and unto ages =59.708= of ages are blessed; before the ages and until
now without body, but now, and unto ages, with your divine and unchangeable
and undivided incarnation. Let us see also what follows. What does the most
divine evangelist say? For when the Lord had entered into the temple, he says,
there came to him blind and lame, and he healed them. But the chief priests
and the Pharisees, seeing the wonders which he did, and the children crying
and saying, Hosanna to the son of David, blessed is he who comes in the name
of the Lord, did not bear his honor. Whence coming to him they said: Do you
not hear what these say? Are you not weighed down, being glorified as God? are
you not vexed hearing from the innocent the things that befit God alone? has
not God long ago made clear through the prophet, My glory I will not give to
another? and, How do you, being a man, make yourself God? But what to these
things does the long-suffering one, who is much in mercy, and slow to anger?
He bears with the mad, he checks the inflammation by a defense, he reminds
them in turn of Scriptures, he brings forward testimonies of the things being
accomplished, and he does not refuse the question. Wherefore he also says:
Have you never also heard me saying through the prophet, Then you shall know,
that I myself who speak am present? nor again, Out of the mouth of infants and
sucklings you have prepared praise because of your enemies, to destroy enemy
and avenger? who are you, who attend to the law, and read prophets, and set at
nought me who am proclaimed in the law. For you think in a pretext of piety to
champion the glory of God, not understanding that he who sets me at nought
also sets the Father at nought. For I came forth from God, and I have come
into the world; and my glory is also the glory of my Father. And the foolish,
thus convicted by our Savior God, ceased from the contradiction, having been
muzzled by the truth; but having drawn to themselves another thought of folly,
they were taking counsel against him. But let us sing, Great is our Lord, and
great is his strength, and of his understanding there is no number. For all
these things have come to pass, that the lamb who takes away the sin of the
world and Son of God may come willingly for us unto the saving passion, and
may be made known at the praetorium, and those who set at nought him who
purchases the world with his life-giving blood may agree for thirty silver
pieces, and the pascha, Christ, may be sacrificed for us, that those who are
sprinkled with his precious blood and seal their lips as doorposts may escape
the darts of the destroyer; and thus Christ who suffered in flesh, and rose on
the third day, may be worshipped with equal honor and equal glory with the
Father and the Holy Spirit by all creation; because to him every knee shall
bow of things in heaven and on earth and under the earth, sending up glory to
him unto the ages of ages. Amen.
