Interpretation of the "Our Father"
=59.627=
Our Father who art in heaven. Do you call God Father, O man? You speak
well. For he is Father of all. But hasten to work works that please your
Father. But if you do evil works, it is clear that you invoke the devil
as father; for he is the overseer of evils. Therefore hasten to flee
from this one, and to please your good Father and maker. Hallowed be thy
name. And what then? Is God not holy? Yes, he is holy; but this is what
you say: In me let thy name be hallowed, so that men may see my good
works, and glorify my Father and maker. Thy kingdom come. What then? Is
God not king, that his kingdom is about to come? Yes, he is king of all,
but just as a city surrounded by enemies seeks the king's power to come
and deliver it; so also we, being surrounded by the opposing powers and
by our sins and wicked thoughts, seek the kingdom of God to come, to
deliver us. And in another way: since the prophet says, God has reigned
over the nations, using the past tense as future, for this reason we
cry: Thy kingdom come, O Lord; that is, Let thy mercies come upon us;
Thy will be done, as in heaven, also on earth. For he speaks thus: Lord,
just as thy will came to be in heaven, and all the angels are in peace,
and there is not among them one who pushes and is pushed back, nor one
who strikes and is struck, but all are in deep peace; so also among us
men on earth let thy will be done; that all the nations with one mouth
and one heart may glorify our maker =59.628= and Savior. Give us this
day our daily bread. We ask to receive daily bread. But the bread of the
soul is the word of God, as one of the saints said: Open, child, your
mouth to the word of God. And for this reason it is good to remember God
more often than to breathe; And forgive us our debts, as we also forgive
our debtors. And he speaks thus. And forgive us our debts, that is, our
sins and our faults, as we also forgive all our brothers who offend and
sin against us, both free and slaves, and all who are under our hand.
Saying these things, O man, if you do not act thus, consider that it is
a fearful thing for us to fall into the hands of the living God; and
having set yourself right, turn back to the maker and Lord. But deliver
us from the evil one. If Satan should seek to sift us as wheat, just as
he sought the apostles, and as was due he failed, but as of old with
Job, do not give him authority against us; but if even a wicked man
should wish to tempt us or to wrong us, do not give us over to his will,
but cover us in the shelter of thy wings. For thine is the kingdom and
the power. Lord, since the kingdom is thine, do not let us fear another
kingdom, or another dominion, even if we are worthy of punishment
because of our sins. Do thou punish us, in whatever way thou wilt, and
do not deliver us into the hands of men; but let us fall into thy hands,
because as is thy greatness, so also is thy mercy, Father almighty, unto
the ages. Amen.
