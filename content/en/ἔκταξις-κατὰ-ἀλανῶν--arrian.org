* Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Let the scout cavalry lead the whole army, drawn up two abreast, with
their own commander. After these, the Petraean horse-archers, these too
two abreast. Let the decurions lead them. After these let those from the
ala named the Auriani be posted. Let those of the fourth cohort of the
Raeti be drawn up with them, whose commander is Daphnes the Corinthian;
after these, those from the ala named the Coloni. Let the Ituraeans and
Cyrenaeans and those from the first Raetian be drawn up with them.

* Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

Let Demetrius command all of these. After these, the Celtic horsemen,
these too two abreast, and let a centurion lead them, as in camp.

* Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

Let the infantry be posted after these, having their standards raised
before them, both the Italians and those of the Cyrenaeans who are
present. Let Pulcher lead them all, who also commands the Italians. Let
the Bosporan infantry go after these, providing Lamprocles as commander,
and after these the Nomads, subordinate to Verus their own commander.

* Section 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

Let the formation be four hoplites deep. Of these themselves, let those
who are archers lead. Let the Aceian horsemen guard the flanks of the
formation on either side. After these let the selected horsemen go, and
after these the horsemen from the phalanx, then the catapults, then

* Section 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

the standard of the fifteenth legion, and around it the commander of the
legion, Valens, and the deputy and the tribunes who are assigned, and
the centurions who are officers of the first cohort. And before the
standard of the infantry, let the javelin-men be posted. And let the
infantry themselves go drawn up four deep.

* Section 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

After the fifteenth legion let the standard of the twelfth legion be
posted, and tribunes around it and centurions. Let this legion too go
drawn up four deep in the same way.

* Section 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

After the heavy infantry let the allied force be posted, both those from
Lesser Armenia and of the Trapezuntians, the hoplites, and the Colchians
and the Rhiziani who are spearmen. Let the Aplanian infantry be posted
after them. Let Secundinus be commander of the whole allied force, who
also leads the Aplanians.

* Section 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

After these let the baggage-train follow. Let the ala of the Getae and
its prefect bring up the rear.

* Section 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

Let centurions and those assigned to this duty dress the flanks of the
infantry, and for protection let the Allactic ala ride alongside, drawn
up in single file on either side, and the horsemen of the Italians. And
let their prefect go along the flanks.

* Section 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

Let Xenophon, the commander of the whole army, for the most part lead in
front of the infantry standards, and let him go along the whole
formation and inspect how they go in order, and let him bring those who
are out of order back into formation, and praise those who go in good
order. Let them go thus drawn up.

* Section 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

When they have come to the appointed place, let the whole cavalry stand
around in a circle into a square formation, and send the scouts to the
higher ground for observation of the enemy. And there, at a signal, let
them arm in silence, and when armed let them take their places in
formation.

* Section 12
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

Let the formation be as follows. Let each wing of the infantry hold the
higher ground, because the battle-array will be of this kind. And on the
right wing let the Armenians around Vasaces and Arbelus be posted,
holding the highest part of the wing, because they are all archers.

* Section 13
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13
:END:

Let the infantry of the Italian cohort be posted in front of them. Let
Pulcher lead them all, who also commands the Italian cohort. And to him
let both Vasaces himself and Arbelus be entrusted, and their cavalry and
infantry.

* Section 14
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14
:END:

And on the left let these too be posted, holding the highest part of the
wing: both the allies from Lesser Armenia and the light-armed of the
Trapezuntians and the Rhizian spearmen. Let the two hundred Aplani be
posted in front of these, and the hundred Cyrenaeans, so that the
hoplites may be a screen in front of the javelin-men, and the latter may
throw over them from the higher ground.

* Section 15
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15
:END:

And of the whole center, let the fifteenth infantry legion hold the
right, beyond the middle of the whole ground, because these are far more
numerous; and let the infantry of the twelfth legion fill the remainder
of the left as far as the end of the left wing.

* Section 16
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16
:END:

Let them be drawn up eight deep, and let their closing-up be dense. And
let the first four ranks be of pike-bearers, whose pikes have long and
slender iron points projecting. And let the front-rank men hold these at
the ready, so that if the enemy should come near them, they may set the
iron of the pikes against the chests especially of the horses;

* Section 17
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h17
:END:

and let the second-rank men and those of the third and fourth ranks hold
out their pikes for throwing wherever they may strike, both wounding
horses and killing the rider, or, the pike having stuck in shield and
breastplate and been bent by the softness of the iron, rendering the
rider useless. And let the ranks next after these be of spear-bearers.

* Section 18
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h18
:END:

And ninth after these let there be a rank, the foot-archers, those of
the Nomads and Cyrenaeans and Bosporans and Ituraeans.

* Section 19
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h19
:END:

And let engines be stationed at each wing, so as to shoot when the enemy
are still approaching from farthest away, and these behind the whole
phalanx.

* Section 20
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h20
:END:

And let the whole cavalry, drawn up by alae and in eight companies,
stand behind the infantry, some at each of the two wings having the
hoplites and the archers as a screen in front of them, two companies;
and some at the middle of the phalanx, six companies μημιον.

* Section 21
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h21
:END:

Of these, let those who are horse-archers stand near the phalanx, so as
to shoot over it; and let those who are spear-bearers or pike-bearers or
sword-bearers or axe-bearers look toward the flanks on either side and
wait for the signal.

* Section 22
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h22
:END:

And let the selected horsemen be around Xenophon himself, and of the
infantry from the phalanx as many as two hundred, the bodyguards, and as
many centurions as are assigned with the selected troops, and the
commanders of the bodyguards, and the decurions of the selected troops.

* Section 23
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h23
:END:

And let there be around him about a hundred light spearmen, so that,
going along the whole phalanx, wherever he should discover anything
lacking, he may see it and set it right.

* Section 24
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h24
:END:

Let Valens lead the whole right wing together with the cavalry, who is
also commander of the fifteenth legion; and the tribunes of the twelfth,
the left.

* Section 25
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h25
:END:

When they have been thus drawn up, let there be silence until the enemy
come within missile range; but when they are already approaching, let
all raise the war-cry to Enyalius as loud and as terrifying as possible,
and let missiles and stones be discharged from the engines and missiles
from the bows, and let the spear-bearers hurl their spears, both the
light-armed and the theiastai. And let stones too be carried against the
enemy from the allied force from the higher ground, and let the whole be
a skirmishing from every side as dense as possible, for the confusion of
the horses and the destruction of the horsemen.

* Section 26
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h26
:END:

And there is hope that under the indescribable multitude of missiles the
Scythians will not come any farther as they ride against the infantry
phalanx; but if they should come near, let them receive the charge as
stoutly as possible, pressing close with their shields and bracing with
their shoulders, and with the closest locking-together, the first three
ranks bracing themselves against one another as violently as possible;
and let the fourth rank hurl their spears over them;

* Section 27
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h27
:END:

and let the first rank strike or thrust unsparingly with the pikes both
at horses and at the men themselves. And if, when they have been beaten
off, a clear flight should occur, let the infantry ranks already open
and let the horsemen ride forward, not all the companies but half of
them;

* Section 28
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h28
:END:

And let those who will also ride first be posted first. And let the
other half follow those who ride forward, but in formation and not
employing a complete pursuit, so that if a strong flight should take
hold, they may take up the first pursuit with horses still fresh, but if
some wheeling-about should overtake them, they may attack

* Section 29
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h29
:END:

those who are wheeling about. And at the same time let the Armenian
archers ride forward and shoot, so as not to give the fugitives a chance
to turn, and let the light-armed spearmen follow at a run; and let the
infantry formation no longer remain in place, but advance faster than at
a walk, so that if anything more stubborn should meet them from the
enemy, they may again be a screen in front of the horsemen. Let these
things happen, if from the first charge

* Section 30
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h30
:END:

flight should take hold of the enemy; but if they should wheel about and
wish to ride past in circles beyond the wings, let the wings of the
light archery itself be extended still onto the higher ground; for I do
not approve that they should ever, seeing the wings become weak by the
extension, push through them and cut through the infantry.

* Section 31
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h31
:END:

And if they outflank both wings or either one, it is then altogether
necessary that their horses become exposed in flank to them, and their
pikes in flank. Thereupon let the horsemen charge into them, no longer
with throwing but closing with their spathae, and others with their
axes. And the Scythians, being unprotected and having their horses
unprotected  [lost]
