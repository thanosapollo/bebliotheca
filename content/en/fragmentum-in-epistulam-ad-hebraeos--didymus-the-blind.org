* Fragment on the Epistle to the Hebrews
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1:6 If all generated nature worships Christ (for through the name of the
angels he means the higher rational nature, as also in "for to which of the
angels has he ever said: sit at my right hand?" and he himself is above this)
and the word of the scriptures forbade worshiping a creature, saying, do not
look up into heaven, and seeing the sun and the moon and the stars and all the
world of heaven be led astray and worship them. And as it is forbidden to
worship these, so also the other creatures, even if some happen to have gone
higher. As has also been said before, it is proper to confirm that Christ is
not a creature but creator, even if for us he united to himself the created
flesh furnished with a rational and intellectual soul, and thus also is
worshiped as God by all creation. 65lem There is a time for all, and a season
for every matter under heaven.
