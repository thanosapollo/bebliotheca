Discourse of John Chrysostom on the nativity of Christ
Well and profitably the priests who went before, having also praised the day,
glorified the Master of the day and spiritually built up us the hearers. It
was necessary for us the unlearned to keep silence, since especially the flesh
is weak and the tongue stammering and the mind has become more sluggish. We
are compelled, on account of you the hearers, to set forth our most paltry
discourse in the church, that we may both praise the day and call you forth to
zeal for godliness. Today Christ was born and the world was enlightened; today
Christ is in a manger and the devil was mocked; today Persians and all the
nations are evangelized through the star, but Herod and the synagogue are
thrown into confusion through the magi; today Joseph and Mary and Christ are
in the cave, as Abraham and Sarah and Isaac in the tent, but Herod and the
synagogue, as Hagar and Ishmael in the wilderness.
Today Herod rages and magi rejoice; those the gifts and this one the sword;
for the magi the star is a way to salvation, for Herod the high priests a way
to murder; the barbarian nations gold and frankincense and myrrh, and the
law-learned Jews wood and nails and a spear; horsemen and bowmen, men who
despised these things, taking the gifts, followed the star; and those who read
the law and the prophets, having despised these, crucified the Master of
these; those honor the one wrapped in swaddling-clothes; but these reject the
one in signs and wonders. Those, having seen the star, passed from unbelief to
faith; but these, having seen the Savior, turned from godliness to impiety.
Those, having left their gods, go about seeking Christ himself; but these,
having left Christ, the wretched ones ask to have Barabbas. Today the nations
skip like lambs, having Christ with themselves; but the Jews melt like
honeycomb, awaiting the antichrist. And that the Jews receive antichrist,
Christ is witness: for if, he says, another comes in his own name, that one
they will receive. Today the churches of the orthodox, like meadows of roses
and lilies and violets, send up their spiritual fragrance to God. But the
conventicles of the heretics, like dark-colored flowers, the wretched ones
breathe a stench instead of fragrance.
Today the Word of God became man for us, and mortal man has been made immortal
by grace. Today Mary gave birth and the world was enlightened, she in the cave
and the inhabited world in the church. And what need was there of the birth
from Mary? That we, approaching the one of like passions, may inherit life and
incorruption. Christ is born of Mary, that he may choose the apostles, that he
may heal the sick, that he may raise the dead, that he may cleanse the lepers,
that he may set free those possessed. He is born of Mary, that he may
enlighten the nations, that he may teach the Jews, that he may open baptism,
that he may convict the betrayer, that he may call Zacchaeus, that he may
bless Nathanael. He is born of Mary, that he may kill the serpent that
deceived Eve, that he may lead Adam into paradise through the robber, that he
may grant us his body and blood for the forgiveness of sins. And for what
reason did he take the form of a slave? That the slaves also, approaching him,
may be set free, ... that he may set up the altar. And why did he humble
himself? Because we were not able

to draw near to his unmixed divinity. For the wise among hunters do this: he
who wishes to hunt a sparrow puts forward a sparrow; he who wishes to hunt a
partridge puts forward a partridge; he who wishes to hunt a dove puts forward
a dove, that like may be hunted through like. So also Christ did: he netted
men through the man, he trampled the devil through humility, he despoiled
Satan through his forbearance, and he prepared the kingdom of the heavens for
those who keep his commandments. Today angels glorify and men worship and the
dead make supplication. Today three ranks offer him gifts: men gold as to a
king, angels frankincense as to God, the dead myrrh as †through the dead†.
They offer different kinds, because different races pay him worship today.
Come then, as many of you as happen to be hearers of my words, let us keep
festival today in a Christian manner. How will this be? Let us repay no one
evil for evil, because it is written in Proverbs: if your enemy hungers, feed
him; for doing this you will heap coals of fire upon his head. Do not become a
cause of stumbling, because the Lord says in the Gospels: Amen I say to you:
whoever shall cause one of these little ones who believe in me to stumble, it
is better for him that a millstone of an ass be hung about his neck and he be
thrown into the sea. Share with the one in need of roof, covering, food, you
who use these beyond need; because again the Lord says: Blessed are the
merciful, for they shall obtain mercy.
Do not think great things of yourself; for he who thinks himself to be
something, being nothing, deceives himself. Deem your priests worthy of much
honor and attendance; for they keep watch over your souls, as those who will
give account. Abstain from fornication and from what is strangled and from
blood. For accursed before the Lord is he who eats blood or what is strangled
or what is taken by beasts or a bird that died of itself or hunted game that
died in a snare; and let his memorial be utterly destroyed from the earth. Cut
out the habit of oaths, glorifying the Christ who has been born, because to
him belongs honor and worship unto the ages of ages. Amen.
