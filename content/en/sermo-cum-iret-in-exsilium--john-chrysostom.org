WHEN HE WAS GOING INTO EXILE.
=52.435=
αʹ. Bright is the word for us, my brothers, and splendid the festival, and
a spacious sea filled full, but not troubled by the storm of the winds.
For the mother of peace has come, she who quenches the storm of the
winds: Mother Sion shall say, A man and a man was born in her, and he
himself established her. My little children, are they about to depose me?
And why do I fear death? For me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.
But will they send me into exile? The earth is the Lord's, and the
fullness thereof. But will there be confiscation of goods for me? We
brought nothing into the world, and it is clear that we can carry nothing
out. But you know, brothers, for what cause they are about to depose me.
Because I did not spread carpets, and I did not put on silk garments,
because I did not soothe their gluttony, I did not bring gold and silver.
And they say to me, You ate and drank, and you baptized. If I did this,
let a curse be upon me; may I not be numbered with the bishops, may I not
be with the angels, may I not please God.
And if I both ate and baptized, I did nothing untimely of the things they
say.
Let them depose Paul the apostle also, because after supper he granted
the jailer
=52.436= baptism; and let them depose the Lord himself, because after
the supper he granted communion to the disciples. I see many waves and
the swell is hard, and spears made ready; and I, as a helmsman in a great
swell, sit upon the two sterns of the ship, that is, upon the Old and New
Testament, and with the oars I thrust away the storm; not with the wooden
oars, but with the precious cross of the Master I turn the storm into
peace.
The Master commands, and the slave is crowned; therefore he himself does
(not) hand him over to the devil. And one thing (read: or do they not
know?) men do not know, that through the unclean the most pure vessel is
made manifest? Brothers, I set three propositions before you: faith,
trial, chastity. If you say you endure faith, imitate the blessed
Abraham, who was past his prime in age and received ripe fruits. And if
you say you endure trial, imitate the blessed Job. You know his manner,
and you have heard of his endurance, and his end has not escaped you.
And if you wish to endure chastity, imitate the blessed Joseph, who
=52.437= was sold into Egypt, and set free Egypt wasting with famine.
For a trial was added to him from an Egyptian harlot enslaved to desire,
who sat beside him, saying, Lie with me. For the Egyptian woman wished
to strip him of chastity in Egypt; and here the Egyptian. But neither
did that woman trip the holy man, nor this one this man; but together
there appeared the chastity of freedom, and the nobility of the
children, and the wantonness of the barbarian woman. βʹ. Brothers, the
thief does not come where there is straw, and grass, and wood; but where
gold, or silver, or a pearl lies; so the devil does not enter where
there is a fornicator, or a profane man, or a robber, or a greedy man;
but where those who lead the desert life. Brothers, do we wish to spread
out the tongue toward the queen? But what shall I say? Jezebel is in
tumult, and Elijah flees; Herodias rejoices, and John is bound; the
Egyptian woman lies, and Joseph is imprisoned. If then they exile me, I
imitate Elijah; if they throw me into the mire, Jeremiah; if into the
sea, the prophet Jonah; if also into the pit, Daniel; if they stone me,
Stephen; if they behead me, John the Forerunner; if they beat me with
rods, Paul; if they saw me, Isaiah. Would that it were with a wooden
saw, that I may enjoy the longing of the cross. The embodied woman wars
against the bodiless; she who is entangled with baths and ointments and
with a husband wars against the pure and spotless Church. But she too
shall sit as a widow, while her husband is still living; because you are
a woman, and you wish to make the Church a widow. In the evening she
called me a thirteenth apostle, and today she named me Judas. Yesterday
she sat with me in freedom, and today she sprang upon me as a beast.
The sun ought to have been quenched among us, and the moon not to
appear, and only that we not forget the saying of Job. For Job, who
endured so great a blow, cried out nothing else but, Let the name of the
Lord be blessed unto the ages. For when his wife cried out saying: Speak
some word to the Lord, and die, he rebuked her saying: Why have you
spoken as one of the foolish women? O thankless woman! O plaster of
pains! Tell me, woman, when you were once sick, did Job utter such
things to you? and did he not wipe away your disease with prayers and
good works? When he lived in royal courts, when he had the money, when
he had the royal attendance, you said nothing of this sort, and now
seeing him sitting on a dunghill, and rolled up by worms, you say this,
Speak some word to the Lord, and die. Was the temporary chastening not
enough for him, but through the word you also procure eternal punishment
for him? But what does the blessed Job say? Why have you spoken as one
of the foolish women?
If we received the good things from the hand of the Lord, shall we not
bear the evil? And why does this lawless and hateful woman, this new
Jezebel, I mean, not also cry out and say from... and crossing....
flee... but she sends consuls and tribunes to me, and only threatens.
And what has she let loose on me? Spiders sent by a spider. All
brothers, because even in toils victory is laid up, and in the contests
a crown is laid up, as the divine Paul was just now saying, I have
fought the good fight, I have finished the course, I have kept the
faith, and henceforth there is laid up the crown of righteousness, which
the Lord will render to me on that day, the righteous judge; because to
him is the glory and the power unto the ages of ages. Amen.
