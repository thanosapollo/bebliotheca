* Commentaries on the Epistle of Jeremiah
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

=93.773= "Copy of a letter." The prophet, being fully assured by the Spirit
that they are about to become captives, is commanded by God even before the
captivity to write the letter, so that when they become captives they may have
it as provision, that they not be deceived and worship the idols of the
Babylonians. =93.776= "Until seven generations." That here he numbers the
generation as ten years. "For my angel." Clearly an angel also defends those
in captivity, so that they not be destroyed with utter destruction by the
enemies. "For their tongue." Of the idols, that is of the false teachers: by
the craftsman of evils, the devil, the tongue becomes glib toward falsehood.
"And they are overlaid with gold." The false teachers, having a form of piety,
but having denied the power itself. "But they are false and cannot speak."
That falsehood does not speak truth. "And to the prostitutes upon the roof."
That by roof he means the brothel. "But these are not saved from rust." The
gods of the nations; but neither the false teachers from the diabolical rust.
"Therefore do not fear them." Against the rottenness of the idols, because
they cannot help themselves. "Their eyes." He says of the idols, but no less
of the heresiarchs, or also of those occupied with earthly things: the eyes of
the mind are full of diabolical dust, unable to look down upon what is true
from the diabolical operations that enter into them. "They light lamps." After
the idols understand also those deceived to look into the law of God.
Concerning which it is written: Your law is a lamp to my feet. But not even by
prophetic word, as by a lamp burning in a murky place. "They have been
blackened." The idolaters, being like the idols. For it is written that Those
who make them would become like them. And all who are operated upon by demons
have the face of the soul without light, being darkened from the diabolical
smoke that is in their heart. "Upon the body." Also their soul, he says, and
the ruling part itself, that is the mind, or also their church, and the
leaders, demons strike, because God did not make a covering. "In which there
is no spirit." After the idols understand also those operated upon by them,
being outside the divine Spirit. "Nor to a poor man." And this too is a proof
of demonic hatred of mankind. "Sitting apart of their sacrifices." It is to be
noted, then, that a woman in childbed and a woman sitting apart must not touch
the sacred sacrifices. For the law also commanded this. And toward
contemplation, it forbids unclean souls to perform priestly service. "Because
women set before." It is to be noted that women must not perform priestly
service. And toward the sense, that the =93.777= souls of the priests must not
be slack, but manly and noble. "Whose heads are uncovered." Not having the
helmet of salvation. Mute, he says, is the voiceless idol. Bel, Zeus, even the
Greeks worshipped. "And the women having put ropes around themselves." Ropes,
he says, are the girdles; for covering only the unseemly parts of the body,
they left the rest naked, calling the lovers. "Burning the bran as incense."
To Aphrodite perhaps, or to some procurer. And perhaps the wretched ones also
did this for purification. "Because it has not been deemed worthy." That it
was not deemed worthy of God's visitation. How indeed would the things
constructed by them be about to be gods, because that which comes to be by
another is not God. "It will be known after these things that they are false."
Which indeed has also come to pass through the appearing of the Savior. "For
just as crows." The crow is an unclean bird. Unclean then are the demons,
being neither heavenly because they are without honor, nor earthly, because
they are falsely named; and otherwise, they were cast out from the heavenly
vaults by God, and they are driven about from the earth by holy men through
the help of Christ. And according to a mystical account the Savior also went
up onto the cross, cleansing the air of their uncleanness; and he has come to
the underworld, dissolving their tyranny there. "But these neither in their
forms." The idols, he says, are neither bright, as sun and moon; nor active,
as fire and wind. And if the things that differ from them incomparably in
forms and in power are not gods, how are these to be thought to be gods? "And
signs among the nations." For this reason perhaps the Pharisees also asked the
Savior, saying: What sign do you show us from heaven? And the star alone
sufficed to show that the one born is God; and the things that came to be upon
the cross showed his godhead, when the sun drew in its own rays; and the voice
brought from heaven, This is my beloved Son. Behold therefore also the signs
from heaven. "A scarecrow." The words of the Greeks call them scarecrows, and
kerambela, which the field-guards set up toward fear of birds or also of men;
and these are also called bogeys. "And they themselves will afterwards be
eaten." By time, clearly. Observing the bounds of the divine command. "Better
therefore is a just man." The "better" is not according to comparison, but
instead of "praiseworthy before God," not having idols, not only perceptible
ones, but not even carrying about according to the soul the idols of sin. "For
he will be far from reproach." For the =93.780= one pure both in deed and in
mind, on the day of judgment, with those being blessed and not those being
cursed, will stand the right-hand station. Let us therefore also entreat that
the God of all become our guide; so that walking by the light of his face
according to the prophetic melody, we may not turn aside right or left; but
may travel the royal road, which leads away into eternal life. Which may it be
granted us to attain by grace and loving-kindness of our Lord and God and
Savior Jesus Christ, with whom to the Father glory, honor, power with the
all-holy Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
