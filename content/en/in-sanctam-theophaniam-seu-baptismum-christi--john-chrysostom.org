ON THE HOLY THEOPHANY.
=50.805=
The spring of the evangelical teachings has its streams opened, and if anyone
who thirsts drinks from that spring, he is also made alive; and he is made
alive according to the spirit and the law of the commandments, receiving a
gladness beyond wine. For the sweetness of the spiritual oracles is
insatiable; it does not gladden the belly, but the heart, and it leads the
thoughts of the pious toward the love of God. For you heard just now in the
Gospel the Baptist John crying out to the multitude of the Jews that had come
together from every side and was being baptized: I indeed baptize you in water
unto repentance; but he who comes after me is stronger than I. He who comes
after me: after, because of the birth of the generation. But he who comes after
me is stronger than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry.
And what sandals was the Lord wearing that had to be carried, that he said, I
am not worthy to carry the sandals? Here he calls sandals the mysteries of the
economy; for the incarnation of the Lord is named sandals. And of this the Lord
is witness, crying through the prophet: Upon Idumaea I will stretch out my
sandal. For this reason the Lord's incarnation was also called a footstool, as
having appeared upon the earth and reached the end of the earth. Which the
prophet also, making the manifestation from both, cried out: Exalt the Lord our
God, and worship at the footstool of his feet. I indeed baptize you in water
unto repentance; but he who comes after me is stronger than I, whose sandals I
am not worthy to carry; he will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire. Whose
winnowing-fan is in his hand; and he will gather the wheat into his storehouse,
but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire. When John was giving these
charges and trumpeting the stronger Lord, those being baptized by him opposed
John, saying: Do you have someone stronger than you, O prophet? Why do you
slander yourself? You are the son of the great Zechariah; you are longed for by
men and feared by beasts. Do you have someone stronger than you? Why do you
prattle? There is none stronger than you. You were born according to promise;
you were announced by Gabriel; you were made manifest at the altar; you loosed
the tongue of him who begot you, which had been bound by Gabriel; you have been
called John, which is interpreted grace of God.
Why then do you slander yourself? You do not have one stronger than you. But
John, as a servant bearing witness for the Master, not deigning to usurp divine
dignity, cried back to the opposing crowds, saying: You say what you do not
know; I bear witness to what I know. One stronger than I is coming. I am for
his sake, he is not for mine; I am from a barren woman, he from a virgin; I am
a soldier, he a king; I am a forerunner, he is heavenly; I have the Spirit on
loan, he supplies life to all men; I am a herald of repentance, he the giver of
adoption; I baptize you in water, pre-cleansing you as vessels soiled by sins,
he will put in the myrrh of grace: He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and
fire, not a burning fire, but a sanctifying one. I move a tongue of
admonition; he, bearing the winnowing-fan of just =50.806= judgment, will
carry up the righteous as fair wheat into the storehouse of the heavens, but
the sinners who have not repented he will hand over as chaff to the
unquenchable fire. But he who comes after me is stronger than I. Wait for him;
receive from him the perfect baptism; I am a lamp in part, he is a sun
everywhere. But he who comes after me is stronger than I. When John was giving
these charges and things like them, the crowds baptized by him waited,
comparing with one another and saying: Let us be patient and see the kingdom or
fantasy that is about to pass through; John has loaded our ears with astounding
words; he says one stronger than himself is coming, bringing a winnowing-fan
and fire and Spirit. While the crowds were thus watching for the presence of a
worldly kingdom, behold, the Master and God of all coming down from above; see
also the Lord in the appearance of the incarnation, alone, plain, in a single
tunic, as one of all men, having reached the Jordan, bent his head to John,
wishing to be baptized by him. When the Baptist John therefore saw this humble
sight of Christ's arrival, contrary to much expectation, he grew dizzy, was at
a loss, and was struck with amazement; he looked round for a place of flight,
wanting to run away; and he did not find one; for he was being held and
constrained invisibly; he cried to the Lord who was holding him, saying: I have
need to be baptized by you, and do you come to me? What are you doing, Master?
Why do you betray me to the crowds unto death? Now the crowds will stone me as
a liar; I proclaimed great things about you, and you have come as one plain and
a stranger.
What are you doing, Master? Both above, Son of a king, and below, Son of a
king; and nowhere a royal scepter? Show your dignity; why have you come alone
and plain? Where is your company of angels? Where the rank of archangels? Where
is your six-winged ministry of the Cherubim? Where the winnowing-fan? Where the
fire? Where is your Holy Spirit? You glorified Moses, you set a bright cloud
beside him, you prepared a pillar of fire to go before him, you flashed round
his face with angelic glory, you clothed the servant in so great a glory, and
you the Master, having come in a private figure, bend your head to me? Lift it
up; you are the head of all; show what you are; you have shown the lowly
things, show also the high; baptize those present, and before all, me. Why do
you wish to be baptized? The Jordan will not receive it; it has recognized you
as the maker, it has turned the streams backward, it does not go forward, it
refuses its order. The elements know you, and shall I not know? If you wish to
be baptized, baptize yourself. I do not stretch out my hands; they refuse the
joining, they grow numb, they draw back; what they were not taught to hold,
they do not accept. I have need to be baptized by you, and do you come to me? I
know who you are, =50.807= and you know that I know you. Why do you hide from
me who know you? While I was in my mother's womb, shut in, and not finding the
way out before the time, I only saw you being carried by her who was carried by
you; I recognized you, and not finding how to speak, using my mother's mouth, I
cried through her my own words, saying: Whence is this to me, that the mother
of my Lord should come to me? There I did not err, and here I do not know? I
have need to be baptized by you, and do you come to me? But the Lord said to
him, as you heard just now: Permit it now; for thus it is fitting for us to
fulfill all righteousness. Permit it now; you do not know what you are saying;
I know what I am working. You seek that the glory of my divinity be revealed to
you; the time does not have it; all things are good in their season.
You speak truly, but you are not believed; for the nation of the Jews is
unbelieving; did you not call them offspring of vipers? How then do you want me
to be revealed to these? They do not receive your witness concerning me; they
suspect you, because of the suspicion, of being a forerunner. Permit it now;
they will receive a greater witness. Permit it now; for thus it is fitting for
us to fulfill all righteousness. And John says to him: How, Master? And the
Lord to him: As I was circumcised, that I might fulfill the law, I am baptized
that I may confirm the grace. If I fulfill a part and leave a part, I leave the
economy truncated; I must fulfill all, that afterward Paul may write: Christ is
the fulfillment of the law unto righteousness for everyone who believes. Permit
it now; for thus it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness. Permit,
John, the nature of =50.808= the water to be sanctified; I must fulfill all.
If I am not baptized by you, a king will not consent to be baptized by a poor
priest. Permit it now; permit the Father to cry from above, This is my beloved
Son, so that the Jews may not begin to say son of the carpenter, but of the
heavenly Father. Permit it now; do not oppose Adam; I have become a second
Adam, wishing to wash away the stain of his offenses; if I am not baptized, how
will he be cleansed? For this reason I am baptized at thirty years also,
awaiting his time, so that mine may be confirmed by his. Permit it now, that
what was spoken through the prophet may be fulfilled: The voice of the Lord is
upon the waters. And there was a fearful sight to see. Trembling, John touched
the Master's crown; and from above, the heavens having been opened, angels were
looking down upon what was taking place; and the grace of the Spirit,
approaching the Master's head in the form of a dove, was thrusting aside John's
right hand.
And the multitude stood still more at a loss, and saying to one another, Who is
this? Then, as answering the crowds who were asking one another, Who is this?,
the Father cried out, saying: This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well
pleased; hear him. The Begetter cries out his affection toward the Son, that
you may learn the Creator's disposition toward the world, that he gave such a
Son for such servants, the beloved for the hated, the sinless for sinners, the
glorious for the inglorious. Let us therefore glorify the Master who has
appeared, with the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, now and always and
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
