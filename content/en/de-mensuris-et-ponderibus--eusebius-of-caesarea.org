* On Measures and Weights
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

A shorter selection of Eusebius of Pamphilus concerning measures and weights.
1 There are many differences of them according to different countries, but the
things set below lie in one sequence in full assurance; and the hypothesis also
has not a little benefit because very many things are brought in in the
God-inspired scriptures.

2 Concerning the liquid measures.
An alabaster 1 litra.
The amphora of wine is a metretes of 48 xestai.
A cab of 6 xestai.
A xestes of 2 litrai.
A cotyle of 1 litra.
A tryblion 4.
An oxybaphon 3.
A cados of 24 xestai.
A prochos of 12 xestai.
A chous of 6 xestai.
A cyathus 2.
A mystron.
A cochlearion 4.
The xestes then is dissolved into 6 cochlearia.

3 Still concerning the liquid measures.
A nebel of wine of 150 xestai.
A cados of oil of 50 xestai.
A bath of 50 xestai.
A colathos of 25 xestai.
A saphitha of 22 xestai.
A maris of 20 xestai.
A hin the great of 18 xestai.
And the holy of 9 xestai.
A hydria of 10 xestai.
A stamnos of 10 xestai.
A campsakes of 4 xestai.
The weight of wine of 10 litrai of oil become 9 litrai, and of honey 15 litrai.

4 Concerning the dry measures.
A cor of 30 sata, 45 modii.
A gomer of 15 modii.
A medimnon of 6 modii.
A metretes of 4 modii.
A leketh of 15 modii.
An artabe of 4 modii; now a modius of 16 xestai.
A metron of 7 xestai.
A small gomer of 7 xestai.
A choenix of 2 xestai. and 3.
A Pontic cyprus of 2 modii.
A saton a sesquialter of the modius, that is the half with the whole, of 24 xestai.
A hin of 2 xestai.
A xestes of 2 litrai.
A drax the holding of the hand.
And the small measures of the dry likewise to the aforementioned liquids.
A Saite of 22 xestai.
An aporyma of 11 xestai.

5 Concerning weights.
A talent of 60 litrai.
A mina of 1 litra.
A litra of 12 ounces.
A denarius 4.
A nummus 1.
A stater the.
A shekel the 4th.
An assarion the 4th.
A quadrans the 4th.
A silver of 18 keratia.
A nomisma of 18 keratia.
A drachma of 18 keratia.
A holke of 18 keratia.
A gramme of 6 keratia.
An obol of 3 keratia.
A light obol an eightieth of the....
A keration 20 folles.
A thermos of 1 keration.
A chalcous 3 folles.
A miliarision 20.
A bag of 45 keratia.
6 The ounce has 2 staters, 4 shekels, 8 drachmas, 24 grammes, 48 obols, 144 keratia.
7 The aforementioned weights were sometimes stamped of old from gold, sometimes
from silver, and there was a time also from bronze and iron, as the times and
the places had.
