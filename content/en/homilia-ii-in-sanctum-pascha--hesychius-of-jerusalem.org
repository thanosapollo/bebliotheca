Of the same, on the holy Pascha
1 A sacred and royal trumpet has assembled this spiritual theatre for us, a
trumpet which Bethlehem filled and Zion kindled, in which the Cross is a
hammer and the Resurrection an anvil. Whose beauty I do not know how to speak
out, how I shall express the light, how I shall announce the joy in it, how I
shall narrate the kingdom in her. And with what hand shall I handle her?
2 With what words shall I greet a tomb begetting life, a memorial free of
corruption, but a procurer of incorruption, a three-day bridal chamber that
put the bridegroom to sleep, a bride-chamber that raised the bride uncorrupted
after the marriage? A dead man the chest being guarded, and God the earth
being shaken; for the body cries him a dead man, but the wonder a God; the
burial a dead man, the resurrection a God; and the tears of the women a dead
man, and the words of the angels a God. As a dead man Joseph buried him, but
the one buried as a man, this one as God spoiled death. Again as a dead man
the soldiers guarded him, and as God the gatekeepers of Hades having seen him
cowered.
3 And you will say this same one both this and that, not another and another,
nor another in another, nor another through another; for being one, the Word
made flesh gathered these and those into one as he willed by an ineffable
word; and he gave the flesh to minister to the passions, but he has used the
Godhead toward the signs and the wonders, but just as it is not lawful for the
Word to be separated from the flesh, so it is necessary that the sufferings be
interwoven with the wonders. For the one who as a dead man went down into
Hades, this one as God freed the dead, since how do angels minister at a tomb,
and how in white garments as at a bridegroom do they appear to the women, and
how were they saying to them: "You seek Jesus the Nazarene who was crucified;
he is not here; for he was raised as he said. That is, heaven is a place for
him, send the myrrhs there. He was raised, we did not raise him. For your sake
we rolled the stone; for before we came down, the tomb had been emptied. He
was raised, as he himself said."
4 What an angel said, not even a prophet has room to interpret; Hosea speaks
the time of the resurrection, Isaiah knows, but the how he does not
understand. For these are the words of Hosea's prophecy: "Let us go and return
to the Lord our God, because he himself" has struck "and he will heal us; he
will smite and he will bandage us after two days; on the third day we shall
rise and we shall live before him." Hear again what things Isaiah trumpeted:
"Lebanon was put to shame, Sharon became marshes. Galilee will be manifest and
Carmel. Now I will arise, says the Lord, now I will be glorified, now I will
be exalted. Now you will see, now you will be put to shame." For he was
stretching the word toward the Jews: now I will arise when I raise Adam whom
the transgression threw down, now I will be glorified when I establish for the
nations the impassibility of the passions, now I will be exalted raising your
firstfruits into the heavens and lifting up to the seat of the cherubim "the
form of the slave" which I have acquired from you, now you will see the types
ceasing, the truth blooming, now you will be put to shame slandering with
words and being defeated by deeds, because to God is the glory, to the Father
and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of
ages. Amen.
