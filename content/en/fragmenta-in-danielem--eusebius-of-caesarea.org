=24.525= 2. Of Eusebius of Caesarea, the text being set forth: The voice of
the Lord dividing a flame of fire.
The flame of the fire in Babylon was indeed cut through, being divided by
the command of God; it received in it a most pleasant breathing and
refreshment, as in some shade of plants, being furnished to the children in
a peaceful condition; for it became, he says, as a whistling wind of dew.
3. As a Son of man. Of Eusebius of Caesarea. I clearly consider that the
universal judgment is indicated, when all men from of old shall stand
before the judgment-seat of Christ; and after the return to life of those
who have died, and after the judgment upon all, the Son of man seen by
Daniel will stand upon clouds, about to receive the authority over all
peoples and tribes from the Father, even the unaging and unending kingdom;
as also our Savior himself teaches concerning himself, saying: But when the
Son of man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then he will
sit upon the throne of his glory, and all the nations will be gathered
before him, and the rest; these things therefore agree with the testimonies
at hand of the prophecy of Daniel, according to which it has been said: And
behold, with the clouds of heaven he was coming as a Son of man; and, He
came as far as the Ancient of days; and he was brought to him, and to him
was given the dominion and the honor and the kingdom, and the rest.
4. To him was given the dominion. Of Eusebius. The prophet does not only
declare the kingdom of the Son of man, but also more, concerning which he
speaks.
5. And they shall receive the kingdom. Of Eusebius of Caesarea. Reigning
together, that is, they too with God. And who would these be, if not the
heirs of God, and joint-heirs of Christ? to whom also has been promised the
kingdom of the heavens, a kingdom that will be established after four
kingdoms, those seen by the prophet, concerning which we have just now
treated briefly. From this I think the apostle Paul takes his start
=24.528= concerning the second coming of Christ, writing such things: That
the Lord himself, with a command, with the voice of an archangel and with
the trumpet of God, will descend from heaven, and the rest. And the same
apostle also presents the last arrival of the Antichrist, even his
destruction, and upon this the glorious coming of our Savior, in accordance
with the prophecy, saying: Let no one deceive you in any way; for unless
the apostasy come first, and the man of sin be revealed, the son of
perdition, who opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or
object of worship, so as to sit in the temple of God, showing himself that
he is God; do you not remember that, while I was still with you, I told you
these things? and the rest. So much did the wonderful Apostle go through in
the words concerning the consummation of life, confirming the things
prophesied through the prophet Daniel concerning the Antichrist and the
glorious kingdom of our Savior.
6. Anointing shall be utterly destroyed. Of Eusebius. Exposing what is
indiscriminate and lawless in them; and you will say that it has happened
thus also upon the sacrifice and the drink-offering; being performed
rightly and according to law before the passion of our Savior, because of
the power that was still then watching over the holy places; but being
taken away immediately after his perfect and God-befitting sacrifice, which
he offered, himself for our sins; he himself also being the Lamb of God who
takes away the sin of the world as a sacrifice for all men; according to
the new mysteries of the new covenant having been handed down, the things
of the old were taken away; for at the same time as the fulfillment of the
saying that declares, And one week shall confirm a covenant with many, the
things of the old covenant were taken away.
