On the Palms.
=61.715=
Already the gifts of the spiritual festival shine forth in advance; already
the signals of the all-praised feast run ahead; already the messages of the
immaterial Bridegroom run ahead; already Lazarus, having received a pledge of
the universal resurrection, reclines with the Lord; already the myrrh of the
gift makes fragrant those who run toward baptism; already the faithful guards
of the King have taken this great city; already the faithful people, having
run to meet the Lord, cry out saying: Hosanna in the highest, as you have just
now heard; Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, King of Israel;
blessed is he who comes, who always comes, and is with us, and is in no way
outside the faithful. Truly that crowd recognized a royal power and entrance.

For just as in the present, when a worldly king has marched against a tyrant,
and is returning with victory, all his citizens meet him before the city,
bearing flowers, weaving praises, repaying the victory with encomia; and what
leads them to these things is not a love of glory, but a victory hymn and
passion; in the same way also in the case of the Master Christ, as in the case
of a trophy-bearing king, the whole multitude, both from the words and from
the palms, made known that the one who had arrived was a victor, or rather
that the one being recognized was God. For the voices then uttered are fitting
to God alone, and they unveil divine mysteries to those who understand,
Hosanna in the highest; that is, Save now, you who are in the highest; for
thus is Hosanna in the highest interpreted in the Hebrew tongue, as salvation
above, and love of mankind below. O the knowledge of God of a crowd that has
shared in angelic ministry! treading earth, and walking the space of heaven;
wearing a body, and pursuing the things of the bodiless; Jews in name, and
Christians in work; worldly in manner of life, and apostolic in confession;
these too received the revelation from above, as Peter did.

For just as the chief of the apostles said to the Lord not from his own study,
but from a divine revelation, You are the Son of the living God, and the Lord,
showing that he received the revelation not from his own reasonings but from
the Father, blessing him said: Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah, because flesh
and blood did not reveal it to you, but my Father who is in the heavens; in
the same way the crowd also, having received the revelation from above, cried
out to the Lord saying: Hosanna in the highest; blessed is he who comes in the
name of the Lord, King of Israel. And it is altogether reasonable to say, what
and from where is our proof that this Jewish crowd received the revelation
from above. From where then shall I show it to you? From the Lord's own words;
For seeing, he says, the chief priests and the scribes the children crying in
the temple, and saying, Hosanna to the son of David, they were indignant and
said to Jesus: Do you hear what these are saying? But Jesus says to them, Yes;
have you never read, Out of the mouth of infants and sucklings you have
prepared praise? And not from the Lord's words alone, but also from the
paradoxical gathering and meeting of the crowds and from the works they were
doing, not only crying out, but also cutting the branches of the olives and
the palms of the palm-trees, and carrying them in their hands, and spreading
their garments underneath, and addressing him as Savior and King and Lord.
Whence it is plain to everyone, I suppose, that they received the revelation
from above.

For if they had not received it from above, from where did the crowd recognize
the Lord as king? He was not wearing a diadem of a worldly king, nor was he
wrapped in purple, he was not accompanied by a multitude of an army, horses
and chariots and gold-inlaid shields =61.716= were not running before him, he
was not riding on a purple-strewn royal chariot; but he sat upon a small
borrowed colt, bringing only twelve disciples. From where then did the crowd
know that he was a king, if it did not receive the revelation from above?
Therefore they did not seek a worldly diadem upon the Lord; for they knew him
reigning without beginning; they did not seek purple; for they beheld him
putting on light as a garment; they did not look around for a multitude of
soldiers, being persuaded by the prophet who said: Ten thousand times ten
thousand ministered to him, and a thousand thousands of archangels stood
beside him; they did not demand horses and chariots; for they recognized that
he is the one who took Elijah up in a fiery chariot; and knowing the
evangelists following him as horses, being persuaded by the prophet who spoke;
and this Zechariah is the one who said, that Behind him horses white, and red,
and dappled, and ash-colored. White horse Matthew, as having set forth more
clearly the things of the Lord's becoming man; ash-colored horse Mark, who
wrote the Gospel more floridly; dappled horse Luke, as having set forth the
things concerning the Lord more eloquently and variously; red horse John, who
flashed forth the divinity of Christ to the apostles, like a lightning-flash
of fire. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the
Word was God.

And the prophet Habakkuk also named the apostles swift-running horses that
carried the Gospel over all the earth, saying: And you made your horses mount
upon the sea; calling the sea figuratively the whole world. They did not seek
upon the Lord a chariot inlaid with stones, spread with purple, adorned with
royal arms; for they knew the Cherubim and the Seraphim, the six-winged lamps,
being goaded as a chariot, being persuaded by the one who said: You who sit
upon the Cherubim, appear. That faithful Jerusalem crowd, then, having
received the revelation from God, was not scandalized by the lowly colt, but
was strengthened by the Master's presence. Therefore as soon as they saw the
Lord mounted on the colt, immediately pricked by prophetic predictions, taking
up the palms of the palm-trees, the crowds said to one another: Why do we
wait, and not go out to meet the intelligible palm-tree, holding the palms of
the palm-trees? His fruit is full of sweetness and immortality; his leaves are
healings of the inhabited world; his stem, that is, the wood of the cross, is
the salvation of the whole world, and the trophy of the victory against the
devil. We have heard the Church from among us crying out in the Song of Songs:
I will go up upon the palm-tree, I will take hold of its branches. Let us
anticipate the Church from the nations, and let us greet him as an
intelligible palm-tree, and as a mighty king.

While the crowds were saying and doing these things, and praising the Lord,
the chief priests and Pharisees, stung very sharply, were rebuking the crowds
saying: What are you doing, why do you talk vanity, you most rash ones? Do you
come as to God to the son of the carpenter, holding the palms? Do you name as
Lord the one who has nowhere to lay his head? Do you clothe with royal voices
the one sitting on a colt of an ass? Are not his father and his mother among
us? Cease, you most rash ones, or else you will be punished. For we were sent
for this, that we might mark the most rash among you, paying the penalties for
the daring. The crowds, hearing the chief priests saying these things, are not
moved toward sedition, but they answer them, grace being at work: Do you blame
us, Pharisees, and threaten us with punishment as if we were putting forth
blasphemy? Who are worthy of punishment? You who read the Scriptures and do
not understand, or we who =61.717= hear and hold fast? Do we err in offering
palms of the palm-trees to the Lord? Is he not himself the palm-tree of
righteousness? Has not the prophet cried out concerning him, The righteous
shall flourish as a palm-tree? Did he not himself cause the recovery of sight
of the blind to bloom? Did he not grant to the lame an unhindered course? Did
he not make the paralytic carry the bed that had carried him? Did he not raise
Lazarus of the fourth day from death? Is it not for this that you wish to kill
Lazarus again? Did you not roll away the stone, and by unbelief are you
hardened as stones? Did he not permit you to loose the grave-bands, so that
you might not, as in the case of the man born blind, tell a lie that another
had leapt out from another tomb? You always envy the good things, O Pharisees,
and you oppose the believing multitude. For this you again plotted to murder
Lazarus, that you might cut out the faith of the crowd.

For if you murder Lazarus, what then? Can he not raise him again? Let us see
who will grow weary, you in murdering, or he in raising. You murder by deed
and toil, he raises by a word. Who rather grows weary? Is not the word of the
one who works lighter? Do we err, O Pharisees, because we came to the Lord as
to a king? Seeing the colt we were rather strengthened. Therefore we also
recognize a mighty king, and we have the Scripture at our feet, that you may
learn with understanding that it is not we who err, but you who are led
astray. You have the prophet Zechariah; unroll his words; fear his sickle of
vengeance; for from many years ago, evangelizing Judea, he cried out, Rejoice
greatly, daughter of Zion; behold, your king comes to you meek and quiet,
sitting upon a colt of an ass, a son of a beast of burden. Are we erring in
reading the Scripture, and recognizing the king, and doing good to ourselves,
and turning those who doubt? You hold the prophet and you do not wish to
understand the one prophesied? Stand off then, O Pharisees. As much as you
press upon us, so much the more you awaken our longing and our zeal. Therefore
we are not led astray crying, Hosanna in the highest, blessed is he who comes
in the name of the Lord.

We know the name, O Pharisees; this name freed us from Egypt, and made the sea
passable for us, and peopled the desert, and settled us in a land flowing with
milk and honey; it prepared the prophet to cry out: Let them know that your
name is Lord; you only are most high over all the earth. That the crowd is
obedient, but the chief priests disobedient, hear the evangelist: And the
chief priests took counsel, that they might kill Lazarus also, because many of
the Jews were going away on account of him, and were believing in Jesus. Did
you see the madness of the chief priests? In order to cut out the faith of the
multitude, they were seeking to kill Lazarus also, saying to themselves, that
If we kill Jesus only, but leave Lazarus living, we gain nothing; for seeing
this one they believe in that one. So that he may not become a stumbling-block
to us even after death, of some believing in Jesus, let us kill Lazarus, that
we may wipe out his memory altogether. And these and other things the chief
priests were saying, as vain-minded men, not understanding that the Lord,
being about to accept the voluntary death for the immortality of the world,
was not going to leave to the world in memory Lazarus only and his raising,
but also the unconquerable cross; which they will be able neither to sack nor
to hide nor to make void, showing through all the inhabited world the power of
his working.

For what house is there that does not have the sign of the cross, being a
trophy against demons and a safeguard of souls? What ship is not steered by
the cross? what city does not have the cross as a fortress? what king is not
bound about with the cross? what virgin does not have the cross as a staff?
what Church is not walled by the cross? The cross gives light in the night,
and bears the torch in the day; it knows how to call a winter of misfortunes
spring and summer, it binds a yoke together, it increases friendship, a
beginning through all good things. What then does the evangelist say? For it
is good also to feel the head of the whole section. You were hearing just now
the evangelist saying, Six days before the Passover Jesus came to Bethany,
where Lazarus was, whom he raised from the dead. And what is this day six
=61.718= days before? What is this? The present day that characterizes the
forerunner John. For just as John ran before the Lord, so also this day ran
before the resurrection; and just as John, pointing with the finger to the
Lord, was saying: Behold the lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world;
so also this day, pointing with the finger to the coming Lord's day, cried out
saying: Behold the mother of those about to be born again.

Six days before the Passover. Hear the day; for it is not set down simply, Six
days before the Passover; but that you may learn to cleanse yourself from
every defilement of flesh and spirit. For this day is a pre-purification, that
you may cleanse yourself from every stain, that you may loose enmity and stop
anger, that you may quench slander, that you may bind love together, that you
may widen love of the poor, that not you alone may keep the feast, but the one
in need may keep the feast with you. For if you do not share with the one who
has need, and he is downcast, and you keep the feast, see lest there come upon
you the recompense of that rich man, who, making merry splendidly every day,
did not share with Lazarus from the crumbs. Share then the table with the one
who has need, and do good to those who lack, so that you too may keep the
feast continually, and the poor man may feast with you. For if you do not do
good to the poor man of the same faith as you, who has need of consolation,
and he, not having been benefited by you, from necessity goes to a heretic,
and that one consoles him, that one has become more loving of mankind than
you. For every heretic barks against the shepherd. What then does the
evangelist say? Six days before the Passover Jesus came to Bethany, where
Lazarus was, whom he raised from the dead. O the wisdom of the Lord; he raised
Lazarus, that he might provoke the devil. Hear in brief. When the devil saw,
the Lord having cried, Lazarus, come out, and all the underworld things
dissolved, and the powers below not enduring to hold, and that as a royal
command having been sent down, or rather as a divine will working, the hairs
of Lazarus were taking root from above, and the dissolutions of the sinews
were being bound together, and the diminishments of the flesh were being
filled up, and the separation of the bones was being fitted together, and the
streams of the ichors were being drawn in, and the vital spirit was entering
in, and the mistress soul was taking again the former and ancient dwelling;
when he saw these and the like things happening, being thrown into tumult and
becoming in agony he was commanding his powers saying, Array yourselves, be
strengthened, do not betray my kingdom.

O what have I suffered? The power of death is being dissolved, which I
acquired through sin; the one whom I was holding for four days, I now give
back with great force; I have fallen into disaster; I have begun to vomit
those whom I swallowed. I do not weep for Lazarus only, but I fear lest I lose
also the others, whom I hold from Adam until now. I have need of haste, and of
much strength; if I do not anticipate the one who is stripping me by the word,
he will show me empty of all my substance. I have found what I will do, I have
known what I will accomplish; I have the Jews as fellow-runners; I hire Judas;
this one always serves me well; for being a lover of money, when a woman came
forward he priced the myrrh at three hundred denarii, giving her trouble; and
he priced the myrrh at three hundred denarii, but he sold the Master of the
myrrh for thirty denarii. I have need then of much haste to prepare a cross.
He took my Lazarus, but I will drag him down here, and number him with the
dead, that he may learn not to campaign against a greater. But the powers
below to him: Do you want to make the whole Jesus cease here? See that you do
not toil against yourself; you have experience of him; you wrestled him in the
desert; did you not come a third time, and fall three times? And if he comes
down, what are you going to do?

Having counseled these things and the like of them, the devil raised the Jews
to greater envy, and provoked them to put to death the one who raised Lazarus
from the dead, and to prepare a cross for the Lord, that the curse might
become a blessing, and the wood of the cross salvation for all. For through
the resurrection of Lazarus the devil was troubled, and the Pharisees were
confounded, Martha was fully assured, Mary was glorified, the resurrection was
shown forth in advance, the disciples became stronger, and many of the Jews
who believed in our Lord Jesus Christ met him with branches and palms =61.719=
crying out and saying: Hosanna in the highest; blessed is he who comes in the
name of the Lord, king =61.720= of Israel; to him be the glory and the power
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
