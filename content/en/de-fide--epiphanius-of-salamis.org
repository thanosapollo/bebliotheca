* On the Faith
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

From Epiphanius of Cyprus, from the discourse on the faith.
And in order to show that he is "mediator of God and men," having both, from
men the flesh, from the Father the invisible substance, he speaks as God the
divine and God-befitting things, and as man the same one speaks the human and
man-befitting things, having in both the authority naturally and essentially,
unconfusedly and indivisibly, in a divine manner and in a human manner.
