* Homily I on the Holy Pascha
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of the blessed Hesychius, presbyter of Jerusalem, on the holy Pascha
1 Bright is the heaven, illuminated by the chorus of the stars, and all
things are brighter when the morning star rises, but the present condition
of the night is not so much lit by stars as it now rejoices because our God
and Savior has conquered; for he says, "Take courage, I have conquered the
world"; and God having conquered the invisible enemy, reasonably we too
shall carry off the victory against the demons. Let us therefore remain by
the saving Cross, that we may carry away the first firstfruits of the gifts
of Jesus. Let us keep festival this sacred night with sacred
torch-processions, raising a god-inspired strain and singing a heavenly
hymn. "The sun of righteousness," our Lord Jesus Christ, has also lit the
present day for the circuit of the inhabited world; he rose through the
Cross, he saved the faithful.
2 Let no one therefore disbelieve the symbols of the Cross, but let him
worship the blessed and thrice-blessed wood of the Cross, and the symbols
of the Cross of him who opened the door of heaven for us. They no longer
"light a lamp and put it under the bushel," and I mean by bushel the Law,
"but on the lampstand," and by lamp the Word; and the Word was under the
Law and was hidden from the unbelievers as under the bushel; but when he
came upon the Cross and was set upon the lampstand, then he shone upon the
circuit of the inhabited world.
3 See for me, beloved, imperial Rome being honored with the symbols of the
Cross in the midst of the marketplace. See for me Paul composing immortal
letters and inscribing himself a slave of the Cross; he was not ashamed of
the Cross, the scandal of the Jews; he drew the folly of the nations as a
phylactery of wisdom; on the wood of the Cross he raised up the churches of
the inhabited world. One Mosaic rod drove away the plagues of the
Egyptians, and a rod by kinship with the wood of the Cross stops the sins
of men. There Pharaoh is drowned while pursuing Israel, but here the devil
is overthrown, and the worshipers of the Savior are saved. There Adam,
stretching out his hands, drew death upon us, but our Master by the
stretching of his hands saved all things.
4 But O wood more magnificent than heaven and surpassing the heavenly
vaults, O thrice-blessed wood ferrying our souls into heaven, O wood that
procured salvation for the world and drove out the diabolic army, O wood
that hurled the robber into paradise and made him dance with Christ: "Amen,
amen, for I say to you that today you shall be with me in paradise." Let us
imitate the good purpose of the man-slayer, or rather of the Spirit-bearer
because of the faith after these things. For what also does he say?
"Remember me, Lord, in your kingdom." And in one consent of faith he dwells
in paradise and ranges through the heavens. "For amen I say to you," he
says, "today you shall be with me in paradise." Let us too remain by the
Cross of the Savior, saying these same words: "Lord, remember me in your
kingdom," that we too may become partakers of paradise and enjoy the
kingdom of the heavens.
5 The present festival is a victory-celebration, brothers, a
victory-celebration of the all-king Son of God. For today the devil was
defeated because of the Crucified, and our race rejoices because of the
Risen One. For the present day cries out at my rising and says: "In my
course I saw a new spectacle, a tomb being opened and a man

being raised and bones rejoicing and souls glad and men being formed and
heavens being split and powers crying: "Lift up the gates, you rulers of
yours" and what follows. Today I saw the heavenly king ascending, girt
with light, beyond lightning and rays, beyond the sun and springs of
waters, beyond clouds and a spirit of power and eternal life." For this one
was hidden first in a womb of flesh, then in the womb of the earth, in the
one place sanctifying those being born through the conception, in the other
giving life to those who had been put to death through the resurrection;
"for pain and grief and sighing have fled away." "For who has known the
mind of God, or who has become his counselor," except the Word who was made
flesh and nailed to the wood and rose from the dead and was exalted into
the heavens?
6 This day is a gospel of joy; for on this day the Lord rose, raising
together the flock of Adam; for he was born for the sake of man and rose in
the man. Today because of the Risen One paradise has been opened and Adam
is given life and Eve is comforted and the calling is multiplied and a
kingdom is prepared and man is saved and Christ is worshiped; for having
trampled down death and taken the tyrant captive and spoiled Hades, he went
up into the heavens, as a king who has conquered, as a glorious commander,
as an undefeated charioteer, saying to the Father: "Behold I and the
children whom you have given me, O God," and what follows. And he heard
from the Father: "Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies as a
footstool of your feet." To him be the glory, now and unto the ages of
ages. Amen.
