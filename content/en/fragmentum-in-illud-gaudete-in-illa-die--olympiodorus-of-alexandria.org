* Fragment on "Rejoice on that day"
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

=93.780= For as in the body the one who is healthy and well-bodied, even
before luxury, luxuriates in this very thing, having pleasure allotted
together with the health, and neither irregularities of airs, nor drought nor
frost, nor cheapness of table, nor anything else of such things could grieve
him, the health sufficing to beat back the harm from these, so also it is
accustomed to happen upon the soul; for this reason also Paul, suffering
myriads of terrible things, rejoiced saying thus: I rejoice in my sufferings
for you not only in the kingdom of the heavens, which lies as a prize of
virtue, but also in the suffering itself, since this too is a greatest prize,
to suffer something for truth; for this reason also the chorus of the apostles
went up from the council of the Jews, rejoicing, not through the kingdom of
the heavens only, but because they were deemed worthy to be dishonored for the
name of Jesus; for this itself also even by itself is a greatest honor and
crown and prize and a hypothesis of unfading pleasure; not a small contest,
but this of slander is even exceedingly great; wherefore also Solomon, wishing
to present the roughness of the contest, I saw, he says, the slanders that
come to be under the sun, and behold a tear of those slandered, and there was
not one comforting them; and if the contest is great, as then it is great, it
is quite clear that also the crown lying upon this is greater; for this reason
also Christ commands those contending this contest to rejoice and leap, with
the fitting endurance.
