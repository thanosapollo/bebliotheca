according to me? For I wonder, if having heard those things from me, you suspect
me to have other things in the mind, and to have preached other things to those
who so honored me. Gal 5:16 Not by a law that threatens slaves, but by a spirit
that leads children of God. Gal 6:12-13 The law having the sentence that cursed
is he who does not remain in all, he then who does not keep all has not kept.
but 52 they wish you to be circumcised, that they may boast in your flesh, that
is that the nations follow us, and they may not be forced to say that we have
agreed with the nations. those wish this, to look well in flesh and to boast.
and I write with my own hand. Gal 6:15-16 The Christian is not circumcised, nor
is he in the Hellenic uncircumcision; for he has his own circumcision. with Jews
he is not circumcised, since he has a fairer, and with Greeks he is not in
uncircumcision, because he has circumcision. he then who is neither with Greeks
nor with Jews is a new creation. And as many as will walk by this canon, that of
the new creation, as many as neither judaize nor hellenize but christianize,
peace upon them and mercy; mercy unto forgiveness of sins, peace unto a joining
toward God. and upon the Israel of God. take up all those such things; the whole
law is in you, because you fulfill the law of Christ. and who is Israel of God,
as of the once-called Israel no longer being of God?
