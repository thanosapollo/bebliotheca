come to be among us, give my books of preachings to our seventy brothers with
the like mystery of the way of life, that they may also furnish those who wish
to take up the part of the teaching; since if it does not come to be thus, the
word of our truth will be divided into many opinions. And this I know not as
being a prophet, but already seeing the beginning of the evil itself. For some
of those from the nations have rejected the lawful preaching through me, having
accepted a certain lawless and babbling teaching of the enemy man. And these
things while I am still remaining some have attempted by certain various
interpretations to reshape my words into the dissolution of the law, as if I
myself also so think, but not preaching from boldness. Which may it be far. For
such a thing is to act against the law of God spoken through Moses and witnessed
by our Lord concerning its eternal remaining. Since he said thus: "The heaven
and the earth will pass away, one iota or one tittle will not pass away from the
law". And this he has said, "that all things may come to be". And they I do not
know how promising my mind, the words they heard from me, attempt to interpret
more prudently than I who said them, saying to those catechized by them that
this is my thought, which I did not even consider.
And if while I am still remaining they dare to lie such things, how much more

after me will those after me dare to do? Therefore that no such thing come to
be, for this sake I thought it right and begged that the books of my preachings
which I sent you be given to no one neither of the same race nor of another race
before a trial, but if someone having been tested is found worthy, then to hand
them down to him according to the way of Moses, according to which he handed
down to the seventy who had taken his seat, that thus they may keep the faiths
and everywhere hand down the canon of the truth, interpreting all things toward
our tradition, and not themselves being dragged down by ignorance, being drawn
into error by the guesses according to the soul, bring others into the like pit
of destruction. And the things that seemed to me I have well signified to you,
and what seems to you, my lord, accomplish fittingly. Be well.
