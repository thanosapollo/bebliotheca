Of our father among the saints Epiphanius, bishop of the city of Constantia in
Cyprus, concerning the mysteries of numbers. Concerning faith. Αʹ. There is
faith. And faith toward God is a thing impalpable, incomprehensible, unseen,
not to be pried into, unsearchable, honored in silence, and worshipped in the
mind, seat of the soul, foundation of life, living root, unfading branch,
fruit of immortality, substance of things hoped for, proof of things not seen,
justification of all the saints. =43.509= Concerning the Holy Trinity. Βʹ. A
Trinity simple, uncompounded, incomparable, inexpressible, ineffable,
undivided: Father, Son, Holy Spirit; consubstantial in all things, in accord,
in power, in energy, in Godhead, in greatness, divided only in hypostasis and
in names, united in power and in deeds. A Trinity existing before the ages,
without beginning, timeless, unaging, immortal, endless, not beginning, not
ceasing, always remaining the same in the same way, not found now, but always
worshipped, proclaimed by the apostles, magnified by the faithful Church;
almighty, all-surveying, creator, lover of mankind, good, needing nothing,
perfect. That even the number of the Trinity itself, as it is brought forward
in the divine Scriptures, is mysterious and a wonder. Γʹ. Thrice the cherubim
hymn Holy, holy, holy Lord. Three are the great elements: heaven and the
earth, and sea. Three are the things in us: mind, spirit, word. Three fathers,
Adam, Seth, Enoch. Three patriarchs, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob. Three angels by
Abraham's tent. Three days of Abraham's sacrifice. Three measures of fine
flour Sarah took and set before the angels. Three rods Jacob peeled and put
into the troughs of the watering-places. Three decades of years, and then
Joseph was king. Three months Moses was hidden with his parents. Thrice three,
nine, are the plagues of the Egyptians. Three months from Egypt, and then the
law was written, and tables of propitiation. Three goods of the wilderness:
water, quail, the manna. Three times, he says, Moses appeared before the Lord.
Three priests, Moses, Aaron, Samuel. Three wondrous captains, Joshua son of
Nun, Caleb son of Jephunneh, and Phinehas. Three who stood on the rock against
Amalek: Moses, Joshua, Hur. Three mighty judges, Gideon, Jephthah, Samson.
Three elders of the sons of Israel: Moses, Job, Daniel. Three weeks was
Daniel's fast. Three were the youths who did not worship the golden image:
Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego. By three things the earth is shaken, as Solomon
said: if a household slave, he says, become king, and a fool be filled with
food, and a hated woman if she obtain a good husband. Three things there are
that are filled: Hades, a woman's lust, crumbling earth. Three things are
incomprehensible: the tracks of a flying eagle, the way of a serpent on a
rock, the path of a ship sailing the sea. Three things there are that go
prosperously: a lion's cub stronger than the beasts, a cock walking among fe
=43.512= males, living, a he-goat leading the flock. Three wise women, the
Tekoite, the Shunammite, Abigail; for one saved her husband Nabal, another
Absalom by craft, and the other, running to the prophet's son and not
despairing, saved him. Three women prophetesses, timbrel-players: Miriam,
Deborah, Judith. Three holy women: Sarah, Rebecca, Leah. Three kings who are
remembered: David, Hezekiah, Josiah. Three orders of beings: heavenly,
earthly, subterranean. Three virtues, faith, hope, love. Three gifts from God,
kingship, priesthood, prophecy. Three times, of things present,



past, future. Three possessions of the righteous: paradise, Abraham's bosom,
the kingdom of the heavens. Three punishments of sinners: darkness, the worm,
Gehenna. Three renewals of the world: that in the time of Noah, that in the
time of the Lord, that at the consummation. A third time Elijah, having poured
upon the members of the ox and the wood the fire from heaven in streams. Three
times a day Daniel prayed. Three months the Virgin spent in the house of
Zechariah. Three gifts were offered to the Lord by the magi: gold,
frankincense, myrrh. Three baptisms: that of the law, that of John, that of
the Lord. Three are the invocations of baptism, into Father and Son and Holy
Spirit. Three immersions, and three emergences of baptism. Three are the
Lord's victories against the devil: not eating after he had hungered, not
worshipping the unclean one, not casting himself down. Three days after the
baptism, and then the water became choice wine. Three are the Only-begotten's
rebukes toward the Virgin: Why is it that you sought me? Did you not know that
I must be in the things of my Father? What is that to me and to you, woman? My
hour has not yet come; who is my mother, and who are my brothers? Three
disciples trusted more than the others: Peter, James, John. Three years is the
time of the Master's signs. Three years the Lord came seeking fruit on the fig
tree, and did not find it. The Lord cursed three things: the fig tree: Let no
fruit come from you any longer forever; the Synagogue: Stone upon stone shall
not remain; Judas: Woe to that man through whom the Son of man is delivered to
be crucified. Jonah spent three days and three nights in the belly of the
sea-monster. Three days was the sentence against the Ninevites. Three days was
the Savior's death, three days also the Lord's resurrection from the dead by
his own authority. Through three days Paul, having been blinded, saw again;
three times he entreated the Lord concerning the thorn, that it might depart
from him; three times he was shipwrecked; he went up into the third heaven.
Three satas of flour also the woman likened to the kingdom of the heavens
took, and having hidden it in a little leaven, =43.513= she leavened the whole
lump, according to what is written.

{That even the number as a double triad has been observed in the divine
Scripture upon great matters.}
Δʹ. In six days God fashioned the world. On the sixth day he formed Adam. At
the sixth hour he sought him who had been stripped by evident sin. A sixth
time he appeared to Abraham: once at Haran *** a third time in dreams; a
fourth at the addition of the letter of the name; a fifth at Mamre at midday,
the sixth hour; a sixth at the sacrifice of the only-begotten on the mountain.
He commanded Hebrews to serve six years. In the sixth month he sent Gabriel to
the Virgin. In six water-jars he made the water wine. At the sixth hour he
spoke with the Samaritan woman by the well. Six days before the Passover he
raised Lazarus. At the sixth hour, being crucified, he was reconciled to the
robber. A sixth time he appeared after being raised from the dead: to the
women, to Cleopas, to the disciples without Thomas, to the disciples with
Thomas, to Peter and John hauling the net, at the last of the ascension. A
sixth time, as Paul says, he appeared to the brothers: to Cephas, to the ten,
to the five hundred at once, to James, to all the apostles, last of all to
Paul himself. Six great mysteries our Lord Jesus Christ accomplished, as Paul
says: He was fulfilled in flesh, justified in spirit, appeared to angels, was
preached among the nations, was believed on in the world, was taken up in
glory. Through six letters the great names are written: Adonai,



Sabaoth, Jesus, Lord, physician, good, cloud, Mary. That the seven also is a
perfect number. Εʹ. On the seventh day God rested from all his works. Seven
things to be avenged he loosed for Cain, who had killed. For the unclean one,
having worked seven sins, envy, deceit, murder of a brother, first teacher of
murders, having become a defilement of life in this way, having grieved his
parents, lied to God. And he fell under seven punishments: he dwelt in a
cursed land, he worked, looking away he groaned, he trembled, he was away from
sight, he was in agony over the passer-by, he had a sign that made him public.
Seventh is Enoch, after Adam, who was well-pleasing to God, and as wheat from
tares was separated from the ungodly. Seven years first, and seven years
second, Jacob served Laban for Rachel. Seven lambs of sheep Abraham gave to
Abimelech as a witness of the ownership of the well. Seven cows stile of
famine, and seven ears pinakes described plenty, Pharaoh's dream. Joshua the
son of Nun carried seven sacred trumpets, when he brought down the walls of
Jericho. In the seventh year of bondage the Jews re =43.516= lease the
Hebrews. From the fifteenth until the first of the twentieth, in which the
Passover of the Jews was celebrated, seven days of the feast. God listed seven
nations enslaved to the Jews: of the Canaanites, of the Hittites, and
Perizzites, of the Girgashites, of the Hivites, of the Jebusites, of the
Abraeans. Seven of the tent Moses' sister spent, having been set apart six
days. Samson was bound with seven vine-shoots, and he snapped them as a cord.
He had seven sacred locks of hair; whose cutting drove away their strength and
power. There are seven eyes that look upon all the earth. Seven pipes, and
seven lamps Zechariah had seen, because there are seven spirits from God.
Seven are the gifts of the Holy Spirit: spirit of wisdom, spirit of
understanding, spirit of counsel, spirit of might, spirit of knowledge, spirit
of piety, spirit of the fear of God. Wisdom supported seven pillars of her own
tent. Seven women in Isaiah will importune the one lord, saying: We will eat
our own bread, and we will put on our own garments. Only let your name be
called upon us; take away our reproach. For seven legal books bear witness to
Christ, that he himself, having become incarnate, took away their reproach:
Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges. For if he
had not become man, they would have been written off as mythical. Having been
sacrificed without being sacrificed, as Isaac, and having suffered impassibly,
and having borne his own cross upon his shoulders, he confirmed the things
lying in the book of Genesis. Having been contained in a virginal womb, and
not having consumed her who bore him with the fire of the Godhead, he
portrayed the bush that burned and was not consumed, which lies in the book of
Exodus. Having served as high priest, as Aaron, having mediated the things
toward God for us, he set forth the things written in Leviticus. Having joined
the New Covenant to the Old, and saying: It was said to the ancients, You
shall not commit adultery; but I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman
to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart; and
again: It was said to the ancients, An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a
tooth; but I say to you, not to resist the evil one, but whoever strikes you
on the right cheek, turn to him the other also; he handed over the things in
Deuteronomy a second time, and taking up again the things that are profitable.
Having led our race into the true rest, and having prepared for us various
mansions in the heavenly places, he revealed the things in the book of
Numbers. Having entered the Jordan, and having sanctified the waters, and
having called back the sun that fled at the cross, and having made a double
day in place of a single, he portrayed the things in the book of Joshua son of
Nun for those who do not read carelessly. Having laid down his beloved so
=43.517= ul for us, and he will give himself a ransom for many, he interpreted
the things in the book of Judges; for Jephthah too offered his daughter as
whole-burnt offerings for the people



to God. Jesse was father of seven children. Moses is seventh from Abraham.
Through seven letters is the name of John. Through seven letters also is the
appellation of Christ. Seven deacons were chosen by the disciples for the
ministry of the Christians: Stephen, Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon,
Parmenas, Nicolas. Seven youths brought down the tyranny of Antiochus. And to
speak briefly, this number is praiseworthy and wonderful; because the Master
himself from above cries in writing: Honor the Sabbath day, and sanctify it;
because on it God rested from all his works which he began to put away. To him
be the glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
