* Paraenetic Alphabet
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Hold God to be the beginning of all things.
Think nothing in life to be firm.
Honoring parents, fear God most of all.
Teach yourself not to speak what is not lawful.
Hasten to please God by works and by words.
Desire the life that has no end.
By yielding yourself to friends you will be conquering.
Being mortal, do not think great things at all.
Track always the doors of the wise.
And adorn the mind more than the form.
Open your mouth, child, to the word of God.
Never forget his memory at any time.
Pray to God soberly each day.
Entertain strangers, lest you become a stranger to God.
Bridle the soul-destroying impulses of the passions.
Secure the body with fetters soberly.
Bear conscience as a rod for yourself.
Clearly give leisure in the God-inspired Scriptures.
Comfort the griefs of the poor.
Wish well for neighbors above yourself.
Be eager to have friends rather than much wealth.
For they are far more glorious than gold.
Hate falsehood, and love the truth.
O child, keeping these things you are saved in a godly way.
