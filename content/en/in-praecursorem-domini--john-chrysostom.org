On the Forerunner.
=59.489=
The most flowery meadows, embroidered with golden-haired flowers, adorned
with sweet-breathing trees, stirred by sweet-voiced birds, both charm the
hearing of men and warm the sight. For as the sun flashes round about its
golden rays, the reflected gleams flash back fire-bright radiances. There
the lisping voices of talking birds send forth a shrill song with pipe-like
cries; there the zephyrs of well-sounding winds, in the rival circlings of
the birds, stirring the valleys of the cliffs, sing in unison in the image
of a lyre; so that even the shepherds and cattle-herds dwelling in the
huts, having the lyric songs of the musical birds still ringing in their
ears, warbling imitations on many-toned reeds, gently lead the lambs over
every pleasant place and every herb.
But there a prize-bearing spring gushes streams, while here a
honey-dropping teaching of the wisdom of God shines; there the shrill notes
of musical birds, here the melodies of holy psalms; there the
wilderness-loving turtledove, walking among the trees, signals the
fair-colored spring with mournful songs, here John, who was fed on locusts
and honey, having stirred the Churches with wilderness proclamations, or
rather spiritual ones, trumpets the heavenly spring that has appeared to
the world. For in those days, it says, John the Baptist comes =59.490=
preaching in the wilderness, and saying: Repent; for the kingdom of heaven
has drawn near. In those days. In the manifestations of Jesus the God, in
the sojournings of the heavenly King, in the shinings-forth of the
everlasting light, In those days John the Baptist comes preaching in the
wilderness. Not in the inhabited world; because the condition of men was a
wilderness, not yet inhabited by Father and Son and Holy Spirit. And what
did he preach? Repent; for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near. O
repentance, harvesting life in the very tears, and arraying herself in the
bridal light in the bridal veil! O repentance, accuser of the devil,
advocate of those who turn back! And John had his garment of camel's hair,
and a leather girdle about his loins. Let us look around, beloved, at the
garment of John; perhaps that garment of his may become for us an enigma of
salvation. That has the image of the peoples turning back from the nations.
He had, it says, the garment of camel's hair. Scripture often likens the
devil to a camel, because of the great bulk and the much-twistedness and
the heavy wrath. He who saw the watch in Isaiah says: I saw a rider of an
ass, and a rider of a camel. Rider of an ass he calls Christ, since seated
on an ass he entered =59.491= into Jerusalem; rider of a camel, the
antichrist who at the last of the days, by the working of the devil, visits
the world. And John had his garment of camel's hair. For already, by the
preaching of piety, having turned to himself the Gentiles who covered like
hairs, and having stripped that one, and having thickened the Church,
having woven the body of the Church in the unity of the faith, he wrought
for himself a wrapping of salvation. And he had a leather girdle about his
loins. Scripture mentions loins and a leather girdle, that it may signify
that the generative passion has been mortified for chastity through the
girdle lying about from without, of dead skin. For that of the inwardly
hidden


virginity was an unlying accuser. He ate, it says, locusts, and wild honey.
The Jews leaping away from the law and the commandments he means, as
Scripture says: Your mixed people leaped out like a locust, or like a
locust on a hedge on a day of frost. The sun rose; and it leaped away, and
it knew its place no more. And wild honey, because the Jews made wild the
sweetness of the law by the transgression of the commandments. Then Jesus
comes to him, it says, to the Jordan, to be baptized by him. But he was
hindering him, saying: I have need to be baptized by you, and do you come
to me? And Jesus answering said to him: Suffer it now; you must now become
a witness of the voice sent upon me; for thus it is fitting to fulfill all
righteousness.
I must teach, that Unless one is born again of water and Spirit, he cannot
enter into the kingdom of heaven. I must therefore first do, and then
teach, that the Jews may not have a pretext: You who teach others,
=59.492= do you not teach yourself? Suffer it now; for the word of the
stout-souled Job must stand, that, No one is clean from filth, not even if
his life be one day; I must wipe away the old sin of the body; I must renew
the soul by the Spirit, and the body by water. Suffer it now; I must open
the womb of water and Spirit, that I may become firstborn among many
brethren. For from this womb the prophet convicts the Jews who have slipped
away, accusing: The sinners were alienated from the womb. Then he suffers
him. And when Jesus had gone down in the Jordan, it was possible to see,
beloved, a great wonder, a fountain bathing in the river, and a river being
baptized in the river. For Christ is a fountain, as he himself says: They
have forsaken me, a fountain of living water. And the bride, the Church, to
him: A sealed fountain, she who sends forth. The same is a river, as the
prophet says: The river of God was filled with waters.
He came down upon the waters, and they were sanctified, and he healed them.
If anyone of you is uninitiated, beloved, in baptism, let him come and
behold how the Father cries from heaven: This is my beloved Son, in whom I
am well pleased; hear him. Jordan cries, the Spirit cleanses the air, the
Father calls the Son, the Son cleanses heaven, the Spirit beautifies the
earth, brightens the air. Now is fulfilled what was said of old by the
prophet: Day unto day utters speech. Day is the Father, day is the Son. The
Father utters speech to the Son, as Paul says: Take the sword of the
Spirit, which is the word of God; by which word he will present us all in
Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom be the glory and the power, unto the ages of
ages. Amen.
