* Encomium on Saint Polycarp

An encomium of Chrysostom on the holy hieromartyr Polycarp. Bless.
Let it rejoice also today, beloved, or rather let it rejoice through the age,
the priestly dignity in Christ of the shepherds of the church everywhere. This
Paul, blessing the bishops of the Philippians together with presbyters and
deacons, was saying, Rejoice in the Lord always; again I will say, rejoice.
Let the crown also of every ecclesiastical seat rejoice with the crowns of the
martyrs. About XX lines, few letters are readable. or rather of God himself
through Isaiah saying, Behold, I lay in Zion a precious stone, elect, a
cornerstone, and he who believes on him shall not be put to shame. IX lines
have vanished. they built the heavenly Zion, whose craftsman and maker is God
according to Paul. He openly praises them in the Holy Spirit. Upon this again
Paul bears witness, saying, You are a temple of God, and his Spirit dwells in
you.
Upon this stone also the blessed Polycarp today built himself, obeying the
same one who says, You are a building of God, having been built upon the
foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the
cornerstone. Having built himself upon these holy ones he received the crown
of incorruption, having run not uncertainly, but having bruised the body and
brought it into slavery. This man left for those coming after him unto
martyrdom a most beautiful memorial through the age; for the righteous remains
eternal. And the whole company of the festival, being pleased, wishes to learn
how he did not fear the vain and wicked point of the devil, with very great
exactness.
He entered into the stadium. Having entered he was being baited by the
proconsul with deceitful words; and when he had confessed, the accusation
destroyed the former one. Then the crowds with haste gathering from the
workshops wood and kindling, and especially Jews ... VIII lines have vanished
of the Spirit. And Stephen one line vanished speaking to them: Which, he says,
of the prophets did your fathers not kill? those who announced beforehand
concerning the coming of Christ. XIX lines have vanished. ... of the fire with
instruments the martyr, they wanted to nail the one who had shattered the
stings of the devil through the confession of Christ. But the blessed Polycarp
said, Leave me thus; for he who grants the endurance will deem me worthy to
keep the body undisturbed apart from the nails. Nevertheless they did not nail
him, but they bound the righteous one. Then as having put his hands behind,
having been bound, praying the Amen that is effective forever, thus he
presented himself as a whole burnt offering to God.
And then when the pyre had been kindled a magical wonder happened to those who
saw. For the fire having been raised to a height was being peaked, making the
form of a vault, and having become bronze-like it encircled the body of the
martyr; it did not separate this from waters, but showed it most brilliant.
Let Isaiah the prophet also now say to the martyr who has toiled for Christ,

breast, once, one man. When that one had thrust, his holy body was wounded, in
