Epictetean Gnomologium (from Stobaeus books 1-2) edited by Heinrich Schenkl
#+END_QUOTE

* 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of Epictetus. Honor the gods with a just and measured disposition, but do not
flatter with unmeasured gifts; for neither does a god rejoice in flattery nor
does the flatterer honor the divine.

Sacrifice to gods not when you have become rich, but when you have become
prudent, for to be rich is also of the ill-fortuned, and to be prudent is only
proper of the well-fortuned.

* 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

Do not ask from gods a continuous health of body, but take continuously
temperance of soul; and you will neither easily be ill nor being ill be
displeased.

* 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

Do not ask from gods the things you desire, but seek from gods how you may be
delivered from the desire itself. Then the gods hear you, whenever you pray not
concerning the pleasant, but concerning the fair. And then they will give you
the fair things, whenever you rejoice not in pleasure, but in virtue.

* 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

Remember to ask the great things from the great; for they would not give small
things. Nothing is greater and higher than God; praying therefore to gods ask
the divine things, of which no fleshly and earthy passion touches.

* 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

As you do not hate a bee because of the sting, but care for it because of the
fruit, so also do not turn away from a friend because of a rebuke, but love
because of the goodwill.

* 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

Flatterers are like drones; for they are idle and without stings and consuming
the toils of others. The envious like wasps; for they are striking and without
resource and without sharing and useless. The useful like bees; for they are
industrious and self-working and economical and well-resourced and gentle and
rebuking and communal. So that, if you wish being a friend to live with many
friends, imitate the bee; let your hive be clean of drones and wasps.

* 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

Test those who let themselves down into friendship with you not from the things
they grant you, but from the things they remember and grant and promised and
disposed for themselves.

* 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

Not moneys are friends, but the friend is moneys. And moneys would never work a
friendship, as neither would earth a god; but friendship would acquire moneys
even otherwise, as a god earth.
