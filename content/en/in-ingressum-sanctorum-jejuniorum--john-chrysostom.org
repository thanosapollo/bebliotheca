On the entrance of the holy fasts.
=62.727=
The bright spring of the holy fasts has risen upon us today, beloved,
and it eagerly prepares us to run together into this God-befitting
paradise, so as to gather the fruits of self-control, and having
plucked the delightful flowers of chastity, to weave the crown of
temperance. Again the angelic life calls us to the ancient delight, and
it opens for us the gates toward that divine and blessed enjoyment.
Voluntary fasting grants great boldness toward God, cleansing the
bodies through self-control, and sanctifying the soul through boldness
toward God, and making us forget all earthly things, and preparing us
to desire the heavenly.
Fasting, leading its lovers up into the heavens, makes them stand
beside Christ, and have communion with the saints. The father of
fasting, beloved, is the law of God; and its mother is self-control,
endurance, virtue; its daughters are ascesis, chastity, temperance; and
its genuine sons are Moses, Elijah, Daniel, John the Baptist, and the
rest of the choir of the saints, especially those who withdrew into the
deserts and the ascetics; and its friends are faith, hope, and love;
and its companions are peace, gentleness, concord; and its handmaidens
are doxology, continual psalmody, pure prayer; and its consort is the
fear of God; and the fruits of their cohabitation are holiness,
incorruption, boldness toward God, the heavenly kingdom, eternal life.
Therefore, beloved, those who are lovers of fasting, having the law of
God in their heart, fast sincerely, but the lawless do not fast. And
the righteous fast continually, living piously, and studying and
fulfilling the law of God, both loving =62.728= chastity, and embracing
temperance, being set free from drunkenness, and clinging to Christ;
but the lawless not only do not fast, but are even filled with every
wickedness and satanic activity; all prodigality and impiety and
lawlessness traffic among them; among them are shameful words,
unrestrained laughter, and diabolical gestures; among them gluttony and
drunkenness and disorder shall be crushed.
For truly many are the evils that come from drunkenness. For those who
have been made drunk with wine have their minds spinning in darkness,
like those who are deranged and beside themselves, and carried about
here and there, dashed from wall to wall, and unseemly colliding with
those they meet, and borne shamefully to the ground. Therefore,
beloved, let us flee such shame, and let us eagerly fast with the fear
of God, being confirmed by faith, strengthened by the law, adorned with
temperance, beautified by chastity, chastening the eye, sanctifying the
thought, praying with much endurance and a pure heart, putting away the
temporal, and acquiring the eternal in exchange. For if anyone fasts
sincerely, he imitates Christ, having come to be on earth he is made
like the angels, he emulates the prophets, he shares with the
apostles, and having wholly exchanged his nature through fasting he
becomes righteous from unrighteous, good from wicked, pious from
impious, and thus he inherits Christ, the treasure of life; of which
may it be granted that we all attain, by the grace and love of mankind
of our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory and the power together
with the unoriginate Father and the life-giving Spirit, now and always,
and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
