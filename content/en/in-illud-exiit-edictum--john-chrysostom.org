* On that saying, There went out a decree
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

HOMILY ON THE TEXT, "There went out a decree from Caesar Augustus;" and on the
enrollment of the holy Theotokos.

=50.795=

αʹ. Beloved, when our common Saviour Jesus Christ was about to be born, a
universal decree goes out from Caesar Augustus into all the inhabited world.
For since a universal grace was about to shine forth upon all the earth, the
public affairs are in a certain manner joined to piety; and one command comes
to pass throughout the whole inhabited world, as the Saviour is being born.
There went out then a decree from Caesar Augustus, so that all the inhabited
world should be enrolled, for a preparation of the harmony of piety, for a
passage of the preaching, for a road-making of the teaching. For formerly the
local jurisdictions happened to be many, since there was no decree of Caesar
of the universal preaching; but when the faith of piety was about to be spread
by a preaching throughout the whole inhabited world, the inhabited world is
joined under one signal; and not only the inhabited world, but also the things
below with the things above, and piety is published in a manner profitable to
men, so that the truth according to God might escape no one. Caesar Augustus
commands, and Joseph obeys, and takes the virgin. Whenever you ask a Greek or
even the Jew himself, why Augustus has been honored with a long time of the
kingdom (for he reigned fifty-six years and four months); they answer nothing,
but they suppose that the longest time came to him by lot, not knowing the
causes of the things, nor believing the grace of God.

For in his time Jesus the Christ was born. If then Augustus is also first king
of the Romans, it was fitting that Christ received the economy according to
the flesh at the founding of the Romans' kingdom. For the iron rod was about
to be brought in, the rod concerning which David said: You shall shepherd them
with a rod of iron; and, Ask of me, and I will give you nations as your
inheritance, and as your possession the ends of the earth; as if the Father
were conversing with the Son and saying: The Jews declined your kingdom, and
they dishonored the =50.796= rule; the nation is easy to despise, few are
those who turned away; Ask of me, and I will give you nations as your
inheritance, and as your possession the ends of the earth, not the corner of
Palestine, nor the circumcised nation, the vain and heartless in soul; but, I
will give you nations as your inheritance, and as your possession the ends of
the earth. And you shall shepherd them since they were not willing to obey the
Gospel; since they were not willing to yield to the miracles, you shall
shepherd them, not with a word, but with a deed and a rod of iron, the rule of
the Romans; for this reason they are subject to the rule of the rod. For when
Jesus came into Jerusalem, and saw in the temple trading and shameful-gain
business; for they were selling inside, and buying, trafficking within on the
pretext of the sacrifices; having then made a rod and a whip of cords, he cast
them out of the temple, saying: Do not make my Father's house a house of
merchandise.

And if they had endured that rod, they would not have been shepherded with a
rod of iron; but since they dishonored the saving rod, they have come to be
not under the whip, nor under the word, which scourges those of good sense,
but under the iron rod; and often they wished to stir, and the iron rod
overtook them, not simply striking, but destroying. When then the iron rod
began to prevail in the time of Augustus, then also Christ was born. Beginning
of the Romans, beginning of the preaching, the fourth kingdom of the times.
For one may see these things stored up mystically among the prophets; for
nothing unwritten lies either in the New or in the Old; but the Old
anticipated the New, and the New interpreted the Old. And I have often
said that two Covenants, and two maidservants, and two sisters, escort the one
Master. The Lord is announced among prophets; Christ is preached in the New;
the new things are not new; for the old anticipated; the old things have not
been quenched; for they were interpreted in the New. For you might see that
Daniel, younger in age, but grey in prophecy, prophesying in Babylon =50.797=.

Why in Babylon? For the people had been taken captive into Babylon, and had
spent seventy years in the land of the Assyrians. There then Daniel is born,
there he is reared, harmed in nothing from the foreign land. For he had the
ancestral mind; and from youth he becomes a prophet, and the Holy Spirit
seized his soul, and becoming eighteen years old, he began the prophecy. And
there are many things which he prophesies, and he also interprets four
kingdoms. For Nebuchadnezzar the king of the Assyrians saw in an image four
differences, one body, but four colors. For the image had the head of gold,
and had the breast of silver, and had the belly of bronze, and had the feet of
iron, and the image was composed of four materials. And the king saw; but
there was no one interpreting. The magi came, the Chaldeans came, the
soothsayers were put forward, those who always deceive the king were called;
and what do they say? Tell us the dream, and we interpret. God then,
arranging, so that they might not deceive him in the usual way, takes away
from the king's memory the image of the dream; and the king was saying: Tell
me my own dream. And when those were clearly confessing that they were unable
to narrate another's dream, but only to interpret if they should hear it,
Daniel is summoned, it having been made known that he would be able for these
things, and he goes in to king Nebuchadnezzar, having through the Holy Spirit
the revelation of the dream, and he says to the king: O king, you saw such and
such things. And together with the prophet's words the hearer recovered the
memory of the dream; and the hearing became a recollection of the vision.

The prophet then interprets it; for the interpreter was now trustworthy after
the announcement of the dream itself, and he says: The image which you saw is
four kingdoms; for four kingdoms shall arise upon the earth. That image,
however, was complete; and one body stood. And you saw, O king, that a stone
was cut without hands, and it crushed the image, and the stone occupied and
filled all the earth. This is what you saw: you saw four kingdoms: You are the
head of gold. For first the Assyrians reigned, exceedingly rich and most
splendid and most luxurious. And he says to him: You are the head of gold; and
after you shall arise a kingdom inferior to you.

For after the Assyrians the Persians and Medes reign; and these too have
become most brilliant. A third kingdom shall arise, the bronze one, the
kingdom of the Greeks and Macedonians; where Alexander being in Greece, having
yoked, overran Persia, and destroyed Darius, and brought the rule under his
own authority. It was compared then to bronze, because of the well-sounding of
the speech and of the tongue. For the Greeks with Alexander, and Alexander
himself, being eloquent in speech, were compared to bronze; for gold is
splendid, yet not well-sounding; and likewise also silver; but bronze is
lesser in material, yet well-sounding according to the sound. He says then: A
third kingdom, that signified through the bronze; and a fourth kingdom, the
last, of iron. You shall shepherd them then with a rod of iron. See the
agreement of the prophecy; he saw, however, that the stone was cut from a
mountain without hands. How was it cut? Not with iron, but without hands; for
the Virgin gives birth of herself; and the stone struck the image, and became
a great mountain and filled all the inhabited world. One stone fills all the
earth; That stone was cut without hands, and became a great mountain, and
filled all the inhabited world.

βʹ. What stone is this? Hear Isaiah saying: Behold I lay in Sion a stone of
stumbling, and a rock of offense; and everyone who believes in him shall not
be put to shame. And not Isaiah alone says this stone, but David also
prophesies: The stone which the builders rejected, this has become the head of
the corner. This has come about from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our
eyes. =50.798= What corner has come about? Every corner joins two walls to
itself; and the stone joined New and Old, two peoples from the nations and
from Jews; therefore This has come about from the Lord, and it is marvelous in
our eyes; not in the eyes of all, but in the eyes of us who believe. When then
you see the Church, through the one stone, held from Jews and from Greeks,
understand especially what is said. But someone would altogether say: And
where among us are those from Jews? for from Greeks we see. but those from
Jews, where are they? From where was Paul? from where was Peter? and from
where Andrew? and from where John? and from where the Church of ten thousand
men in Jerusalem? First from Jews, then from Greeks
.For when those among them who had the hope of piety believed, then the iron
rod came, and scattered
those who remained in unbelief. For you shall shepherd them with a rod of
iron; as a potter's vessel you shall crush them. Why, As a potter's vessel? If
the clay falls while still wet, it is taken up again; but if it becomes a
vessel, and once has converse with fire, and becomes a most dry shard,
whenever it is crushed, it no longer admits restoration. You shall shepherd
them with a rod of iron; as potter's vessels you shall crush them. In the iron
kingdom, however, Christ is born; a stone was cut without hands. See the
things in agreement, and the words alike; and it struck the image, and made
the kingdoms upon earth to cease; and it became a great mountain, and filled
all the earth. This is the dream king Nebuchadnezzar had seen; and the dream
was filled, so that he might not narrate it to the magi, and be deceived by
them in the usual way, having heard other things instead of other things.
After a little time then Daniel himself sees the dream in another manner; and
it was
not a dream, but a revelation of a vision; he seemed to be by the sea; and
Daniel says: Four great beasts were coming up from the sea. And quietly and
little by little let us follow the type of the prophecy; There were coming up,
he says, four great beasts from the sea, differing from one another, and, he
says, very great difference among them. The first, as a lioness; and her wings
as of an eagle. You have gold there, here a lioness; why a lioness here and
gold there? There gold because of the luxurious and rich; here a lioness
because of the female and womanish, yet a beast. The first, as a lioness. He
saw a second beast as a bear, and it stood on one side; there silver, here a
bear, because of the heavy. Third, he says, I saw a beast as a leopard, a most
swift beast. Since Alexander the king of the Macedonians came upon the East
most swiftly, he was compared to a leopard. There bronze because of the
well-sounding of the Greek tongue, here a leopard because of the most swift
onset. And the fourth he names a beast, but he does not say a name, but he
says fearful; exceedingly most fearful; its teeth large; eating exceedingly,
and grinding fine, and treading down the remainder with its feet; and it also
differed exceedingly beyond all the beasts before it; and the beast was
fearful; and he did not say its name. For since the place of the prophecy was
about to become clear, bringing it forward upon the Romans, so that the
kingdom might not be insulted, he leaves it an unnamed beast.

When however, he says, I saw the fourth beast, I feared and trembled. For it
was exceedingly most fearful; it was treading down all, crushing all,
devouring all; it was master of all, wherever it went, it subjected. And what
does he say? Suddenly I saw a river of fire; a throne was set, the Ancient of
days sat, a thousand thousands were ministering to him, and ten thousand times
ten thousand stood by him, the Ancient =50.799= of days. Books were opened,
and behold upon the clouds of heaven one as a Son of man coming, and he came
as far as the Ancient of days. And what happened immediately? No longer the
beasts, no longer the sea, no longer the fear of the beast; but he received
the rule, and he himself was master; His kingdom is an eternal kingdom, which
shall not be dissolved; for his rule also is unto generation of generations.
Thus far, he says, the end of the word; and the prophet thus sees the four
kingdoms; for it was fitting for him since he kept away from the affairs of
life; but the king, as it suited him, sees four kingdoms as in gold and silver
and bronze and iron. For if the king had seen the kingdoms as beasts, then
Daniel had interpreted to him, it would have been out of place to say to him:
You are the first beast; for it would have seemed to insult the hearer; but a
king sees as a king, and a prophet sees similar things as in another type.
These things have been said to us, beloved, not in vain, nor having taken up
the old history at random, but so that you may be fully assured that the
kingdom of Augustus was glorified, was honored, was praised, was long-lived,
since in his time Christ was born, and the man was so honored, that even his
name became a title of the dignity. For you must know that he reigned still
fifty-six; and when he had completed forty-two years, Christ is born; the
fifteen years then of his reigning, Christ is reared. And Tiberius succeeds
Augustus, and in the eighteenth year of Tiberius reigning, he comes to the
cross, and suffers, and is raised, and is taken up.

So that there are of Augustus fifteen years, and of Tiberius eighteen; and
Jesus, having become thirty years old while Tiberius Caesar was reigning,
revealed his own godhead upon the disciples, and upon these he preached three
years; for everywhere the Trinity is precious; therefore having preached three
years, in the eighteenth year of Tiberius he meets the passion, having
completed the economy of the flesh.

γʹ. It came to pass then in those days, that we may take up the reading, there
went out a decree from Caesar Augustus that all the inhabited world should be
enrolled. This enrollment is a clarity of things, a first laying-bare of the
truth, so that the race of men might not wander; this is the first enrollment.
Has it then been passed over in silence even by the prophets themselves? Let
us run back to the Old, let us examine the Jewish things and the things of the
unbelief of the Jews. I call David to witness, a trustworthy man, honorable to
Jews, as far as the bare memory, but faithful to the Church. What then does
David say? The Lord shall recount in the writing of peoples and of these
rulers. These are the words of the prophet, these the things of the Gospel.
This is the first enrollment; and Joseph also, he says, went up from Galilee
from the city of Nazareth into Judaea into the city of David, which is called
Bethlehem, because he was of the house and lineage of David, so as to be
enrolled with Mary his betrothed wife, being with child. For the conception
came to pass upon the betrothals alone. And while they were there, the days
were fulfilled for her to give birth, and she brought forth her firstborn son,
and wrapped him in swaddling-clothes, and laid him in the manger, because
there was no place for them in the inn.

Nothing is cheaper than this affair, nothing poorer than the birth of Jesus.
Jesus became poor for us, being rich; Mary laid the Saviour in a manger; there
was nothing under, nothing over; for neither did the Saviour have need of such
attendance, nor did he need =50.800= the care from without, just as he is not
conceived by a law of nature. For the use of the Virgin was sufficient; and he
himself was the one arranging all things. Thus, he says, she laid him in a
manger, and there were shepherds in the same country lodging in the fields.
Matthew narrates that magi arrived; and Luke, that there were also shepherds
in the country lodging in the fields; there magi, here shepherds. And an angel
came to them crying great things; I bring you good news of a great joy today,
that a Saviour has been born to the world in the city of David; and while the
one angel was speaking, the heavens were opened, and one symphony was seen of
those hymning and saying: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace,
among men good will.

For all things are about to be joined, heaven and earth to come into unity, so
that the things below may become above, so that there may be peace to those
far off and to those near. Shepherds then are evangelized; why shepherds?
Since we had gone astray after the manner of sheep, there came to the world a
shepherd, Christ Jesus, the Saviour of the inhabited world, and shepherds are
evangelized because of the shepherd, and Glory to God in the highest. For men
were about to glorify, and to give thanks to the one who sent peace upon
earth; for men were being delivered from the enmity toward God. Among men good
will; for the things toward piety were about to seem good to men. The
shepherds then, having been evangelized and having heard the word, saw the
things; they came to the cave; they did not see a bed, they did not see a
couch, they did not see a preparation of a table, but Mary sitting and Joseph,
and the child lying. The evangelist then does not say father first, but he
says Mary first; for she is mother truly; or rather not even she is mother,
since she did not even give birth naturally, but conceived supernaturally. For
she did not know what was coming to pass, but she was a tool of the craftsman.
They saw, however, Mary and Joseph and the child; having heard the word they
saw the things, they wondered, they were saved. The infant however was lying,
submitting to the still incomplete construction of the body, and bearing the
increase of it; and as a craftsman he was awaiting and constructing the tool,
so that as the kithara-player may use the kithara, and as the archer may use
the bow and the arrow as needed; so also Jesus, sharing with all, grows little
by little. Why then not all at once? why did he not arrive complete at once?
He came to imitate our nature, so that he might sanctify the lump through the
firstfruits. If however he had sojourned complete according to age, the
heretics were about to allege: A virgin did not bear him, he did not take the
lump from our race. He was born then of a virgin, taking from the common lump,
so that he might sanctify Adam his own formation; and for this reason he grows
little by little, and he associates with men as a man, but being Lord of
nature, he lived without sin.

When then you hear him a child, take up that prophecy of Isaiah, which he was
saying: A child has been born to us, whose rule has been born upon his
shoulder; Mighty God, ruler of peace, father of the age to come. When you hear
child, do not despise the name, but wonder at the loving-kindness of God, and
wonder at the salvation of the economy, so that you may become worthy to hear
the evangelical words, following through faith, being trained through
endurance, contending through reverence, being perfected through love,
becoming complete through all things in Christ Jesus our Lord; with whom to
the Father together with the Holy Spirit be glory, might, honor and worship
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
