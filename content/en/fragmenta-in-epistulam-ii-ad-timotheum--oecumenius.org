* Fragments on the Second Epistle to Timothy
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

2 Tim 1:18 Or that it belongs to one distinguishing to speak thus of the holy
hypostases, as there: the Lord rained from the Lord fire and brimstone. 2 Tim
2:2 For also, as I think, he commands these things to him concerning bishops
or presbyters about to be ordained by him; for he would not have written such
things concerning laymen. For what would it have been, if he did not find
someone both faithful and able to teach, would he not catechize into the
faith, Paul himself preaching the word without observation both to murderous
Jews and to Greeks. through many witnesses, that is of law and prophets; for
the apostle made these witnesses of his own preaching. Thus Clement in the
seventh of the Hypotyposes. 2 Tim 3:9 The newly-minded; for going on he said:
deceiving and being deceived. Therefore their strength is among those easily
deceived. 2 Tim 3:11 I think that by a certain belittling he mentioned Lystra
last, as if he said: yes, in the populous cities I suffered what I suffered.
What also in Lystra? From where were found the men harming me in Lystra? 2 Tim
4:6 Or understand I am being poured out, as I dissolve the war toward all,
neither warring nor being warred upon; for I am being perfected. He calls
death a loosing, either because of loosing from the earth from which we were
taken, or toward Christ who created.
