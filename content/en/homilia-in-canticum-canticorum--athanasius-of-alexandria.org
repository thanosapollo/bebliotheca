* Homily on the Song of Songs
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of the same Athanasius, archbishop of Alexandria, on the Song of Songs. The
words are longed-for and lovely, and they lead up into the heavens, and they
give wings to the faithful. For the divine Scriptures truly give wings to the
faithful by the longing for the heavenly things. For having this longing, one
of the saints said: "Who will give me wings as of a dove, and I will fly, and
I will be at rest?" The longing for the heavenly things was drawing him, and
the mind of the saints was being given wings, as they forgot the earthly
things and were anxious for the heavenly. For having given their own mind into
heaven, and longing for the heavenly things, they were forgetting the earthly.
For having this longing, Paul the apostle also said: =27.1352= "I press on
toward the goal unto the prize of the upward calling." Do you see the saints
being given wings? For these words truly give them wings. For whose mind was
not given wings, of those who have heard the reading? For see the longing of
this blessed soul saying: "Awake, O north wind, and come, O south wind, blow
through my garden, and let my spices flow." See the confidence of this blessed
soul, how, taking courage in herself, she calls forth the temptations for her
own proving. "Awake," she says, "O north wind, and come, O south wind." What
she says is of this sort: I have been established upon the rock; let the winds
come and strike against me, the one on this side, and the other on the other
side; I fear no one; I have the rock that bears me; for I built my house upon
the rock, and when the winds came I was not afraid. But do you wish to know
what the rock is, upon which this blessed soul has stood? Hear Paul, how he
interprets it and says: "And the rock was Christ." Standing upon this rock she
is not dismayed, but she does not even fear the devil who comes from the
north, nor again the one from the south. For so far as I have been founded and
have stood upon the rock, I fear nothing; even if he threaten me with death, I
am not dismayed, I do not fear persecution. I am ready to confess; I do not
fear famine, having the Word who strengthens me; and I too say after the most
blessed Paul: "I can do all things in Christ who strengthens me." If
affliction come upon me, I endure it; if persecution come upon me, I bear
witness; if famine come upon me, I have the Word who feeds me; if a sword
come, I have the two-edged one that is above every sword, that which pierces
as far as joints and marrows; if pleasure of flesh come upon me, with the
two-edged sword I cut it off, and I cast it away and make myself a eunuch; not
laying a sword upon myself, as some of men do, but, as the Lord said, that:
"There are eunuchs who have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of the
heavens." For men make men eunuchs not for the kingdom of the heavens, but for
the kingdom of their own women and for keeping, and for pride. But do you wish
to hear who they are who have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of the
heavens? Behold, Joseph made himself a eunuch for the kingdom of the heavens;
for having been seized by the mistress into the bedchamber, and no one being
in the place, and she compelling him to accomplish her own wantonness, he made
himself a eunuch by not consenting to her. And this the blessed Susanna also
has done, having been found alone in the garden; for the two elders, having
laid hold of her, wishing to commit adultery with her, lawless old men hated
by the Lord; and to these Solomon also was saying, or rather the Lord through
Solomon, that "Three sorts my soul has hated, an adulterous old man, and a
proud poor man, =27.1353= and a rich liar"; do you see that those old men are
truly hated by the Lord? When therefore they rose up against the blessed
Susanna, no one being in the place, wishing to accomplish their own
wantonness, she, having taken counsel with herself in her reasoning, made
herself a eunuch for the kingdom of the heavens. For a man cannot keep a man;
nor a mother her own daughter, nor a maidservant a mistress, unless the pious
reasoning become a guardian of the person. Therefore, O man, having made
yourself a eunuch for the kingdom of the heavens, you also, O woman, having
understood the saying still more securely, having examined it, prove yourself,
saying this: "Awake, O north wind, and come, O south wind; blow through my
garden, and let my spices flow." For when our Lord and Savior was crucified,
when the sun was darkened, and the earth was shaken, when the rocks were
split, and the tombs were opened; fear then held the disciples, who did not
know the saying: "I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will
be scattered"; then the disciples, because of the fear of the Jews, were with
one accord hidden in a cell. But let no Jew being present here mock, nor let a
Greek hearing laugh. For in this we have the hope; therefore I also speak with
boldness. For this one hanging upon the wood, he himself is our salvation; for
his crucifixion was a cleansing of sins. And there was then a great spectacle
taking place, that, while the body was being crucified, sin was falling. For
if this one had not been crucified until now, we would have had the bruises
and the weaknesses in the body. And the devil, seeing our weakness, was
exulting over us, rising up against us. For the enemy said: "I will pursue, I
will overtake." Therefore the Lord, having come down, came to no one except to
the devil; for that one was the one giving us the wounds, and having corrupted
us in the weaknesses. Therefore the Lord says, saying: "Behold, I am against
you, O corrupting mountain, that corrupts the whole earth." Therefore then he
says: Let not a Jew laugh, while the disciples are hidden, and the body is
being crucified. For while the body was being moved on the cross, the Word
spoiled Hades; and the doorkeepers of Hades, seeing, cowered; then the women
sat beside the body in the tomb weeping. Then let the disciples of the Lord,
hidden within in the cell, say: "Awake, O north wind, and come, O south wind.
Blow through my garden, and let my spices flow." Let the body that lies in the
north be awakened, and let the Word come from the south. Was it then in the
north that the body of the Lord lay? And we have been in that place in which
the Lord was crucified, and it was from the north of Jerusalem. Therefore
David said with careful observation: "The mountains of Sion, the sides of the
north, the city of the great king." Therefore we also, when we have been in
that place where Christ was crucified, and we were standing in the east, and
praying. =27.1356= And the place where the Lord was crucified was northerly.
Therefore then let the disciples, hidden within in the cell, say: "Awake, O
north wind, and come, O south wind, awake with the north," that is, from the
south into the body. For this also has been written in the prophets, that "God
will come from Teman." And while the body has lain three days in the north,
the Savior comes from the south, and takes the body that lies in the north,
and comes to the disciples in the place where they were. Then at last, Jesus
having entered, the garden blew, and the spices flowed. Then let the disciples
say to the Lord: O Lord, when you were crucified, and were spoiling Hades, we
were in a cell coming together with one accord and seeking the light from you;
and your presence is light to us. But not seeing your presence, we were held
in much darkness; and this was being said against us, that "Their soul was
melting in evils." For we were in evils then, we were not seeing you; but now
when we have seen that the north has been awakened and the south has come,
then the garden blew, and the spices flowed. Then let Thomas come forward to
consider the body, and to see the prints of the nails, and in considering it
and seeing the prints of the nails let him say: "The garden blew, and the
spices flowed." And our spices are the incorruption of the body, which he put
on. Therefore Paul also was saying: "This corruptible must put on
incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality." But when did the body
put on incorruption if not through the rest of the Word? Indeed when the
Savior rose, and the women came to touch him, he was saying to them: "Do not
touch me; for I have not yet ascended to my Father." Therefore, if you have
made yourself a eunuch, O man, you also, O woman, hear the longing of this
blessed soul, how, longing for the Word, she says: "Let my kinsman come down
into his garden, and let him eat the fruit of his orchard-trees." For then she
was calling upon the Word to come down into the garden and eat the fruit of
his orchard-trees; for she takes the garden to be the world, into which she
was calling upon him to come down and eat the fruit of his orchard-trees; and
the orchard-trees to be men; and the fruit of the orchard-trees to be the
deeds of the law; which she was calling upon him to come down and eat. Thus we
also, if we have the fruits of the law ready to render to the Lord, let us
also call upon the Word to come down and eat the fruit of his orchard-trees;
for we are his orchard-trees and workmanship, created upon good works.
Therefore we also call upon him. But how do we call upon him? Through
petitions and prayers, not that we should cry with the mouth, but praying with
the reasoning within ourselves. For why, when you pray, do you cry, as if God
were sleeping? For behold, he who keeps Israel will not slumber, nor will he
sleep. And this Jeremiah also was saying: "He has watched over my ungodly
deeds"; =27.1357= and this Isaiah also was saying: "While you are still
speaking I will say: Behold, I am present." For God does not wait for the
words from the lips. Indeed the Savior in the Gospel was saying, that "God
knows of what things you have need before you ask him." Therefore let us not
cry as those who pray from a work; but if you pray also with the mouth, pray
also with the mind; for this Paul also was saying: "I will sing with the
spirit, I will sing also with the mind." For "Out of the depths," he says, "I
have cried to you, O Lord." Therefore also the woman in the Books of Kings,
when she was praying crying out, the priest silenced her mouth, having
supposed concerning her that she was drunk; and yet she, praying with the
mind, was also heard, and she bore Samuel. Therefore you too, why, when you
pray, do you cry, as if God were not hearing? Hear the one who says, that "He
who planted the ear, does he not hear?" For if you, having ears, hear, how
much more the maker of ears? Therefore let us not, praying before the Lord,
cry as if God were not hearing; for God hears even those who are silent. For
you see the longing that is in this blessed soul, how, as she calls upon him
and says: "Let my kinsman come down into his garden, and let him eat the fruit
of his orchard-trees"; how immediately the Word says: "I have entered into my
garden, my sister, my bride"? as nothing is swifter; for while she was still
speaking, he immediately hearkened; you see what is written: "That even while
you are still speaking I will say, Behold, I am present"? For at the same
time, in her calling upon him and saying: Come down, O Word, into your garden,
and eat the fruit of your orchard-trees, he immediately hearkened to her
saying: "I have entered into my garden, my sister, my bride." But why does he
name her both bride and sister? For every bridegroom does not name his bride
sister; but here he names her both sister and bride, in order to show that the
bridal-chamber is not one chanted of flesh. For when he names her bride, it is
because of the Word, who has been betrothed to the soul. For this Paul also
was saying: "I have betrothed you to one husband, to present a pure virgin to
Christ." But when he also names her sister, it is because of the flesh which
he wore. For this one of the prophets, signifying concerning him, was saying:
"I will declare your name to my brothers." Therefore let no one suppose these
words to be fleshly. For when this blessed soul was calling upon him saying:
"Let my kinsman come down into his garden, and let him eat the fruit of his
orchard-trees," when you hear "soul," do not take it as one soul, but as the
whole world. Therefore then we call upon him saying: Come down, O Word, into
your garden, and eat the fruit of your orchard-trees; and immediately he
himself hearkened: "I have entered into my garden, my sister, my bride; I have
gathered my myrrh with my spices." Did you then call upon me to come down and
eat the fruits? Behold, I have come down; give me the fruits. The word of the
Jews then immediately =27.1360= brings thorns instead of grapes, and instead
of figs thistles. But when did they offer him these fruits, if not when he was
being crucified? For he asked to drink from the fruits of the vine, and
instead of wine they offered him vinegar. Do you see that their fruit has gone
sour? Let the Savior then say: You called upon me to come down, and to eat
from the orchard-trees, and behold I came thirsty, and you offered me vinegar;
and I came again hungry upon the fig tree, and I found leaves only. Would that
I were finding even leaves only, and not the wood dry; for this reason I also
cursed it. For I have come down to eat the fruits of the orchard-trees, and
behold, having come down, I found bloods mixed upon bloods, fornicators and
adulterers, and innocent bloods being poured out. I found you carving stones,
and naming them God; and then instead of orchard-trees, myrrh; and I am led to
death because of you. I have come down to eat the fruits of the orchard-trees;
and behold I gather death because of you; I gather myrrh with spices. But
would that, when I had gathered the myrrh, you had been saved and had ceased
from the evils, and let not that which is said in the twenty-ninth psalm be
said against you: "What profit is there in my blood, in my going down into
corruption?" For I have come down because of you, and I have gathered death
and myrrh; but not the myrrh only, but with spices. But how also with spices?
For when I gathered the myrrh, that is death; when he was laid in the tomb,
and he raised the body incorruptible, then the spices appeared; and the spices
were the incorruption of the body. Therefore he says well: "I have gathered my
myrrh with my spices; I have eaten my bread with my honey; I have drunk my
wine with my milk." Do you see the Word who comes to be toward every age? also
giving to the Jews, being infants, food of honey and milk; and if you hear
honey and milk, take it as the law; for as to them being infants the milk was
given; but to the perfect the bread and the wine are given; for both the bread
and the wine are solid food, that is the Gospel. Therefore then the Savior
says well: "I have eaten my bread with my honey, I have drunk my wine with my
milk." Do you see how he says it everywhere? For both the New and the Old are
his. Therefore woe to the Jews who eat the honey and milk. And likewise also
the Manichees eat the bread and the wine; but the Catholic Church eats the two
together, the bread with the honey, and drinks the wine with the milk; and
eating the bread we consume together with the Word also the honey. And it has
been written to eat the sheep, the Passover. I take the sheep and I consume it
together with the Word; and I do not eat the sheep only, but also the Word who
is led as a sheep to slaughter. Therefore he says well: "I have eaten my bread
with my honey, and I have drunk my wine with my milk"; =27.1361= and
immediately he says: "Eat, my neighbors, and drink, and be drunken, brothers."
See how the Savior commands infants to eat; but to those who are greater he
says, "And drink"; but to the perfect he was saying, that "And be drunken,
brothers." Indeed Paul, being perfect, and having been made drunken with this
divine drunkenness, was saying: "For whether we are beside ourselves, it is to
God, or whether we are of sound mind, it is for you." But do not suppose
concerning him that he was being beside himself from wine; for he himself
commanded: "Do not be drunken with wine, in which is dissipation"; but, "Use a
little wine for your stomach," he writes to Timothy; and if he commands
others, how much more himself? For that one had been made drunken with a good
and divine drunkenness from the Word. And may it come to pass that we also be
numbered together with him in the kingdom of the heavens in Christ Jesus.
Amen.
