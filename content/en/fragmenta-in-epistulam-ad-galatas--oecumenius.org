Gal 2:11-16
Eusebius Pamphili in the Ecclesiastical History shows that this Cephas is not
Peter, but one of the seventy who has the same name as he. And the account is
plausible; for Peter would not have needed withdrawal again, he who had
already made a defense about this in Jerusalem and persuaded all, when,
having eaten with Cornelius, he scandalized some of those of the
circumcision. And if, having done this at the beginning of the preaching, he
persuaded all that he had not done badly, the Spirit leading him to this, how
now after so much time, after Peter had given proof of his own virtue, would
he have needed such an economy again? And that he was condemned, you can also
understand thus: it became for me, he says, a pretext for opposing him to his
face and refuting him. He had been condemned by the Jews for nothing else
than the matter of eating with those in uncircumcision when he ate with
Cornelius. For if he had not been previously condemned by them, they having
been scandalized there, he would not now have withdrawn; and if he had not
withdrawn, there would have been no need of the rebuke, nor of the opposition
to the face. And what at all does the opposition to the face benefit? That it
made it timely for this to be said before the Jews: we being Jews by nature
and not sinners from the Gentiles, knowing that a man is not justified from
works of law except through faith of Jesus Christ, and 447 we believed in
Christ Jesus, that we might be justified from faith of Christ and not from
works of law. Therefore because these things were to be said before all, the
opposition to the face took place, since it was necessary, if indeed there
was some fault, to accuse privately and correct, the Lord saying: if your
brother sins, rebuke him privately, but if he disobeys, set others also and
the church over it. gal 3:23 He made clear that the faith toward Christ was
predetermined from of old, but now it has become manifest to men, when also
the incarnation. gal 4:9 He calls them weak and poor, not because of their
cheapness; for what is more magnificent than heaven and earth? And what more
precious than sun and moon and stars? But because they are deprived of mind
and life and sensation. gal 4:17 Not for good, he says, do they welcome this
same faith of yours toward Christ, but that having shut you out from it they
may persuade you to follow themselves, because such a thing has been
contrived by them for your deception. gal 4, See love. I am not content, he
says, with the letters; I was seeking to be present with you and to exchange
the teaching through words for lamentations and wailings, that, he says, I
might have taught you with lamentations. For I am at a loss what to say
concerning you. Whence have you who were lifted up in the knowledge of the
gospel been so thrown down and dragged about on the ground, clinging again to
the law? gal 6:2 Or that to bear one another's burdens and shortcomings is
according to this a fulfillment of the law of Christ, inasmuch as it comes
from love; and love is fulfilling of the divine law. 448 gal 6:15 A new
creation is, he says, all things; for all, both the circumcised and the
uncircumcised who have believed, have become a new creation, the old man
having been buried and we having been regenerated in the font.
