Of Evagrius on the pipi.
Because of the Hebrew notation upon the Lord these things must be said. God is
named by ten names among the Hebrews. Of which one is called Adonai, which is
Lord, and another Ia, which itself was taken over in the Greek into the Lord.
And another besides these is the tetragrammaton, being unpronounceable among
the Hebrews, which by a misuse among them is called Adonai, and among us Lord.
And they say this has been written upon the golden petal upon the forehead of
the high priest according to what is said in the law "an impression of a seal,
a holiness to the Lord pipi". And the remaining names are these: El, Elohim,
Adon, Sabaoth, Saddai, Aie eser ie and the three written before, of which is
the tetragrammaton, being written with these letters yod he vav he. pipi the
God. The unpronounceable name arranged upon the Lord is written through four
letters, through the yod, through the he, through the vav and through the he.
Of these a letter called shin among the Hebrews, which is teeth, having been
inserted in the middle after the first two, so that the sequence of the five
letters is thus: this beginning, teeth in it the living. The four letters are
interpreted, the yod beginning, and through the he this, and through the vav
and through the he. Of these a letter called shin among the Hebrews, which is
teeth, having been inserted in the middle after the first two, so that the
sequence of the five letters is thus: this beginning, teeth in it the living.
The four letters are interpreted, the yod beginning, and through the he this,
and through the vav in it and through the he the living.
