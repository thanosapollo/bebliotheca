=96.477= Of Nyssa, from the discourse on Saint Romanus.
The wonder-workers of Christ do not run along a thin cord, nor, tumbling over
naked swords, do they escape the wounds by art, but instead of a rope they run
with unshaken step the narrow and precipice-flanked road of piety. And they
blunt the edges of the tyrants' swords by their eagerness for wounds =96.477=,
not making it their art not to suffer, but training themselves to conquer in
suffering.
