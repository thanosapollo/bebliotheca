ON THE 132ND PSALM.
Hear the blessed David speaking through the psalm: Behold now, what is
good, or what is pleasant, for brothers to dwell upon the same? For he
rejoiced not only in his own successes, but was also gladdened at the
good order of the others; as somewhere he himself, having seen the zeal
of those hastening to the worship, said through the Psalmist: I was glad
at those who said to me: We will go into the house of the Lord. Our feet
were standing in your courts, Jerusalem. Surely then, having seen the
presence upon the same, and having been delighted by the brotherly
disposition, he cried out, saying: Behold now, what is good, or what is
pleasant, but that brothers should dwell upon the same? The subject of
the assembling of the brothers from a divine command had the good
unsurpassable; and from this good the pleasant also is not separated.
For each of those present, enjoying the love from the neighbor, we were
established in much gladness. So that also consequently he said: What is
good, or what is pleasant, but that brothers should dwell upon the
same? As ointment upon the head, that which comes down upon the beard,
the beard of Aaron. The grace being poured out from above from heaven
from the Savior upon the Church, reasonably the souls of the saints
partake of the bodiless and spiritual supply.
As dew of Aermon, that which comes down upon the mountains of Zion. For
just as the dew that comes down upon the mountain Aermon makes much
brightness for it; so also the intelligible dew, coming down invisibly
upon the mountains of Zion, where the gathering of the brothers from
everywhere was taking place, procured the benefit analogous to their
souls. Concerning this divine dew, I think, which is likened to the
energies of the Holy Spirit, Isaiah discoursing says: For the dew that
is from you is healing to them. The dew then of Aermon, that which comes
down upon the mountains of Zion; because there the Lord commanded the
blessing, life. In many places God promised, as Moses said: And the Lord
will keep the covenant and the mercy, as he swore to your fathers, and
he will love you, and he will bless you, and he will multiply the
offspring of your belly, and the fruit of your land. And he added
saying: Life unto the age. Behold now, what is good, or what is
pleasant, but that brothers should dwell upon =30.117= the same? even as
you also were called. One Lord, one faith, one baptism; one God and
Father, who is over all and through all and in all. Behold now, what is
good, or what is pleasant, but that brothers should dwell upon the
same? not in a bodily house only, but also in this holy and spiritual
one. For my house, he says, shall be called a house of prayer. But we
also who have been enlightened have been deemed worthy of the same
ointment. For Christ our Savior anointed us all with his righteousness;
through whom and with whom to the Father and to the Holy Spirit be glory
and magnificence unto the ages of ages. Amen.
