Of the same, on the betrayal of Judas.
=50.715=
Now Daphne is delightful and God-beloved, not only because she pours forth
waters of the clearest kind, not only because she nurses the loveliest tresses
of trees, but because she has taken to herself a strange plant, the Cross.
Now she is a spring truly chaste, and terrible to the Pythian daemon. She no
longer spreads her ground for the feet of impious men, =50.716= but grants
the grove to you who are pious, an image of that longed-for place, I mean the
garden, in which the betrayal of the Savior was dared, and the things of our
salvation were planted.
But I do not know what to say to the =50.717= present festival. For the
festival moves my tongue toward the accusation of Judas, but the love of
mankind in the Savior turns my mouth toward itself. And I am held by these
two things: hatred of a traitor, longing for the Master. But longing conquers
hatred, being greater and more powerful. Therefore I leave the traitor and
hymn the Benefactor, not as much as he is worthy, but as much as I am able
to sing. How did he bow the heavens and come down to earth? How did he who
fills all creation come to me, becoming for my sake what I am? How did he
take as a disciple the one he knew would be a traitor, and bid the enemy
follow him as a friend? How was he not anxious about the betrayal, but took
thought for the salvation of the one who was betraying him? For when evening
had come, it says, Jesus reclined with his twelve disciples, and as they were
eating he said: Amen, amen, I say to you, that one of you will betray me.
He foretold the betrayal, that he might hinder the transgression. He made
the prediction without a name, so that it could not make a spectacle of the
disciple's crime while it was still hidden from those reclining with him.
Who has seen such love of mankind in a Master? He is betrayed, and he loves
the traitor. Who is despised and yet has mercy? He is sold, and he takes the
wicked merchant as a companion at table, and he spares the one who plotted
against him? And as they were eating he said: Amen, amen, I say to you that
one of you will betray me. As man he was eating, and as God he was declaring
what was to come. He takes up the things that belong to my nature, for my
sake. And when all the disciples were struck by the word, and were scourging
their own consciences terribly, and were making the time of the supper a time
of despondency, each saying, Is it I, Lord? and wanting by the word of the
question to find relief from the evil suspicion: healing the souls of those
struck out of season, the Savior makes the unknown one plain by his answer.
He who dipped his hand with me in the dish, he says, this one will betray me;
and the Son of Man goes as it is written concerning him. But woe to that man
through whom the Son of Man is betrayed; it were good for him if that man
had not been born. He has mercy on the one who will not have mercy on himself;
he spares the one who did not spare his own soul.
He declined to strip bare the one who had long since stripped himself, giving
even to him a time for repentance, and healing the remaining disciples in
their despondency; but the traitor became no better from these things. For
he ought at once to have risen from the supper after those most dreadful
words; he ought to have taken his fellow disciples as ambassadors; he ought
to have clasped the knees of the Savior, and to have won him over with such
words as these. I have sinned, O Lover of mankind, I have sinned, I have
broken the law, having sold to men for a little price the priceless pearl;
I have broken the law, casting away the inexhaustible wealth for a few coins.
Forgive me, the merchant of loss and of ruin; forgive me, whose reason was
stolen by gold; pardon the one wickedly deceived by the Pharisees. Of these
words he said nothing, nor even thought them, but by a quicker cry he showed
the shamelessness of his soul, shouting: Is it I, Lord? O shameless tongue!
O stubborn soul! As if ignorant he asks about the very things he has been
practicing, and he thought to escape the unsleeping eye. He carried the deceit
in his soul, and through =50.718= his tongue he brought forth the speech of
ignorance. In his mind he worked the betrayal, and with his mouth, as he
thought, he hid the sin.
He used the same words as the other disciples, not using the same manner.
Being a wolf in purpose, he answered with the voice of the sheep. What then
does the Savior say to him? You have said it. With a long-suffering voice he
exposed the wicked man's play. For he could have said to him: What are you
saying, you abominable and wicked man? What are you saying, slave of money
and true partner of the devil? Do you dare to play at ignorance? Do you dare
to hide what cannot be hidden? Was I not present by my Godhead when you were
doing evil? Did I not see you with the eye of Godhead going to the chief
priests? Did I not hear, though absent, when you said, What will you give me,
and I will deliver him to you? Do I not know also for how much you have sold
him? What then? After the exposure are you still so shameless? Why do you
contend to cover what you mean to do? To me all things are bare. Christ,
though able to answer thus, did not speak thus, but simply and without
resentment and mildly: You have said it; teaching us to deal thus with our
enemies.
Yet after so great a course of treatment, Judas remained sick, not through
the physician's neglect, but through that of the patient. For the one brought
every medicine of salvation, but the other would not receive them, and knowing
only the love of money, he loved gold more than Christ, and became well
disposed and faithful toward those who had hired him. And Judas came and said
to Christ: Hail, Rabbi, and he kissed him. This is a strange manner of
betrayal, coming from a kiss and a greeting. And Jesus said to him: Friend,
for what are you here? Why do you bid me hail, when you choose to grieve me?
Why do you soothe with the word, while you strike with the deed? Do you call
me teacher, not being a disciple? Why do you wound the just claims of love?
Why do you make the token of peace a sign of betrayal? Whom have you imitated
in doing this? Did you see the harlot lately kissing my feet? Did you behold
the centurion kneeling? Did you see the demons falling down? I know who showed
you the way of your treacherous kiss. The devil suggested to you the manner
of this embrace, and you, persuaded by an evil counselor, fulfill his will.
Friend, for what are you here? Fulfill the wicked covenants you have made
with the Pharisees; complete the bill of sale; sign what you dictated;
deliver the one who is willing to be delivered; take now, along with the
money-box, the purse of injustice as well; give way to the robber who is
about to receive by confession the rank which you lost by betrayal. Then they
came and laid their hands on Jesus, and seized him. And that prophetic saying
was receiving its fulfillment: They surrounded me like bees a honeycomb, and
they were kindled like fire among thorns; and again, Many dogs have surrounded
me, fat bulls have encompassed me.
But O the forbearance that belongs to him alone! In heaven the Cherubim and
the Seraphim, not daring to look upon the surpassing glory, cover their faces
with their wings as with hands; and on earth, while his flesh was held by
lawless hands, he endured it. You have seen of what a long-suffering and
mankind-loving Master you have been made servants? Be then such toward the
=50.719= enemies who are your fellow servants, as you have seen the Lord
become toward those who warred against him.
For you too are about to be called to a spiritual supper; you are about to
recline with the Master. Let no one of Judas's manner be found among you.
Let all come forward with calm =50.720= and peace; let us all run to the
Savior with a clean conscience. For he is the fast of the faithful and the
feast; he is nourisher and food; he is shepherd and lamb. To him be the glory
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
