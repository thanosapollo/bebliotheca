Of our father among the saints ATHANASIUS, archbishop of Alexandria, a letter
to JOHN and ANTIOCHUS the presbyters.
Athanasius to John and Antiochus, the beloved sons and fellow-presbyters,
greeting in the Lord. I rejoiced having received your letter also now, that
you wrote especially from Jerusalem; and I have thanks to you, that you made
clear concerning the brothers, those gathered there, and concerning those
wishing on a pretext of questions to trouble the simple. But concerning these
let the Apostle command, Not to attend to those =26.1168= fighting with words,
and seeking nothing other than to say something new and to hear. And you,
having the foundation firm, which is Jesus the Christ, our Lord, and the
confession of the Fathers concerning the truth, turn away those wishing to
speak more than this or less, and rather become of the benefit of the
brothers, unto fearing God, and keeping the commandments; that both from the
teaching of the Fathers, and from the keeping of the commandments they may be
able to appear well-pleasing to the Lord on the day of judgment. And I have
altogether wondered at the boldness of those daring to speak against our
beloved, the truly servant of God Basil the bishop. For from such babble they
will be able to be convicted, as not even loving the confession of the
Fathers. Greet the brothers. Those with me greet you. I pray that you be well
in the Lord, beloved and most longed-for sons.
