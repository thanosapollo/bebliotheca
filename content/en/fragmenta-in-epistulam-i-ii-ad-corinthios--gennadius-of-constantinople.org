1 Cor 10:15 In these he wishes to teach them the fitting things from a setting
beside of the ancients, showing those too as in a certain type having been
deemed worthy of the equal things; for thus, the example appearing corresponding,
he was found reasonably exhorting them not to err the like things, lest they
suffer the like. Therefore their being under the cloud was a showing of the
grace of the spirit; for the cloud shading those delivered them from the harm
from the Egyptians, and the grace takes us out of the demonic wickedness. And
their going through the sea is like in this to our things, that what the
crossing through the sea furnished to those, having altogether delivered them
from the enemies, and having granted them a firm freedom henceforth, this
baptism has become for us toward our enemies. And they were baptized into Moses
in the cloud and in the sea, this signifies that as those, having obtained these
things, promised to live by the Mosaic laws, so also we, bringing the spirit of
sonship in baptism, set covenants and confessions of living henceforth according
to the commands of Christ.
{1 Cor 12:28} the workings clearly of the powers.
{1 Cor 13:2} Now he did not say faith of the faithful, the common and catholic,
but the gift of faith; for there was a certain form of gift called homonymously
thus with faith, whence upon a manifestation of a spiritual partaking it was
supplied to many in that time of the pious. Concerning this also the Lord 419 in
Gospels says: if you have faith as a grain of mustard, you will say to this
mountain: be lifted and be thrown into the sea, and it will happen thus. For not
surely as toward unbelievers did he discuss these things toward them concerning
the common faith.
