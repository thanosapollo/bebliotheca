THE DISCOURSES AGAINST THE ARIANS
54 But the paradoxical mid-chorus of Arius' stage say that Christ has
committed sin; 55 But that God was in Christ reconciling the world to himself,
they neither bear in memory nor utter with the organs of the tongue. But like
terrible slanderers and accusers, having amputated the passage, they make the
contest of the accusation.
56 But the lawful combatants must, in love of truth, having included all
things without omission, not for deceit keep silent a part, and tearing off a
part put it forward. 57 For if created, then not begotten; but if begotten,
not created; since it is not possible for each kind to turn about the same
nature.
58 Everything that has a beginning also admits an end, everything that admits
an end is capable of corruption.
