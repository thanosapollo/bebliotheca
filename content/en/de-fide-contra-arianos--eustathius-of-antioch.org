63 For the man of Christ has become for us the beginning of the fairest ways
of righteousness, leading us to the better practices. He fittingly attaches to
the Spirit, he escorts as a stranger into paradise, he restores to his own
pasture. Therefore Christ also said: I am the way and the truth and the life.
By the name of the way he shows the wrapping according to man, which is of
bodily and visible things; for the way is a portion of earth and a perceptible
floor; but the name of truth and of life [shows] the nature of the Father; for
truth is something intelligible, falling under neither eyes nor touch.
