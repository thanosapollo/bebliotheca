OF PHOTIUS, PATRIARCH OF CONSTANTINOPLE; but others say OF LEO THE PHILOSOPHER
Bitter love, but a chaste life, as the tale of Clitophon displays; and the
most chaste life of Leucippe amazes all, how, beaten and shorn and dishonored,
and what is greatest, having died three times, she endured. And if you too
wish to be chaste, friend, do not look at the incidental spectacle of the
writing, but first learn the course of the story; for it decks as brides those
who desire wisely.
