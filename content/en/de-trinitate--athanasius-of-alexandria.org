OF THE SAME ATHANASIUS
Glorify one nature, the Godhead of Father and Son and Holy Spirit, and these
three hypostases, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, even as we have also been
baptized.
For Go, he says, make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the
name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. One nature,
because to glorify more and to cut the one Godhead into many gods is of the
Greeks; and three hypostases, that is, persons, so that the opinion of the
Jews may not have place, and so that the Father may not be barren and
without Word, from whom every family; and the Word more deficient than our
word, not having Spirit; and so that the perfect may be in three perfect
hypostases. For the image of the mind in us, and of word and of spirit, is
Father, and Son, and Spirit. And hearing Spirit, understand Holy,
enhypostatic. For us, being corruptible, the spirit also is dissolved; but
of the immortal the spirit is altogether immortal, as some enhypostatic
essence. One then is the nature of the three hypostases, the Godhead, and
one will, and one operation, one power, one purpose and movement, and leap
of the brightness of the three. God the Father, God the Son, God the
Spirit, the three one; perfect God the Father, perfect the Son, perfect the
Holy Spirit; of the same honor, of the same authority. Creative of all
things that are, and cohesive, and providential, all things likewise to the
three, and unchangeably and identically, as many as belong to being, and as
many as belong to not being something, and as many as belong to how it
stands toward the created, and as many as belong to operation, and as many
as belong to power, as many as belong to eternity, and as many as are said
to be according to preeminence, or according to cause. For example, being
the same, being incorporeal, the incomprehensible, reigning, creating,
deifying, being able, being eternal, =28.1605= being a beginning
beyond-beginning, being an essence beyond-essence, being called light. And
darkness, the things said symbolically, such as being wrathful and being
angry, sleeping and being awake. Fitting all these to the one nature, we do
not make it clear or think it from the things according to it, but from the
things around it. Knowing then one nature of the three hypostases,
differing in none of the other things except by these properties, I mean
begetting and being begotten, and proceeding, and that the one is Father,
the other Son, the other Spirit; and that the Father is begetter and
projector, begetter of the Son, projector of the Spirit; and the Son
offspring, and the Spirit that which is projected. And the Father
unbegotten, and the Son begotten with two n's, and the Spirit proceeding.
And the Father cause, and the Son and the Spirit caused. And the Father
projector, and the Son Son only, and the Spirit proceeding; for they do not
depart from their properties, neither the Father from being Father, nor the
Son from being Son, nor the Spirit from being Spirit, but the essence is
common, one Godhead of the three, just as three lights united to one
another and not confused. These things are sufficient to know concerning
theology.
The end, and thanks be to God.
