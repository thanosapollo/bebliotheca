OF THE SAME CYRIL From the second discourse toward the things of Theodore Whose
beginning Those who have looked around the holy and God-inspired scripture with
a pure eye of mind.
1. He said to the disciples "Do not call a teacher upon the earth; for one is
your guide, the Christ;" for not when he commanded this to the apostles did he
divide his own Godhead from the appearing body, nor when he witnessed himself to
be Christ did he so divide of soul and flesh being both God and man, seen a
slave and recognized Lord, hiding the high of the Godhead with the humble
thought of the incarnation, and lifting the humble of the seen body by the
energy of the Godhead.
2. Let men not deceive, nor be deceived receiving a mindless man the Lord's, as
they say, rather our Lord and God; for we do not divide the man from the
Godhead, but we dogmatize one and the same, formerly not a man, but God and only
Son and pre-eternal unmixed with body and the things as many as are of body; and
at the last also a man taken on for our salvation, passible in flesh, impassible
in Godhead, circumscribed in body, uncircumscribed in Godhead, the same earthly
and heavenly, seen and thought, containable and uncontainable, that by a whole
man the same and God the whole man who fell under the sin may be refashioned. 3.
For since the Only-begotten Word of God being life according to nature was
incarnate, the nature of man bloomed again into life; for he became "himself
first in all." and because of this cause the life-giving Word of God made his
own a flesh held by death, that having shown it better than death and
corruption, he may send the grace also unto us. For as we were brought down into
death in Adam, so in Christ having pushed aside the tyranny of death, we are
re-formed toward incorruption. 513 4. Of the same from the first discourse
against Theodore. For as from soul and body one man, although of the properties
belonging to each having a very great difference toward one another according at
least to being of such a sort I say; for a soul is other beside the body; so it
would also be thought upon Christ the Savior of us all.
