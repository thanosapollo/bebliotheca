On Prayer.
=62.737=
Come, all of you, brothers, let us come to know him who made us, our Lord
Jesus Christ, the good, the lover of mankind, and the compassionate, who
does not will the death of the sinner, but that he should turn and live.
Watch, all of you, and be ready, for you do not know at what hour our Lord
comes. Come, hear him saying: Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has
drawn near. Therefore, brothers, since the judgment is at the doors, there
is need of many tears, of much lamentation, of much prayer, prayer not done
today and tomorrow only.
For prayer is an unfailing treasure of every good, a harbor unshaken by
storm, a ground of calm, a healing-house of the soul. Prayer is psalmody, a
sanctification of soul and body. And, to speak simply, prayer, opening the
gates of heaven, brings every man to God, small and great, rich and poor,
and makes the one who prays with his whole soul each day greater than the
angels. But what is that cold word of the many? I am a sinner, he says, and
without boldness, full of shame, and I cannot lift my eyes and look upon
the height of heaven, because I have sinned much, both in words and in
deeds and in thought, and every day and hour I have spent my life in
dissipation; and with what face or boldness shall I enter the church? How
shall I open my wicked and unclean mouth? How shall I move my lips, or for
which of my wicked deeds shall I first beseech God? Do you then speak such
satanic words as these?
Learn whom Christ calls, having come upon the earth: the righteous, or
sinners? For he speaks thus: I did not come to call the righteous, but
sinners to repentance. I am a sinner, he says, =62.738= and how shall I
enter the church, that I may become righteous? You speak well: I hear, and
I do not keep; and what is the profit to me? And I say to you again:
whether you keep or whether you do not keep, only enter the church. You
hear the divine Scriptures; hear the reading.
I know truly that if you enter the church, and you hear the divine Gospels,
and the teachers, the apostles, and the divine fathers, even if you have a
heart of stone, even if you have a beast's mind and wildness, you will at
last, and easily, be pierced with compunction. Are you a sinner? Enter the
church, fall down, weep, lament, groan, shed tears. Have you sinned?
Confess your sins to God. Say only this: I have sinned. Say it to God, who
knows before a thing comes to be. Say it to God, who searches hearts and
reins. Say it here, that you may not be put to shame and convicted there
before myriads of angels and men. Tell me, which is better: here in the
church to confess to God alone and to your spiritual father, or there to be
published before so many myriads? Are you a sinner? Say only this; I demand
nothing else of you, only groan. What necessity is this? What affliction?
What spending of money? What difficulty is it to say only, I have sinned?
But you say that I am ashamed. Wretched and miserable man, when you were
doing the sin, you were not ashamed, and when you wish to repent and be
saved, you are ashamed? When you were wrapping yourself about the wicked
harlot, and doing a wicked deed, you were not ashamed, and now you are
ashamed? When you were sinning, when you were fornicating and committing
adultery, you were not ashamed, and when you repent and wish to be saved,
then you are ashamed? When you were seizing what belonged to others, when
you were oppressing widows and despising =62.739= the poor, you were not
ashamed, and now you are ashamed? When you were swearing by the fearful
name of God, and bearing false witness, when you were perjuring, when you
were doing every evil and the wicked works that God hates, you were not
ashamed,

and now you are ashamed? O what a good Master we have, and we ourselves are
careless and do not turn back! Christ counsels: Do not steal, do not
fornicate, do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not bear false
witness, honor your father and your mother, and love your neighbor as
yourself. Become gentle, good, merciful, long-suffering. Become without
strife, peaceable, that you may be counted worthy of the kingdom of the
heavens. And there is no one who understands, no one who obeys, no one who
pays heed, but we all are slothful.
But the devil counsels the opposite, saying: Become an adulterer, murder,
perjure, steal; become inhuman, a blasphemer, unmerciful, without pity, a
corrupter. Become a lover of money, a hater of men, or rather a hater of
God. Become a knave, an unjust man, a greedy man. Become a robber, a
slanderer, a teller of idle tales, a reviler, a perjurer. And we know how
many of us, doing such things, obey; for like slaves bound hand and foot,
so we run and submit our nature.
See, we have spent all our youth with demons. See, we have grown old, and
we have grown accustomed to sins. We cannot do the good, so as to obtain
the kingdom of the heavens. But the devil procures the fire and the eternal
punishment, and we run toward the devil. What shall we do for you, O man?
What do you will? What do you desire? To be with Christ, or to be with the
demons? Do you desire to be with Christ? Then at last cease from luxury,
cease from living riotously, cease from fornicating, cease from stealing,
cease from getting drunk, cease from seizing what belongs to others, cease
from mocking, from lying, from being puffed up. Let no one deceive you; for
none of such people shall enter the kingdom of the heavens. But if you
desire to be with =62.740= the demons, if you desire to enter the gehenna
of fire, then do as you will, conduct yourself as you please; there is no
one who hinders. Enjoy the world and its shameful desires, fornication,
adultery, drunkenness, incontinence, and every demonic pleasure; there is
no one who stands in the way. For the Gospels do not persuade you, the law
does not chasten you, the prophets do not charge you, the apostles do not
put you to shame, the admonitions of the fathers do not profit you, the
company of brothers does not turn you back. You have become wholly
shameless; a harlot's face has become yours. Therefore enjoy your goods in
your life. Then do not expect salvation, nor an inheritance on the coming
day of Christ. For the hour will escape you then, when you stand naked and
laid bare before the judgment seat of Christ, made a spectacle before
angels and men. There you will appear as you are. There all your injustice
will be convicted. God is not mocked. And then you will hear that painful
voice: Bind his hands and feet, and cast him into the outer darkness; there
will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth. Who then will not lament
such a one? Who will not weep for him who, for a temporary enjoyment, is
tortured eternally?
Do not be deceived. God is not mocked. For it is written that neither
fornicators, nor adulterers, nor revilers, nor the greedy, nor drunkards,
nor the unjust, nor the effeminate, nor men who lie with men, nor
idolaters, nor blasphemers, nor lovers of money, nor anyone of such a kind
has an inheritance in the kingdom of the heavens. Considering all these
things, brothers, let us abstain from such wicked deeds, and from our whole
souls let us not delay to repent, that we may both flee from the wrath that
is to come and attain the heavenly and eternal goods; of which may it be
granted to us all to obtain, by the grace and loving-kindness of our Lord
Jesus Christ, with whom to the Father be glory together with the Holy
Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
