* On False Prophets

Discourse concerning false prophets, and false teachers, and godless heretics,
and concerning the signs of the consummation of this age. And it was spoken
when he was about to go abroad from the body.
=59.553=
αʹ. The discourse is painful, inasmuch as it is also last, as has been shown
to me, but it is also full of much joy. Painful, because I shall no longer
speak to you; but full of much joy, because my time is at hand to depart, and
to be with Christ; and as the Lord said, I will no longer speak with you. But
still now I will speak in pain of heart concerning the false prophets, and
false teachers, and godless heretics, concerning whom the Apostle said: Evil
men and sorcerers will proceed to the worse, deceiving and being deceived;
concerning whom I have often reminded you. For I have made many discourses by
the grace of Christ, as you also know, you who love God; and almost in every
discourse we have reminded you concerning these, if perhaps you remember the
things said yesterday; and I know that you remember, and especially the lovers
of labor, and the lovers of books, and the lovers of Christ. For the lover of
books would justly be called a lover of Christ, according to what was said by
the Master, that He who loves me keeps my words; He who loves me shall be
loved by my Father. He who loves me will meditate in the law day and night;
that is, in the Gospel and in the remaining Scriptures. For such a one has in
his heart an indelible memory of God; awaiting him from the heavens, he always
watches the hour of his coming. Always holding the sacred books in his hands,
he will not forget those fearful books, concerning which it is written: The
court sat, and books were opened. See how great is the gain of searching the
Scriptures, as you have often heard. And what is it that we say? That the
divine Scripture has lacked nothing, nor has it kept silent any of the things
that profit us, but it cries everywhere, through both prophets and apostles,
proclaiming beforehand and securing each one beforehand, as a mother who loves
her children, loving her own children, she testifies beforehand; and she
reminds concerning the things that have passed and concerning the =59.554=
things present, and concerning the things to come, having overlooked nothing,
as was said before, of the things that profit us; and this not speaking in
secret, nor with a small voice, but everywhere crying with toil and power
through both the law, and prophets, and apostles, and the Master himself. For
through Isaiah the prophet he says: Cry out with strength, and spare not, lift
up your voice as a trumpet; and again he says, Go up upon a high mountain, you
who bring good tidings to Zion; and going on he says, Lift up, do not fear.
And again David says: I have grown weary with crying, my throat has become
hoarse. And John the theologian in his catholic Epistle says: Look to
yourselves. And Paul, what? See how you walk; and the Lord, I have spoken
openly to the world, and in secret I have spoken nothing. And elsewhere the
Evangelist concerning him, that He stood, and cried saying: If anyone thirsts,
let him come to me, and drink. Do you see how great a struggle, how great
eagerness, how he cries with boldness, and no one understands? And again the
blessed Paul says: Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil workers, Beware of
the concision. See how you walk, because the days are evil. And John: Look to
yourselves, that you may not lose the things you have worked, because many
deceivers have gone out into the world. And there are many such things in the
divine


Scriptures of both the Old and New Testament, crying out and waking us from
our sloth. But we do the contrary, as Isaiah the prophet said, that With their
ears they heard heavily, and they closed their eyes. O how many and how great
writings we hear and do not understand! Whence we shall also be without excuse
on the day of examination, when he comes who lights up the hidden things of
darkness, and makes manifest the counsels of the hearts, when the court sits,
and books =59.555= are opened, which we now mock as we hear them, and do not
receive. For each of us has gone astray in his own way, leaving the straight
way, and we have become as from the beginning, when you did not rule over us,
Lord. And truly in us has been fulfilled the prophecy of David saying: And
they were mixed among the nations, and learned their works. For this reason we
are humbled in all the earth because of our sins, and upon us have come the
ends of the ages, as it is written; and the shepherds are wolves, and the
sheep are crushed; and the famine is at the doors, not a famine of bread and a
thirst of water, but a famine of hearing the word of God. Let no one then of
those who hope in the Lord despair. Do not then be downcast, always looking
beforehand to him who said, that I am with you.
βʹ. Take courage then; for the Lord will not starve the souls of the
righteous. Concerning this famine the Lord says through the prophet: And it
shall be in those days, I will bring a famine upon the earth; not a famine of
bread, nor a thirst of water, but a famine of hearing the word of God. And
they shall run about from east even to west, seeking the word of the Lord. Do
you see that he is not speaking about bread? For God was not speaking about
bread. O famine terrible, and soul-destroying, and harmful! O famine that
procures eternal punishment! This famine is the supplier of every wicked deed;
this famine is the cause of every evil. This famine the prophet David,
foreseeing it coming, was entreating God, saying: My God, do not keep silence
from me. And concerning the zealous faithful he prophesied saying: To deliver
their souls from death, and to nourish them in famine. And elsewhere he says:
And in days of famine they shall be filled; and again, The Lord is merciful
and compassionate, he has given food to those who fear him. These things David
said concerning those who search the Scriptures, that they shall not hunger.
Let us search the Scriptures, brethren, that we may not hunger when the
soul-destroying famine comes; let us search, that we may not be deceived, and
be carried about by every wind. Therefore the Lord says to those who neglect:
You err, not knowing the Scriptures. For truly such men are deceived. For from
where did the soul-killing famine come? Was it not from not searching the
Scriptures? O how many good things we have deprived ourselves of, having left
the divine Scriptures! For the command is the Master's. Search laboriously,
search and seek, and you will find much incomparable wealth, and a treasure
hidden in the field; you will understand the divine Scripture. Search, and you
will find, and having found, sell all that you have, and buy that field, the
good knowledge of the holy Scriptures, in which the Son is hidden, the true
Wisdom of the Father; which having found, you shall be blessed, as it is
written, that Blessed is the man who has found wisdom. Search, beloved,
whether you happen to be rich or poor, whether slave or free, whether male or
female. Search the Scriptures. For the divine Scripture is a storehouse of
every good. But let us return to the matter at hand; for the discourse is
concerning the consummation, and concerning false prophets, and false
teachers, and concerning godless heretics, who have flooded everywhere like
torrents, and deceive many. And from where has this happened? It is clear that
it is from the ignorance and inexperience of those who preside; for where
there is inexperience of shepherds, there is of sheep


destruction. What then shall I say first? concerning our consummation? or
shall I pillory the profane doctrines of the godless heretics? If there were
time, I would have triumphed over and published their profane doctrines, and
their unlawful deeds; but great is the struggle, and long is the discourse, to
strip and triumph over their unclean doctrines. Yet for the present it is
necessary to present these from the holy Scriptures as enemies of Christ, and
wolves of his flock, driven everywhere, and cast out from the sheep of Christ.
Therefore they were also justly called wolves by the prophets, and by the
Master himself, and by the blessed apostles; and not only wolves, but also
plagues, and impious, and adversaries, and enemies, and plotters, and
blasphemers, and hypocrites, and thieves, and robbers, and abominable, and
false prophets, and false teachers, and blind guides, and deceivers, and
wicked, and antichrists, and scandals, and sons of the evil one, and tares,
and godless, and fighters against the Spirit, who have blasphemed the =59.556=
Spirit of grace; to whom it shall not be forgiven either in this age, or in
the one to come; Because of whom the way of truth shall be blasphemed. And
besides these also children of the evil devil, as the theologian says:
Manifest are the children of the devil. One must then first occupy oneself
with these, and then speak concerning the consummation. Since then the ends of
the ages have come upon us, as the Apostle says, and the hard times have
arisen, and lawlessness has been multiplied, and the love of the many has
grown cold, and those who deceive are many, and those who are deceived are
more; come then, let us unfold the sacred books, in which, having found the
way of truth, let us walk, running up to the holy mountains, I mean prophets
and apostles, that we may not be deceived, and Be carried about by every wind
of teaching, in the dice-playing of men, in craftiness toward the method of
deceit. Let us not remain below, but let us go up together with the holy
disciples upon the blessed mountain, and let us hear our shepherd saying: See
that you are not deceived; See that no one deceives you; see everywhere. And
John says like things: That you may not lose the things you have worked; and
Paul, Beware of the dogs; See how you walk. And the See, and See, is nothing
other than what has been said for your safety concerning those who deceive
with the fleece, and hide the wolf and deceive the more careless. And very
necessarily everywhere in the Scriptures has been said the See, and Look, and
Watch, and Take heed, not only to yourselves, but also to all the flock. For
the divine Scripture has kept silent nothing of the things that profit us.
γʹ. But it comes upon me to weep, whenever I hear some from our Church saying
that these things have not been said in the divine Scriptures; and this not
only from laymen, but also from those who seem to be shepherds, and who occupy
the places of apostles and prophets, but not their ways; to whom it is timely
to say: Woe to you, the blind guides, and unlearned, and unstable; beautifiers
of garments, and not of books; who have left the word of God, and serve the
belly; Whose God is the belly, and the glory in their shame; who consume the
milk and the wool and the flesh of the flock, but do not care for the sheep?
How then will you escape, having neglected so great a salvation? I, being
lifted by the word into the midst, will add more testimonies, proving from the
holy Scriptures, both Gospels and prophets and apostles, Christ supplying, how
the mouth of those who speak unjust things is stopped, and the heart of those
who long for the word is illumined. And from where shall we begin, or from him


who said: I am the beginning and the end? And let each one set his own mind,
having put away every worldly care. The Lord said: See that you are not
deceived. For many will come upon my name saying that I am the Christ; and
they will deceive many. And again: Take heed to yourselves from the false
prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening
wolves; but from their fruits you will know them, he says; that is, from the
words, from the false prophecy, from their hypocrisy, from their evil
doctrine, from their blasphemy you will know them. For a rotten tree cannot
make good fruits. For every tree is known from its own fruit. So also you will
know such men from their own fruits; and having known them, do not receive
them into a house, do not bow down, do not deem them worthy of a word, do not
cast your pearls before them; but take heed from their leaven, that is, their
heresy.
Let no one of you grow weary, or hear as a side-work; for it is God who
speaks. Hear, you shepherds, the voices of the Chief Shepherd; hear how he
unveils and triumphs over the hidden wolves; hear and take heed both to
yourselves, and to all the flock. Beware of the dogs, beware of the thieves.
For he who does not enter through the door into the court of the sheep, but
climbs up from elsewhere, is a thief and a robber. And again concerning them
he says: He who is not with me is against me. And again he was saying to them:
You are of your father the devil. Woe to you, because you shut the kingdom of
the heavens.
=59.557=
For you do not enter, nor do you allow those who are entering to enter. And
elsewhere again: You are not of my sheep. Do you see how everywhere the Master
pillories and triumphs over the impious, that we may not be deceived? O the
unspeakable condescension; O the incomparable goodness. What shall we repay to
the Lord for all these things? what shall we answer to the chief shepherd? O
shepherds and fellow-shepherds; for behold a few out of many. How everywhere
the Master, making us secure through the undefiled Gospels, cries: See, watch,
take heed, be eager, struggle? And he cries not only through the Gospels, but
also through the God-bearing prophets, and the theologian disciples he utters
the like things; for in all of them he speaks as he wills; and Paul bears me
witness saying: Of Christ who speaks in me. Come then, let us bring into the
midst the theologians, and let us hear from them what they command us
concerning heretics. Speak, blessed Peter, whom our Lord God Jesus Christ
blessed; speak to us concerning those who deceive and who are about to deceive
the flock of Christ, which the chief shepherd and bishop of our souls handed
over to you and entrusted; speak, as much as the Holy Spirit supplied to you,
and establish your flock; show us the hidden wolves, as your teacher Christ
the Lord. Peter said: This first know, that in the last of the days mockers
will come, walking according to their own desires. And again he says: And
among you there will be false teachers, who will bring in heresies of
destruction, even denying the Master who bought them. And many will follow
their lasciviousness; for whom the judgment does not idle, and their
destruction does not slumber; children of a curse, leaving the straight way.
These things Peter uttered, Peter truly the blessed, Peter the rock of the
faith, upon which Christ built the Church, Peter who holds the keys of the
heavens, Peter who walked on foot over waves, the warm lover of the Master
Christ, who threw down Simon the magician in Rome, as chief and front-rank man
the first robber and disciple of the


devil in the heresies. John said: Manifest are the children of the devil. Do
you see how in all these Christ speaks? For also in the Gospels he said the
like things to them, that You are of your father the devil. I know many saying
that God also fashioned them; for apart from God, they say, nothing came to
be, understanding neither the things they say, nor concerning the things they
affirm. That God created me, I too agree; but he created me for good works,
that we may follow in them, and become children of God through the right
faith.
δʹ. But let us return to the matter at hand. John says: And now many
antichrists have come to be; and again, Look to yourselves, that you may not
lose the things you have worked; because many deceivers have gone out into the
world. Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits whether it
is from God; because many false prophets have entered into the world. And
again he says: If anyone comes to you, and does not bring this teaching with
him, do not receive him into a house, and Do not say to him Hail. For he who
says hail to him shares in his evil works. And again, Everyone who
transgresses, and does not remain in the teaching of Christ, does not have
God. These things John exhorts us, John the son of thunder, who was loved
above all saints, who established the Church from the ends even to the ends of
the inhabited world, and stopped the mouths of the heretics by theology. James
said: Whoever seems to be a friend of them is established as an enemy of God.
Hear, all =59.558= who eat together with the heretics, a painful sentence;
that you are enemies of Christ. For neither can he who is friendly with the
enemies of the king be a friend of the king; but he is not even counted worthy
of life, but he will perish with the enemies, and will endure the worse
things. Judas of James said: Certain men have crept in who were long ago
written beforehand for this judgment, impious, changing the grace of our God
into lasciviousness, and denying the only Master and Lord Jesus Christ.
And again he says: In the last of the times there will be mockers, walking
according to their own desires of ungodlinesses. These are those who shepherd
themselves without fear, waterless clouds, carried about by every wind,
wandering stars, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept unto ages. These
things and more than these Judas the good exhorts us. Come then, Paul, the
vessel of election, speak to us yourself also according to the grace given you
from God, speak to us concerning the present evil age; make manifest the
hidden wolves, triumph over and pillory the thieves of the holy flock of God.
Paul said: I know that after my departure heavy wolves will enter in among
you, not sparing the flock. Do you see everywhere the theologians agreeing
with the Teacher concerning the godless heretics, calling them dogs and
wolves? as also elsewhere Paul says: Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil
workers, beware of the concision. See that there will not be anyone who
carries you off as spoil through philosophy and empty deceit. See accurately
how you walk, because the days are evil. Who will have an excuse of neglect,
hearing so many commands? And elsewhere again: Do not receive a heretical man
after a first and second admonition; and again, Do not be carried aside by
various and strange teachings; and again, Heretical men will proceed to the
worse, deceiving and being deceived; and elsewhere, To the defiled and
unbelieving nothing is clean. Hear again, you who make love-feasts with them:
how will you flee from the wrath that is coming upon you, you who are defiled
together with them in food, in drinks? how do you dare


to approach the divine and fearful mysteries of Christ? or did you not hear
the blessed Paul crying, that You cannot drink the cup of the Lord, and the
cup of demons; you cannot share the table of the Lord, and the table of
demons. Come out from the midst of them, and do not touch an unclean thing.
Shall we then put you to shame? or do we toil in vain and speak into the air?
Yet because of those who wish and are eager to hear the word and to do it, I
will not shrink, we will not stand off from the words of Paul, but again I
will say the same things: hear, Do not become unevenly yoked with unbelievers.
For what communion has light with darkness? Where are the bold-mouthed, who
say that these things have not been said in the divine Scriptures; Whose God
is the belly, and the glory in their shame, who mind the earthly things? These
things Paul exhorts and teaches and admonishes, Paul, the vessel of election,
the fence of the Church, the much-contending and noble, Paul the God-sounding
lyre, the herald of Christ, the writer of the doctrines, the trumpet of the
Word, the orator of piety, the net of the nations. And the blessed theologians
uttered these things and more than these concerning the godless and
unbelieving; and the prophets going before of old uttered the like things; for
it is necessary to bring these also into the midst. The prophet David said:
There is no truth in their mouth. And again he says: Lord, did I not hate
those who hate you, and was I not consumed over your enemies? With perfect
hatred I hated them, and they became enemies to me. And Solomon says, that
Those acting impiously pretend piety. And again he says: Son, let not =59.559=
impious men deceive you, nor go in a way with them. And Isaiah the prophet, or
rather the Lord through the prophet, says: I have begotten sons, and I have
raised them up, but they have set me aside; and again, There is no rejoicing
for the impious, says the Lord.
εʹ. Are these things enough? or shall I bring into the midst also all the
prophets? But even these are enough for those who wish to hear. For he who
does not attend to the things said will not be persuaded even by more. But let
us still linger a little on the words of the prophet David, and let us see how
he pillories and triumphs over the deceit hidden in them, and says: There is
no truth in their mouth; their heart is vain, and the rest. And see the
understanding of the prophet, how he publishes and unveils, triumphing over
the evil-doctrined, that we may not be deceived. Hear, you orthodox, and do
not condescend to the heretics; hear, shepherds, and shudder, and do not be
silent, but preach the word; do not give place to the devil, do not give
hunting to the wolves. Imitate the blessed apostle Peter, how in Rome, when
the thrice-accursed Simon was blaspheming, and saying that he himself was the
power of God, he was not silent even for an hour, nor did he delay, but having
refuted him, and shown him a liar, and a robber and an opponent of God, having
thrown him down he handed him over to destruction?
And likewise also his son, or rather the devil's, Montanus, the defiled and
unclean and godless, with the two adulteresses, the Apostle with much
eagerness having refuted, and having shown him an opponent of God and a false
christ and a false prophet, muzzled him, and stopped his defiled mouth in the
name of Christ, not being long-suffering, not delaying over this man's
blasphemy. So then do you also, shepherds, and do not share in the unclean
works of darkness; but rather even refute, just as also the apostles and David
the ancestor of God laid down many toils concerning them, and many struggles,
refuting and rebuking, pillorying them and meeting God against them, and
saying: How long shall sinners, Lord, how long shall sinners boast? Scatter
them in your


power. Give them, Lord, according to their works, because they did not
understand your works, Lord. Lord, in your city you will bring their image to
nothing. And again David entreats, bringing prayers to the Master, that he
himself coming down by himself may do his request, crying and saying: Lord,
bow the heavens, and come down; Lord, do not delay. Let your compassions
quickly overtake us, Lord. And what of the man-loving God, who wills all men
to be saved, and to come to knowledge of truth, because he is near to all who
call upon him in truth? He did not refuse to hear nor overlook the prayer of
the saints, but He bowed the heavens, and came down, and economized all things
toward the salvation of our race, and showed all things, doing and teaching.
Then wishing to teach, that those about to preside over the Churches should
thus drive out the heretics, he made a whip from cords, and having entered he
cast all out of the temple, and thrust them away, and drove them out, saying:
My house is a house of prayer; but you have made it a cave of robbers. Hear,
you who preside over the Churches. For he showed you the good, that you may
follow his traces, taking heed from every side accurately, and driving out the
wolves, and guarding the flock. Then having cast all outside, that is, those
who mind the contrary things, and finally also having foretold their
desolation and disappearance, which is about to be from generation to
generation for those who mind the contrary things, saying: Behold, your house
is left desolate. See how the words became a deed; for the enemies and
plotters of the Church, that is, the heretics, in each generation are handed
over to destruction, according to the word of the Lord, which he has said,
that Every plant which my Father has not planted shall be rooted out; which
also has come to pass. For he himself first did this and showed it; and after
his taking up into the heavens the blessed apostles arrayed themselves against
them. And after these, their divine teachings and the teachers of the Church,
and the holy synods that came to be according to times, having rooted these
out as they were unrepentant, handed them over to destruction according to
what is writ =59.560= ten, that You will destroy all who speak the lie.
Therefore all failed and perished because of their lawlessness. For where are
those who once warred against the Church, kings and rulers and wise men? were
they not scattered and did they not perish, and become into nothing?
ςʹ. For where is the swagger and the boldness of the Jews? where is Simon the
magician, the first in heresy, the disciple of the antichrist and forerunner?
where is this man's evil offspring, and the successor of his madness and
lasciviousness, Montanus, the leader of evils with the two adulteresses, and
their so-called mysteries, things worthy of deep silence, the abominable and
unclean, concerning which the Apostle also was saying: For the things done by
them in secret, it is shameful even to speak? Where is Marcion, where Valens,
where Manes, where Basilides, where Nero, where Julian, where Arius, where
Nestorius, where are all who array themselves against the truth, concerning
whom the Church was crying, that Many dogs have encircled me? Have they not
all perished? For they were scattered because of their blasphemy, and they
were driven out as wolves. For they found the antagonists and noble warriors
and shepherds in truth, the blessed men who then presided over the Churches.
But I see much difference of the shepherds then compared with those now. Those
were warriors, these are fugitives; those were beautifiers of books and of
doctrines, these of garments and of trinkets. These as hirelings let the sheep
go, and flee; those laid down their soul for the sheep, having imitated the
good shepherd. O the


blessed men of theirs, whose names are in the book of life; whom demons
shuddered at, and heretics trembled; and The mouth of those who speak unjust
things was stopped. I too then will utter things near to David, who lamenting
said: Where are your ancient mercies, Lord? I too will say in tears: Where is
that blessed choir of bishops and teachers, who shone as luminaries in the
world, holding the word of life? And what prevents us from bringing these also
into the midst, even if few upon many? for even to remember them is a
sanctification of the soul. Where is Evodius the sweet savor of the Church,
and successor and imitator of the holy apostles? where is Ignatius the
dwelling-place of God? where is Dionysius the bird of heaven? where is
Hippolytus the sweetest and most well-minded? where is Basil the great, and
all but equal in weight to the apostles? where is Athanasius the holy, and
greedy of the virtues? where is Gregory the second theologian, and invincible
soldier of Christ, and his namesake? where is Ephraim the abundant, the
consolation of the downcast, the training of the young, the hand-leading of
those who repent, the sword against the heretics, the receptacle of the
Spirit, the vessel of the virtues? Do you see how great the interval, and how
great a difference of those blessed and holy men compared with those now? I
know also other God-bearing teachers, but it is enough for the present at
least. Those laid down their soul, as was said before, for the sheep; but
these having left the sheep fled; those were powerful both in word and in
deed; but these both in money and in possessions and of horses and mules, and
of fields and flocks, and of cooks and a splendid table. Much is the discourse
among them concerning these things each day and night; but concerning the
rational flock there is no discourse, concerning which they will be required
to give a word on the great day of judgment. Then if someone asks them
concerning books, they answer saying: I am poor, and I do not have means to
acquire books. Then they come forward not as poor, but wearing gleaming
garments, possessing fat purses, and necks like those of stall-fed bulls,
dragging a multitude of disciples, or rather of cooks; the things it is
shameful even to speak, for from the excess of wealth they have acquired women
brought in on the pretext of slaves. O deep shame; O the evil plenty! O bitter
love of money! O the unfilled belly! Then from there scandals, whisperings,
reproaches, revilings, tumults. Then being accused they answer: I wrong no
one, I have authority over my own money. Then if some one of the godless
heretics is out of his mind speaking twisted things, there is no one who
contradicts, the one who wars is nowhere; all become poor then, all silent,
all fugitives. O the evil root of all evils, of the love of mon =59.561= ey!
Do you wish to be saved from wealth? But It is easier for a camel to enter
through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man into the kingdom of God.
Feasting, being drunk, and being puffed up, do you wish to conquer the
heresies? But woe to you, who feast and are puffed up, beautifying yourselves
in gold and in varied garments; how will you show to others the good poverty
of Christ, who became poor for us, who commanded his disciples not to have
copper in their belts. Truly you err, not knowing the Scriptures. Do you not
hear the Lord saying, that Blessed are the poor; and again, Woe to you who are
rich; and, Do not treasure up upon the earth. Your wealth has been multiplied,
and your word has failed; your garments have become moth-eaten; concerning
which you will give a word to the chief shepherd Christ. For you are not
ignorant that each of us will give a word concerning himself to God; but you
both for yourselves and for the sheep, high priests and priests, and deacons,
each as he was entrusted. But see, do not forget the talents. Take heed to
yourselves and to all the
