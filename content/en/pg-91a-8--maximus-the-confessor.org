Then having conversed a little toward one another concerning the life
according to God and the keeping of the divine commandments, Theodosius the
bishop, turning toward abba Maximus, said:
THEOD. Behold, all the scandals have been dissolved, and peace has come to be
through God and will come to be; but for the Lord's sake, do not hide me; do
you not say in any manner whatsoever one will and one operation upon Christ?
MAX. It is not possible for me to say this ever. And I say the cause: because
the voice is foreign to the holy Fathers, to say of two natures one will and
operation. Then also, through every manner, the one who says this finds the
absurdity meeting him straight on. For if I say natural, I fear the confusion;
if I say hypostatic, I divide the Son from the Father and the Spirit, and I
shall appear introducing three wills, not agreeing with one another, just as
also the hypostases; if I say the one will and operation as of one, I am
compelled to say also the will and operation as of one of the Father, and as
of one of the Spirit, even if I do not will, and the word will be found
falling into polytheism; if I say relative, I introduce the personal division
of Nestorius; if I say contrary to nature, I destroy the existence of the one
who wills; for corruption to nature is what is contrary to nature, as the
Fathers have said.

THEOD. We ought in every way and altogether to say one will on account of the
union of our Savior, just as Sergius and Pyrrhus, as I think, having
understood well, have written.
MAX. If you command, master, receive from me about this two (≡15Γ_044≡> words.
If on account of the union one will of our God and Savior has come to be, just
as Sergius and Pyrrhus and Paul have written, the Son will be, according to
them, of another will than the Father, on account of the nature, but not on
account of the union, the Father having one will according to the Son, if
indeed union and nature are not the same. If on account of the union one will
of our Savior has come to be according to them, it will altogether have the
union as its cause, but neither of the natures from which he is, and the will
according to them will clearly be of relation, but not of nature; for the word
of truth knows the union as relation, but not as nature. If on account of the
union, as they said, one will of our Savior has come to be, by what will do
they say that the union itself has come to be? For they would not, I suppose,
say it was by the one that came to be through it, if they care for truth and
flee the irrational. If on account of the union one will of the Savior has
come to be, it is clear that before the union he was either of many wills or
altogether without will. And if he was of many wills, having been reduced
toward one will, he underwent a lessening of the many, and he clearly received
a passion of change, the lessening of the many wills naturally present to him.
But if he was altogether without will, he declared the union to be better than
nature, from which he gained a will which nature lacked, and again he declared
himself changeable, having acquired by relation what was not present to him by
nature. If on account of the union one will of our Savior has come to be
according to one of the things from which he is, he has become God recently by
will, the same remaining by nature eternal through the union and


beginningless man by will, remaining by nature recent, which is impossible,
not to say impious. If on account of the union one will of the natures has
come to be, why then on account of the same cause has one nature of the
natures not come to be?

And having cut off at these things the course of the word, Theodosius the
bishop said:
(≡15Γ_046≡> THEOD. What then has come to be on account of the union, if none
of these has come to be through it?
MAX. It showed without falsehood that the one who is by nature God without
flesh has become incarnate, and it presented clearly the creator of all as
having become by nature man, not by change of nature or by lessening of
anything of nature, but by a true assumption of flesh intellectually ensouled,
that is, of unimpaired humanity, pure by nature of every ancestral charge. And
by the word according to exchange, the thing truly wonderful and astonishing
to all: the same wholly God in the human things, remaining entirely within his
own, wholly man in the divine things, remaining entirely without falling from
his own. For a perichoresis into one another of the natures and of the natural
things present to them has come to be, according to the teaching of our
God-speaking Fathers, but not a transference or lessening on account of the
union, which is proper to those who wickedly make the union a confusion, and
for this reason are variously mixed up in innovations, and through lack of
stability of the word according to them persecute the pious.

Hearing these things, Theodosius the bishop seemed, with the rest of those who
had come with him, to receive what was said. And again the same bishop says
toward abba Maximus:
THEOD. Do charity: What is that which you said to us, that no one operates as
someone the hypostasis, but as something the nature? For what was said goes
forward for me as I understand it.
MAX. No one operates as someone the hypostasis, but as something the nature;
for example Peter and Paul operate, but not in a Petrine and Pauline way, but
in a human way; for both are men naturally according to the common and
defining word of the nature, but not hypostatically according to the
individually qualified. Likewise Michael and Gabriel (≡15Γ_048≡> operate, but
not in a Michaelic and Gabrielic way, but in an angelic way; for both are
angels. And so upon every nature, predicated of many in number, we contemplate
the operation as common but not as sole. Therefore the one who says a
hypostatic operation introduces the nature itself, being one, as having become
infinite in operations, and differing from itself according to the multitude
of the individuals brought under it, which, if we receive it as holding well,
we destroy together for every nature the word upon it of how it is.
