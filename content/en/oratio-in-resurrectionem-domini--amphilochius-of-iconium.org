...a day of joy and gladness, beloved, a day of exultation and salvation, a
day of illumination and sanctification, a day of peace and reconciliation; a
day of the remaking and renewal of our souls, a day truly great and wondrous
and manifest. On this day Christ raised us up with himself, who had been cast
down by sin; on this day Christ made us alive together with himself, who had
been dead in our trespasses; on this day he opened paradise, that we might
enjoy the tree of life, which is his precious and life-giving body and 69
blood, through which we are cleansed and sanctified and illumined and made
new. For Christ gave himself as a ransom for us all, and he led us out from
death into life, from darkness into light, from slavery into freedom, and from
enmity into genuine friendship. He redeemed us from the curse and from sin by
becoming a curse for us, that we might receive adoption as sons, that we might
no longer be slaves but free, no longer subject to passions but free from
passion, no longer lovers of the world but lovers of God, no longer walking
according to the flesh but according to the Spirit. ... he sanctified, he
glorified us in the Holy Spirit, that we might walk in newness of life. But
what shall we render to the Lord for all these things that he has rendered to
us ... what shall we render equal to his surpassing gift and grace? ... let us
give thanks and fall down before the Lord and worship him, and let us offer
the myrrh and the hymns as gifts with fear and reverence. For the Master 70 is
a lover of mankind and a lover of the good, and he receives all things, even
if what we bring him is small and paltry. Let us too love, my brothers, him
who loved us by grace; let us die for him who died for us as a gift; let us
walk after his holy commandments; let us cleanse ourselves from every
defilement of flesh and spirit; let us offer to our God good works, faith,
love, hope, and endurance, a broken heart, the gift of compunction, purity of
conscience, the putting to death of the members that are on the earth,
fornication, uncleanness, passion, evil desire, and covetousness. Let us serve
the Lord with understanding and steadfastness and perseverance and patience.
Let us take to heart the love of the true God and our Savior. Let us sing him
a new song; let us all sing; let us clap our hands and shout to God with a
voice of exultation, for the Lord Most High is fearsome, a king over all the
earth ... great is the Lord, and of his greatness there is no end; great is
the Lord, and great is his strength. He has abolished and struck down our
boastful and arrogant enemy, the devil, and he has trampled down death and
raised all up, granting us eternal life. 71 ... let us all enjoy, beloved, the
good gifts of this fair festival. Let us enter rejoicing into the joy of our
Lord ... rulers and ruled ... elders with the young, men and women, rich and
poor, slaves and free: glorify and magnify the Lord our God, and hymn and
marvel at and honor this bright day and radiant feast, you who have fasted and
labored ... keep the feast today. Rich and poor, dance with one another; the
temperate and the slack, honor the day; those who have fasted and those who
have not, be glad today. The table is full; let no one go away hungry and
lacking. All of you enjoy the wealth of his kindness, for Christ our Passover
has been sacrificed for us. All of you draw from the inexhaustible fountain of
God the Savior and

be glad. Taste and see that Christ is the Lord. The Lord is God, and he has
appeared to us. Let no one lament poverty, for the common kingdom has
appeared. Let no one mourn his faults, for pardon has dawned from the tomb.
Let no one fear death, 72 for the Savior's death has set us free. For Christ
our God has saved us and delivered us from Hades. He snatched us from the
tyranny of the devil and freed us from his slavery. Hades was embittered when
it tasted the precious and holy flesh of the Master, as Isaiah foretold: Hades
was embittered when it met you below. It was embittered, for it was abolished.
It was embittered, for it was mocked. It was embittered, for it was bound. It
took a body and encountered God. It took what it saw and fell upon what it did
not see. Death swallowed in ignorance, and having swallowed it knew whom it
had swallowed. It swallowed life, and was swallowed by life. It swallowed the
one with all, and lost the one for the sake of all. It seized like a lion, but
its teeth were crushed. Death had its power through sin; but finding no sin in
Christ it remained powerless. So it is timely to say: Where, O death, is your
sting? Where, O Hades, is your victory? Christ is risen, and you are cast
down. Christ is risen, and the angels rejoice. Christ is risen, and the demons
have fallen. 73 Christ is risen, and we have been redeemed from corruption.
Christ is risen, and we have been delivered from the curse. Christ is risen,
and we have risen with him. Christ is risen, and we have been made alive.
Christ, risen from the dead, has become the firstfruits of those who have
fallen asleep. To him be the glory and the power and the worship and the
majesty, now and always and unto the ages of ages. Amen.

Oration on the Resurrection of the Lord
Bright and great and wondrous is the present day, and a day truly of joy and
gladness and rest and spiritual delight and exultation, a day of freedom and
illumination and sanctification, a day of reconciliation and peace and
salvation and help for our souls. On this day Christ raised us up with
himself, who had been cast down by sin; on this day he made alive those who
had been dead in trespasses; on this day he opened to us the paradise which
the disobedience and transgression of our first parents had closed; on this
day he counted us worthy to enjoy his precious and life-giving body and blood,
through which we are cleansed and illumined and sanctified. For Christ gave
himself as a ransom for us, and he led us out from death into life, from
darkness into light, from slavery into freedom, from enmity into genuine
friendship. «He redeemed us from the curse « and from sin «by becoming a curse
for us « that we might receive adoption as sons, that we might no longer be
slaves but free, no longer subject to passions but free from passion, no
longer lovers of the world but lovers of God, no longer «walking according to
the flesh but according to the Spirit «. He sanctified and glorified us in the
Holy Spirit «that we might walk in newness of life «. «What shall we render to
the Lord for all that he has rendered to us «, what shall we render to the
surpassing gift and grace of God? Let us give thanks and worship and offer him
the myrrh and the hymns as gifts with fear and reverence and humility. For the
Master is a lover of mankind and compassionate, and he receives all things,
even if what we bring him is small and paltry. Let us love, my brothers, the
Lord who loved us by grace; let us die for him who died for us as a gift;

rulers and ruled, slaves and free, men and women, elders and younger. Glorify
the Lord our God, and hymn and magnify and marvel at this bright day; those
who have fasted and those who have not, be glad today; the temperate and the

proclaimed beforehand, saying «Hades « «was embittered when it met you below
