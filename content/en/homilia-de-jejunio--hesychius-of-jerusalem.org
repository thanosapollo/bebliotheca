* Homily on Fasting
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of Hesychius, presbyter of Jerusalem, on fasting.
1 Fasting according to God, performed with reverence and holy prayers and
almsgiving, is the root and foundation of the devout. For God does not
desire abstinence from bread and water, but abstinence from wicked deeds:
"For it is not what goes into the mouth that defiles a man, but what comes
out of the mouth, that defiles a man." Let us fast, beloved, a fast
fitting to God, of which the Lord says: "But when you fast, anoint your
head" <through> good works and almsgiving, "and wash <your> face,"
stripping away all the filth of sin. There is a blameworthy fast, and
there is a praiseworthy fast. Many who have fasted have committed murders,
and many who have fasted have saved others.
2 The Ninevites fasted and were saved, and through the fast they turned
aside the wrath of God. And the fast stopped a sentence that had been brought
out against them; for the prophet says: "Yet three days, and Nineveh shall
be overthrown." He did not say "after three days Nineveh shall be
overthrown"; for if he had said "after three days," it would have been
overthrown, but "yet three days," showing this: "I have borne with you so
long a time, and you have not repented. Yet three days I bear with you,
and if you do not repent in the three days, then the city shall be
overthrown." And for so long a time they did not repent, except through
the three days, that is, through the Father and the Son and the Holy
Spirit.
3 The Jews fasted and took counsel that they might kill the apostle Paul.
Jezebel proclaimed a fast and killed Naboth and took the poor man's
vineyard. A fast and a murder: what sort of fast is this? Daniel fasted
when he was thrown into the den of lions; for when he had been thrown into
the den, he cri<ed> to Christ, saying: "Out of the dep<ths> I have cri<ed
to you, Lord;> Lord, hear my prayer." And seeing the lions on the right
and on the left, he cried out again, saying: "Do not deliver to the beasts
a soul that confesses you." See that the Lord does not abandon those who
hope in him. The earthly king sealed the den, but the heavenly King sealed
the mouths of the lions. The fire fasted in the furnace and did not use
the fellow servants for food, but having fasted it leapt out of the
furnace, yielding place to the three because of the Fourth.
4 The whale fasted in the sea when it received Jonah in the storehouses
of its belly; for having the prophet as a deposit, it displayed the sign
of fasting. For becoming a ship in place of a ship for the shipwrecked
man, it carried the stranger through sea and deep and great fear, and,
that it might not destroy the stranger in the storehouses of its belly, it
restored the deposit intact, handing it over to the Master who had
entrusted it. David fasted, and through the fast and repentance God
forgave him the sin concerning Uriah's wife. Elijah fasted forty days and
received the keys of the heavens, and when he wished he held back the
rain, and when he wished he brought it by fasts and prayers.
5 Our Savior fast<ed> forty days and put the devil to shame, he who did
no sin, nor was deceit found in his mouth. And he fasted, giving us a
pattern that through fasts we can obtain the propitiation of

sins. Let us therefore fast, beloved, a pure fast, of which the apostle
James says that "this is pure religion: to visit" brothers "in their
affliction, to keep oneself unspotted from the world." Jesus the son of
Nun fasted and commanded the sun and the moon.
6 The apostles, having nothing on earth, received authority to bind and
to loose, and in them is fulfilled what was said: "as having nothing and
possessing all things." For Peter, he says, and John went up to the temple
at about the ninth hour to pray. And a certain man lame from his mother's
womb was sitting, who had never walked. This man expected to receive
something from the apostles. And Peter answered him, saying: "What you
expect to receive I do not have, but what you do not expect to receive I
have. You expect to receive silver; I give you health. For health of body
is better than boundless wealth. I will confess the truth to you: 'Silver
and gold I have none.' For Christ delivered to us, saying: 'Do not acquire
gold or silver or copper.' And he gave us authority, saying: 'Cast out
demons, heal the sick, cleanse lepers. Freely you received, freely give.'
'In the name of Jesus Christ, rise and walk.'" And taking him "by the
right hand he raised him." The servant raises by taking hold; the Master
heals by a word: "For a servant is not above his lord."
7 But as healing-places are being opened throughout the inhabited world,
today I see also one physician set over all, Christ. O how novel a wonder.
Different passions, in different places of healing, are healed by one
physician. One, held elsewhere by a wound of sin, comes forward and,
having received from the fast a plaster of healing, goes away. Another,
elsewhere reeking with the rot of fornication, receives through it from
Christ, the physician of souls and bodies, a medicine that heals the
stench. Another, burning with the fever of love of money, obtains from the
fast herbs that cool the disease.
8 Fasting also knows how to make barren women pregnant in short order, and
to make the fruitless womb a fruitful field, and to give prophets as sons,
the wage of abstinence. For what are we taught from the divine Scripture?
"And Hannah was downcast," it says, "and she wept and did not eat." But
fasting granted to the barren woman Samuel the prophet. You have seen the
gifts of fasting. You have seen what fruits it sprouts for those who need
it, how it dries up the springs of fornication, and opens the wombs dried
by barrenness, and cuts out the thorns of sin that grow from thoughts, and
no longer yields the fruits of impiety. And fasting according to God can
do not only these things, but it also makes the savage gentle, and turns
the unfeeling toward compassion, forging and softening the hardness of the
unyielding.
9 This is useful not only for sinners, but already also for the righteous
at a fitting season, as we have shown before. Moses, having fasted forty
days and forty nights, entered into that darkness, and saw God as it has
happened to no man, according to God's own testimony concerning him,
saying: "If there be a prophet among you for the Lord, I will make myself
known to him in a vision, and I will speak to him in sleep. Not so my
servant Moses, mouth to mouth," face to face, "as if a man should speak to
his own friend." This exceptional vision fasting has brought as a gift to
the prophet, and it changed the face of the one who fasted into a more
divine flash of unapproachable light, and it has given the prophet tablets
written with the finger of God.
10 Let us therefore come to it with a bright face, greet it with cheerful
souls, and not abstain from foods while we cling to

sins; nor, showing ourselves humble of face before men, appear proud of
soul before the Master; and giving to the poor, let us not plunder others;
and while flattering God with words, let us not in deed slaughter our
brothers, always remembering the apostle who says: "Pursue peace with all,
and the holiness without which no one shall see the Lord." But fasting
from all evils together with foods, let us say to him: "You have mercy on
all, because you can do all things, and you overlook the sins of men unto
repentance"; because to you belongs all glory, to the Father and to the
Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages.
Amen.
