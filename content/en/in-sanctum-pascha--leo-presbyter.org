All things are signs of the Master's resurrection, both in the bodily feast
and in the soul's salvation: deceit has ceased, envy is banished, fighting is
driven off, enmity is trampled down, war is dissolved, peace is honored,
greeting is multiplied, love is recognized, affection is intertwined, the
saying that declares has been fulfilled: Behold now, what is good or what is
pleasant but that brothers dwell together, and that they be in mind what they
are by nature.

Truly the present things are signs of the Master's resurrection. Why? Because
no longer do we mourn Adam, but we glorify Christ; no longer do we reproach
Eve, but we bless the virgin Mary; no longer do we turn away from the tree,
but we bear the cross; no longer do we fear the serpent, but we entreat the
Holy Spirit; no longer do we go away into earth, but we go up into the
heavens; no longer do we come to be outside paradise, but we lodge in
Abraham's bosom; no longer do we hear in Jewish fashion: I have likened your
mother to night; my people became as one not having knowledge, but we sing
spiritually: This is the day which the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be
glad in it. Why? Because no longer is the sun darkened, but all things are
enlightened; because no longer is the veil rent, but the church is
established; no longer do we carry palm branches, but we lead about the newly
illuminated. This is the day which the Lord has made. This one, and not these;
for there is one queen and not many tyrannies. This is the day, the one named
for the Lord, the trophy-bearer, the exclaimer of the resurrection, the
beautifier of grace, the feast-giver of the faithful, the divider of the
rational lamb, the milk-giver of those born again, the rest-giver of those who
toil. This is the day which the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in
it, not running to taverns but hastening to the martyr-shrines, not honoring
drunkenness but loving measure, not leaping in Jewish fashion but feasting in
apostolic fashion, not playing in the marketplaces but singing in the houses.

For the present day is of resurrection, not of wantonness; no one dancing goes
up into the heavens, no one drunk stands before a king. Let none of you grieve
this day, but let him sing in a godly way; for on this day Adam was set free,
Eve was released from sorrow, humanity was redeemed from pain. For today our
Master Christ, having risen from the dead by night, appeared before all to
Mary Magdalene and the other Mary and said to them: Rejoice, and through you
the whole race. Rejoice, the Lord to the women. But some will surely say: And
why, when the Lord was raised from the dead, was he not seen first by the
apostles, but by the women, and to these he said: Rejoice? Why? Because grief
blossomed through a woman, the Lord again made joy sprout through a woman,
that the saying that declares might be fulfilled: Where sin abounded, grace
overflowed still more. But when the Lord had been crucified and had kept
company with the tomb for us, not for himself, all the apostles, procuring
their safety by flight, were scattered as sheep not having a shepherd; but
these women, keeping watch in fear and spending the whole night waiting for
the Savior, whence until today the race of women is fond of all-night vigils.
Since, then, these remained at the tomb, the Lord necessarily, meeting them,
said: Rejoice, because you also wept; for those who sow in tears will reap in
gladness. Rejoice, recognize the voice; for I have changed my appearance, not
according to the account of existence, but according to that of clothing.
Rejoice, you women; for there a military watch beats at the tomb, not seeing
the one in the tomb; they hold the seal, having lost the treasure; they sow
silver that they may reap the lie. The children of the Jews thought me dead,
the raiser of the dead. Let us learn a dead man's power. For you all know, you
genuine sons of the resurrection, you living luminaries of the world, you who
hold the word of life, you city-rulers of the Jerusalem above, that after the
Lord's voluntary burial the chief priests and the Pharisees came to Pilate and
said to him: Sir, we remember that that deceiver said while still living:
After three days I rise. Now therefore command that the tomb be made secure
until the third day. And Pilate said to them: You have a military watch; do as
you know. And the Jews went and, it says, made the tomb secure, sealing the
stone with the watch. O fear of a dead man, O trembling at a tomb. The living
have feared the dead man; the Jews who turn away from the Lord now sit beside
the Lord's tomb; they stationed soldiers at the monument; unwillingly they
confess that the one laid there was a king; for soldiers are a king's guards.
But O Pharisees, if Master Christ was a deceiver, why have you feared the
deceiver? Why do you hire soldiers for help? Who ever makes war on a dead man?
Who ever arrays himself against a tomb? If, O Jews, Master Christ feared the
seal and the stone and the military watch, he who lay below in the body yet
was nowhere absent in the Godhead, and did not rise from the dead, you do well
to call him a deceiver. But if he rose, as he said, why do you entreat Pilate?
Do you not rather consider the scriptures? For did the Lord say only in the
incarnation that After three days I rise? Hear him, O Jews, that many years
before, through the prophet Zephaniah, he made known in advance and foretold
the resurrection that was to come, that you may know that the matter is not
new, but the prize is ancient. Hear him saying to the assembly of the nations
through Zephaniah the prophet; for it is good at the resurrection to set forth
the things of the resurrection. What then does the Lord say to the assembly of
the nations? To the Jewish assembly he has given a bill of divorce, but to the
Gentile one he has dictated a dowry of grace. Wait for me, says the Lord,
until the day of my resurrection for a witness, because my light is for an
assembly of nations. What will you say to these things, O Jew, you who hold
the letter and kick against the spirit? Who says, Wait for me until the day of
my resurrection? Was it David, or was this said of David? But what is said is
false; for these things were spoken after David; and besides, David was king
of the Israelites, not a leader of the nations. And if the children of the
Jews contend (for their tribe is warlike) that this was said of David, let
them hear that not only were these things not prophesied of David, but David
himself also, singing many years before of the Lord's resurrection in the
flesh, said: His soul was not left in Hades, nor did his flesh see corruption.

If these things have been said truly, O Jews, why do you hire soldiers that
you may steal the resurrection? Who shuts the sun up in a tomb? Hear him, O
children of the Jews, through Isaiah the prophet reproaching you and your
father the devil and trumpeting this resurrection besides, and saying thus:
Now I will arise, says the Lord, now I will be glorified, now I will be
exalted. Now you will fear. Vain is the strength of your father. And who is
the father of the Jews? The devil. And who is witness of this? The Lord
himself, saying to them face to face that You are of the devil, and your
father is Satan. Vain is the strength of your father, and the word is true;
for that one planted a tree of curse, I carpenter a cross of blessing; that
one hewed a tomb, I work it into an altar; that one left me to Jews for death,
I choke him all the sooner. Truly vain is the strength of their father, the
devil; for already even today behold how many children he has been deprived
of, I mean the newly illuminated who are present; for their brightness is
<his darkness>, their feast his nausea; <these are in church,> that one in a
waste;
these with angels, that one with swine; from where he fell, these were tossed
up and there they were rooted; those once slaves now free, and he who was once
an archangel now a devil; those once enemies now friends, and he who was once
a friend now condemned; those once poor now rich, and he who was once rich now
a rag-wearer; those once without glory now glorious, and he who was once
glorious now in iron fetters; those once captives now redeemed, and he who
once took captive now holding no one. And that the newly illuminated were
drawn as from captivity by the oversight of King Christ, hear the Holy Spirit
saying through the prophet David concerning these newly illuminated from many
years past: Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom he redeemed from the
hand of enemies, and gathered them from the lands, from east and west and
north and sea. The things said are not false; for from every race and every
tribe and land the net of the font has taken these rational fish alive through
the fishermen apostles. For such is the craft of Christ's fishermen: they do
not set linen nets, but they take alive by faith. Let the redeemed of the Lord
say so, from the hand of the enemy, the tares-sowing devil, who sowed and did
not reap; for the desert has become paradise, no longer putting forth nettles
of enemies, but blossoming newly illuminated lilies. For Isaiah too, seeing
these many years before, said: Let the desert of the Jordan rejoice and let it
blossom as a lily. And why are the newly illuminated also called lilies?
Because of the white of the garment without, and the gold-gleam of faith
within. Let the redeemed of the Lord say so. What should they say? Blessed are
they whose iniquities have been forgiven and whose sins have been covered. The
snare was broken and we were delivered. Let the redeemed of the Lord say so: I
have put off my tunic, how shall I put it on? Let my soul rejoice in the Lord;
for he has clothed me with a garment of salvation and has put about me a tunic
of gladness. What garment? The clothing of faith. And a tunic of gladness,
which? As many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ, that
is, the confession of the undivided Trinity. Let the redeemed of the Lord say
so. Let them say and not be silent, let them not send the benefaction away
into forgetfulness. Let the redeemed of the Lord say so: One Lord, one faith,
one baptism, one God who is over all and through all and in all.

Let them not say one thing below in the font and babble another above. As you
were taught, O newly illuminated, worship; as you were baptized, glorify; as
you were born again, believe and grow; what you were entrusted with, keep;
what you put on, do not tear apart; what you acquired, <do not> sell; you were
born again as an eagle, seek the things above; you came out of Sodom, do not
turn back, lest you become a pillar of salt like Lot's wife; you were called a
child, do not imitate Absalom; you became a disciple, do not fall in with
Judas; you entered the ark, do not draw near to the raven; you were freed from
Egypt, do not make a god of a bull-head; you were redeemed from demons, do not
suppose the stars to be holy; you were counted worthy of freedom, do not
become a slave of pleasures. You put off the old man and put on the new, who
is Christ; keep your soul and body safely from defilements; you were pulled
from the nations, do not worship the sun; for the sun is for you, not you for
the sun. Let the redeemed of the Lord say so. What should they say? In the
beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. These
things John the evangelist has just now set before us, the tracker of the
heavenly things, the unharmed pillar of the Holy Spirit, the only one who
dared to search out the Father's bosom, who dissolved Hellenism, who made
Judaism vanish, who tore every heresy as a spider's web, who bore so great a
height on a clay tongue, who leaned on the Lord's breast as on an ever-flowing
spring and from there drew the stream of the knowledge of God with the bucket
of faith. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the
Word was God. Hear with understanding. Everywhere the "was", nowhere the
"became" or "was created"; everywhere God, ******** everywhere maker, nowhere
that which is under authority. Whom should we obey? John the Theologian, or
Arius the blasphemy-speaker? I have no word toward Arius the weigher of the
Godhead. Let the redeemed of the Lord say so. What should they say? I have no
communion with Mar<a>thonius the cutter of the Holy Spirit. But on a good day
there is forgetfulness of evils; therefore, having driven every heretic today
also from the sacred precincts as a dog that bites in secret, let us obey our
forefather Jacob, who speaks and himself also contributes concerning the
Lord's resurrection thus: Having lain down he slept, he says, as a lion and as
a lion's cub; who shall raise him up? These things Jacob prophesied of our
Master Christ.

And why did Christ sleep as a lion? On the one hand because of the kingly
dignity, on the other because of the power of the resurrection; for just as no
one dares to wake a sleeping lion, unless he himself wakes himself, so also no
one raised the Master Christ from the dead, but he raised himself, as one may
hear him saying: I have authority to lay down my soul, and I have authority to
take it; and again: Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it.
Having lain down he slept as a lion, he says, since Christ is named a lion.
Why? Just as the bodily lion, when sleeping, has his eyes open (for this is
nature to the lion), so also our Master Christ, having slept according to the
account of the incarnation in the three-day period, did not close the eye of
the Godhead, because to him belongs honor and might, now and always and unto
the ages of ages. Amen.
