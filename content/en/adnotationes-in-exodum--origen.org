ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON THE EXODUS.
=17.16= CH. 15. Verse 25. There God set for him justifications and judgments,
and there he tried him. God tries for benefit. For he is untried of evils. But
for this reason he tries, that if they obey him, they may obtain goods, and be
delivered from evils. CH. 20. Verse 4. You shall not make for yourself an
idol. One thing is an idol, and another a likeness. For a likeness is, if you
make a likeness of a fish or a four-footed, or a beast, either through
craft-work or through painting; and an idol, as many things as a soul forming
makes from things not existing prototypically; for example, some mixed animal
from a human and a horse. Verse 4. As many as in the heaven above, and as many
as in the earth below, and as many as in the waters under the earth. You shall
not worship them, nor shall you serve them. One thing is to worship, and
another to serve. For he who from a whole soul is a slave to these not only
worships, but also serves; and he who pretends, and does through the customs,
does not serve, but worships. And you will find such in the Numbers granting
to women, and worshiping idols.
=17.17= CH. 28. Verse 30.
Since it is fitting for the high priest both in front and behind, as a shield,
to carry around the protective care, lest somewhere some contrary counsel of
the spirits of wickedness should wound the rational, and disturb the approved
of the judgments, and make the manifestation and the truth stand away by the
mixing of wickedness. CH. 31. Verse 1. And the Lord spoke to Moses. In all
forty days the all-wise God held Moses to give the law, though being able in
three days to give the Law. But that the time of Moses' absence might convict
the hidden of the impiety in the depth of the heart of the Jews.
