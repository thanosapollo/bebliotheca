* Syllogisms of the Holy Fathers
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1. Every evil is to be punished; but nothing that is to be punished is
incorruptible; therefore no evil is incorruptible.
2. Nothing corruptible is ungenerated; but evil is corruptible;
therefore evil is not ungenerated.
3. The divine Scriptures say that <not only the other wicked, but also>
the devil is delivered to punishment; but everything <that> is
delivered to punishment is changeable; and nothing changeable is
ungenerated; therefore none of the evils is ungenerated.
4. Nothing ungenerated is changeable; but evil is changed when overcome
by the good; therefore evil is not ungenerated.
5. Particular good and evil are changed; for they are not ungenerated.
But essential good is unchangeable. But evil, which the Manichaeans
say is essential, is changeable, if indeed they themselves also
confess that it is to be punished.
6. Nothing is evil according to essence, because evil is a quality; but
no quality is an essence. Therefore evil is not an essence.
7. All things contrary to one another are destructive; but ungenerated
things are incorruptible; therefore contrary things are not
ungenerated.
8. Everyone who reasonably desires something has the desire fulfilled of
that which he reasonably desires; but God desires to destroy
wickedness; therefore wickedness belongs to the corruptible; and
being of the corruptible it will vanish; and vanishing, it is not
ungenerated.
9. If evil is ungenerated, it belongs to it by nature to be evil; but no
one acting according to nature sins; therefore evil does not sin;
that which does not sin is not blameworthy; but Satan is blameworthy;
therefore he is not ungenerated.
10. Some say, if punishment transforms the wicked, it is good; but if
punishment is good, and the promise of good things is contrary to
this, and contraries cannot both be good at once, then the promise is
evil. <But God promises this; therefore it is not evil.> And the
promise being not of evil but of good, punishment is evil. And if
punishment is evil, then it is not God but Satan who inflicts it; but
he does not inflict the punishment on his own; for they are wicked as
he himself is, and it is necessary that he too, being wicked, fall
under the punishment. Therefore he will inflict it on the good; but
this is impossible; therefore it is not the wicked one who inflicts
it, but God. To these it must be said: everything inflicted by God is
inflicted usefully, being good or productive of good or preventive of
evil; for God, wishing to hinder the evil, inflicts this; but he
would not have attempted to hinder the evil, if it were not possible
for the evil to be hindered; and this being hindered cannot be by
nature and ungenerated.
11. Evil is found in activities; for it is evil that rational beings
handle the things according to nature not as they ought ...
12. The ungenerated is not desirous of any destruction, nor indeed
destructive, but evil is desirous of destruction; therefore evil is
not ungenerated.
13. Of things contrary through and through there is nothing in common, so
that of all necessity what belongs to the one does not belong to the
other; but it belongs to the good to be ungenerated; therefore
neither of these belongs to the evil. Therefore evil is not-being.
