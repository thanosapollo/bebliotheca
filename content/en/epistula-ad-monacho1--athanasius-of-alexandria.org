OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS ATHANASIUS.
To those practicing the monastic life, and established in the faith of God,
beloved and most longed-for brothers greeting in the Lord.
=26.1188= I give thanks to the Lord, who has given you to believe into him, that
with the saints you also may have the eternal life; and since there are some who
think the things of Arius, going around the monasteries, for no other thing
except that, as having come to you and turning back from us, they may deceive
the simple; and there are some who confirm they do not think the things of
Arius, but condescend, and pray with them upon the same; of necessity, some most
sincere brothers commanding, I have been eager to write to you, that keeping the
pious faith, which the grace of God works in you, purely and without guile, you
may not give a pretext of scandal to the brothers. For when some, having seen
you the faithful in Christ coming together with them and communicating,
altogether having supposed such a thing to be indifferent, will fall into the
mire of impiety. Therefore that this may not happen, be willing, beloved, to
turn away those who openly think the things of the impiety, and to guard those
who think they do not think the things of Arius, but communicate with the
impious; and especially those whose thought we turn away, these it is fitting to
flee from the communion. And if someone pretends to confess a right faith, but
appears communicating with those, urge such a one to keep from such a custom;
and if he promises, have such a one as a brother; and if he remains
contentiously, refuse such a one. For thus continuing you will keep the faith
clean; and those seeing you will be benefited, having feared lest they be
thought as impious and thinking their things.
