XI. A discourse of Saint John Chrysostom on the knowledge of God, and
on the holy Theophany, and on the newly illumined.
=64.43=
Just as those whose bodily sight is injured need guides, so that they
may not, from ignorance, fall into a pit and die; so also those who
have the eyes of the soul darkened need those who guide them toward
piety, so that they may not, from ignorance, fall into a depth of
evils and perish. And all humanity formerly was poured over with such
an evil of blindness; and just as it happens with those of dimmed
sight, that instead of what is they often see those that are not, so
also those who have the eyes of the soul darkened, instead of the one
and only God, saw many that are not. But the physician of bodies and
souls sent forth law and prophets, healing the blindness of men and
leading them to the knowledge of himself; but men turned away from the
healing through the law and the prophets. For they closed their eyes
so as not to know, and they stopped their ears so as not to hear,
according to the word of Isaiah, lest they should turn and be healed.
The like of these another prophet also, Jeremiah, cried out saying: We
have healed Babylon, and she was not healed; healing has not gone up
in her. And Babylon is interpreted confusion; for humanity has been
confused with blindness. But this is the manner of the best
physicians: the healing of chronic wounds surpasses the disciples. So
also the only-begotten Son of God, Jesus Christ, seeing that the
impiety of men was not healed through the law and the prophets, comes
from the heavenly vaults, born of the holy Virgin Mary, first routing
the devil through the cross, and second taking death captive, and
through his three-day resurrection granting peace to those near and
those far, he led all to the knowledge of himself. Where, O death, is
your sting? Blessedness is from God. And seeing this the prophet David
says: Blessed are they whose iniquities have been forgiven, and whose
sins have been covered. And who is able to forgive sins, except one,
God? For men blessing men benefit nothing, and often even harm, as the
prophet Isaiah says: My people, those who bless you lead you astray,
and they disturb the path of your feet; but he who blesses, together
with the word, also grants the grace. And take the proof of the word.
The Lord said to Peter, when he asked the disciples, saying: Whom do
men say that I am? Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah, for flesh and
blood has not revealed =64.44= it to you, but my Father who is in
heaven, not the one supposed to be earthly. You have spoken the
beatitude; give the grace of the kingdom of the heavens. Did you see
the grace following the word? And just now, in the beatitudes
recognized among us, we have heard each of them having the
corresponding grace. But let us return to the proposed subject of the
present feast. For the presence of an earthly king is accustomed to
grant joy and gladness to those present; but when a heavenly king
comes from the heavens to the earth here, not only we who are present
rejoice, but also all the earth is glad.
For if when the sun rises everything that lies beneath is illuminated,
when the sun of righteousness has risen, how shall not every soul be
illumined in this light-bearing night, concerning which the prophet
David, seeing by the Holy Spirit, said: For darkness shall not be
darkened from you, and night shall be illumined as day; and again: As
is its darkness, so also is its light. Do you see that he is relating
concerning the present night? For just as its darkness is seen by the
eyes of the flesh, so its light appears to the eyes of the soul. O
light-bearing and wondrous night, brighter than every day. Night
succeeds every day, but night does not succeed this light-bearing
night itself; For the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness
did not overtake it. Do not attend to the letter, but understand by
the spirit. The only-begotten Son of God is light; and night is the
works of darkness. When therefore you have illumined the soul that is
in darkness with the present light, the works of darkness can no
longer overtake you; for this is why the light came, that it might put
darkness to flight; life came, that it might abolish death; and first
he evangelizes shepherds through angels, not that they should set the
wonders upon words, but that, taking occasion as shepherds of the
rational sheep, they might proclaim the loving-kindness of God to the
peoples.
We have hastened to render the due worship in the holy place of
Bethlehem to the Master God of us all, who deigned to become man for
our sake. For he himself appeared upon the earth, having come down
from heaven for our salvation, not leaving those places there empty of
his presence; For I fill heaven and earth, says the Lord, says Isaiah;
and again David: If I go up into heaven, you are there; if I go down
into Hades, you are present. He has not yet become incarnate upon
earth, and already the prophet announces the presence in Hades. It is
no great thing, then, if we also, having traversed this small distance
from the city, have come to this place to worship =64.45= Let us go
again also to the Jordan, to see the mysteries accomplished in it, how
the sea saw its own Master and fled, And the Jordan turned back. Let
us see again also John baptizing the One who takes away the sin of the
world, and the Father from above bearing witness to the wondrous
condescension of his own Son, and the kindred Spirit descending in the
form of a dove and remaining upon the one who is witnessed. O wondrous
wonders of a wondrous king! How was the clay hand that touched the
head of the whole world not burned? Who has seen, or who has ever
heard, grass touching fire and not receiving harm from the fire. For
truly blessed, and thrice-blessed, is the =64.46= shoot of Zachariah
and Elizabeth. Blessed are we also henceforth, because we behold the
fruits of our loins shining forth from the holy font, like certain
stars running through the fullness of the Church.
But, O Father, and Word, and Spirit, the trihypostatic essence, and
power, and will, and energy, grant us who confess your unconfused and
undivided hypostases also to be counted worthy of standing at your
right hand, when you come from the heavens to judge the world in
righteousness; for to you belongs glory, honor, and worship, to the
Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and always, and unto
the ages of ages.
