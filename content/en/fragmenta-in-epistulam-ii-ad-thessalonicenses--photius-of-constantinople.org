* Fragments on the Second Epistle to the Thessalonians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

2 Thess 1:3 If one must give thanks concerning the things accomplished by
others, what should those suffer who envy those who accomplish them? 2 Thess
1:8 Either by fire of flame he means the form of the punishment, which those
who do not know God and have not obeyed the gospel of Christ will pay. Or by
fire of flame he indicates the Master's coming itself, that it will happen
with a burning fire. For so also those before us have understood it. 2 Thess
2:6 And now you know what restrains, he says, that he may not now be revealed,
he says, but in his own time, that is: whenever the time defined for him and
conceded to him by God arrives. And if he were not restrained, he would even
now have arrived. Thus he always rages against the faithful, and the evil one
desires their harm, but he is restrained and hindered. 2 Thess 3:11 The word
is still about the idle; and it fits also to be said about those who sin.
