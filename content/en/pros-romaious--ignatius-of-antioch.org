* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to the church that has received mercy
in the majesty of the Most High Father and of Jesus Christ his only Son,
loved and enlightened in the will of him who willed all things that are,
according to the love of Jesus Christ our God; which also presides in the
region of the Romans, worthy of God, worthy of honor, worthy of blessing,
worthy of praise, worthy of success, worthy of holiness, and presiding in
love, named after Christ, named after the Father, which I also greet in
the name of Jesus Christ, Son of the Father; to those united in flesh and
spirit to his every commandment, filled with the grace of God without
wavering, and filtered from every alien dye: abundant greeting in Jesus
Christ our God, without blame.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
Since I have prayed to God and obtained to see your faces worthy of God,
as I even asked to receive still more; for bound in Christ Jesus I hope
to greet you, if it is his will that I be counted worthy to reach the
end.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
For the beginning is well ordered, if I obtain grace to receive my lot
without hindrance. For I fear your love, lest it itself do me wrong. For
it is easy for you to do what you wish; but for me it is hard to attain
to God, unless you spare me.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
For I do not want you to please men, but to please God, as indeed you
do. For I shall never have such an opportunity to attain to God, nor
will you, if you keep silent, have a better work set down to your name.
For if you keep silent concerning me, I am a word of God; but if you
love my flesh, I shall again be a voice.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h2
:END:
Grant me nothing more than to be poured out as a libation to God, while
an altar is still ready, that becoming a choir in love you may sing to
the Father in Christ Jesus, that God has counted the bishop of Syria
worthy to be found in the West, having summoned him from the East. It is
good to set from the world toward God, that I may rise in him.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
You have never envied anyone; you have taught others. And I want those
things also to be firm which you command when you make disciples.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
Only ask power for me, both from within and from without, that I may not
only speak but also will, that I may not only be called a Christian but
also be found one. For if I am found one, I can also be called one, and
then be faithful, when I no longer appear to the world.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h3
:END:
Nothing that appears is good. For our God Jesus Christ, being in the
Father, is the more plainly seen. Christianity is not a work of
persuasion, but of greatness, when it is hated by the world.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
I write to all the churches, and I charge all, that I die willingly for
God, if only you do not hinder me. I beg you, do not become an untimely
kindness to me. Let me be food for the beasts, through whom it is
possible to attain to God. I am God's wheat, and I am ground by the
teeth of beasts, that I may be found pure bread of Christ.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h2
:END:
Rather, flatter the beasts, that they may become my tomb and leave
nothing of my body, lest when I have fallen asleep I become a burden to
anyone. Then I shall truly be a disciple of Jesus Christ, when the world
will not even see my body. Entreat Christ for me, that through these
instruments I may be found a sacrifice.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h3
:END:
I do not command you as Peter and Paul. They were apostles; I am a
condemned man. They were free; I am still a slave even now. But if I
suffer, I shall become a freedman of Jesus Christ, and I shall rise in
him free. Now I am learning, being bound, to desire nothing.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
From Syria as far as Rome I fight with beasts, by land and sea, night
and day, bound to ten leopards, that is, a company of soldiers; who even
when they are treated well become worse. But by their wrongs I become
more of a disciple; yet I am not justified by this.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
May I have joy of the beasts prepared for me, and I pray they may be
found ready for me. I will even flatter them to devour me quickly, not
as they have done with some, whom in cowardice they would not touch. And
if they themselves are unwilling and will not, I will force them.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h3
:END:
Pardon me; I know what is to my advantage. Now I am beginning to be a
disciple. May nothing of things visible and invisible envy me, that I
may attain to Jesus Christ. Let fire and cross and packs of beasts,
cuttings, rending, scatterings of bones, chopping of limbs, grindings of
the whole body, the evil torments of the devil come upon me, only that I
may attain to Jesus Christ.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
The ends of the world will profit me nothing, nor the kingdoms of this
age. It is good for me to die into Christ Jesus, rather than to reign
over the ends of the earth. Him I seek, who died for us; him I want, who
rose for us. My birth-pangs are upon me.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
Pardon me, brothers; do not hinder me from living, do not wish me to
die. Do not give to the world him who wants to belong to God, and do not
seduce him with matter. Let me receive the pure light; when I have come
there, I shall be a man.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h3
:END:
Allow me to be an imitator of the passion of my God. If anyone has him
in himself, let him understand what I want, and let him suffer with me,
knowing what constrains me.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
The ruler of this age wants to snatch me and to corrupt my mind toward
God. Let none of you who are present help him; rather become mine, that
is, God's. Do not speak of Jesus Christ and yet desire the world.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
Let no envy dwell among you. Even if I myself, when present, should
entreat you, do not be persuaded by me; rather be persuaded by these
things I write to you. For I write to you while living, longing to die.
My love has been crucified, and there is in me no fire that loves
matter; but living and speaking water is in me, saying within: Come to
the Father.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h3
:END:
I take no pleasure in food of corruption, nor in the pleasures of this
life. I want the bread of God, which is the flesh of Jesus Christ, of
the seed of David, and I want as drink his blood, which is love
incorruptible.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
I no longer want to live after the manner of men. And this will be, if
you will it. Will it, that you also may be willed.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
I ask you in a few lines: believe me. Jesus Christ will make these
things plain to you, that I speak truly: the mouth that does not lie, in
which the Father has spoken truly.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h3
:END:
Ask concerning me, that I may attain. I have not written to you
according to the flesh, but according to the mind of God. If I suffer,
you have willed it; if I am rejected, you have hated me.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
Remember in your prayer the church in Syria, which has God for its
shepherd in my place. Jesus Christ alone will oversee it, and your love.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
But I am ashamed to be called one of them; for I am not worthy, being
the last of them and an untimely birth; yet I have received mercy to be
someone, if I attain to God.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h3
:END:
My spirit greets you, and the love of the churches that received me in
the name of Jesus Christ, not as a passer-by. For even those that did
not lie on my way according to the flesh went before me from city to
city.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
Now I write these things to you from Smyrna through the Ephesians who
are worthy of blessing. And Crocus also is with me, along with many
others, a name dear to me.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h2
:END:
Concerning those who went before me from Syria to Rome for the glory of
God, I believe you have learned; make known to them also that I am near.
For they are all worthy of God and of you; and it is fitting for you to
refresh them in every way.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h3
:END:
I have written these things to you on the ninth before the kalends of
September. Farewell to the end in the endurance of Jesus Christ.
