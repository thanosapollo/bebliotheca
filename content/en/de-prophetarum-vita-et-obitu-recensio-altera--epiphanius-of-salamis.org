OF EPIPHANIUS CONCERNING THE 16 PROPHETS, WHENCE THEY WERE AND WHERE THEY WERE
PERFECTED.
1. Hosea son of Beeri was from Belemoth of the tribe of Issachar, and having
died he was buried in his own land in peace. And he gave a sign =56= that the
Lord would come upon the earth. If, he says, the oak that is in Shiloh be
split of itself into 12, and there become 12 oaks.
2. Amos was from Tekoa and was killed by the son of Amaziah, who struck him
with a sword upon the temple of the head. And still breathing he came into his
own land and after 3 days, having died, he was buried there.
3. Micah son of Ieramos, the Morasthite, was from the tribe of Ephraim, and
having done many things to Ahab he was killed by Joram his son by a precipice,
because he was rebuking him for the impieties of his fathers, and he was
buried in his own land.
4. Joel son of Bathuel was from the tribe of Reuben, from the field of
Theboron, and having died in peace he was buried there.
5. Obadiah was from the land of Shechem, he who was also the third captain of
fifty whom Elijah spared; and he went down to Ahaziah. And after these things,
leaving the service of the king, he prophesied; and having died he was buried
with his fathers.
6. Jonah son of Amittai was from the land of Kiriath-jearim near the city of
Azotus by the sea; and having been cast up from the sea-monster and having
gone away to Nineveh, and having returned he did not remain in his own land,
but taking his mother he sojourned in Sur, the country of the foreigners. For
he said that: thus I will take away my reproach, because I lied when I
prophesied against the city of Nineveh. And Elijah was then rebuking =57= Ahab
and, having called a famine upon the land, he fled. And coming he found the
widow with his son; for he could not remain with the uncircumcised. And he
blessed her, and Jonah her son, who had died, God raised again from the dead
through Elijah. For he wished to show him that one cannot flee from God. And
after the famine, having come in the land of Judah in Saar, and having died he
was buried there. And he gave a sign concerning Jerusalem, saying: when they
see a stone crying piteously, the end draws near; but when they see all the
nations in Jerusalem, then the city shall be razed to the ground.
7. Nahum son of Elkesaios was from Jesbe of the tribe of Simeon; and after
Jonah he gave a sign to Nineveh, that it would perish by waters and
underground fire. Which also came to pass; for the lake surrounding it
collapsed in an earthquake, and fire came out of the desert and burned the
higher part of it. And having died he was buried in his own land.
8. Habakkuk was from the field of Bidzochar, of the tribe of Simeon. And he
saw before the captivity concerning the capture of Jerusalem, and he mourned
exceedingly; and when Nebuchadnezzar came to Jerusalem, he fled to Ostracine,
and he was a sojourner in the land of Ishmael. But when the Chaldeans
returned, and the remnant who were in Jerusalem went down to Egypt, this man
was sojourning in his own land and ministering to the harvesters of his =58=
people. And when he took the food to carry it to the workers, he prophesied to
his own, saying: I am going to a far land, and I will come quickly. But if I
delay, carry their breakfast to the harvesters. And having come to be in
Babylon and having given the breakfast to Daniel, he stood by the harvesters
as they were eating, and he told no one what had happened. And from this he
understood that the people would return from Babylon more quickly. And two
years before the return, having died, he was buried there in the field. And he
gave a sign to those in Judea, that they would see light in the temple, and
thus they would see the glory of the temple. And concerning the consummation
of the temple he foretold that it would come about by a western nation, and
then the veil, he says, of the Debir will be torn into small pieces, and the
capitals of the two pillars will be taken away, and no one will know where
they will be; and these will be carried into the wilderness by an angel, where
in the beginning the tabernacle of witness was pitched, and to those at the
end the Lord will be made known.
9. Zephaniah, son of Cushi, was from the field of Sabaratha, of the tribe of
Simeon; and he prophesied concerning the city and concerning the end of the
nations and the shame of the ungodly; and having died he was buried in his
field.
10. Haggai came young from Babylon to Jerusalem and openly prophesied
concerning the return of the people, and he saw in part the building of the
temple. And having died he was buried before the tomb of the priests,
gloriously.
11. Zechariah son of Barachiah came from the Chaldeans to Jerusalem, already
advanced in years; and there he prophesied many things =59= to the people, and
he gave signs for proof. Who also said to Josedek that you will beget a son,
and he will serve as priest in Jerusalem, and he blessed Salathiel concerning
a son, and he laid on the name Zerubbabel; and he gave a sign to Cyrus for
victory and he foretold concerning his service, which he would perform in
Jerusalem, and he blessed him; and he prophesied concerning the end of the
nations, and of Israel, and of the temple and the idleness of prophets and
priests. And Joash king of Judah killed him between the temple and the altar,
as he was exhorting both him and the people to abstain from impiety and to
turn back to God, and the priests took him and buried him with his father. And
from then there came about many phantasmal signs in the temple. And the
priests were not able to see a vision of angels of God, nor to give oracles
from the Debir, nor to answer among the people through the lights as before.
12. Malachi is born after the return of the people in Sopha, who while young
had a life very good. And since all the people honored him as a holy man, they
called him Malachi, which is angel. For he was both fair in appearance and a
fulfiller for the people. For as many things as he said to them in prophecy,
on that =60= very day an angel of the Lord appearing confirmed them. And while
still young he was perfected and was buried in his field.
13. Isaiah son of Amos was from Jerusalem; and having been killed by Manasseh,
sawn in two, he was buried under the oak of Rogel, near the crossing of the
waters which Hezekiah destroyed, having blocked them up. And God made the sign
of Siloam on account of the prophet, because before dying, being faint, he
prayed to drink water, and immediately it was sent to him from it. And for
this reason it was called Siloam, which is Sent. And Hezekiah at the prayers
of this man made the pools and the reservoirs. For it was a little water that
had come out, when the people were in a siege of foreigners; and that the city
might not be destroyed as not having water, he did this. And so when the
enemies came to draw, water did not come; but when Jews came, the water also
came. Whence even until now the water comes out suddenly, that the mystery of
God may be shown. And since this came about through Isaiah, for the sake of
memory the people also buried him nearby gloriously, that through his prayers
they might likewise have the enjoyment of the water, because an oracle was
given them concerning this. And the tomb is next to the tomb of the kings,
behind the tomb of the priests toward the south side. For Solomon =61= made
the tombs of David, who had designed them, toward the east of Zion, which has
an entrance from Gibeon, twenty stades distant from the city, and he made a
winding construction not to be guessed at, and it is even until now unknown to
the many. There the king had the gold from Ethiopia and the spices. And since
Hezekiah showed to the nations the mystery of David and Solomon, and defiled
the bones of his fathers, for this reason God cursed his seed into slavery to
his enemies, and God made it barren from that day.
14. Jeremiah was from Anathoth and in Taphnas of Egypt, having been pelted
with stones by the people, he dies. And he lies near the house of Pharaoh,
because the Egyptians glorified him, having been benefited through him; for he
prayed, and the asps left them and the beasts of the waters, which the
Egyptians call Ephoth, and the Greeks crocodiles, which were putting them to
death, and when the prophet prayed the race of asps was prevented from that
land and likewise from the river the lurking of the beasts. And as many as are
faithful of God, until now they pray in the place where he lies, and taking of
the dust of the place they heal bites of asps, and they put to flight the
beasts of the water. And we heard from the children of Antigonus and Ptolemy,
certain old =62= men, that Alexander the Macedonian, having stood at the tomb
of the prophet, and having recognized the mysteries concerning him,
transferred his remains to Alexandria, wrapping them gloriously; and the race
of asps was prevented from the land, and likewise the beasts from the river.
This prophet gave a sign to the priests of Egypt, that their idols must be
shaken and their handmade things fall, when a virgin giving birth to a godlike
infant sets foot in Egypt. Wherefore even until now they honor a virgin in
childbed and, setting an infant in a manger, they worship. And to Ptolemy the
king inquiring the cause they said that: it is a mystery handed down from the
fathers to us, delivered by a holy prophet to our fathers, and we await the
end of our mystery. And this man before the capture of the temple snatched the
ark of the law and the things in it, and he made them to be swallowed in a
rock, saying to those standing by: the Lord has departed from Zion to heaven,
and he will come again in power. And let this be a sign to you of his
presence, when all the nations shall worship a tree. And this ark no one
brings out except Aaron; and the tablets in it no one of priests or prophets
unfolds except Moses the elect of God. And in the resurrection first the ark
of God will arise and will come out of the rock, and will be set on Mount
Sinai, and then all the saints will be gathered there, awaiting the Christ.
And in the rock he sealed =63= with his finger the name of God, and the type
became as an engraving of iron, and a cloud covered the name, and no one
understands the place until the consummation. And the rock is in the
wilderness where first the ark came to be, between the two mountains, in which
Moses and Aaron lie; and at night a cloud as fire comes about according to the
ancient type, because the glory of God shall not cease from his law. And God
gave grace to Jeremiah, that he himself should perform the end of his mystery,
so that he might become a partaker of Moses.
15. Ezekiel son of Buzi was in Sarira, of the priests; and a leader of Israel
killed him in the land of the Chaldeans during the captivity, being rebuked by
him over the worship of idols; they buried him there in the field of Maour, in
the tomb of Shem and Arphaxad, he having prophesied many things to those in
Judea. And the tomb is a double cave, because Abraham also in Hebron made the
tomb of Sarah after its likeness. And it is called double, because it is
spiral-shaped and a hidden upper story from ground level. And he gave a sign
to the people so as to attend to the river Chebar, and when it should fail, to
expect the sickle of desolation unto the end of the land, and when it should
flood, the return of Jerusalem. For there the holy man also dwelt, and many
were gathering to him, so that the Chaldeans feared they might rise up.
Wherefore he also came upon them for destruction; and he made the water stand,
that they might flee to the other side; and those of the enemies who dared
=64= to pursue were drowned. This man through prayer spontaneously furnished
them abundant food of fishes, and to many who were failing he granted life.
This man, when the people were being destroyed by the enemies, approached the
leaders, and they, having been frightened through wonders, ceased. Then he
said to them that: we have failed; our hope has perished, and by signs of the
bones of the dead he persuaded them that there will be hope for Israel, both
here and in the age to come. This man, being there, showed to the people of
Israel the things happening in Jerusalem and in the temple. This man was
snatched from there to Jerusalem for the rebuke of the unbelieving. This man
according to Moses saw the type of the temple, whose wall and surrounding wall
are broad, as Daniel said, that it will be built. This man judged in Babylon
the tribe of Gad, because they were impious toward the Lord, persecuting those
who kept the law, and he made the serpents consume the infants and all their
cattle, saying that: because of them the people will not return to their land.
From among whom he was also killed.
16. Daniel was from Bethoron the Upper of the tribe of Judah; of the race of
the most distinguished of the royal service, and while still an infant he went
away to the land of the Chaldeans. And he was a temperate man, so that the
Jews thought him to be a eunuch. And he mourned much over the city and
practiced in fasting =65= from every desirable food. And he prayed much on
behalf of Nebuchadnezzar, while Baltasar his son was entreating him when he
had become a beast and a cattle, that he might not perish. For his foreparts
were as an ox together with the head, and the feet with the hinder parts as a
lion. And it was revealed to the holy man concerning this mystery, that he
became a beast because of his love of pleasure and his stiff-neckedness, and a
cattle because he was under the yoke of Beliar and obedient; and as an ox he
ate grass; but food of human nature was coming to be for him. And his tongue
was held so as not to speak; and understanding he immediately wept, and he was
entreating God in mind every day and hour; and his eyes became fleshy from
many tears. And many of the city saw him and marveled. But Daniel alone did
not wish to see him. But all the time of his alteration he was in prayer
concerning him, beseeching God. For he said that he would become a man again,
and they did not believe him. And Daniel made the seven years which he said,
seven times, to become seven months, and the mystery of the 7 times was
completed upon him after the remission of his lawlessness. And God gave him
back his kingdom again, and he ate neither bread nor meat, nor drank wine,
confessing to God, because Daniel commanded him to propitiate =66= God with
soaked pulse and greens; for this reason he called him Baltasar, because he
wished to appoint him joint-heir of his children; but the holy man said: far
be it from me to leave the inheritance of my fathers, and to be joined to the
inheritance of the uncircumcised. And for the later kings of the Persians he
did many wonders, as many as I have not written; and a second time they were
thrown to the den of the lions for piety and love of God. For the first time,
because when the Babylonians were praying to Darius the king he alone was
praying to God. And the second, because he overthrew Bel and slew the dragon
under Cyrus the king, and he himself was saved, and came up from the den
unharmed by the lions. But those who had plotted against him were thrown into
the den of the lions, and immediately they were eaten up by them; and after
these things having grown old and died in peace, he was buried there in the
royal cave alone, gloriously. And he gave a sign in the mountains above
Babylon, saying: when the things from the north shall smoke, the end of
Babylon will come; but when it burns as with fire, the end of all the earth.
But if the mountains in the south flow with waters, the people will return to
their land; and if they flow with blood, there will be a slaughter of Beliar
in all the earth.
1. Elijah the Tishbite was from the land of the Arabs, of the tribe of Aaron,
dwelling in Gilead, because Tishbe was a house for the priests. And when he
was about to be born, Sobacha his mother saw that white-appearing men were
addressing him, and they were swaddling him in fire =67=, and they were giving
him a flame of fire to eat, and his father Asom came and announced it in
Jerusalem, and the oracle said to him: Take courage; for his dwelling will be
light and his word a decree, and he will judge Israel with sword and fire. For
having become a zealot and an accurate guardian of the commandments of God and
having been deemed worthy of greatest mysteries and divine gifts, he was taken
up in a fiery chariot, who will come again before the consummation, as God
says through Malachi: Behold I will send you Elijah the Tishbite before the
great and manifest day of the Lord comes, who will restore the heart of a
father toward a son, and the heart of a man toward his neighbor; lest coming I
strike the earth utterly.
2. And his disciple Elisha was from Abelmaul; of the land of Reuben, of the
tribe of Levi; son of Jehoshaphat; and this man too, when he was born in
Gilgal the golden heifer cried shrilly, so as to be heard in Jerusalem, and
the priest said through the lights that a prophet has been born in Jerusalem,
who will pull down their carved things and their molten things. For the spirit
having rested upon him became double in him, double in spirit both the gifts
and the workings; whence not only in his life he worked wonders, but also
having died and been buried in Samaria, a dead man who had lived but once was
thrown into his tomb. And immediately the dead man lived again.
