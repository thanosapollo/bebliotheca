* Fragments on the First Epistle to the Corinthians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1 Cor 2:6-9 The joining of the whole thought is thus: we speak wisdom among
the perfect, not of this age, but the hidden, as it is written that eye has
not seen and ear has not heard and it has not gone up upon a human heart what
God has prepared for those who love him. And to us God has revealed this
knowledge concerning these hidden things.
1 Cor 4:19 And I will know not the word of the puffed-up but the power. From
here some wish to show that the one in Corinth who had fornicated with his
stepmother was of those who teach and baptize, with whom also Eusebius of
Emesa advocates. John indeed the famous and golden-mouthed in certain of his
discourses also puts this one in the dignity of a presbyter. 660
1 Cor 5:9 Another letter to the Corinthians not being found, and in neither of
these a saying being found lying thus, not to mix with fornicators, what shall
we say? that this has been written in this one. Where? when he said that they
did not rather mourn, that the one who did this work might be taken from their
midst; for the taking of such a fornicator from their midst appears a command
not to mix with fornicators.
