But let him consider, if indeed he is not hindered by the covering that lies

their name. And you shall take the Levites for me instead of all the firstborn

Take the ransoms of those who exceed beyond the Levites from the firstborn of
the sons of Israel, and you shall take five shekels a head according to the
holy didrachm.
If then according to the sacred Letter the Levites are ransoms of the
firstborn of the sons of Israel, as also the exactness of the numbering makes
clear, so that of those exceeding among them a weight defined by the law was
given in return; by what account did the law declare ransoms to be given for
each of the firstborn? For concerning the beasts not acceptable for sacrifice,
the law says either to exchange these, or ransoms to be given for them; but
concerning men, we learn both that ransoms are given, and that already the
exchange has come to be through the Levites; which appears discordant even
with the legal letter itself, as the legislation concerning the beasts makes
clear, and as the very nature of the matter suggests. For if after the
allotment of the Levites to the service of God instead of the firstborn
=46.1164= it is declared that ransoms are given instead of them, it is clear


that the Levites do not have what is equal in weight and acceptable as a
counter-ransom. And if this, much more unworthy is the redemption brought
through silver, by as much as all irrational and insensible nature has been
set more dishonorable than human substance. Yet since according to the prophet
they have shut their eyes, and the consequence of this they cannot see; will
they then be able to hear even the most wise Micah himself crying out? I for
my part do not think so. For they hear heavily also with their ears; yet let
this blessed prophet come crying these things with a great voice, and hinting
beforehand by the prophetic Spirit the rejection of the Jewish firstborn,
likewise with the offering of the bodily sacrifices; for he says: With what
shall I take hold of the Lord, shall I lay hold of my God most high? Shall I
take hold of him with yearling calves? Will the Lord receive in thousands of
rams, or in tens of thousands of fat he-goats? Shall I give my firstborn for
ungodliness, the fruit of my womb for my sins? Let those who have a thick
heart hear what the prophet accused of them concerning us, that the blessed
Micah named the firstborn of Israel according to the flesh firstborn of
ungodliness. How then have they supposed these holy, which by prophetic word
were named firstborn of ungodliness? Let the tongues that truly stammer tell
us, that are not able to speak the truth aright, how the prophet, speaking in
the Holy Spirit, dared to call these a fruit of ungodliness? How does the
account concerning their redemption not appear to have smoothness and sequence
for itself, so far as concerns the surface of the letter? and yet the law is
understood among us and believed to be both holy and spiritual. You see the
bound axles of the Jewish chariots, just as of old the cars of the Egyptians
upon the Red waters. But let there be sent upon the sea of the word, instead
of a rod, the power and help of the true lawgiver and king of glory Christ,
and let him lead his people into a straight way of anagogy and contemplation,
to go into a city of a dwelling of knowledge and truth, and let him cover the
adversaries with the unconquerable of the divine glory; of which the whole
earth has been filled, to know the Lord, as the Scripture says, as much water
to cover seas.
Just now the God who became flesh both put to death and made to vanish the
firstborn offspring of the devil, that is, sin, at the same time as being born
according to the flesh, having made for himself the condemnation of
wickedness, and having worked in himself the complete vanishing of it
=46.1165= he filled the opposing powers with lamentations, taking as it were
certain vessels from them the Magi who came from the east, and by the worship
and recognition of his lordship having become useful vessels to the Master for
cooperation in the building of the Church, the truly God-wrought tent, which
the Lord pitched, and not man; and just now, as of old Israel from clay and
brickmaking, so having redeemed in himself the human race from earthy
thinking, he has made this flee Egypt, that is, the hateful and gloomy
fortress of death. And he sanctifies to God and Father not only the firstborn
of men, but already also the whole human tribe, through the firstfruits in
himself of our lump, that is, of the flesh taken from us by him,
intellectually ensouled, as it were leavening the whole compound unto
sanctification of the human substance. For if the firstfruits is holy, so also
the lump, I too would now reasonably say according to the divine Apostle. And
just as of old the lawgiving word typically brought to God the tribe that
obtained the priesthood instead of the firstfruits of the firstborn; so now in
the bright character of the truth we know the high priest and Apostle of our
confession, the holy and harmless and undefiled, and separated from every
sinful defilement and thinking, to have been offered bodily to God and Father


instead of the humanity consecrated to him through faith; and so much the
more, inasmuch as we do not even see the whole of humanity exceeding and
overabounding beyond the one who has given himself a ransom for many,
according to the account of holiness (just as in the shadow of the law the
firstborn were found in excess beyond the Levites), but left behind him by a
great multitude, inasmuch as without any contradiction the one blessed is less
than the one blessing. And then the dignity of the Levitical priesthood was
reckoned toward the nation of Israel alone, and the Levites have been
witnessed to be ransoms of their firstborn alone; but Christ who has come as
high priest of the good things to come, as the all-wise Paul says, and not
according to the order of Aaron, but according to the order of Melchizedek, as
the divine oracle foretold, by the transposition of the priesthood, by his
being from another tribe (for it is evident that our Lord Jesus Christ has
risen from Judah) also worked the transposition of the law, transferring us
from the shadow according to the law upon the Gospel of the kingdom of the
heavens, just as from the Levitical upon the royal tribe the dignity of the
priesthood; and by the likeness according to the order of Melchizedek he
offered himself to the Father as a ransom not for Israel alone, but for all
the nations, and has been set as high priest of the confession of all men,
since Melchizedek also is known to be from the Gentile portion, and is found
from the race of Abraham.
And the legal valuation of the five didrachms =46.1168= for each of the
firstborn knows to show enigmatically, that each of us must sanctify every
sense to God, and the things in these, clean and alien to every unseemly
pleasure, bring to the Creator as a holy and lawful ransom. This sacred rite
we celebrate today in remembrance, this spiritual unfolding of the mystery has
assembled the present holy panegyric; according to which the Savior was
offered legally according to what appears, but noetically and spiritually
bringing the firstfruits of our lump to the Father, he showed the whole of it
holy, and fit for a divine offering. For this the law of old was in labor, and
toward this both the legislation concerning the firstborn and the exchange of
the Levites and the valuation of the didrachms looked. Thus the grace of the
Spirit ferried us through the sea of the holy Scriptures.
But let us see also the shutting in and the demonstration of the adversaries.
Already then above the whole legislation concerning the Levites and the
firstborn has become clear to us, but just as the great Moses, encouraging the
people, confirmed that they would no longer see the Egyptians; so I too now am
led to evangelize the peculiar people of God, that we shall no longer behold
the bodily camp of the shadowy worship. For where, after the manifestation of
the great High Priest, is the liturgy of the Levites? Where, after the
purifying ransom of the world, is the valuation of the firstborn? Where the
remaining exactness of the legal rite? Do you not see that for as long as the
night of ignorance held all things, after the manner of stars the worship
according to the law furnished a certain dim and measured gleam; but when the
sun of righteousness rose, and the dayspring from on high appeared, and the
day of salvation shone, the torch-bearing of the legal ordinances became
unseen, conquered by the surpassing light of grace? Has not that famous temple
been leveled, as the captains of three and the chariots, and all the
high-priests' pride and lifting up upon it? Has not all their legal good order
been flooded and confused? Has not the exactness of the customs among the Jews
utterly perished? For where would they bring the sacrifices for women just
delivered, the chosen place having fallen, and it having been clearly defined
that it is not permitted for the sacrifices to be offered by them anywhere
else? Where the priestly robe


and dignity and all the rest? But that we may not, going through each,
frighten the eye of the soul as by a continual touching and viewing of
corpses, let us leave those dead beside =46.1169= the water of the legal
letter; but let us sing the victory hymn to the man-loving God, and having
been fed with the truly heavenly food, and having been given to drink of the
stream from the true rock, let us come upon the mountain of God, Horeb; that
is, the high teaching of the Gospel, that we may apply the inner hearing to
the voice of the divine trumpets, to the words of the blessed Luke, as he
cries from the holy inner sanctuaries of the mystagogy of the Spirit: And it
came to pass when the days of their purification were fulfilled, the parents
brought up the child Jesus to present him to the Lord, as it is written in the
law of the Lord, that Every male opening the womb shall be called holy to the
Lord; and to give a sacrifice according to what is said in the law of the
Lord, a pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons. And behold there was a man
in Jerusalem, whose name was Simeon, and this man was righteous and devout,
waiting for the consolation of Israel; and the Holy Spirit was upon him, and
it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death,
before he should see the Christ of the Lord in flesh, and he came in the
Spirit into the temple. What is, Waiting for the consolation of Israel? That
is, awaiting the manifestation of the truth, and the departure of the
Israelite people from the legal shadow toward this; upon being released from
being under the elements of the world, to be schooled in the school of the
truth, and to be fitted to the perfection according to it. For neither could
anyone say that, the Jews being in hardship, and being held in some captivity,
he was expecting their recovery and return to cheerfulness and the ancient
state; all their old custom being well ordered, and the temple standing, and
the sacrifices in it being offered, and every other private constitution
belonging to their race being kept.
And if one puts forward their being under the Romans, and Herod having been
set as king of Judea being of a foreign race; let such a one know, that the
constitution according to the law was in nothing harmed by this from Israel's
being ruled by one of another nation, so that the righteous elder should wait
for consolation concerning this. For it has come to pass variously, just as a
certain factious son is given over by the father to a certain austere
pedagogue toward chastening, so Israel has often come under a foreign kingdom.
And the sojourn of the people in Egypt for so long a time makes this clear,
and the prophet Jeremiah in the times of Zedekiah the king, conversing from
divine command toward him and the people under him, to come under the scepter
of Nebuchadnezzar, and to go over to the Chaldeans.
=46.1172= And not only these, being worthy of a more striking way of life, was
it not unseemly to have been subjected to a foreign scepter; but already also
certain of the righteous, and of whom the world was not worthy, to speak
according to Paul, not ruling, but being ruled by barbarian and godless men
the long-suffering of God displayed; so that the proven character of their
piety might strongly shine forth. And Abraham shows it, having come to be in
Egypt, and having his wife taken away by Pharaoh, and dwelling in sojourn in
the land promised to him. Likewise also his son, and that one's offspring, I
mean Isaac and Jacob, and again that one's most temperate child Joseph, having
become a slave, and being condemned to dwell in the prison. Therefore it was
not the consolation of Israel unto worldly good repute and boasting that the
wise Simeon was expecting; but the truly true, the transposition into the
beauty of the truth. For it having been revealed to him that before the change
of life he would see the Christ of the Lord to appear together, he was
spiritually expecting the consolation of the people. He came then


this one in the Spirit into the temple, and when the parents brought in the
child Jesus, to do according to the custom of the law concerning him, he also
received him into his own arms, and blessed God and said: Now you release your
servant, Master, according to your word in peace; for my eyes have seen the
Salvation, which you have prepared before the face of all the peoples, a light
for revelation of nations and glory of your people Israel. O truly blessed and
thrice-blessed elder! How blessed was that truly sacred entrance of yours into
the temple, through which you ran toward the exit of life! How blessed your
eyes of soul together and of body! The one having come into the reception of
the visible theophany; the other not having looked toward the thing seen only,
but illuminated by the very light of the Spirit, and having recognized the God
Word in the body, knowing the divine salvation, that prepared before the face
of all the peoples. How blessed your hands that handled the Word of life! How
blessed your tongue that evangelized the revelation of the life-giving light!
And why do I hasten to fit praise to you for each of the members particularly,
the evangelist having already taught you wholly praiseworthy and blessed, and
possessed by the Holy Spirit? But come, O blessed Father, by the sounding of
the Spirit in you teach as children of the Church the nurslings the hidden
treasure, as in earthen vessels by your few words; since toward you and those
according to you, adorned with the gray hair of the mind above and being truly
elders, and named Fathers of the Church, the divine word leads us to be
discipled in the saving teachings, saying, Ask your father; and he will
announce to you your elders, and they will tell you.
=46.1173= Therefore let the power of the Spirit that then moved that tongue
toward theology give also to us a word now in the opening of the mouth. Now
you release, he says, your servant, Master, according to your word in peace.
For from when Christ became man, and made to vanish the war-making sin, and
reconciled us to the Father, the departure of the saints comes to be in peace.
For this also the great David showing beforehand prophetically said: In peace
in the same I will lie down and sleep; hinting not at what was present, but at
what was future according to the presence of Christ. And as this hope (I mean
the peaceful falling-asleep in Christ) having been housed in the saints in a
single manner through the Spirit, he says: For you, Lord, alone have housed me
upon hope. But the blessed elder Simeon, having already seen the time present,
says, Now you release your servant, Master, according to your word in peace;
showing the present of the divine promise. For my eyes, he says, have seen
your Salvation, which you have prepared before the face of all the peoples;
that is, the salvation that has come through Christ to the whole world, and
not to Israel alone. How then has he been witnessed above to wait for the
consolation of Israel, but now he is seen uttering that the salvation of God
has appeared before all the peoples? Because then he knew by the Spirit that
the consolation of Israel would be, when also the salvation has been prepared
for all the peoples. And see the exactness of the divine inspiration. For a
light, he says, for revelation of nations, and glory of your people Israel;
speaking things agreeing with the great Paul in the numbering of Israel. For
when, he says, the fullness of the nations shall have entered, then also all
Israel shall be saved. Therefore he too, having set first the calling of the
nations, stored last according to the prophecy the turning of all Israel; even
if already the remnant has been saved through those who have already run from
the Jews to the grace. And behold for me also the proper diction of the lofty
thoughts. For having said a light for revelation of nations, he gives us to
understand the revelation of the divine salvation, as having in complete
darkness and perfect gloom no gleam of knowledge of God. For before the


presence of Christ the nations stood, just as also the blessed Isaiah,
evangelizing the people sitting in darkness, declared that they would see the
great light. But since Israel, even if moderately, yet at least was
illuminated by the typical law, he did not say a light upon that one; but he
prophesied that he would inherit glory, recalling an ancient history; that
just as of old the wonderful Moses, having become a converse of God, had a
glorified face; so also they, having conversed by faith with the divine light
of the incarnation, and having been shone upon by the power of the Spirit,
will appear in glory, having put away the ancient covering, and being
transformed into the same image, from glory to glory, as the Apostle says, as
from the Lord the Spirit =46.1176=.
And the God-minded Simeon's having said that the Lord is set for the fall and
rising of many in Israel, shows by the coincidence of the thoughts toward the
prophetic announcements, that one and the same God and lawgiver has spoken
both in the prophets and in the New Covenant. For the stone of stumbling and
the rock of scandal the cause of those who believe upon him not being put to
shame, the prophetic word declared. Therefore there is a fall for those who
from unbelief strike against the lowliness of the flesh; but a rising, for
those who have recognized the firmness of the divine economy. But also saying
that a sword would be driven through the soul of the Theotokos herself, he
clearly prophesies the passion on the cross. For to the things both said and
done toward him in a God-befitting way from the paradoxical conception itself
until the time of the passion, as it were one uncut body, the Theotokos having
a higher and more God-befitting supposition concerning him, we will suppose
her to have suffered as it were a certain cut, in the inglorious of the
crucifixion, even if the wonder of the resurrection worked a complete closing
of the cut. But he signifies that not she alone was held in such a passion,
but he foretells that from many hearts reasonings of doubt and dissension
would appear; so that in the difference of the suppositions concerning him,
the Virgin Mother of God might not have any shaking. And since the prophetess
Anna from old age speaks something weak and not very audible, nor did the
lofty Gospel bring forward the things said from her in her own words; first
let us learn from the evangelist who and from whence she is, and upon what
things and when she has stood at the temple, and again let us search out the
mind of the things of which she spoke. The blessed Luke then says, that There
was Anna the prophetess daughter of Phanuel of the tribe of Asher; she
advanced in many days having lived with a husband seven years from her
virginity; and she was a widow of about eighty-four years; who did not depart
from the temple, serving with fasts and petitions night and day; and she,
having come up at that very hour, gave thanks to God and spoke concerning him
to all who were waiting for redemption in Jerusalem. What is the account of
the evangelist's having made so exact a narration concerning this prophetess,
so as to make mention even of her father, and of the tribe, even what is found
recorded upon almost no other? The exact account concerning this only the God
of the saints himself would know, and those who by his inspiration =46.1177=
have been taught to speak mysteries by the Spirit according to the divine
Apostle; but according to what is reachable for us, we say that there were
also others synonymous with this blessed woman at that time, and holding to
the same sacred pursuits as she, of persisting in fasts and prayers. Therefore
toward her person being distinct, he added the particular by the mention of
her father and of the tribe; and he crowned her with great praises, and they
have altogether the trustworthiness among the hearers, to whom she also spoke
concerning the Lord.
But since, as has been said, the sounding-through and loud-voiced of the word
has been taken away by old age, having set as it were the ear


of the mind near her mouth through the search of the present subject, let us
see what she too was speaking to those waiting for the redemption in
Jerusalem. For according to the sequence of the thought from the prophetic
words, she made the discourse concerning the Lord; since these also, toward
whom the Gospel said she spoke these things, have altogether learned to wait
for redemption from the divine oracles brought forth in the prophets.
Altogether ready for her toward a sketch of the mystery has stood the word of
the great Isaiah: A child has been born to us, a son also has been given to
us, whose rule has been born upon his shoulder, and that his name is called
Angel of great counsel. And indeed, Shine, shine, Jerusalem; for your light
has come, and the glory of the Lord has risen upon you. And David the great
was brought in: Mercy and truth have met, righteousness and peace have kissed;
and, All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God. And
indicatively unto him: Before the sun his name remains, and in him all the
tribes of the earth shall be blessed. All the nations shall bless him, and
they shall serve him and they shall worship him; and that The Lord has made
known his salvation to the sons of Israel. And again the precious and
angel-befitting voice of the wise Malachi: Behold I send my angel, and he
shall prepare a way before your face and suddenly the Lord shall come into his
temple, and the angel of the covenant, whom you desire. And from other
prophetic oracles from elsewhere with mystic flowers she wove the theological
and blessed crown, still with a weak and trembling hand, as indeed advanced in
many days. For the narration of the lofty thoughts concerning the Lord was not
then contained by the hearers, when even after so great a display of the signs
and the proclamation of the inspired mysteries Christ the treasurer of the
treasures of wisdom and knowledge said to his own disciples: Many things I
have to speak to you, but you cannot bear them now.
Yet even if dimly the aged and prophetess made the word concerning the Lord,
as having the person of the law =46.1180= which having grown old and aged and
having come near vanishing, yielded to the New Covenant; yet the discourse
from her has not been unprofitable to the intelligent hearers, just as neither
the pre-announcement from the law in shadows and enigmas of the great mystery.
Come then also you, Fathers and spiritual brothers, who have been taught to
feast in newness of spirit, and not in oldness of letter, who in unleavened
things of sincerity and truth ought to feast the precious and life-making
Pascha of the sacrifice of Christ; who by the divine regeneration of holy
baptism have put away the old man, who is corrupted according to the desires
of deceit, and have put on the new, who is renewed unto knowledge according to
the image of the one who created; Come, let us rejoice in the Lord, let us
shout to God our savior. Let us say, How fearful are your works, Lord! even if
in the multitude of your power your enemies have lied, having voluntarily shut
their eyes toward your divine light that has appeared to the world. Let us
come before his face in confession, and in psalms let us shout to him. Let us
offer a sacrifice of praise continually to God, that is fruit of lips
confessing to his name, and let us not forget well-doing and fellowship; for
with such sacrifices God is well pleased that we may wash off the defilement
of sin, and our spiritual youth shall be renewed as of an eagle; just as also
of the blessed elder Simeon, that we too may run peacefully and with great joy
in good confidence and saving hope toward the exit of life, and not

Let us imitate the intense and unyielding of the blessed Anna in fasts and in

accustomed to be begotten in the souls; just as she too has been witnessed to
be inseparable from the temple, let us not wander the reasonings from the
divine fear, but from it and in it let us nail our flesh and our thoughts,
according to the prayer of the prophet. Let us hear the great Paul teaching: I
have betrothed you to one husband to present a chaste virgin to Christ, and
let us not set aside the covenants toward him through the mixing toward the
corrupter of our life from doing his willings; and in this imitating the
temperate and single-yoked of the prophetess; but according to what is at hand
do not be left behind of the imitation, as many of you as have been loosed
from monogamy, and if possible =46.1181=, do not come to a second marriage.
For thus Paul confirms concerning this that you will be more blessed.
Let us too bring upon the altar above as a pair of turtledoves the purity of
soul and of body; or as two young pigeons the taking thought of good things,
not only before the Lord, but also before men, that we too, having
accomplished all things spiritually according to the divine law, advancing in
wisdom and in grace, and arriving at the measure of the stature of the
fullness of Christ, and being perfected as to the inner man, and becoming a
dwelling of the Holy Spirit, may return to our true fatherland, the heavenly
Jerusalem, in which may we be deemed worthy to enjoy the blessed and continual
gladness and kingdom in Christ Jesus, to whom be glory and might with the
Father and the Holy Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of the ages.
Amen.
