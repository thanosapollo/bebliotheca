* Fragments of a Homily on the One Son
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of the same from the homily concerning the one Son.
For if there is one Son properly, the Word from God the Father, and likewise
one other properly besides him, the one from the seed of David, honored by
communion with the Son by nature and truly, how is it not clear to all that
the one gives, and the other receives; the one crowns, and the other is
crowned; and the one is Lord by nature, and the other is not? Who then are
those who say and think these things, or of what sort is their judgment? Let
us ask the holy disciples. Hear him saying clearly "For certain men crept
in, who also of old were written beforehand unto this judgment, ungodly,
changing the grace of God and denying the only Master and Lord of us Jesus
Christ." How then is Jesus Christ only Master, he who has become a partner
of another's lordship, if he has this according to grace, if even the
dignity of the adoption itself is given to him, if nothing at all is his,
but rather all things of another who gave to him? He said the Word from God
is the only Master and Lord of us Jesus Christ; how then do you join to him
as partner the one from the seed of David? For you do not allow him to be
only Master, 453 dividing his lordship and assigning it to another.
Therefore we say to those who have chosen to think thus, the thing through
the voice of Elijah "How long will you limp upon both your hams?" Nothing
among you is straight, but all things limp and there is much rottenness of
thoughts; but we will not follow the vain counsels of some, rather we
confess Christ one, the Word from the Father by nature and truly, and
having come to be in assumption of flesh and blood.
From the same discourse. We who have believed in Christ are under a curse,
if he who was incarnate from a virgin is not God by nature and truly; for
the prophet Isaiah said somewhere concerning him "And the root of Jesse
shall be, and he who rises to rule nations, upon him nations shall hope."
How then, having the hopes upon him, are we not accursed if the prophet
Jeremiah is true saying "Cursed is he who has the hope upon a man?" But we
who have believed in Christ have not had the hopes upon a man; therefore he
is God, one and only and according to truth Son both before flesh and after
flesh, who being mounted on the colt according to the faith of the
evangelists was going up into Jerusalem, then youths came to meet him
praising and they were saying "Hosanna to the son of David, blessed is he
who comes in the name of the Lord." At this voice of the children the
Pharisees were indignant; for since being seen a man he was receiving the
hymnody fitting to God; and indeed 454 the bold ones were coming forward
both protesting and saying "Do you not hear what these are saying?" For
they all but say Being a man you endure being glorified and being hymned as
God and you receive the praises fitting to God from the children? And what
toward these things Christ? Was he then indignant against the children? Did
he shake off the doxology? Did he rebuke those praising him as God? By no
means; for rather he said toward those fond of accusing "Have you not read
that out of the mouth of infants and sucklings you have prepared praise?
Amen I say to you if these are silent, the stones will cry out." You see
how he who was riding the colt and according to flesh son of David has
witnessed to himself the glory of the Godhead? But he was not a partner of
another's glory as they say nor another besides the Word from God the
Father as Son by himself. Therefore he is named both God and Lord and Most
High even through the voice of the angels. And at any rate the blessed
Gabriel announcing the birth of John and making his coming ministry clear
to Zachariah says that "many at his birth will rejoice and he will turn
many of the sons of Israel upon the Lord their God."

How then was he turning the sons of Israel upon the Lord their God? For he
was announcing good news to them saying "After me comes a man who has
become before me, because he was first of me, and I did not know him but he
who sent me to baptize in water, that one said to me Upon whom you see the
Spirit descending from heaven and remaining upon him, this is he who
baptizes in the Holy Spirit, and I have seen and have witnessed that this
is the Son of God." And he did not say the Son of God is with this one nor
even that he is in this one, but that this is he.
