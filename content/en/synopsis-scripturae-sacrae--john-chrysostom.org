Ezra. The book is called Ezra, since Ezra himself, being a priest and a
reader, narrates and writes up the return of the sons of Israel that came to
be from Persia to Jerusalem. And the return came to be first from the decree,
and then when Cyrus and Darius the kings commanded, by the hand of Jesus son
of Josedek, and Ezra and Zerubbabel, who contended in problems, a promise
having been set, that the one who wins ask whatever he should wish from the
king. When then one said that wine wins, and the other said that the king
wins, Zerubbabel said that women win, and above all truth. And since having
said these things he has won, and he heard that he should ask what he wishes,
he himself asked that the captivity be released, and that Jerusalem be built.
And it came to be as he asked, and the captivity was released. For then the 70
years of the wrath were also fulfilled. There are then those who went up from
the captivity from Judah and Benjamin, and the Levites, the number 4 myriads
and two thousand, horses 330, mules 245, camels 435, their menservants and
maidservants 7334, the singers 200, asses 6720, and those building were
Zerubbabel, and Jesus the son of Josedek, and Nehemiah. And Ezra, gifted in
the law, himself brought the law, and read, and he formed all the things
concerning the temple and the Levites. He himself showed the law, and he
caused the alien women to be thrown out, those who had taken them in the time
of the captivity. And they all threw them out, and they were cleansed, and he
made the passover lawfully, as is written, and the


fast, and thus the first book of Ezra is completed. 2 Ezra. In this book Ezra
says the same things as the first concerning the return, apart from the things

he himself asked concerning the building of the temple; and that Ezra was
reading, and Jesus and Baneas and Sarabias were making the people understand.
And Ezra reading divided in knowledge of the Lord; and the people understood
in the reading, and he made the passover, and in the seventh month he made the
fast and the booths, as is written: That, he says, they have not done thus
from the days of Joshua the son of Nun. And Ezra, having seen Azotian women
mixed with the Hebrews, persuaded all to promise to keep the law of God. And
he threw out the women, as an unlawful marriage, and they swore to keep the
law. And thus being sanctified and cleansed they were glad, and each went away
into his house. And this also is historied concerning Ezra, that when the
books were perishing from the people's neglect through the long captivity,
=56.359= Ezra himself, being a lover of the beautiful and gifted, and a
reader, kept all, and thereafter he brought them forward, and again published
them; and thus the books are preserved. The book of Esther. The book is called
Esther, since through Esther God, when the Jews were about to perish with
their whole race, both saved them, and struck Haman who wanted to plot. For
Artaxerxes the king, throwing out his own wife, sought in his kingdom to take

Esther was found, being a Jew by race. And Mordecai was also a kinsman of hers
