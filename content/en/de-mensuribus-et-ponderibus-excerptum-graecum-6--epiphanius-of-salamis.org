Of Epiphanius, bishop of Cyprus, from the discourse On measures and weights
43 For twenty-two works, O lover of the beautiful, God made from the beginning
until the seventh day, which are these: on the first day the higher heaven,
the earth, the waters, from which are snow, ice and hail, frost and dew, the
spirits that minister before him, which are these: angels before the face and
angels of glory, and angels of clouds and glooms and snows and hail and frost,
angels of voices, thunders and lightnings, angels of cold and heat, of winter
and autumn, and of all the spirits of his creatures, those in the heavens and
on earth and of the chaos, the darkness above the abyss of the waters that
were once above the earth, from which darkness evening and night, the light of
day and of dawn. 44 These seven greatest signs God made on the first day, and
on the second day the firmament in the midst of the waters; on that same day
the waters were divided; the halves of them went up above the firmament, and
the halves of them below the firmament in the midst, upon the face of all the
earth; this work alone God made on the second day.
