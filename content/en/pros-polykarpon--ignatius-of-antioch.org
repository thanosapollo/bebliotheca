* Inscription

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to Polycarp, bishop of the church of the
Smyrnaeans, rather overseen by God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:
abundant greeting.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
Welcoming your mind in God, established as upon an immovable rock, I give
exceeding glory, having been counted worthy of your blameless face, of which
may I have joy in God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
I exhort you in the grace with which you are clothed to add to your course
and to exhort all, that they may be saved. Vindicate your office with all
diligence, both fleshly and spiritual. Have a care for unity, than which
nothing is better. Bear all, as the Lord also bears you. Endure all in
love, even as you already do.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h3
:END:
Give yourself to unceasing prayers. Ask for understanding greater than you
have. Be watchful, possessing an unsleeping spirit. Speak to each man after
the manner of God. Bear the diseases of all as a perfect athlete. Where the
toil is greater, the gain is much.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
If you love good disciples, it is no credit to you. Rather, bring the more
pestilent under in meekness. Not every wound is healed with the same plaster.
Stop the sharp fits with fomentations.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h2
:END:
Be wise as the serpent in all things, and always innocent as the dove. For
this reason you are fleshly and spiritual, that you may humour the things
that appear before your face; but ask that the unseen things be made
manifest to you, so that you may lack nothing and abound in every gift.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h3
:END:
The season requires you, as pilots require winds and as one storm-tossed
requires a harbor, to attain unto God. Be sober, as God's athlete. The prize
is incorruption and eternal life, of which you also are persuaded. In all
things I am your ransom, and my bonds which you loved.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
Let those who seem trustworthy and yet teach strange doctrine not strike you
with alarm. Stand firm as an anvil when it is struck. It belongs to a great
athlete to be beaten and to conquer. Especially for God's sake we must
endure all things, that he also may endure us.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
Be more earnest than you are. Study the times. Look for him who is above
time, the timeless, the invisible, who for our sake became visible, the
intangible, the impassible, who for our sake became passible, who in every
way endured for us.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
Let widows not be neglected. After the Lord, be you their guardian. Let
nothing be done without your consent, nor do you anything without God, which
indeed you do not. Be steadfast.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h2
:END:
Let assemblies be held more often. Seek out all by name.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h3
:END:
Do not be haughty toward male and female slaves; but let them not be puffed
up either. Rather let them serve the more to the glory of God, that they may
obtain from God a better freedom. Let them not long to be set free from the
common purse, lest they be found slaves of desire.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
Flee evil arts; rather hold discourse about these things. Speak to my
sisters, that they love the Lord and be content with their husbands in flesh
and spirit. Likewise charge my brothers in the name of Jesus Christ to love
their wives as the Lord the Church.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
If anyone is able to remain in purity to the honor of the Lord's flesh, let
him remain without boasting. If he boasts, he is lost, and if it becomes
known beyond the bishop, he is corrupted. It is fitting that those who marry
and those who are given in marriage make the union with the bishop's
consent, so that the marriage may be according to the Lord and not according
to desire. Let all things be done to the honor of God.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
Give heed to the bishop, that God also may give heed to you. I am the ransom
of those who submit to the bishop, to the presbyters, to the deacons; and
with them may it be granted me to have my portion in God. Labor together,
contend together, run together, suffer together, sleep together, rise
together, as God's stewards and assessors and servants.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
Please him under whom you serve as soldiers, from whom you also receive your
wages. Let none of you be found a deserter. Let your baptism remain as
weapons, faith as a helmet, love as a spear, endurance as full armor. Let
your works be your deposits, that you may receive your credits as worthy.
Be long-suffering therefore with one another in meekness, as God is with
you. May I have joy of you always.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
Since the church in Antioch of Syria is at peace, as it was made known to
me, through your prayer, I also have become more cheerful in God's freedom
from care, if through suffering I may attain unto God, so that I may be
found a disciple in your resurrection.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
It is fitting, Polycarp most blessed of God, to convene a council most
worthy of God and to appoint someone whom you hold very dear and who is not
sluggish, who will be able to be called God's courier; to count this man
worthy, that going into Syria he may glorify your unwearied love to the
glory of God.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h3
:END:
A Christian has no authority over himself, but gives his time to God. This
work is God's and yours, when you complete it. For I trust the grace, that
you are ready for a good work belonging to God. Knowing your intensity for
the truth, I have exhorted you through a few lines.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
Since I was not able to write to all the churches because I am sailing
suddenly from Troas to Neapolis, as the will commands, you will write to the
churches ahead, as one possessing the mind of God, that they also may do the
same (those who can sending people on foot, and others letters through those
sent by you, that you may be glorified by an eternal work), as you are
worthy.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
I greet all by name, and the wife of Epitropus with her whole household and
the children. I greet Attalus my beloved. I greet the one who is about to
be counted worthy to go into Syria. Grace will be with him always, and with
Polycarp who sends him.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h3
:END:
I pray that you fare well always in our God Jesus Christ, in whom may you
remain in the unity of God and of the episcopate. I greet Alce, a name dear
to me. Farewell in the Lord.
