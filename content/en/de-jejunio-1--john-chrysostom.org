On fasting.
=62.757=
He who has prayer and fasting and almsgiving is then exceedingly rich in
Christ, and a worker of piety. For what profit is it, brethren, to fast
with the body, and to murder with the works? what is the profit of
abstaining from food, and being defiled by fornication? you do not eat
meat, but you devour the flesh of your brother. What gain is it not to be
gladdened with wine, and to be drunk with money? what profit is it not to
eat bread, and to be drunk with anger? what gain is it to waste away with
fasting, and to revile the neighbor? what is the benefit of being
continent of foods, and seizing what belongs to others? what necessity is
there to dry up the body, and not to feed the hungry? what is the benefit
of crushing the body, and not showing mercy to widows and orphans? If you
wish to fast, hate love of money; love of money is the great evil.
You fast? Flee adultery, flee fornication, flee slander, falsehood,
reviling, enmity, blasphemy, and every meddlesomeness. You fast? Flee
greed, robbery, strife, and soul-destroying envy. You fast? Flee
gluttony, the mother of every evil, which separates us even from God
himself, and brings us down as far as the abysses of destruction. If you
fast for God, flee every thing which =62.758= God hates, and he receives
your repentance, as merciful and a lover of mankind; let us run,
brethren, toward repentance, let us run; a time of confession and
mourning and groanings has been given to us, and not of negligence and
sloth. For repentance is the procurer of the kingdom of heaven, and the
entrance of paradise, and enjoyment of eternal delight. If you fast,
beloved, do not overlook the poor man, clothe the naked, look at the
widow's tears, look at orphans groaning. If you have, show mercy; but if
you do not have, at least do not seize what belongs to others. You fast?
Stop your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deceit; it is
enough for you toward salvation. If you fast, see that you do not lend
your silver at interest. You fast? Have mercy on him whom you wronged,
never envy a brother, nor again ever hate anyone. You fast? Do not be
vainglorious. You fast? Flee fornication, which separates us from God
and from the saints, the procurer of the eternal and unquenchable fire.
You fast? Feed the hungry, give drink to the thirsty, visit the sick, do
not forget those in prison, have mercy on the oppressed, take care of
the afflicted and those who lament; become compassionate, meek, good,
gentle, long-suffering, merciful, not remembering injuries, devout, not
quarrelsome, true62.759, godly; that God may both accept your fasting,
and grant you the gain of repentance with much addition. If you fast,
beloved, fast thus as Christ commanded, lest you both endure the toil
and lose the wage. These things I exhort, brethren, that we may keep
them securely, and receive a wage from God, and hear that blessed voice
which says, Come, you blessed of my Father; inherit the kingdom prepared
for you from the foundation of the world. For nothing, brethren, can
help us on that fearful day of judgment, and as procuring this voice, as
repentance and almsgiving. As bread is more necessary than all foods, so
the thought of death than all works. Let the memory of eternal fire
sleep with you each evening, and rise with you, and sloth shall never
have dominion over you in a time of psalmody. As when light has
appeared, the darkness withdraws, so also from humility every bitterness
and anger vanishes. Let anger then be bound, as a tyrant, in the bonds
of meekness; never accept the one who slanders the neighbor to you, but
rather say to him, Cease, brother, I sin every day in more grievous
faults, and how can we condemn that man? Falsehood is the vanishing of
love, and perjury is =62.760= a denial of God; having acquired the fear
of God he drove out falsehood, having his own conscience as judge. He
who has flattered a lion has often tamed it.
He who tends his own belly, and wishes to conquer a spirit of
fornication, is like the one who quenches some conflagration with oil.
The tongue is sinewed by a multitude of foods. Commit to the Lord the
weakness of nature, knowing altogether your own inability. Do not pray
in sophistical words, but from the whole soul, and from humility of
heart; and before all things say, Have mercy on me, Lord, for I am weak.
For us an unswerving faith and love of God cut off cares; and the memory
of death even denies the body. By some small fire they burned much
matter, and by one virtue all the passions fled. You who consider that
you stand before God in the perception of the heart, in prayer become an
unshaken pillar, hindered by none of the cares of this life. If anyone
wishes to present a chaste body to Christ, and to show him a pure heart,
let him keep freedom from anger and self-control. For without these, one
wishing to present either a body or a mind pure to God, and being shaken
by the cares of life, is like one who has bound his own feet strongly,
and tries to walk swiftly in Christ Jesus the Lord. Amen.
