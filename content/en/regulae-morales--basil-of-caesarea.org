BEGINNING OF THE MORALS.
RULE 1.
That those who believe in the Lord must first repent, according to the
preaching of John, and of our Lord Jesus Christ himself; for those who do not
now repent are condemned worse than those condemned before the Gospel.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. From that time Jesus began to preach and to say: Repent;
for the kingdom of heaven has drawn near ... Then he began to reproach the
cities in which most of his mighty works had been done, because they did not
repent. Woe to you, Chorazin, woe to you, Bethsaida; for if the mighty works
that were done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have
repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes. But it will be more
tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the day of judgment than for you, and the
rest. That the present is the time of repentance and of remission of sins; but
in the age to come the just judgment of recompense. Chapter 2. MARK. But that
you may know that the Son of man has authority on earth to forgive sins, he
says: MATTHEW. Amen I say to you, whatever you bind on earth will be bound in
heaven; and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven. Again amen I
say to you, that if two of you agree on earth concerning any matter, whatever
they

ask, it will be done for them by my Father who is in heaven. JOHN. That the
hour is coming in which all who are in the tombs will hear =31.701= his voice,
and will come forth, those who have done good things, to a resurrection of
life; but those who have practiced worthless things, to a resurrection of
judgment. ROMANS. Or do you despise the riches of his kindness, and of his
forbearance, and of his longsuffering, not knowing that the kindness of God
leads you to repentance? But according to your hardness and unrepentant heart
you store up for yourself wrath in the day of wrath and of revelation and of
the just judgment of God, who will render to each according to his works.
ACTS. The times of ignorance therefore God overlooked, but now he commands all
men everywhere to repent; inasmuch as he has appointed a day in which he is
about to judge the world. That those who repent must weep bitterly, and show
from the heart the rest of the things that belong to repentance. Chapter 3.
MATTHEW. And Peter remembered the word of Jesus who had said to him, that
Before a cock crows, you will deny me three times. And going out, he wept
bitterly. 2 COR. But he who comforts the lowly comforted us by the coming of
Titus. And not only by his coming, but also by the comfort with which he was
comforted over you, announcing to us your longing, your mourning, your zeal
for me. And a little later: For behold, this very thing, that you were grieved
according to God, how much earnestness it worked in you; but a defense, but
indignation, but fear, but longing, but zeal, but vindication; in everything
you commended yourselves to be pure in the matter. ACTS. And many of those who
had believed came, confessing and announcing their deeds. And a good number of
those who had practiced curious arts, having brought the books together,
burned them before them. That for those who repent withdrawal from sins alone
is not enough for salvation, but they also need fruits worthy of repentance.
Chapter 4. MATTHEW. And seeing many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to
his baptism, he said to them: Offspring of vipers, who showed you to flee from
the coming wrath? Produce therefore fruits worthy of repentance, and do not
think to say in yourselves: We have Abraham as father. For I say to you that
God is able from these stones to raise up children to Abraham. And already the
axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that does not
produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into fire. That after departure from
here there is no time for achievements, God having measured out the present
time =31.704= in longsuffering for the working of the things toward his good
pleasure. Chapter 5. MATTHEW. Then the kingdom of heaven will be likened to
ten virgins, who, taking their lamps, went out to meet the bridegroom. And
five of them were prudent, and five foolish; who, being foolish, taking their
lamps, did not take oil with them; but the prudent took oil in their vessels
with their lamps. And while the bridegroom delayed, they all nodded and slept.
And in the middle of the night a cry came: Behold, the bridegroom comes, go
out to meet him. Then all those virgins rose and trimmed their lamps. And the
foolish said to the prudent: Give us of your oil, for our lamps are going out.
And the prudent answered,
saying: Lest there not be enough for us and you; go rather to those who sell,
and buy for yourselves. And while they were going away to buy, the bridegroom
came, and those who were ready went in with him to the wedding, and the door
was shut. And afterward the remaining virgins also come, saying: Lord, Lord,
open to us. And he answering said: Amen I say to you, I do not know you. LUKE.
Struggle to enter through the narrow gate, because many, I say to you, will
seek to enter, and

will not be able. From the time the master of the house rises and shuts the
door, and you begin to stand outside and to knock at the door, saying: Lord,
Lord, open to us; and answering he will say to you: I do not know you, where
you are from. 2 COR. Behold, now is an acceptable time, behold, now is a day
of salvation, giving no occasion in anything, that the ministry may not be
blamed, but in everything commending ourselves as ministers of God. GAL.
Therefore then, as we have time, let us work the good toward all.
RULE 2.
That it is impossible for one who also mixes himself with things alien to
piety to serve God.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. No one can serve two lords; for either he will hate the
one and love the other, or he will hold to one and despise the other. You
cannot serve God and mammon. 2 COR. Do not become unequally yoked with
unbelievers. For what sharing is there of righteousness and lawlessness? or
what communion of light with darkness? and what agreement of Christ with
Belial? or what portion of a believer with an unbeliever? and what consent of
a temple of God with idols? That one who has obeyed the Gospel must first be
cleansed from every defilement of flesh =31.705= and spirit, that he may thus
become acceptable to God in the achievements of holiness. Chapter 2. MATTHEW.
Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you cleanse the outside
of the cup and of the dish, but inside they are full of plunder and
incontinence. Blind Pharisee, cleanse first the inside of the cup and of the
dish, that the outside of them also may become clean. 2 COR. Having therefore
these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of
flesh and spirit, accomplishing holiness in the fear of God. That it is
impossible for one who is attached to any of the present things, or who
endures any of the things that even for a little draw away from a commandment
of God, to become a disciple of the Lord. Chapter 3. MATTHEW. He who loves
father or mother above me is not worthy of me. And he who loves son or
daughter above me is not worthy of me. And whoever does not take his cross and
follow after me is not worthy of me ... If anyone wishes to come after me, let
him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me. For whoever wishes to
save his soul will lose it.
RULE 3. That the first and great commandment in the law was witnessed by the
Lord, to love God from the whole heart; and a second, to love the neighbor as
oneself.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. And Jesus said to him: You shall love the Lord your God
from your whole heart, and from your whole soul, and from your whole strength,
and from your whole mind. This is the first and great commandment; and a
second like it is this: You shall love your neighbor as yourself. That the
proof of not loving God and his Christ is not keeping his commandments; and
the demonstration of loving is the keeping of the commandments of Christ, in
endurance of his sufferings unto death. Chapter 2. JOHN. He who has my
commandments and keeps =31.708= them, he is the one who loves me. He who does
not love me does not keep my words. If you keep my commandments, you will
remain in my love, just as I have kept the commandments of my Father and
remain in his love. ROMANS. Who will separate us from the love of Christ?
tribulation? or distress? or persecution? or famine? or nakedness?

or danger? or sword? As it is written: That for your sake we are put to death
all the day, we were reckoned as sheep of slaughter. But in all these things
we more than conquer through him who loved us, and the rest.
RULE 4. That he honors and glorifies God who works his will; and he dishonors
him who transgresses his law.
Chapter 1. JOHN. I glorified you on the earth; I finished the work which you
have given me to do. MATTHEW. So let your light shine before men, that they
may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in the heavens. PHIL.
That you may be sincere and without offense unto the day of Christ, filled
with fruits of righteousness that are through Jesus Christ, to the glory and
praise of God. ROMANS. You who boast in the law, through the transgression of
the law you dishonor God. RULE 5. That one must, being clean of all hatred
toward all, love even the enemies; and lay down the soul for the friends, when
need calls, having such love as God had toward us, and his Christ. Chapter 1.
MATTHEW. You have heard that it was said to the ancients: You shall love your
neighbor, and hate your enemy. But I say to you: Love your enemies; And a
little later: You therefore shall be perfect, as your heavenly Father is
perfect. JOHN. For God so loved the world that he gave his only-begotten Son.
This is my commandment, that you love one another, as I have loved you.
Greater love than this no one has, that one lay down his soul for his friends.
LUKE. And you will be sons of the Most High, because he is kind upon the
ungrateful and evil. Become therefore merciful, as your Father also is
merciful. ROMANS. And God commends his own love toward us, that while we were
still sinners Christ died for us. EPH. Become therefore imitators of God as
beloved children, and walk in love, as Christ also loved =31.709= us, and gave
himself up for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God. That the proof of the
disciples of Christ is the love in him toward one another. Chapter 2. JOHN. In
this all will know that you are my disciples, if you have love among one
another. That the proof of not having the love of Christ toward the neighbor
is to do any of the things that harm or grieve him unto a cutting off of the
faith, even if the thing done is conceded by Scripture in its own account.
Chapter 3. ROMANS. For if because of food your brother is grieved, you no
longer walk according to love. Do not destroy him by your food, for whom
Christ died. That the Christian must in every way, as far as it is in him,
heal even the one who is grieved against him. Chapter 4. MATTHEW. If therefore
you offer your gift at the altar, and there remember that your brother has
something against you, leave your gift there before the altar, and go first,
be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift. 1 COR. Being
reviled we bless, being persecuted we endure, being blasphemed we exhort. That
he who has the love according to Christ sometimes also grieves the one loved,
toward what is profitable. Chapter 5. JOHN. But now I go to him who sent me,
and none of you asks me: Where are you going? But because I have spoken these
things to you, grief has filled your heart. But I tell you the truth: it is
profitable for you that I go away. For if I do not go away, the Paraclete will
not come to you. 2 COR. So that I rejoiced rather, that, even if I grieved you
in the letter, I do not regret it, even if I did regret it. For I see that
that letter, even if for an

hour, grieved you. Now I rejoice, not that you were grieved, but that you were
grieved unto repentance. For you were grieved according to God, that you might
suffer loss in nothing from us.
RULE 6. That one must speak freely without fear and without shame in the
confession of our Lord Jesus Christ and of his words.
=31.712= Chapter 1. MATTHEW. What I say to you in the darkness, say in the
light; and what you heard into the ear, proclaim upon the housetops. And do
not fear those who kill the body but are not able to kill the soul; but fear
rather him who is able to destroy both soul and body in gehenna. Everyone
therefore who will confess in me before men, I also will confess in him before
my Father who is in heaven. LUKE. For whoever is ashamed of me and of my
words, of this one the Son of man will be ashamed when he comes in his glory
and of the Father and of the holy angels. 2 TIM. Do not therefore be ashamed
of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner; but suffer hardship with
the Gospel, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.
RULE 7. That even if someone seems to confess the Lord and hears his words,
but disobeys his commandments, he is condemned, even if he is conceded to come
to be in spiritual gifts because of some economy.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. Not everyone who says to me, Lord, Lord, will enter into
the kingdom of the heavens, but he who does the will of my Father who is in
heaven. Many will say to me in that day: Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in
your name? and in your name cast out demons? and in your name do many mighty
works? And then I will confess to them that I never knew you. Depart from me,
you who work lawlessness, and the rest. LUKE. And why do you call me, Lord,
Lord, and do not do what I say? and the rest. TITUS. They confess to know God,
but by the works they deny him; being abominable, and disobedient, and for
every good work unapproved.
RULE 8. That one must not be in two minds and doubt over the things said by
the Lord, but be fully assured that every word of God is true and possible,
even if nature fights. For here also is the contest of faith.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. And in the fourth watch of the night Jesus comes toward
them walking upon the sea. And the disciples, seeing him walking upon the sea,
were troubled, saying: It is a phantom; and from fear =31.713= they cried out.
And immediately Jesus spoke to them saying: Take courage, it is I, do not
fear. And Peter answering said to him: Lord, if it is you, command me to come
toward you upon the waters. And he said: Come. And Peter, coming down from the
boat, walked upon the waters to come toward Jesus. And seeing the strong wind,
he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried out saying: Lord, save me. And
immediately Jesus stretching out the hand took hold of him, and says to him:
Little-faithed one, why did you doubt? JOHN. The Jews therefore fought toward
one another, saying: How can this man give us the flesh to eat? He therefore
said to them: Amen amen I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of
man and drink his blood, you do not have life in yourselves. LUKE. And the
angel said to him: Do not fear, Zacharias,

because your petition has been heard; and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a
son. And a little later: And Zacharias said toward the angel: According to
what shall I know this? For I am old, and my wife is advanced in her days. And
the angel answering said to him: I am Gabriel, who stands before God; and I
was sent to speak toward you and to announce these things as good news to you.
And behold, you will be silent and not able to speak until the day these
things come to be, because you did not believe my words, which will be
fulfilled in their time. ROMANS. And not weakening in faith, he did not
consider his own body already dead, being about a hundred years old, and the
deadness of Sarah's womb. And toward the promise of God he did not waver in
unbelief, but was strengthened in faith; giving glory to God, and being fully
assured that what he has promised he is able also to do. Therefore it was also
reckoned to him unto righteousness. That he who does not believe the Lord in
the lesser things is clearly unbelieving much more in the greater. Chapter 2.
JOHN. If I told you earthly things and you do not believe, how, if I tell you
the heavenly things, will you believe? LUKE. He who is faithful in a very
little is also faithful in much; and he who is unjust in a very little is also
unjust in much. That one must not be established by one's own reasonings unto
a setting aside of the things said by the Lord; but know that the words of the
Lord are more worthy of trust than one's own full assurance. Chapter 3.
MATTHEW. Then Jesus says to them: You all will be scandalized in me in this
night. And Peter answering said to him: =31.716= And if all are scandalized in
you, I will never be scandalized. Jesus said to him: Amen I say to you, in
this night, before a cock crows, you will deny me three times ... And when
evening came, he reclined with the twelve disciples; and as they were eating,
he says to them: I say to you that one of you will betray me. And being
exceedingly grieved, they began to say to him each of them: It is not I, Lord?
ACTS. And a voice came toward him: Rising, Peter, sacrifice and eat. And Peter
said: By no means, Lord; because I have never eaten anything common or
unclean. And a voice again a second time toward him: What God has cleansed, do
not you make common. 2 COR. Casting down reasonings, and every height lifted
up against the knowledge of God, and taking captive every thought unto the
obedience of Christ.
RULE 9. That one must not neglect the knowledge of the things that fall to us;
but hearing the words of the Lord with attention, understand, and do his will.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. And Peter answering said to him: Explain this parable to
us. And Jesus said: Are you also still without understanding? Do you not yet
perceive that everything that goes into the mouth proceeds into the belly and
is cast out into the drain? but the things that go out from the mouth come out
from the heart, and make the man common? ... Of everyone who hears the word of
the kingdom and does not understand, the evil one comes and snatches what was
sown in his heart. This is he who was sown beside the way. And a little later:
And he who was sown upon the good earth, this is he who hears the word and
understands, who indeed bears fruit and produces, one a hundred, another
sixty, another thirty. MARK. And calling all the crowd, he said to them: Hear
me, and understand. EPH. See therefore how you walk accurately; not as unwise,
but as wise; buying up the time, because the days are evil. For this reason do
not become foolish, but understanding what the will of God is. That concerning
the things that do not fall to us one must not be a busybody. Chapter 2. JOHN.
And after the morsel, then Satan entered into that one. Jesus says to him:
What you do, do more quickly. And none of those reclining knew toward what

he said to him. ACTS. Those therefore who had come together asked him saying:
=31.717= Lord, is it in this time that you restore the kingdom to Israel? And
he said toward them: It is not yours to know times or seasons, which the
Father has set in his own authority. That it belongs to those who have zeal of
good pleasure toward God also to ask concerning the things one must. Chapter
3. MATTHEW. And his disciples came to him saying: Explain to us the parable of
the tares of the field ... And behold, one coming said to him: Good teacher,
what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life? LUKE. He therefore
said to the crowds going out to be baptized by him: Offspring of vipers, who
showed you to flee from the coming wrath? And a little later: And the crowds
asked him saying: What shall we do? Likewise tax-collectors, likewise those
serving as soldiers. ACTS. And hearing they were pricked in the heart, and
said toward Peter and the rest of the apostles: What shall we do, men
brothers? That one who is asked must take care to answer worthily. Chapter 4.
LUKE. And behold, a certain lawyer stood up testing him, and saying: Teacher,
having done what shall I inherit eternal life? And he said toward him: In the
law what is written? how do you read? And he answering said: You shall love
the Lord your God from your whole heart, and from your whole soul, and from
your whole mind, and your neighbor as yourself. And he said toward him: You
have answered rightly. Do this, and you will live. COL. Let your word always
be in grace, seasoned with salt, to know how you must answer each one. That
the judgment of those who understand and do not do is worse; and neither is
sinning according to ignorance without danger. Chapter 5. LUKE. And that
servant who knew the will of his lord and did not prepare, nor did toward his
will, will be beaten with many. And he who did not know, but did things worthy
of blows, will be beaten with few.
RULE 10. That the end of sin is death.
Chapter 1. JOHN. And he who disobeys the Son will not see the life, but the
wrath of God remains upon him. =31.720= ROMANS. For when you were slaves of
sin, you were free in regard to righteousness. What fruit therefore did you
have then, of the things of which you are now ashamed? For the end of those
things is death. And a little later: For the wages of sin is death ... And the
sting of death is sin. That the end of a commandment of God is eternal life.
Chapter 2. JOHN. Amen amen I say to you, if anyone keeps my word, he will not
behold death unto the age. But the Father who sent me, he gave me a
commandment, what I should say and what I should speak, and I know that his
commandment is eternal life. ROMANS. But now, having been freed from sin and
enslaved to God, you have your fruit unto sanctification, and the end eternal
life.
RULE 11. That one must not despise the judgments of God, but fear, even if
they do not have the recompense immediately.
Chapter 1. MATTHEW. But fear rather him who is able to destroy both soul and
body in gehenna. LUKE. But if that servant says in his heart: My lord delays
to come; and begins to beat the menservants and the maidservants, and to eat
and drink and be drunken; the lord of that servant will come in a day in which
he does not expect, and in an hour which he does not know, and will cut him in
two, and will set his portion with the unbelievers. JOHN. See, you have become
healthy; sin no longer, that something worse may not come to you. EPH. Let no
one deceive you with empty words;

for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of
disobedience. That he who has been disciplined for the first sins and has been
counted worthy of remission, if he sins again, prepares for himself a worse
judgment of wrath than the former. Chapter 2. JOHN. See, you have become
healthy, sin no longer, that something worse may not come to you. That, some
having fallen under a judgment of wrath of God, the rest must be disciplined
by fearing. Chapter 3. LUKE. And some were present at that very time,
announcing to him concerning the Galileans whose blood Pilate mixed with their
sacrifices. And Jesus answering said to them: Do you think that these
Galileans became sinners beyond all =31.721= the Galileans, because they have
suffered such things? No, I say to you; but unless you repent, you will all
likewise perish. Or those eighteen upon whom the tower in Siloam fell and
killed them, do you think that these became debtors beyond all the men who
dwell in Jerusalem? No, I say to you; but unless you repent, you will all
likewise perish. ACTS. And Ananias, hearing these words, falling, expired. And
great fear came upon all who heard these things. 1 COR. Nor murmur, just as
some of them murmured, and perished by the destroyer. And these things

1 COR. And I wish you to be without care. The unmarried man is anxious about

anxious about the things of the world, how he will please the wife. And the
woman and the virgin have been divided. The unmarried woman is anxious about

a Christian? Faith working through love. What is proper to faith? An undivided
full assurance of the truth of the God-inspired words, shaken by no reasoning,
neither one brought in by natural necessity, nor one fashioned toward piety.
What is proper to a faithful one? To be disposed together in such full
assurance with the power of the things said, and to dare to set aside nothing,
or to add further. For if everything which is not from faith is sin, as the
Apostle says, and the faith is from hearing, and the hearing through a word of
God; everything outside the God-inspired Scripture, being not from faith, is
sin. What is proper to the love toward God? To keep his commandments according
to an aim of his glory. What is proper to the love toward the neighbor? Not to
seek one's own things, but the things of the one loved toward what is
profitable both for the soul and for the body. What is proper to a Christian?
To be born from above through the baptism from water and Spirit. What is
proper to him who has been born from water? That, as Christ died to the sin
once for all, so he also is dead and =31.869= unmoved toward every sin,
according to what is written, that As many as we were baptized into Christ
Jesus, we were baptized into his death. We were therefore buried with him
through the baptism into the death, knowing this, that our old man was
crucified with him, that the body of sin may be abolished, that we may no
longer be slaves to sin. What is proper to him who has been born from Spirit?
To become that, according to the measure given, which that is from which he
was born, as it is written, that What has been born from the flesh is flesh;
and what has been born from the spirit is spirit. What is proper to him who
has been born from above? To put off the old man with his deeds and the
desires, and to put on the new who is being renewed unto knowledge according
to the image of him who created him, according to what has been said, that As
many as you were baptized into Christ, you put on Christ. What is proper to a
Christian? To be cleansed from every defilement of flesh and spirit in the
blood of Christ, and to accomplish holiness in fear of God and love of Christ,
and not to have a spot or wrinkle, or any of such things, but to be holy and
unblemished, and so to eat the body of Christ, and to drink the blood. For he
who eats and drinks unworthily eats and drinks judgment to himself. What is
proper to those who eat the bread, and drink the cup of the Lord? To keep a
continual memory of him who died for us and was raised. What is proper to
those who keep such a memory? No longer to live to themselves, but to him who
died for them and was raised. What is proper to a Christian? That his
righteousness abound in everything, more than that of the scribes and
Pharisees, according to the measure of the teaching according to the Gospel of
the Lord. What is proper to a

Christian? To love one another, as also Christ loved us. What is proper to a
Christian? To see the Lord before him through everything. What is proper to a
Christian? To watch on each day and hour, and to be ready in the perfection of
good pleasure toward God, knowing that in an hour he does not think, the Lord
comes.
