Of the same. We are not taught the word concerning the Trinity so much from
the divine Scripture as from the natural sequence. Of the same, concerning
theology.
Upon theology one God is glorified and worshiped in three hypostases; one
Godhead, one nature, one essence, one power and authority, one will; and three
persons, and three hypostases, each =95.229= with the property. For it is
proper to the Father to be unbegotten; and proper to the Son to be begotten;
and proper to the Spirit to be proceeding. God the Father without beginning;
God the Son co-without-beginning, even if from the Father; God the Holy
Spirit, co-eternal and co-without-beginning, going forth from the Father, not
being begotten son-wise as Son, but proceedingly. The three are one in the
Godhead, even in three hypostases. And upon the economy of God and the Word of
God, who was incarnate by good pleasure of the Father and cooperation of the
Holy Spirit, we confess two natures, and one hypostasis; and one Son our Lord
Jesus Christ, God together and man, and one person; and two wills and two
energies; each nature with the property, indivisibly and unconfusedly. For it
is proper to the Godhead to raise the dead, and to accomplish the God-signs;
and proper to the humanity to hunger and thirst and toil, and to undergo the
sufferings. For he was united to the flesh unconfusedly and indivisibly, with
which flesh he was taken up, and sat at the right of the Father, and will come
with glory at the completion of the ages to judge living and dead, God
