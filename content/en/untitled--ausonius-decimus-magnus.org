** AUSONIUS TO HESPERIUS HIS SON, GREETING (To be appended to the Consular Book which I compiled as one from all the Consuls. To Gregory from the Prefect.)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:

Lest the times of eternal Rome be unknown to you,
drawn under kings and the commands of the fathers,
I have arranged the fasti and the names of flying age,
if any lie scattered through Latin history,
let this be your fruit, receive nights watched through:
our lamp obeys your study.
You too, to list the coming Januses through a long span,
as my page has arranged the past.
You already have the father's example, that forthwith you too
the purple may join to the Ausonian consuls.

* Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** CALCULATION FROM THE FOUNDING OF THE CITY TO OUR CONSULSHIP
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:

Join four to eleven hundred years,
and bind eleven and one besides a three-year period.
This will be the series of eternal Rome from its origin.

* Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** ADDED AT THE END OF THE SAME BOOK
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:

Thus far I have written the fasti; if lot will, further
I will add; if not, you who read will add.
You wish to know who I am? Seek the title which is fourth from the bottom:
you will read the name of the consul Ausonius.

* Section 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** ON THE SAME
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:

When, Proculus, you receive the series of years
drawn from Quirinus the king of the eternal city,
you will read that a thousand years and a hundred and twice nine have flowed
up to the name of the consul Ausonius,
it may be that, when a lustrum has piled itself on these,
Proculus may mark a completed Olympiad.
