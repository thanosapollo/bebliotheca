88 Not only man, but also God, as Jeremiah the prophet also says: this is our
God, another shall not be reckoned beside him. He found out every way of
knowledge and gave it to Jacob his child and to Israel his beloved. After
these things he was seen upon the earth and associated with men. And when did
he associate with men, if not when he was born with them from a virgin and was
an infant with them and grew with them and ate with them and drank with them
and the rest? 90 For if prayers must be made, he says, for the catholic Church
from the ends of the inhabited world, much more must providence also be shown
for all of her and likewise to care for all and to be anxious for all.
