Testament to the citizens.
31 They put forward, however, a testament arranged toward the citizens,
according to which it enjoins not to set up images in churches or in
cemeteries. 32 Take heed to yourselves and hold the traditions which you
received; do not turn aside to the right or to the left. 33 And in this have
remembrance, beloved children, not to set up images in churches nor in the
cemeteries of the saints; but always have God through memory in your hearts.
But not even according to a common house; for it is not permitted for a
Christian to be lifted up through the eyes and wanderings of the mind, but let
the things toward God be written and impressed for all. 34 If anyone attempts
to contemplate the divine character of the Word of God according to the
incarnation from material colors... let him be anathema.
