PENANCES FOR THE CANONICAL WOMEN.
1 She who has sworn a chance oath, let her be set apart two weeks. 2 She who
has blasphemed one of the elder women entrusted to arrange the things in the
asceterium, let her be set apart one week. 3 She who secretly says something
against an absent sister with the aim of slandering her, let her be set apart
one week. 4 She who has insulted someone with a chance insult, let her be set
apart one week. 5 Whoever is found whispering to another apart from the elder
women entrusted to serve, let her be set apart one week =31.1316= 6 Whoever
without a necessary need putting forward an untimely converse talks idly, let
her be set apart one week. 7 Whoever utters jests, or an unfitting laughter, let
her be set apart one week. 8 Whoever being in the work of the service, and
having a necessary need, will converse with the neighbor either with a cry or
with bold-speech, let her be set apart one week. 9 Whoever holds to the one
being condemned upon a fault by those entrusted after testing to manage and
arrange, let her be set apart together with that one to whom she holds two
weeks. 10 Whoever will converse with one of those coming in from outside, before
she is permitted by the one entrusted with the care in all authority of good
order, let her be set apart one week. 11 Whoever is found murmuring in the
narrowness of the things toward the need, or in the toil of the works, let her
be set apart one week. 12 She who falls against the command of those entrusted
with the care, let her be set apart one week. 13 She who contemptuously, with a
supposed quiet, does not fill the order, let her be set apart one week. 14 She
who wishes to be adorned, after a first and second admonition, to be rebuked
before the one set over. 15 She who without the knowledge of those entrusted
with the care has gone away to a mother or to a sister, let her be set apart one
week. 16 She who has nodded eyes with a wicked form grieving the neighbor, let
her be set apart one week. 17 She who after the dismissal of the synaxes
converses with one of the women outside, let her be set apart two weeks. 18 She
who goes from work to another work without an order of those having the care,
let her be set apart one week. 19 Whoever would throw off the penance, or
contradicts, or murmurs, not recognizing her own benefit in the correction, let
her be set apart two weeks.
