On Mid-Pentecost.
=61.741=
Wherever the Master comes bodily as a fountain of life, he pours forth many
and various streams of loving-kindness. He went up into a mountain with the
disciples, and set forth to those who heard the streams of the beatitudes; he
came down from there, and poured out channels of healings upon those held by
various diseases; he stood at the Sheep Pool, and gave the paralytic to drink
of strength; he entered the house of Peter, and cooled with dew his
mother-in-law who was burning with fever; he reached Bethany, and gave the
four-days-dead Lazarus the cup of life to drink; he met the leper, and wiped
away the scales of the disease with the sponge of loving-kindness; he stood
in the synagogue, and moistened the withered shoot of the hand toward active
movement; he came to the wedding, and mixed for those invited the wine of
wonder-working; and a thousand other things, which it is hard to number.
He went up also today into the temple, and set forth to those who heard the
rivers of teaching. For you heard the evangelical reading, which the
evangelist, standing on a high platform, fittingly for us and at the middle
of the feast, cried out with a loud voice, using the voice of the Levite as a
kind of trumpet. Now, it says, about the middle of the feast Jesus went up
into the temple, and taught; the writing itself also is full of
loving-kindness. For he did not say, persecutions being at hand, or, grief
being present, or, despondency holding the disciples, and such other
narration, about which the Acts of the Apostles continually made a comedy;
but what does he say? Now about the middle of the feast. Why about the middle
of the feast, tell, John? Did Herod lay hands? Did the people of the Jews
gather a wicked council? Judas again toward betrayal? No, he says; but what?
Jesus went up into the temple, and taught. You have set beside Christ the
things that befit Christ; you have fitted to him the action that suits him.
For it is the work of the Word to speak, and of a teacher to teach, and of
Christ to send forth to the hearers words of benefit. Jesus went up into the
temple, and taught. O the unsurpassable loving-kindness! Where he was being
stoned little by little, there by the teachings of his words he was enriching
those who stoned him. Jesus went up into the temple, and taught. Where the
ordinances of Moses were being read, there the teachings of grace were set
forth, not abolishing the law, but becoming the fulfiller of the law. For he
himself also cried out in the Gospels, I came not to destroy the law, but to
fulfill; and Paul, having the Master speaking through his mouth, For Christ
is the end of the law, he says, unto righte =61.742= ousness. Now about the
middle of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple, and taught, teaching all
the place of prayer, making there as Word the word of catechesis, where also
the plotting sanhedrin is gathered. The priest according to the order of
Melchizedek went up into the temple, and taught, many hearing, but none of
the Jews learning.
For concerning them the one who spoke beforehand cried, They have eyes, and
see not; they have ears, and hear not. Jesus went up into the temple, and
taught, exposing the mindlessness of those who fight against God; he went up
into the temple, that spreading the nets of heavenly teaching, he might make
a rational hunt from among men; Jesus went up into the temple, he went up;
but the Jews went down from the faith. Now about the middle of the


feast, Jesus went up into the temple, and taught. The Jews therefore marveled,
saying, How does this man know letters, not having learned? They marveled,
but they did not believe; they were struck by the excess of wisdom, but they
did not repent, being filled with the terribleness of wickedness. They
beheld the physician setting forth the medicines, and being sick they did
not receive the healing; they saw the one who delivers, and they loved the
captivity; having in the midst of the goods the meadow, they gathered the
thorns; the harbor being present, the terrible ones were shipwrecked; wisdom
pouring forth the divine teachings, they did not wish to wash away the stench
of the murders; Christ spreading the wings of loving-kindness, the offspring
of vipers would not endure to be sheltered. For how often, he says in the
Gospels, would I have gathered your children together, as a hen her own
chicks; and you would not? The Jews therefore marveled, saying, How does this
man know letters, not having learned? O the affairs! You are ignorant of the
law, O Jew, and you are ignorant of the teachings of the law? You say, How
does this man know letters, not having learned?
Tell me, O enemy of the truth, how did Adam, not having learned letters, put
upon the beasts names that fit them? You do not question the servant, and you
call the Master to account, saying, How does this man know letters? Jesus
answered and said, My teaching is not mine, but of the Father who sent me.
And when is God sent by a change of place? And when does the one who fills
all things come where he is present? The word is toward the Jews, not toward
those who know how to learn piously is the saying. My teaching is not mine,
but of the Father who sent me, who also set =61.743= forth for me the
testimony from heaven before all of you, saying, This is my beloved Son.
Concerning me John also cried out, Behold the Lamb of God, who takes away
the sin of the world. For Christ is truly a lamb, who today went up into the
temple, and John set forth the voice of truth concerning him. For being a
voice, he fittingly praised the Word; being a lamp, he made known the lamps
of the sun; as forerunner, he announced the one who was coming; as a gleam,
he proclaimed the morning; as a soldier, he published the name of the king.
Behold the Lamb of God, who takes =61.744= away the sin of the world; a
lamb, whom a virgin ewe conceiving without seed bore without corruption, and
the manger received him as a grass-bearing child, and the Jordan washed him,
John baptized him, Judas sold him to the Jews, Pilate slaughtered him, the
tomb set him forth, and we bought him as our provision, and with the fleece
he clothed all who were naked from the transgression of Adam. To him be the
glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
