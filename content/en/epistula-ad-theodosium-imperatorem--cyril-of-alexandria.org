* Letter to the Emperor Theodosius
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Therefore we confess the only-begotten Son of God, perfect God, of the same
substance with the Father according to the Godhead and of the same substance
with us, the same one, according to the humanity. For a union of two natures
has come to be, whence we confess one Christ, one Son, one Lord. And if it
seems good, let us show as an example the composition according to ourselves,
according to which we are human beings. For we have been composed of soul and
body and we see two natures, one of the body, another of the soul, but one
human being from both according to union, and the having been composed from
two natures does not prepare the one to be thought two human beings, but one
human being. For if we take away that from two and different natures the one
and only Christ is, those opposite will say: if the whole is one nature, how
did he become man, or what flesh did he make his own? And those who say that a
blending or confusion or mixing of the God Word with the flesh came to be,
these the catholic and apostolic church anathematizes.
