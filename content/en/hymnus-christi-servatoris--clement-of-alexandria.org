HYMN OF THE SAVIOR CHRIST OF SAINT CLEMENT
Bit of untaught colts, wing of unwandering birds, unswerving rudder of ships,
shepherd of royal lambs; gather your simple children to praise holily, to hymn
without guile with harmless mouths of children Christ the leader. King of
saints, all-subduing Word of the highest Father, president of wisdom, support
of toils, eternal-joy, Savior Jesus of the mortal race, shepherd, ploughman,
rudder, bit, heavenly wing of an all-holy flock, fisher of mortals of those
being saved from the sea of wickedness, baiting pure fishes of a hostile wave
with sweet life. Holy shepherd of rational sheep, lead, king of unharmed
children; tracks of Christ, a heavenly way. Ever-flowing Word, measureless
age, everlasting light, fountain of mercy, doer of virtue for those who hymn
God with a solemn life. Christ Jesus, heavenly milk of sweet breasts of a
bride of the graces of your wisdom, being pressed out. We little children,
nurtured with tender mouths, filled from a rational teat with dewy spirit, let
us sing together simple praises, true hymns to King Christ, holy wages of the
teaching of life. Let us send simply a strong child, a chorus of peace, we the
Christ-born, a temperate people, let us psalm together the God of peace.
