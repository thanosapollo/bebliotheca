* Encomium on the Holy Martyrs
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

An encomium of our father among the saints John Chrysostom on the holy
martyrs.
Fearful is the contest of the martyrs but immortal is the memory of the pious,
terrible is the death of the saints but "unfading is the crown" of the courageous,
sword-bearing is the stadium of the athletes but crown-bearing is the prize of
Christ. Swords and fire and water are the contests of the wrestlers, but heaven
and paradise and a kingdom of God are the prizes of the victors. Upon earth they
contended being tortured, and in heaven they were armed being crowned. Below
they underwent the despite and above they stored the honors. They did not fight
back against the swords being warred upon, but they underwent "being
strengthened by the faith"; for their battle was not about earthy things. Not
about corruptible moneys, not about empty wealth were the causes of the war, but
about a kingdom of heavens, about "a paradise of delight", about an incorruptible
life. About God the contest, about faith the wrestling, about confession the
victory, that they might not fall out of the covenants but that they might remain
pious.
And the war was glorious and the battle heavenly, divine the array and angelic
the encampment that they might be co-glorified with God, that they might receive
back the ancient honor, that they might always remain in Christ, that they might
have a painless life. For this reason they also contended with great joy, not
bearing swords but preaching the word, not sending out bows and darts but
announcing the confession of Christ, not "putting on" weapons but being
"breastplated" with the faith. For swords were sharpened against the body of the
martyrs, but the word sharper than the sword worked the edge of the slaughter
blunter. For they did not perceive the toil, being pricked by the longing; for
the longing of the confession lightened the toil of the body and the weakness.
And the things said by us are not worthy of their athletics; for to the just "the
praise is not from men but from God", according to the apostle. To God then let
us send up glory who crowns the martyrs, because to him glory is fitting now,
and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
