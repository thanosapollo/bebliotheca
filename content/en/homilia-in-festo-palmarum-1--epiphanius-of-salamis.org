* Homily on the Feast of Palms
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

From a certain discourse of our father among the saints Epiphanius the
bishop, concerning Palms.
He who is unceasingly hymned by angels and glorified, below as a man
kneels to the Father and prays; and above he himself receives prayers and
kneelings from all visible and invisible creation. Below he hungers as a
man, but from above the same one, having come as heavenly bread, thirsts
according to the economy; yet he himself is the fountain of life in a
manner befitting God. Below he toils according to the assumption; yet he
himself is the rest of those who toil and are heavy laden.
This same one is he who today comes willingly toward death, that by his
own death he might abolish him who has the power of death, and granting
immortality to those who died under him, who were baited by a hope of
immortality. For this reason he came, not bringing scepters and trumpets
=43.504=, but displaying utter poverty; not swords and weapons and
spears, but bearing extreme humility; not armed men, not shields inlaid
with gold and bucklers and helmet and breastplate, but extreme meekness,
and measureless guilelessness, and moderation beyond measure, the Lord
Christ, God in Zion. And the whole city was shaken, it says, saying, Who
is this? And the crowds said, This is Christ the son of David. And having
entered into the temple, he who is truly the temple and God more sacred
even than the temple, he also cast out the defiled priests, the lawless
and ill-disposed. And the children, standing in a manner befitting the
temple or befitting God in the temple of God, cried out Hosanna to the
God of the temple, to Christ Jesus; Hosanna to the son of David, who
grants peace in heaven, and glory in the highest.
Blessed are the expectations of the Churches!
Blessed are the hopes of the Christians! Believing in the prince of
peace, or having been enriched with peace, men have become fellow
citizens of angels; the things above and below Christ has mixed
together; he has united the earthy with the bodiless. Rejoice in Christ;
again I will say, Rejoice. Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion, the new
Jerusalem, the Church of God; consider the more mystical things of your
feast; run higher than the things seen, that you may know also by anagogy
the things accomplished in the senses. For what reason did Jesus, who
everywhere went on foot, now and only now sit upon an animal, himself
being lifted up, and hinting at his own lifting up upon the cross? What
is the village opposite? Surely the opposing mind, contrary to the world,
which the man acquired who was banished opposite paradise, to whom
Christ sent two of the disciples, as it were his Old and New Covenant,
the two Covenants. What is the ass? Surely the old and adulterous
Synagogue, whose life is weighed down toward burdens, upon which Christ
sat up to a point. What is the colt? I, the new and untrained people of
the nations, upon whom no one of men sat; not law, not fear, not an
angel, not a prophet, not Moses, not Scripture; but the God Word himself
who shared a manger with us the irrational ones in Bethlehem, that he
might free us from our irrationality, later establishing us as
theologians. And what is the loosing of the colt? Surely the freedom from
idolatry, and the abolition of the idolatrous bloods, which the touching
of the woman with the issue of blood also signified. Who are those who
grumbled at the loosing of the colt, saying: Why do you loose the colt
that is bound? The Jews who are also demons. What is the spreading
underneath of the children's garments? what the stripping of the old
man? The unseemly nakedness of the Synagogue together with its
desolation. Who are the infants that speak theology? Symbols surely of
the faithful people, offspring of the Church of Christ. Who are their
mothers? The baptismal fonts which regenerate the faithful.


What are the breasts of the mothers? The two Covenants, both our
teachers, giving us to drink the spiritual and heavenly milk. =43.505=
What is Christ's descent from the Mount of Olives, but surely the
condescension of the God Word from heaven toward us? What are the
branches of the olives? the souls of the merciful. What is the road, on
which they well spread the deeds of the virtues, stripping off the old
man? What are the palm branches of the palms? the pure hearts of the
righteous, which blossom as palms, and are multiplied as the cedars of
Lebanon, which are planted in the house of the Lord, and blossom forth,
which look rightly above toward the true palm, surely also signifying
the victory over the tyrant and over Hades, they themselves having been
freed from their tyranny by the power of Christ the victor. Who are those
going before? The righteous prophets. Who are those following? apostles,
and we who from the nations have believed with these. And the city is
the Jerusalem above; and the temple is the kingdom of the heavens, from
which Christ led out the Jews as unworthy.
Thus understand the things of your feast; thus keep the mystery of the
Master. And surely also the things of your feast *** Let us run together
dancing with angels, with the clapping crowd, leaping with the colt,
marveling with mothers, preaching with apostles, and going to meet
Christ as he enters Jerusalem, praising, blessing, saying Hosanna.
Blessed is he who came, and who comes again in the name of the Lord to
judge his world. To him be the glory, to the Father, and to the Spirit,
unto the ages. Amen.
