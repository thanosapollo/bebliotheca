faith and with Christ; for sheep well led out by the shepherd and recognizing
his voice and following him as he leads are the human beings from Israel who
leave the people remaining in the bodily practices and have followed the Lord
going out of the world, those who understand that the things of Christ are
truly divine and that he leads on from small things to greater, not from good
things into evils, which is not of a shepherd but of a thief and robber, that
