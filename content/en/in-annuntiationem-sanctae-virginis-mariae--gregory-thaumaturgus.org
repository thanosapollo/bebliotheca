Of our father among the saints Gregory, bishop of Neocaesarea in Pontus, the
Wonderworker, first discourse on the Annunciation of the all-holy Theotokos
Virgin Mary.
Today with an angelic array hymns are made bright, and the radiance of the
presence of Christ shines upon the faithful. Today the cheerful spring for us,
Christ the sun of righteousness, has shone round us with bright light, and has
illumined the thoughts of the faithful. Today Adam has been renewed [al. has
been recalled], and he dances with angels, flying up into heaven. Today the
whole earth of the inhabited world has put on joy, since the coming of the
Holy Spirit upon men has come to pass. Today the divine grace, the hope of the
unseen, flashes forth the wonders beyond thought, looking openly upon the
mystery hidden from the ages =10.1148= for us. Today crowns of everlasting
virtue are being plaited. Today God, wishing to crown the sacred heads of
those who love hearing and who love the feast, has called the lovers of
unswerving faith as called ones and heirs; and the kingdom above is eager,
calling those of heavenly mind, to join them to the divine liturgy of the
bodiless choirs. Today the saying of David has been fulfilled, which says: Let
the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice. The plains will rejoice [al.
it will rejoice], and all the trees of the wood from [al. before] the face of
the Lord, for he comes. And David said trees, but the forerunner of the Lord
called them trees producing fruits worthy of repentance, or rather of the
Lord's presence. And our Lord Jesus Christ promised the everlasting joy to all
who believe in him. For he says: I will see you, and you will rejoice; and
your joy no one takes from you. Today the celebrated and unutterable mystery
of the Christians who have sealed their hope in Christ willingly [al. holily]
is openly announced to us. Today Gabriel, who stands by God, comes to the pure
Virgin, announcing to her the Hail, full of grace. And she was reasoning of
what sort this greeting might be; and the angel immediately added, saying: The
Lord is with you; fear not, Mary; for you have found grace with God; therefore
[al. behold] you will conceive in the womb, and will bear a son; and they will
call [al. you will call] his name Jesus. This one will be great, and will be
called Son of the Most High. And the Lord God will give him the throne of
David his father, and he will reign over the house of Jacob unto the ages, and
of his kingdom there will be no end. And Mary said to the angel: How shall
this be to me, since I do not know a man? Do I still remain a virgin? Is the
dignity of virginity not being betrayed? The holy Virgin being at a loss about
these things, the angel briefly brings forward the chief point of the word,
saying: The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High
will overshadow you. Therefore also the holy thing born of you shall be called
Son of God. For what he is, this he will also in every way be called.
Fittingly then has grace chosen the holy Mary alone from all generations. For
she was
truly wise in all things; no one like [al. like, masc.] her has ever come to
be from all generations. Not as the former [al. first] virgin Eve, dancing
alone in paradise, with slack mind, received the word from the original-evil
serpent without inquiry, and so the thought of the mind was corrupted [al. by
the thought of the heart]; and through her the deceitful one, pouring out the
venom and mixing death, brought it into the whole world; and for this reason
all [al. the whole] the toil of the saints came to be; but in


the holy Virgin alone that one's fault [al. fall] has been restored. And
=10.1149= the holy one did not first endure to receive the gift, before she
learned who the sender is, and what the gift, and who the bearer.
These things the holy one was at a loss about in herself, saying, he says, to
the angel: From where have you brought us the blessing thus? From what
treasures has the pearl of the word been sent to us? From where does the gift
possess its ground [al. promise] for us? You have come from heaven, but you
walk upon earth; you show a human form, and you flash a lamp of light. These
things the holy one was considering in herself, and with such words the
archangel resolved her discernment, saying to her: The Holy Spirit will come
upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore also
the holy thing born of you shall be called Son of God. And fear not, Mary; for
I have not come to strike you with fear, but to drive away the ground of fear.
Fear not, Mary; for you have found grace with God. Do not examine the grace
according to nature. For grace does not endure to come under the laws [al.
law] of nature.
You have known, O Mary, the things that escaped the patriarchs and prophets.
You have learned, O Virgin, things that until now were hidden even from the
angels. You have heard, O all-holy one, things of which the choir of the
God-bearers has not yet been counted worthy. Moses and David, and Isaiah, and
Daniel, and all the prophets proclaimed beforehand concerning him; but the how
they did not know. But you alone, O all-holy Virgin, now receive the mysteries
unknown to all these, and you learn [al. receive, and learn] the whence. For
where the Holy Spirit is, all things have been readily prepared. Where divine
grace is, all things are possible with God. The Holy Spirit will come upon
you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you; therefore also the
holy thing born of you shall be called Son of God. And if Son of God, also
God, of like form with the Father, and coeternal, in whom the Father possesses
the whole manifestation; the imprint in the person, and through the radiance
the glory shines forth. And as from the everlasting fountain the rivers come
forth; so also from this everlasting and ever-living fountain comes forth the
light of the world, the everlasting and true, Christ our God. For concerning
this the prophets have cried: The streams of the river make glad [read: the
stream makes glad] the city of God; not one city, but all; for as one city, it
makes glad the whole inhabited world. Fittingly then did the angel [al.
archangel] first of all signify to the holy Virgin Mary: Hail, full of grace,
the Lord is with you; since with her the whole treasure of grace was stored.
For from all generations she alone has become a holy virgin in body and
spirit; and she alone bears him who bears all things by the word. And it is
possible to wonder not only at the beauty of the holy one in the body, but
also at the virtuous manner of the soul. Therefore also the Hail, full of
grace, the angel [al. archangel] first addressed to her: The Lord is with you,
and not an earthly bridegroom; =10.1152= but he himself the Lord of
sanctification, the father of purity [al. of the holy], the creator of
incorruption, and the giver of freedom, the guardian of salvation, and the
steward and supplier of true peace, who formed man from the virgin earth, and
from that one's side took [al. put forth] Eve; this Lord is with you, and
again from you. Come [al. come, plural] then, beloved, and we also, following
the angelic praise, let us, being grateful [read: gratefully], pay the debt
according to our power, saying: Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you. For
truly to rejoice is yours; since with you the divine grace, as it knows, has
tabernacled; with the servant, the King of glory; with the fair one, he who is
fair in beauty beyond the sons of men; with the undefiled, he who sanctifies
all things. With you is God, and perfect man, in whom dwells all the fullness
of the Godhead. Hail, full of grace, the fountain of the light that enlightens


all who believe in him. Hail, full of grace, the rising of the intelligible
sun [al. the rising of the sun of righteousness], and the undefiled flower of
life. Hail, full of grace, the meadow of fragrance. Hail, full of grace, the
ever-living vine, which gladdens the souls of those who glorify you. Hail,
full of grace, the untilled, fair-fruited field. According to nature, on the
one hand, because of what is according to us, and because of the time of the
exercise [gestation]; but contrary to nature, or rather above nature, because
of the God Word who sojourned from above, and refashioned Adam in your holy
womb, and the fertility the Holy Spirit furnished to the holy Virgin; but the
truth of the body was taken from her body. And as the pearl comes forth from
the two natures, from lightning and water, from the unseen signs of the sea;
so also our Master Jesus Christ comes forth unconfusedly and unchangeably from
the pure and chaste and undefiled and holy Virgin Mary, perfect in Godhead,
and perfect in manhood, like the Father in all things, and of one essence with
us in all things, apart from sin. Very many of the holy Fathers, and
patriarchs, and prophets, desired to see, and to become eyewitnesses, and they
did not become so. And some of them beheld him typically in visions in enigmas
[al. in an enigma]; and others came to be in hearing of a divine voice in a
pillar of cloud, and met with visions of holy angels; but to the holy Virgin
Mary alone did Gabriel the archangel [al. the angel] come in an illuminating
manner, announcing to her the Hail, full of grace; and thus she received the
Word, and in the time of the bodily completion she brought forth the pearl of
great price.
Come then you also, O beloved, let us sing the things shown to us from the
God-inspired lyre of David, saying: Arise, O Lord, into your rest; you, and
the ark of your sanctuary. For truly an ark is the holy Virgin, gilded within
and without, who received the whole treasure of the sanctifica =10.1153= tion.
Arise, O Lord, into your rest. Arise, O Lord, from the bosom of the Father,
that you may raise up the fallen race of the first-formed.
These things David, interceding toward the rod that was about to spring from
him, that fair-fruited flower, said prophesying: Hear, O daughter, and see,
and incline your ear, and forget your people, and the house of your father;
and the king will desire your beauty; for he is the Lord your God, and you
will worship [al. they will worship] him. Hear, O daughter, the things
formerly prophesied toward you [al. concerning you]; that you may also behold
the matters with the eyes of knowledge. Hear me foretelling to you, and the
archangel plainly announcing to you the perfect mysteries. Come then, beloved,
and we also, having run back to the memory of those who went before, let us
glorify, hymn, bless, praise the rod from Jesse that has thus sprung up beyond
nature. For Luke, bearing witness beforehand [al. bearing witness with] not
only to Joseph, but also to the Theotokos Mary, relates in the divine Gospels
her kinship with David: For Joseph, he says, went up from Galilee into a city
of Judea, which is called Bethlehem, to be enrolled with Mary his betrothed
wife, being with child, because they were of the house and lineage of David.
And it came to pass while they were there, the days were fulfilled for her to
give birth, and she bore her son, the firstborn of all creation; and she
wrapped him in swaddling-clothes, and laid him in the manger, because there
was no place for them in the inn. She wrapped in swaddling-clothes him who
wraps himself in light as a garment. She wrapped in swaddling-clothes him who
fashioned all creation. She laid in a manger him who sits above the cherubim,
and is hymned by myriads of angels. In the manger of the irrational creatures
the Word of God rested, that to those who are truly irrational in human things
[read: to men], according


to choice, he might impart rational perception. On the table of the cattle
the heavenly bread was set forth [al. the bread of life was added], that he
might
furnish to the beast-like among men a participation of mystical food. And
there was not even a place for them in the inn. He had no place, who
constructed heaven and earth by the word; since, being rich, he became poor
for us, having chosen [al. having taken up] extreme humility, for the
salvation of our nature, from his inborn goodness toward us. He who by
unutterable mysteries [al. by an unutterable mystery] of the economy fulfilled
every administration [al. righteousness], in heaven in the bosom of the
Father, and in a cave in a mother's arms, rested in the manger. Angelic choirs
surrounded him, announcing glory in heaven, and peace on earth. In heaven he
sat at the right hand of the Father; and in the manger he rested as upon the
cherubim. =10.1156= This too is truly a cherubic throne; a royal throne; Holy
of holies, alone glorious upon the earth, holier than the saints; in which
Christ our God rested. To whom be glory, honor, and the might, with the
undefiled Father, and the all-holy and life-giving Spirit, now and always, and
unto the ages of ages. Amen.

Of the same our father among the saints Gregory, bishop of Neocaesarea in
Pontus, the Wonderworker, an encomium on the Annunciation of the all-holy
Theotokos and ever-virgin Mary, second discourse.
We ought to offer all feasts and hymns to God after the manner of sacrifices;
but first of all the Annunciation of the holy Theotokos, the Hail, full of
grace, which came to her from the angel; since also first of all wisdom [al.
before all wisdom] and saving teaching upon the new covenant, the Hail, full
of grace, was sent to us from the Father of lights. And by saying full of
grace, he included the whole nature of men. Hail, full of grace, I announce to
you great joy, which shall be to all the people, upon the holy conception and
upon the glorious gestation. Again the Lord, coming toward the saving passion,
said: I will see you, and you will rejoice, and your joy no one takes from
you. And after the rising, again through the holy women, first the hail [al.
hail, plural] is announced as good news to us. Again the Apostle has cried
things like these: Always, he says, rejoice; pray without ceasing; in
everything give thanks. See then, O beloved, how the Lord has given us the joy
beyond thought and everlasting, undividedly everywhere. For since the holy
Mary, being in flesh, possessed the incorruptible way of life, having
conducted herself in virtues of every kind, and having lived better than
according to man; for this reason the Word from God the Father deigned to take
flesh, and a perfect human being from her; that through the flesh by which sin
entered into the world, and through sin death, through the same flesh sin
might be condemned in the flesh, and the tempter of sin might be conquered, in
the burial [al. in the touch] of the holy body; and the beginning of
resurrection might at the same time be received, and eternal life might be
lived in the world, and communion of God the Father with men might come to be.
And what shall we say, or what shall we omit? Or who will interpret the
incomprehensible of the mystery? But now let us return to the matter at hand.
To a pure Virgin Gabriel has been sent; the bodiless one to her who in the
body has passed over into the incorruptible way of life, in purity, and in
=10.1157= her, first the Hail, having lived in virtues. And coming toward


full of grace, the Lord is with you, he thus announces the good news to her:
Hail, full of grace. For you do the things worthy of true joy; since you have
put on the spotless tunic, and you wear the girdle of temperance. Hail, full
of grace, being a vessel of the heavenly gladness. Hail, full of grace; for
through you joy is awarded to all creation, and the race of men receives back
the ancient dignity. Hail, full of grace; because in your arms the creator of
all things is about to be carried. And she was troubled at this word; for she
was unaccustomed toward all the discourses of men; and she embraced stillness,
as mother of temperance and purity, being a pure and unmixed and undefiled
image; she did not fear the angelic sight, as most [al. many] of the prophets;
since true virginity is also a kinship toward angels, and an equal share. For
the holy Virgin, carefully guarding the lamp of virginity, had much
forethought that it not be quenched or stained. And just as one who has put on
a bright
robe makes much account lest some defilement or dirt come upon it from
somewhere; so the holy Mary considered in herself, and said: Does this
attendance perhaps possess some device or flattery? Will this hail become a
cause of grief to me, as of old to the first-mother Eve the serpent-demon's
cooperating reception of the fair-speaking promise of deification? Has the
original-evil demon, transformed again into an angel of light, and envying my
betrothed for the extreme temperance, and having put this forward to him
through perhaps a fair conversation, and not having been able, as a firm mind,
to deceive the man, come to me as to one adorned with a softer mind, and does
he cry the hail that is graceless afterward? Has the blessing and greeting
perhaps come in irony? Is some venom hidden in the honey? Is the word of one
announcing good news, but the aim of one who plunders? For how shall he greet
her whom he himself does not know? These things she was at a loss about in
herself, and said.
Then again the archangel addresses to her a joy faithful to all, and
undivided, foretelling and saying to her: Fear not, Mary [al. an undivided
joy, and he says: Fear not, Mary], for you have found grace with God. You have
a brief proof of the things said. For not only that you should fear nothing,
but I also hint the manner of the fearlessness. For through me all the
heavenly powers greet you, the holy Virgin; and rather he himself who is lord
of all the heavenly powers, from all creation, has chosen you the holy and
world-wide one; and through your holy and chaste and pure and undefiled womb
the illuminating pearl comes forth for the salvation of all the inhabited
world; since you the holy one have been born more glorious, and purer, and
holier than all human nature [al. creation]; having the mind brighter than
snow, and the body more purified than all =10.1160= gold more proven; and a
womb such as Ezekiel beheld, and of what sort he related, saying such things:
A likeness of living creatures above the head, as it were a firmament, and as
the appearance of terrible crystal, and a likeness of a throne upon them, as
the appearance of a sapphire stone; and above the throne, as a likeness of a
man, and as the appearance of amber; and within it, as the appearance of fire,
round about. And clearly the prophet beheld typically him who was born of the
holy Virgin; whom you, holy Virgin [al. blessed Virgin], were not able to
bear, unless you flashed forth with all glory and virtue according to that
time [al. according to that one's throne]. With what words of encomiums then
shall we describe the virginal dignity? With what nods and proclamations of
praises shall we hymn the spotless character? With what spiritual hymns and
words shall we glorify her who is exceedingly glorious among angels? She,
planted in the house of God, as a fruitful olive, in whom the Holy Spirit
overshadowed [al. overshadows]; through whom he has called us sons and heirs
of the kingdom of Christ.


She is the ever-living paradise of incorruption; in which the life-giving
tree, having been planted, furnishes to all the fruits of immortality [al. the
fruit of incorruption]. She is the boast of virgins, the gladness of mothers.
She is the support of those who believe, and the achievement of the pious. She
is a garment of light, and a hearth of virtue [al. of truth]. She is an
everlasting fountain, in which the living water gushed forth the incarnate
presence of the Lord. She is a fortress of righteousness, and as many as have
become lovers of her, and love the genuine character and purity of the
virginal, will enjoy the angelic grace. As many as abstain from the wine of
drunkenness [al. from wine and drunkenness], and from the strong drinks of
sweet passion, will be gladdened from the produce of the life-bearing
planting. As many as have kept the lamp of virginity unquenched will bind on
the unfading crown of incorruption. As many as have procured the spotless
tunic of temperance will be taken into the mystical bride-chamber of
righteousness. As many as have become nearer to the angelic rank will more
genuinely feast on the Master's blessing. As many as possess the illuminating
oil of the mind, and the pure incense of the conscience, will inherit the
promise of the spiritual fragrance, and of the adoption [al. of spiritual
adoption]. As many as worthily keep the feast of the Annunciation of the
Theotokos Virgin Mary more genuinely receive the recompense of the message,
the Hail, full of grace. We must therefore keep this feast, since it has
filled all the inhabited world with joy and gladness. And we keep feast in
psalms and hymns, and spiritual songs. Israel also once kept feast, but in
unleavened bread, and bitter herbs; concerning which the prophet says: I will
turn their feasts into griefs, and into mourning, and their merriment into
dejection.
And our Lord, through the fruits of repentance, has given assurance that he
will turn our griefs into =10.1161= joy. The first covenant also had
ordinances of worship, as in the case of the forefather Abraham, but in pains
of fleshly suffering through circumcision, until times of fulfillment. A law
was given to those through Moses for training; but to us grace and truth have
been granted [al. came to be] through Jesus Christ. The beginning of all good
things for us has been the Annunciation of the full-of-grace Mary, the
much-hymned economy of the Savior, his divine and above-world teaching. From
here the rays of the noetic light rise for us. From here the fountains of
wisdom and of immortality gush for us, pouring out the clear and pure streams
of piety. From here the treasures of the knowledge of God flash for us. For
this is eternal life, that we may know the true God, and Jesus Christ whom he
sent. And again: Search the Scriptures, because in them you will find eternal
life [al. you think you have eternal life]. For this reason the treasure of
the knowledge of God is unveiled to those who search the divine oracles. Today
the Paraclete has spread out for us a treasure of the God-inspired Scripture.
And let a treasure of wisdom for us be the prophetic tongue, and the apostolic
teaching; for it is not possible, apart from law and prophets, or evangelists
[al. Gospels], and apostles, to have the exact hope of salvation. For through
the tongue of the holy prophets and apostles our Lord speaks, and in the
voices of the saints God is adorned, not needing the diction, but rejoicing in
the disposition, harvesting nothing from men, but resting upon the grateful
soul of the righteous. For Christ is not magnified from the things we speak,
but from the things by which we are benefited we gratefully proclaim the
benefaction; not that we may attain to the dignity, but that we may pay the
debt according to our power. And when the Gospel is read, or an apostolic
writing, do not attend to the book, or to the reader; but to God who speaks
from heaven. For the book is the


thing seen; but Christ is what is theologized; he therefore announces to us
as good news the much-hymned economy of the Savior; that being God, through
love
of man he became man; not putting off the dignity before the ages, but putting
on the saving economy; he announces to us as good news the much-hymned economy
of the Savior, that a physician sojourned with those who labor, not healing
with drugs, but curing by a nod of love of man. He announces to us as good
news the much-hymned economy of the Savior; that to those who had wandered the
way of salvation was shown, to the despairing the saving grace, benefiting all
in various ways, seeking the wanderers, enlightening those in darkness, giving
life to those who had been put to death, freeing the slaves, redeeming the
captives, and becoming all things to all of us, the way [al. into a way] of
salvation; not because of our gratitude, but because of the goodness that
befits the benefactor himself. For the Savior did all things, not that he
might =10.1164= procure virtue for himself, but that he might acquire eternal
life for us. For having fashioned man in the image of God, he ordered him to
spend his life in a paradise of delight; but this one, deceived by the envy of
the devil, and becoming a transgressor of the divine commandment, became
liable to death. Whence also those born from him were by succession subject to
the paternal debt, being required [al. being demanded] of the reckoning of the
condemnation. For death reigned from Adam until Moses. But the man-loving
Lord, seeing his own fashioning held by death, did not turn away to the end
him whom he made according to the image; but visiting in each generation, he
did not cease; and formerly appearing in the Fathers, and being proclaimed in
the law, and being likened in the prophets, he foretold the saving economy.
And when the fullness of the times of his glorious presence came, he sent
forth beforehand the archangel Gabriel to the Virgin Mary announcing the good
news. And he, coming from the unutterable powers above to the holy Virgin,
first addressed to her the Hail, full of grace; and when the voice of the
Hail, full of grace, came to her, the Holy Spirit entered through the hearing
into the undefiled temple of the Virgin; and the thought was sanctified
together with the members; and nature stood over against, and intercourse far
off, with amazement beholding the Master of nature working a thing contrary to
nature, or rather above nature, in the body; and through the weapons with
which the devil contended, through the opposites Christ saved us, taking up
the body of like passions with us, that he might give greater grace [al. joy]
to what was lacking. And where sin abounded, grace superabounded. And
fittingly has grace been sent to the holy Virgin. For thus also the saying of
the
evangelical history contains: And in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was
sent to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house and
lineage of David; and the name of the virgin was Mary, and the rest. And this
is the first month toward the holy Virgin; as he says in the legal book: This
month is for you the beginning of months; it is first for you among the months
of the year. Keep [al. you will keep] a holy Passover to the Lord unto all
your generations. And the sixth month was toward Zechariah. And reasonably the
holy Virgin was of the race of David, and possessed Bethlehem as fatherland,
and was justly betrothed to Joseph according to the laws of kinship. And the
betrothed was a guardian, and the housemate of undefiled incorruption. And to
the holy Virgin a name exceedingly fitting. For she was called Mary; which is
interpreted illumination. For what is brighter than the light of virginity?
Whence also those who guess well at their essences name the virtues virgins.
And if it is so great a good to have a virgin heart, how great is the flesh
that has practiced virginity together with the soul? Thus also the holy Mary,
being in flesh, =10.1165= possessed the incorruptible way of life, and the
things said by the archangel she


received by faith. And then with haste she went to the hill country to
Elizabeth her kinswoman. And entering into the house of Zechariah, she greeted
Elizabeth, imitating the angel. And when Elizabeth heard the greeting of Mary,
the infant leaped in exultation in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the
Holy Spirit. Therefore the voice of Mary worked, and filled [al. filled]
Elizabeth with the Holy Spirit; and as from an everlasting fountain she sent
forth through the tongue a river of gifts, through the prophecy, to her
kinswoman; and she made the infant, with feet bound in the womb, to leap and
skip. Which was a symbol of a wonder of dancing.
For where the full of grace has come, all things have been filled with joy.
And Elizabeth cried out with a great voice, and said: Blessed are you among
women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb; and whence is this to me, that
the mother of my Lord should come to me? Blessed are you among women. For you
have become for them a beginning of the refashioning [al. of the
resurrection]. You have given us boldness of the entrance into paradise, and
you have driven away the ancient pain. For no longer after you is the race of
women reproached. No longer do the heirs of Eve fear the ancient curse, nor
the pang of childbirth. Since Christ the Redeemer of our race, the Savior of
the whole nature, the spiritual Adam, who healed the wound of the earthy one,
comes forth from your holy womb. Blessed are you among women, and blessed is
the fruit of your womb. For the planter of all good things has been shown as
your fruit. Bright indeed we see also the words of the barren one; but
brighter again did the holy Virgin speak, and she offers to God a song full of
thanksgiving, and fragrance, and theology; announcing the new things together
with the ancient; proclaiming the things at the completion of the ages
together with the things from the ages, and in a brief word having summed up
the mysteries of Christ. And Mary said: My soul magnifies the Lord. And my
spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior, and the rest. He has helped Israel his
servant, to remember his mercy, and the covenant which he made [al. disposed]
with Abraham, and with his seed, unto the age. You see how the holy Virgin
both surpassed the patriarchal perfection, and confirms the covenant of God
toward Abraham, who says:
This is the covenant which I will make between me and you. Therefore having
come he confirms [al. fulfills] the covenant toward Abraham, having mystically
received the sign of circumcision, and having become the fullness of law and
prophets. This song of prophecy then [al. this song then] the holy Theotokos
sent up [al. assigned] to God, saying: My soul magnifies =10.1168= the Lord.
And my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior. Because the Mighty One has done

his arm, for us, against death, and against the devil, and having torn [al. he
tore] the handwriting of our sins. He has scattered the proud in the thought
of heart [al. by the thought of their heart]; he has scattered the devil
himself, and all the demons who campaign for that one. For that one was proud
in the heart in the proper sense, since he dared to say: Above the clouds of
heaven I will set my throne, and I will be like the Most High. And how he now
tore him apart, the prophet made clear through what follows, saying: But now
you will go down into Hades [al. you will be brought down into Hades], and all
your armies. For everywhere he scattered his altars, and the worships of the
vain gods; and from the nations he


prepared for himself a peculiar people. He has put down the mighty from
thrones, and has exalted the humble. Accordingly in a short compass he
signified the casting out of the Jews, and the bringing in of the nations. For
the elders of the Jews, and scribes in the law, and those who were rich in the
remaining dignities, because they used the wealth and the power badly and
unlawfully, from here he put them down from every throne, both of prophecy and
of priesthood, of legislation and of teaching, and of ancestral wealth, of
sacrifices and of a multitude of feasts, and of the dignity of the kingdom.
Empty of all these, and fugitives, and naked, those were sent out into
captivity; but humble ones, instead of them, the peoples from the nations,
hungering for righteousness, were exalted; since, having shown their own
humility, and the famine of the knowledge of God that held them, they
interceded for the divine word, after the manner of crumbs, like the Canaanite
woman; and for this reason they were filled with the wealth of the divine
mysteries; for the whole inheritance of the divine good things Christ our God,
born of a Virgin, has transferred to the nations. He has helped Israel his
servant; and not of any chance Israel; but of his servant, who by the deed
ennobles the nobility of Israel. And for this reason the Theotokos called [al.
made clear] him a servant and heir. For finding him weary with the letter, and
laboring with the law, he comforted him by grace; therefore having called such
an Israel, he has helped him, to remember mercy. As he spoke to our fathers,
to Abraham, and to his seed, unto the age. And in these few words she takes up
the whole mystery of the economy. For wishing to save the race of men,
=10.1169= and to fulfill the covenant toward the fathers, Christ once bowed
the heavens and came down. And he appears thus as we can contain, that we may
be able to see him, and to handle him, and to hear him speaking. And for this
reason the God Word deigned to take flesh and a perfect man from a holy Virgin
woman, and was born man, that he might pay our debt, and might fulfill up to
himself the ordinances of the covenant toward Abraham, both of circumcision
and of the lawful things that follow. And after these words the holy Virgin
went to Nazareth; and from there again a decree of Caesar prepared her to go
up to Bethlehem; and she, as being beforehand from the royal lineage, was sent
into the royal lineage of David with Joseph the betrothed. And the mystery
beyond all wonder followed. A virgin giving birth, and carrying in her hands
him who by a word carries all creation. And there was not even a place for
them in the inn. He had no place who founded all the earth by the word.
Feeding with milk him who nourishes and gives life to every breath. Binding
with swaddling-clothes him who by a word binds all creation. She laid in a
manger him who is borne upon a seat of cherubim. Light from heaven shone round
him who illumined all creation. Heavenly armies stood by, giving glory, to him
who before the ages is glorified in the heavens. A star torch-bearing made
known those who came from the earth from far toward the true Dayspring. From
the East they have come bearing gifts to him who became poor for us; and the
holy Theotokos kept these words, comparing them in her heart, as being a
vessel of all the mysteries. Your praise, O all-holy Virgin, is above all
eulogy, because of the God who from you wore flesh, and was born man. To you
every nature of heavenly and earthly and subterranean [perhaps under-earth]
beings offers the fitting reverence. For you have truly been shown a cherubic
throne. You, at the extremities of the intelligible [al. of the south]
kingdoms, flash a radiance of light; where the beginningless Father is
glorified, whose power you had overshadowing; the Son is worshiped, whom you
have borne according to the flesh; the Holy Spirit is doxologized [al. is
glorified], which in your womb worked the birth of the great King. Through
you, full of grace,


the holy and consubstantial Trinity is made known in the world. With you,
count us also worthy to share in your perfect grace, in Christ Jesus our Lord;
with whom, to the Father be glory, together with the Holy Spirit; now and
always, and unto ages of ages. Amen [al. to whom be glory, and the might, unto
the ages of ages. Amen].
