* Fragment on the First Epistle to the Corinthians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1 Cor 4:4-5 He exhorts us not to consider it a serious matter to avenge
ourselves, nor to judge those who seem to have sinned, nor to be troubled at
those who at present escape the sight of mortals, since the Lord himself, when
he comes, will light up the hidden things of darkness of the ungodly, and of
the orderly and godly who through gentleness and attending to their own
affairs also escape the notice of the many he will make manifest the counsels
of the hearts, of whom he also says the praise will come from God.
