* Titles of the Catecheses
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1.1 Introductory catechesis to those who have come forward to baptism. and a
reading from Isaiah: wash yourselves, become clean, and the rest. 1.2
Catechesis concerning repentance and remission of sins, and concerning the
adversary. and a reading from Ezekiel: the righteousness of the righteous
shall be upon him, and the rest. 1.3 Catechesis concerning baptism and a
reading from the one to the Romans: or do you not know that as many of us as
were baptized into Christ Jesus. and the rest. 1.4 Catechesis concerning the
ten dogmas cursorily. and a reading from the one to the Colossians. see that
there not be anyone who leads you captive, and the rest. 1.5 Catechesis
concerning faith. and a reading from the one to the Hebrews: now faith is the
substance of things hoped for, and the rest. 1.6 Catechesis concerning the
monarchy of God, on I believe in one God, and against heresies. and a reading
from Isaiah: be renewed toward me, O islands, and the rest. 1.7 Catechesis
concerning the Father and a reading from the one to the Ephesians: for this
cause I bow my knees to the Father, and the rest. 1.8 Catechesis concerning
Almighty. and a reading from Jeremiah: the God the great and the mighty Lord.
and the rest. 1.9 Catechesis on Maker of heaven and earth, of all things both
visible and invisible. and a reading from Job: who is this that hideth counsel
from me, and the rest. =1.10= Catechesis on and in one Lord Jesus Christ. and
a reading from the first to the Corinthians: for even if there are those
called gods. and the rest. =1.11= Catechesis concerning the generation of the
Son from the Father before the ages and his divinity, and on through whom all
things. and a reading from the letter to the Hebrews: God having spoken of old
in many parts and in many ways, and the rest. =1.12= Catechesis concerning the
birth from a virgin of the Holy Spirit and the incarnation of Christ. and a
reading from Isaiah: and the Lord added to speak to Ahaz saying, and the rest.
=1.13= Catechesis on the one crucified and buried. and a reading from Isaiah:
Lord, who has believed our report, and the rest. =1.14= Catechesis concerning
the resurrection of Christ from the dead and the ascent into the heavens, and
the throne at the right hand of the Father. and a reading from the first to
the Corinthians: now I make known to you, brethren, my gospel, and the rest.
=1.15= Catechesis concerning the second glorious coming of Christ, and the
unending kingdom, and concerning antichrist. and a reading from Daniel: I was
considering until thrones were set, and the rest. =1.16= Catechesis concerning
the Holy Spirit the Paraclete. and a reading from the first to the
Corinthians: now concerning the spiritual things, brethren, and the rest.
=1.17= Catechesis of the remaining things concerning the Holy Spirit. and a
reading from the first to the Corinthians: for to one is given through the
Spirit a word of wisdom, and the rest. =1.18= Catechesis concerning the
catholic church, and concerning the resurrection of the dead, and concerning
eternal life, and concerning other catecheses and an epilogue of temperance.
and a reading from Ezekiel: and the hand of the Lord came upon me and he led
me out in the Spirit, and the rest. 2.τ Mystagogical catecheses 5 of John
bishop of Jerusalem. 2.1 Mystagogical catechesis, and a reading from the first
catholic letter of Peter: be sober, be watchful, because your adversary the
devil, as far as to him the power and the glory unto the ages of ages. amen.
2.2 Mystagogical catechesis concerning baptism. and a reading from the letter
to the Romans: or do you not know that as many of us as were baptized into
Christ Jesus were baptized into his death, and the rest. 2.3 Mystagogical
catechesis concerning chrism, and a reading from the catholic letter of John:
and you have an anointing in yourselves from the Holy One, and the rest. 2.4
Mystagogical catechesis concerning the body and blood of Christ. and a reading
from the first letter to the Corinthians: for I received from the Lord that
which also I delivered to you, and the rest. 2.5 Mystagogical catechesis and a
reading from the first letter of Peter: wherefore laying aside all malice and
guile and slander, and the rest.
