* What is Man
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Letter of the most blessed and most philosophical lord John, presbyter of
Damascus, sent to a certain person. What is a human being?
=95.244=
A human being is a mortal rational animal, capable of receiving intellect
and knowledge; and he is composed of four elements, that is of blood,
phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile. And the blood is in the heart, and in
the heart the breath; and the yellow bile, in the liver and in the
stomach; and the black bile, in the spleen, and in the loin; and the
phlegm, in the brain and in the bed. And those who happen to be of pure
blood, these are always rejoicing and playing and laughing, and they are
blooming, and of good complexion; those who happen to be from yellow bile
are swift and bold, irascible and bitter and of strange complexion; those
who happen to be from black bile are sluggish and faint-hearted, sickly
and lazy and cowardly; those who happen to be from phlegm are sorrowful,
and indeed also cold, forgetful and oblivious, sleeping for the most part,
and of white complexion. And in the age of childhood the blood increases
until fourteen years; and in the youths the yellow bile increases, until
twenty eight years; and in full age the black bile increases, until forty
two years; and in the old the phlegm increases, until eighty years. And
the children from the blood are hot and moist; and the youths from the
yellow bile, hot and dry; and those of full age from the black bile are
cold and dry; and the old, from the phlegm are cold and moist. The temple
then of the head is the ruler of the body; and so far as the brain should
be well settled and healthy, all the members of the body also are in
peace; and the power and the prudence are sent up from the liver to the
brain, and the soul itself is there, and the depth of the thoughts. And
the head has five sutures; and there is also a single-sutured head, and
this is always healthy. And the brain has three meninges; the first
hypozomatic; the second, characoid; and the third, ....toid. And of the
brain there are three parts; the one, cyreboid; the second, the thitos,
and the third noetic. And it is a heat-like substance. And the brain
differs from the marrow both as anelypeis and bloodless, and the marrow,
filthy and bloody. The body of the human being is composed of four
elements, since it has in itself blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black
bile. And each of these fashions its own place; the blood to the right
part above the liver; the yellow bile to the left part above the spleen;
and the black bile to the loins above the kidneys; and the phlegm to the
chest above the =95.245= breath. And the blood is hot and moist and
