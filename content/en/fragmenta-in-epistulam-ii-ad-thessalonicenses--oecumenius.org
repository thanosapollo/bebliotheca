* Fragments on the Second Epistle to the Thessalonians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1

2 Thess 1:3 Or that, for he says this, you will understand it is just; or the
"greatly" is to be heard, that it may be: greatly as is worthy of the one who
furnishes great things. 2 Thess 1:7 As if he said: with the angels that excel
in his power, or as the angels being powerful. that you may change the syntax,
with the power, he says, of his angels, that is with his powerful angels. 2
Thess 1:10 And God will be wondered at, since his testimony and our preaching
was believed upon you. And how from their believing the preaching of them is
God about to be wondered at? that if you had not heard us, he says, you would
not have been about to be deemed worthy of so many goods, and not being deemed
worthy you would not even have furnished a pretext of doxology and wonder to
Christ for the things in which he glorified you. For on that day it will
become manifest that you believed; for now many also pretend the faith. 2
Thess 2:8 Or the Holy Spirit. 2 Thess 2:9-10 Or in every deed able to deceive
and to wrong those who believe; or, as has been said, he will do the signs in
fantasy and seeming. 2 Thess 3:5 Blessed Basil in the things Toward
Amphilochius notes this, that Paul prays to the Spirit; for what lord besides
the Spirit was about to direct their hearts into the love of God and Father
and into the endurance of Christ? and no one prays except to God.
