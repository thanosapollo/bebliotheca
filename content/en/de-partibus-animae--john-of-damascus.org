* On the Parts of the Soul
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

And of the soul there are 3 parts. Desiring, spirited, reasoning. And the most
holy mind is enthroned by four virtues: of prudence, of courage, of justice,
of temperance.
=95.232= Threefold is the law. Natural, written, and the spiritual in grace.
Four-part is the body, being composed as a certain world; and tripartite in it
is the soul, prefiguring the holy Trinity, and this making it live by the
energies. And we say the soul is tripartite, having rational, spirited, and
desiring; that through the rational it may reason; and through the spirited it
may be angry at the demons, playing the man against them; and through the
desiring it may love God. And this desiring is divided into three: into
divine, natural, and midway the fleshly, which is the diabolic. Divine, to
love God; natural, to love the things according to nature; fleshly, to love
the things beside nature; for example, according to nature is marriage; beside
nature, fornication, and such things. In another I found. Of the soul there
are five powers: mind, thought, opinion, fantasy, sensation. Mind is an
intellectual sensation of the soul, according to which it can see the things
autoptically, and without inquiries. Thought is a power of the soul, according
to which with a syllogism it can know the things; wherefore it is also called
thought, from opening some way. Opinion is a power of the soul, according to
which without a cause it knows the things. Fantasy is a power of the
irrational soul. Sensation is a power of the soul, able to take hold of the
materials.
