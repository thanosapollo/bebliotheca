DISCOURSE ON JOSEPH
72 But you were sold to a eunuch man, and being preeminent in way of life you
were honored, his wife loved your beauty. A divine law had not yet written
saying: you shall not commit adultery; from where then, setting out, did you
keep an unwritten command of law? But you feared a master, inscribed in name
and seeming as husband of a profligate woman (for how was it possible for a
eunuch man to take a woman in the yoke of lawful marriage? but she, dwelling
with him clearly and being steward of his house, seemed to guard the name only
of the guardianship).
Did you then store up to be with the one who acquired you with a clean
conscience, not wishing even to defile the thing said in name, and to
vindicate a natural law for yourself, you were tried, training yourself by an
excess of temperance. 73 Reuben and Judah advised best, yet otherwise, for
they had to do with savage men. For those around Simeon and Levi, having
tasted manslaughter not moderately, had already once come into a habit of the
deed, and they longed to creep about such savory smells in a tracking way, and
they were so daring and rash men that they alone slaughtered the Shechemites
nation-wise.
