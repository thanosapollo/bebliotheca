OF GREGORY BISHOP OF NYSSA ON THE LIGHT-BEARING AND HOLY
RESURRECTION OF THE LORD
Blessed is God. Let us praise today the only-begotten God, the begetter of the
heavenly things, who has risen above the hidden recesses of the earth and with
light-bearing rays has overshadowed the whole inhabited world. Let us praise
today the burial of the Only-Begotten, the resurrection of the victor, the joy
of the world, the life of the nations of the world. Let us praise today him
who put on sin. Let us praise today the God Word, who refuted the wisdom of
the world, confirmed the prophecy of the prophets, gathered the bond of the
apostles, and unfolded the calling of the church and the grace of the Spirit.
For behold, we who were once strangers to the knowledge of God have come to
know God, and what is written has been fulfilled: All the ends of the earth
shall remember and turn to the Lord, and all the families of the nations shall
worship before him. What shall they remember? The ancient fall, the new
resurrection, the old transgression and the later correction, the death of
Eve, the birth from the Virgin, the restoration of the nations, the
forgiveness of sinners, the prophecy of the prophets, the preaching of the
apostles, the rebirth of the font =9.316=, the dwelling in paradise, the
return to the heavens, the Creator raised up, him who stripped off
unseemliness, him who by divine mighty working transmuted the corruptible into
incorruption. And what unseemliness did he put off? That which Isaiah spoke:
And we saw him, he says, and he had no form nor beauty, but his form was
without honor, failing beside the sons of men.
When was he without honor? When he consorted with the accursed Jews and was
called a Samaritan and one having a demon, when Judas Iscariot and the
offspring of darkness were prevailing unto murder against him who cannot be
contained. Not without purpose did John say to them: Offspring of vipers, who
showed you to flee from the coming wrath? For truly the wrath of God remains
upon them. When was he without honor? When they took the shoot of gentleness
with blows and with oaths questioned him who is the judge of oaths. When was
he without honor? When the judge was on trial and the judge of the world was
being judged, the household-slave asked and the master was silent, the light
was still and the darkness exulted, the creature grew bold and the Creator
endured. When was he without honor? When the bulls gored and the calf stood
by, when the lion roared and the bulls were arrogant, as it is written: Many
calves have surrounded me, fat bulls have enclosed me; they opened their mouth
against me as a lion seizing and roaring. When was he without honor? When the
dogs barked and the master endured, when the wolves seized and the sheep stood
by, =9.317= when the robber was called into life, but the life of the world
was being dragged to death, when they cried that disorderly and deadly voice,
Away with him, away with him, crucify him, His blood be on us and on our
children, the lord-killers, the prophet-killers, the fighters against God, the
God-haters, the insulters of the law, the enemies of grace, the aliens to the
faith of the fathers, the advocates of the devil, the offspring of vipers, the
whisperers, the slanderers, those darkened in mind, the leaven of the
Pharisees, the council of demons, the accursed, the utterly base, the
stone-throwers, the haters of the good. For they cried fittingly: Away with
him, away with him, crucify him. For the sojourning of the Godhead with flesh
weighed upon them, and their manner was pained at the rebuke; for it is the
custom of sinners to hate the company of the righteous. When was he without
honor? When they


scourged him and tortured his holy body as he bore sufferings willingly, that
he might heal the ancient bruises of our sins, when he bore the wood of the
cross upon his shoulders, the trophy against the devil, when they set a crown
of thorns around him who crowns those who trust in him, when they clothed in
purple him who grants incorruption to those being born again through water and
Holy Spirit, when they nailed to the wood him who is Lord of life and of
death. When was he without honor? When the soldiers, mocking, triumphed over
the master of the army of the heavens. When was he without honor? When,
putting a sponge on a reed and filling it with vinegar, they gave him to
drink, and they gave gall to him who had rained down the manna =9.318= upon
them, when the rocks were split and the veil of the temple was torn, struck at
the daring of the accursed, when the sun mourned and the darkness put on
sackcloth, mourning the fall of the Jews; for the day lamented the disasters
of the Jews, when in the midst of robbers the Life was hanging, one of them
reproaching and slandering, the other by repentance stealing paradise. When
was he without honor? When the body was given over to burial. When was he
without honor? When the soldiers kept watch and the earth hid him who had
founded the earth upon the waters, when the apostles were hiding, not able to
bear the bulk of the trials. But see, beloved, the wonders of God and after
the passion the achievements of joy. He who was without honor was changed into
good glory, and the joy of the world has been raised incorruptible together
with the body. Then the earth was in labor and the day was with child and
death spat out the life of all; for it was not possible for him who holds all
things by the word to be held by death.
Let us therefore keep festival of the three-day resurrection, the procurer of
eternal life. For just as Mary the Theotokos, without loosing virgin and
unwedded pangs, by the will of God and the grace of the Spirit bore the maker
of the ages, the God Word from God, so also the earth, loosing from her own
recesses the pangs of death, spat out, when commanded, the master of the Jews;
for she could not hold a body that had become a vehicle of immortality.
Therefore the prophet David, looking at the restoration of comeliness, the
dissolution of death, the freedom of those once slaves, cries and says: The
Lord has reigned, he has put on comeliness. What comeliness did he put on?
Incorruption, immortality, the assembly of the apostles, the crown =9.319= of
the church. No longer does Judas betray, no longer does Caiaphas threaten, no
longer is Herod armed for child-killing, no longer does Pilate sit in judgment
nor do Israelites hold power; for the corruptible has become incorruptible,
and he who was thought by them a mere man has been shown to be true God.
Therefore we also cry: Where, O death, is your sting? Where, O Hades, is your
victory? The Lord has reigned, he has put on comeliness; the Lord has put on
power and has girded himself. By power he means the economy through flesh; for
nothing is more powerful than that, since through a body the bodiless one cast
down demons, through the cross he stormed the opposing powers. For since at
first sin was shaking the earth, our Lord Jesus Christ, having risen, as he
foretold, made it firm with the wood of the cross, that it should no longer
walk toward the slip of destruction nor be tossed by the storms of error. And
let us bring as witness of the word the blessed Paul saying thus: For this
corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality.
Therefore the psalmist also says: Your throne is prepared from then, from
everlasting you are, and: Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, which shall
not be corrupted, and again: Your kingdom is a kingdom of all the ages, and
again: The Lord has reigned, let the earth exult, let many islands be glad,
for to him is the glory and the power unto the ages of ages, amen.
