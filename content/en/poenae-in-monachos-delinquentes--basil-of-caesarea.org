* Penalties for Delinquent Monks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

PENANCES.
1 If someone being healthy in body should neglect the prayers, or the learning
of the psalms, alleging allegations in sins, let this one be set apart, or let
him fast one week. 2 If someone should be rebuked upon a fault with love, and
should not accept the rebuke, entreating with all piety, and asking forgiveness
for the things in which he erred, let him also be set apart one week. 3 If
someone should know a sin of someone, and will not correct him, or rebuke in
peace with the love in Christ, but in grief, as rather triumphing the brother,
than healing the cause of the sin, let him also be set apart together with the
one rebuked upon the sin two weeks, since he rebuked as an enemy, and not as a
brother. 4 If someone insults or contradicts, being ordered by a brother the
things according to power, and does not obey with all the joy in Christ, as
fully assuring him, similarly let him be set apart one week. 5 If someone should
talk idly or jest, not keeping with fear and trembling the teaching of the
blessed Apostle and of the Gospels, let him also be set apart one week.
=31.1308= 6 If someone besides the yes and the no swears another oath, let him
also be set apart one week. 7 If someone should know a brother having faulted,
and after it has passed should reproach, as making an example, let him also as
uncompassionate and rejoicing in evils be set apart one week. 8 If someone
should know a sin of someone, and sees him bearing it indifferently, and not
propitiating the Lord with a crushing of heart and many tears, let him entreat
and remind him to repent, and to show works of repentance. And if when these
things are coming to be he should neglect his own salvation, let such a one be
separated from the company altogether. For a little leaven leavens the whole
dough, according to the holy one who said it. 9 If someone should go forth
beside the necessary needs, going around untimely, let such a one be set apart,
that his feet may be quiet in a house, as it is written. 10 If someone should be
angered in vain, and should not immediately entreat the brother whom he
grieved, that he may let go to him the sin which he sinned into him, let him
also be set apart one week. 11 If someone should be insulted by a brother, and
having been entreated by the company, should not forgive the sin, having
remembered the one who says: If anyone has a complaint toward someone; as also
Christ granted; let him also similarly be set apart one week.
