On the thief, and on the betrayal of our Savior Jesus Christ.
=59.719=
1. O how great is the power of our faith! O how much a petition of a
Christ-loving people avails when it intercedes for a sinful priest! And
let what is said not surprise you; for I have been taught to say this to
you by the divine Paul, who writes expressly in his own letters to the
disciples: Pray for me, that utterance may be given me in the opening of
my mouth. And if Paul, the vessel of election, the heavenly man, the
orator of grace, needed prayers as a gift from his own disciples, how
much more I, whom you all have known as a sinner!
Yet even a tax-collector once, turning from his wicked purpose, became a
disciple of the Lord; and I know also a harlot who brought tears of
repentance to the Master's feet and washed away the much-spoken stain of
fornication; I know also that prudent thief, who by faith made a fine
plunder of great things; for the gain of such robbery of his became
paradise. I too perceive that I have taken hold of such an anchor.
For we have such a Master who loves mankind, who, receiving only the
petition from us in sincerity, at once gives mercy in return. For he
gave a law; but the law, having a shadow of the good things to come, not
the very image of the things, was not able to give light. He sent the
prophetic people as an ambassador; but the prophetic people also, held
by death, cried out saying: I am earth and ashes. An angel could not
take flesh without change. Not an angel, he says, nor an ambassador
redeemed them, but the Lord himself. For he saw the world stripped of
help, and crying out to him: Bow your heavens, O Lord, and come down.
For if you, he says, do not come down to us, we cannot through anyone
else go up to you. He saw, then, and did not overlook the petition, but
he bowed the heavens and came down upon earth, without emptying the
heavens. He was conceived in a virgin womb broader than all creation.
For he whom a patriarchal tent did not contain, a virgin womb contained.
He was made flesh as he himself knows, not as the heretics interpret,
who are ignorant of their own things and measure the things of God. He
was born as he was well pleased.
=59.720= He mounted the wood, healing the wood of paradise, and he
cries: I have trodden the winepress alone, and there was no one with me,
not an angel, [not an ambassador, not an archangel,] not a prophet, not
a king, none of the righteous with me; and one alone was with me, not
that he crushed death with me, but that he inherited paradise with me;
with me on the wood, not with me in the war against death. For the Lord,
he says, is crushing wars; the Lord is his name. One thief confessed. O
the strange and paradoxical things! The thief with Christ, and the
disciple Judas with the devil; the light became darkness, and the
darkness was changed into light; the lamb a wolf, and the wolf a lamb.
The thief, his hands pierced through by the nails, greeted the Master
with a faithful voice, saying, Remember me, Lord, when you come in your
kingdom; and the disciple, offering a kiss with his lips, made ready the
sword in his heart; with his lips he uttered joy, and from his heart he
belched out deceit, saying, Hail, Rabbi; then he also kisses him, and
turning he said to the Jews, by a nod, not by a word: Bind him and lead
him away. O lawless Judas, where do you command to be led away the one
who says, Heaven is my throne, and the earth the footstool of my feet?
2. But I see your love more eager, and the body hard pressed; and I
wanted to rest the discourse here; but since your love of hearing urges
us to lengthen the word, let us return to the sequence of the discourse.
Hail, he says, Rabbi. But the Master who loves mankind, overcoming by
forbearance the wickedness of the betrayer, said well, Friend, for what
you have come. I greet you, Judas, not that I may give you the love
which you have thrown away, but that I may empty out the grace which I
have given you; for an unworthy vessel cannot bear heavenly myrrh. But
why do I speak of Judas? Peter, who received the key of the kingdom,
seeing the King of glory being crucified, threw down the keys and fled;
and the thief who used to pry open the doors of men himself snatched the
keys of paradise. Peter who said, Even if I must die with =59.721= you,
I will not deny you, denied him three times; and the thief, not
promising in words, but in deeds dying with him unto the resurrection,
said: Lord, remember me when you come in your kingdom. The thief did not
stand in awe of principalities and powers, and Peter could not bear a
girl's threat; the thief cried, Lord, and Peter, I do not know this man,
whom a little before he called Son of God. Peter who walked through the
sea denied the one who walked upon the wet nature; and the thief, not
having walked upon the back of the sea but upon the gates of the rich,
cried, Lord. The sons of Zebedee, who said, We are able to drink the cup
which you are about to drink (for this it was), fled at the time of the
cup; and the thief snatched from the hand of the sons of Zebedee the cup
of salvation, saying: I will take the cup of salvation, and I will call
upon the name of the Lord. Thomas, the curious investigator of the
incomprehensible mystery, said to his fellow disciples: Let us also go,
that we may die with him; and seeing him crucified he fled. He sees him
crucified and flees; he sees him rising and doubts, saying: Unless I put
my finger into the print of the nails, and my hand into his side, I will
not believe. But the God who loves mankind, who came down for one sheep,
did not despise the one sheep; but he takes it upon his shoulders, and
says: Bring your hand here, and put it into my side. I endured a
soldier's spear, and shall I not endure an apostle's right hand? But O
you good thief (for my word is to you), you learned to rob, you were not
taught to believe; you did not become a disciple, and how do you call
him Creator, saying: Lord, remember me when you come in your kingdom?
You spoke well, you believed piously.
You often robbed at risk, you often opened the treasuries of the rich,
and you did not find such a pearl as Christ. You did not become an
apostle, you did not hear the lawgiver saying, You shall see your life
hanging; and how from a tree did you gather life? You did not hear
Isaiah saying: By the bruise of his passion the passions of men receive
healing; and how in sufferings did you find a remedy? =59.722= Yes, says
the thief; I see all creation shaking and trembling, and I recognize the
one hanging on the wood as Master of creation. For if this one were not
Maker of all things, the sun, seeing him stripped, would not in shame
have hidden its own rays, and darkened those who fight against God. If
this one were not Maker of heaven and earth, heaven, seeing him
crucified, would not have clothed itself in darkness as in sackcloth. If
this one were not Master of all the earth, the earth, receiving the
blood from his side, would not have been shaken from its foundations. If
this one were not Master of life and death, death drawing near him would
not have been thrown down, and the tombs of the saints would not have
been opened.
If this one were not Lord of the temple, the temple, seeing him
crucified, would not have torn its own veil. If this one were not the
one who does not will the death of sinners, he would not have interceded
for the sinners saying: Father, forgive them; for they do not know what
they are doing. For his death set all things in order. For he goes down
into Hades, and comes up with all. Therefore I cry out saying: Lord,
remember me. I do not plead as a slave, Remember me when it goes well
with you, but, Remember me when you come in your kingdom. And what does
the Master say to him? Amen I say to you, today you shall be with me in
paradise. Let the exact accountants of the incomprehensible begetting
say how he said to the thief, Today you shall be with me in paradise?
Let them interpret this first, and then his unutterable birth, of which
he cries prophetically, Who shall declare his generation, because his
life is taken from the earth? The Fathers widened this faith; thus they
glorified the incomprehensible, believing, and not prying.
Today you shall be with me in paradise. He spoke, and it came to pass.
But, O Master who loves mankind, all the righteous stand outside
paradise, and you bring a thief into paradise? Yes, I bring a thief
first into paradise, that I may persuade the world that I did not come
to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance. To Christ himself be
the glory unto the ages of ages. Amen.
