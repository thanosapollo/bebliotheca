* Homily Delivered in the Church of Cyrinus
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of the same, from a homily spoken in the church of Cyrinus, on the thirteenth
of the month Phamenoth according to the Egyptians, in which he interprets the
saying of Isaiah that says: Death swallowed, having grown strong, and again
God took away every tear from every face.
For the God of all, being life and life-giving, wished man to be an
incorruptible living being on earth, even if he is of a corruptible nature.
But he, since he was wicked and envious, plotted against the race, set himself
against the will of the Creator, craftily brought [man] down into
disobedience, knowing that through it he would fall into death.
