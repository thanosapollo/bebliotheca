ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON JESUS NAVE.
=12.820= You, and all this people. He promised to give what he had said
beforehand to those who keep the law; for it was not to Jesus the son of Nave.
For the Israelites, not having kept it, did not receive, as what follows makes
clear. Every place upon which you shall tread. Those at Gai withstood because
of the transgression of Achar. And otherwise: Because, God having delivered
the nations into their hands, they did not utterly destroy them, and they
became a thorn to them. And they entered into the house of a harlot woman,
whose name was Rahab. The spies entrust their own salvation to a harlot; and
very reasonably. For it is shown from the things the harlot speaks, that she
alone is faithful and fears God; wherefore also through her the kindred is
saved. Of the same. Rahab is interpreted breadth, being a symbol of the Church
from the nations, saying: "The place is narrow for me; make me a place that I
may dwell." Of the same. She is saved not from the welcome alone, but first of
all from the faith and the disposition toward God. And she hid them in the
flax-stalks. She hid them with the woods of the flax-stalks which she had upon
the housetop, preparing to work the fine linen for them as being worthy of a
high-priestly garment. For the flax-stalk produces the fine linen from which
the priestly garment comes. And she let them down through the window. It is to
be known that her house was in the wall. The new things are akin to the old;
for what happened in the case of Paul is similar, when they let him down in a
basket through the wall. Of the same. The Apostle was saved a second time from
the enemies in like manner, as he says in his second Letter to the
Corinthians: "And through a window I was let down in a basket through the
wall, and I escaped." Again in the Acts: "And they were watching the gates
also by day and by night, that they might kill him; but his disciples, taking
him by night, let him down from the wall, having lowered him in a basket." And
they remained there three days. Not because the way was of three days; for the
Jordan is very near =12.821= Jericho, but because of the precise searching of
the spies. "For the pursuers," it says, "sought all the ways, and did not
find." Forty thousand well-girt. It is to be noted that the four, whether of
units, or of hundreds, or of some other greater number, is set upon
affliction. Make for yourself knives of stone from a sharp-cut rock. It is to
be inquired why those born in the wilderness were not circumcised; or why
someone gathering wood on the Sabbath in the time of Moses became stoned by
command of God. But so great a multitude remaining uncircumcised in the
wilderness was not even blamed, although God on the one hand had declared that
the soul not circumcised on the eighth day is to be utterly destroyed, but on
the other hand no longer having ordained this upon those who transgress the
Sabbath. Already then even from this it is quite clear, that circumcision and
Sabbath are not good in themselves, but because they were useful for a season.
And it is to be known that for the sake of a sign he wished them to be
recognized through circumcision, and not being content to appear from faith,
as irrational sheep by the brand even if it wander, has an easy finding of
itself; so wishing the Jews, mixing with the nations, to be manifest from the
circumcision. And a proof of this is that in Egypt they were circumcised, but
in the wilderness, since they were alone and separated from Egyptians and from
the other nations in Palestine, they remained uncircumcised. But when, having
crossed the Jordan, they were about to be in the midst of the nations, they
were again commanded to be circumcised. Wherefore he said a second time,
because they were not circumcised as eight-day-olds, but already having become
men, and some having inclined toward old age. And the stone knife shows us the
Lord, who gave us the intelligible circumcision, that into mind and heart. For
on this account the Apostle also used to say to us a circumcision of Christ,
not in the putting off of our flesh. I am commander-in-chief of the power of
the Lord; now I have come. One must remember that when they made the calf God
says to Moses, that "Lead this people up, for I will not go up together,
because the people is stiff-necked. And behold I will send my angel before
your face. Take heed therefore to yourself, and do not disobey." But Moses
says to him: "If you do not go up with us, do not lead me up from here." And
having said this he persuaded; and he was with the people, until the end of
Moses. But after this one's death, he appears and converses with Jesus the son
of Nave. After the superintendence of the people, then, unto the disciple of
Moses, he thereafter delivers him to the commander-in-chief Michael. This one
was ruler of the people, as in Daniel another angel appears saying. Going and
trumpeting. These very trumpets are a symbol of the things on the last day.
"For," it says, "the Lord himself with a command, with the voice of an
archangel, and with a trumpet of God, will come down from =12.824= heaven."
And the priests circling the city for six days would be a sign that through
the six days of the present age all the saints trumpet and proclaim remission
of sins to those who wish to repent. And Jesus said to the sons of Israel, Cry
out. The cry, however, the one of the words, is likely a doxology and a voice
of praise toward God, fighting against the error of the men of Jericho, and
winning the paradoxical victory, one full of wonder. Thus Ozan did. From this
it is shown that it was not Jesus the son of Nave, but some other after many
generations who wrote these things. And this, understood as true, is not
contrary to what we have said before. For Jesus wrote the things that happened
in his time, or that were revealed, just as Moses also the things according to
the times upon him. And after these things, the temple already having been
built by the wise Solomon, and the people having declined into much impiety,
and God having left the temple desolate so that the Deuteronomy was scarcely
found thrown as it happened; for this reason, the people having undergone the
Babylonian captivity, Ezra being most skilled in the law, and reciting by
heart all the Old Testament, wrote the law, and certain other things that had
happened and been revealed. These things were of old spoken beforehand, but
after these things they came to pass. Such then is also that of which we have
now made mention. I am still strong today, as when Moses sent me. In both
words then the holy one is strong. For he is strong in old and in new
mysteries. This is the being strong in the time of Moses and in the time of
Jesus.
