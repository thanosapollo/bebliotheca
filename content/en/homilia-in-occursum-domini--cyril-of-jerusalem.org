* Homily on the Meeting of the Lord
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of our father among the saints Cyril, archbishop of Jerusalem, a discourse on
the Meeting of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ and on Simeon who received
God. 1 Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion. Proclaim joy, daughter of Jerusalem.
Dance, people of the city of God. Leap, gates and walls of Zion and all the
earth. Cry out, mountains, and you hills, leap greatly. Rivers, clap your
hands, and crowds of Zion, gather round, seeing God's presence in her. Let the
heavenly things be in concert today with the earthly, and let the Jerusalem
above hymn with the Jerusalem below, because of Christ who is in her, heavenly
and earthly. Dance around the heavenly one, you intellectual powers; hymn the
earthly one, you who are on earth, together with angels. 2 For today the God
of gods has been seen in Zion. Today glorious things have been spoken of you,
O city of God, Jerusalem, city of the great King: open the gates to him who
opened the heavenly gates for all, and opened the gates of paradise for all,
and opened the gates of the tombs upon the cross, and crushed the age-old
gates of Hades, and shut, beyond expectation, the gates of virginity. Today he
who of old gave oracles to Moses on Mount Sinai in a manner befitting God
fulfills the law by becoming under the law in a manner befitting a servant.
Today God comes from Teman into Zion. Today the heavenly bridegroom arrives in
the temple with the bridal chamber of the Mother of God. Daughters of
Jerusalem, go out to meet him. Kindle the lamps brightly for the true Light;
make ready the tunics of your souls for the bridegroom Christ. 3 Together with
Zion let us of the nations go to meet him, bearing light. In the temple let us
enter together with the Temple, even God and Christ. With angels let us cry
the hymn of the angels: Holy, holy, holy Lord of Sabaoth, heaven and earth are
full of his glory. The ends of the world are full of his goodness. All
creation is full of his praise. All humanity is full of his condescension. The
heavenly things, the earthly, the things under the earth are full of his
compassion, full of his mercy, full of the mercies, full of the gifts, full of
his benefactions. 4 Therefore all you nations, clap your hands. All you ends
of the earth, come and see the works of God. Let every breath praise the Lord.
Let all the earth worship, let every tongue sing, let every one chant, let
every one glorify a child who is God, forty days old and before the ages, a
little child and Ancient of Days, a nursing child and maker of the ages. I see
an infant and I know my God, an infant nursing and feeding the world, an
infant wailing and granting life and joy to the world, an infant swaddled and
redeeming me from the swaddling-bands of sin, an infant in a mother's arms
with flesh truly and without lack on earth, and him also in the bosom of the
Father truly and without lack in the heavens. 5 I see an infant entering from
Bethlehem into Jerusalem and in no way parted from the Jerusalem above. I see
an infant bringing a sacrifice to the temple according to the law on earth,
but himself receiving the pious sacrifices of all in the heavens; him in the
old man's arms according to the economy, and him on cherubic thrones in a
manner befitting God; him being offered and purified, and him purifying and
cleansing all things. He is the gift and he is the temple. He is the high
priest and he is the altar, he is the mercy-seat [and he is the intercessor].
And he is the one who offers and he is the one offered as a sacrifice for the
world, and he is the trees of life and of knowledge, he is the lamb and he is
the fire. He is the whole-burnt offering and he is the sword of the Spirit. He
is the shepherd and he is the little lamb. He is the sacrificer and he is the
one sacrificed. He is the one carried up and he is the one who receives the
sacrifice. He is the law and he is the one now coming under the law. 6 But
come then, let us hear the things of the day from the sacred Gospels. For the
marvelous Luke says concerning Christ that, when the days of their
purification were fulfilled according to the law of Moses, they brought Jesus
up to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord, as it is written in the law of
the Lord, that every male who opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord.
Samuel, then, and Isaac together with Jacob and Joseph and others who were
born beyond hope from barrenness and opened barren wombs of mothers were
called holy to the Lord; but Christ, the only one from the only one, the
Only-begotten of the Only One, born of a virgin and not opening virginal
gates, is not holy to the Lord, but Holy of Holies and Lord of lords and God
of gods and firstborn of the firstborn, ruler of rulers and King of those who
reign, and he shall be called and believed and worshipped, and now in the
temple he shall be proclaimed by Simeon. 7 For there was, it says, a man in
Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. And this man was righteous, and the Holy
Spirit was in him. And it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he
should not see death before he had seen the Christ of the Lord. And he came in
the Spirit into the temple. And when the parents brought in the child Jesus,
for them to do according to the custom of the law concerning him, Simeon
himself received him into his arms and blessed God and said: Now you let your
servant depart, Master, according to your word, in peace, for my eyes have
seen your salvation, which you have prepared before the face of all the
peoples, a light for revelation to the nations and glory of your people
Israel. Who shall speak the mighty deeds of the Lord, who shall make all his
praises heard? He who holds the whole earth in his handful is contained in an
old man's arms, and he who bears all things by the word of his power is
carried. 8 Let Adam rejoice through Simeon, saying to Christ: Now you let your
servant depart, Master, according to your word, in peace. Now you release me
from the eternal bonds; now you release me from corruption. Now you release me
from death. Now you release me from sorrow, my Son and God. Taking him in his
arms in the temple, Simeon leaps and rejoices, proclaiming to all the nations
and to the Jews the strange mystery of the economy, and with a bright and
piercing voice he has cried out concerning him, saying: This is the One who is
and who was before and who is always present with the Father, consubstantial,
sharing the throne, sharing the glory, sharing the power, equal in power,
almighty, without beginning, uncreated, unchangeable, uncircumscribed,
invisible, ineffable, incomprehensible, intangible, inconceivable,
inscrutable. 9 This is the radiance of the Father's glory. This is the imprint
of the constitution of all things. This is the light of lights, rising from
the Father's bosom. This is the God of gods, and God known from God. This is
the fountain of life proceeding from the fountain of the Father's life. This
is the river of God, going forth from the divine abyss, but not parted from
it. This is the treasure of the Father's goodness and everlasting blessedness.
This is the water of life and that which grants life to the world. This is the
uncreated ray, begotten from the sun that is light beforehand, but not cut
off. 10 This is he who, God the Word, by word alone constituted all things
from non-being into being. This is the Morning Star who before the morning
star ordered the bodiless powers of the heavenly, bodiless, invisible armies
and ranks. This is he who stretched out the heaven alone and walks upon the
sea as upon a floor. This is he who swaddled the abyss with mist. This is he
who established the earth upon the waters. This is he who walled the sea with
sand. This is he who divided the light from the darkness. This is he who
arranged the circling order of the stars. This is he who in wisdom constituted
the whole visible and intelligible world. This is he who with hands not made
by hands formed and shaped man from clay. This is he who created us according
to the image of God, and now himself has become man according to our image.
Man, and yet also God, the same. Man in what is seen, wholly as I myself am,
with what is mine, that having cleansed me he might save me; God in what is
thought, perfect, having the essence from a perfect Father. Who, being in the
master's form of God, has now taken my servant form, not diminishing the
dignity of the Godhead, but sanctifying the lump of my nature. Above wholly
and below wholly, the same is known, begotten above without time and below
without seed, maker of the things above as God and a creature below as man. 11
Hear these things, all you nations; hear, Israel. The Lord your God is this
one whom I, your priest Simeon, carrying in my arms, proclaim with a great
voice to the people in the temple, bearing witness. Therefore attend, my
people, to my word; incline your ear to the words of my mouth. This is the
child of whom Isaiah proclaimed beforehand, saying that a child has been born
to us, a son also has been given to us, and his name is called Angel of Great
Counsel (that of the Father), Wonderful, Counselor, Mighty God, Sovereign, O
Israel, Prince of Peace, Father of the age to come. If then this child is
Mighty God, it is clear that David said concerning him: The God of gods shall
be seen in Zion. Concerning this child Jeremiah cries: that God was seen upon
the earth and conversed with men. 12 This child is he who of old split the sea
for Israel and sank Pharaoh and gave the law to the Israelites and rained down
the manna and led the race of the Hebrews by a pillar of fire and split the
rock and kept the bush unburnt of a fire-dewed flame. This child made Moses at
forty years a slayer of the Egyptian people and showed him for forty years a
shepherd of sheep and set him for forty years as a guide to Israel, and
empowered him to fast forty days, as the child himself also fasted forty days
after the baptism, and after forty days from the resurrection from the dead
went up into the Jerusalem above, and after forty days from the birth from the
Virgin has now entered into the earthly Jerusalem. 13 Concerning this child
Habakkuk proclaimed beforehand, saying: God will come from Teman, that is,
from the south. For from the south of Jerusalem, from Bethlehem, he has now
entered into Zion. Concerning this child Moses bore witness to you, saying
that the Lord God will raise up for you a prophet from your brothers, and
every soul that does not hear him shall be utterly destroyed. Concerning this
child the prophet David prayed, saying: You who sit upon the cherubim, appear;
stir up your might and come to save us. And again: Let your mercies quickly
overtake us, O Lord. And again: Lord, bow your heavens and come down. And
otherwise: You, Lord God of hosts, attend to visit all the nations. Therefore
know, nations, and be defeated. 14 See, Jews, and be persuaded that angels
hymn this child, archangels worship him, authorities tremble at him, powers
glorify him, the cherubim serve him, the seraphim speak of him as God. The sun
serves him, the moon ministers to him, the elements obey him, the springs are
subject to him. Seeing this child the gates of Hades were crushed, but the
heavenly gates were flung open, and Hades seeing him trembled. This child
abolished death, put the devil to shame, loosed the curse, made sorrow cease,
crushed the serpent, cut through the middle wall, tore up the wicked bond of
sins, trampled sin, dissolved error, raised up creation. This child saved
Adam, refashioned Eve, called the nations, enlightened the world. 15 Therefore
come also, you who love Christ and love God. At the meeting of the Lord and
Master let us all go to meet him, bright and clean, not according to the law
but spiritually, not luxuriating in the belly but leaping in the spirit, not
drunk with wine but fervent in the Spirit. Thus today, being bright, let us
brightly adorn the lamps. Thus as sons of light let us bring the tapers to the
true Light, Christ, because a light for revelation to the nations has shone
upon the world. Therefore as lights from light let us shine out beyond snow,
curdled beyond milk, gleaming beyond a sapphire stone, flying up into the
heavens beyond spotless doves; thus in clouds let us go out to the meeting of
God. 16 Let us all today and through all time cry the things of the feast. Let
us dance with angels. Let us be illumined with shepherds. Let us worship with
Magi. Let us keep festival with Bethlehem. Let us go to meet him with Zion.
Let us be sanctified with the temple. Let us rejoice with the Virgin,
magnifying. With Joseph let us offer as two turtledoves the soul and the body.
With Simeon let us take Christ in our arms, and with Anna let us give thanks.
That we may come within the eternal goods, by the grace and mercies and love
of mankind of our Lord and God and Savior Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory
and the might with the Father and the Holy Spirit unto the ages of ages. Amen.
