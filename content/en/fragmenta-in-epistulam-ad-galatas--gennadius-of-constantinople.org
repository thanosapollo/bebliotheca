* Fragments on the Epistle to the Galatians

:CUSTOM_ID: h1

Gal 3: He says then that Moses indeed mediated toward the giving of the law, but
he mediated for one nation only; and he who is truly going to mediate toward the
one God of all nations must make the mediation not for one nation but for all
commonly, since also of all nations there is one God. And this would not be a
bare man such as Moses, but Christ the son of God, being both truly God the same
and at once truly a man, and by the kinship toward each gathering both into one
in himself; for he himself is our peace, who made both one.
{Gal 3:21} Therefore it is not possible nor even reasonable to say that the law
has been given upon a taking-away of the blessings. {Gal 3:23} Since then, he
says, not from a beginning but later. it was seeming to God for us to enjoy
justification by grace, reasonably our nature was meantime walled around by
laws, being both hindered and held back toward the energy of each sin, that we
might be released from the guard according to law ready into the justification
spread out through faith.
4 {Gal 4:3} In the order of the guardians and stewards who pedagogue those
incomplete in age toward the profitable he took the elements of the world to
which we were profitably enslaved because of the incomplete of knowledge. He
would not, as I think, have taken sun and moon into an order of guardians
because of the unprofitable being the subjection to these, but he says elements
of the world or the elementary introductory law; for he wrote to the faithful
from Jews, and it shows both the things said a little above and the if you are
circumcised Christ will benefit you nothing. or he calls elements days, water,
fire of which the observations were from the law that led into slavery; of days
in sabbaths and new-moons and circumcisions, of water in the bodily purities and
baptisms, of fire in not kindling fire on the sabbaths but eating stale things.
And see what strange thing Eusebius said unto this in the fourth discourse of
the Evangelical Demonstration. {Gal 4:17 1} Not upon a good, he says, do they
embrace the same faith of you into Christ, but that having shut you out of this
they may persuade you to follow themselves, toward your deceit such a thing has
been machinated for them.
