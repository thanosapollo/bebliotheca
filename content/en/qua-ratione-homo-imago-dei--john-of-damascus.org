* In What Way Man is the Image of God
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of John the monk, presbyter of Damascus, surnamed Mansur, concerning theology:
how a human being images the Divine.
=95.228= That man is according to the image and likeness of God, this shows
that his intellectual and rational soul is in three properties; that is mind
and word and spirit, as upon the divine substance, Father and Son and Holy
Spirit. For as the Father is Father and not Son; and the Son is Son and not
Father; and the Spirit is Holy Spirit and not Son or Father; in the same way,
the mind is mind and not word; the word is word and not spirit; and the spirit
is spirit and not word or mind. These therefore are neither confused by unity
nor divided by number; for the three are one, and the one into three, of the
same nature and of the same substance, coexisting with one another always.
