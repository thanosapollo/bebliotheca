=27.1344= OF ATHANASIUS. Now as for Scripture's saying: And there were born to
him seven sons, this too will serve us against the Ariomaniacs, who hold as a
great support of their heresy what is said by the Apostle concerning the
Only-begotten: Being faithful to him who made him; and they contend that the
Son of God is a thing made. But this prop of theirs is a bruised reed. For
when the substance is a thing made and a creature, then "he made" and "he
created" are said of them in the proper sense, and they signify the thing
made; but when the substance is a begotten one and a son, then "he made" and
"he became" and "he created" are no longer laid down of him in the proper
sense, nor do they signify a thing made, but one uses the verb "he made"
indifferently in place of "he begot." So that the Arians will condemn
themselves, if they learn the idiom of Scripture; for even if parents should
speak of those who spring from them as being made and becoming and being
created, they none the less do not deny the nature. Hezekiah, at any rate, as
it is written in Isaiah, said in prayer: For from today I will make children,
having said "I will make" instead of "I will beget," and having named those
who spring from him. And there is no difference. And here too it is said
concerning Job: And there were born to him seven sons; just as Moses also says
in the law: If they be born. One must first inquire whether he is a son. For
when this is shown, the suspicion concerning a thing made and a creature
ceases, and it is a clear demonstration to all that the phrase which says, to
him who made him, is no profit to their heresy, but rather a condemnation. For
it has been shown that the phrase "he made" is laid down in the divine
Scripture even of genuine and natural children. Hence, when the Lord is shown
to be by nature a genuine Son, even if "he made" or "he became" is said of him
(sic), it is not said as of one who is a thing made, but the saints use the
phrase indifferently. =27.1345= And God said to the devil: From where have you
come? Of Athanasius. At the same time it is shown in these things that, even
if all generated things are in divided places, the lights are in the
firmament, and the clouds in the air. And for men he set the bounds of the
nations; but the angels are sent forth unto ministrations, and they go up and
go down upon the ladder. And here: They came, he says, to stand before the
Lord. And a question is put to the devil: From where have you come? So that
they are in a place, and where they are sent, there they are present; but God
alone is uncircumscribed; they seek the one present everywhere, that they may
find the unbounded one shut up in some district. But to stand before him, this
is to be formed by his wills, and to stretch oneself wholly toward him. For
all creation is before God, even the apostate and most wicked itself,
according to the uncircumscribed character of that substance; but the angels
are present to God in a special way, according to the insatiable desire of
divine love. Athanasius. Job then was contending, and the trial was great; but
the athlete appeared greater than the trial. And the tempter was rushing on in
force, and the one fighting was hard; but the one being tried appeared more
manly, overcoming the attempts and assaults of the evil one by the greatness
of his mind. Endurance is the mark of manliness. For by this each of the
saints, bearing the toilsome and grievous things that have come about in life,
was strong against the devil; which things the Spirit, wishing us to acquire
both, commands saying in the psalm: Be manly, and let your heart be
strengthened, and wait for the Lord. For the one who endures is manly, and the
manly one would be enduring. Wherefore the blessed Job also was strengthened
through enduring, and through bearing the pains manfully he endured, and he
thrust away the wife, who had been deceived by the serpent and whose thoughts
had been corrupted. For since the inventor of wickedness is altogether a
hater, and appearing only seeking whom he may snatch and swallow; for this
reason he hides and covers what he himself is; but feigning in appearance the
truth longed for by all, he casts his own poison upon those who have followed
him. Thus he also deceived Eve, not speaking his own things, but feigning the
words of God, and falsifying their meaning. Thus he also put forward the wife
of Job, persuading her to feign love of her husband, but teaching her to
blaspheme against God. Thus the deceitful one plays with men by appearances,
stealing them away and dragging each into his own pit of wickedness. If he
does not trust his servants, and against his angels he devised something
crooked. Of Athanasius. But the nature of generated and created things
=27.1348= is changeable, inasmuch as it is outside the substance of God, and
came to subsist from things that are not. Wherefore every man is a liar; and
all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God. But the devil, being in
the midst of the cherubim, and having become a seal of likeness, fell from the
heavens as lightning. And we shall judge angels, namely those who did not keep
their own beginning. And here: if he does not trust his servants, and in a
word created things have such a nature, and they change. But the divine nature
alone is unalterable. Beside papyrus, and reed, and rush. Perhaps through the
reed and the papyrus Scripture hints at the cheapness and poverty of the
devil. His inward parts are bronze asps. Athanasius. Of old then the enemy was
much given to craft against men, and by his appearances he deceived many, and
the grasping of his deceit was difficult; so that the spirit also says
concerning him: Who will uncover the face of his garment? and what follows.
For he did not appear as what he was, but toward whatever he was crafting,
toward that he also made a show of himself. For this reason many also were
deceived by him; but when the Saviour sojourned, then all his appearance was
put to shame; for the things he was doing of old unknown are known by the
grace of Christ, and in the things in which of old he deceived men, in these
he is trampled by them. For faith is stronger than every diabolical assault
and madness of demons.

FROM THE COMMENTARY ON JOB. Chap. I, v. 7.

is also shown in these things, and that all generated things are in divided

set the bounds of the nations, but the angels are sent forth unto
ministrations, and they go up and go down upon the ladder; and here they came,

seek the one present everywhere as in a place, that they may find the

stealing them away and dragging each into his own pit of wickedness. Chap. IX,
v. 5. Who makes mountains old, and they do not know, who overturns them in
wrath. Mountains are the prophets, Moses and those in succession. And
Scripture makes it clear: I lifted my eyes to the mountains, from where my
help will come. 23 He who has given his understanding to the words of the
prophets has lifted his eyes to the mountains, seeing that his help will come
from the words understood in the prophets from the Lord who made heaven and
earth, who filled the pure heart of the prophets with mysteries. So long then
as the new covenant does not come the prophets were not old, for nowhere in
connection with a covenant was it said before this; but Christ is the one who
makes mountains old, Moses and the prophets. And they do not know: Who then do
not know? those to whom he was saying: If you believed Moses, you would
believe in me; for concerning me he wrote. If the Jews had known that the
mountains have been made old, they would have left them, that is the oldness
of the letter, and they would have changed and come to the new covenant; and
they would have served God in newness of Spirit, and not in oldness of letter;
he adds then: the Jews therefore do not know either the one who made them old,
or the things that have been made old. Wherefore he who overturns them in
wrath, he says, that is Jerusalem according to the letter or of the letter.
Where is the sensible altar? Has my Jesus done a strange thing? having come he
both made his own prophets old and renewed them, he made them old, how? the
ministry of death that was stamped in the letter was, he says; he renewed,
how? the law, he says, is holy and his commandment holy and just and good; but
those who had not learned the spiritual name did not know these things. 24 v.
6. Who shakes what is under heaven from the foundations. Christ is the one who
shook what is under heaven; many earthquakes happen in part; an earthquake
happened here; but those in the neighboring city did not know; but in the case
of Christ it did not happen partially, therefore who shakes what is under
heaven from the foundations; and its pillars shaking, clearly the apostles
were shaken at the time of the passion. All of you, he says, will be
scandalized in me in this night. v. 7. Who says to the sun, and it does not
rise, and upon the stars he seals. And this happened at the cross; And upon
the stars he seals; it is reported that in the three hours in which the sun
failed, stars appeared in heaven as in night. v. 9. Who makes Pleiades, and
Hesperus, and Arcturus, and the chambers of the South. These studies are
beyond me. For though there are many of the Pleiad stars, planets and fixed
being listed by those who do not know as they themselves suppose (there is
also Hesperus, there is also Phaethon, and Phaenon, and Stilbon and
Phosphorus) why then did Job, speaking in the Spirit, from so many stars in
the fixed sphere name the Pleiad alone? and from so many so-called planets did
he mention Hesperus alone? and one other, Arcturus who is in the north, the
one called by some the bear-ward?
