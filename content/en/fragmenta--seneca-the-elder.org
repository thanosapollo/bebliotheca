** 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h3
:END:
/But the new and especially the declaimers imagine more boldly, and not without a certain emotion, as Seneca in the controversia whose sum is that a father killed a son and a stepmother caught in adultery, the other son leading him in: "Lead, I follow: take this aged hand and press it wherever you wish" and a little later: "Look, he says, at what you long did not believe. But I do not see; night rises and a thick fog."/

/For to swear in general, except where it is necessary, little befits a serious man, and it was said elegantly by Seneca that this belongs not to advocates but to witnesses./
