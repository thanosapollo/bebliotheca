RUTH.
=12.989= And the name of the second was Ruth. Ruth bears a type of the
nations: she who left her ancestral things and was united to the Israelite way
of life. But it must be asked how, though the law forbids a Moabite to enter
the assembly of God, Ruth, being a Moabitess, entered. And seeking this, use:
"The law is not laid down for a righteous person, but for the unrighteous and
insubordinate."
