Another prayer of the same. Let us beseech the Lord. You who rebuked all
unclean spirits, and by the power of a word drove out the legion, appear even
now through your only-begotten Son, upon the creature whom you also made as
your image, and deliver it that is being overpowered by the adversary; that
having been shown mercy and cleansed it may be enrolled in your holy flock,
and be kept a living temple of the Holy Spirit and of the divine and undefiled
sanctifications, by the grace and compassions and love of mankind of your
only-begotten Son, with whom you are blessed, together with your all =64.1064=
holy and good and life-giving Spirit; now and always, and unto the ages of
ages. Amen.

Another prayer of the same. Let us beseech the Lord. We call upon you, Master
God Almighty, Most High, untempted, peaceful King, we call upon you who made
the heaven and the earth. For from you sprang forth both the Alpha and the
Omega, the beginning and the end, who gave to men that four-footed and
irrational animals should obey; because you subjected them, Lord. Stretch out
your mighty hand, and your high and holy arm, and in visiting visit this your
creature. Send down upon it a peaceful angel, a mighty angel, a guardian of
soul and body, who shall rebuke and drive away from it every wicked and
unclean demon. For you alone are Lord Most High, Almighty, blessed, unto the
ages of ages. Amen.

Another prayer of the same. Let us beseech the Lord. We make the divine and
the holy and great, and fearful, and unbearable naming and invocation toward
your expulsion, apostate, and likewise a rebuke unto your destruction, devil.
The holy God, the beginningless, the fearful, the invisible in essence, the
incomparable in power, and incomprehensible in divinity, the King of glory,
and Almighty Master, shall rebuke you, devil: he who from non-being into being
constituted all things well by a word, he who walks upon the wings of the
winds. He rebukes you, devil: he who calls the water of the sea and pours it
out upon the face of all the earth; Lord of hosts is his name. The Lord
rebukes you, devil: he who is ministered to and hymned in fear by the
innumerable heavenly fiery ranks, and from a multitude of angelic and
archangelic choirs is worshipped and glorified in trembling. The Lord rebukes
you, devil: he who is honored by the powers standing round about, and by the
most fearful six-winged and many-eyed Cherubim and Seraphim, who cover their
own faces with two wings because of his unseeable and unsearchable Godhead,
and with two wings cover their own feet, that they may not be burned from his
ineffable glory and inconceivable majesty, and with two wings fly and fill the
heaven from their cry, Holy, Holy, Holy Lord of Sabaoth, heaven and earth are
full of his glory.

The Lord rebukes you, devil: God the Word who came down from heaven from the
Father's bosom, and through the ineffable and undefiled incarnation from the
holy, worshipful Virgin appeared unspeakably in the =64.1065= world to save
it, and by his sovereign power cast you down from heaven, and showed you to
all as one dismissed. The Lord rebukes you, devil: he who said to the sea, Be
silent, be muzzled; and at the command it immediately abated. The Lord rebukes
you, devil: he who with the undefiled spittle made clay, and fashioned the
lacking member for the man born blind, and granted the light. The Lord rebukes
you, devil: he who by a word made the daughter of the ruler of the synagogue
live, and snatched the widow's son from the mouth of death and granted him
whole and healthy to his own mother. The Lord rebukes you, devil: he who
presented Lazarus from the dead, four days, uncorrupted as one who had not
died, and unstained, unto the astonishment of the many. The Lord rebukes you,
devil: he who through the slap abolished the curse, and through the spear of
his undefiled side withdrew the flaming sword that guards paradise. The Lord
rebukes you, devil: he who through the spitting upon his precious countenance
wiped away every tear from every face. The Lord rebukes you, devil: he who
planted the cross as one support and salvation of the world, but unto your
fall and that of all the angels under you. The Lord rebukes you, devil: he who
gave voice upon his cross, and the veil of the temple was torn, and the rocks
were split, and the tombs were opened, and those who had died from of old
rose. The Lord rebukes you, devil: he who by death put death to death, and by
his rising granted life to men. May the Lord rebuke you, devil: he who went
down into Hades, and shook out its tombs, and freed all the prisoners held in
it, and called them back to himself; whom the gatekeepers, seeing, shuddered,
and hiding in the agony of Hades they vanished. May the Lord rebuke you,
devil: Christ our God who rose from the dead and granted his resurrection to
all. May the Lord rebuke you, devil: he who was taken up in glory into the
heavens to his Father, and sits at the right hand of the Majesty upon a throne
of glory. May the Lord rebuke you, devil: he who is coming again with glory
upon the clouds of heaven, with his holy angels, to judge the living and the
dead. May the Lord rebuke you, devil: he who prepared for you the unquenchable
fire, and the sleepless worm, and the outer darkness unto eternal punishment.
May the Lord rebuke you, devil: he at whom all things shudder and tremble
before the face of his power, because unbearable is the wrath of his threat
against you.
The Lord himself rebukes you, devil, through his fearful name: shudder,
tremble, be afraid, withdraw, be destroyed, be put to flight, you who fell
from heaven, and with you all the wicked spirits, every wicked spirit, spirit
of licentiousness, spirit of wickedness, spirit of night and of day, of midday
and of evening; midnight spirit, spirit of apparition, spirit of encounter,
whether of the land, or of the water, or in groves, or in reeds, or in
ravines, or in =64.1068= passages and crossroads, in lakes, and in rivers, in
houses, in courtyards, and in baths, running by and harming, and altering the
mind of man.
Depart quickly from the creature of the Creator, Christ our God, and be
released from this servant of God, from mind, from the soul, from the heart,
from the kidneys, from the senses, from all the members, so that he may be
healthy and whole, and free, recognizing his own Master, and Creator, the God
of all, who gathers those who have gone astray, and gives them a seal of
salvation through the birth and renewal of divine baptism, that he may be
counted worthy of his undefiled, and heavenly, and fearful mysteries, and be
united to his true flock, pitching tent in a place of green pasture, and being
nourished upon water of rest, and being led pastorally and safely by the staff
of the cross, unto forgiveness of sins and unto eternal life. For to him
belongs all glory, honor, and worship, and magnificence, together with his
beginningless Father, and his all-holy, and good, and life-giving Spirit; now
and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
