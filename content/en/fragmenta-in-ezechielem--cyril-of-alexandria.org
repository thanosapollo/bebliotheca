* Fragments on Ezekiel

And four wings to the one. Cat. Ezech. f. 12. b. For the intellectual and
upper powers stand always, as it were, not crouching toward the baser things,
but they dart along the straight and blameless path of the life that befits
them. And they shall be upon the mountains as doves. Cat. 18.b. The blessed
disciples; for they went about all under heaven, speaking the mystery of
Christ, and having it as song and lyre. And if these three men be in the midst
of her, Noah, and Daniel, and Job. Cat. f. 203. b. Since I think one must not
leave the prophet's words unexamined, come, let us say why, when Noah and Job
and Daniel were already dead, =70.1460= and perhaps even Daniel himself, he
says they shall be saved in their own righteousness, even though all the rest
of the earth, against which the sentence of death would be brought, is being
destroyed. It seems, then, that he likens to Noah those who do good to him
through faith toward God; and to Daniel the wise and discerning; and again to
Job those who, through endurance, clearly that which is toward every one of
the things that are praised, are well-pleasing to him. And I will take from
the choice ones of the cedar from the top. Cat. f. 208. He likens those from
Israel to the high and most well-ordered cedars, among whom the choice one,
and as it were a heart, was the blessed David, from whom God the Father took
and planted Emmanuel, who also rose up into a great cedar, and received all
into his own shade; and thereafter the whole world under the sun knew the
power and authority of the God who is able in all things.
For he has humbled the green tree, and he prepared the dry tree to grow
and sprout, that is, the Church from the nations, κατευανθέν by the avarices
of the devil ita cod. And I gave them statutes that were not good. Cat. 210.
b. For as far as it pertains to spiritual contemplation, the law is holy and
the commandment holy and just and good. But as far as it pertains to the
vanity of the matter, and to the worship in shadows alone, the statutes are
not good, nor even procurers of eternal life. For no one is justified in the
law, according to what is written. Behold, you eat the milk. Cat. f. 224. For
demanding according to the law tithes, firstfruits, thank-offerings, and
wantonly reveling in the gifts from the peoples, they took no account of their
salvation. And was it not enough for you that you fed on the good pasture?
Cat. f. 224. b.
For those of the blood of Levi, being learned in the law, and having as it
were a most beautiful pasture, and thin and clear water, the instruction
through Moses, which is not insufficient to guide toward the truth, they
trampled the pasture in a certain way; and they also troubled the water,
setting before those who follow them in the rank of sheep as rams a teaching
that is thick and muddy and full of earthy uncleanness. And I will raise up
over them one shepherd ... my servant David. Cat. f. 225.) And that he says
such things concerning Christ is not hard to see; for when the blessed David
had already died beforehand and was already lying in tombs, the blessed
Ezekiel prophesied. And God the Father made the promise as one that would be
in due times, and not as already fulfilled; for promises are upon things that
will be, no longer upon things already done.
