* Address to the Emperor Constantine
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

We give thanks to God, most mighty emperor, who co-steers the earthly kingdom
with you; who through you has brought to nothing the error of the idols, and
has established the cheerful of the faithful in boldness. The savor of demons
has ceased; the worships of Hellenic polytheism have been dissolved; the
darkness of ignorance is driven away; the inhabited world is lit by the light
of knowledge of God; Father is glorified; Son is co-worshiped; the Holy Spirit
is announced; consubstantial Trinity, one 676 Godhead in three persons and
hypostases is preached.
By this, emperor, the power of your piety is walled; keep this inviolable for
us. Let no heretic, having crept under the Church, take away one of the
Trinity, leaving the remaining without honor. Arius is for us the namesake of
madness, the cause of the word and of the gathering; who, as we do not know
how, having been enrolled in the presbytery of the Church of the Alexandrians,
escaped our notice, being alien to the teaching of the thrice-blessed prophets
and apostles.
For he is not ashamed to deprive the only-begotten Son and Word of the Father
of the consubstantiality of the Father, and the creature-worshiper is eager to
number the creator with the creation. This man you would persuade, autocrat,
changing his mind, not to stretch against the apostolic teachings; or, lying
in the impieties of evil opinion in which he was caught, you would utterly
make him vanish from the fold of Christ and of us, that he may not make the
souls of the simpler a prey of his turbid word-flattery.
