=24.76=.1 7. Let us fill our houses with spoils. Of Eusebius. The demons strip
those whom they conquer, taking from them the panoply of God, that is =24.78=
the helmet and breastplate and the sword of the Spirit; and their houses are
the assemblies of the evildoers, which they also wish to fill with spoils,
that is fruits which they snatch from the righteous; but how do you also call
spoils things not taken by the law of war,
