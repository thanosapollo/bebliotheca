OF THE SAME CONCERNING HERETICS 96
Question. If one who has been found somewhere by chance with heretics or with
Greeks or Jews ought to eat with them or greet them.
Answer.
The greeting, that is the common one, the Lord forbade of no one, saying: "If
you greet our friends only, what extra do you do? Do not even the nations do
this?" But concerning eating together we have a command of the apostle upon
which persons one must decline this, who said: "I wrote to you in the letter
not to mix with fornicators, not at all with the fornicators of this world, or
the greedy or robbers or idolaters, since you would then have to go out of
this world; but now I wrote to you, if anyone named a brother is a fornicator
or greedy or an idolater or a reviler or a drunkard or a robber, not to eat
with such a one."
