* To the Carthaginian Council
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

And they subscribed. The Lord keep you for us for most times, most blessed
brother. They subscribed similarly: Alypius, Augustine, Possidius, Marinus and
the remaining bishops. There begin the copies of Cyril the bishop of the
Alexandrians toward the synod in Africa, in which he sent the authentic things
of the synod in Nicaea, transferred from Greek through Innocent the presbyter;
which letters with the synod in Nicaea through the aforementioned Innocent the
presbyter and Marcellus the subdeacon of the church in Carthage were sent to the
most holy Boniface, bishop of the Roman church, on the day before six Kalends of
December. To the most honored and most holy lords, brothers and fellow-bishops
Aurelius, Valentinus and all the most holy 423 synod gathered in Carthage,
Cyril greeting your love in the Lord. The things written by your honor, having
much religion, I received with all joy through our child Innocent the
presbyter.
These things having been hoped from us, so that from the scrinium of our church
we send to your love the most true copies of the authentic synod in the
metropolis of the Nicaeans of Bithynia, the things defined and confirmed by the
holy fathers under the confession of our faith, I thought it necessary, most
honored lords brothers, a greeting going first, through the same our child
Innocent the presbyter to send to our love the most faithful copies of the
authentic synod in the city of the Nicaeans of Bithynia, which 424 you will also
find seeking in the ecclesiastical history.
And concerning Pascha, as you wrote, we announce to you that we will accomplish
it on the day before seventeen Kalends of May in the incoming indiction. The
subscription. God and our Lord keep your holy synod, which we pray, most
honored brothers.
