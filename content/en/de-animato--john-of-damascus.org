On the ensouled.
=95.229=
Ensouled is said that which is nourished, and grows, and begets a like to
itself. And this is divided into three: into animal; and the animal does not
have only these three natural movements, but also to change from place to
place; into plant; this has the three only; into zoophyte; the zoophyte is
midway of the animal and the plant. For the plant having the three, and the
animal besides the three also the movement from place, this is midway. For it
too has movement, and not that from place to place, but that of contraction,
if someone should touch it. The body is divided into ensouled and soulless;
the ensouled into sensory animal and insensate; the soulless into zoophyte and
plant; the zoophyte into fig; the plant into herb, bush, tree; the animal into
rational and irrational; the rational into mortal and immortal; the immortal
into angel, demon; the mortal into human, ox, dog, and such things. Innovation
is a thing altered beside the bounds and laws of nature. Economy is a thing
that ought not to come to be; it comes to be also according to condescension
and the benefit of some. Of beings some are essence; some fitness; some
energy.
