* Compendium of Supposed Letters of Cyril and Julius the Pontiff
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Cyril once wrote, he who wrote the letter to Constantine, but the one who after
that one succeeded to his throne, to Julius bishop of Rome, wrote saying, that a
great place and a deprivation comes to be in the great and bright feasts,
because they are accomplished in one day, and the readings, and the following
remain half-complete of the two feasts, because of not being able to complete
the birth and the baptism of the Lord. As indeed we cannot in one day meet into
a place of the baptism (since Bethlehem is three miles away from Jerusalem
toward the south, and the Jordan is 15 miles away toward the east), let your
holiness then command to search all the writings of the Jews, which Caesar Titus
having spoiled led away from Jerusalem into Rome. Perhaps you might find firmly
the day of the birth of Christ and our God. Then Julius of Rome carefully sought
concerning this request; and having gathered all the letters of the Jews, those
taken captive and brought into Rome, he found a certain writing of Josephus the
chronographer, written by him; that in the 7th month in the feast of
Tabernacles, into the day of the atonement, the angel of God was seen, and the
priest having been made deaf remained speechless, until his wife Elizabeth bore
in old age. And Juvenal the patriarch of Jerusalem wrote to Julius the patriarch
of Rome concerning this, that I cannot in one day meet into Bethlehem, and into
the Jordan. For also Jor =33.1209= dan is away from the city of Jerusalem toward
the east 25 miles; and the holy Bethlehem, toward the south of the city, 6
miles; and I cannot in one day complete the two feasts. I entreat then your
holiness, Father, that you search the memorials, or also give us in an exact
search through your writing, honored one, the knowledge concerning these, on
what day Christ the Lord was born, and on what day he was baptized. For as we
have known exactly, that the memorials from a beginning from Jerusalem into
Rome. Julius the patriarch of Rome having received this writing, having searched
the memorials, found, that on the twenty-fifth of December our Lord Jesus Christ
was born, and after thirty years of his birth he was baptized by John, in the
Jordan river, on the 6th of the month January. According to this search then the
Fathers having divided the feast, there was a murmuring among the many, etc.
