** PRAYER OF THE CONSUL AUSONIUS, IN RHOPALIC VERSES
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:

Hope, God, reconciler of an eternal station:
if we keep watch, pardonable, with chaste prayers,
to these prayers, Father, as one to be appeased, give your assent.
Grant, Christ, an irreproachable pattern to be known,
good king, life-giver of serving worshipers.
with the Father, highest majesty, unbegotten.

Grant a triple support, the Paraclete associating,
that a celebrated devotion may be continued long:
they hasten to you, to keep watch fittingly.
Night will bring back a light to be preferred to torches,
night bearing an undoubted light for believers,
night a meditator of starry works with flames.
You divide religious fasts with a table,
you, promising rising goods, are celebrated:
grant, ruler, that the modest may speak the Almighty.
Your fountain cleanses those justified by a re-creation,
giving a mind forgetful of committed shameful deeds,
giving snowy lambs to shine, purified.
Light brought in by the Word, a helper for sinners,
as a new washing of Jordan, has sanctified,

when those deemed worthy have merited their dippings.
And Christ, a rule for restless elements,
brings a wave of unstained medical baptism,
that he might take away a thinned harm from mortals.
The cross, the extreme of punishment, hastened for the immaculate,
that, having been put to death, he might renew lost life,
liable to so many titles of immoderate things.
Who will worthily continue praises for the Lord?
or does earth enrich him with human commemoration,
whom the melody of angelic voices makes resound?
Giving a precious hall to Stephen, stoned,
giving the keys above to the first of the cathedra,
yes, you also joined hostile Paul by a gathering.
He becomes a teacher of the people, the inciter of those who stoned,
as the confessing robber shared paradise,
so, I believe, joining the most dire things to those to be made glorious.
You will allow us late servants to grow
taught under such a high priest of religion;
grant a sense established with solid belief.
Make me be joined, glorified, to the revived number,
inviting to heaven the fellowships of earth-born,
HOPE, GOD, RECONCILER OF AN ETERNAL STATION!
