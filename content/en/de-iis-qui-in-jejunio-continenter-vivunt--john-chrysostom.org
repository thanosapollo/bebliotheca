II. Of the same, concerning those who live temperately in fasting, and
the manner of life of various prophets.
=64.15=
Athletes, exercising every day, both increase their skill and easily
take the victory against their opponents; and runners, in the daily
practice of agility, making their joints nimble, are sharpened toward
the swiftest expectation. In the same way, then, the fathers also bid
us to teach more continually, that we may quickly acquire for ourselves
the security in teaching from the practice of the word. Come then,
beloved, having tracked Joseph, leaving the Egyptian sin, let us run
out naked of hatred toward temperance. For even if Joseph left the
covering of the flesh, did he not have a more splendid garment of the
glory of temperance? The purity of temperance, having bound the fathers
together, underpinned adamantine pillars for the inhabited world,
stretching out prayers to heaven, and healing the souls of men upon
earth. Blessed Moses, having bound himself about with temperance,
became a pillar of salvation to the Hebrews in Egypt, and he dashed to
the ground the doors of the firstborn of the Egyptians, and blessed the
light-bearing children of the Hebrews; and as a pillar he stood against
Pharaoh and the magicians, and Joshua the son of Nun, having temperance
as his consort, commanded the heavenly luminaries; for he hindered the
courses of the sun, and fixed the bounds of the moon, and lengthened
the light of the day. And when was this? When for the war over Gibeon
he said: Let the sun stand still over Gibeon, and the moon over the
valley of Elom. This one divided a river, saved a people, and turned
the Hebrews back again into the holy land; this one threw down walls,
and overthrew cities of the ungodly, saved Rahab, punished the aliens,
glorified those of his own race. And Samson the most manly, keeping
temperance, throttled a lion, and from the jawbone of an ass he made
water spring, and from there received the healing of his thirst. And
Elijah the Tishbite, having dwelt with temperance, became a giver of
life to the poor widow; for a good blessing made her oil spring, and
multiplied the handful of meal each day. The same Elijah, through
temperance, possessed treasures of fire and water in the clouds; for he
rained heavenly fire upon the ungodly, and upon the failing inhabited
world he emptied out heavenly springs.
And Elisha his disciple, possessing the same temperance of the teacher,
in the barren womb of the Shunammite, through prayer made the ear of
the child sprout; and since he had to show wonders equal to the
teacher, he raises one dead man; and after death the remains of his
bones raise the dead. And Daniel the most wise, having girt himself
with temperance as with a two-edged sword, throttled the stealth-killing
wolves, and kept the =64.16= lamb of Helkias unharmed. For neither was
her bright fleece of temperance cut by a defiled kiss, nor were her
breasts defiled by the two-edged sword of sin. And blessed Habakkuk,
snatched up by the Holy Spirit, conveyed the laborers' meal to Daniel,
the laborer of temperance. For could not God have nourished this one
also, as Elijah, through ravens? But that he might not leave unconsoled
the one dwelling with beasts, he sends the fellow-prophet to comfort
the affliction. For consider, beloved, how Daniel was in the pit of the
lions, having neither bench, nor bread, nor table, nor lamp, nor bed,
but standing crosswise in the midst of the two lions he imitated the
Christ who was to come. And the three most holy youths, having wrapped
themselves in temperance, wrestling with the fire, overcame by force
its bubbling flame. O virginity that without toil blossomed forth
Christ, the hope of the world! O wisdom of God, who both did not
deprive Mary of the glory of virginity, and through her made Christ,
the boast of the world, shine forth like light! O holy child of an
unwedded bride, fountain of incorruption, breath of immortality,
luminary of the Church, stater of righteousness, fair pearl of the
merchants, and good crown-winner of the fears, sold for much, and
bought by the poor; bought for three obols, of faith, hope, love, and
lodging in the heart of the humble; the grain of mustard, and commander
of the angels; the untilled cluster of all-fair Mary, and the unseen
Word of all-fruitful wisdom; who dissolved the mourning of death upon
earth, and prepared the radiance of the bridal chamber in heaven; who
put on the green shoot of our body, and granted us the unfading breath
of the flower; these are the teachings of your wisdom, these the sweet
things of your fasting, these the adornments of your temperance, these
the supports of your Church.
Thus you are accustomed to nourish your people, Jesus, not as being
absent, but as being present; for existing with the Father, you are not
separated from us, and lodging with us, you do not depart from the
Father. With water you washed our bodies, and with spirit you loosed
our faults; with the salt of piety you seasoned the souls of your
sheep, and you drew us up into the pasture of the angels; with a seal
you nerved our feet, and as a ladder from earth to heaven you raised up
faith for us, and you set angels beside us as guides by the hand; you
set a tree against a tree, and put a side in place of a side; you
stretched out hands into the air, and for our sake you entreated the
Father of yourself, and from a storm you awarded a deep calm. Therefore
both by night and by day, both being silent and speaking, we send up
glory to you, having in you the hopes of life, now and always, and unto
the ages of ages. Amen.
