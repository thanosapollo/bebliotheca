* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also called Theophorus, to the holy church which is in
Tralles of Asia, beloved of God, the Father of Jesus Christ, elect and
worthy of God, at peace in flesh and spirit through the passion of Jesus
Christ, who is our hope in the resurrection unto him; which I also greet
in the fullness, in apostolic character, and I pray that it may have
abundant joy.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
I have known you to possess an unblemished mind, unwavering in endurance,
not from habit, but by nature, as Polybius your bishop made clear to me,
who came by the will of God and of Jesus Christ to Smyrna, and so
rejoiced with me in my bonds in Christ Jesus, that I beheld your whole
multitude in him.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
Having therefore received through him your goodwill according to God, I
gave glory, finding you, as I had learned, to be imitators of God.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
For when you are subject to the bishop as to Jesus Christ, you appear to
me not to live according to man, but according to Jesus Christ, who died
for us, that by believing in his death you may escape dying.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h2
:END:
It is necessary, therefore, as you are doing, that you do nothing without
the bishop, but be subject also to the presbytery as to the apostles of
Jesus Christ our hope, in whom, as we continue, we shall be found.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h3
:END:
And the deacons, being of the mysteries of Jesus Christ, must in every
way please all. For they are not deacons of food and drink, but servants
of the church of God. It is necessary, then, that they guard themselves
from accusations as from fire.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
Likewise let all respect the deacons as Jesus Christ, even as the bishop
who is a type of the Father, and the presbyters as the council of God and
as the college of the apostles. Apart from these a church is not called.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
Concerning these things I am persuaded that you are so disposed. For I
received the pattern of your love, and have it with me, in your bishop,
whose very bearing is a great lesson, and his gentleness is power; whom I
think even the godless respect.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h3
:END:
Loving you, I spare you, though I could write more sharply on this. I did
not think myself entitled, being a condemned man, to give you commands as
an apostle.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
I have many thoughts in God, but I measure myself, lest I perish through
boasting. For now I must fear the more, and not give heed to those who
puff me up. For they who speak thus to me scourge me.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h2
:END:
For I love to suffer, but I do not know whether I am worthy. For the envy
is not apparent to many, but it wars against me the more. I have need,
then, of gentleness, by which the ruler of this age is brought to
nothing.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
Am I not able to write heavenly things to you? But I fear lest I should
do you harm, since you are infants. And pardon me, lest, being unable to
receive them, you be choked.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
For even I, not because I am bound and can understand heavenly things,
and the stations of the angels, and the arrays of the rulers, things
visible and invisible, am I already a disciple on that account. For many
things are lacking to us, that we may not lack God.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
I exhort you therefore, not I, but the love of Jesus Christ: use only
Christian food, and abstain from alien herbage, which is heresy;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
who interweave Jesus Christ with themselves, making themselves credible,
as those who give a deadly poison with honeyed wine, which the ignorant
takes gladly, and in an evil pleasure dies.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
Guard yourselves then from such people. And this you will do if you are
not puffed up, and are inseparable from God Jesus Christ and from the
bishop and from the ordinances of the apostles.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
He who is within the sanctuary is clean; but he who is outside the
sanctuary is not clean. That is, he who does anything apart from the
bishop and the presbytery and the deacons, this one is not clean in
conscience.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
Not that I have found any such thing among you, but I am guarding you in
advance, since you are my beloved, foreseeing the ambushes of the devil.
You then, putting on gentleness, refresh yourselves in faith, which is
the flesh of the Lord, and in love, which is the blood of Jesus Christ.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
Let none of you hold anything against his neighbor. Give no occasion to
the Gentiles, lest through a few foolish ones the multitude that is in
God be blasphemed. For woe to him through whom my name is blasphemed
among some in vanity.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
Be deaf, then, when anyone speaks to you apart from Jesus Christ, who was
of the race of David, who was of Mary, who was truly born, both ate and
drank, was truly persecuted under Pontius Pilate, was truly crucified and
died, while those in heaven and on earth and under the earth looked on.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
who also was truly raised from the dead, his Father having raised him;
who in like manner will also raise us who believe in him, his Father, in
Christ Jesus, apart from whom we have not the true life.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
But if, as some who are godless, that is unbelievers, say that he suffered
only in appearance, they themselves being the appearance, why am I bound,
and why do I also pray to fight with the beasts? Then I die in vain. Then
I am lying against the Lord.

* Chapter 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h1
:END:
Flee then the evil offshoots that bear deadly fruit, of which if anyone
tastes, he dies at once. For these are not the Father's planting.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h2
:END:
For if they were, they would appear as branches of the cross, and their
fruit would be incorruptible; through which in his passion he calls you,
who are his members. A head, then, cannot be born apart without members,
God promising unity, which is himself.

* Chapter 12
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h1
:END:
I greet you from Smyrna, together with the churches of God that are
present with me, who have refreshed me in every way, both in flesh and in
spirit.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h2
:END:
My bonds exhort you, which I carry about for the sake of Jesus Christ,
asking that I may attain to God. Remain in your concord and in your
prayer with one another. For it is fitting for you, each one, and
especially the presbyters, to refresh the bishop, to the honor of the
Father, of Jesus Christ, and of the apostles.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h3
:END:
I pray that you hear me in love, lest I become a testimony against you by
writing. And pray also for me, who have need of your love in the mercy of
God, that I may be counted worthy to attain the lot in which I am set,
lest I be found reprobate.

* Chapter 13
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h1
:END:
The love of the Smyrnaeans and of the Ephesians greets you. Remember in
your prayers the church in Syria, of which I am not worthy to be called,
being the last of them.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h2
:END:
Farewell in Jesus Christ, being subject to the bishop as to the
commandment, and likewise to the presbytery. And you, each man, love one
another with an undivided heart.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h3
:END:
My spirit is devoted to you, not only now, but also when I attain to God.
For I am still under danger; but the Father is faithful in Jesus Christ
to fulfill my petition and yours, in whom may you be found blameless.
