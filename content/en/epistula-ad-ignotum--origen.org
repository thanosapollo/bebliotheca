* Letter to an Unknown Person
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

"The holy Ambrose, genuinely dedicated to God, greets you much; who, thinking
me to be industrious and very thirsty for the divine word, convicted me by his
own industry and by his love toward the holy studies; whence he has so far
surpassed me that I am in danger of giving out before his proposals. For it is
not possible to dine except while collating, nor after dining is it permitted
to walk about and rest the little body, but even in those times we are
compelled to study and to make the copies exact, nor indeed is it permitted us
to sleep the whole night for the care of the body, the study being prolonged
far into the evening; and I pass over also the hours from dawn until the ninth
and sometimes even the tenth hour; for all who wish to be industrious dedicate
these times to the examination of the divine oracles and to the readings.
