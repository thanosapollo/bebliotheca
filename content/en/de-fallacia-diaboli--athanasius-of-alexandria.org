against us, both painting and the plastic art through the things it molds, and
the stone-cutting art through the things it carves, and farming; it used to
give the fruits for burning, and the fine calves for a stumbling-block, and
the shady plants for our deception. And he devised all creation against us, in
order that through all things the power of the Savior might not appear. And
let the Greeks tell me whether the myths are false or true. Let them have the
shameful things; such a good has been granted to us. Let us not cut off the
things of the ancestors; let us drag that evil inheritance itself into the
open. Those who have been ennobled from slaves often do not hesitate to set
forth the slavery of their fathers, in order to give thanks to the one who
freed them. Just as those saved from a storm do not remember the surrounding
terrors unclearly, and just as those raised from disease recount the
affliction in order to give thanks to the physician, let us say by what things
we were held, in order that we may know the greatness of the grace. If the
myths are false, thanks to the one who delivered us from the falsehood; but if
the myths are true, thanks to the one who saved us. For whether the one who
said that Heaven begot spoke falsely, or his son cut him off, it ascribes
passion to him. For if the son cut off the father, neither is the father who
suffered a god, nor the son who cut him off, having done things which our sons
do not do. Of him, he says, he swallowed children, lest he suffer from those
he begot what he did to his father, and one called a god swallowed those he
begot; and a mother, sparing a son, gave a stone, and he, rushing greedily to
swallow the child, did not perceive whether it was a stone or a son.
7 These things are shameful and I am ashamed as I speak; but when light came,
the very things of which we are now ashamed, day having come, we feel shame. I
rejoice with you and I give thanks with you that those whom your fathers and
ancestors used to worship you hesitate to name with the mouth and you cannot
bear to hear with the ears. Since then so great a change has come about, let
us say what we have hated and let us give thanks to the one who loved the
whole conversion. He clothed the so-called father of gods and men with this,
in order that he might corrupt many virgins. And those so-called gods
corrupted virgins, but he even somehow keeps virgins until old age; and the
one descended contrary to nature, but the other ascended above nature. But
that these deeds are false ... For if gods used to beget then, what has
happened to them that they do not beget now? Have they too grown old, or have
they died, and who is the one who became chaste? Or do they beget even now,
and yet so many do not appear? For if immortals beget, the universe would have
been filled, death not cutting down those who are born; but those who do not
exist did not beget, and the poets, composing fine words, put a wicked
falsehood into them. For from the time the poets ceased, births of gods do not
appear; and if they were of such a kind from the beginning, busying themselves
with the virgins among men, what has happened to them in so long an age since
the foremost of the poets talked nonsense? Did they not see a beautiful virgin
upon the earth? Was their desire not stirred? He no longer changed into a
bull, no longer gold into laps, no longer does the virgin flee, and the
pursuer pursue; and the virgin according to the myth, leaning on chastity,
fled, and the so-called god, dissolved by wantonness, did not overtake her?
But since the virgin fled in order to remain a virgin, and the god pursued in
order to plunder the woman (I am ashamed to speak, but it is necessary to
say). And still even after light has risen some are shameless and are angry
over shameful statues and they want temples to exist in which these things are
stored up ... and the remnants are useful, in order that we may learn from
what we have been delivered. And the pursuing god will be known from his
works. Joseph, being young and serving below his worth, when the mistress was
promising and forcing and dragging, left the tunic and fled decently; and the
decent man rather with chastity into a prison and with dignity as a slave than
as a master with incontinence; but the so-called god of the falsely named ones
pursued a virgin and did not overtake her. For chastity strengthens even a
female, and pleasure dissolves even males; and when the earth also, herself
called a goddess, being what she is, drew near, having spared the virgin
according to the myth (for none of these things is true) and having condemned
the pursuer, she changed the virgin into a plant, he says; but he, even after
the virgin had been changed, did not change his desire along with her, but
plucks a branch and crowns the defeated head from the one who conquered. And I
point out, then, the crowns in contest for the victors; furnishing a symbol of
the one who was defeated, they bear upon the head a crown of Daphne, a wreath
of the one who was conquered, those who now seem to conquer. But since these
things are false, thanks to the truth that we have been delivered. But if
these things are true, let those who hasten after them have the shame. For if
men fashioned these things, and the powers that are served do not rejoice in
them, let those who are slandered not inhabit the temples; for if the statue
lies about the story, let nothing attend upon the lie. If then it does not
attend, you worship in vain; but if, these things being so done, it attends,
it rejoices in them, and those who rejoice in these things, being demons, are
worthy to be hated and not worshipped.
8 And what would you say concerning the pouring out of blood, and what
concerning smoke, and what concerning the stench? Those who worship stop up
their nostrils, and those who are worshipped receive it. And in your house you
do not wish to see a drop of blood, but in the temples myriads and thousands;
and in your house you do not want a flute to sound, but in the festivals they
pipe throughout; and you do not want your daughter to utter shameful things,
but they sing shameful songs in the temples; and you do not want anyone to see
your wife naked, nor even yourself, as far as is possible, but you worship the
goddess naked, a bad example of chastity; and you will not choose to acquire a
slave who betrays nature (for it is necessary, as has been said before, to use
a more decent tongue), but the youth who was seized for abuse, this one you
worship; and you cannot bear to acquire a slave-girl who prostitutes, but the
one who practices prostitution, this one you worship. And the defense is worse
than the accusation, and the drug more bitter than the wound; for when we
charge these things, what do they answer? We too know, he says, that they are
not gods at any time ... having learned when the sun rose. They are not gods,
and why do you worship? Relatives of God, he says. The defense is that they
are relatives of God. But let the falsehood not escape you; for he who created
man, to men ... for the relatives of God do not commit outrage nor do they
pervert the creation of the Maker, so as neither to allow a man ... and not to
fall into a woman .... ... is shown through the things she works to be hostile
and not belonging to the household. God granted marriages for succession and
consolation of mortals, in order that men who are sick may be tended and women
may obtain kindness, but the demon who practices prostitution, having
corrupted the decent women, does things alien and not belonging to the
household; for just as of a decent king the incontinent are not household
members, but the decent are.
9 I seek his promise, I will examine the deeds; for it is not the speaking of
fine things that is sought, but the pursuing of truth. When I hear that a
temple ... at once the mind was brought low ... if God has a temple upon earth
and on the ground, and beyond the heavens. For I seek

according to promise the deeds. If you are a god, why do you seek upon earth,
although God is also upon earth, but not shut up in a chamber, not bound into
a statue, nor subjecting himself to evildoers, nor shut in by magic; for if he
was not overshadowed from us but was stripped by us, let us see the things
that are hidden and let us know the things that are being exposed. Since then
they promise a god and gods, let them give the promise; let them not show us
temples but heavens beyond; let them not pour out blood, let them not smoke
the statues, let the seer not rave; for if a god is at work, let him not fall,
but even having fallen let him rise; let the one who speaks the future not be
out of his mind, let him not be ignorant of the present; for if he becomes
witless and falls out of his thoughts and does not see the present, he
scarcely recognizes the future.
10 I wish to say all things, in order that we may confess the whole grace, but
we were held down by the myths for a long time and much, not of one day's
choosing; we were weighed down by the falsehood, we were not able to sober up.
Jesus came, the one able to bear all things. For what do you wish? Shall I
examine the myths? Those who wrote them are ashamed; the allegories did not
heal the myths, nor the doctrines of the wise; they have not yet ceased
raving. When, conquering, we persuade, then if indeed we shall be persuaded
.... ... But shall we let the evils go? †since having set free to heavens† and
leaving the myths, let us receive the truth, and having withdrawn from
pollutions, let us receive goodness, and having withdrawn from the gods who
are not, let us worship the God who is; let us deny the darkness, let us love
the light; having cast off drunkenness let us love fasting; having condemned
wantonness let us practice chastity. How shall we give thanks, how shall we
fulfill the word, and how shall I be silent? Jesus does not cease doing good;
even now he still saves, but with the benefaction it also increases, and the
benefaction does not cease doing good, in order that we may not cease giving
thanks. He still calls, he still saves, he still enlightens, he still
admonishes (the one who yesterday worshipped a naked woman now confesses God)
and bearing witness he enlightens, and through peace he trains, and through
persecutions he strengthens, and through the cross he has conquered, and
through death he saved, and through a curse he freed the curse, and he has
done all things and has undone for us the plot from of old. Since then we can
neither worthily recount the wickedness of the one who plotted, nor the
greatness of the one who did good, through whose grace we fled the evils,
having given thanks, not as much as we owe but as much as we are able, let us
also assemble, in order that we may be heard and let us attend, in order that
we may be confirmed. Do not set aside the grace; it is not a man who has
called; not one like you taught thus upon earth, but God from above, the Son
before the ages, the Only-begotten before time, the one in glory with the
Father, the master of all, the judge of the universe was judged for you, in
order that you might be exalted. If then he has supplied all things to you,
through all things give thanks to God the all-holy who made† from the
beginning and honored, and to the one who after these things has made†,
through whom he made and renewed and saves, glory, might, honor with the Holy
Spirit, both now and always and unto all the ages of ages. Amen.
