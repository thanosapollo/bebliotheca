* Fragments on the Gospel of Matthew

to be said, lest I fall into the doctrine alien to the church of God
concerning metensomatosis, neither handed down by the apostles nor
appearing anywhere in the scriptures; for it is also contrary to the
things seen being "temporary" and to this age suffering a consummation,
but also to the fulfillment of <the> "heaven and earth will pass away"
and "for the form of this world is passing away" and "the heavens will
perish" with the things following it. For if (by hypothesis) the same
soul can come to be twice in the body in the constitution of the world
from 5 the beginning until the consummation, for a cause for which it
would come to be in it, or (ἢ edd.) having come to be twice in a body
because of sin, why will it not also be in it three times and more
often, the punishments for this life and the things sinned in it being
to be rendered in this way alone (according to metensomatosis)? Which
if it is granted as a consequence, there will perhaps never be a time
when a soul will not be metensomatized. For always because of the
former sins it will sojourn in the body, and thus the destruction of
the world, according to which "heaven and earth will pass away," will
have no place. But in order that (according to this hypothesis) it may
be granted that the one altogether sinless will no longer come into the
body through generation, after how many times do you think 6 one soul
will be found that has become altogether pure and does not need
metensomatosis? Nevertheless even so, with some one always being
transferred from the defined number of souls and no longer coming into
a body, generation will at some time fail, as through certain as it
were infinite times, the world having arrived at some one or a second
or a few more, after whom, they too having been perfected, the world
will be destroyed, those coming into a body having failed. Which does
not please Scripture; for it knows a multitude of sinners at the time
of the destruction of the world. Which is clear when one examines
together "yet when the Son of man comes, <will> he find faith on the
earth?" as we found in Matthew 7 thus said "And as the days of Noah
were, so will be the coming of the Son of man; for as they were in the
days of the flood" and the rest. And for those then the collection of
the sins will not be in metensomatosis; for if they are taken while
still sinning, either they will be punished after this by another
manner of punishment (and according to this there will be two generic
manners of punishments: one in metensomatosis, and the other outside
such a body. And let them state the causes and differences of these),
or those left at the consummation will not be punished, as having
suddenly cast off the sins, or (which is better) there is one manner of
punishment for those who have sinned in the body: to suffer outside it,
of the constitution of this life, according to the worth of the things
sinned 8. And each of these, to one able to look into the matters, is
subversive of metensomatosis. And if the Greeks who introduce
metensomatosis necessarily (as setting down things consistent for
them) also do not want the world to be destroyed, it is time for these
too, having looked at the scriptures that show that the world will be
destroyed, either to disbelieve them or to invent explanations
concerning the narrative of the things according to the consummation;
which even if they want to do they will not be able.2
