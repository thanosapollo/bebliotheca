:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1

Of Saint Gregory, bishop of Nyssa, from the letter to Philip the monk.
Things of the same substance possess identity; things of different substance,
the reverse. For even if by an ineffable union the two are one, it is not by
nature, because of the unconfused. Christ therefore, being two natures, but
truly recognized in them, has a single person of sonship.
