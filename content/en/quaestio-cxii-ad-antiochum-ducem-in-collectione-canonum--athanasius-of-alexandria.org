OF THE SAME TO ANTIOCHUS D
Concerning not communicating with heretics
Question. If a man is overtaken at a time of a feast in a country in which
communion of the catholic church is not found, what ought he to consider
because of the feast, to communicate with the heretics or not? Answer. If the
danger is great and hard for the man, to leave his own wife and come together
with another, even if he happens to be abroad, how much more to betray the
right faith because of communicating with the heretics? And as those who wish
to sell their own business, however long a time they delay in a foreign land,
do not accept to take instead of the imperial stamp an outside stamp, so also
upon the communion of Christ one must reason. With all power therefore let us
guard not to partake a partaking of heretics, nor to give a share; "For do not
give the holy things to the dogs, says the Lord, nor throw your pearls before
the swine," lest we become partakers of the evil opinion and of their
condemnation. For if there is altogether a union toward Christ, also toward
one another altogether; and we are united according to choice to all who
partake together with you; for this union comes to be from choice, not without
our judgment; "For we are all one body, because we partake of the one bread,"
as the divine apostle says.
