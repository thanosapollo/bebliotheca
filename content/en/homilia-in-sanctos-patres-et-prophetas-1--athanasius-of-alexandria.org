* Homily on the Holy Fathers and Prophets 1

OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS ATHANASIUS. A homily on the holy Fathers and
prophets, and on the all-venerable Church, and on the 34th psalm. The faith
that guides the feet onto the road of the knowledge of God, and the virtuous
and incomprehensible way of life, does not have a recent beginning, as the
lawless and accursed Jews say; but from of old, from the holy and pious
Fathers, I mean Abel, and Enoch, and Noah, and Abraham, and of the rest
=28.1064= of all the righteous. And this is clear from the vessel of election
saying it, the myrrh-bearing vessel, the pillar of the truth, Paul, writing
these things word for word and saying: By faith Abel offered a greater
sacrifice than Cain, and God accepted it; whence he also lives unto the age.
By faith Enoch, having been well-pleasing to God, was translated into
paradise, not even having seen the common death of nature. By faith Noah
constructed the ark for the salvation of his house, imaging the ineffable
Church, which has two stories and three stories, the invocation of Father and
Son and Holy Spirit. By faith Abraham, having gone out from the land of the
Chaldeans, sojourned in the land of promise, awaiting the heavenly city, the
Jerusalem above, the mother of the firstborn, whose craftsman and maker is God
alone. By faith Isaac, being offered up upon the altar, did not weep, knowing
that he images the lamb who was to take away the sin of the world. By faith
Jacob worshipped upon the top of his staff, openly making known the
victory-making and thrice-blessed cross. By faith Joseph, dying, commanded the
sons of Israel concerning his bones, to bring them out of Egypt, so that the
Egyptians might not (sic) be about to worship them as God. By faith Rahab did
not perish with the city of Jericho, having received the spies with peace. By
faith Moses made the Passover and the pouring of the blood, so that we too
might accomplish the saving Passover by faith, and not in hypocrisy ("For
everything that is not of faith is sin"); anointing with the blood not upon
the two doorposts of the doors, but sealing soul and body; so that Pharaoh
together with the devil might be drowned in the deep of the Red Sea. And let
us, the new Israel, with Moses sing the song of victory. By faith the prophets
overthrew kingdoms, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions, namely the
wise Daniel; they quenched the power of fire, that is the three children,
having trodden the flame of the fire as softer than dew. And others were
beaten on the drum about the axles of the wheels, being shaken limb from limb,
not having accepted the invitation of the tyrant Antiochus, that "Sacrifice
and become my friends," that they might obtain a better resurrection. Stephen,
who bears the name, was stoned, having set upon his temples the crown of
righteousness. Isaiah was sawn asunder, but he is praised unto the age. John
and James died by swords; Zechariah was murdered; Elijah and Elisha went about
in sheepskins, and in goatskins, being in want, being ill-treated by the
senseless, remaining in caves, and in the holes of the earth, of whom the
whole world was not worthy. For they themselves were worthy, as men beloved of
God and lovers of virtue. And David also, the spiritual lyre, says, as we have
just now heard through the hymn that was chanted to us: "Judge, O Lord, those
who wrong me; war against those who war against me." Not making these things
plain, =28.1065= God says to him: Do you wish, David, that those who are
hostile to you
be overcome in war? And David: Yes, Lord, and I very much wish them to be
overcome in war. And the Only-Begotten: And if your enemies undergo these
things, where is your freedom from remembering wrongs? And how can you also
sing, speaking truly: "Lord, if I have repaid those who repay me with evils,
let me then fall empty from my enemies"? And David: Yes, Master, because I
thus sing things that are true, but if you war against them, then also through
the things that come from you all things are unknown and incomprehensible. And
thus you also commanded me: Do not avenge yourself; "Vengeance is mine, I will
repay them." And the Only-Begotten: And how must those who are hostile to you
be warred against? Speak and make the cause plain. And David: You have no
need, Master, of questioning or reminder, being a knower beforehand and
knowing the things of foreknowledge, and of what manner of choice the man
about to be born is to come to be. Nor are you, as one of the rulers, ignorant
of the laws, or needing an advocate or a fellow-assessor, who give judgment
upon each several heading. For you are enthroned with the Father, finder of
the laws, corrector of the wise, dispenser of righteousness, champion of those
who are worn down, avenger of those who are slandered. For you rescued
Susanna, slandered by the unchaste elders, from the death sentence, having
sent into the pleading of the case the wise and unbribable orator Daniel.
Since then you have knowledge of all things, and you understand how they must
be warred against; but if you permit me, I speak; for I am a servant of your
loving-kindness, and that an unprofitable one, and I cannot act against your
command. And the Only-Begotten: I permit you, and speak without trembling and
without holding back. And David: "Take hold of weapon and shield, and arise to
my help." And the Only-Begotten: And of what weapon am I to take hold, or of
what shield? And how am I to arise to your help? Surely I am not to take hold
of that weapon or that shield which you took from the alien Goliath, whom you
also struck in the valley, when I guided the stone into his unmarked and
unillumined face? But know this clearly, that I have no need at all and
altogether of these weapons. And if I even hold back the spirit, all flesh
that moves upon the earth dies. And David: I also know, Master, that you have
no need of these weapons, being a mighty king, almighty, unconquerable,
everlasting. But I beseech your incomparable goodness to take hold of weapon
and shield, that is, of incarnation and passion; and to arise to my help on
the third day. And the Only-Begotten: And from where do you know these divine
and hidden mysteries, "into which the angels desire to look"? And David: I
received the knowledge of them from the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete. And the
Only-Begotten: =28.1068= Along with these things that you know, speak, if you
have anything else also; for I will hear you concerning it also, kindly and
justly. And David: "I said to my soul, I am your salvation." And the
Only-Begotten: This you have, and take courage; for I will save your soul
through my incarnation and the passion. And David: "I was made very
courageous;" and immediately he brings in reply: "Lord, who is like you?" And
the Only-Begotten: The Father. Whence I also say: "He who has seen me has seen
my Father." Go in peace, awaiting the blessed hope of the promises. Therefore,
these provisions having been dismissed, the angels of God, going out, cried
with a great voice: If anyone else also of the prophets has a case, let him
enter unhindered, pleading the truth. At the exhortation there enters Naboth
the Israelite, whom the Only-Begotten also, having seen him, says: And with
whom do you have a case, or with whom do you wish to go to judgment? And if
you yourself through yourself cannot introduce the subject of your matter, and
you have an advocate, let him also enter unhindered, pleading the truth for
you. And Naboth: Yes, Master, I have a most just advocate, Elijah the
charioteer; and my case is held with the false witnesses. Whence beginning my
matter I say in order: "Unjust witnesses rose up against me, they asked me
things I did not know," affirming that I blessed God and king; whom the Lord
also, examining, said: Tell me, as friends of the truth, who compelled you to
slander my servant and keeper of the law? And they answered: Ahab and his wife
Jezebel. And the Only-Begotten: Let Ahab then in the war be shot with a
well-aimed arrow through the liver, or rather through the lung, which breathed
unjustly into the greedy desire. And let Jezebel be thrown on a building-plot,
and let her become a tomb of dogs. And let Naboth inherit, in place of the
vineyard that is being made to vanish, the luxury of paradise and of eternity.
And when this sentence had been recorded, there enters the most loud-voiced
Isaiah crying: Lord, hear me kindly and justly. And the Only-Begotten: Speak
against the one who wronged you, since you rather are a just prophet,
spiritual, able to set in order for yourself the subject of your matter. And
the prophet: I have a case with Manasseh, since while I was earnestly and
without sloth proclaiming the divine and God-inspired word, he himself,
without pity and without mercy, destroyed me with a wooden saw. And the
Only-Begotten: If Manasseh did this, let him be delivered to the Chaldeans
unto captivity, and mockery and slaughter; that kings may learn not to insult
my prophets, or to kill them, but to honor and magnify them; considering that
through me every king reigns, and the rulers write =28.1069= righteousness.
You then, in place of the earthly Jerusalem, dwell in the heavenly mother of
the firstborn; and he too the place "from which pain, grief, and groaning have
fled." And when this sentence also had been completed, there enters the most
wondrous Jeremiah, whom the Only-Begotten also, having seen, says: And with
whom do you have a case, or with whom do you wish to go to judgment? And
Jeremiah: I have a case with the Jews, and I wish to go to judgment before
you, the Master who is no respecter of persons. And the Only-Begotten: And
what do you accuse your kinsmen of? And the prophet: I accuse them, because
they repaid me evils in return for good things, and wicked things in return
for good. While I was praying for their health and salvation, they planned to
destroy me with poison, having hidden it with much deceit in my bread. And the
Only-Begotten: And what do you wish to happen to them? And the prophet: Do to
them that which is written in the Proverbs: "He who repays evil in return for
good, evils shall not be moved from his house." And the Only-Begotten: You
have asked nothing out of place. See, evils are not moved from the house of
the Jews of Jerusalem, until it is laid waste from the foundations. And you,
rest in the unconfined bosom of the forefather Abraham. When this sentence
also had been completed, there enters the prophet Zechariah crying: Lord, hear
me also kindly and justly. And the Only-Begotten: Speak against those who
wronged you. And Zechariah: Those who wronged me are the high priests and the
teachers of the law. And the Only-Begotten: And what such thing did the high
priests and the teachers of the law do to wrong you? And Zechariah: They
murdered me between the temple and the altar. And my blood also was fixed upon
the rock, for the conviction of their bloodstained mind. And the
Only-Begotten: Therefore let them also die beneath the altar at the hands of
the Romans; and through the things by which one sins, through these he is also
punished; and with what measure one measures to his neighbor, with such he
shall be measured in return from heaven justly. And when those have suffered
the equal things and the things of which they were worthy, you then enter into
the intelligible bridechamber, hearing from me the most longed-for voice:
"Well done, good and faithful servant, you were faithful over a few things, I
will set you over many; enter into the joy of your Lord." Then, when this
sentence also had been fulfilled, there enter also the children slain in
Bethlehem, crying and saying: Lord, we also through your name were destroyed
by swords at the hands of the most impious and lawless Herod. And the
Only-Begotten: If for my sake you endured the profitable death and the one
that is better than life, even if in ten thousand parts, do not grieve,
knowing clearly that those who die for my sake do not perish; for they live
unto the age. Whence I command Herod =28.1072= to be subjected to the nine
incurable sufferings, and you to be ranked in the choirs of the prize-bearing
martyrs, singing with them the hymn of victory. And last of all there enters
also the Church that does not deny, whom the Only-Begotten also, having seen,
says: And what do you have, my bride and the unstained maiden, and the
spotless virgin? And the Church: I am grieved, for which reason I also say:
"Judge, O Lord, those who wrong me, war against those who war against me." And
the Only-Begotten: And why will you be warred against? Speak and make the
cause plain. And the Church: I am warred against, Master, because of the
exactness of the evangelical doctrines, and the making of the true and firm
Passover, and because of your august and undefiled image, which the
God-bearing apostles handed over to me to hold as a pattern and earnest of
your incarnate economy. And the Only-Begotten: Even if you will be warred
against for this, do not be downcast or grow weary in mind, or in reasoning.
For see clearly, that he who denies the Passover and my undefiled image, I
also will deny him before my heavenly Father, and his elect angels. And he who
again suffers with me through the Passover will also be glorified with me. Or
did you not hear what I commanded the lawgiver Moses: Make for me two Cherubim
in the tent of the testimony, as a fore-announcement of my image? And again,
when the Jews were testing me saying: "Teacher, is it permitted for us to give
census-tax to Caesar, or not?" and I said to them: "Show me a coin," and I say
to you; and they brought me a denarius; and I say to them: "Whose image is
this and inscription?" And they said: "Caesar's;" and I said to them: "Render
then the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God." And again
to Peter: "Go, cast a hook into the sea, and the fish that is to come up, open
its mouth, and you will find a stater; give it for me and for you." That is,
give my image and yours in the world. Or did you not hear Paul who betrothed
you saying: "If we suffer with him, that we may also be glorified with him"?
and to the Athenians: "I observe you as in all things more religious. Going
about your objects of worship, I found an altar among them with the
inscription: To an unknown God; and whom you worship not knowing, this I
proclaim to you." And to the Galatians: "O foolish Galatians, who has
bewitched you not to obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was
portrayed among you, and this crucified?" And to the Corinthians: "If anyone
does not worship our Lord Jesus Christ come in flesh, he has maranatha the
anathema." And you, my bride, admonish your children to remain in my
commandments, and do not fear the rages of kings, or threats of tyrants, or
the snortings =28.1073= of heresiarchs, or fire, or sword, or wild beasts, or
takings-away of money, or of possessions, or of houses, or of like things. For
whatever defeat the transgressors think, they shall be defeated; and whatever
counsel they counsel, I will scatter; for you have from the foundations the
firm and living rock. Amen.
