319 Not all those in captivity who were "led away to Babylon" through
Nebuchadnezzar came to "Babylon" because of sin. The multitude of the people
did so because of sin, but the righteous among them did not, such as Daniel,
Ananias, Azarias, Misael, this Ezekiel, Zechariah, Haggai, and those like
them. God, being good, and punishing the sinners and handing over into
captivity those who cannot be in the holy land because of their sins (for
opposites cannot coexist), at the same time sends prophets, lest the sinners,
having become captives, be left 320 wholly without help. For on the hypothesis
of the sin of the sinners "led away to Babylon," if righteous men had not been
among them, no healing would have come about for the sinners. This then is of
unspeakable goodness. For he does not hand the sinners over to unmixed
abandonment, but he visits them through his saints, of whom he said that "you
are the light of this world, and the salt of the earth," not only concerning
the Apostles, but also concerning those like them. 323 "The charioteer of
these four living creatures" was not wholly "fire," but "from the loins
downward," and "from the loins and as far as above, electrum." For the Word
does not have punishments only, but he also has that through which he gives
rest, and he punishes through the powers below. For he did not see "fire"
about the head, nor from "the loins upward" was the "charioteer fire," but
"from the loins downward" he was "fire"; that he might show that those who are
in generation have need of "fire." For "loins" are a symbol of generation.
=327.20= Ezekiel bears a type of Christ in many things, and you will begin to
see this in the prefaces of the prophecy. "It came to pass in the thirtieth
year, in the fourth month, on the fifth of the month, and I," he says, "was in
the midst of the captivity by the river Chebar; and the heavens were opened."
These things have also been written concerning Christ, when he was in this
world, in this "captivity" at the Jordan, that "Jesus was beginning to be
about thirty years of age," and that "the heavens were opened to him." But the
thirty years in the prophecy, according to the sensible, is the time of the
prophet's life, and of "the captivity," from when they went down, the fifth
year. But indeed according to the name also Ezekiel has a symbol 328 of
Christ, who is interpreted "strength of God." And the "son of man" taken into
an anagoge of him who was named "Son of man." 329 He says, as it were: "I" who
have nothing of captivity "was in the midst of captivity" by economy. Thus
also Christ, not having been taken captive, was in the place of the captives,
but for the sake of their redemption, as also the prophet was not "in the
midst of the captivity" because of sin, but for healing. 336 "A great cloud in
the lifting spirit." When you have been benefited from "the lifting spirit,"
which "lifted away" from you every base thing that exists in your soul, then
337 you will enjoy the "cloud" that exists in "the lifting spirit." And this
"cloud" is akin to the "cloud" in the Gospel, from which "there came a voice
saying": "This is my beloved Son." A "lifting spirit," then, then "a great
cloud in it," then a certain bright "light round about it." Your base thing
"has been taken up," a "cloud" has been given you, so as "to rain rain upon
the vineyard." "Fire flashing forth and in the midst of it as a vision of
electrum." In two ways God "lifts away" the base things from us, by "spirit"
and by "fire." If we become good and noble and are educated by the word, the
base things "are lifted away" by "spirit," according to what is written: "But
if by the spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you shall live." But
if "the spirit" did not "lift away" the base things from me, there is need, I
think, "of the fire." =340.20= It is possible to see in each "living creature"
the soul, and in each "face" its powers, the rational in the "man," the
irascible in the "lion," the appetitive in the "calf," and in the "eagle" the
helping power. Whence he said these things neither "on the right" nor "on the


left," but clearly "above" the three. This is, as it appears, the spirit of
the "man" that is in him, that which helps the soul ... Thus then all things
are driven by the Word of God ... the things above the heavens, therefore, are
driven, or rather governed, "the heavenly and the earthly and the underworld."
=354.30= As many as toil about the affairs of the world, nailed to the
sensible things, and have no 355 care about the spiritual life and the
contemplation according to the intelligibles, but about the things of this
life, these "set their own thoughts upon their heart" and not the words of
God. And one must not be so, and especially "elders of Israel." Wherefore such
as these "set the punishment of their injustices before their face." For we
too are the cause of our punishments, as also he makes clear who says: "Walk
in the light of our fire and in the flame which you kindled." 378 "But if it
be revealed to another sitting," the first has a command "to be silent
altogether." The prophets were not beside themselves when they prophesied to
the people, but standing in their natural state, both willingly and knowingly,
they served the word that came to them; which word was never borne according
to the will of a man, but is borne from God to men, which men willingly serve
it. For this reason also Balaam is commanded "to speak the things submitted to
him," as having free will also not "to speak" them. And Jonah also confirms
this very thing. 390 A varied opposing power "passes by" our soul and "seeks"
a place to enter and "fornicate" with the soul, as it were the demon of anger,
the demon of vainglory, the demon of grief, and in a word the power of each
passion "passes by" through the soul and wants to "fornicate" with it, having
been intertwined with its choice. (Nathan hinted at some such thing toward
David, calling the demon of fornication a "wayfarer.") ... "The garments" in
this part are the sacred scriptures and the mind in them. The soul, then, that
has fallen from the truth splits the "garments" given to it for seemliness. As
it were it divides the scriptures and sews saying to saying not with the
fitting weaving; and having sewn them it makes "idols," the impious thoughts,
in which serving it "fornicates." Such is the soul of the heretic. And a
heretic is not only the one cut off from the truth according to thought, but
also the one according to deed. 396 "And you built for yourself a house of
fornication." If you see a soul lying open to the lovers who wish it, you will
see that she "has made a dwelling of fornication" and receives all who choose
to "fornicate" with her, that is, the unclean powers. And a little later: The
soul has beauty above the adulterers; for those have lost their own beauty.
For this reason they are also adulterers and they love to corrupt her beauty
... In bodily fornication bodies "are corrupted" and in spiritual fornication
"thoughts are corrupted." And the Apostle makes this clear. 426 According to
the letter the first "eagle" is Nebuchadnezzar, who "entered into Lebanon,"
that is "Jerusalem," and "took from the tops of the cedar" "the king of
Jerusalem and her rulers" and "brought them into the land of the Chaldeans,"
"into Babylon"; for he "planted" in the captivity, having taken the sons of
Israel together with "the royal seed" and the ruling, and the rest of the
multitude became a "vine," not so strong as when it was with God. "The second
eagle" is "Pharaoh." There was a "war of Pharaoh" against Nebuchadnezzar, and
the people, taking occasion, wished to throw off the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar,
to which God had handed them over because of their sins, and to incline to the
yoke of "Pharaoh" not according to 427 the will of God, and not rather to
incline to God. For this reason they will suffer more terrible things from
"Pharaoh" than those from Nebuchadnezzar. And these things are concerning the
people that was left behind and that was taken by Nebuchadnezzar according to
the letter. 434 Because of sins someone is mastered by the evil one, "giving"
him "place" to step 435 upon him. And


knowing this Paul said: "Do not give place to the devil." 450 "The son of man
Ezekiel" describes many "gates" and concerning each he related something; 451
but now he relates "to those who have ears" concerning "the outer gate of the
holy things that is toward the east," concerning which it has been written
that: "This gate shall be shut." Not only "because the Lord the God of Israel
passes through the gate, it has been shut," but also because "the ruler sits
in that gate to eat bread before the Lord according to the way of Ailam,"
which is interpreted porch of a gate. 452 If "knowledge" has a "key," as the
Lord says to the lawyers, ... then it is a "gate" shut to men ... and that
which is written in Isaiah, "a sealed book," has a sense like this "gate."
This same "book" you will find more clearly also in the Apocalypse of John,
and the things written concerning it, and how "he of the tribe of Judah
opened" this, and he alone. For until our Lord Jesus Christ came, the law was
"shut," the prophetic word.
