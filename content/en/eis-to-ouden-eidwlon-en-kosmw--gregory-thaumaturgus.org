* That an Idol is Nothing in the World
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Honor paid to the God who truly is is altogether an honor also of the one who
honors; but honor paid to the god who is not is, on the contrary, a dishonor
of the one who honors.
