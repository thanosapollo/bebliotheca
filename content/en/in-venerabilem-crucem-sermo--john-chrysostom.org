ON THE HONORABLE AND LIFE-GIVING CROSS, AND CONCERNING THE TRANSGRESSION OF
THE FIRST HUMAN BEINGS.
=50.815=
What shall I say, or what shall I speak? Or what shall I call you? Sheep, or
shepherds? Sailors, or =50.816= helmsmen? disciples, or teachers? How shall I
marvel at your zeal, more vehement than fire? Shall I call you sheep? For you
are meek and quiet. Shall I call you =50.817= shepherds? For you are imitating
your own Master. Shall I call you sailors? For you have endured a storm by
experience of the billows. Shall I call you helmsmen? For you are. For just as
some excellent helmsman sitting at the tillers, escaping the great waves by
his craft, and saving the vessel whole, leads it by the hand to a fair-weather
harbor: so also you. But shall I call you disciples? For you are ready for
obedience. But shall I call you teachers? Again I will say, You are, for you
have become teachers of the whole inhabited world. What then shall we say? I
wish today to set before you a spiritual table, not feeding the body, but
walling round the spirit, or rather both feeding the body and scouring the
soul. For such is the spiritual table, not as the table of gluttons producing
satiety. For it is a spiritual word breathing fragrance; for a man, when he
recalls it, as if chewing myrrh, sends forth a certain fragrance, not only
comforting himself with the fragrance, but also causing those standing by him
to be filled with that fragrance.
Stretch yourselves then; for I wish to bring forth a word. For there are some
of the divers who throw themselves from the vessel into the sea, going about
the deep, wishing to draw up a pearl; but we, having instead of a vessel the
Old and New Testament, instead of a rudder the cross, instead of a helmsman
Christ, instead of a lookout the Father, instead of a west wind the Holy
Spirit, instead of a sail the grace, instead of sailors the disciples, instead
of passengers the prophets, therefore let us also throw ourselves from the
vessel upon the sea of meanings, not that we may draw up a pearl, but
something more precious than a pearl.
Stretch yourselves for me, I entreat, giving me your obedience; for as I said
before, I am about to draw up from the depth of meanings something more
precious than a pearl. Do not therefore dismiss it, and treat yourselves as
irrational. For it is written: Do not cast your pearl before swine, and do not
throw the holy things to the dogs. But when you hear swine, do not think these
irrational animals; and when you hear dogs, do not think these dogs. For he
calls swine those who live in dissipation; and he calls dogs those who rage
without cause against the Master. Make yourselves then worthy of the pearl
which I am about to set before you today. For husbandmen do not simply cast
down the seeds, unless first they yoke oxen, and draw a plow, and pull up
thorns, and cut furrows, and then the seeds are cast down upon the earth, that
the rain, finding them, may carry them down into the flanks of the earth; and
then the earth, having received them, brings forth the ear. But those things
wait for winter, and summer, and heavenly rain, and the rays of the sun, and
the season of harvest, and then they gather them into the storehouses; but I
wait for no winter, nor summer, nor rays of the sun, nor rain, nor do I yoke
oxen, nor draw a plow, nor cut furrows, nor pull up thorns; for today I sow,
and today I also reap. For I do not work irrational earth, but rational earth,
not


insensible, but sensible, not soulless, but soulish. And blessed am I, of what
sort of earth I have been allotted as farmer; for then I am blessed, when I
speak into ears that hear. And again it says: Disobedience works death.
Blessed then am I; blessed also are you. For Jesus in the Gospel says to the
disciples: Blessed are you, if you keep my word.
Having stretched yourselves then, attend to what is said. And I wish to show
you by examples what God is; using a word I speak: God is like a man sitting
upon a lookout-rock, and beholding all the sea, and ships going in it, and it
being surge-tossed, and raising itself with great waves, because of strong
=50.818= winds blowing in it; and some excellent helmsman contending with the
waves to moor the vessel at a fair-weather harbor, because evening is
overtaking them, fearing lest the ship become submerged, other ships going the
straight way, but others by the rush of the winds becoming submerged and
broken up, and some having died, and some having survived, and laying hold of
some pitiable rudder, and being dragged by the great waves onto the solid
land, and the bodies of those already dead thrown out. And the one sitting
upon the lookout-rock beholds all these things; this one is God; and as I said
before, using a word I speak. But when I say God sitting in the high places,
do not think him to be in the high places only, but also everywhere. For he
has no interval of place, being himself his own place, himself containing all
things, but nothing contains him. For what can contain the bodiless nature,
both immaterial and boundless and unsearchable, and indestructible and
incorruptible and incomprehensible? For among the pious one must think of God
thus. For this is our hope, as it is written: This is eternal life, that they
may know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent.
From this the heretics lay hold of us, who are ungrateful, who read, and do
not recognize; but you, when you hear Christ, do not reckon God only, nor the
incarnate economy only, but both together, both the God Word and the incarnate
economy. Since I know Christ hungering, and I know Christ from five loaves and
two fishes feeding five thousand besides women and children; I know Christ
thirsting, and I know Christ changing the water into wine; I know Christ
sailing, and I know Christ walking upon the waters; I know Christ dying, and I
know Christ raising the dead; I know Christ standing before Pilate, and I know
Christ sitting together with the Father; I know Christ being spat upon by
Jews, and I know Christ being worshipped by angels; and the one I ascribe to
the divinity, the other to the humanity. For this reason both together have
been said, since I know Christ born of Mary, and I know Christ being before
the ages.
Let us therefore glorify Jesus the Christ, the unbreakable wall, the
foundation that has been founded upon the rock. He himself says to Peter: You
are Peter, and upon this rock I will build my Church, and the gates of Hades
shall not prevail against it. And likewise Paul also says: Another foundation
no one can lay besides the one that is laid, which is Jesus Christ. I entreat
you therefore that with all meekness we attend to one another, For in the
present life we are baited with food and drink and various pleasures, but in
the one to come, judgment and justice. Let us not therefore give place to the
devil. For our wrestling is not against blood and flesh, but against the
principalities, against the powers, against the world-rulers of the darkness
of this age. Let us hasten therefore to flee the eternal punishment. For God
did not make the punishment that he might cast us in, but that he might cast
you out when you have fallen in; for such a philanthropic and merciful God we
have. For David bears witness saying: God is a righteous judge,


and strong, and long-suffering, and not bringing on wrath every day. If you do
not turn, he will make his sword gleam; he has stretched his bow, and prepared
it, and in it he has prepared vessels of death. These things God says, not
that he may judge us, but that we may not become more careless. For if he
wished to destroy us, he would not have poured out his blood for us. He died
then, that he might grant you immortality; he hungered, that he might fill you
with his own flesh; he thirsted, that he might give you his own blood to
drink; he sat in a boat, that he might seat you above the Cherubim; he was
baptized, that he might enlighten you; he was struck, that he might set you
free; he journeyed, that he might make you unwearied; he sailed, that he might
make you fearless; he slept, that he might make you without care; he came from
a woman, that he might pity the transgression in paradise; he was called man,
that he might call you =50.819= god; he was called Son of man, that he might
call you son of God; he took our things, and gave us his own; he prayed, that
he might make you faithful, as he says over Lazarus: Father, glorify your Son,
that your Son also may glorify you. And there came a voice from the heavens,
saying: I have both glorified, and I will glorify again. I know that you
always hear me, but I said it because of the crowd standing by, that all may
believe that you sent me. If then he underwent anything, he did not undergo it
for himself, nor for his own Father, but that he might save the human race
through the cross. And if you wish to know, beloved, the power of the cross,
and as many encomia as there are concerning the cross, listen.
The cross is hope of Christians, the cross resurrection of the dead, the cross
guide of the blind, the cross hope of the despairing, the cross way of the
wandering, the cross avenger of the wronged, the cross staff of the lame, the
cross consolation of the poor, the cross bridle of the rich, the cross
overthrow of the proud, the cross repentance of the unrestrained, the cross
trophy against demons, the cross victory over the devil, the cross pedagogue
of infants, the cross abundance of the destitute, the cross helmsman of those
who sail, the cross harbor of those storm-tossed, the cross wall of those
warred against, the cross father of orphans, the cross protector of widows,
the cross judge of the unjust, the cross pillar of the just, the cross relief
of the afflicted, the cross guardian of infants, the cross head of men, the
cross end of elders, the cross light for those sitting in darkness, the cross
magnificence of kings, the cross philanthropy of barbarians, the cross freedom
of slaves, the cross wisdom of the uninstructed, the cross law of the lawless,
the cross preaching of the prophets, the cross proclamation of the apostles,
the cross boast of the martyrs, the cross ascesis of monks, the cross chastity
of virgins, the cross joy of priests, the cross foundation of the Church, the
cross security of the inhabited world, the cross demolition of temples, the
cross overturning of altars, the cross vanishing of the sacrificial smell, the
cross scandal of the Jews, the cross destruction of the impious, the cross
power of the powerless, the cross physician of the sick, the cross cleansing
of lepers, the cross tightening of paralytics, the cross bread of the hungry,
the cross fountain of the thirsty, the cross covering of the naked; but when I
say nakedness, I do not mean that of the body, but that of the faith. But when
having believed you are baptized, then you will hear yourself clothed. And
hear Paul saying: As many as were baptized into Christ, you put on Christ. But
when you hear Christ, do not reckon God only, nor the incarnate economy only,
but both together. For I have said it often, and now speaking I will not
cease; for to me to speak is not irksome, and to you it is safe; for I wish
all to be teachers. Christ then has been named, because he put on flesh; Jesus
Christ has been named, the name of his flesh; Word has been named, because he
is Son from someone; Son, because from the Father; Only-begotten, because
alone from God alone; God, because Maker. And hear John saying: In the
beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God; this
one was in the beginning with God; all things came to be through him, and
without him came to be


not even one thing that has come to be. Do you see that this is the one who
stretched out the heaven by a word, and adorned the varied choir of the stars,
and showed the heaven as a meadow? This is the one who kindled the sun, and
assigned to it its courses; this is the one who marked the moon toward the
west; this is the one who founded the earth upon the waters; this is the one
who commanded the earth to send up herb of grass, and the springs to gush
waters, and rivers to flow, and lakes to be gathered; and setting bounds to
the sea by sand, and =50.820= commanding the waters to bring forth living
souls, and the winds to blow, and having weighed the mountains with a balance,
clouds to be moved, cattle, four-footed things, creeping things, winged things
to run about upon the earth.
And why do I speak of angels, archangels, thrones, the Cherubim, the Seraphim,
Principalities, Powers, ranks, times, and years, and months, and weeks, and
days, and hours, and nights, and light, and darkness, and the essences of
things? And last having made man according to his own image and according to
likeness, he placed him in paradise. And seeing the human race being conquered
by the wickedness of the devil, he willed to put on our first-fruits, and to
nail it upon the wood, that, as through the wood the transgression, so again
through the wood the salvation. The devil then, jealous because he himself had
been thrown from heaven, but Adam was in paradise, using the serpent as a
weapon worked in him; and thus he conversed beforehand with them being in
paradise; for being bodiless he was not able to converse with bodily ones; and
he says to Eve, that This tree is good and fair for food; and, In the day in
which you eat from it, you shall be as gods knowing all things. Having
persuaded Eve then, he deceived her; then she together persuaded her own
husband, and they ate from the tree. And when they had eaten they were seen
naked; and God says to Adam: Where are you? When you hear, Where are you, do
not reckon ignorance to God; but he said, Where are you, because the devil was
saying to them, that In the day you eat, you shall be as gods; as they were
supposing that they were about to become as gods, for this reason God was
saying to him: Adam, where are you? in return for this, that you ate and
remained a man; did I not command you not to eat from this tree? but you ate,
and you transgressed; this is the, Where are you? And they had been clothed
with fig leaves, covering the form of the sin. And what does Adam say? I heard
your voice walking in paradise, and I hid. And he says: Why have you done
this? And he says to God: The woman, whom you gave me, she deceived me. Then
to Eve, Who you? And what does she say? The serpent deceived me. O Eve,
granted, I gave you to your husband, did I also give you to the serpent?
And they were making these excuses beforehand, as wishing to withdraw
themselves from the punishment. And God says to Adam: Cursed is the earth in
your works, thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you; in the sweat of
your face you shall eat your bread. Then to the woman: In pains you shall bear
children, and multiplying I will multiply your pains; and your turning shall
be toward your husband, and he shall rule you. Then to the serpent: Upon your
breast and your belly you shall go, and earth you shall eat all the days of
your life; and the man shall rule you; he shall keep your head, and you his
heel. And why did he curse the serpent to go upon the breast and the belly,
and to eat earth all the days of his life? He cursed the breast, because in it
sits the heart that conceived the evil; the belly, because through eating he
deceived; and he commanded him to eat earth all the days of his life, because
Adam was of earth, earthy. The three then received the sentence, because the
three also made the transgression. Since from a tree the transgression came to
be, as I said before, for this reason he willed to nail this first-fruits upon
a tree, that as through a tree the transgression, so through a tree the
salvation. And for all these things let us send up glory


to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, now and always, and
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
