* Discourse on Mary and Joseph
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

The Lord was called firstborn from the Virgin, not because he summoned some
second of like kind, but because he is firstborn incomparably toward the sons
of men. For neither because the Apostle said concerning the Lord's
beginningless generation from the Father, Who is the firstborn of all
creation, did he already also write a brother of the Lord. Nor again did the
same, having called him firstborn from the dead, already also introduce a
blood-relative of the Lord who similarly trampled Hades. But the Lord is
firstborn from the Father and from the Virgin and from the dead, that through
all the unique dignity of sonship might be known. For the Master's birth is
incomparable; for where the mother is a virgin, how is a birth interpreted?
