things that have been read concerning Israel, that a "wilderness" he did not
become to him nor "a land made barren." Who then, having come to be in the
place, would not seek, examining the will of what is written? Be it so, God in
Israel has not become a wilderness, has not become in Israel a land made
barren; has the Lord then become a wilderness to Israel today, or is a land
made barren now to him? What then? When to Israel he was not a wilderness nor
a land made barren, was he a wilderness and a land made barren to the nations?
For if to all he is always not a wilderness and to all he is always not a land
made barren, what need is there of it being said privately toward Israel by
way of exception: "Did I become a wilderness to the house of Israel or a land
made barren?" But it is possible to come upon the universal benefactions of
God, then after his universal benefactions upon the particular ones. 3.2 To no
one is God a wilderness, "making the sun rise upon evil and good"; to no one
is he a land made barren, "raining upon just and unjust." How a wilderness,
making a day rise and making night for rest? how a wilderness, making the
earth bear fruit? how a wilderness, managing each according to the soul, that
he may be rational, that he may take up knowledge, that his understanding may
be exercised, according to the body, that he may have "the senses" in health?
