* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Polycarp and the elders with him, to the church of God sojourning at
Philippi: mercy and peace from God Almighty and Jesus Christ our
Savior be multiplied.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
I rejoiced with you greatly in our Lord Jesus Christ, that you received
the patterns of true love and sent on their way, as was due from you,
those wrapped in bonds befitting the saints, which are the diadems of
those truly chosen by God and our Lord;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
and that the firm root of your faith, proclaimed from ancient times,
remains until now and bears fruit unto our Lord Jesus Christ, who for
our sins endured even to come unto death, whom God raised, having
loosed the pangs of Hades;

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h3
:END:
in whom, though you have not seen him, you believe with joy unspeakable
and glorified, into which many desire to enter, knowing that you have
been saved by grace, not from works, but by the will of God through
Jesus Christ.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
Therefore, having girded up your loins, serve God in fear and in truth,
leaving empty chatter and the error of the many, believing in him who
raised our Lord Jesus Christ from the dead and gave him glory and a
throne at his right hand; to whom all things in heaven and on earth
were subjected, whom every breath worships, who comes as judge of
living and dead, whose blood God will require from those who disobey
him.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h2
:END:
And he who raised him from the dead will raise us also, if we do his
will and walk in his commandments and love what he loved, abstaining
from all injustice, greed, love of money, slander, and false witness;
not returning evil for evil, or insult for insult, or blow for blow, or
curse for curse;

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h3
:END:
but remembering what the Lord said as he taught: Do not judge, that you
may not be judged; forgive, and it will be forgiven you; show mercy,
that you may receive mercy; with the measure you measure, it will be
measured back to you; and that blessed are the poor and those
persecuted for righteousness' sake, for theirs is the kingdom of God.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
These things, brothers, I write to you concerning righteousness, not as
one who has taken the matter on himself, but because you invited me
first.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
For neither I nor anyone like me can keep pace with the wisdom of the
blessed and glorious Paul, who, when he was among you in the presence
of the men of that time, taught the word concerning the truth
accurately and firmly, and who also, when absent, wrote you letters; if
you look closely into them, you will be able to be built up in the
faith that was given you;

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h3
:END:
which is the mother of us all, with hope following and with love going
before, love toward God and Christ and toward the neighbor. For if
anyone is within these, he has fulfilled the commandment of
righteousness; for the one who has love is far from all sin.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
The beginning of all troubles is the love of money. Knowing then that
we brought nothing into the world, and that we cannot take anything
out, let us arm ourselves with the weapons of righteousness and teach
ourselves first to walk in the commandment of the Lord;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h2
:END:
then also our wives, in the faith given them and in love and purity,
cherishing their own husbands in all truth and loving all equally in
all self-control, and to train the children in the discipline of the
fear of God;

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h3
:END:
the widows to be sober-minded concerning the faith of the Lord,
interceding without ceasing for all, far from all slander, evil speech,
false witness, love of money, and every evil; knowing that they are
God's altar, and that everything is examined for blemish, and that
nothing escapes him, neither reasonings nor thoughts nor any of the
secrets of the heart.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
Knowing then that God is not mocked, we ought to walk worthily of his
commandment and glory.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
Likewise deacons, blameless before his righteousness as servants of God
and Christ and not of men; not slanderers, not double-tongued, not
lovers of money, self-controlled in all things, compassionate,
diligent, walking according to the truth of the Lord, who became the
servant of all; if we please him in the present age, we shall also
receive the age to come, as he promised to raise us from the dead; and
that if we live as citizens worthily of him, we shall also reign with
him, if indeed we believe.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h3
:END:
Likewise also the younger men, blameless in all things, taking thought
above all for purity and bridling themselves from every evil. For it
is a good thing to be cut off from the desires that are in the world,
because every desire wars against the spirit, and neither the sexually
immoral nor the effeminate nor those who lie with men will inherit the
kingdom of God, nor those who do what is unseemly. Therefore it is
necessary to abstain from all these things, being subject to the
elders and deacons as to God and Christ; and for the virgins to walk
in a blameless and pure conscience.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
And the elders also, compassionate, merciful toward all, turning back
those who have gone astray, visiting all who are sick, not neglecting
widow or orphan or the poor; but always taking thought for what is
good before God and men, abstaining from all anger, partiality, and
unjust judgment, being far from all love of money, not quickly
believing anything against anyone, not harsh in judgment, knowing that
we are all debtors of sin.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
If then we ask the Lord to forgive us, we too ought to forgive; for we
are before the eyes of the Lord and God, and all must stand before the
judgment seat of Christ, and each must give an account for himself.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h3
:END:
So then let us serve him with fear and all reverence, as he himself
commanded, and the apostles who preached the gospel to us, and the
prophets who proclaimed beforehand the coming of our Lord; being
zealous for what is good, abstaining from offenses and from false
brothers and from those who in hypocrisy bear the name of the Lord,
who lead empty men astray.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
For everyone who does not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the
flesh is antichrist; and whoever does not confess the witness of the
cross is of the devil; and whoever twists the sayings of the Lord to
his own desires and says there is neither resurrection nor judgment,
this one is the firstborn of Satan.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
Therefore, leaving the emptiness of the many and the false teachings,
let us turn back to the word handed down to us from the beginning,
being sober toward prayers and persevering in fasts, asking in our
petitions the all-seeing God not to lead us into temptation, as the
Lord said: The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
Let us then persevere without ceasing in our hope and in the pledge of
our righteousness, which is Christ Jesus, who bore our sins in his own
body upon the tree, who did no sin, nor was deceit found in his mouth;
but for us, that we might live in him, he endured all things.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
Let us then become imitators of his endurance, and if we suffer for
his name, let us glorify him. For this pattern he set us in himself,
and this we believed.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
I exhort you all, then, to obey the word of righteousness and to
practice every endurance, which you also saw with your own eyes not
only in the blessed Ignatius and Zosimus and Rufus, but also in others
from among you, and in Paul himself and the rest of the apostles;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
being persuaded that all these did not run in vain, but in faith and
righteousness, and that they are in the place due them with the Lord,
with whom they also suffered. For they did not love the present age,
but him who died for us and for our sake was raised by God.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
Stand therefore in these things and follow the Lord's example, firm in
faith and unchangeable, lovers of the brotherhood, loving one another,
joined in truth, waiting upon one another in the Lord's meekness,
despising no one.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h2
:END:
When you can do good, do not delay, because almsgiving delivers from
death. Be subject to one another, having your manner of life
irreproachable among the Gentiles, so that from your good works you
too may receive praise, and the Lord may not be blasphemed in you.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h3
:END:
But woe to him through whom the name of the Lord is blasphemed.
Therefore teach sobriety to all, in which you also live.

* Chapter 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h1
:END:
I am greatly grieved for Valens, who was once made a presbyter among
you, because he so little understands the place that was given him. I
warn you, therefore, to keep yourselves from avarice and to be chaste
and truthful. Keep yourselves from every evil.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h2
:END:
But he who cannot govern himself in these things, how does he proclaim
this to another? If anyone does not keep himself from avarice, he will
be defiled by idolatry and will be judged as among the Gentiles, who
do not know the judgment of the Lord. Or do we not know that the
saints will judge the world, as Paul teaches?

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h3
:END:
But I have neither perceived nor heard of anything of this kind among
you, among whom the blessed Paul labored, who are in the beginning of
his letter. For he boasts of you in all the churches which then alone
knew the Lord; but we had not yet known him.

** Section 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h4
:END:
I am therefore greatly grieved, brothers, for him and for his wife; may
the Lord grant them true repentance. Be sober, then, yourselves also
in this; and do not regard such people as enemies, but call them back
as suffering and straying members, that you may save the body of you
all. For by doing this you build yourselves up.

* Chapter 12
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h1
:END:
For I am confident that you are well exercised in the sacred writings,
and that nothing is hidden from you; but to me this has not been
granted. Only, as it is said in these scriptures, Be angry and do not
sin, and let not the sun go down upon your wrath. Blessed is he who
remembers this; which I believe is the case among you.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h2
:END:
But may God and the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, and he himself
the eternal high priest, the Son of God, Jesus Christ, build you up in
faith and truth and in all meekness and without wrath and in patience
and in long-suffering and endurance and chastity; and may he give you
lot and portion among his saints, and to us with you, and to all who
are under heaven who shall believe in our Lord and God Jesus Christ
and in his Father who raised him from the dead.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h3
:END:
Pray for all the saints. Pray also for kings and authorities and
princes, and for those who persecute and hate you, and for the enemies
of the cross, that your fruit may be manifest among all, that you may
be perfect in him.

* Chapter 13
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h1
:END:
Both you and Ignatius wrote to me that if anyone is going to Syria, he
should also carry your letter; which I will do, if I find a convenient
opportunity, either I myself or the one I send as envoy also on your
behalf.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h2
:END:
The letters of Ignatius that were sent to us by him, and as many others
as we had with us, we have sent to you, as you commanded; they are
appended to this letter; from them you will be able to receive great
benefit. For they contain faith and endurance and all edification that
belongs to our Lord. And concerning Ignatius himself and those who are
with him, let me know what you have learned more certainly.

* Chapter 14
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15-h1
:END:
I have written these things to you by Crescens, whom I commended to you
recently and now commend again. For he has lived among us blamelessly;
I believe he will do likewise among you. And you will have his sister
commended when she comes to you. Farewell in the Lord Jesus Christ in
grace, with all who are yours. Amen.
