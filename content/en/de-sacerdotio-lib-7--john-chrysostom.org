Of the same Chrysostom: On the priesthood, seventh discourse.
O strange wonder! O unspeakable power! O fearful mystery of the priesthood:
noetic and holy, august and blameless, which Christ, when he came, bestowed
upon the worthy. I fall down and beseech with tears and groanings, that I may
look as in a mirror into this treasure of the priesthood, for those who keep
it worthily and holily. For it is a bright and incomparable shield, and an
unshaken tower, an undivided wall; for it is a firm foundation from earth
reaching even to the heavenly vault. And what am I saying, brethren? That it
touches the vaults of the highest, it comes unhindered into the very heavens
of heavens, and in the midst of the angels together with the bodiless ones it
treads brightly and easily.
And what am I saying, in the midst of the powers above? It becomes a companion
of the very Master of the angels and Creator and Giver of light, and as far as
it wills it immediately receives its petitions with much boldness. I do not
cease, brethren, hymning and glorifying the rank of the dignity which the
Trinity has given to the sons of Adam, through which the world has been saved,
and creation has been enlightened, through which mountains and hills and glens
and ravines have been filled with the bright and august way of life of the
blessed race; I mean of the monks, as Isaiah the most clear-voiced said, that
From the tops of the mountains men shall give their voices unto doxology;
through which also lawlessness has been taken away from the earth, through
which also temperance dwells on the earth, through which the devil, having
fallen, has been brought to nothing, the licentious have become sanctified
vessels, the fornicators chaste and undefiled, the foolish have become guides
of righteousness; through which also the dominion of death has been abolished,
and Hades has lost its strength, and the curse of Adam has been loosed, and
the heavenly bride-chamber has been made ready; through which the nature of
men is changed toward the power of the bodiless. What shall I say, or what
shall I praise? For the gift of the rank of the priesthood surpasses both word
and thought. And, as I think, this is what Paul, struck with amazement, hints
at when he says: O the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God!
How unsearchable are his judgments, and his ways past tracing out! It is
high-flying from earth to heaven, most swiftly offering to God our petitions;
it intercedes with the Master on behalf of the servants.
And let us attend, brethren, with clear sight to this mystical and fearful
account, that without the august priesthood forgiveness of sins is not given
to mortals; set your mind, brethren; for you are lovers of piety, shining
forth the light of the teachings. What is it that we said just now concerning
the mystery of the priesthood? The vine-shoot of the vine, and the grain of
wheat, and the priesthood possess concord; the vine-shoot and the wheat are
maidservants, but the priesthood is free. When therefore the three are
gathered to dwell upon the treasures, each offers to the king the power of her
own fruits for a sweet savor; the vine-shoot sends forth the blood, likewise
also the wheat the bread, but the priesthood with ready boldness soars from
earth to =48.1068= heaven, until it behold the Invisible Himself, and having
worshiped before the high throne, standing it entreats on behalf of the
servants toward the Master, bearing tears and groanings of the fellow-servants
and offering them to the Master; likewise asking both consolation and
repentance, pardon and mercy of the compassionate King, that the Paraclete
Spirit may come together and sanctify the gifts laid upon the earth. And when
fearful mysteries are brought forward, full of immortality, through the
presiding priest making intercession for all, then indeed souls approach,
receiving through the mysteries purification of their stains.
See, you who love God, how the two do not work upon the earth, unless the
heavenly decree come and sanctify the gifts. Have you seen, O man, a bright
wonder-working? Have you seen a holy rank, how easily it cleanses the spots of
souls? Blessed is the Savior who wrought upon the earth this most brilliant
and purifying gift, having enlightened the priests with grace, that they may
shine as luminaries in the world. The people before us obtained an oil-bearing
horn as their advancement; but we, the unprofitable servants of the Blessed
One, have received neither a horn nor sensible oil; but he himself who is the
skull, the high and fearful, came down from heaven, and bestowed upon us in
the laying on of hands His own Spirit, which came as fire upon the apostles. O
unspeakable power that has come to rest to dwell in us through the laying on
of the hands of the priests! O what a great rank the fearful priesthood has!
Blessed is he who lives in it purely and blamelessly. Peter who was called
Cephas, who was netted by the lip of the sea, who was witnessed by the great
Shepherd that Upon this rock I will build my Church, through priesthood has
received also the keys of the heavens. Likewise also Paul, once a persecutor,
having been counted worthy of this, ran winged preaching on land and sea the
resurrection from the dead. Let us return to Abel the righteous, who became a
priest at the beginning of creation, and let us learn from him that he offered
as priest at the beginning his sacrifice to God. Did not fire coming down from
heaven devour his sacrifice? And when he offered to God the firstfruits, as
the Scripture says, God looked upon the sacrifice of Abel, but upon that of
Cain He was not well pleased. And again Noah, who was saved in the ark, when
the water ceased, and it settled upon the mountains of Ararat, immediately
partook of this priesthood again, and offered a sacrifice purely to God for an
odor of sweet savor.
Whence God made a covenant with him, that He would no longer bring a flood
upon the earth, and He gives him a blessing to increase and multiply. Have you
seen the wonder-working of the priesthood? Have you seen the first priest Abel
in the former creation, how he brought down the fire from heaven upon earth
through his blameless whole-burnt offering? Have you seen again Noah the
august priest in the second creation, how God made a covenant with him?
Abraham also was counted worthy to partake of this, =48.1069= and he offered
Isaac to God, and he offered as a priest his own inward parts. There God shows
him a great wonder, the birth of the Lord in the plant of Sabek, the growth in
a moment, and that of the blessing with which He Himself blessed him: In your
seed shall all the nations be blessed. Moses also was counted worthy of this,
having gone up into the mountain of Sinai toward God, and having received the
law from Him; whence also his face was glorified, so as to be seen brighter
than the sun. Likewise Aaron also was counted worthy of this, becoming an
ambassador for the sins of the people toward God. For Moses and Aaron are
among His priests. Likewise also Phinehas, by this all-august thing, hindered
death from that people; and Elijah again, having clothed himself with this,
was heard in fire, and slew the priests of shame. Let us learn therefore,
brethren, that great and exceeding is the dignity of the priesthood. Glory to
the Only-begotten and only Good, who granted it to His own apostles, through
His new and holy covenant, that they also might show us a pattern in the
laying of their hands upon the worthy. Let us all therefore purely honor those
who have the rank of the priesthood, knowing that if anyone loves the friends
of the king, the king loves this one far more. Let us therefore love the
priests of God, since they are friends of God, and they intercede for us
always; honor the priests, fulfilling the commandment of Christ which says,
that He who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet shall receive a
prophet's reward. But if you do not know a given priest, whether he is worthy
of the rank or unworthy, do not overlook him, on account of the commandment of
Christ.
For just as gold is not harmed when mixed with clay, nor is a bright pearl
altered when it is joined to some unclean forms; in the same way then neither
is the priesthood stained from anyone, even if the one receiving it be
unworthy. If one is counted worthy to come into this dignity, and walks in it
worthily and blamelessly, it procures for him life and an incorruptible crown;
but if anyone dare to come into it unworthily, he procures for himself the
outer darkness and a judgment without mercy. I give you another example, O
man, that you may not dare in your arrogance to take unworthily the rank of
the priesthood, since God is not well pleased in those ordained arrogantly.
Know therefore what those wretched men suffered, who once withstood Moses and
=48.1070= Aaron, and who rushed forward shamelessly and stubbornly to burn
incense to God; did not fire devour them all, because they dared against a
rank of which they were not worthy? And again also Miriam the prophetess of
God, that she might reproach Moses with a brief word concerning priesthood,
the Most High appointed such a blemish for her, that being leprous she was
separated seven days outside the camp. And now, brethren, live in it purely,
imitating Moses and Aaron and Eleazar. Behold these pious priests, how in it
they warded off the camps of the enemies. Moses, having this, stretched out
his hands toward God, and struck Amalek with an incurable blow; Abraham,
having girded himself with this, put the kings to flight; Melchizedek, having
taken this up, blessed Abraham with an excellent blessing. Have you then been
counted worthy, brother, to receive the rank of priesthood? Have zeal to
please Him who enlisted you, in purity and in righteousness and spiritual
wisdom and in bright virginity. Become a fervent zealot as Elijah, a guardian
as Jeremiah, temperate as Joseph, and pure as Joshua, hospitable as Abraham,
affectionate as David, and meek as Moses; gather the one who has gone astray,
bind up the lame, raise the fallen, help the weak, and as many like things.
But I am amazed, beloved, at what some of the foolish are accustomed to dare,
and they attempt shamelessly and rashly to take priesthood, not having been
called by the grace of Christ, not knowing that the wretched heap up fire and
death for themselves. I do not tell you, O man, only not to receive the
priesthood rashly, but not even to touch any other of the vessels of the
all-august worship, if indeed you have read what Uzzah suffered, when he
touched the ark of God.
Always remember, beloved brother, this divine word of the Most High, spoken
through Isaiah the prophet: Upon whom shall I rest, but upon the meek and
humble and who trembles at my words? Always remember this commandment, and
acquire as a treasure the meek thought, that so you may be able to go up
noetically into the Jerusalem above, and to offer noetic sacrifices to the
King and God upon the altar above, where crowns of incorruption are woven; and
there before the angels Christ will bind upon you a crown of immortality, and
with the armies above you will hymn a victory-hymn to the Holy Trinity, unto
the ages of ages. Amen.
