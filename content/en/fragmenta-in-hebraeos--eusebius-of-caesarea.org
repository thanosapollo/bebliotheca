=24.605= For a divine descent upon the mountain is said to have occurred at
the time when he was giving the law, that all might behold the energy of God;
for this is a clear descent, yet not a local one. At any rate, though the
things growing on the mountain were burning fiercely, the fire consumed
nothing; and voices of trumpets were heard more vehemently, though no such
instruments were present, nor the one who sounded, but all things happening by
a divine arrangement. So that it is clear through these things that the
descent was divine, and that God without any [local movement] shows his own
majesty through all things.
