CONCERNING THE 72 PROPHETS AND PROPHETESSES OF EPIPHANIUS.
1. Adam. 2. Enoch. 3. Noah. 4. Abraham. 5. Isaac. 6. Jacob. 7. Moses. 8.
Aaron. 9. Joshua. 10. Eldad. 11. Modad. 12. Job. 13. Samuel. 14. Nathan. 15.
David. 16. Gad. 17. Jeduthun. 18. Asaph. 19. Heman. 20. Ethan. 21. Solomon.
22. Ahijah. 23. Shemaiah. 24. The man of God, Seth. 25. Eli, who is also
Shiloh. 26. Joad. 27. Addo. 28. Azariah. 29. Hanani, who is also Ananias. 30.
Jehu. 31. Micaiah. 32. Elijah. 33. Oziel. 34. Eliad. 35. Jesus son of Ananias.
36. Elisha. 37. Jonathan. 38. Zechariah, who is also Azariah. 39. Another
Zechariah. 40. Hosea. 41. Joel. 42. Amos. 43. Obadiah. 44. Jonah. 45. Isaiah.
46. Micah. 47. Nahum. 48. Habakkuk. 49. Obed. 50. Abdadon. 51. Jeremiah. 52.
Baruch. 53. Zephaniah. 54. Uriah. 55. Ezekiel. 56. Daniel. 57. Ezra. 58.
Haggai. 59. Zechariah. 60. Malachi. 61. Zechariah the father of John. 62.
Simeon. 63. John the Baptist. And still we have known others found having a
least part of prophecy or also by a prophetic pretext, and some even having
prophesied something more extra, some from Adam, and some from the times in
sequence, whom I thought it necessary not to pass over again after the 63rd
number, but to join to the sequence of the recapitulation, such as also these
are. 64. Enos. 65. Methuselah. 66. Lamech. 67. Balaam. 68. Saul. 69. Abimelech
who is also Ahimelech. 70. Amase. 71. Zadok. 72. the old man in Bethel. Agabus
in the Acts.

AND AS MANY PROPHETESSES.
1. Sarah. 2. Rebecca. 3. Miriam the sister of Moses. 4. Deborah. 5. Huldah. 6.
Anna the mother of Samuel. 7. Judith. 8. Elizabeth, the mother of John. 9.
Anna the daughter of Phanuel. 10. Mary the Theotokos.
