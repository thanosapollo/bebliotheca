Admonitory verses of Chrysostom.
Whoever wishes to see that light must keep these things in the heart: from
fleshly passions and useless thoughts, and from every oath, and from wrath and
turmoil, and from distraction and remembering of wrongs, and from not judging
men at all, and to be himself rational and sound in mind, and clean of fleshly
pollutions, to be meek, humble, quiet, confessing and a child of peace, to be
continent of foods and drinks, and to attend to prayer without yielding, in
all these things to have as beginning and end the sum of virtues, love.
