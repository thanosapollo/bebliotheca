:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Jn 9:3 In this the Savior himself overturned a lawless doctrine. For some
supposed that souls that sin are sent from heaven into a body for punishment,
and that sometimes the soul, preexisting by itself, remains before the body,
and sometimes for the sake of its own sins is entangled with bodies, and that
then a woman can be with child when some soul sins; abolishing such an error,
Christ when asked said: neither this man sinned nor his parents, but that the
works of God might be made manifest.
