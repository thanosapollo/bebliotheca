On the Ascension. Sermon 5.
=52.801=
The day of the Master's ascension has arrived; and many having gone on
the way toward it, whom the father has also urged our own smallness
toward discourses. And it seemed just to us to bring silence as a
covering of our brevity of speech, and to hide the greatness of our
lack of education; but since together with the father and the powers
above you urge the multitude toward discourses, and you command us to
speak with them the things which of old David also cried out concerning
the Master's ascension: Lift up your gates, O you rulers, and be lifted
up, you everlasting gates, and the King of glory shall enter in; come,
today let us also sound forth the things fitting of the day. For today
our Master Christ goes up to the paternal throne; today the one who
took a body sits together with the Father, neither having been
separated before, nor now sitting with him for the first time; for he
always rests in the bosom of the Father; alone containing the Father,
and alone contained by the Father; for I, he says, am in the Father,
and the Father in me; and the Word is in the Father, and the Word is on
the earth. And neither having come down did he empty the paternal bosom
as nothing; nor having gone up into the heavens did he leave the earth
deserted of his own presence; but even while associating with men he
sat together with the Father, and sitting together with the Father he
remains inseparable from men.
For behold, I, he says, am with you all the days unto the consummation
of the age. Today the Master Christ is borne up from among men into the
heavens, not being translated after the manner of Enoch, not going up
toward heaven =52.802= on a chariot of fire after the manner of Elijah;
but he is taken up, that he may send to the disciples the power of the
Holy Spirit. David, having seen the joy of this ascension many
generations before, proclaimed it beforehand saying: God has gone up
with a shout, he who formed Adam from dust, who justified Abel, and
translated Enoch. God has gone up with a shout, he who piloted Noah,
and chose Abraham as faithful. God has gone up with a shout, he who
received Isaac as an unslain sacrifice, and filled Jacob with
blessings. God has gone up with a shout, he who guided Joseph when he
was wandering, and accepted the endurance of Job, God has gone up with
a shout, he who glorified Moses, and granted the high-priesthood to
Aaron. God has gone up with a shout, he who strengthened Jesus the son
of Nun, and granted the prophet to Samuel. God has gone up with a
shout, he who empowered David, and made Solomon wise. God has gone up
with a shout, he who inspired the prophets, and granted to the apostles
the gifts of healings. God has gone up with a shout, he who above is
without mother, and below without father. God has gone up with a shout,
the pre-eternal offspring of the Father, and the unplanted shoot of the
Virgin mother. God has gone up with a shout, the giver of life, and the
dispenser of wonders. God has gone up with a shout, he who by death put
death to death, and granted life to the race of men. God has gone up
with a shout, the Lord with the voice of a trumpet. To him be the glory
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
