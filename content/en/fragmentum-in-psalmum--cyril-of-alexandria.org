Of Cyril. And there follows for them at times also a compassion, so that they
may not fall into the same punishments as the impious. Therefore these too
will come to life again, yet not in the counsel of the righteous; and by
counsel of the righteous he means, as it were, the rank and the dignity. For
even if some of those who have sinned should be worthy of mercy or of pardon,
they would not be numbered in the choirs of the saints, for another is the lot
of those. And the boasts stand raised, and the grace is hung about them, and
the delight is unending. For they have harvested the fruit of deeds of valor
and they are enriched with the rewards of manly excellence toward every good
whatsoever, Christ saying to them: Come, you blessed of my Father; inherit the
kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.

Fragment on Psalm 2.7
The Lord said to me, You are my Son, today I have begotten you. He who alone
knows the Father and is known by the Father alone, his wisdom and his Word,
the angel of great counsel, very well makes clear to us the mystery of the
economy with flesh. For being Son by nature and truly, and having shone forth
from the very substance of God and Father, he became flesh, that is, man, and
underwent with us the birth according to the flesh, although being pre-eternal
and always co-existing with the one who begot him. Yet even if he became
flesh, the Father knows him as his own Son even thus, for he said, The Lord
said to me: You are my Son, today I have begotten you. You see at the
beginning the confession, that is, the proclamation; for he has set "you are,"
in order to show the pre-eternal; for he did not come to be in time, but was
always this, that is, Son. And he added "Today I have begotten you," in order
to show his most recent birth also according to the flesh, which he underwent
willingly through the holy Virgin. For this reason she has also been named
Theotokos; for she bore not a man simply, joined to God according to an
outward relation, but rather the Word himself from God the Father, who put on
human poverty and became man according to us. Confessing this faith we have
been baptized, and we will not deny the Master who bought us with his own
blood. Therefore when the Father says that he has today begotten the Son who
always is and is pre-eternal from him, "today" signifying the present time,
that is, that of the becoming-man, let us consider this: that even having
become flesh, that is, man, the Father has confessed him as his own Son, and
he appropriates, as it were, also his second birth according to the flesh. For
not because he became man did he depart from being by nature Son of God and
Father, but he has remained even thus Son and in the innate dignities, even if
being rich he became poor for us.
