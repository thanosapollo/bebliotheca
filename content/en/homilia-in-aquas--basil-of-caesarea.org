ON THE WATERS AND ON HOLY BAPTISM
Having yesterday given the discourse to the prophets and patriarchs, I almost
left behind the created works of God *** for all things both are washed and
are watered and are purified by the waters. And the nature of the waters is
one, but it is divided in many parts through the creatures: in the vine it
becomes astringent, in oil gentle; in the lily white, red in the rose; in
produce nourishing, in trees increasing; it waters the four-footed beasts and
rears the fishes; it purifies the bodies of men. As often as we live, we live
through waters; as often as we are rich, <through waters we are rich>; as
often as we rest, we rest through waters. Does the power of the waters then
seem small to you? But I pass by all these things because of the length of the
discourse. For as often as we interpret, we leave the greater part aside; for
human speech does not suffice to explain accurately the much-hymned grace of
the waters, as neither is it possible to measure the sea by speech, nor how
many goods of the sea waters have been allotted to men from the wisdom of God.
And again I will hasten to the new covenant; for having spoken much concerning
waters I do not know from where I thirsted, and swimming through the waters of
the present I thirst again; but I will remember our Savior Jesus Christ
proclaiming in the gospels and saying: If anyone thirsts, let him come to me
and drink; for he who believes in me, as the scripture said, rivers of living
water will flow from his belly. Therefore let us run to him and let our thirst
be healed, being watered with spiritual streams and irrigated with heavenly
waters. Lest somehow then [I be found carrying a river also in myself] and
seeking only a river I be found possessing the whole spring? For the immortal
possession of our souls is Jesus Christ and a heavenly spring. But where shall
I run? Who is the one leading by the hand? Who is the one showing this cup of
life? Lead me, the stranger, by the hand to the waters, guide me toward the
spring and river and streams [leaping] toward heaven, welling up above and
bubbling forth from there, and sending those who drink in faith into the
kingdom of the heavens; for we greatly long to find salvation of such waters.
Come, blessed of the apostles, John; call us to the Jordan. For having gone
around many waters we await from you the divine crown; for I know prophets and
patriarchs saved through waters [symbolically] and all creation being washed
with waters, but the sinless God being baptized with water. This one comes
beside the Jordan to be baptized and that the great and paradoxical wonder may
be marveled at, the only-begotten God, who is in the bosom of the Father, who
in the beginning was with God, God, Word and Son of God, worshipped by the
angels and glorified by the archangels, whom the Cherubim hymn and the
Seraphim glorify; having spread out a great purification and gospel from the
Jordan to the world, he teaches the race of men not to despise this heavenly
grace. And the sinless one is baptized and sanctifies the waters of the
inhabited world.
For the Lord is upon many waters, but he fully assures those present who it is
that is being baptized; for the Holy Spirit was coming down upon him in the
form of a dove, and the Father from above from the heavens was crying: This is
my beloved Son, in whom I was well pleased. True is the prophecy that says:
The Lord is upon many waters. The God of glory thundered. Who then after so
great a glory disbelieves? Who doubts concerning this heavenly grace? It is
not possible for anyone to see the kingdom of the heavenlies who has not been
deemed worthy of baptism, nor is it possible to be found perfect, even if he
live according to the angels, unless he has received the crown of this gift.
The Savior and our Lord Jesus Christ himself declared, saying to us: Unless
one is born of water and Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of the
heavens. Even if he accomplish the life in an angelic manner and dedicate
himself to God according to the apostles, but despise the grace of baptism, he
cannot be found perfect. Thus he was saying to the blessed Cornelius:
Cornelius, your prayers and your alms have gone up as a memorial before God.
But upon these is the perfect crown, the faithful gift through the waters.
Therefore he sent for the chief of the apostles, Peter, and filled up what was
lacking; he came, he admonished, he baptized, he crowned. Stand then firm in
the faith, remain founded upon the grace of the waters. Do not look at waters
and doubt again; see how through waters God grants you the forgiveness of
sins. Do not look at waters, and take the easiness of the gift into contempt.
When you see waters, run with faith; for these waters quench Gehenna for you,
these refresh you toward the fiery river, these alone put the mind of Gehenna
to shame. Thus the rich man was saying in the gospels, seeking and seeing
Lazarus resting in the bosom of our father Abraham: Have mercy on me, father
Abraham, and send Lazarus, that he may dip his finger in water and cool my
tongue, because I am in pain in this flame. Therefore, when you see waters,
run with eagerness; for these waters open heaven for you, these waters
transfer you from slavery into freedom; these waters open paradise for you and
grant spiritual eyes; these waters send you on to heavenly mysteries, into
which angels desire to look. You have then been given wings in thought into
the heavens; you saw God sanctifying the nature of the waters; you saw the
undivided agreement of the Holy Trinity upon the waters; you received from God
prophetic promises, announcing the good news of your salvation; the crown of
the soul is manifest, the forgiveness of long-standing sins is near. Prepare
all things purely and firmly toward the readiness, a pure faith, an unwounded
reasoning, a divine fear, a true worship, an angelic robe, bringing all
perfect things to the Perfect one; for you are about to be served by the
Levites, to be led by the hand by the priests, to be escorted by the angels,
to be purified by the waters, to be crowned by God and the Father, because to
him belongs the glory together with the Son and the Holy Spirit unto the ages
of ages. Amen.
