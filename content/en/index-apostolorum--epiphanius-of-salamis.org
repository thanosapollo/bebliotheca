THE NAMES OF THE TWELVE APOSTLES WHO PREACHED THE
GOSPEL OF CHRIST AND HOW THEY WERE PERFECTED.
OF EPIPHANIUS.
1. Simon Peter, the chief of the apostles, as he appears to make known through
his epistles, preached the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ in Pontus and Gala
108 tia and Cappadocia and Bithynia and in Italy, Asia and in Illyricum; later
in Rome under Nero the emperor he is crucified head downward, having asked to
suffer thus, and he is buried in Rome itself three days before the Kalends of
July, which is Epiphi 5.
2. And Andrew his brother, as those before us have handed down, preached to
the Scythians and Sogdians and Gorsini and in Sebastopolis the Great, where
the camp of Apsarus is and the harbor of Hyssos and the river Phasis, where
109 Ethiopians dwell, and he is buried in Patras of Achaia, bound to a cross
by Aegeas the king of Patras.
3. And James the son of Zebedee, brother of John the evangelist, preached the
gospel of Christ to the twelve tribes of the dispersion, and was slain with
the sword by Herod the tetrarch of the Jews and was buried there in Judea.
4. And John his brother preached the gospel of Christ in Asia, and having been
banished by Trajan the emperor of the Romans to the island of Patmos because
of the word of the Lord, being there he wrote the Gospel according to John,
and published it through Gaius 110 the host; and after the death of Trajan he
returns from the island to Ephesus and there he buried himself while still
living, being 120 years old, by the will of God.
5. And Philip the apostle: this man was from Bethsaida, from the village of
Peter and Andrew, and he preached the gospel in upper Phrygia, and he dies in
Hierapolis and is buried there gloriously with his own.
6. And Bartholomew the apostle preached the gospel of Christ to the Indians
called the Happy, and having written for them the holy Gospel according to
Matthew in their own dialect, he fell asleep in Albania, a city of Greater
Armenia, and was buried there.
111 7. And Thomas the apostle, as the tradition holds, was from the city of
Paneas in Galilee, and he preached the gospel of the Lord to the Parthians and
Medes, and also to the Persians and Germans and Hyrcanians and Indians and
Bactrians and Magi, and he fell asleep in the city of Calamina in India.
8. And Matthew the evangelist was from Jerusalem and there he wrote the gospel
of the Lord in the Hebrew dialect, and he gave it out to the holy apostles,
and James the brother of the Lord interpreting it; and he fell asleep in
Hierapolis of Parthia and is buried there.
112 9. James who was surnamed Thaddaeus, having become the brother of the Lord
according to the flesh, who was also first appointed bishop in Jerusalem by
the apostles; and when a pretext of disputes arose for the Jews, being struck
with stones by them he fell asleep in Jerusalem and was buried there
gloriously near the tomb of the priests. Simon the Zealot, having gone through
all Mauretania and the country of the Africans and preached Christ, and later
being crucified by them in Britain and perfected, is buried there.


10. And Thaddaeus, who is also Lebbaeus, his brother, who was surnamed Judas
of James, preached the gospel of the Lord to the Edessenes and all Mesopotamia
and under Abgar the king of the Edessenes, and he died in Berytus and was
buried there gloriously.
113 11. Judas the brother of the Lord, after James his own brother and Symeon
the cousin of the Lord, and leaving him bishop in Jerusalem for 7 years,
preached the gospel of Christ in all Judea and Samaria, and he also made a
catholic epistle, and formerly he had two sons, James and Zoker, and he died
in Jerusalem gloriously.
11. Simon surnamed Judas, who also became bishop after the death of James in
Jerusalem, under Trajan the emperor was bound to a cross and was perfected in
Ostracine of Egypt, having lived 120 years.
11. Simon the Cananaean, the son of Clopas, who is also Judas, became bishop
in Jerusalem after James the Just, and having lived 120 years was delivered to
the cross and was martyred under Trajan the emperor.
12. And Matthias, being one of the 70 disciples, whom 114 our Lord appointed
after the manifestation from the dead, who was also numbered together with the
eleven apostles in place of Judas Iscariot, preached the gospel of our Lord in
outer Ethiopia and there was martyred by the Ethiopians at the harbor of
Hyssos.
13. And Paul the apostle, after the Lord's ascension into heaven, began to
preach the gospel of the Lord, beginning from Jerusalem he went as far as
Illyricum and Italy and Spain, whose epistles also, with wisdom, are current
among us. And under Nero the son of Claudius the em 115 peror, in the city of
Rome, his head was cut off; he was martyred on Epiphi 5, three days before the
Kalends of July; and there he was buried near the holy apostle Peter; they are
there until this day in Christ.
Martyrdom of Paul the apostle. Under Nero Caesar of the Romans, Paul the
apostle was martyred there, having his head cut off with the sword in the
thirty-sixth year of the saving Passion, having fought the good fight in Rome
on the fifth day, according to the Syromacedonians, of the month of Panemos,
which is called among the Egyptians Epiphi, and among the Romans three days
before the Kalends of July, in the month of June, on which the holy apostle
was perfected in the martyrdom that belongs to him, in the sixty-ninth year of
the coming of our Saviour Jesus Christ. The whole time, then, from when he was
martyred is three hundred and thirty years until this present consulship, the
fourth of Arcadius and the third of Honorius, the two brothers, emperors
Augusti, the ninth indiction of the fifteen-year cycle, the twenty-ninth day
of the month of June.
14. Mark the evangelist was a Cyrenaean of Libya, and he preached the gospel
to the Alexandrians and all their surrounding country as far as the Pentapolis
and in all Egypt, it having been given out by Peter the apostle; he imparted
the mystery of pie 116 ty in the great city of Alexandria, being dragged with
ropes he died and, reduced to ashes by the idol-mad Greeks, he was laid in the
places of Boukolos and is there until this day, and the day of his falling
asleep is Pharmuthi kyrie, which is April. Mark the evangelist and first
bishop of Alexandria preached the gospel of the Lord to the Alexandrians and
all its surrounding country, from Egypt as far as the Pentapolis; and in the
reign of Trajan in Alexandria of the Boukoloi, taking a blow on the neck and
being dragged from the so-called places of Boukolos as far as the so-called
Angels, there he was burned with fire by the idol-mad in the month Pharmuthi
30 and was buried there in the Boukolos. Mark the evangelist, having preached
the gospel of the


Lord to the Alexandrians and all its surrounding country as far as the
Pentapolis, is buried in Alexandria in the Boukoloi with Iktaros the
protomartyr in Lycos, whom Alexander brought down and placed, where all the
bishops lie.
15. And Luke the evangelist, a physician by craft, himself wrote the Gospel
according to Luke, by commission of Paul the apostle. And it was preached in
Greece and in Rome and in Italy, and he also wrote the Acts of the Apostles by
commission of Paul the apostle, for he 117 traveled with the apostles,
especially with the holy Paul, whom the blessed Paul also remembering writes
in epistles: Luke the physician greets you in the Lord; he died there as
bishop of Thebes and was buried.
Luke the evangelist was an Antiochene by race, and a physician by craft, and
he wrote the gospel by commission of Peter the apostle, and the Acts of the
Apostles by commission of Paul the apostle; for he traveled with the apostles
and especially with Paul, whom Paul also remembering wrote in an epistle: Luke
the beloved physician greets you in the Lord. And he died in Ephesus and was
buried there, and later he was translated to Constantinople together with
Andrew and Timothy the apostles, in the times of Constantius the emperor, son
of Constantine the Great. The one who narrates the Acts of the Apostles is
Luke the evangelist. For this man, being an Antiochene by race, and a
physician by science, traveled with the apostles and especially with Paul, and
knowing accurately he writes; and he narrates how the Lord was taken up,
angels receiving him, and the outpouring of the Holy Spirit that took place at
Pentecost upon the apostles and all those then present, and the appointment of
Matthias in place of Judas the traitor, and the appointment of the seven
deacons, and the election of Paul and what he suffered, and what wonders the
apostles worked through prayer and faith in Christ himself.
