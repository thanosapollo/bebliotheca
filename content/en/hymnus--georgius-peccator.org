HYMN OF GEORGE THE SINNER
Remember, Christ, Son of God who rules on high, the heart of your household
servant, the sinner who wrote these things; and grant me release from the
heart-fed sufferings that grow in my filthy soul; and grant me to see, Savior
Jesus, your holy radiance; there appearing I will sing a song to the healer of
souls, healer of limbs, with the great Father and the holy Spirit.
