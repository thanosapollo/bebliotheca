Of Saint Epiphanius concerning measures and weights.
A kor of wheat is thirty modii, which is called chor; chor is named from the
setting of the hills; for the hills are called choriai. A medimnos is five
modii, so as to be four modii of measures of twenty-two xestai. A phakel of
barley is fifteen modii, which is called eparma. A kadus of oil of fifty
xestai. And this is of the work of the oil-press. A bath is heaped modii so as
to be one modius. And for liquids, of fifty xestai. An artabe is of
seventy-two xestai. A complete measure. Three measures of fine flour. Each
measure had a gomor. And the gomor is a tenth of the great measure of the
artabe, that is seven xestai and a fifth. Three kana of groats or groat. The
kana he calls little baskets. And chondrites is a kind of wheat cut in two. A
nebel of wine, a measure of one hundred fifty xestai. A kotyle is half a
xestes. And from there those who sell wine or oil are called kotylistai, that
is they have been named. A tryblion in form is an oxybaphin or oxybaphon, that
is a side-dish. And it holds half of a xestes. An aporryma has the half of the
saiton. And the saiton is of twenty-two xestai. And it is in a ceramic tylos.
Saphitha is a measure of twenty-two xestai among some. And among others
eighteen; and the holy one nine. A kampsakes of water of four xestai. A chous
is a chouza, the great one of twenty-eight xestai, and the holy one of six
xestai. An alabastron of myrrh. An alabastron is bissin, round-shaped, holding
one litra, that is a mina. A siklon. The siklon is the fourth of the ounce,
which is one nomisma and a half. Medok he calls the kyathoi. And the masmarod
he calls the strainers. A kyathos sometimes unto a simple cup, which is of one
xestes, and sometimes unto a triple, which is of three xestai. And a
drawing-vessel is from a ceramic, drawing through a long handle. A kyathos has
one ounce, and that of oil one ounce and a half, and that of wine one ounce
and a sixth, and that of honey two ounces and a half. The great gomor, which
is also lethek. And lethek, as it is said in the prophet Hosea, "I hired for
myself a lethek of barley." So it is the same. For fifteen modii are
signified. A drachma, 3 grammata. A drax of flour. A drax is the grasp of a
hand. The great ein, of eighteen xestai. And the holy one of five xestai and a
half, of metretai of seventy-two xestai. Silver pieces and the bronze ones of
a weight of three grammata. Perfect silver in one hundred litrai. An argyrous
and a mna and a manes, twenty ounces, which becomes one litra eight ounces. An
amphoreus of twenty-four xestai. Two assaria, lepta. One assarion, weights of
six grammata. Three bags, which are also pholoi, of two hundred fifty denarii,
become three hundred twelve litrai. A denarius weighs one litra three ounces.
A didrachm of six grammata. A lepton saton carries three grammata. A lepton is
a little weight, the fourth of an ounce. For asar is Hebrew, and being
diminished it is called assarion, concerning which the Lord says "Are not two
sparrows sold for an assarion?" A miliarision and a golden and a half of the
silver carries ten ounces. A nomisma, one hundred twenty-five litrai. A
nomisma of other grammata. A nummus. Numa a certain king of the Romans made
it, and it was called after his name. An obol that is not of silver, but made
from iron, carries three grammata. An obol of silver carries three grammata. A
holke carries two grammata. A holy shekel carries twelve grammata. A stater
carries twelve grammata. A pholes and a bag has two argyroi and a half. Two
hundred fifty denarii, litrai. Nyron the holy shekel in the Numbers thus of
fifteen litrai he says your firstborn of five shekels" according to the holy
shekel twenty. An oxybaphon has eight xestai. A cochliarion has seventy-two
xestai. He shows the shekel of four obols. The obol carries three grammata. So
then the holy shekel is of six grammata and the royal of six. A denarius was
sixty assaria, and the argyrous denarii. The argyrous was struck from
Archinoos, and from the assaria Abraham went into the land of Canaan, he says,
he has brought this type. They say a drachma has three grammata. The hair of
Absalom they weighed in this shekel at the time when he was shearing his head,
one hundred twenty-five shekels.
