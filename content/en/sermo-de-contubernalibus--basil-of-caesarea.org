A DISCOURSE OF OUR HOLY FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS, BASIL THE GREAT, ON THE
SYNEISAKTOI.


Let us too begin to speak, strengthened by the prayers of the holy servants of
Christ through the Holy Spirit by the Only-begotten Son of God Christ our
Lord, with words of piety and sobriety. Let us utter words of grace and truth,
strengthened by the great grace. Opening our mouth in Christ, let us bring
forth some small treasure laid up in our heart, small in its sensible
substance but great in its intelligible nature; and let us set forth a coin of
pure gold, and having purified it as in a furnace with sincere understanding,
display it most radiant before you all. For the mind is eager, and =30.813=
the spirit is eager, but the flesh is weak.

For I wished to stand up and sound forth as through a trumpet about the
highest summits, and to send forth like thunder over the whole world the words
of sobriety, so that they might be for a testimony to all who corrupt the
truth. But the thing is impossible; for I am a man of brief life, and mean in
age, and small in voice, and besides, weak in soul, woven through with many
sins. But even if I should raise my voice to the height like thunder and cry
out loudly, I shall not be heard. For Christ the Only-begotten Son of God, who
fastened the heavens as a vault, and founded the earth upon the waters, and
established the sea; who prepared fire and the divine and gehenna and fear and
trembling of eternal judgments for sinners, this one, crying out through the
Gospels and giving command, is not heeded. And again the holy Apostles,
perfect disciples of Christ, and named sons of thunder, writing through
epistles and exhorting are despised; and neither the holy prophets of God, nor
the Law, nor men admonishing put them to shame; and who else then will be able
to correct so hard and untamed a thing? When therefore the wound has come upon
the whole world, and it seems to me to apply many words like cauteries to the
passion. For the disease has been poured out, and it has prevailed over the
many, not to say all, and like the darkest mist it has seized the sight of the
mind of the many.

But I shall speak with boldness, kindling a lamp of piety through holy words
from my mouth, and narrating venerable examples of the virtue of conduct,
fearing no man, but having before my eyes the one who judges without respect
of persons the living and the dead, and not wishing to become a partaker of
those caught in the net of evil, so that the word, like the sun spreading out
its rays, may dissolve the darkness of the understanding of those who hear
from a pure heart, and may put an end to the passion with its own gleamings.
For the love of money is a root of all evils, says the divine Apostle; truly a
root bearing out many branches of wickedness, through which all things in the
evil life are accomplished. But the love of women is a root of all evils and a
leader of destruction, the madness, I mean, of the beloved women, budding a
root of destruction, and blossoming into madness. For it is Jewish, having
been transferred from Sodom, since it possesses a certain hypocritical and
counterfeit fantasy of outward love. And taking up that saying of the Apostle,
bring it forth into the midst, the one that says: Have we not authority to
lead about a sister as a wife, as also the other apostles and Cephas?

On which those enslaved to the passion of misery lean, taking hold of what
was
written well for benefit, through their own vanity, for destruction of souls
=30.816=.

And let us examine in parallel the things done by the apostles and by those
now believing. Who was the writer, let us first learn, and whether the blessed
Apostle permitted through an epistle, and did not rather forbid them, offering
himself as a type to all of us; for we know that he spent all his time with
the most devout men, and did not live with the most unseemly women; and not
even are they ashamed to say this. Let those who compare themselves to Peter
the foremost of the apostles, and key-bearer of the kingdom of the heavens,
pour shame upon themselves, who say: If he had a beloved one, we also follow
in his footsteps.

And perhaps you think it well. Raise Tabitha for me from the dead as he
himself did, through faith in our Lord Jesus Christ, that I may believe you
that you too are a spirit-bearer, filled with divine power. Cast out for me
the demons whom he cast out, work the cures of diseases and the other
healings, as many as he accomplished in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ; for
I let the rest go. When you show me this, then I shall believe you, that you
do not kindle toward the beast. Or do you not hear what our Lord Jesus Christ
says, that Whoever looks upon a woman to desire her has already committed
adultery with her in his heart? Or does the word of the Gospel seem a myth to
you? You see how he forbade not only the doing, but turned away even the
character of the look, knowing that the snare is a stumbling-block to the soul
of the man?

But the thing seems to you, the suffering one, a plaything; for you have
puffed up your mind with shamelessness, having put on your face like a mask;
for you do not know of what shipwreck you have fallen into. Let us return to
the characters of the holy prophets, who spent the whole time of their life in
mountains and caves, freed not only from seeing a woman, but even from
thinking of one. Or will even this seem to you assuredly nonsense? For I see
you consoling yourself with many empty antitheses of words. Remember the
expulsion from Paradise, and the destruction of the flood, and the overthrow
of the Sodomites, that through a woman all evils come from the beginning to
the end; and the betrayal of righteous Samson, and the plot against that
venerable Joseph. Remember the destruction of the chosen race of the Jews. But
if these things do not persuade you, let wise Solomon persuade you, saying
somewhere: Wine and women will turn away the hearts of the wise. Of the wise,
he said, lest you make some exception even here, and think cheaply of the
saying. For wine, sinking into a man and working drunkenness in him, like a
river in flood sweeps away the whole mind with its roar, and leads the man
into madness. The same things does the woman also, like wine, work against
man.

Wherefore also the proverb-writer likened wine to a woman =30.817=, and all
the prophets likened the race of women to various evils and to venomous
beasts, the destroyer of the souls of men. But not even these things persuade
you; for everything seems, as it appears, a plaything to you. For you have
stopped your ears like a deaf asp, not heeding the divine Scriptures. And
finally you have even changed your own nature into a bestial manner, becoming
like in disposition to the beast with which you dwell; whose words are indeed
articulate, and its voice sweet, but its soul wild, and its mind of dragons,
and its choice of creeping things, and its look breathing fire, and its kiss
venomous; and I contend with her, so that through the flattery of her words,
and the deception of her countenance, and the assault of her kiss, she may
spit out the venom of the beast into the throat of the wretched man. For it
sweeps away the understanding, and makes the mind drunk, and even the ruling
faculty it beheads and drives the mind out of itself; and as the drugs given
to men for killing darken the understanding, and sweep the whole man into
insensibility with the vehemence of the poison, in the same way also the woman
through the kiss harms the rational man, led away by an irrational impulse;
and the self-governing, godlike faculty of the soul, composed of many chords
and virtues, through one deceitful word, or a hiss of the lips, or a nod of
the eyes, like some lifeless instrument she turns about here and there
wherever she wishes, and like a sea wave-tossed she sweeps the whole body
along with her own impulses.

And one may then see the wretched man, the pitiable one, worthy of all pity,
the sensible in understanding, the one experienced to the height of
grammatical science, and a craftsman of sophistical teaching, the one
possessing the science of medicine, the one experienced in all kinds of
letters, or who has given himself to the reading of the divine words, and who
has often chosen the solitary life, and holds the forefront of the Church,
like some mean animal bound with a rope and dragged here and there; for he was
bound through the counterfeit love, or rather, if the truth must be said, the
shameful. And such a one becomes worthy of a funeral lament, wept while alive,
and mourned when dead. For while living he died, dwelling as in a tomb in his
own body; and dying, he died doubly, punished with the judgment of the gehenna
of eternal fire. What do you say now, you who are more wretched than the
wretched, you neighbor of wickedness? Where are your venerable ways? Where is
your education to the height? Where the labor of wisdom? Where is the
venerableness of your conduct? Where the good order? Where are the many vigils
performed by you for the sake of philosophy? Where is understanding? Where the
love of God? Where is your love of Christ? But far from these you have become,
having dwelt with an evil beast, or rather having cast yourself down into the
abyss of destruction. For who compelled you to enter into the workshop of
lawlessness?

And who compelled you to enter into its bosom, as into the depth of a raging
sea =30.820=? For were you not your own master? You have made yourself
self-condemned. Since you were not willing, before the height of the passion,
to release the burdensome thing from yourself, surely you were afraid lest the
mysteries, being cast out, should triumph over you. Why did you entrust to her
the mysteries well laid up in you? For did she not give you straitness, being
guarded in your treasures of wisdom? Then the Savior and Lord of us Jesus
Christ the Only-begotten Son of God commands the one scandalized by his own
right eye to cut out the scandalizing member through himself.

But you, an enemy and hostile thing, not only scandalizing but putting to
death the soul, do you not cast out from yourself? Let her be beloved through
Christ until the farewell; but if she harms the soul, not even the farewell to
her; only to visit the sick, and not to scandalize the healthy, or in time of
affliction or persecution, or in the church; since we are all one in Christ,
but now we are not. Why? Since we do not wish it, having preferred the hatred
of brethren to the love of brethren. Do not scandalize the world, you who have
the syneisaktoi. For did not the Scripture permit you to marry? You have
become a stumbling-block both to Jews and to Greeks, and to the Church of God.
Or do you attempt to oppose the holy Scriptures, and provoke the Lord to
jealousy? Are you stronger than he? Look upon your own weakness, be ashamed of
your own passion, heal yourselves in due season, before suddenly like a flash
of lightning his presence comes, before the fire-bearing, before the outer
darkness, before the end and the sleepless worm. Trembling we say: hear with
fear; and let not the thing appear ridiculous: sons of God are delivered in
chains to Tartarus through a woman.

But you will say to me that, Dwelling with her, I am not harmed. If the dragon
dwelling with the asp is not harmed, surely you also will not be harmed; for
the dragon, consorting with the drakaina, receives death as the wages of its
cohabitation, the female beast being of such a nature. See then, lest the
thing of the woman is similar. But let us bring forth proofs, convicting
rational men: If fire does not kindle the stubble, but you thrust the male
stone against the female, a conflagration comes about. But since you say that
a woman can also be temperate: this I know clearly. And a man remaining by
himself can be comely: this I know exactly. But a man with a woman is an evil;
and a woman with a man a worse evil. If again a woman, departing from her own
kinsfolk, attempt to dwell with a strange man on account of the counterfeit
love, this is then a mixture working a conflagration of an unlawful union.

For being rekindled in time by the companionship of cohabitation, they kindle
the fuel, and set the house aflame, or rather burn their own bodies into
gehenna and eternal punishment. Tell me, you many-wiled beast, you who have
the garment of the leopard, and have bound to your breast the mind of the asp
=30.821=: why do you anoint yourself, why paint yourself under the anointing?
why do you take on an idol-like form with borrowed, lifeless colors? why do
you torture your hairs? why do you raise upon your head locks and curls added
from foreign things, both of dead and of irrational animals, and through the
head, and the look, and through the variety of foreign garments deceive, and
soften, and drug with hellebore the souls of men? For the unbelieving woman,
gathering many kinds of lifeless materials, and varieties of colors through
all sorts of dyes, and pitch, and lime, and herbs, which are external to her
own body, having fastened them on, has made herself like some many-formed
beast. But the believing woman ought to be a stranger to these things,
especially the one who professes to be a Christian. Or do you find fault with
God, woman, that he made you such? Be as you were fashioned; add nothing to
yourself through cleverness; for you are not more experienced than the one who
fashioned you.

And desiring to please whom do you do these things? Your husband? You have no
husband, as you say, virgin; but if you have, he knows your many-wiled
composition. Why, having armed the body with manifold lures, do you hunt the
souls of men to destruction? and how do you dare to call upon Christ, or to
tread the threshold of the church, bearing the forms of lifeless idols in the
whole body through gold that perishes, or rather being carried in procession
by them, and drawing a great throng of wanton men to the unlawful spectacle of
yourself? But to whom do we say these things? To a rational animal having the
choice of creeping dragons. Do the Scriptures not persuade you, nor does
gehenna frighten you, nor does the admonition of men pierce you? Even have
shame before your own nature; change your look; do not make it into an
idol-like character; do not imitate those surpassing in harlot and adulterous
shame. For those, being daughters of lawlessness, have the custom of doing
such things. But who gave you such a command? What prophetic Scriptures, or
what Gospels, or what apostolic ordinances? I know that the divine Scriptures
turn away even those who have this custom from their nation from doing any
longer the idol-forming assimilations.

It has been spoken with boldness, but nothing according to the worth of the
things done. But someone will say: Hard is this word; who can hear it? And
this hypocritical and most offensive race once said it to the Savior; but
know, all of you, what answer he gave them. So also here: let the one who
wishes be pierced. For the words of the wise, as goads, and as nails driven
in, says the Scripture. I think these things are trustworthy, even if moderate
in word, for the madness of those who have the syneisaktoi. Let the word
therefore cut off the snorting like a bridle, and =30.824= bring into order
the irrational impulse of the feminine stream being swept along. Fear is set
before, and trembling, and the fire resounds. Let no one despise. For if
someone is bestial, he will be tamed by fire; if someone is dragon-like in
choice, Tartarus has been prepared; if someone carries about a Sodomite heart,
fire from heaven rains down; for the whole heavens have been kindled with
fire. Let no one disregard the voice of the speaker, but let him look to the
fearful power of the words, and have before his eyes the judgment of God:
Merciless is the judgment to him who has not wrought mercy.

It remains for me then, in due season, also to speak moderately to you, men,
enemies of the brethren, and friends of the women, boiling toward the muddy
friendship of the women, and coldest toward the pure love of the brethren. But
you will say that you do this through Christ, providing for the weak vessel.
Yes, I know. Why then do you not do the same for your brother who suffers need
through Christ, but look upon the brethren as plotters, and the plotters as
sisters? But you will say to me: How am I able to take my brother with me? She
nourished me; she flattered me; she made for me garments of many colors, soft
things, and chains, and newly-tanned things; her voice is sweet, and her look
tender. You do not know what you think.

These are the words spoken in inspiration. Her sweet words will be found
afterward more bitter than gall to you; her tender look leads you down to the
bolts of Tartarus; but the love of the brother procures for you the kingdom of
the heavens. Why do you prefer the treacherous love of the asp-tortoise to the
genuine love of the brother? why do you pursue the destroyer, thrusting away
the savior? For if the Ninevite men, who believed upon the preaching of the
prophet, rising in the judgment will condemn those who disobeyed the words of
our Lord Jesus Christ, surely also the wise of the Greeks, who exercised
philosophy and chose misanthropy toward women, will condemn those who are in
the true wisdom and are entangled with wanton women; because those, through
grammatical experience and rhetorical skill, which is foolishness with God,
exercised self-control, though worshipping lifeless idols. But also those who
run in the racecourse of madness despise the thing, not looking at a woman at
all, for the sake of the corruptible prize; but you, having known the
mysteries of the heavenly things, and having heard beforehand the fearful
judgments of the age to come, did not keep sobriety, nor guard self-control,
led away by a vain reasoning to the companionship of the co-dwellers. And how
will any one of you be able to provide for the Church, and speak the word of
catechesis in the church, not having first purified his own conscience? To
whom one might rightly say: Hypocrite, cast out first the beam out of your own
eye, and then you will be able to cast out the mote in the eye of your brother
=30.825=. And how will you dare to speak of sobriety, yourself enslaved to
woman-madness? or how teach the things concerning justice, yourself held by
injustice?

Let such a one then heal his own wound first, and then he will be able to
heal
the bruise of his brother, strengthening his soul with divine words; for the
wound of the soul, consoled by the holy words, is lifted. Exhort therefore all
men, those kindling with passion, if they wish, to marry in Christ (knowing
however that one was fashioned for one; but Lamech, being of the race of Cain,
first took for himself two wives; and to take for himself is to take not
according to the will of God.

For polygamy has the name of marriage, but is a person-seeking fornication);
but those exercising self-control, to train completely in Christ, and not to
philosophize in name, but to do the deed covered over. And do not again attend
to the eyes of men, that you are not seen, walls surrounding you on all sides;
but fear the threat of God, that all things are naked and laid open before his
eyes, the things done by men in night and day. And it comes to me to wonder
greatly, being astonished, at the insensibility of those who are
short-sighted, how the females of their own free judgment have given
themselves over into slavery, purchased at a price by the one who valued them.
For often a girl, not having served her own parents, and not having repaid
recompense of righteous labors to those who begat her, this one is found
earnest toward the beloved, and strives by every way to please him, and not to
serve God piously, laboring by night and day, and weaving and making garments
for him; and there are many other things, which we do not ignore, but do not
wish to say now; for it is not lawful to bring into the midst the things done
secretly by them, which are even shameful to speak. But he, like a fish
wrapped in an evil net, through the garments and the deadly service, done
according to man-pleasing and not according to piety, is enslaved to the
woman, making his mind drunk, and being wholly shaken, is loosened under the
feminine words, and neither knows any more the season of prayer, nor if he
bows to pray does he know what he does, mirroring to himself the character of
a harlot's disposition.

The like things also the woman suffers with the suffering one. And when the
Scripture is read, they are blind of mind; and when discourses are spoken in
the church, they do not know what they hear, enslaved to the shameful passion
of one another; so much does the erotic and mad passion dull the soul, as to
make them forget even their own names. So much does a woman dwelling with a
man turn aside the mind of man; so much does a man cohabiting lead the woman
to madness of desire =30.828=; so much does a woman dwelling with a man
prevail, as to produce forgetfulness of the remembrance of God. Let us cast
ourselves out from gehenna and eternal fire. Why do you bury yourselves alive
in Hades and fearful Tartarus? You who have the syneisaktoi, and dwell with
them, unlearn the evil lesson, the procurer of destruction, and treacherous to
salvation. Let us imitate the apostles; let us imitate the prophets, and walk
in their footsteps, and let not the lure of the woman deceive us; nor let us
again lure a woman ourselves through vainglory and voluntary humility; but let
all things be done by us soberly according to true and firm humility in
Christ, so that we may be able to be delivered from the present evil season,
according to the will of our Father, and Lord Jesus Christ, his Only-begotten
Son, through whom and with whom glory, might, honor to the invisible Father
with the holy and Comforting Spirit, now and unto all the ages of ages. Amen.
