* On Those Who Corrupt Virgins
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

A lament against those who corrupt the virgins of Christ.
=60.741=
What then, wretched and miserable men, guides of evil pleasures? How do
you wish to escape the hands of the living God? What will you do when he
who betrothed her comes to judge the living and the dead? And what
defense will you offer, having done such lawless things against the
brides of Christ? Have you shown such great contempt? Did you not
tremble? Did you not fear? Did you not shudder? Did you not spare your
own souls? Do you not know that there is no other lawlessness like this?
Do you not know that the virgins are a temple of God? Do you not know
that the Spirit of God was dwelling in them? Those who day by day
converse with Christ concerning the dowries that do not fail and remain
unto the ages, and whom Paul brought forward to present as pure virgins
to Christ, you have mixed together with mire and stench? O how did you
dare to corrupt the brides of Christ, procurers of evil desire? If
anyone dares to do such things against a fellow-servant maid, he does
not go away unpunished; but to arm yourselves thus against the brides of
Christ, of the Master of both them and you, do you then expect to escape
the judgment? Did you not hear the Apostle saying, Judgment is without
mercy to the one who has not done mercy? And what then is more merciless
than this? For with one sword you work two murders, killing both
yourselves and them; for this is death among those who think soundly.
O who would not groan? Who would not mourn? Who would not beat the
breast and wail over the untimely murder? Who would not weep over the
death before its time? Who would not feel pain at the unjust wound? The
dowry and the marriage-writings are kept for the lawful husband joined
in incorruption, the heavenly bridegroom Christ; and is virginity
betrayed to the adulterer? But someone will say: I was joined to Christ
by necessity; I was not of a mind to be joined to him, but to a
fellow-servant. Did you dare at all, wretched woman, to make a speech
against yourself, that even when you had no purpose toward him or love,
the Master desired you? Hear, O miserable one, and I will speak to you,
and be persuaded even from the affairs of the world. I mean those of
ordinary life: if one man snatches a girl in place of another, when
equal honor is being tried, the snatching is valid for the one who did
it, and law and king yield to the snatching. For he did not snatch and
take in order to insult, but in order to honor, and the dowry-gifts and
presents hold force. If then among men there is no change, since it
follows the purpose that went before, what will you answer, you who were
snatched by the Master beyond =60.742= hope? For I do not call that a
necessity through which you were counted worthy to be joined to the
Master, but the Master's dispensation and love for mankind; so that
under the form of necessity you, the unworthy, might gain the
incorruptible marriage that is above you.
Do not then say, I chose to live as a virgin by necessity. Do not be
ungrateful to the heavenly bridegroom, because he counted you worthy to
take you, without dowry and of no birth and a slave; for you are his
slave, even if you do not wish it. So I beg you, O virgin, to become
obedient without any contradiction and most shameful reasonings. And if
you have not yet set aside the spiritual marriage, keep the covenants
safely; but if you have transgressed and fallen, arise, arise, I beg
you. For the good Master who loves mankind stands by you; he who was
dishonored by you does not reject your dwelling with him; give him your
hand. For since you insulted the first right hand, at least do not set
this one aside. He does not disdain your wounds; for he heals the blow
of soul and body; for he is a compassionate physician. Uncover the
passion to him through confession, so that he may lay on invisibly the
saving medicine; for he did not come to call the righteous, but sinners
to repentance. He came to you who were lying down; arise then, take up
your bed, and go to your house, that is, to the harbor of repentance.
No longer look, either with the eyes of the flesh or with those of the
mind, upon that adulterer, the procurer of Gehenna and of the sleepless
worm. Take up, he says, take up from here; for I do not wish you to lie.
Tear the token of sin; wash with tears, not in the font, but in the
church; for God is able to grant you the first dignity. Do not teach the
potter how to set right his own vessel. Have you sinned? Be still; do
not say, And how is it possible for me to come to the first dignity? Do
not say this, O virgin; for it is God who justifies, and who is the one
who condemns? Hear how the Master rebukes despair through Jeremiah the
prophet, saying: Is there no resin in Gilead, or is there no physician
there? Why has the healing of the daughter of my people not gone up?
O the compassionate physician! He does not accuse for the fall, but for
the neglect; he did not say, Why were you struck? but, Why were you not
healed? He did not say, Why did you fall? but, Why did you not rise?
And that the Lord wishes to present you more splendid through
repentance, hear him saying through Isaiah the prophet: If your
sins =60.743= are as purple, I will whiten them as snow; and if they are
as scarlet, I will whiten them as wool. Nor would the Apostle have
suffered an untimely pain, if he had not first received the covenants of
the Master's goodness; he would not have said, My little children, of
whom I travail again until Christ is formed in you. For since, he says,
you betrayed the soul made according to the image of God to the
soul-destroying demon, making the members of Christ members of a harlot,
I do not cease mourning and being afflicted until I see Christ formed in
you again, you clothed with the beauty of chastity. Do not throw away
the prize of repentance, I beg, I ask, and I entreat; be manly, be
purified, anoint yourself spiritually; the Apostle multiplies the
exhortation: I am an ambassador for Christ; be reconciled to God. The
Apostle begs, so that he may call back again her who was betrothed to
Christ and loosed the covenants. The Master who loves mankind says: I
bear no grudge for the insult that was done; my bed has been insulted, my
union has been dishonored; but I accuse none of the things that have
come to pass, which you have done.
And why, when the adversary was making war, did you not stand against
him? How did you not put to flight his venomous arrows by the sign of my
cross? Did I not for this dwell in a virgin womb, that the nature long
ago dishonored through deceit might be honored through virginity? For
the enemy took Eve captive while she was a virgin, and through virginity
I brought her into paradise again. Did you not hear me saying that there
is no one who has left father or mother or house, and denies all the
things of life, who shall not receive a hundredfold and inherit eternal
life? Did you not hear me saying through Isaiah the prophet: Those who
serve me shall rejoice? Did I not through that same servant of mine,
Isaiah, promise those who keep the body in chastity that they would
inherit many goods, and fruits better than sons and daughters? Did he
not take hold of my head through virginity? Was not John the son of
Zebedee called by me, through virginity, a son of thunder, and a son of
my mother? How did you not hear my apostle Paul saying, Make no
provision for the flesh? Yes, Lord, she says; but have pity on your
unworthy handmaid, and do not convict me in your wrath, nor chasten me
in your anger. For the enemy who is the first of evil and a murderer of
men hurled a venomous arrow at me, and it ran about my heart and my
kidneys and the rest of the senses, and I forgot your fear and your just
recompense; for the fire that arose within darkened the eyes of my mind.
Yet I am without excuse, Master, because I did not call upon you then
for my help; but I have sinned, Lord, I have sinned, having abandoned
you, and having served a passion of shame and dishonor. Then he himself
will say to you: It would have been well for you to remember, lest those
who demand your soul from you be sent in haste; and where then is the
confession and the repentance? For this is why I say, Watch, because you
do not know the day nor the hour. Yet from now on sin no longer, nor,
leaving me, become an adulteress. And we too therefore beg, Be
reconciled to God, and say thus, O virgin: I will wash my hands in
innocence, and I will compass your altar, O Lord, because you raised
her who was lying down, you made alive her who had been put to death,
you made bright her who was soiled, you drew =60.744= up her who was
sunk in sin with the hook of piety, you made your own her who had gone
astray, you bought the captive, you did not take vengeance on her who
insulted, you called back her who ran away, you washed her who was
stained with blood, you snatched away her who was hunted by pleasure,
you made her who was worthy of Tartarus a citizen of heaven, you freed
her from Gehenna, you redeemed her from the sword, you loosed her from
the bonds, you counted her worthy of everlasting life, you snatched her
from the danger, you put the devil to shame, you tore the handwriting.
O tiller of good tears, what is more splendid than these wonders?
Come together then, as many of you as have fallen into such a danger of
shipwreck; know, be taught, be disciplined, that the fruit of sin is
death, but the fruit of the Spirit is life and peace and
sanctification. As many as now and always wish to escape this death,
take all that is said as a ground of salvation; and always study this,
all of you, and especially the monks, and you virgins who have dedicated
your bodies to the Lord: Walk by the Spirit, and you shall not fulfill
the desire of the flesh. For the desire of the flesh has sprouted the
fruit of sin, death; but the spiritual fruit is eternal life and
incorruption and sanctification and peace, in soul and in body.
Therefore let this be known to you all, that no virtue is more honorable
than virginity among every plant of virtue, nor is anything like it
found, if that monk, or the virgin, does not commit fornication in the
mind; for the consent of the thoughts has completed the whole deed. For
he who said it does not lie: Everyone who looks at a woman to desire her
has already committed adultery with her in his heart. And there is
nothing set apart from the male and the female; for we are all of the
same lump, as the Gospel says: it names the will of a man, and the will
of the flesh, having said that they are one and the same throughout.
Therefore the virgin of Christ must also keep her whole self blameless
and incorrupt, and her heart toward her bridegroom and maker Christ, and
with the eyes of the mind as seeing him who is fair in beauty above the
sons of men, turn away every idle word that is not for building up, and
not keep company fearlessly with every male, and especially with those
who walk disorderly.
But if there is some assurance concerning the benefit of the soul, one
must not refuse men who walk becomingly. One must not be bold, nor
linger, nor laugh together with unseemly women; and let no oath at all
come out of the mouth; for the Master's command is that he does not even
suffer you to swear by your own head, saying that whatever is more than
Yes and No is of the evil one; and to drive away the filthy and unseemly
thoughts, and all the trials of the flesh, by the fear and the longing
of Christ and by the sign of his cross.
See, fathers and mothers and brothers and sisters and all children, I
have set before you the things that are profitable, I mean for the monks
and the virgins. Keep these things, and you shall not stumble, but you
too shall be counted worthy to be shown as heirs of Christ; and of all
the goods that have been prepared the Father of mercies and God of all
consolation shall show you worthy and heirs, by the grace and mercies
and love for mankind of our Lord Jesus Christ, with whom to the Father
belongs glory, honor, and worship, together with the all-holy Spirit,
now and always, and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
