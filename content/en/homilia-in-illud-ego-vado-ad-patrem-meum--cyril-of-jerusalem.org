* Homily on "I go to my Father"
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of Cyril, bishop of Jerusalem, on the [text] "I go to my Father; and whatever
you ask in my name, this I will do, that the Father may be glorified in the
Son."
On both the things said concerning the Godhead and concerning the humanity he
places the "I," so that the sense of the words appears from the sequence, and
the discernment of the natures from the difference of the things said. And
from his discoursing in both as concerning some one, he signifies the
conjunction of the person.
