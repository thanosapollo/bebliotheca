* Two Fragments on Numbers
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

You shall take away a heave-offering from it. Those called to priesthood
according to the law of Moses received tithes from the sons of Israel, as a
portion and lot of God. And they themselves brought the tenth to the one who
led and presided over the priesthood, that is to Aaron; who has also been set
as a type of Christ, not ministering in the first tent that has standing, but
in the inner and hidden one. Take the rod and assemble the congregation.
Observe that confirming unto faith, he brings it to a reminder of the
wonder-working in Egypt, saying: Take the rod in your hand with which you
struck the river. And shortly: Christ is likened to a rock because of the
unbreakable and unshaken.
