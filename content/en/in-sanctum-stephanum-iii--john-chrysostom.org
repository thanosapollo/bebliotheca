On Saint Stephen, III.
=63.933=
Many and wondrous are the trophies of the blessed Stephen, and not even death
tore him away from the pious battle-line. For while living he fought against
the Jews, and even having died he is armed against heretics, having an
unconquerable weapon, the faith of the Trinity. And look, I pray you, at the
phalanxes of the adversaries then, and be astonished at the soldier's courage.
Alexandrians come against him, and Libertines were shooting arrows, Cilicians
and Asians were laying siege, they dragged him into the council, they hired
false witnesses, they made ready showers of stones, like lions they gnashed
their teeth against him; yet the soldier, armed with wisdom and the Spirit,
was victorious, flashing upon those who looked with his appearance, and
unnerving those who fight against God with his words.
But did you see the companies of your enemies? Look again, I pray you, also at
the alliance from God: he spread open the vaults of heaven, waving before the
athlete the prizes of the awards; he beautified the face with an angelic form,
he beat back showers of stones with showers of words; and some were casting,
and others were bearing, and they were handing the crown to Stephen. Look
therefore even now, and you will see the kinship of the trophies. Then he
pleaded with the voices of the cross, and now Stephen is the crown of the
Church; for he first trampled the stings of death. A crown in palaces; for the
queen and Virgin has taken him into the bridal chamber; a crown among rulers;
for he has made the child of the one who received him as a guest the father of
a king; a crown in love of the poor; for he became a rich man of widows and
orphans and a faithful steward; a crown against the Jews; for he pilloried
=63.934= the synagogue that had committed adultery with idols; a crown against
heretics; for he stops the mouths of those who fight against God, crying,
Great is our Lord.
Great; for he is not subject to the measure of the tongue; great; for he
surpasses the imagination of the mind; great; for the uncircumscribed is
proper to the divine nature; great; for from the paternal bosom he rose
without beginning; great; for as Word he was begotten without passion; great;
for the divine birth-pang did not divide the essence; great; for he sits upon
the throne, he has not been cast to the footstool; great; for as is the root,
such also is the branch; great; for he who has seen the Son has seen the
Father; great; for there is nothing unequal in the Trinity; great; for through
water he has begotten many again; great; for though seen he does not grow old;
great; for he did not have a small Father. Great therefore is the Lord the
Son, even if it does not seem so to the neighbors. Almighty is the Son; for
all things came to be through him; the Son is a king without beginning; for
our king is before the age; the Son is consubstantial with the Father; for I
came forth from God and I have come; the Son is true light; for I am the light
of the world.
Why do I say many things? God and Almighty, the Son of God who was incarnate
from a Virgin, and who shepherds the Church, as the prophet bears witness
saying: Let the Lord God Almighty visit his flock; to whom be the glory and
the power, with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and always, and unto the
ages of ages. Amen.
