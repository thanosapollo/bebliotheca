OF THE SAME, FROM THE DISCOURSE ON PASCHA 15
Concerning the fast of Wednesday and Friday.
No one will accuse us for observing Wednesday and Friday, on which we have
also reasonably been commanded to fast according to tradition; Wednesday,
because of the council held by the Jews for the betrayal of the Lord; and
Friday, because he suffered for us. For we keep the Lord's day as a day of joy
because of him who rose on it, on which we have also received the tradition
not even to bend the knee.
