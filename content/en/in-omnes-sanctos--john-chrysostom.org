* On All the Saints

An encomium of John Chrysostom, archbishop of Constantinople, on all the
saints.
Come today, brothers, and let us keep the memory of the holy martyrs with a
bright festival. Come, let us share in that heavenly grace itself. For the
martyrs are a defense for the betrayal in paradise, propitiations for fallen
nature, gifts of the humanity that struck against God, patterns for the race
that was shaken, champions against the robber-chief's breaking-in, pledges of
the hope of the faithful, God-written tablets of endurance, hidden armories of
Christianity, secret bows of him who was crucified first, mighty arms of
piety, trees more blossoming after the cut, roots more flourishing after they
are destroyed, nets buried for the demons. Sparrows walking the fields are not
so caught in buried snares as demons, grazing among the tribes of the holy
martyrs, are scorched by the buried ash and suffer something like Cain. For
they shudder at those they slaughtered, as he did at Abel once slain.
Since there are also demons in this air, barbarian and wild, always raising
wars and enemies of peace, he set the camps of the angels against them, so
that by appearing alone they might put them down, so that they might always
procure peace for us. And that you may learn that they are angels of peace,
hear the deacons always saying in the prayers: Ask for the angel of peace. You
see that both angels are present and martyrs .... of this festival of all the
saints. The martyrs gain a wealth that procures immortality. They obtained
that glory whose kingdom is eternal, having laid up the whole treasure of life
in the heavens. For such is the victory of the saints that they even chose
death for the sake of immortal goods.
Their flesh was scraped by the tortures, but the aim of the soul remained
without pain. Their limbs were cut by the swords, but the longing toward
heaven was not cut .... The teeth of beasts tore their entrails, but they did
not tear away the pious reasoning of the heart. Truly the bodies are shield
and breastplate for the martyrs' souls; for these alone are wounded by the
tyrants' arrows. For the good soldier of the faith, persuading his members to
receive arrows, offers the body naked to the war and works a victory in the
very things in which he is thought to be defeated. For the devil has been
conquered by having tortured the martyrs' bodies. And the proof of this is the
power of the relics, putting demons to flight by an invisible blow. These
trophies glorify =304= the martyrs. These showed that those who willed to die
in this way have lived immortally. These the armies of angels call blessed;
these creation holds everywhere as pledges of salvation.
For henceforth every city and country of the inhabited world finds itself
holding the martyrs as patrons. For as much as we run to them with pious
honors, so much do we find them guardians in all things. Honor to a martyr is
uprightness of life, a good conscience toward God. For the martyrs are our
teachers of piety and our fellow citizens, having Christ as guide. Thanks be
to the Master who loves mankind, who has given even to the earth the
treasures, all the saints. For in it have been laid the treasures of the
heavens, the precious pearls, the proven gold, the chosen vessels. For these
are the relics of the martyrs, and for a while heaven has yielded to earth,
granting that the life of men be blessed. For there is the longed-for dwelling
of the martyrs, there the much-desired rest from the contests, there the
costly crowns of victory. And these things heaven has been commanded to keep,
waiting there for their enjoyment; but for a while the earth holds them. And
heaven also .... was received of brightness. It had the stars as lamps of
fire; the earth had a multitude of saints, as that one has angels giving
glory. These blessed martyrs, punished for Christ, racked, given over to
beasts, consumed by fire and sword, are now escorted, honored, glorified; they
reign in the heavens, they entreat God, they help sinners, they guard the
inhabited world, they sanctify cities, they people deserts, they bless the
countries, they hold peoples together, they turn barbarians back .... being
portioned among relics. And this is clear from the ark laid down today, which
has the manna, an ark that conquers foreigners, an ark more secure than
Noah's. For that one was saved as it swam upon the waters, but this one, borne
in death itself, redeems. For this reason it is also buried in the bosom of
the earth, giving life even to those men being sunk in Hades. Such is our
treasure today, such wealth the house of the martyrs has received, such grace
the place has acquired.
Here, then, the myrrh of holiness springs forth; here the streams of the
healing of the passions make their way; from here the channels of healings
flow; from here a hungry city is gladdened; from here thirsty flocks are
filled; from here a people running together is sanctified; from here Christ is
moved with compassion as he is glorified. Consider how many have also bound on
the crown of =305= martyrdom. For some were scourged, some were shut in
prison, some were bound with chains, some fell from their fatherland, some
lost their property, some were removed to exile, some were slaughtered: some
in the ordeal itself, some also in their resolve. For though spears were bared
and swords sharpened and threats came every day, and those in office breathed
fury, and voices were stretched against them, and countless kinds of
punishments and penalties, they did not yield or give way, but stood unmoved
upon the rock, choosing to do and suffer all things so as not to share in the
lawlessness of those who had dared such things: not men only, but women also.
For women too stripped for this contest, and in many places showed themselves
more manly than men. And not women only, but also youths and quite young boys,
of whom may it be .... to whom be the glory with the Father and the Holy
Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages. Amen.
=338= I am rightly in anguish, held by the bonds of cowardice: how shall I
fill the thirst of your love of hearing? What table of the word shall I set
out? What shall I become as I receive so great a people, for whom Christ alone
will suffice for the feast? The joy of the world is at the doors at last; the
harbor of the Master's resurrection draws near. Again the unreasoning nation
of the Jews is in uproar =339=, again the dogs bark against the shepherd,
again the slaves revolt against the Master, again the workers of darkness plot
against the light, again Judas rehearses the lawless sale, again the Jews
prepare silver, that they may send down to Hades him who is free among the
dead and may learn that death is taken captive, that tombs may be shaken and
the dead take wing toward rising; again a disciple sells, and those learned in
the law buy, and a robber on a cross confesses God. But let us remember the
traitor's voice: "What will you give me," he says, "and I will hand him over
to you?" What do they want to give [you], Judas? The rope of the noose, the
outer darkness, alienation from the kingdom, separation from the saints, a
fall from which there is no rising: these are your profits from the bargain
with the Jews. ["What will you give me?"] What can they give you that is such
as what you are about to lose, you utterly wicked man? And why do you sell so
cheaply the architect of heaven and earth, who bears all things in his palm?
When you saw your Master being honored, you saw the costly myrrh poured upon
his fearful head, and condemning the woman's lavish gift you cried: "To what
purpose is this waste?" "Why was this myrrh not sold for three hundred denarii
and given to the poor?" And the myrrh compounded of various kinds and prepared
by some art of the perfumer you valued at three hundred denarii, but the true
myrrh, which lets the dead catch the scent of the resurrection, you sell for
thirty denarii? You do not know what you are selling, Judas. The drunkenness
of love of money has darkened you. When did you ever know a master sold by a
slave? When did you ever learn to sell light and buy darkness? And why did you
insult the token of love? And why do you work the betrayal with a kiss? Well
did the prophet, posting you as a public shame from afar, cry out =340=: "His
mouth is full of cursing and bitterness and deceit; under his tongue is toil
and pain." "You have worked deceit like a sharpened razor." And you were
kissing your Master. Come, kiss him. The Savior bears it with long-suffering,
not turning away from the stench of your kiss, but calling back the grace of
the debt which he gave you from your own lips.
O how terrible is the love of money. Through it the disciple, torn from the
apostolic chain, inherits the rope of strangling and shows his neck worthy of
the noose, the neck through which the words of the betrayal came forth. O how
great a good is sobriety. For through it the robber leapt from the cross into
paradise. For when he saw the Master hanging in the midst, and all creation
shaken with fear, and the sun veiled in a hard mourning, and the light of day
fleeing away, the elements denying their order, the rocks splitting: when he
had seen all this, then reading his own conscience and not ignorant of a
fellow robber's deeds, when he saw creation carried this way and that and
trembling, he recognized the Master in the midst of those that trembled, and
recognizing him he cried out at once: "Remember me, Lord, when you come in
your kingdom." Truly swift is the robber, the worker of the eleventh hour. For
he completed the whole day's work of life, and in the hour he was hired he
worked eagerly. He did not falter in faith, he did not look away at the
insults, he did not glance aside at the blows, he parried the Jews' taunts, he
took creation as teacher, and recognizing the Master of creation he gave a
worthy worship.
What then does Christ, the payer of wages, the one lavish in benefits, do in
answer to this? The robber asked for the future; he himself showed the grace
without delay. For when that one had said, "Remember me, Lord, when you come
in your kingdom," the Master answered at once, saying: "Amen, I say to you,
today you shall be with me in =341= paradise." True is the Scripture that
says: "While you are still speaking I will say: Behold, I am here." To Christ
himself be the glory with the Father and the Holy Spirit unto the ages of
ages. Amen.
