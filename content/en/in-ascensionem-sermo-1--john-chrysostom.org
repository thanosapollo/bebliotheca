On the Ascension of our Lord Jesus Christ. Homily 1.
=52.791=
Three paradoxical wonders unknown from the beginning of time, overshooting
our very nature itself, have been woven together, remaining unbreakable and
unshaken; for a threefold cord is not quickly broken. These are the
birth-pangs of an unwedded mother, the resurrection of a three-day passion,
the taking-up of flesh into the heavens. Time has known barren women giving
birth, but not apart from marital union; it has known the dead coming back to
life, but not into endless life; it has known a prophet being taken up, but
as if into heaven; the "as" is second to the truth. For Elijah changed from
place to place, but the Savior went up whence he came down, many men and
women looking on, until the gaze straining toward the height grew faint,
angels bearing witness to the =52.792= spectacle, and announcing the second
coming, And behold, it says, two men stood by them in white apparel, who also
said: Men of Galilee, why do you stand looking into heaven? and the rest. O
how many things the Good One transacted for salvation through wicked
servants! he came down from the heavens, he went up into the heavens, from
the heavens he will come again; and how will he come? Not dissolved into
another hypostasis, but in the form of the flesh, in the human shape; yet not
doing lowly things, as before, not sleeping on a boat, not held at a well by
the toil of thirst, not sitting on a colt of an ass, but riding upon a
cloudlike colt; not taking fishermen with him, but escorted by angels as a
bodyguard; not standing at a judge's tribunal, but himself judging the ends
of the inhabited world, and having granted to those very fishermen judicial
=52.793= authority; as he himself also teaches, saying to the disciples: When
the Son of man shall sit upon the throne of his glory, you also shall sit
upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel; and to the Jews:
But if I by Beelzebul cast out the demons, by whom do your sons cast them
out? therefore they shall be your judges; because, being from the Jews, they
did not think the things of the Jews, but preferred the sufferings of Christ
and the marks of their kinsmen who killed the Lord.
A great wonder, a strange court, to see a fisherman judging, and a Pharisee
being judged, a tentmaker sitting with boldness, and a high priest being
called to account with groanings. For why did they strike the Master on the
cheek, punch him on the head, and at the last kill the heir outside the
vineyard? And why did they imprison Peter? And why did they torture Paul five
times with forty stripes save one? But the wondrous high priest Ananias
ordered the servants to strike the mouth of Paul, the mouth of Paul, the
mouth of grace, the mouth of truth, the plectrum of life, more noble than
every trumpet, more tuneful than every harmony, the instrument of Christ, the
pipe of the Spirit; for the things that seemed good to the Holy Spirit Paul
published through his tongue; whence I think David speaks in the person of
Paul: My tongue is the pen of a swift-writing scribe. What then does Paul say
to Ananias, foreseeing the judgment coming upon him? God shall smite you, you
whitewashed wall. For a whitewashed wall is a beautified tomb, an enemy
without, a corpse within.
Since therefore an accounting of the things lived is set before us, and a
world-wide tribunal, demanding the working of the talents, the rejection of
the mystical marriage, the lamp-bearing of the bridechamber, the increase of
the seed, the =52.794= fruit of the vineyard, and as many things as he
revealed to us through the parables, let us weigh our life, let us transact
the things of life, not money-changing, nor mining gold, nor lending heavily;
for these things even the judge turns away from; but feeding the poor,
showing hospitality, calling upon God, and as many things as know how to
soften the judge's wrath. Learn the gain of hospitality, looking to Abraham,
reckoning the benefit of the Ninevites' supplication, having seen the
treasure of almsgiving, Tabitha lying lifeless on a bed, about to be buried
in a tomb; but the widows, circling Peter and displaying the gifts of the
dead woman, drew the deceased up from Hades.
And if the tears of widows animated a dead body, which was clearly going to
die again, what sort of things do you think the judge will grant in the
recompenses of good things to those who have practiced feeding the poor with
an unsparing right hand, and have not broken off the testimony of the poor?
For then the gift is inalienable, death no longer mediating, but a continuous
life stretched out. For the trumpet shall sound, it says, and the dead in
Christ shall rise incorruptible and unchangeable, after the imitation of the
Master. And just as a cloud received him, so also we, according to the voice
of Paul, shall be caught up in clouds. And as he himself went up with a shout
and the voice of a trumpet, in the same manner we also, through the
archangelic trumpet, having shaken off the cloud of death as sleep, shall be
with the pioneer of life; And so, it says, we shall always be with the Lord,
minding the things of the Lord, having kept his commandments. And may it be
that we all be counted worthy of that blessed voice: Well done, good and
faithful servant; you have been faithful over a few things, I will set you
over many; enter into the joy of the Lord. His is the glory and the might,
unto the ages of ages. Amen.
