** Preface

Through ten five-year spans already,
unless I am mistaken, we have been; a seventh
pivot besides turns the year, while we enjoy the rolling sun.
The bound is at hand, and God already applies
the day neighboring old age.
What useful thing have we done in so great a space of time?
The first age wept
under the rattling rods. Soon the toga taught
one stained with vices to speak false things, not without crime.
Then wanton boldness
and petulant luxury (alas, it shames and irks!)
defiled the young man with the filths and mud of wickedness.
Thereafter disputes armed
turbid spirits, and the badly stubborn
eagerness to win lay under harsh chances.
Twice by the governance of the laws
we held the reins of noble cities,
we restored civil right to the good, we terrified the guilty.
At last piety of the prince raised
one advanced in the grade of service,
bidding him, taken nearer, to stand in the next rank.

While flying life does these things,
suddenly grayness crept upon the old man,
convicting me of having forgotten the old consul Salia,
under whom my first day,
how many winters it has rolled, and how many roses
it has given back to the meadows after ice, the snow of the head proves.
Will such things, whether good or evil,
profit after the death of the flesh
when already death has abolished whatever it is that I had been?
I must say: 'Whoever you are,
your mind has lost the world it cultivated.
Those things it studied are not of God, whose you will be held.'
And yet under the last end
let the sinning soul put off folly:
at least let it celebrate God with the voice, if it cannot by merits,
let it continue the days with hymns,
nor let any night be empty of singing the Lord;
let it fight against heresies, let it set forth the catholic faith,
let it trample the rites of the nations,
let it bring ruin, Rome, upon your idols,
let it devote song to the martyrs, let it praise the apostles,
while I write or speak these things,
O that I might flash forth free from the chains of the body,
whither a mobile tongue shall have carried with its last sound!
