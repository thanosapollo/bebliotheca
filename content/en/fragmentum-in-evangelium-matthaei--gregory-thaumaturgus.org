=10.1189= The lamp of the body is the eye.
If therefore your eye is simple, your whole body will be light. But if your
eye is evil, your whole body will be dark. And if the light that is in you is
darkness, how great is the darkness? A simple eye is unhypocritical love,
through which, when the body is lighted, it also brings forth through the
outer parts the same things as those thought within; and the evil eye is
pretended love, which is also called hypocrisy; through which the whole body
of the man is darkened. Thinking within things worthy of darkness; but through
the outer parts bringing forth words that seem to be of light; for they are
truly wolves, having put on skins of sheep; who wash only the outside of the
cup and the dish, and do not know that if the inside of these is not cleansed,
neither can the outside become clean. Therefore clearly convicting these, the
Savior says that: If the light that is in you is darkness, how great is the
darkness? That is: if the love that seems to you to be light is, because of
the hypocrisy hidden in you, a work worthy of darkness, what are your obvious
trespasses?
