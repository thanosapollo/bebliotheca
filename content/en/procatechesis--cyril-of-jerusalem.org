Of our father among the saints Cyril, Archbishop of Jerusalem, the Catecheses.
Procatechesis, that is, a prologue of the Catecheses of our father among the
saints Cyril, Archbishop of Jerusalem. 1 Already a scent of blessedness is
toward you, O YOU WHO ARE BEING ILLUMINATED; already you gather the
intelligible flowers toward a weaving of heavenly crowns; already the
fragrance of the Holy Spirit has blown. Already you have come about the
forecourt of the palaces; and may it be that you also be led in by the king.
For now the flowers of the trees have appeared; and may it be that the fruit
also [may be perfect]. An enrollment of names has so far been made for you,
and a calling of a campaign; and lamps of a bridal escort, and a desire of the
heavenly citizenship, and a good purpose, and a hope that follows; for he who
said is without falsehood, that to those who love God all things work together
for good. For God is lavish toward benefaction; but he waits for the genuine
choice of each. For this reason the Apostle added saying, to those who are
called according to purpose; the purpose, being genuine, makes you called; for
even if you have the body here, but do not have the mind, you profit nothing.
2 Simon the magician also once came forward to the bath; he was baptized, but
he was not illuminated; and he dipped the body with water, but he did not
illuminate the heart with Spirit; and the body went down, and came up; but the
soul was not buried together with Christ, nor raised together. And I tell the
records of the falls, that you may not fall in. For these things happened
typically to those, but they have been written toward admonition of those
coming forward until today. Let none of you be found tempting the grace; let
no root of bitterness springing up trouble; let none of you enter saying: Let
me see what the faithful do; having entered let me see, that I may learn the
things that are done. You expect to see, but you do not expect to be seen? and
you think that you indeed busy yourself about the things that happen, but God
does not busy himself about your heart? 3 Someone once busied himself about
the wedding in the Gospels; and taking an unworthy garment he entered, and
reclined, and ate; for the bridegroom permitted it. But he ought, having seen
the white-clad state of all, himself also to have taken up such a garment. But
he partook of equal foods; yet he had inequality of forms and of choice. But
the bridegroom, even if lavish, is not without judgment; and going around each
of those reclining and looking, [for it was a care to him, not how they should
eat, but how they should be seemly] seeing a certain alien, not having a
wedding garment, he was saying to him: Friend, how did you enter here? with
what color? with what conscience? Be it so, the doorkeeper did not hinder,
because of the lavishness of the one providing; be it so, you had ignorance of
what sort of form one must enter into the banquet; you entered, you saw the
forms of those reclining flashing as it were; ought you not even from the
things appearing to have been taught? ought you not to have gone out in good
season, that you might also enter in good season? But now you entered
unseasonably, that you might be cast out unseasonably. And he commands the
servants: Bind his feet, the ones that boldly burst in; bind his hands, that
did not know how to put on a bright garment; and cast him out into the outer
darkness; for he is unworthy of bridal lamps. See what happened to that one
then; make secure the things of yourself. 4 For we, the ministers of Christ,
have received each one, and holding as it were the rank of doorkeepers, we
have left the door slack. But it is possible for you, having the soul bemired
with sins, and the choice stained, to enter. You entered; you were deemed
worthy; your name was enrolled. Do you see this august constitution of the
church? do you behold order and knowledge? a reading of scriptures, a presence
of the canonical, a sequence of teaching? Be put to shame even by the place,
and be instructed from the things appearing; go out in good season now, and
enter tomorrow most seasonably. If you have a money-loving form of the soul,
having put on another enter; [put off the form which you had, do not cover it
over] put off for me fornication and uncleanness, and put on for me the most
brilliant robe of temperance. I give charge, before Jesus the bridegroom of
souls enters, and sees the forms. The appointed time is much for you; you have
repentance of forty days; you have much good season both to put off and to
wash off, and to put on and to enter. But if you persist in a bad choice, the
one speaking is without blame, but do not expect that you will receive the
grace; [for] the water will receive you, but the spirit will not receive you.
If anyone is conscious to himself of the wound, let him take the plaster; if
anyone has fallen, let him be raised; let there be no Simon among you, nor any
hypocrisy, nor curiosity of the matter. 5 It is possible for you to come also
on another pretext; for it is possible even for a man to wish to entreat a
woman, and for this reason to come forward; the word turns likewise also upon
women; and a slave often has wished to please a master, and a friend a friend.
I receive the bait of the hook, and I accept you, having come with a bad
choice, but to be saved with a good hope. Perhaps you did not know where you
come, nor of what sort is the net that takes you; you have come inside
ecclesiastical nets; be caught alive; do not flee; for Jesus angles you, not
that he may put to death, but that having put to death he may make alive; for
you must die and rise; for you heard the Apostle saying: Dead to sin, but
living to righteousness; die to the sins, and live to righteousness; from
today live. 6 See for me how great a dignity Jesus grants [to you]. You were
being called a catechumen, being sounded around from without; hearing hope,
and not knowing; hearing mysteries, and not understanding; hearing scriptures,
and not knowing the depth. You are no longer sounded around, but sounded in;
for the indwelling Spirit henceforth works your mind into divine houses. When
you hear the things written concerning the mysteries, then you will understand
what you did not know. And do not think that you receive a small matter; being
a pitiable man, you receive a title of God. Hear Paul saying: God is faithful;
hear another scripture saying: God faithful and just. The Psalmist, foreseeing
this, was saying from the person of God, since men are about to receive a
title of God: I said, you are gods, and all sons of the Most High; but see,
lest the title be of a faithful one, but the choice of an unfaithful. You have
entered into a contest, labor the course; you do not have another such time.
If days of a wedding were set before you, would you not have despised all
things, and have been about the preparation of the feast? and being about to
consecrate the soul to the heavenly bridegroom, will you not be idle from
bodily things, that you may take up spiritual things? 7 It is not possible to
take the bath twice and thrice; since it would be possible to say: Having
failed once, I succeed a second time; but if you fail the once, the matter is
without correction. For one Lord, and one faith, and one baptism; for only
certain heretics are rebaptized; since the former was not a baptism. 8 For God
seeks nothing else from us, except a good choice; do not say: How are my sins
wiped out? I tell you, by the willing, by the believing; what is more concise
than this? But if your lips say the willing, but the heart does not say, the
knower of hearts is the one who judges. Be idle from today from every
worthless deed; let your tongue not speak unseemly words; let your looking not
sin, nor wander about the things not useful. 9 And let your feet hasten to the
catecheses. Receive the exorcisms with eagerness; even if you are blown upon,
even if you are exorcized, the matter is salvation for you. Suppose there to
be crude gold, and adulterated, mixed with various materials, with bronze, and
tin, and iron, and lead; we seek to have the gold alone; gold is not able
without fire to be purified of the alien things; so without exorcisms a soul
is not able to be purified; and they are divine, collected from divine
scriptures; your face has been covered, that the mind thereafter may be at
leisure; that the looking wandering may not make the heart also wander. And
with the eyes covered, the ears are not hindered from receiving the saving
thing. For in the way that those experienced in the goldsmith's work,
inserting the breath to the fire through certain fine instruments, and blowing
the gold hidden in the crucible, stirring the flame that lies beside, find the
thing sought; so of those who exorcize, inserting the fear through divine
Spirit, and as in a crucible, in the body, rekindling the soul; the enemy
demon flees, but the salvation remains, and the hope of the eternal life
remains, and thereafter the soul having been purified of the sins has the
salvation. Let us remain then in the hope, brethren, and let us give
ourselves, and let us hope; that the God of all, having seen our choice, may
purify us from the sins, and may furnish us good hopes of the matters, and may
give us repentance of salvation. God called, and you were called. 10 Remain by
the catecheses; even if we stretch out saying many things, let your mind never
be dissolved; for you receive weapons against an opposing energy; you receive
weapons against heresies, against Jews, and Samaritans, and Gentiles; you have
many enemies, take many darts; for you hurl against many; and there is need
for you to learn how you may shoot down the Hellene, how you may contend
against a heretic, against a Jew and a Samaritan; and the weapons are ready,
and the sword of the Spirit most ready; but one must also stretch out right
hands through a good choice, that you may war a war of the Lord, that you may
conquer opposing energies, that you may become unconquered to every heretical
matter. 11 And let this also be a charge to you: learn the things said, and
keep them unto the age. Do not think them to be the customary discourses; for
those too are good, and worthy of faith; but if we neglect today, we learn
tomorrow; but the teachings concerning the bath of regeneration handed over in
sequence, if they are neglected today, when will they be set right? Suppose
[for me] it is a season of plantings of trees; if we do not dig and go deep,
when else is that which has once been planted badly able to be planted well?
Suppose for me the catechesis is a building; [if we do not go deep, and set a
foundation,] if we do not in sequence join the house with bonds of building,
that something hollow may not be found, and the building become rotten, there
is no profit even of the former labor; but one must in sequence have stone
follow stone, and corner follow corner; and as [we] scrape off the superfluous
things, thus a perfect building to go up; so we bring to you stones as it were
of knowledge; one must hear the things concerning the living God; one must
hear the things concerning judgment; one must hear the things concerning
Christ; one must hear the things concerning resurrection; and there are many
things said in sequence, now spoken scatteredly, but then brought according to
harmony; but if you do not join them in the one, and remember the first and
the second, the one building builds, but you will have the building rotten. 12
When therefore the catechesis is being said, if a catechumen examines you,
what the teachers have said, say nothing to the one outside; for we hand over
a mystery to you, and a hope of a coming age; keep the mystery for the
rewarder. Let not anyone ever say to you: what are you harmed, if I also
learn? And those who are sick seek the wine; but if it is given unseasonably,
it works frenzy; and two evils happen, both the sick one perishes, and the
physician is slandered; so the catechumen, if he hears from a faithful one;
both the catechumen is frantic, for he does not know what he heard, and he
convicts the matter, and sneers at the thing said; and the faithful one is
condemned as a traitor. And already you stand on a boundary, see for me that
you do not speak out; not because the things said are not worthy of speech,
but because the hearing is unworthy to receive; you too were once a
catechumen, I did not narrate to you the things set forth; when by experience
you take the height of the things taught, then you would know, that the
catechumens are unworthy of the hearing. 13 You have become sons and daughters
of one mother, you who have been enrolled; when you enter before the hour of
the exorcisms, let each one of you speak the things toward piety; and if
anyone of you is not present, seek him out. If you were called to a table,
would you not have waited for the one called together? if you had a brother,
would you not have sought the good for the brother? Do not busy yourself
thereafter about unprofitableness of matters; not, what did a city do? not,
what did a village do? not, what did a king do? not, what did a bishop do?
not, what did a presbyter do? look up; your season has need of that. Be at
leisure and know, that I am God. If you see the faithful serving, and not
being anxious; they have the secure thing, they know what they received, they
hold the grace; but you still stand in a balance, either to be received, or
not to be received; do not imitate those who have the freedom from care, but
pursue the fear. 14 And when an exorcism happens, until the other ones being
exorcized arrive, men with men, and women with women. For now I have need of
the ark of Noah; that Noah may be apart and his sons; and the wife, and the
wives of his sons. For even if the ark was one, and the door was shut; yet the
matters had been arranged in form. Even if the church has been shut, and you
are all within; yet let the matters be distinguished, men with men, and women
with women; let not the occasion of salvation become a pretext of destruction;
even if the occasion is good, to sit near one another, yet let the passions be
far. Then the men sitting, let them also have a useful book; and let one read,
and let another hear; and if a book is not present, let one pray, and let
another speak something useful. And again let the virginal gathering be
collected thus, either singing or reading quietly; so that the lips speak, but
the alien ears do not hear. For I do not permit a woman to speak in church;
and let the married woman [too] likewise imitate; and let her pray, and let
the lips be moved, but let a voice not be heard; that Samuel may pass by, that
your barren soul may bear the salvation, God having hearkened. For Samuel has
this interpretation. 15 I shall see the eagerness of each man, I shall see the
reverence of each woman. Let the mind be fired toward reverence, let the soul
be forged, let the hardness of unbelief be hammered, let the superfluous
scales of the iron fall away, let the pure remain; let the rust of the iron
fall away, but let the genuine remain. Sometime may God show you that night,
the darkness that appears as day, concerning which it has been said: Darkness
shall not be darkened from you, and night as the day shall be illuminated;
then may a door of paradise be opened for each of you men and each of you
women. Then may you enjoy Christ-bearing waters, having fragrance. Then may
you receive a title of Christ, and an energy of divine matters. Already lift
up for me the eye of the mind; already conceive for me angelic choirs, and God
the master of all seated, and an only-begotten Son sitting together on the
right, and Spirit being present together, and Thrones and Lordships
ministering; and each of you men and each of you women, being saved. Already
make your ears as it were to be catechized with that good sound, when the
angels, you having been saved, will cry out: Blessed are they whose lawless
deeds were forgiven, [and whose sins were covered] when, as stars of the
Church, you enter bright in the body, and luminous in the soul. 16 Great is
the baptism set before you; a ransom for captives; a forgiveness of sins; a
death of sin; a regeneration of soul; a luminous garment; a holy seal
indissoluble; a vehicle toward heaven; a delight of paradise; a procurer of a
kingdom; a gift of adoption. But a dragon watches beside the way those who
walk. see that he may not bite with unbelief. He sees so many being saved, and
he seeks whom he may swallow down. You enter toward a Father of spirits, but
you pass through that dragon; how then may you pass through him? Shoe the feet
in readiness of the gospel of peace; that even if he bites, he may not harm;
have indwelling faith, a mighty hope, a strong foundation, that you may pass
through the enemy, and enter toward the master. Prepare your own heart for a
reception of teaching, for a communion of holy mysteries. Pray more
frequently, that God may deem you worthy of the heavenly and immortal
mysteries. Be idle neither by day nor by night; but when the sleep falls from
your eyes, then let [your] mind be at leisure toward prayer. And if you see a
shameful thought having gone up into your mind, take a reminder of judgment of
salvation; set the mind at leisure toward the learning, that you may forget
worthless matters. If you see someone saying to you: And do you enter to go
down into the water? for has the city not now baths? Know that the dragon of
the sea prepares these things for you; do not attend to the lips of the one
speaking, but to God who is at work. Guard your own soul, that you may become
unseizable; that remaining by the hope, you may become an heir of eternal
salvation. 17 We on the one hand, as men, both give charge of these things and
teach. But do not make our building grass, and reed, and chaff; that the work
having been burned, we may not be damaged; but make the work gold, and silver,
and precious stones. For in me is the saying, and in you the purposing, and in
God the perfecting. Let us sinew the mind; let us stretch the soul; let us
prepare the heart; we run concerning a soul; we hope concerning eternal
matters. And God is able, [the one who has known your hearts, and knowing who
is genuine, and who a hypocrite] to guard the genuine one, and to make the
hypocrite faithful. For God is able even to make the unfaithful one faithful,
if only he gives the heart. And may he wipe out the handwriting against you;
and may he furnish you an amnesty of the former trespasses; and may he plant
you into the Church, and may he enlist you for himself, having put around you
weapons of righteousness; and may he fill you of heavenly matters of a new
covenant, and may he give a seal of Holy Spirit inerasable unto the ages, in
Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom the glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.
A little prologue. [Furnishing these catecheses of those being illuminated,
for reading, to those coming forward to baptism, and to the faithful who
already have the bath, do not give them at all either to catechumens, or to
any others who are not Christians; since you will give account to the Lord.
And if you make a copy, write these things in front as before the Lord].
