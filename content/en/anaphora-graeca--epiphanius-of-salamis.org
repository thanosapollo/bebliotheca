* Greek Anaphora
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

"It is worthy and just, our God, to give thanks to you unceasingly for all your
benefactions which you have granted us, first that you sent us from heaven your
holy child, the co-throned of your kingdom, your co-eternal son, the God Word,
who 299 having come at the last of the days, having taken our humanity, the Word
having become flesh not having undergone a turning, through the Holy Spirit
having flesh and a mind-bearing soul of a man and all the things of the man
except sin, not as having dwelt in some man, but himself wholly the God Word,
perfect, having been perfectly eaten, not having altered the humanity, not being
two, but one king, one Christ, one Lord in Godhead and humanity, unmixedly and
indivisibly confirming the divine and the human nature by the God-signs and by
the human sufferings, being about to come upon the voluntary and famous and
life-giving death for us, having taken bread upon his holy and undefiled hands
and having shown it to you the God and Father, having given thanks, having
blessed, having sanctified, he gave to his disciples the holy apostles saying:
Take, eat, this is my body which is broken for you unto forgiveness of sins.
Likewise after having supped, having taken a cup and mixed from two elements,
from fruit of a vine and water, he sealed a cup of immortality and gave to his
disciples saying: Drink from it all, this is my blood of the new covenant which
is poured out for you and many unto forgiveness of sins." "That this bread having
been turned may become a body of Christ, a divine body, a saving body of souls
and bodies, and that mixed in this cup upon the same may be blood of Christ,
blood of a new covenant, saving blood is for us, unto our partaking wholly of
your holy mysteries in newness of the spirit and not in oldness of the letter."
