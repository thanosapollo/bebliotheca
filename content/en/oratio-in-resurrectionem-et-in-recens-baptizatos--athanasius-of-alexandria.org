* Oration on the Resurrection and on the Newly Baptized
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of Saint Athanasius, Archbishop of Alexandria, on the resurrection
of our Savior Jesus Christ and on the newly illuminated.
1 Now choirs of angels, now leapings of archangels, bearing crowns and
celebrating the spiritual sacred festival, reasonably have unspeakable
delight. You hear: "Christ is risen from the dead," and you marvel.
Look at your kinsfolk who in one hour died to sin and lived to Christ,
and be saved rather, being astonished at the power; that one in three
days, these through a threefold invocation. 2 The potter took up the
clay that had fallen, he did not allow the shattered vessel to be
thrown away to the end, he did not overlook it as once crushed; for it
had been crushed, but it had not yet been given over to the fire; he
made it another vessel, fashioning for himself a useful implement. 3
All these the devil took captive, all these Christ set free, all these
the rashness of Eve betrayed, all these the childbirth of Mary showed
released. We did not touch the tree with Adam and we sinned together.
We were not nailed together with Christ and we rose together. There,
having lived together, we died; here, having been buried together, we
were raised. Not having sinned, then we sinned; not having done well,
now we have been justified. 4 O life at the point of death, O death
that begets life, O passionate pleasure, O grief full of joy. Let us
clap our hands, let us raise the voice with a cry higher than a
trumpet, taking up a song of thanksgiving. Now let us exult over the
fall of the devil, hymning our king upon the victory. 5 The sting of
sin has been crushed, the pride of the devil has been trampled, the
fault of Eve has been healed, the bands of the demons have been
dissolved, the sentence of disobedience has been loosed, the bars of
paradise have been flung open, the flaming sword has been changed into
the salvation of the cross, the prize of resurrection is sung, the
boldness of the nations is celebrated, the kingdom of the heavens,
unseen, is seized, the gift of the Holy Spirit has been poured out, the
grace of the Father is proclaimed, the passion of the Son has been
hymned. 6 O fairest change, O those who through a fault have enlisted
themselves for Christ; once bound in sin, once barren toward
righteousness, once both thinking and speaking the things of the
devil; now stretched into a many-stringed lyre they send up a unison
song. 7 O the wonder: not having been put to death they died, not
having been buried they rose. They have remained the same, and they
laid aside what they were in stature, each of them measured. And they
went in and they came out and just now they are understood as infants
of the mind. They were not changed in nature and they were shown forth
as sons of God. They were stripped of nothing and they put off
corruption. 8 They did not behold a master and they were slaves. They
did not lay down money and they carried off freedom. They bear nothing
and they carry about a heavenly treasure. They received nothing
according to what appears and they have grown rich in spiritual gifts.
They did not converse with toils and they appeared as victors. They
did not know a sword and they laid the enemy low. 9 They knew no one
and they hold the token of adoption. They perceived no suffering and
they were circumcised in their hearts by a divine word. They agreed in
word and they shared in the realities. They have given a purpose and
they were not cheated of the truth. No longer do they work together on
the ground with the dragon, coiling about earthly things; but having
received wings as of a dove they travel the air, lightened by the
gifts of the Holy Spirit. 10 They went in with bare shoulders bearing
many evils and they came out having received forgiveness of all things
by unwritten gifts. The water itself became for them both tomb and
mother; for it both killed them to sin and regenerated them to
righteousness. 11 I want to ask you, newly illuminated. Did you not
confess the slavery before, you did not pay anything, did you? Do you
not now boast the freedom. You were full of evils, you have now been
filled with goods. A place enslaved you and set you free; it enslaved
you to God, it freed you from sin. O how many rays did "the sun of
righteousness" bring forth from himself? O how many children did the
heavenly bridegroom, having joined with this undefiled bride, acquire
besides? 12 Not by pleasure, not by flux, not by passion, but having
regenerated them "of water and Spirit," and though they are many by
the cuttings of the body, he mixes all together in one mind, so that,
just as he and the Father are one according to nature, so they also
may be one with him according to will. 13 Upon these things what is
fitting to do? To dance with the blessed David; for he lives and even
while silent he sings, and because from his seed according to the
flesh the liberator has dawned for us. See him dancing together today.
For he says: "Sing to the Lord a new song, for he has done marvelous
things." 14 For the rite of the devil has ceased, and oracular waters
no longer work, but the grace of baptism sets free. These are the
things which Christ granted to the church. For these things he became
man, remaining God, not harming himself, that he might set us free.
For these things he became man, that having hidden and escaped the
notice of the enemy he might set the captive free. 15 What the newly
illuminated gained, we have said, beloved; but those still being fed
with milk must yet be set at the table; for there is a time for every
matter toward the settlement of more necessary things. Be zealous for
these, O catechumen, and do not love the slavery, do not remain in the
bonds of sin, do not linger in the captivity. Come down then from the
shoulders of your mother and come to the solid food. 16 God calls you;
hearken. Is it not enough for you toward grief, that when we are about
to be initiated into our saving economy, you are cast outside as an
alien? You worship Christ and you do not know the things of Christ.
You are rich in the things of Christ and you do not understand the
treasure. Food is set before you and you hunger. 17 And may it be that
you come together into the same, so that the whole church, having
become bright, may be wholly filled with stars. May she become heaven
instead of earth, scattering the mist of the devil. And for all these
things let us give thanks to the Father and to the Son and to the Holy
Spirit, unto the ages of ages. Amen.
