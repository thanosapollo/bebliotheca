* Fragments on the First Epistle to the Thessalonians

The Christians in Thessalonica, those who believed from the nations, endured
many persecutions from those still being Greeks, concerning which he consoles
them. And he also mentions the resurrection of the dead, consoling them in the
losses of their own. And the thanksgiving to God set before the preface is a
testimony of the piety of the Thessalonians. 1 Thess 1:3 Faith indeed raises
toward a work of the good, and love makes [one] remain in the toils, and hope
makes those who contend endure with long-suffering. And faith indeed raises
toward labors, and love makes [one] persist in the toils. 329 1 Thess 1:4-5
Knowing, he says, beloved brethren, that the gospel did not come to be unto
you in word only, but also in power. Not only, he says, did we preach the word
to you, but we also showed you powers and wonder-workings. 1 Thess 1:8 Instead
of it is distributed and noised abroad. 1 Thess 2:2 The evils in Philippi, he
says, did not take away from us the boldness toward Christ, but coming to you
we preached the word without fear. 1 Thess 2:4 The gospel through which we
comfort those who are afflicted, that is the resurrection and the recompense
in it, is preached not according to error but according to truth. 1 Thess 2:7
Instead of burdensome and grievous through demanding honors which apostles of
Christ ought to be honored with. 1 Thess 3:3 He did not say that none should
give way, lest he seem to accuse, but he set a word not able to be convicting
of the slackening, having said to be fawned upon, that none should be
surprised. 1 Thess 4:15-17 He himself was not about to be left remaining, he
says, but the we is either instead of so few and 330 easily numbered will be
left remaining, they will not precede the saints who have fallen asleep unto
the meeting of Christ; for even if all are raised, yet not all will meet
Christ but the righteous. It is clear then that in the coming of Christ some
living also are overtaken. Or also what he says is of this sort: if it
happened that Christ come now, we who are left remaining today will not
precede in the coming of Christ those who have fallen asleep. And one must
punctuate after the we who are left remaining unto the day of the Lord will
not precede those who have fallen asleep. In a voice of an archangel. The Lord
also says the same: and at midnight a cry was made: behold the bridegroom
comes. And the dead in Christ shall rise first. This again the Lord himself
says in the Gospels, whence both the Lord and the apostle hint that the coming
of the Lord will be by night. Then we who are living who are left remaining,
instead of those 329, col 2 righteous around us. He wants some still living to
be overtaken in the coming of Christ, and this to be shown 330,ξολ2 from we
who are left remaining in the coming of the Lord. And what the savior says is
of this sort: for as in the days of Noah they were marrying and being given in
marriage, and suddenly the flood came and took all, so shall be the coming of
the son of man. Therefore, he says, some living are overtaken. In a command
and in a voice of an archangel, and the dead in Christ shall rise. This the
Lord himself says in the Gospel: and it being midnight, he says, a cry was
made: the bridegroom comes. And that living [people] are overtaken, who are
also taken up with those who have fallen asleep, the Lord makes clear in the
Gospel saying: two shall be in a bed; the one shall be taken, and the one
shall be left. And two shall be grinding; the one shall be taken, and the one
shall be left. And both the Lord and Paul hint that the coming will be by
night. 1 Thess 5:4-5 He calls the coming of him the day of the Lord. And he
likened it to a thief, because it comes hidden and contrary to expectation.
And in night, because the day standing over convicts the base things in the
night. 331 1 Thess 5:6-8 But the bride and the soul being adorned with the
ornament of the virtues, sleeping the sleep of the day, the [sleep] without
share of night, the one yoked with wakefulness, and very reasonably said: I
sleep, and my heart keeps watch. And so the voice of the bridegroom did not
escape her thus sleeping; for she perceived him knocking the door and said:
the voice of my kinsman knocks upon the door. 1 Thess 5:9 That not among those
being condemned, but among those being saved. 1 Thess 5:10 For this reason he
says: Christ died for us, that whether we watch, that is we live, or we sleep,
that is whether we die, for here he took sleeping otherwise and not as above,
we may live together with him. For those who have acquired the said arms will
live together and reign together with Christ. 1 Thess 5:19-21 Then the spirit
is quenched, when the manner of life is unworthy of the faith. Despise not
prophesyings. Prove all things. Neither, he says, lie in wait for the hidings
of the spirit, nor receive every announcement untested. 1 Thess 5:23 Nowhere
upon an unbeliever has he set the three, spirit soul body, but upon those who
believe only, of whom soul and body are of nature, but the spirit of the
benefaction, that is the gift of those who believe. And he did not say
"blameless" might be kept, lest you think that he prays for the gifts, but
blamelessly, that is of us living blamelessly and keeping [them] through
piety.
