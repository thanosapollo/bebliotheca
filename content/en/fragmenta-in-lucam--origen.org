* Fragments on Luke
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1a Since it was an oversized attempt for one being a man to write the teaching
and words of God, reasonably he apologizes in the proem. And he did not say
simply of things believed, but "of things fully assured", witnessing the
untransgressable to the things said. And he says "of deeds", since Jesus did
not dramatize his incarnate presence according to fantasy according to the
children of the heretics, but being "truth" he worked the deeds toward truth.
2 [and similarly also Luke] who wrote the present gospel, having been urged by
the chief Peter; so that "they attempted" means that some came without a gift
to the writing of the gospels, and "to arrange" means to set out, to
interpret, to write. 3 "to arrange" instead of to compose in writing and to
make the word manifest 4 And you can also otherwise ... expl. the disciples
being present. 5 That then he received from those who had seen with their own
eyes and become hearers, he clearly confessed saying: "as those who from the
beginning became eyewitnesses and servants of the word handed down to us". And
one must be persuaded by this man and Mark, as having written accurately what
they have heard, and by John and Matthew, as writing what they have seen.
Therefore the lawful gospels must be honored equally, not honoring as a law
the sight of those who have seen, but also the hearing of those who have
heard; for true successors of true ones also send through to us the light
handed down to them, receiving a just praise from us. 6 [he confirms that he
followed ... "all"] the eyewitnesses and servants, that you may not, being
carried here and there, be turned from the straight way; for having been
catechized other things, you will not now be sounded other things from this
writing, but "concerning the things" of which you have taken knowledge you
will receive a more perfect "assurance".
