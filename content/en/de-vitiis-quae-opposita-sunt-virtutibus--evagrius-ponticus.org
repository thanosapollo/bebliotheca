* On the Vices Which Are Opposed to the Virtues
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of the same, a discourse to the same concerning the vices yoked opposite to
the virtues.
=79.1140= CHAPTER Αʹ.
I consider it necessary also to set down in brief for your love of labor the
vices yoked opposite to the virtues; up to this point, having plowed the mind,
we have thrown up the thorns of the thoughts from the sowing-ground; and we
have not come to this from works which we ourselves have done, but having a
pattern of sound words which we heard from the Fathers, and having become
witnesses of some of the practices. But the whole is grace from above, which
both shows even to sinners the plots of the mind-deceivers, and also makes
secure, and says: "For what have you that you did not receive?" so that
through receiving we may give thanks to the giver, because we do not assign to
ourselves any boast of glory, denying the gift. Wherefore he says, "And if you
received it, why do you boast as if you did not receive it?" "Already," he
says, "you have become rich," you who are poor in works; already you who have
begun to be taught have been filled, of whom just now the error has passed
over to me; through which accounting I have borrowed, covering the poverty of
works, and through which opinion I have exhorted, turning the other way the
discipleship of being taught. But nevertheless
=79.1141= if any pardon has been held among you, allot this also to me, so
that we may also set down in brief the vices yoked opposite to the virtues,
signifying: what gluttony is, against which is self-control; and what
fornication is, and chastity; and what love of money is, and
possessionlessness; grief, against which is joy; and what anger is, and
long-suffering; and acedia, and patience; and what vainglory is, and freedom
from vainglory, envy, and freedom from envy, pride, and humility; these being
yoked opposite, and opposed, and contrary to one another, we have thus set
down in brief.
CHAPTER Βʹ. Gluttony then is mother of fornication, nurse of thoughts,
slackness of fasting, muzzle of ascesis, scarecrow of aiming, portrayal of
foods, sketch-painter of seasonings, a loosed colt, unbridled madness,
receptacle of disease, envy of health, blockage of pipes, groaning of bowels,
limit of outrages, mixed with fornication, filth of the mind, weakness of the
body, burdensome sleep, unsmiling death. Self-control, bridle of the belly,
scourge of greediness, balance of measure, muzzle of voracity, renunciation of
rest, composition of hardship, taming-place of thoughts, eye of vigil,
flowing-away of burning, pedagogue of the body, tower of labors, and wall of
ways, settling of habits, and check of passions, death of members, revival of
souls, imitation of resurrection, polity of sanctification. Fornication,
offspring of gluttony, softener of the heart, furnace of burning, bridesman of
idols, unkindred deed, shadowed form, imagined coupling, bed of dreams,
insensible intercourse, deceit of the eyes, shamelessness of the glance, shame
of prayer, disgrace of the heart, guide of ignorance. Chastity, robe of truth,
axe of wantonness, charioteer of the eyes, overseer of thoughts, circumcision
of thinking, and excision of incontinence, counter-grown to nature, and
opposite to burning, worker of works, and fellow-worker of self-control, lamp
of the heart, gnomon of prayer.
CHAPTER Γʹ. Love of money, storehouse of idols, prophecy of the people, vote
of stinginess, accounting of deposits, wealth of captivity, race of injustice,
fatness of diseases, seer of many years, enchanter of love of work, counselor
of vigil, poverty of the belly, cheapness of foods, insatiable madness,
many-caring vice. Possessionlessness, uprooting of love of money, and rooting
of freedom from love of money, fruit of love, and cross of life, painless
life, unenvied treasure, carefree heaven, undistracted sun, unmeasured matter,
incomprehensible wealth, sickle of care, practice of the Gospels,
indissoluble order, swift-running contestant. Grief, inhabitant of loss,
companion of failure, forerunner of sojourning abroad, memory of kinship,
assessor of constriction, fellow-traveler of acedia, charge of provocation,
reminder of outrage, and darkening of the soul, dejection of habits,
drunkenness of prudence, hypno =79.1144= tic antidote, cloud of form, worm of
flesh, grief of thoughts, people of captivity. Joy, destruction of grief, and
misfortunes, vision of prayers, and gladness of labors, cheerfulness of
well-doing, deposit of renunciation, receptacle of hospitality, refuge of
hopes, food of those who practice ascesis, exhortation of mourners,
consolation of tears, help of affliction, voting-together of love, and
fellow-vote of long-suffering. Anger, plunder of prudence, overthrow of
settled state, confusion of nature, savage form, furnace of the heart,
belching flame, law of wrath, wrath of outrages, mother of beasts, silent
battle, stumbling-block of prayer. Long-suffering, weapon of prudence,
tribunal of anger, infirmary of the heart, admonition of the bold, calm of the
troubled, unstormed harbor, well-doing toward those who grieve, gentleness
toward all, being blasphemed it blesses, being outraged it rejoices,
consolation of the constricted, mirror of things hoped for, prize of the
afflicted.
CHAPTER Δʹ. Acedia, aerial friendship, leading-around of steps, hatred of love
of work, battle of stillness, storm of psalmody, hesitation of prayer,
slackening of ascesis, untimely drowsiness, turning-about sleep, weight of
madness, hatred of the cell, opponent of labors, counter-yoke of endurance,
muzzle of meditation, ignorance of the Scriptures, sharer of grief, hour-clock
of hunger. Patience, cutting-off of acedia, cutting-down of thoughts, care of
death, meditation of the cross, nailed fear, beaten gold, law-chest of
afflictions, book of thanksgiving, breastplate of stillness, weapon of labors,
boiling-up of fair labor, underscription of virtues. Vainglory, fantasy of
encounter, shaping of love of labor, contrary to truth, foundress of heresies,
appetite for the first seat, last of appellation, slavery of praises,
many-shaped spirit, myriad-toothed beast; vainglory in the middle is
interwoven with pride and envy, being in one another, and warring through one
another, the three-stranded chain of evils, the three-drug mixture of the
passions, the triple tongue of the heretics. Freedom from vainglory, working
of humility, apostasy from people-pleasing, blindness toward praises,
contemplation of knowledge, contrary to the world, good-perception of the
soul, dogma of cheapness, hiding-place of labors, enemy of good report, hidden
treasure in a corruptible body. Envy, garment of pride, stripping of humility,
root of slander, ophthalmia of good cheer, hypocrisy of friendship, deceit of
boldness of speech, enmity of love, jealousy of those who succeed, tumult of
those who stand well, mockery of those well spoken of, alteration of the eyes.
Who is the one pure in heart, who is not taken of himself upon a rejection of
a commandment of God, or a falling-short, or in negligence? And let Christ be
for us a good path in every work of his will. To him the glory unto the ages.
Amen.
