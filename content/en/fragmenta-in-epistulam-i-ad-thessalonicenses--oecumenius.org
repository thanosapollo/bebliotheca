* Fragments on the First Epistle to the Thessalonians
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1 Thess 1:3 Or he says a work of faith is to show the faith not only in words
but also in works, that for the love of God or of me, he says, you underwent
both toils and dangers; for this is truly to love, to endure to suffer all
things for the one loved. 1 Thess 2:2 Having taken courage in our God, he says.
1 Thess 2:13 Or it works in you, that is it shows its own power, perhaps through
certain signs; or it is worked, that is it grows through your life. 1 Thess 2:16
And these things they did both of old upon the prophets and now upon Christ and
upon us, that their sins might always be filled up; for since they always did
these things, they always filled up their sins. 1 Thess 3:2 For the comfort came
to be for the sake of not being shaken into the faith. 1 Thess 3:3 And to be
fawned upon as to be tumulted and troubled; from a metaphor of dogs troubling
the tails. 1 Thess 3:9 Reasonably we rejoice before God; for he himself is cause
of this joy to us and his is the gift. 1 Thess 4:10a For this reason we have no
need to write, that you do not need a reminder concerning this, but you do it.
1 Thess 4:10b For Thessalonica is a metropolis of Macedonia. 1 Thess 4:14 What
then, someone might say toward the second interpretation, will the resurrection
be partial, as of the unbelievers not being raised? not this, but that all will
be raised, but not all will be led; for to be led shows the snatching from
everywhere in clouds with honor and the leading toward the judge. For the
faithful and those who do not have this name plastered will be snatched in
clouds into a meeting of Christ, and the unbelievers will wait below for him
coming, not being deemed worthy to meet. For why did he not say: that you may be
punished as not confessing a resurrection? or as this being confessed he passed
it by, and says: that you may not with the punishment there also be grieved at
the things here. or that they did not disbelieve the resurrection and it shows
them doubting concerning the times, which he says below but they only were
distressed over those who die. 1 Thess 4:15 Perhaps this too was one of the
unspeakable things; and it was not permitted to speak all, but this it was
permitted to speak.
