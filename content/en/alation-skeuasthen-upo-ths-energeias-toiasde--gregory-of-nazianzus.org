A little salt prepared by the following operation
It does not produce ophthalmia until old age, teeth do not ache, there is no
rheum, hair does not fall, it produces sharp sight, it does not let go phlegm,
nor bile, nor humor, nor gout, nor spleen. And its preparation is as follows:
2 Cretan hyssop, 1 ounce; Ethiopian cumin, 1 ounce; ammoniac salts, 2 ounces;
scammony, 3 ounces; ginger, 1 ounce; celery seed, 1 ounce; mountain
pennyroyal, 3 ounces; Macedonian parsley, 2 ounces; silphium, 1 ounce; ammi, 1
ounce; common salt, 2 ounces; leaf, 2 ounces. Having cut these and shaken
them, use in foods as you wish.
