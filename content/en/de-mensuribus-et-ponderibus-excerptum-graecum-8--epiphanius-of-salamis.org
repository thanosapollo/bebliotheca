* On Measures and Weights, Greek Excerpt 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1

And it must be known that there are twenty-two books of the old covenant
according to the letters of the Hebrew voice. For they have twenty-two letters,
of which five are doubled, so that they become twenty-seven; for doubled is Kaph
and Mem and Nun and Pe and Tsade. Wherefore also the books according to this way
are numbered twenty-two, and are found twenty-seven because five of them are
doubled. For Ruth is joined to Judges and is numbered among the Hebrews one book;
the first and second of Kingdoms one book; the third and the fourth of Kingdoms
one book; the first and the second of Chronicles one book; the first and the
second of Ezra one book. Thus then the books are composed in four pentateuchs,
and two others remain, so that the canonical books are thus: five legal: Genesis,
Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy; this is the first pentateuch, which is
also legislation. Then another pentateuch, the so-called Writings, and by some
Hagiographa, which are thus: Joshua son of Nave, Judges with Ruth, first of
Kingdoms with the second, one book, third of Kingdoms with the fourth, one book,
and the two of Chronicles, one book; this is the second pentateuch. Third
pentateuch the verse books: of Job, the psalter, Proverbs of Solomon, Ecclesiastes
of the same, the Songs of songs of the same. Fourth pentateuch, the prophetic:
the twelve-prophet, one book, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel. Then of Ezra,
the two joined into one book, and Esther. And the Panaretos, that is the Wisdom
of Solomon, and the Wisdom of Jesus, which the father of Sirach set out in
Hebrew, and in Greek Jesus his grandson, son of Sirach, interpreted, are
virtuous and fair, but they are not numbered nor were they laid in the ark.
