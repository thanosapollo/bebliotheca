* Fragments on the Second Epistle to Timothy
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

2 Tim 1:1 In the former epistle, being about to hand down ecclesiastical
canons, he said according to commandment, but in the second he said according
to promise; for the apostleship that is according to the commandment of God
orders the things that are profitable, while the [other] [gives] to those who
hear a promise of life, not of the present but of the everlasting, that in
Christ Jesus. In the first epistle he says according to commandment, being
about to order, but in the second epistle according to promise; for the
apostleship orders, yet in hope that those who obey may obtain eternal life. 2
Tim 1:3 He does not mean the uninterruptedness of life, but of faith toward
God. 2 Tim 1:8-10 Since the trial against those who preach the gospel was
making a cutting-off for the gospel, for this reason he says: suffer hardship
with the gospel. And he added: according to the power of God, since the very
suffering of hardship needs the help of God, who abolished death, and brought
life and immortality to light through the gospel. There was life also upon
Adam and upon those after him, but it was darkened by the death of
disobedience. It has therefore now been brought to light by the resurrection
of Christ; it was darkened by death, it is brought to light through Christ.
And how is it possible for the gospel to be tried? As those who preach were
tried. But since the trial against those who preach the gospel was making a
hindrance for the gospel, for this reason he says these things. And having
brought life to light. That which is, is brought to light. Since then life was
given before the disobedience of Adam, but was darkened by death, again by the
resurrection of the savior life is brought to light. 2 Tim 1:12 What deposit?
He taught him the sound teaching. 343 2 Tim 2:4 Since he said beforehand that
the one serving as a soldier must not be entangled in the business of life, it
was then asked how, not being entangled, he has the things for need. He
indicated that one must live from the gospel. 2 Tim 2:17 Gangrene is a
disease, rotting and easily running down upon the bodies. 2 Tim 4:1 He does
not charge as condemning one who is slack, but that in the charge he may keep
for him throughout his own purpose. I charge [you] before God and the Lord
Jesus Christ, who is about to judge the living and the dead, according to his
appearing and his kingdom, instead of that when the son of man comes, I may
have a defense that I spoke. And he says these things, that he may give him
boldness toward preaching the word. 2 Tim 4:2 For through this command he
wants the teacher to stand over those being taught at every time, the in
season indicating the reminder after the sin; for then it somehow seems timely
to refute the one who has sinned. But since this stands outside of the
preventive help, namely waiting for the man to sin first, then so to rebuke
him and turn him toward repentance, correcting the word he says, not only in
season but also out of season, so that the out of season may be that before
the sin comes into deed. For it is thought by the many superfluous and not in
season to converse with those who have not yet gone astray. But this
superfluous and untimely [thing] is exceedingly necessary and timely to those
who know how to discern. For one must also speak with the chaste man about
fornication, lest he fall into the nets of the passion, and to the one who
honors self-sufficiency and the equal and just it is fitting to go through the
words about greed and love of money, lest 344 somehow he unguardedly receive a
shoot of the root of evils. For the love of money is the root of all evils.
Paul the apostle defined. 2 Tim 4:5 The fulfilling of the ministry hints at
the full and unfailing [character] of the words, as many as belong to the
high-priesthood, and the filling up from every side of the needs of the
rational flock so as to be sound in all the members, both in priests and in
ministers; for those who have been allotted the dignity of the episcopate were
appointed to build up and to plant, even if it happen to suffer hardship on
this account. Wherefore also in the things said the apostle put the hardship
of the work first; for he said: suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist.
2 Tim 4:6 I am poured out. Instead of I am put to death, I am slain so as to
sprinkle my blood upon the earth.
