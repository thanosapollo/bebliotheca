Hebr 1:12 To those around Abraham both and Moses and those in succession.
Differently then, he says, and suitably to each time having spoken with our
forefathers through their own prophets, God, the whole of time already being
fulfilled, has spoken to us in a Son, that is, with laws 421 first having
trained our race of each generation with ones suitable toward acquisition of
righteousness both and knowledge of God through his own servants, the times
already having been fulfilled in Christ and the economy according to him he
evangelized to us the self-complete grace of these. And he means the after the
cross through his resurrection toward the incorruptible and sinless of our
nature restoration.
{Hebr 1:3} Glory indeed he has named eulogistically the Father, and radiance
the Son, teaching from an example that he is both from a Father and co-eternal
with the Father; for such is the radiance, being both from the sun and
coexisting with it. Therefore one would never say the sun older than its own
radiance. And he said it without the syllable of Father according to the idiom
of the scripture.
{Hebr 5:8} Instead of: by the suffering he displayed having the lesson of
obedience most complete, and this while being a Son. And thus we also are
accustomed to speak. Often at any rate when someone has gone through a speech
most excellently, we say that this man truly learned from the words he has
spoken, that is, by the announcement and the phrasing he firmly showed that he
has accomplished the learning of the words.
{Hebr. 6:1} The saying is hyperbaton; for the of teaching in the middle is
referred to all the inserted, and this is the composition according to its
sense: not laying again a foundation of teaching, and then thus the things
shown next in enumeration concerning which the foundation of the teaching.
And he wants to say that for those now first approaching the faith we lay down
beforehand the teaching about these things as a kind of foundation, reasonably
first exhorting them to repent from the death-making works, and those
repenting to believe upon God, and those believing to be baptized, and those
being baptized to submit themselves to the laying-on of the priestly hands
toward participation of the Spirit, and those having shared in this no longer
to disbelieve concerning the resurrection from the dead, and those about to
rise and again come to life to know there will be a judgment, and 422 to await
a perpetual and just one and the recompense of the things we have lived here.
{Hebr. 9:2} According to one account then I think the lampstand, as has been
said, hints at the light from these perceptible stars, and the table the
earth, and the setting-forth of the loaves the distribution from the earth of
the things suitable for us.
