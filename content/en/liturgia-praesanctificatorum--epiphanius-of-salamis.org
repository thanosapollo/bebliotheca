Liturgy of the catechumens
And at the lamp-lighting, after the readings and the Let my prayer be set
forth and the Lord have mercy, there takes place a prayer of the catechumens
at the presanctified. O God, our God, the creator and fashioner of all things,
who wills all to be saved and to come to the knowledge of truth, look upon
your servants the catechumens and deliver them from the ancient error and from
the craft of the adversary and call them unto the life eternal, enlightening
their souls and bodies and numbering them with your rational flock, upon which
your holy name has been invoked. Aloud: That they also with us may glorify the
Prayer for those being prepared for the holy illumination. Make your face to
shine, Master, upon those being prepared for the holy illumination and longing
to shake off the stain of sin.
Illuminate their understanding; confirm them in the faith; establish them in
hope; perfect them in love; show them precious members of your Christ, who
gave himself a ransom for our souls. Aloud: For you are our illumination and
to you we send up the glory. Liturgy of the faithful First prayer of the
faithful O God the great and praiseworthy, who by the life-giving death of
your Christ transferred us from corruption into incorruption; do you free all
our senses from passionate deadness, setting over them as a good guide the
inward reason. And let the eye be without share in every wicked glance, and
let hearing be unapproachable to idle words, and let the tongue be kept clean
from unseemly utterances. And purify also our lips that praise you, Lord, and
make our hands to abstain from base deeds, and to work only the things
well-pleasing to you, fencing all our members and the understanding with your
grace. Aloud: For to you belongs all glory, honor, and worship.
Prayer of the faithful
2nd Holy Master, exceeding good, we beseech you who are rich in mercy, to be
gracious to us sinners and make us worthy of the reception of your
only-begotten Son and our God, the king of glory. For behold his undefiled
body and the life-giving blood, at the present hour entering in, are about to
be set forth upon this mystical table, invisibly escorted by a multitude of
the heavenly host; grant us the partaking of them without condemnation; that
through them, the eye of the understanding being illuminated, we may become
sons of light and of day.
Aloud: According to the gift of your Christ, with whom you are blessed
together with the all-holy
Entrance And after the completion of Now the powers of the heavens, the priest
prays: O God of the ineffable and unseen mysteries, with whom the treasures of
wisdom and of knowledge are hidden; who revealed to us the ministry of this
liturgy and appointed us sinners through your great love of mankind to offer
you gifts and sacrifices for our sins and the ignorances of the people;
yourself, invisible King, who do great and unsearchable things, glorious and
marvelous, of which there is no number, look upon us your unworthy servants
who stand at this holy altar as at your cherubic throne, upon which your
only-begotten Son and our God through the dread mysteries set forth takes his
rest, and having freed us all and your faithful people from uncleanness,
sanctify the souls and the bodies of us all



with an inalienable sanctification; that in a pure conscience, with unashamed
face, with enlightened heart, partaking of these divine sanctified things and
being quickened by them, we may be united to your Christ himself, our true
God, who said: He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood abides in me and I in
him; that, your Word dwelling in us and walking in us, Lord, we may become a
temple of your holy and worshipful Spirit, redeemed from every devilish craft
working in deed, or word, or according to thought, and may obtain the good
things promised to all the saints who from of old have been well-pleasing to
you.
Aloud: And count us worthy, Master
The people: Our Father
The priest aloud: For yours is the kingdom
The people: Amen.
The priest: Peace to all
The people: And to your spirit
The deacon: Our heads
The priest prays: O God, who alone are good and compassionate, who dwell in
the heights and look upon the lowly, look with a compassionate eye upon all
your people and guard them and count us all worthy to partake without
condemnation of these your life-giving mysteries. For to you we have bowed our
own heads, awaiting the rich mercy from you.
Aloud: By the grace and compassions and love of mankind of your only-begotten
Son, with whom you are blessed together with your all-holy and good and
life-giving Spirit, now
The people: Amen
The deacon: Let us attend
The priest: The presanctified holy things for the holy
The people: One holy, one Lord Jesus Christ.
Prayer after the communion We give thanks to you, the God Savior of all, for
all the good things you have granted us and for the partaking of the holy body
and blood of your Christ. And we pray you, Master who love mankind: guard us
under the shelter of your wings and grant us until our last breath to partake
worthily of your sanctified things unto illumination of soul and body, unto
inheritance of the kingdom of heavens.
Aloud: For you are our sanctification and to you we send up the glory, to the
Father and to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto the ages
of ages; Amen.
The deacon: Let us go forth in peace.
The people: In the name of the Lord.
Prayer behind the ambo at the presanctified Master, God the Almighty, who
fashioned the creation in wisdom, who through your unspeakable providence and
by free will have led us into these all-venerable days for purification of
souls, for continence of resurrection; who through the forty days delivered
into the hand the tablets, the God-inscribed letters, to Moses; grant also to
us, good Master, to fight the good fight, to finish the course of the fast, to
keep the faith undivided, to crush the heads of the invisible dragons, and
that we may appear victors over sin and arrive without condemnation at your
holy resurrection. For holy is the name of the Father and of the Son and of
the Holy Spirit.
