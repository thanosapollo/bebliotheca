On Jn 8:44 Of John the grammarian: As Cain, wasting with envy, became
first-worker of murder, so also the Jews, carried by envy, desire to kill
Christ. For here he called Cain a devil, whom he also named their father as
being a type of them. And he has not stood in the truth. For being asked by
God: Where is Abel your brother? the wretched one speaks falsely, thinking to
beat back the one who knows all things.
On Jn 10:18 Of John the grammarian: This commandment I received from my
Father, to have authority to lay down the soul for his own sheep and to take
it again, since the Father loves me; and this has been said of necessity
toward the disobedience of the Jews, who say he is opposed to God and as not
being from him; and he reminds them that he fulfills that commandment which
the Father says to Moses: I will raise up for them a prophet from their
brothers like you and I will give my word in his mouth and he will speak to
them according as I command him.
