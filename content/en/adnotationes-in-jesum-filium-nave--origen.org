ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON JOSHUA SON OF NAVE.
=17.36= CH. 3. Verse 14. And all the people departed from their tents. That
is, as a filling of a threshing-floor. For it was not only fixed, as in the
Red [Sea], but was heaped as a stack of wheat. Clearly as a type of the
apostles. CH. [6]. Verse 15, 16. Cry out; for the Lord has given you the city.
Why did he command Jericho to be devoted? They took this first city after
crossing the river. As therefore he commanded them to offer the firstfruits of
the =17.37= fruits, so he ordered them to bring the first-spoils of the
spoils. And besides these he also trains them through these laws, so as to
keep the necessary laws. And showing them his own power, he prepared them to
take the first city without weapons and engines, and he overthrew it by the
sound of the trumpets alone; that whenever they are defeated when arrayed,
they may know that they themselves furnished the starting-points of the
defeat, having despised the divine laws. CH. 8. Verse 2. And you shall plunder
for yourself the plunder of the cattle. Because it was not necessary as in
Jericho that all things be devoted. Verse 18. And Joshua stretched out the
spear, and his hand. For Joshua remained having his hand stretched out; that
those of the ambush, having seen, might occupy the city beforehand.
