OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS GREGORY THE WONDERWORKER
HOMILY ON ALL THE SAINTS
Bless, Master.
I wished to keep quiet, and not to publish a rustic tongue; for silence is
great when the word is cheap; and stillness is admirable when lack of
education is present; and a philosopher of the first rank covering ignorance
by silence. This I too preferred to do, knowing the wea =10.1200= kness of
my own tongue; but the sight of the things seen draws me toward speaking.
Since therefore the festival is brilliant and the assembly continuous, and
our gathering is fervent in the faith, I shall turn with confidence to the
beginning of the discourse; especially as the Father is praying, the Church
contending together, holy martyrs strengthening this weakness toward this.
For these persuaded even old men with much sweat to complete a long journey,
and compelled them to steady the stumbling steps with the staff of the word;
and they urged the female to travel along with young men; and they brought
here the creeping infant age, martyrs leaping against death, laughing at
swords, snatching away tyrannical fury, snatching in return death as the
procurer of immortality, claiming the victory in their own fall, leaping
through the bo =10.1201= dy toward heaven. Scattering the members in order
that they might bind the souls, bursting the bars of life in order that they
might open the keys of the heavens. Let him who disbelieves that death has
been dissolved, Hades has been trampled, that its bonds have been burst;
that the tyrant has been bound; look at martyrs tumbling against death, and
celebrating the victory of Christ. O the wonder. From the time that Christ
spoiled Hades, men have danced down death. Where, O death, is your sting?
Where, O Hades, is your victory? Has Hades been spoiled, and the devil? And
he has been stripped of the ancient panoply; and he has been cast out of his
own power; and just as Goliath had his head cut off by his own sword, so
also the devil, having begotten death, is routed through death; and he finds
the easy method of his own deceit a strong instrument of his own
destruction; as if pierced through like a hook on the Godhead, and having
swallowed the customary bait, he who thought he was hunting is clearly
hunted; having seized God instead of a man. For this reason martyrs leap
upon the head of the dragon; and they look down on every kind of torture.
For from the time that the second brought up the first Adam from the depth
of Hades as Jonah from the sea-monster, and showed the one who was deceived,
unto the shame of the deceiver, to be a citizen of heaven; from that time
the gates of Hades were shut; and the gates of heaven were opened; granting
unhindered entrance to those going up with faith.
Of old Jacob saw a ladder set up as far as heaven, and the angels of God
going up and going down; but now, having become man for the sake of man, the
Lover of man, having kicked the inhuman one with the foot of the Godhead,
and having borne the man with the hand of the Christhood, prepared the
untraversable aether to be walked with human feet. Then the angels went up
and went down; but now the Angel of great counsel does not go up and go
down; for from where to where will he be transferred who is present
everywhere, and fills all things, and holds in his hand the [world's]
limits?
For he came down once and went up once; not =10.1204= using a change of
nature, but a condescension of man-loving Christhood; the Word sitting with
the Father, and the Word lodging in the womb, and the Word found everywhere,
and never separated from [the] [God] of all. The devil once laughed a broad
laugh against human nature, and at our misfortunes he is gladdened with
festivals. But the laughter was of three days; the lament is eternal; and a
broad laugh prepared for him a broader lament, unceasing tear, inconsolable
weeping; a dart in the heart. This dart in the virginal furnace, such as he
willed, and as he willed, the war-leader forged through fire against the
enemy; and having tempered it with the energy of the unconquered Godhead,
and having dipped it in the water of the undefiled baptism, and having
sharpened it with the impassible passions, and having burnished it through
the mystical resurrection; through himself with the whole army he put the
accursed one to death. What word then will narrate either our joy or his
misfortune? For he who was once an archangel is now devil; he who once dwelt
in the heavens is now seen dragged as a serpent on the earth; he who once
rejoiced with the cherubim is now shut up in the prison of the swine, in
pain; whom we shall put to flight, having thought things contrary to his
purpose; by the grace and love of mankind of our Lord Jesus Christ; to whom
be the glory and the power unto the ages of ages, Amen.
