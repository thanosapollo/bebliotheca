ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON THE JUDGES.
=12.949= And Jephthah vowed a vow to the Lord. If the Spirit of the Lord was
upon him, how does he vow what is not fitting? For it was possible even for a
dog to meet Jephthah as he went up. Was then the Spirit at fault? in no way; but
the Spirit has come to help, not to suggest the unfitting vow; and he himself is
at fault having promised without consideration. And they said to him Speak now
an ear of grain. And they said an ear of grain; it means sebela, which is an
ear. And they said sebelo and they were not able to say clearly sebela. For even
if they happened to be Hebrews, yet according to a measure they differed by a
shortness in the bringing-forth of the dialect. Therefore the token was sebela,
which those asked, saying sebelo, were convicted of not being of the household
of Jephthah, but of those who rose up. And his wife was barren and did not bear.
Not from nature, but from the ministry he calls her barren upon the present.
Iron has not gone up upon my head because I am a Nazirite of God. Through the
whole history concerning Samson the hairs appear to furnish him all the
strength. Of the same. He names a Nazirite the guarded one according to the
voice of the Syrians. And God commands Samson not to follow the order of the
Israelites, at that time having the head shaved because of being marked toward
all. And he made an Ephod and Teraphim. An Ephod was a priestly weave, which,
being laid around the priest, the grace of the Holy Spirit came. And the
Teraphim of such a sort, not divine however, but idolatrous, as is shown in the
kingdoms. And the sons of Benjamin went out. For whose sake are they defeated,
having gone out justly? Or it is clear because of a preceding cause. And the
psalm says: "When I take a time, I will judge uprightnesses." Then also after
being defeated, both a first and a second, wrath went over upon those who had
done wrong, and they win a just victory.
