Scripture, saying these set out from Paradise, histories the most true thing.
For carrying from it the first beginnings of the streams, they go forward up

desert having received them, being swallowed there toward the depth, they do
not sooner stand from the downward bearing, before they arrive at the very
watertight and rocky of the earth there. And the foundation there having
provided them a standing of the downward advance, there already, their
currents being gathered, by multitude and strength of what is always being
brought on they go forward, forcing the way straight. But these rivers,
traveling underground, a not small portion of them being taken away by the
earth in the middle, already meet lesser and weaker at the places of the
uprisings. And that they go through very much earth in recesses, it is not
reasonable to disbelieve. For there are many other currents everywhere even of
the exceedingly greatest and strongest arriving under the earth. And it is
clear; for both a great sound is heard from them as they are borne with
confusion and much rushing; but also certain men, digging wells above them,
and having wounded a little from below the slab that met them, under which the
stream already seething was forcing the bearing upward, were only just taken
up, snatched by those standing at the lip of the well-digging, and the stream
having accompanied went forward into a channel, no longer distressed by any
drought through the everlasting of the supply. For the unspeakable wisdom of
God worked the runnings-through of the streams as veins supplying the
necessities, some unseen, some evident. In this way then also the prophet
David sang: "He himself founded it upon seas and prepared it upon rivers"; as
having stored up the seas, as it were, in its greatest gulfs, and having
strengthened its base toward bearing up so great a bulk and multitude of the

well-devised dispositions, providing by the leadings-down and lownesses of the
places from the higher to the more low-lying a place for the inclination of
the heavy water. =3.10= That this man, using conjecture, says Paradise lies
