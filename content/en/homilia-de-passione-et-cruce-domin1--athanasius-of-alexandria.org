OF ATHANASIUS ON THE PASSION.
"Death reigned from Adam until Moses." And now, having dared against the Lord,
he sees himself thrown out of the all, and being trampled by all, and being
crushed under our feet; and the great and paradoxical thing, that even upon the
cross itself of the Lord hanging, (for the body was his, and in it was the Word;)
the sun was darkened, and the earth trembled, the rocks were split, and the veil
of the temple was split, and many bodies of those who had fallen asleep before
arose; and no one, as now Arians dare, reasoned whether one must obey the Word
incarnate; but even seeing a man, they recognized him being their creator; and
hearing a human voice, they did not say because of the human that the Word is a
creature; but they even rather trembled, and no less knew that he spoke from a
holy temple; for the creation does not worship a creature; nor again because of
the flesh did it decline to worship its own Lord, but it saw its own creator in
the body; and in the name of Jesus Christ every knee bent and will bend, and
every tongue will confess that Jesus Christ is Lord; for the flesh did not bring
ingloriousness to the Word; may it not be; but rather it itself has been
glorified by him; nor since the Son who is in the form of God took a form of a
slave, was he lessened from the Godhead, but rather he himself has become a
liberator of all flesh and of all creation; who also being crucified was
witnessed Master of the creation; the sun having contracted the rays, and the
earth trembling, and the rocks being split, and the executioners recognizing
that truly the one crucified is Son of God; for the body seen was not of some
man, but of God; in which being even when he was crucified, he raised the dead;
when also the sun, being angry, and not bearing the bodily despite that came to
be against the common Master of all, which he himself voluntarily underwent for
us, turned away; and having contracted the rays, showed that day sunless.
