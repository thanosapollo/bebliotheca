* On the Great Feast
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Rightly I am in agony, held by the bonds of timidity, how I shall fill the
thirst of your love of hearing, what table of the word I shall set forth, what
I shall become receiving so great a people, whom Christ alone will suffice
for feasting. The joy of the world is now at the doors; the harbor of the
Master's resurrection draws near. Again the irrational nation of the Jews is
in uproar 339, again the dogs bark against the shepherd, again the slaves
revolt against the Master, again the workers of darkness take counsel against
the light, again Judas meditates the lawless sale, again the Jews prepare
silver, that they may send to Hades him who is free among the dead and may
learn death taken captive, that tombs may be shaken and the dead may be
winged toward rising, again a disciple sells, and those learned in the law
buy, and a robber on a cross theologizes.
But let us remember the voice of the betrayer: "What will you give me," he
says, "and I will deliver him to you." What do they wish to give [you],
Judas? The rope of the noose, the outer darkness, alienation from the
kingdom, the separation from the saints, the unrestorable fall: these are
your profits from the bargain with the Jews. ["What will you give me?"] What
can they give you such as what you are about to lose, most wicked one? And
why do you sell so cheaply the architect of heaven and earth, him who bears
all things in his palm? When you saw your Master being honored, you saw the
precious myrrh poured upon his fearful head, and condemning the munificence
of the woman you cried: "To what purpose is this waste?" "Why was this myrrh
not sold for three hundred denarii and given to the poor?" And the myrrh
compounded from various kinds and prepared by some perfume-art you valued at
three hundred denarii, but the true myrrh that makes the dead smell the
resurrection you sell for thirty denarii? You do not know what you are
selling, Judas. The drunkenness of love of money has darkened you. When did
you know a master being sold by a slave, and when did you learn to sell light
and buy darkness? Why did you insult the token of love, and why do you work
the betrayal with a kiss? Well did the prophet from afar, branding you, 340
cry out: "whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness and deceit, under his
tongue is toil and pain;" "you have wrought deceit as a sharpened razor"; and
you were kissing your Master. Come near, kiss; the Savior endures with
long-suffering, not turning away from the stench of your kiss, but calling
back the grace of the debt which he gave you from your own lips. O how
terrible is the love of money. Through this the disciple, torn from the
apostolic chain, inherits the rope of strangling and shows his throat worthy
of the noose, through which the words of the betrayal came forth.
O how great a good is sobriety. For through this the robber leaped from a
cross into paradise; for when he saw the Master hanging in the midst and all
creation shaken with fear, and the sun covered in a hard mourning, and the
light of the day fleeing away, the elements denying their order, the rocks
being split, when he had beheld all these things, then reading his own
conscience and not ignorant of the deeds of his fellow-robber, when he saw
creation being borne this way and that and trembling, he recognized the
Master in the midst of those that trembled, and having recognized him he
cried out at once: "Remember me, Lord, when you come in your kingdom." Truly
swift is the robber, the worker of the eleventh hour; for he fulfilled the
whole work of the day of life, in the hour in which he was hired he worked
eagerly. He did not buckle in the faith, he did not look away toward the
insults, he did not look toward the blows, he brushed aside the reproaches of
the Jews, he took creation as teacher and, recognizing the Master of
creation, he gave worthy worship.
What then toward these things does the payer of wages, Christ who is
munificent in his benefactions? When the robber asked for the future, he
himself displayed the grace without delay; for when that one had said,
"Remember me, Lord, when you come in your kingdom," the Master answered at
once saying: "Amen, I say to you, today you will be with me in para 341
dise." True is the Scripture that says: "While you are still speaking I will
say: Behold, I am here." To Christ himself be the glory with the Father and
the Holy Spirit unto the ages of ages. Amen.
