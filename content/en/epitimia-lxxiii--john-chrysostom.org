* Seventy-Three Penances
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Penances of our father among the saints, John Chrysostom.
1 He who has killed voluntarily, let him be without communion for
years; and making each day 50 prostrations, 150 times "Lord, have
mercy" together with "O God, be merciful to me," abstaining from meat,
cheese, and egg, and on Wednesdays and Fridays abstaining from oil,
fish, and wine; if possible, let him also eat dry food. 2 Let the
involuntary killer be penanced for 3 years. 3 Let the adulterer be
without communion for 5 years, making 100 prostrations a day; but if he
serve with tears and humility, let him also communicate in three years.
4 He who has fallen with his own child's godmother, let him be penanced
for 7 years, making each day prostrations, 300 times "Lord, have mercy,"
and eating dry food. 5 He who has committed adultery with the wife of
his sworn brother, let him be without communion for 8 years, 100
prostrations, 100 "Lord, have mercy." 6 If a father and a son go in to
one woman, the first receives the penance of the fornicator, that is 3;
and let the second be without communion for 12 years, 300 prostrations
each day. 7 Let the thief and the temple-robber be without communion
for 3 years, 100 prostrations. 8 He who blasphemes unjustly is condemned
to the portion of Cain, and let him be without communion for 6 months,
100 prostrations. 9 He who swears unseasonably a false oath concerning
his encounter, let him be excluded from the church for 12 days, and let
him eat dry food. 10 Let the one who treads on the cross be excluded
for 3 years, 100 prostrations.
11 He who has fallen into softness, let him not communicate until 50
days, eating dry food, 40 prostrations. 12 He who falls with his own
baptismal godmother, let him be without communion for 12 years, 200
prostrations. 13 He who has fallen with an androgyne, whether he be man
or woman, has a penance of 7 years, without communion, 200 prostrations.
14 If anyone fall with his mother, he has a penance of 14 years,
without communion, 300 prostrations, eating dry food. 15 If an
unbaptized child die in the midst of its parents at 12 days or more,
they have a penance of 2 years; but if it is baptized, at 40 days, they
have a penance of 3 years, as being contemptuous. 16 If an unbaptized
infant die near its parents within 7 days, its parents have a penance of
40 days, 12 prostrations morning and evening, fasting Monday, Wednesday,
and Friday, eating dry food, bread only and water. 17 A woman nursing
her own infant, having neglected to provide for it, and her infant was
found dead: if it is unbaptized, being of 40 days, she has a penance of
1 year; but if it lies outside and dies, let her be penanced 50 days.
18 If a woman, having given herself over to softness with her own hands,
let her be without communion for years, let her be excluded, 150
prostrations each day. 19 If any woman take leaven and wipe her body
with it, let her be without communion for 7 years, 200 prostrations. 20
A woman who has mixed with an unbelieving and foreign man, let her be
penanced 7 years without communion, let her be excluded, and remaining
let her do 100 prostrations.
21 A woman who has neglected the infant in her own womb and casts it
out, let her be penanced 3 years or two, without communion,
prostrations. 22 If anyone does not accept to have his feet washed by
another, let him be anathema. 23 If any bishop or presbyter or monk
does not receive the sinner who has come to him for confession, he is an
enemy of God, and he grieves Christ who said: "I came not to call the
righteous, but sinners to repentance," and: "For joy comes about in
heaven and on earth over a sinner who repents." 24 If anyone partake of
the body and blood of Christ and our God, and then bathe, let him be
accursed. 25 If anyone, having committed fornication in dreams and
having communicated in the morning, let him be anathema. 26 If anyone
says to his brother "fool," let him be accursed, as the Lord said. 27
If anyone converses in the temple of God, let him be anathema. 28 If
anyone offers drugs and astrology for the theft of things, or asks
anything else whatsoever, let him be anathema. 29 If anyone eat the
blood of a beast, let him be accursed. 30 If any presbyter or cleric or
deacon exacts interest from lenders, anathema.
31 If any presbyter or cleric or deacon enter a tavern, not by
necessity and not in sojourning abroad, let him be anathema. 32 If
anyone listens to the mysteries of brethren, let him be anathema. 33 If
any Christian make weddings in the holy and great Forty Days, let him be
anathema. 34 If any woman be found with two brothers, let her be
without communion for 7 years, 100 prostrations each day, eating dry
food. 35 If any presbyter or monk be drunk from much wine, let him be
penanced 40 days, 50 prostrations. 37 If anyone, jesting, displays his
shame openly, let him be anathema. 38 If a great-schema monk commit
fornication, let him receive the penance of an adulterer; but the
little-schema monk, if he fall with a free person, receives the penance
of a fornicator. 39 The monk who has fallen into softness has a penance
of 40 days, 50 prostrations, eating dry food. 40 The monk who has
fallen into a flux in his sleep, either from meditation or from looking,
ought not to serve until 7 days, and making 40 prostrations each day,
eating dry food.
41 The monk who has fallen with a nun, let both be deposed, and let
them come to the places of novices; let their penance be 12 years,
without communion, to make 300 prostrations each day, because he who
defiles the temple of God, God will defile him. 42 The monk who has
fallen with an androgyne, let him be deposed; his penance 7 years,
without communion, 300 prostrations, eating dry food. 43 If any monk
behaves insolently, he is reckoned as a fornicator; but if he strike
anyone unjustly, let him be excluded for 40 days, eating dry food. 44
If any monk accompany a woman for seven hours, God reckons him a
fornicator. 45 If any monk walking on the road set his staff upon his
shoulders, he is outside the monastic estate, and God reckons him a
robber. 46 If any monk eat uncovered, or sleep so, he is an enemy of
God. 47 If any monk lift his rag above his knees, God reckons him a
fornicator. 48 If anyone, fighting with another, and one of them die
unforgiven by the other, the living one ought to be penanced eating dry
food for 12 years, and praying to God for the forgiveness of the dead.
49 If anyone seize another's property, and others have sworn unjustly,
let him be penanced 12 years, and let the thing be returned threefold.
50 If anyone bind a virgin by oath, the canon does not have that they
be separated, lest they trample the life-giving cross.
51 If anyone be defiled in the holy Forty Days, he fasts from Pascha
until Pentecost; but one must break the fast on Great Sunday. 52 If
anyone turn a brother away from the way of truth through his cunning,
let him be penanced 40 days, 50 prostrations, eating dry food. 53 If
anyone [be found] talking idly and jesting, let him be penanced 2 days,
50 prostrations, eating dry food. 54 He who is vainglorious toward any
Christian becomes an enemy of God. 55 The canonarch, if he chant the
verses carelessly, and through his sloth cuts off the service fitting to
God, let him eat dry food 5 days, 50 prostrations. 56 The same, if he
lie carelessly after the hour of the semantron, and not taking care of
his books, and not searching out the commemorations of the saints, let
him eat dry food 50 days. 57 The cook, if he neglect his ministry and
not with fear and faith have all things cooked well, he ought not to
drink wine. 58 The same again if he not cover his vessels with a
covering, or leave them unwashed, let him be penanced 3 days, eating dry
food. 59 He who sits at table and jests or finds fault with the foods
set before him, let him be driven from the table, and let him be
penanced also to chant the 50th, and let him stand at the table, and let
him make 50 prostrations. 60 He who sends or who receives anything
whatsoever secretly from the abbot, let him eat dry food 3 days.
61 He who does not confess his deeds and thoughts to his spiritual
father or to the abbot, let him be penanced 15 days, eating dry food.
62 He who through negligence is left behind at the time of common
teaching and spiritual admonition, let him eat dry food 5 days. 63 He
who tastes anything whatsoever at the hours without the blessing of the
superior, let him be penanced 15 days, not to drink wine. 64 He who has
his own property apart from his spiritual father, let it be taken from
him and let him be penanced 40 days. 65 He who does not work, let him
not eat, according to the divine Apostle; if he work moderately, let him
also eat moderately. 66 He who eats and drinks without measure does not
serve the Lord, but makes a god of his own belly. 67 If any bishop
strives for livelihood and neglects the temple of God, let him be as a
heretic, as the Lord also says: "No one can serve two masters." 68 If
any presbyter fall into fornication, let him be deposed without
advancement; but let him be shown sympathy regarding the penances
according to the word of the Lord: "For you shall not punish twice for
the same thing." 69 If any deacon commit fornication, let him be
deposed, and let him have the place of a reader. 70 Likewise also the
priest, and let him be shown sympathy with the same penance. 72 If the
wife of any deacon or priest fall, let they themselves be deposed, and
remain without advancement; likewise also a reader's; but from the
penances let them be shown sympathy, communicating without hindrance, if
they keep themselves. 73 If any bishop become possessed of this dignity
through money, or presbyter or deacon, let both be deposed; let them
also be deprived of the church, as Simon Magus by Peter.
