Of the Damascene.
=95.233=
Does the God and Father of all will insofar as he is Father, or insofar as he
is God? But if insofar as Father, his will will be other than that of the Son;
for the Son is not Father. But if insofar as God, and the Son also is God and
the Holy Spirit; therefore the will is of nature, that is natural; and you
then, having reasoned according to this, ask: Did the God of all receive the
blood of the Only-begotten insofar as Father, or insofar as God? But if
insofar as Father, his power and energy will be other than that of the Son;
for the Son is not Father. But if insofar as God, and the Son also is God and
the Holy Spirit; therefore the same holy Trinity, insofar as God, received the
blood of the Only-begotten, of the one of the holy Trinity.
