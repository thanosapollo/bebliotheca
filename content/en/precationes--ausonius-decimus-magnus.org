JANUARY, THE FASCES HAVING BEEN TAKEN
JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.  [omitted]

[omitted]

about to see the Latin curule of the consul Ausonius,
have you now from August majesty anything second
that you may wonder at? That Rome and the house of Quirinus
and the purple toga, ruddying, bordered of the senate
with this peak marks its times in eternal fasti.

JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.

Year, begun with good auspices, give to a wholesome spring
sunny souls of winds, give to Cancer dewy
solstices and a chilly Boreas in September hours,
let a subtle cold bite with autumn frosts
and let summer, thinned, cease with moderate delays,
let Notus moisten the sowing, let winter be snowy,
while father March of the ancient year is renewed.

JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.

Let a new grace of flowers breathe for scented May,
and let July cook the crops and temper the sea with Euri,
let Sirius not increase burning Leo with fire,
let many-colored Pomona vary the tree flavors,

let autumn soften what summer has ripened,
and let genial winter enjoy a dowry born for itself,
let the world do peace nor let turbid stars reign.

JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.

Let no star offend your household gods, Gradivus,
which is not equal to you; not Cynthia, not swift Arcas
neighbor to the lands; not you, Saturn, last
in a highest circuit: far removed from Pyroeis
you will hasten a tranquil way. You go close,
star of health-bringing Jove and Cytherean Vesper:
let Cyllenius never easy to guests be absent.
JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.
The enemies having been tamed, where Francia mixed with Suebi
contends toward obedience, that it may soldier with Latin arms,
where the wandering Sarmatian had joined to himself the bands of the Hun,
and where the Gete with allied Alans leaped at the Hister
(this victory announces to me with swift wings):
already Augustus comes, that he may adorn our honors,
adorning by office those which he had wished to share.
JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.
Stretch golden joys, Sun, to Janus about to come:
Caesar will succeed to the fasces of Ausonius for a year,

about to have a fifth bordered [toga] of Romulean honor,
behold where my purple piles itself (hear this with mild
ears, Nemesis) after me Augustus the consul deigns to rise,
more than he seems to confer me to himself, who ordered our fasces to go before.
JANUS, COME: NEW YEAR, COME: RENEWED SUN, COME.
You grant the tropic to yield to the sun, and again that
to give the back, that a double may be varied from a tropic star
and four times a change may hasten from three signs,
drive the summer days and let the promised year of Caesar
accelerate winter delaying with nights,
if I see him, then three times and four times blessed,
then I will be twice consul, then I will touch heaven with the head.

Year, begun with good auspices, you see the fortunate
beginnings of the consul Ausonius: put forth a flashing
head, eternal Sun, and more illustrious than the accustomed kindly
light put out a purple ray of eastern light.
Year, father of things, which in the month of two-faced Janus
you roll into wintry December at an icy end,
kindly, come and add a feast, new, to old Janus.
Compel the months about to follow through twice six mouths;

go through the solemn ways and to the world twice six
courses, varying through times with equaled signs,
carry through a headlong course with eternal whirling,
so snatched by a prone pole, that Phoebus may bring
contrary moments, a part of the days having been kept,
and new repair his lights by a wintry pulse,
while the monthly horns of the moon return three times ten,
you will roll risings and settings with a turning hand,
holding the lawful going of Phoebus through the signs.
