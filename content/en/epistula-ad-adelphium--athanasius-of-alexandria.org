OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS ATHANASIUS, ARCHBISHOP
OF ALEXANDRIA, LETTER TO ADELPHIUS BISHOP AND
CONFESSOR, AGAINST THE ARIANS.
Having come upon the things written by your piety, we truly approved your
piety toward Christ, and we glorified God first of all, who has given you such
grace, so as both to have right understanding, and not to be ignorant, as far
as possible, of the wiles of the devil; but we marveled at the malice of the
heretics, seeing how they have fallen into so great a pit of impiety, as no
longer even to keep their senses, but to have their mind corrupted from every
side. And this attempt is a suggestion of the devil, and an imitation of the
lawless Jews. For just as those men, being convicted from every side, invented
pretexts against themselves, only that they might deny the Lord, and might
draw upon themselves the things prophesied; in the same manner these also,
seeing themselves everywhere posted up as a spectacle, and beholding their
heresy become abominable among all, become inventors of evils, in order that,
not ceasing from their wars against the truth, they may remain truly
Christ-fighters. =26.1073= For from where has this evil also sprung up for
them? How have they dared at all to utter this new blasphemy against the
Savior? But, as it seems, the impious man is wicked, and truly reprobate
concerning the faith. For formerly, denying the Godhead of the only-begotten
Son of God, they pretended at least to acknowledge his incarnate presence; but
now, descending little by little, they have fallen even from this conceit of
theirs, and have become godless from every side, so as neither to acknowledge
him as God, nor to believe that he has become man. For if they believed, they
would not have uttered such things as your piety wrote against them. You
therefore, beloved and truly most longed-for, have done things befitting the
ecclesiastical tradition and the piety toward the Lord, having refuted, and
having exhorted and having rebuked such men; but since, being provoked by
their father the devil, they have not known, nor understood, according to what
is written, but they walk in darkness: let them learn from your reverence that
such malice of theirs is of Valentinus and Marcion and Manichaeus; of whom
some introduced appearance instead of truth, and others, dividing the
indivisible, denied the saying, The Word became flesh, and tabernacled among
us. Why then, holding their opinions, do they not also become heirs of their
names? For it is reasonable that of those whose evil opinion they have, they
should have also the names, so that henceforth they may be called Valentinians
and Marcionites and Manichees. Perhaps even thus, being ashamed because of the
stench of the names, they may be able to perceive into how great a depth of
impiety they have fallen. And it would have been just no longer to answer
these men, according to the apostolic charge: Reject a heretical man after a
first and second admonition, knowing that such a one is perverted, and sins,
being self-condemned; especially since the prophet also says concerning such
men: The fool will speak foolish things, and his heart will understand vain
things; but since according to their leader they also go about, as lions,
seeking whom they may swallow of the simple: for this reason it has become
necessary for us to write back to your reverence, in order that the brethren,
learning again through your admonition, may condemn still more their vain
talk. We do not worship a creature; may it not be! For such error is of the
Gentiles and of the Arians; but we worship the Lord of the creation, the Word
of God made flesh. For even if the flesh itself according to itself is a part
of the creatures, =26.1076= yet it has become a body of God. And neither,
dividing such a body by itself from the Word, do we worship it; nor, wishing
to worship the Word, do we remove him from the flesh; but knowing, as we said
before, the saying, The Word became flesh, we acknowledge this one, even
having become in flesh, as God. Who then is so foolish as to say to the Lord:
Depart from the body, that I may worship you? Or who is so impious as, with
the foolish Jews, to say to him because of the body: Why do you, being a man,
make yourself God? But the leper was not such; for he worshiped God being in a
body, and he knew that he was God, saying: Lord, if you will, you can make me
clean. And neither because of the flesh did he think the Word of God a
creature; nor because the Word is Craftsman of all creation did he despise the
flesh which he had put on; but as in a created temple he worshiped the Creator
of the universe, and was cleansed. Thus also the woman with the issue of
blood, having believed and having touched only the hem, was healed; and the
sea, foaming with waves, heard the Word made flesh, and has ceased from the
storm; and the man blind from birth was healed by the spittle of the flesh
from the Word. And the greater and more paradoxical thing (for perhaps this
also scandalized the most impious), even while the Lord was hanging upon the
very cross (for the body was his, and in it was the Word), the sun was
darkened, and the earth trembled, the rocks were split, and the veil of the
temple was split, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep arose.
And these things came to be, and no one, as now the Arians dare, reasoned
whether one ought to obey the Word made flesh; but even seeing a man, they
recognized him as being their Craftsman; and hearing a human voice, they did
not because of the human thing say that the Word is a creature; but they even
trembled the more, and none the less knew that he was speaking from a holy
temple. How then do the impious not fear lest, as they did not approve to have
God in knowledge, they be given over to a reprobate mind, to do the things
that are not fitting? For the creation does not worship a creature; nor again
because of the flesh did it refuse to worship its own Lord, but it saw its own
Craftsman in a body; and in the name of Jesus Christ every knee bent, and will
bend, of those in heaven =26.1077= and on earth, and under the earth, and
every tongue will confess, even if it does not seem good to the Arians, that
Jesus Christ is Lord to the glory of God the Father. For the flesh did not
bring dishonor to the Word; may it not be! but rather it has been glorified by
him. Nor, because the Son who exists in the form of God took the form of a
servant, was he lessened from the Godhead; but rather he himself has become
liberator of all flesh and of all creation. And if God sent his Son, born of a
woman, the matter does not bring shame upon us, but rather good repute and
great grace. For he became man, in order that he might deify us in himself;
and he came to be from a woman, and has been born of a Virgin, in order that
he might transfer our strayed generation into himself, and we might become
henceforth a holy race, and partakers of divine nature, as the blessed Peter
wrote; And the impossibility of the law, in which it was weak through the
flesh, God, having sent his own Son in the likeness of flesh of sin, and
concerning sin, condemned sin in the flesh. How then do those who set at
nought the flesh assumed by the Word, for the freeing of all men, and the
raising of all from the dead, and the redeeming from sin, or those who because
of this accuse the Son of God as a thing made or a creature, not appear
ungrateful, and are they not worthy of all hatred? For they all but cry out to
God saying: Do not send your only-begotten Son in flesh; do not make him take
flesh from a Virgin, lest he redeem us from death and from sin. We do not wish
him to come to be in a body, lest he take upon himself the death on our
behalf; we do not want the Word to become flesh, lest in this he become our
mediator of the approach to you and we dwell in the mansions in the heavens.
Let the gates of the heavens be shut, lest through the veil of his flesh your
Word inaugurate for us the way in the heavens. These voices of theirs are put
forth with devilish daring, through which they invented malice for themselves;
for those who do not wish to worship the Word become flesh are ungrateful
toward his becoming man; and those who divide the Word from the flesh think
that there has not come to be even one redemption from sin, nor a destruction
of death. And where at all will the impious find even by itself the flesh
which the Savior took, so that they may dare also to say: We do not worship
the Lord with the flesh; but we divide the body, and we worship Him alone?
Therefore the blessed Stephen saw the Lord in heaven standing at the right;
and the angels said to the disciples: Thus he will come in the manner in which
you beheld =26.1080= him going into heaven. And the Lord himself has said,
addressing the Father: I will that where I am, they also may be with me
always. And in general, if the flesh is indivisible from the Word, how is it
not necessary that these men put away the error, and worship henceforth the
Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ; or, not worshiping, nor serving
the Word become in flesh, that they be cast out from every side, and no longer
be numbered as Christians, but either as Gentiles, or with the Jews? Such
then, as we said before, is their madness and daring; but our faith is right,
and starting from apostolic teaching and tradition of the Fathers, confirmed
from both New and Old Testament, the prophets saying: Send out your Word and
your truth; and, Behold the Virgin shall have in the womb and shall bear a
Son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which is interpreted, God with us.
And what is this but that God has come to be in flesh? And the apostolic
tradition teaches, the blessed Peter saying: Christ therefore having suffered
for us in flesh; and Paul writing: Awaiting the blessed hope, and appearing of
the glory of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ, who gave himself for us,
in order that he might redeem us from all sin, and purify for himself a
peculiar people, zealous of good works. How then has he given himself for us,
if he had not been wearing flesh? For having offered this, he has given
himself for us, in order that, having taken upon himself death in that, he
might bring to nought the devil who has the power of death. Wherefore we also
always give thanks in the name of Jesus Christ; and we do not set aside the
grace that has come to be for us through him. For the incarnate presence of
the Savior has become a ransom of death and salvation of all creation. So
then, beloved and most longed-for, let those who love the Lord be reminded
from these things; but let those who have imitated the manner of Judas, and
have left the Lord, in order that they may be with Caiaphas, be re-instructed
in these things, if perhaps they should will, if perhaps they should be
ashamed. And let them know that, worshiping the Lord in flesh, we do not
worship a creature, but the Creator who has put on the created body, as we
said before. And we wished your reverence to ask this of them: when Israel was
commanded to go up to Jerusalem to worship in the temple of the Lord, where
the ark was and above it the cherubim of glory overshadowing the mercy-seat,
did they do well or the contrary? If then they acted badly, how did those who
neglected this law come under penalty? For it is written: Whoever shall set it
at nought and shall not go up, shall be utterly destroyed from the people; but
if they did well =26.1081=, and in this became well-pleasing to God: how are
the polluted Arians, most shameful of every heresy, not worthy many times to
have perished, because, accepting the people of old on account of the honor
toward the temple, they do not wish to worship the Lord in flesh, as being in
a temple? And yet the temple of old was constructed of stones and gold, as a
shadow; but when the truth came, the type has henceforth ceased, and there did
not remain, according to the Lord's voice, stone upon stone in it which was
not thrown down. And neither, seeing the temple of stones, did they think that
the Lord speaking in that temple was also a creature, nor, setting the temple
at nought, going away far off did they worship; but coming into it they
lawfully served God who gave oracles from the temple. And this having come to
be thus, how is the body of the Lord, the all-holy and truly all-venerable,
announced by the archangel Gabriel, and formed by the Holy Spirit, and become
a garment of the Word, not to be worshiped? At any rate, stretching forth a
bodily hand, the Word raised the woman with fever; and having sent forth a
human voice, he raised Lazarus from the dead; and again stretching forth the
hands upon the cross, he cast down the ruler of the authority of the air, who
now works in the sons of disobedience, and he was making the way in the
heavens clean for us. Therefore he who dishonors the temple dishonors the Lord
in the temple; and he who divides the Word from the body sets aside the grace
given to us in him. And let the most impious Ariomaniacs not, because the body
is created, think that the Word also is a creature, nor because the Word is
not a creature, slander his body. For one must marvel at their malice, that
they both confuse and mix all things, and invent pretexts, only that they may
number the Creator with the creatures. But let them hear: If the Word were a
creature, he would not have assumed the created body, in order that he might
make it alive. For what help will there be for creatures from a creature,
which itself also needs salvation? But since, being Creator, the Word himself
has become Craftsman of the creatures; for this reason also at the
consummation of the ages he himself put on the created thing, in order that
again he himself, as Creator, might renew it, and might be able to recover
this. But a creature would never be saved by a creature, just as neither were
the creatures created by a creature, unless =26.1084= the Word were Creator.
Wherefore let them not lie against the divine Scriptures, nor scandalize the
simple of the brethren; but if they wish, let them also repent, and no longer
worship the creation rather than the God who created all things; but if they
wish to remain in their impieties, let them alone be filled with these things,
and let them gnash their teeth according to their father the devil, because
the faith of the catholic Church knows the Word of God as Creator and
Craftsman of all things, and we know that In the beginning was the Word, and
the Word was with God; and we worship him who became also man for our
salvation, not as having become equal as a peer of the body, but as Master who
has assumed the form of the servant, and Craftsman and Creator become in a
creature, in order that, having freed all things in it, he might bring the
world to the Father, and make peace of all things, the things in the heavens
and the things upon the earth. For thus we both acknowledge his paternal
Godhead, and we worship his incarnate presence, even if the Ariomaniacs burst
themselves. Greet all who love our Lord Jesus Christ. We pray that you be in
health and remember us to the Lord, beloved and as truly most longed-for, if
there is need for these things to be read to Hieracas the presbyter.
