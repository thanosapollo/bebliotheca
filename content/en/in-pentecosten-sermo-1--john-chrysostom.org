On Pentecost. Sermon 1.
=52.803=
The earth has become heaven for us today, not because stars have come down
from heaven to earth, but because apostles have gone up above the heavens,
since the grace of the Spirit has been poured out. He made the inhabited world
a heaven, not by changing the nature, but by setting the will aright. He found
a tax-collector, and he made him an evangelist; he found a blasphemer, and he
made him an apostle; he found a robber, and he brought him into paradise; he
found a harlot, and he made her more honorable than a virgin; he found magi,
and he made them evangelists. He cast out wickedness, and he brought virtue
back; he brought the grace of God. The earth has become heaven. For what are
stars, compared with apostles? Stars are in heaven; apostles are above heaven.
Set your minds on the things above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand
of God. Stars are from sensible fire; apostles from spiritual fire. Stars
shine by night, and by day they are hidden; but apostles shine their own ray
by night and by day. Stars, when the sun is shining, do not shine; but
apostles, when the Sun of righteousness is shining, flash forth their own
radiances. Stars at the resurrection fall like leaves; but apostles at the
resurrection are caught up in clouds. Among the stars one is the evening star,
another the morning star; but among the apostles there is no evening star, but
all are morning stars.
For this very reason the apostles are brighter than the stars; and no one
would err in calling them luminaries of the inhabited world, not only when
they lived, but even now when they have died. For the grace of the saints is
not cut off by death, is not blunted by their end, is not dissolved into the
earth; and the facts bear witness. They were fishermen, and the fishermen have
died; but their nets are still at work; and the multitude of those who are
saved day by day bears witness. They were vinedressers, and they have gone
away, and the vine is thick with leaves and heavy with fruit. For they were
vinedressers, and fishermen, and pillars, and physicians, and generals, and
teachers. Pillars, because they bore up the roof of the faith; =52.804=
harbors, because they stilled the waves of impiety; helmsmen, because they
piloted the inhabited world from earth to heaven; shepherds, because they
drove off the wolves and saved the flock; vinedressers, because they pulled up
the thorns and sowed the seeds of piety; physicians, because they set our
wounds right. And that you may learn that the words are not a boast, I bring
forward Paul, who did all these things. Do you want to see him as a planter? I
planted, Apollos watered. Do you want to see him as a builder? As a wise
master-builder I have laid a foundation. Do you want to see him as a
pancratiast? So I box, not as one beating the air. Do you want to see him as
an athlete? Our wrestling is not against blood and flesh. Do you want to see
him as a runner? So that from Jerusalem and round about as far as Illyricum I
have fulfilled the Gospel of God. Having put on the breastplate of faith. Do
you want to see him as a combatant? I have fought the good fight. Do you want
to see him as a crowned victor? Henceforth there is laid up for me the crown
of righteousness. Being one, he became all things, imitating his own Master.
For our Master too, being one in substance, was fashioned toward all things.
How toward all things? For our salvation. You became a branch, and he became a
root: For I am the vine, you are the branches. You wished to walk; he became a
way for you: If anyone enters through me and goes out, he will find pasture.
You became a sheep; he became a shepherd for you: For I am the good shepherd;
the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. You became a sinner; he
became for you the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. You wished
to eat; he became a table for you. You wished to drink; he became a cup for
you. He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood abides in me, and I in him. You
wished to be clothed; he became a garment for you: For as many of you as were
baptized into Christ have put on Christ. You wished to become a bride; he
became a bridegroom for you: =52.805= He who has the bride is the bridegroom.
You wished it; he became a husband for you: For I betrothed you to one
husband, to present you as a chaste virgin to Christ. You were a harlot, and
you became a virgin. O new and strange things! Among those outside, marriage
undoes virginity; but among spiritual things he took a harlot, and he made her
more honorable than a virgin. Interpret, O heretic, how the harlot is a
virgin. I cannot pry into it; but I am held by faith. And all these things the
grace of the Spirit has done. The grace was poured out, And there appeared to
them tongues being distributed as of fire: not of fire, but As of fire.
If it was fire, how were they not burned? the Jew asks.
Rather I ask the Jew, how did it not burn the bush, a wood of dry brush?
What is fiercer than fire? What is more worthless than a bush? And neither was
the wood burned, nor was the fire quenched. And again, how did it not consume
the bodies of the three children, but the furnace was changed into dew, and
they thought the coals a rose-garden? There was a battle of fire and bodies,
and the victory went to the bodies; for being softer than wax, they became
harder than iron. Interpret for me the things of the ancients; but those
things came first, that the truth might be believed. Why is the Holy Spirit
in fire?
Because of the keenness of mind of those who receive it. And not for this
only, but also just as fire consumes the thorns, so the Spirit consumes the
sins. The holy one was thrown into a pit, and he muzzled the lions. The
fishermen went round the inhabited world, taking it sick, and they brought it
back to health; taking it in revolt, they led it to good order, not moving
spears, not shooting bows, not sending a dart, not having an abundance of
money, not of rhetorical words; but they were naked of garments, and clothed
with Christ; poor and rich, stripped of money, but possessing the kingdom;
alone and not alone, alone of men, but having their own Master. For I am, he
says, with you until the consummation of the age.
The sheep went round the whole inhabited world among the wolves. Who has seen?
Who has heard? He says: I send you as sheep in the midst of wolves. What
shepherd sends sheep in the midst of wolves? But when the shepherd sees the
wolf, he drives the sheep together; but Christ did the opposite: he sent the
sheep into the wolves, and the sheep did not become a prey of beasts, but the
wolves were changed over into the gentleness of the sheep. And lest they
should say, We are sheep, and you send us as food for the wolves, and a table
for the beasts, with one word he took away their thought. He did not say: Go
away; for he was not sending them by their own power; but, Behold, I send you.
You are weak; but he who sends you is mighty. Who are you? I am he who fixed
the heaven, who founded the earth, who broke the sea apart, who nailed to the
cross the handwriting against you, who overthrew the old law, who brought you
back to the ancient fatherland, who unstrings the strong, and strengthens what
has been unstrung. What is stronger than the sea? But the little sand holds
that unbearable force. Behold, I send you as sheep in the midst of wolves. The
beasts become a wall for the sheep, so that they may not even by flight
procure their salvation. O power of him who sent them! In the midst the sheep,
and round about the wolves; but they were changed, and they became sheep. For
they were not wolves by nature, but by choice. Behold, I send you. What then
do you command? Be wise as the serpents, and harmless as the doves. What are
you saying, Wise as the serpents? The serpent, even if it receives ten
thousand wounds, if it saves the head, is wholly sound. So you too, if the
adversary wants to take money, give it; if possessions, even life itself; only
keep your faith. Be wise. While that remains, all things are easy and ready.
Naked you came out from your mother's womb, and naked you will also go away.
But someone banishes you? The earth is the Lord's, and the fullness of it. But
you die? But =52.806= death is a sojourn abroad for you. Only do not lose the
faith, the treasure that cannot be spent, the faith that cannot be consumed.
Witnesses of these things are these martyrs; because they did not lose the
faith, even after death they are more powerful than those who live. For this I
became man, for this I took on flesh, that I might make the inhabited
world a Church.
For the Church is much sought after by God, not secured by walls, but walled
by faith. For the Church heaven was set up, and the sea was poured out, and
air was spread, and earth was founded, paradise was planted, law was given,
prophets were sent, wonders came to be, the deep was split and sewn together
again, the rock was broken, and again joined, manna was brought down, and a
table was made ready as it was being improvised. For the Church prophets, for
the Church apostles; and why do I say many things? For the Church the
only-begotten Son of God became man, as Paul says: Who did not spare his own
Son. He did not spare the Son, that he might spare the Church; he poured out
the blood of the Son for the Church. This blood waters her; therefore the
plants cannot wither, nor do they shed their leaves. She is not subject to the
necessities of seasons, nor does she serve times, thick with leaves in summer,
but stripped in winter. For it is not the necessity of seasons, but the grace
of the Spirit that tills her; therefore she is not pulled up. But it is not
wonderful that she is not pulled up, but that even when many make war she is
not pulled up. How many from the beginning have warred against the Church?
When she was newly planted, many things were moved against her. For now,
through the grace of God and the power of the Spirit, even pious emperors, and
generals, and the whole inhabited world have been filled with the faith; but
at the beginning and in the preludes, emperors in impiety, impious generals,
edicts of rulers, altars kindled, smoke everywhere and the savor of fat. The
air was polluted, the earth was defiled, demons raved in Bacchic frenzy, the
devil danced, a father killed a son, a son dishonored; nature was split, and
piety was not divided. Where now are those who dared to array themselves
against the Church? When she was newly planted, she was not harmed; when she
has touched heaven, do you expect to get the better of her? When the apostles
were eleven, no one mastered them, but the eleven netted so many myriads; now
when the sea, and the inhabited world, and the uninhabited land, and cities,
and all the ends have been filled with piety, do you then hope to master her?
But you cannot; for Christ has declared: The gates of Hades shall not prevail
against her. It is easier for heaven to vanish, and for the earth to perish,
than for the Church to suffer anything. Christ himself: Heaven and earth shall
pass away, but my words shall not pass away.
And well; for the word of God is mightier than heaven itself; for heaven is a
work of the word. God said, Let heaven come to be; and the word became a work.
Nature ran, and there was nothing that hindered. For he was Master of nature,
both to make it such, and to change it so. I built her, I who set up the
heavens; but I would not have taken a body above the heavens, that you may
learn that the Church is more honorable than heaven and angels and all
creation. For this reason Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall
not pass away. Let the Jew be present, let the Greek be present. If the words
are a boast, let him refute them; but if what has been said is truth, let him
worship the power. Christ said: Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words
shall not pass away. And what are his words? Let us put them forward in the
midst, let us see whether they have fallen through. I do not speak as one in
doubt, but to stop the heretic's mouth. For this reason, Heaven and earth
shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away. What words? You are Peter,
and upon this rock I will build my Church, and the gates of Hades shall not
prevail against her. Upon this rock. He did not say, Upon Peter; for he built
his Church not upon the man, but upon the faith. And what was the faith? You
are the Christ, =52.807= the Son of the living God. He called the Church a
rock, receiving waves, and not being shaken. For the Church too receives
temptations, but is not conquered. What then is, Upon the rock? Confession
in the words. Do you not put down stones? not woods? not iron? No, he says;
for it is not such a building, which, of whatever sort it may be, is dissolved
by time; but not even demons can conquer a confession. And the martyrs bear
witness, who have their sides scraped, and do not lose the faith. O new and
strange things. The wall is dug through, and the treasure is not plundered;
the flesh is spent, and the faith is not consumed; such is the power of the
martyrs. For upon this rock I will build my Church; and the gates of Hades
shall not prevail against her.
Attend to the precision of the saying. The gates of Hades shall not prevail
against her. For why, he says, does he not prevent the temptations from coming
upon the Church? Is it not that I may show my power? If no one wars against
her, they can say: If she had been warred against, she would have been
conquered. For this reason he lets her be warred against, so that the victory
may not be credited to the absence of those who war. And the gates of Hades:
he did not say, Shall not assault her, but, Shall not prevail against her; she
will be warred against, but she will not be conquered. The Church is
storm-tossed, but she is not sunk; she is tossed by waves, but she does not go
under water; she receives arrows, but she does not receive wounds; she
receives siege-engines, but the tower is not shaken. And why do I speak
of the Church?
The fisherman uttered one word, and it has stood as an unshaken tower. How
many tyrants strove to make this saying vanish, but they were not able? For it
had been founded upon the rock. Consider: =52.808= tyrants, emperors, diadems,
sharpened swords, teeth of beasts, deaths threatened, frying-pans, furnaces.
And indeed the devil emptied his quiver, but he did not harm the Church. Why?
The gates of Hades shall not prevail against her. How many from the beginning
have warred against the Church? Where are those who warred? They have been
silenced, and they are given over to oblivion; the Church blooms. Where is
Claudius? Where is Augustus? Where is Nero? Where is Tiberius? They are bare
names; and no one remembers these. Because they warred against the Church,
they lost even the memory; but the Church shines above the sun. Wherever you
go, to the Indians, to the Moors, to the Britons, to the inhabited world, you
will find, In the beginning was the Word, and a virtuous life. For before this
even cities were filled with impiety; but now even the desert has been filled
with piety. Before this even queens danced; and when Herod's birthday came,
the daughter of Herodias danced; but today a maidservant does not think it
worthy to do this. Before this Persians married mothers; today they practice
virginity.
Before this Goths killed fathers; today they are eager to pour out their blood
for piety. Before this even private men were impious; today even emperors are
pious, and peoples. The present emperor, having laid aside the diadem, and
taken a cross, in an untimely age, but showing hoary wisdom, in church not
recognizing imperial rank; outside spears, and here mysteries; outside
shields, and here initiations into the mysteries; son of Theodosius, and the
father of Theodosius. And for these things let us give thanks to God; to whom
be the glory and the might unto the ages of ages. Amen.
