* Fragments on the First Epistle to Timothy

458 1 Tim 3:16 And with what righteousness was he justified, who is
righteousness itself and redemption, the sun of righteousness according to the
prophet Malachi? Which signifies the most unmixed and purest righteousness,
namely the fulfillment of the legal ordinances, concerning which he also said
to John: suffer it now; for thus it is fitting for us to fulfill all
righteousness. For since the law also commanded concerning baptisms, he came
to the Jordan to fulfill these also, just as indeed circumcision and the
offerings for the firstborn. And what does "in spirit" mean? Israel was bound
by a spirit of slavery through the punishing law; for it did not have the
spirit of adoption; for this has been granted to Christians, as 459 Paul also
says: but the Jerusalem above is free, which is the mother of us all. He says
then: even if he fulfilled the legal righteousnesses, yet not by the spirit of
slavery; for how could he who also sets others free? But by the Holy Spirit of
adoption, which he received according to the human, the genuine Son procuring
the gift for us and not for himself, just as indeed he also prayed, making our
prayers acceptable, and called his own body a temple, procuring that we become
temples of God. 1 Tim 5:4 That is, to dispose well. 1 Tim 5:9 And they say he
means those enrolled in the order of widows. 1 Tim 5:10 He does not say: if
she had children, but: if she reared them according to the word of God, if she
brought them up as she ought; for one must understand this to have been said
as something exceptional concerning the widows. 1 Tim 5:13 And how do they
learn to be idle? Having the imported men supplying them with what is needed,
they are lazy about works, but they take care how they may appear fair-faced
to the men. 1 Tim 5:21 Either he said "the being," or by way of contrast with
those who were cast out. 1 Tim 5:24-25 I know having read such an
interpretation in one of the saints: of whom, he says, the sins go before,
these are manifest to all; therefore let them be so to you also. But those
whom they follow, these you shall watch with the foreseeing eye. If therefore
you do anything contrary to this, you will share in the sins. But the blessed
Basil interpreted it thus, as if the chapter tends nothing toward ordination:
those, he says, who themselves sinned, they alone have the sins going before
unto judgment. But as many as even after departing from here have left to
others 460 occasions of sins, such as Nestorius and the rest of the heretics,
to these even after they have departed from here the sins follow; for of those
who go astray because of them the sins are reckoned also to those, and
likewise also the good works, that is similarly. Whatever, he says, has been
said concerning sins, receive these also upon the good works; for there are
those who have become an occasion of salvation to others. Therefore the
achievements of these are also credited to those who gave the seeds. 1 Tim
6:16 Who alone has immortality. Interrogatively: how does he say concerning
God: who alone has immortality, when also angels and souls and demons have
immortality? He therefore who says: who alone has immortality concerning God,
either takes away immortality from those mentioned, or he lies. We shall say
that these of whom the word is do not have immortality, but partake of
immortality; for they do not have it by nature, but they partake of it; for
this has been granted to them from God; for God is he of whom we all partake,
just as of the other things conceived toward the better, so also of
immortality.
