HOMILY XVII. ON THE PARABLE OF THE VINEYARD.
The kingdom of heaven was likened to a man, a householder, who went out
early in the morning to hire laborers into his vineyard. You have heard,
beloved, the evangelical parable of the Lord; hear also the interpretation
which we shall speak according to our power. Householder of the great
house, =77.1097= that is, the Master God; for the house is the world; and
its Master, God who created it; his vineyard, human nature; laborers of
the vineyard, as many saints as minister to the salvation of men; the
first hour the time of those God-fearing ones around Abel and Enoch and
Noah; for these were first God-fearing, these first laborers of the
vineyard. For Noah planted a vineyard: understand it intellectually; for
the words are spiritual; these worked until the third hour; after the
third hour the time of those around Abraham and Isaac and Jacob; for these
too are laborers of our salvation, three in number; therefore they also
worked until the sixth hour; from the sixth hour, the very middle of the
day, when the sun more fiercely shines upon the inhabited world, the time
of the law; because in truth our God, the true light, enlightened humanity
through the commandments of the law.
For the commandment of the Lord is far-shining, enlightening the eyes. And
again, that Your ordinances are light. From the sixth hour, the half of
the day; for when the times from the beginning of creation even until the
consummation had reached the middle, then at midday Moses and Aaron were
sent. For indeed the worship according to the law, until the eighth; and
the ninth hour, the time of the Spirit-bearing prophets; for they too as
good laborers were sent by the householder to work the vineyard of our
soul until the tenth. The eleventh hour, the sojourn of the Only-begotten;
for at the last of the times, at the consummation of the ages, the Son of
God, Word of God, sojourned among us; for just as from the eleventh to the
twelfth there is one other hour, the last, so from the sojourn of Christ
until the consummation there is one other hour. Hear John saying: Little
children, it is the last hour. You see that at the eleventh, the master of
the vineyard having gone out, finding idle ones, hired them. Who are these
idle ones? We who are from the nations; for we were idle from knowledge of
God, idle from good works. And he says to them: Why have you stood here
all the day idle? All the day he signified the time from the beginning of
the creation of the world until his sojourn; for until then the nations
were idle, not knowing God; For no one, they say, hired us. The nations
are fair-minded; for no one hired them, neither laws nor prophets; those
were sent to Israel, not to the nations; wherefore they say well that No
one hired us. But the householder is good, For go you also, he says, to
work in the vineyard, and whatever is just, I will give you. The idle then
became laborers; for this Church, once idle, now works; the choir of the
apostles works, of the martyrs, of the ascetics, of those living as monks,
of the holy virgins, of the continent, of those in honorable marriage; for
this Church received the one denarius worthily. For someone working keenly
is able in one hour to bring a work as one working from morning; the thief
=77.1100= in one hour heard, Today you will be with me in paradise.
But hear what those say: We who have borne the burden and the heat of the
day; They speak truly; for they did not in the law bear the burden of the
yoke of the law, but these also became equal to those through the work;
Friend, for, he says, I do not wrong you. The judge of righteousness does
not wrong; and the I wish to grant to this one as also to you, do not
think it to be respect of persons, nor that the wage is given according to
favor, but understand thus: since however we may do, however we may work,
we do nothing worthy; for all righteousness of man is as a rag of a woman
sitting apart; he named the whole grace, as Paul says, Being justified
freely by his grace, and not of works, that no one should boast; the gift
of God, not because we do not work, but because we do nothing according to
worth; For whenever, he says, you have done all these things, say that we
are unprofitable servants; what we were bound to do, we have done. At any
rate then you give bread to the poor, and you inherit the kingdom of
heaven: you see the I wish to grant. And the, Or is your eye evil, because
I am good?
He did not say that the saints are evil, but are you perhaps evil, because
I am good? For truly good from good; not according to the work also the
wage; for the work is little, but the wage is much. You gave a cup of
water to one thirsty to drink, and you receive what eye has not seen, nor
ear heard; you see that he is good; and the beginning from the last until
the first, since the first shall be last; and I will call my people, my
people. And again, When the fullness of the nations has entered, then also
all Israel shall be saved, in Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom be the glory
and the power unto the ages of ages. Amen.
