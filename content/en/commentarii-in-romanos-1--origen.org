Letters of Paul the apostle. To the Romans. 1.1 Paul, a slave of Jesus Christ,
a called apostle, set 1.2 apart for the gospel of God, which he promised

name, among whom you also are called of Jesus 1.7 Christ, to all who are in
Rome, beloved of God, called saints; grace to you and peace from God our
Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. 1.8 First I thank my God through Jesus
Christ concerning all of you, because your faith is proclaimed in the whole
1.9 world. For God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the gospel of

law only but also to the one from faith =4.17= of Abraham, who is father of us

=4.24= only that it was reckoned to him, but also for us, to whom it is about

the dead, who was delivered over because of our trespasses and was raised

body so as to obey its desires =6.13=, nor present your members as weapons of

=8.26= through endurance we wait for it. And likewise also the Spirit helps
together with the weakness


of us; for what we should pray as we ought we do not know, but the Spirit

things work together unto good, to those who are called according to =8.29=
purpose. Because whom he foreknew, he also predestined to be conformed to the

things we more than conquer through the one who loved us. =8.38= For I am
persuaded that neither death nor life nor angels nor =8.39= rulers nor things
present nor things to come nor powers nor height nor depth nor any other
creation will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ
Jesus our Lord. 10.1 Brothers, the good pleasure of my heart and the petition
10.2 toward God for them is unto salvation. For I bear them witness that 10.3
they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge; for being ignorant of
the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own, they did not
submit to the righteousness 10.4 of God. For Christ is the end of law unto
10.5 righteousness to everyone who believes. For Moses writes that the man who
does the righteousness that is from the law will live in 10.6 it. And the
righteousness from faith speaks thus: Do not say in your heart: Who will go up
into the heaven? that is, to bring Christ 10.7 down; or: Who will go down into
the abyss? that 10.8 is, to bring Christ up from the dead. But what does it

and with the mouth it is confessed =10.11= unto salvation. For the scripture

And how will they hear =10.15= without one who preaches? And how will they
