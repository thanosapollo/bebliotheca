* Homily on the Feast of Palms
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Of our father among the saints Epiphanius, bishop of Cyprus, on the Palms.
Lord, bless.
Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion; take delight and exult and be glad, all the
Church of God; for behold, again the King comes to you. Behold, your
bridegroom comes, seated upon a colt as upon a throne. Let us go out to meet
him, let us hasten to see his glory, let us go before him brightly in honor of
his entrance. Again salvation for the world, again God comes toward the cross.
Again the king of Zion, the expectation of the nations, comes upon Zion; again
he grants salvation to the world; again the light stands near; again error has
ceased; again the truth blossoms; again the Church dances; again the Synagogue
is left a widow; again demons are put to shame; again the curse is undone;
again the Hebrews are troubled; again the dragon is crushed; again the nations
rejoice; again Zion is adorned. Christ comes seated upon a colt as upon a
throne; heavens, exult; angels, hymn; mountains, be glad; hills, leap; rivers,
clap; you who are in Zion, dance; Churches, rejoice; priests, hymn; prophets,
go before; disciples, proclaim; peoples, go to meet him; elders, run together;
mothers, dance; you who nurse, sing; young men, cry out; nations, be gathered.
Let every creation, every nature, every rank, every breath, all the earth,
every dignity, every age, every ethnarchy, every kingdom, meet the King of
kings in kingly fashion, worship the Master of masters in a manner befitting a
master, sing to the God of gods in a manner befitting God, and dance to the
=43.429= bridegroom of incorruption in a manner befitting a divine bride;
being bright, let us kindle the lamps brightly; let us change the tunics of
our souls in a manner befitting God; let us prepare well the ways of our life;
let us open the entrances of our souls to the King; let us bear the palms of
victory as to the conqueror of death, shaking the branches of the olives in a
manner befitting God toward the branch from Mary; let us hymn the things of
angels to the God of the angels; with the children let us cry out in a manner
befitting God; with the crowd let us, the crowds too, shout the things of the
crowd: Hosanna in the highest. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the
Lord; God is the Lord, and he has appeared to us; he has appeared to us who
sit in darkness and in the shadow of death. There has appeared the
resurrection of those who have fallen; there has appeared the deliverance of
the captives; there has appeared the recovery of sight of the blind; there has
appeared the consolation of those who mourn; there has appeared the rest of
those who toil; there has appeared the refreshment of those who thirst; there
has appeared the vindication of those who are worn down; there has appeared
the redemption of those who have despaired; there has appeared the consolation
of those who have despaired; there has appeared the union of those who are
separated; there has appeared the vindication of those who are worn down;
there has appeared the healing of those who are sick; there has appeared the
calm of those who are storm-tossed.

Therefore we also, the peoples, with the crowd let us cry out, saying today to
Christ, Hosanna, that is, Save now, O God in the highest. O new and
paradoxical deeds and wonders! Yesterday Christ raised Lazarus from the dead,
and today the same one comes toward death. Yesterday, as life, he granted life
to another, and today the giver of life comes toward death. Yesterday he
loosed the grave-bands of Lazarus, and today the same one comes willingly to
be bound in grave-bands. Yesterday he led a man out of darkness, and today he
comes to be laid in dark places and in the shadow of death for the sake of
man. Yesterday, six days before the Pascha, through the five senses the One of
three days grants to the two the one brother of four days, and today he comes
toward the



cross. But to Mary he grants a dead man of four days; to the Church
Christ grants himself of three days. There Bethany alone marvels; here the
whole Church keeps feast. She keeps the feasts of feasts, having in her midst
the king of the bodiless ones as bridegroom and king together; she keeps
feasts, as a fruitful olive in the house of God, covering densely without
shedding her leaves; she keeps feasts, being as a spring lily of paradise, in
whom is Christ, the truly flourishing lily, who does not =43.432= judge, but
saves the world. In whom is Christ, the violet that truly heals the passions
of the sick; in whom is the vineyard, who says: I am the true vine; in whom is
the olive-grove, who truly has mercy on those who hope in him; in whom the
pre-eternal branch from the root of Jesse has sprouted, without tillage and
without digging of human seed; in whom is the ever-flowing fountain; in whom
not Phison, Geon, Tigris, Euphrates, but Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, who
water the paradise of the Church of Christ.

Where all the youth today, holding the olives as a fruitful olive, ask
merciful Christ; planted in the house of the Lord, blossoming in springtime in
his courts, we keep a feast, seeing the winter of the Law withdrawn. Keep
feast, Church of Christ, dancing not in type, not bodily, but spiritually.
Keep feasts, seeing the fall of the idols, and having the rising of the
churches. For with Paul I cry to you with the holy and great voice: The old
things have passed away; behold, all things have become new; and indeed
paradoxical too. Therefore rejoice in the Lord, youth of Christ, rejoice; may
you rejoice, Church of Christ; take delight and be glad, daughter of God. For
all your glory, as of the daughter of the king Christ, is within and without
together; no longer husbandless, but blossoming God-wedded; no longer from the
left of God because of idolatry, but standing at the right, and adorned
because of the knowledge of God; no longer mixed with slavish blood, but
sealed with divine blood; no longer worshipping Obel, but Emmanuel; no longer
glorifying the Troad, but the Trinity; no longer honoring Plato, but our
almighty God; no longer Heracles the averter of evil, but the Paraclete and
maker of all. No longer do you worship Aristotle who made you wise, but God
who has saved you unto the ends of the ages. Cronus has fallen, because the
God Word has been made flesh; not indeed from a man's passion, but appearing
from Mary in a God-manly manner. See the grace of the day; see the brightness
of the feast. Rejoice therefore and be glad, daughter of Zion; take delight
and exult, all the Church of God. Lift up your eyes round about, and see
gathered your children of the nations, once scattered. See the acclamation of
the feast; see the concord of the people; see every tongue unto one glory; see
every mouth as one mouth; see those who were formerly beast-nations now
becoming sheep in you; see the festal cry of nations keeping feast, being the
imitation of the angelic bodiless ones; =43.433= see concord, a choir-array of
the bodiless; see stations as ranks of angels. I see the hymns as hymns of
angels; I see the children as newly planted lambs, saying to Christ, Hosanna
in the highest, blessed is he who comes; with whom, having clapped greatly
with the hand, cry out with a bright and lion-like voice, saying things both
festal and of thanksgiving together: Behold I and the children whom God has
given me, to her who was once childless and an unknowing barren woman; blessed
is he who has come, and indeed who comes in the name of the Lord; God is the
Lord and he has appeared to us. He who comes, who is contained nowhere;
coming, who is held nowhere; blessed is he who comes, and is not separated
from the heavens; blessed in a manner befitting himself, and again coming in a
manner befitting God; blessed is he who has come upon me upon a colt in type,
as upon a cherubic colt.



For hear what the sacred herald, the evangelist of the feast, says: And
it came to pass, when the Lord drew near to Bethphage and to Bethany, toward
the mount called of Olives, he sent two of his disciples, saying: Go into the
village opposite, and as you enter you will find an ass and a colt tied;
having loosed them, bring them to me. And the disciples did as Jesus commanded
them; and they spread their garments upon the colt, and Jesus sat. And it came
to pass as he drew near to the descent of the mountain, the crowd that had
come to the feast took palm-branches of the palms, and went out to meet Jesus.
And those who went before, and those who followed, cried out: Hosanna to the
son of David, blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord. This is the
Master's presence of the present feast; this is the sojourn in Zion, of old
and now, of the King of those who reign; this is the bright and public coming
of the maker of all things on the present day.

Therefore now, brethren, the crowd having come to the feast, let us go out to
meet him, both the visible world and the invisible, and the prophets who go
before in time, and teachers, and those who follow the colt, who agree with
God by faith. Today let the things of heaven hymn together with the things on
earth and the things under the earth; every mouth and spirit being opened
toward the praise of God. Cherubim, cry out, Holy, holy, holy Lord, the
thrice-holy, Sabaoth; heaven and earth are full of his glory; seraphim, hymn;
prophets, proclaim. And let one say: Let the heavens be glad, and let the
earth exult; and another: Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion, proclaim,
daughter of Jerusalem; and another, gazing at the king Christ, let him cry:
Behold the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world; and another
concerning the Lord himself let him proclaim: This is our God; another shall
not be reckoned beside him. =43.436= And another: Behold a man and God
together. Dayspring is his name; and let David, gazing at Christ who is from
him according to the flesh, say: God is the Lord, and he has appeared to us;
and again another, bending the knee, let him cry to Christ: Let all the earth
worship you; and another let him charge the peoples, saying: Appoint a feast
among those who cover densely, even to the horns of the altar.

For thus of old also was accomplished the sojourn of the Master in Zion with
the colt: a public concord, the choirs of the fathers, the peoples of the
righteous, the spirits of the prophets, the children of the Hebrews, the
infants of the mothers, the multitudes of the angels. Some spread wings,
others held palms, others came from behind, others cut the branches, others
wove the shoots, some loosed the colt, others spread tunics, some opened the
gates, others cleared the roads, others made ready a colt, others proclaimed
victory, others shook the branches, others said to infants, Praise the Lord,
you children; the children answered, Hosanna, blessed is he who comes in the
name of the Lord. O new and paradoxical deeds and wonders of the feast!
Children theologize Christ as God, and the priests blaspheme him; those who
nurse worship, and those who teach are impious; the children say Hosanna, and
the Hebrews cry Crucify him; some come to Christ with palms; others come to
him with swords; these cut branches, those make ready the wood of the cross;
infants spread tunics under Christ, and the priests divide tunics for Christ;
infants lift Christ upon a colt, and old men hang Christ upon a cross; the
children worshipped the feet of Christ, but those nailed the feet of Christ
with the nails; children offer praise, these offer vinegar; children the honor
to Christ, these the gall; children shake palms, these pierce with a sword;
children hymn Christ upon a colt, these sell the one upon the colt.

The ox knew him who acquired it, born at a manger, and the new people of the
nations was subjected to their Lord, but Israel alone did not know Christ his
God.



The tribes of old have become God-loving, and the lawless have become
lawful, and the lawful lawless. Be it so: you did not reverence the prophets,
you killed priests, you twisted the Scriptures, you dissolved the law, you
sawed the righteous, you set Moses aside, you slaughtered the sons, you
defiled the temple, you disobeyed God, you did not believe Christ, you
insulted the divine signs, you disbelieved concerning Lazarus, you did not
believe the blind who recovered sight; then what do you say concerning these
children? what do you legislate concerning the hymn of those who nurse? who,
tell me, enlightened these? who taught them? =43.437= or who raised them up?
who made them wise? who suddenly gave speech to those inexperienced in speech,
if not Christ the pre-eternal Word? Now the young, children and infants
together, holding the breast with one hand, and with the voice singing an
angelic hymn; holding the breast with one hand, and with the other hand
shaking palms toward Christ; the nature inexperienced in speech, having become
a theologian; at once offering the prophetic word to God as a hymn of the
bodiless ones as gifts, and crying together, Hosanna in the highest, blessed
is he who comes in the name of the Lord, God is the Lord, and he has appeared
to us.

Toward which things, both very joyful and festal, I, having taken courage, ask
these sacred children in turn: What do you say, O children of God, children
who hymn? Whence is this well-balanced hymn of yours, as you cry out the
cherubic hymn? And how, seeing Christ upon a colt in a manner befitting man,
do you cry Hosanna in the highest in a manner befitting God? Yes, say the
God-speaking children to us, Christ is seated also upon a perceptible colt,
but he is nowhere separated from the paternal bosom; and he is seated upon the
colt of an ass, but he does not leave the cherubic throne. But he himself
below with flesh to mortals without deficiency, he himself thus also above
with the Father, God who does not lie, and true; he himself the almighty, and
world-ruler, and ruler of the nations, and true God, and giver, and planter,
and guide, and Savior of all.

He himself enters into the Jerusalem below, and is not separated from the one
above; this is the maker of the ages and from ages, and unto ages; he himself
is the one who stretched out the heaven alone; this is he who walks upon the
sea as upon ground; this one swaddled the sea with mist; this one set the bars
around for men; this one assigned bounds to the seas; this one, having spread
out the earth upon nothing, hung it; this one mixed the mixtures of the
elements all-wisely; this one adorned the beauties of the plants; this one
stretched out heaven as a garment; this one adorned it with bright luminaries.

This one the cherubim shudder at, and the seraphim tremble; this one the sun
hymns; this one the moon glorifies; this one the stars praise; to this one the
springs serve; this one the abysses shudder at; this one the tartaruses fear;
to this one the sea-monsters yield; this one the dragons tremble at; to this
one the rains serve; this one the spirits reverence; this one established
natures; this one set creations in place; this one arranged ranks; this one is
the maker of the creations above and below, God our Lord; and to him be the
glory and the might unto the ages of the ages. Amen.
