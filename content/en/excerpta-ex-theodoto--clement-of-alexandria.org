And when the Apostle says: "Since what will they do who are baptized for the

=1.22=.4 But "those baptized", they say, "for us the dead" are the Angels who

help on behalf of Sophia left outside, and by the good pleasure of the Aeons
Jesus is put forth as Paraclete for the Aeon that had passed. And in the type

But they do not know that the Paraclete, who now works immediately in the

brothers through the common origin. =1.27=.6 So that of the economy it was to

become God-bearing, being worked immediately by the Lord and becoming as it

generation to those who disobey", the followers of Basilides say according to
the embodiments. But the followers of Valentinus that the three places on the
left are indicated, and fourth generation their seeds; "but doing mercy unto
thousands", upon those on the right. Silence, they say, being Mother of all
those put forth by Depth, what she did not have to say, she has kept silent
about the Ineffable, and what she grasped, this she named Incomprehensible.
Then, forgetting the glory of God, they say impiously that he suffered. For
that the Father shared in suffering, "being rigid in nature", says Theodotus,

about, the Whole also shared in suffering, for the correction of the one who
suffered. =2.31=.1 But also if he who came down was good pleasure of the Whole
("for in him all the Pleroma was bodily"), and this one suffered, it is clear
that the seeds in him also suffered with him, through which the Whole and the
All is found suffering. =2.31=.2 But also through the persuasion of the
twelfth Aeon the All "having been instructed", as they say, shared in
suffering.


=2.31=.3 For then they recognized that what they are, "they are by grace of
the Father"; a nameless Name, Form and Knowledge. But the Aeon who wished to
take that which is beyond Knowledge came to be in =2.31=.4 ignorance and
formlessness. Whence he also worked an emptiness of Knowledge, which is a
shadow of the Name; which is Son, Form of the Aeons. Thus the name of the
Aeons according to part is a loss of the Name. =2.32=.1 In the Pleroma then,
there being unity, each of the Aeons has its own pleroma, the syzygy. Whatever

=2.33=.1 with the Paraclete. Yet Christ became adopted, as having become
"Elect" in relation to the pleromas and "Firstborn" of the things here.
=2.33=.2 This account then is a mishearing of ours, which says the Savior is
"Firstborn" from the underlying; and he is as it were Root and "Head" of us;
and the Church his fruits. =2.33=.3 Of Christ, they say, having fled what was
foreign and having been drawn together into the Pleroma, having come from the
maternal thought, the Mother again brought forth the Ruler of the economy, as
a type of him who fled her, according to desire for him, he being better, who
was a type =2.33=.4 of the Father of the all. Wherefore he also becomes
lesser, as being composed from a passion of desire. Yet she "was disgusted"
having looked upon his "severity", as they themselves say. =2.34=.1 But also
the left-hand powers, put forth first of those on the right by her, are not
formed by the presence of the Light, but those on the left were left behind to
be formed by the Place. =2.34=.2 When then the Mother with the Son and the
seeds has entered into the Pleroma, then the Place will receive the authority
of the Mother and the rank which the Mother now has. =2.35=.1 Jesus, "our
Light", as the Apostle says, "having emptied himself" (that is: having come to
be outside the Limit, according to Theodotus), since he was "Angel" of the
Pleroma, led out with himself the Angels of the superior =2.35=.2 seed. And he
himself had the redemption, as having come forth from the Pleroma; but he led
the Angels for the correction of the seed =2.35=.3. For as they are in need on
behalf of a part they also plead, and being held on our account, hastening to
enter, they ask forgiveness for us, in order that we may enter with them;
=2.35=.4 for they almost have need of us, in order that they may enter, since
without us it is not permitted to them (for this reason neither has the Mother
come with them without us, they say), reasonably they are in need on our
behalf. =2.36=.1 Yet in unity our Angels were put forth, they say, being one,
as having come forth from one. =2.36=.2 But since we were the ones who had
been divided, for this reason Jesus was baptized, that the undivided be
divided, until he unite us with them into the Pleroma; in order that we, the
many, having become one, all may be mixed with the one who was divided for us.
=2.37=.1 Those who came out from Adam, the Righteous, making their way through
the created things, were held at the Place, according to the Valentinians; but
the others, in that which was created of darkness among those on the left,
having perception of the fire. "A river of fire goes forth from under the
throne" of the Place, and flows into the void of the created, which is
Gehenna, not filled from the creation of the fire flowing. And the Place
itself =2.38=.2 is fiery. Therefore, he says, it has a veil, in order that the

him, of whom according to image also the high priest once a =2.38=.3 year used
to enter into the holy of holies. From there also Jesus, having been called,
sat with the Place, in order that the spirits may remain and not rise before
him, and in order that he may tame the Place and provide the seed a passage
into the Pleroma.

from himself the passions that were darkening and mixed together with him. And
Adam the Creator, having held him beforehand in thought, brought him forth at
the end of the creation. =2.42=.1 The Cross is a sign of the Limit in the
Pleroma; for it separates the unbelievers from the believers as that one the
world from the Pleroma. =2.42=.2 Wherefore also the seeds Jesus, having borne
through the sign upon the shoulders, leads into the Pleroma. For shoulders of
the seed Jesus is said; but Head is the Christ. =2.42=.3 Whence it is said:
"Who does not take up his Cross and follow me, is not my brother." He then
took up the body of Jesus, which was consubstantial with the Church. =2.43=.1
They say then that those on the right knew the names of Jesus and of the
Christ even before the advent; but they did not know the power of the sign.
=3.43=.2 And the Father having given all the authority, and the Pleroma also
having consented, "the Angel of counsel" is sent forth. And he becomes Head of
the =3.43=.3 all after the Father; "For all things were created in him, the
visible and the invisible, Thrones, Lordships, Kingdoms", Divinities,
Ministries. =3.43=.4 "Wherefore also God highly exalted him and gave him a
Name that is above every name, that every knee should bow and every tongue
confess that Jesus Christ is Lord of glory" Savior, "himself having gone up
and =3.43=.5 having gone down; and what is 'he went up' except that he also
went down? He who went down is himself into the lowest parts of the earth and
having gone up above the heavens." =3.44=.1 And Sophia, having seen him
similar to the Light that had left her, recognized and ran to him and rejoiced
and worshipped; but having beheld the male Angels sent out with him, she was
ashamed =3.44=.2 and put on a veil. Through this mystery Paul commands the
women "to wear authority upon the head on account of the Angels". =3.45=.1
Immediately then the Savior brings upon her a formation according to
knowledge and a healing of the passions, having shown from the unbegotten
Father the things in the Pleroma and the things as far as her. =3.45=.2 And
having stood the passions off from her who had suffered, he made her
impassible, but having distinguished the passions he kept them; and not as
those of the inner were dispersed, but he brought them into substance, both
them and those of the second disposition. =3.45=.3 Thus through the appearing
of the Savior, Sophia becomes impassible, and the things outside are created;
"For all things came to be through him, and without him came to be nothing."


=3.46=.1 First then from incorporeal passion and accident into still
incorporeal matter he drained them and changed them; then thus into compounds
and bodies =3.46=.2 (for it was not possible to make the passions substance
all at once); and to the bodies he produced fitness according to nature.
=3.47=.1 First then the Savior becomes universal Creator; "but Sophia" second
"builds a house for herself and has supported seven pillars." =3.47=.2 And
first of all she puts forth as image of the Father a God, through whom she
made "the heaven and the earth", that is "the heavenly and the earthly", those
on the right and those on the left. =3.47=.3 This one as image of Father
becomes father and puts forth first the psychic Christ, image of Son; then the
Archangels, images of Aeons; then Angels of Archangels, from the psychic and
luminous substance of which the prophetic word says: "And Spirit of God was
borne upon the waters", according to the intertwining of the two substances
made for him, having said the unmixed "to be borne upon", but the heavy and
material =3.47=.4 to be borne under, the turbid and coarse. And this too he
hints as incorporeal in the beginning by saying "invisible"; for it was
neither invisible to man who was not yet, nor to God; for he was creating; but
the formless and shapeless and unfigured of it he somehow thus uttered.
=3.48=.1 And the Creator, having distinguished the pure from the heavy, as
having looked upon the nature of each, made light, that is he manifested and
brought it to light and form, since the solar and heavenly light he works much
later. =3.48=.2 And he makes from the material things, one from grief,
creating substantially "Spiritual things of wickedness, against which is our
wrestling" (wherefore also the Apostle says: "And do not grieve the Holy
Spirit of God, in whom =3.48=.3 you were sealed"); and that from fear, the
beasts; and that from amazement and perplexity, the elements =3.48=.4 of the
world. And in the three elements the fire is suspended and sown and lurks and
is kindled by these and dies with these, not having an assigned place of its
own as also the other elements from which the compounds are created. =3.49=.1
And since he did not know her who was working through him, thinking he creates
by his own power, being fond of work by nature; therefore the Apostle said:
"It was subjected to the vanity of the world, not willingly, but on account of
him who subjected it, in hope that it also will be set free =3.49=.2", when
the seeds of God are gathered. And a proof especially of the unwilling is
"blessing the sabbath" and embracing the rest from labours. =3.50=.1 "Having
taken dust from the earth", not of the dry, but a part of the many-parted and
varied matter, he fashioned an earthy and material soul, irrational and
consubstantial with that of the beasts; this is the man "according to image".
=3.50=.2 But he "according to likeness", that of the Creator himself is that
one whom into this he "breathed" and sowed, having placed through Angels
something consubstantial with himself. =3.50=.3 Inasmuch as it is invisible
and incorporeal, he called its substance "breath of life"; but having been
formed, it became "living soul"; which even he himself confesses it is in the
prophetic writings. =3.51=.1 Man then is in man, psychic in earthy, not part
in part, but whole with whole, by unspeakable power of God. Whence in
Paradise, the fourth =3.51=.2 heaven, he is created. For there earthy flesh
does not go up, but the material was as it were flesh to the divine soul.
These things signify: "This now is bone from my bones" (he hints at the divine
soul hidden in the flesh and firm and hard to suffer and more powerful), "and
flesh from my flesh" (the material soul being body of the divine soul).
=3.51=.3 Concerning these two the Savior also says "one must fear him who is
able to destroy this soul and this body" the psychic "in gehenna".


=3.52=.1 This little flesh the Savior called "adversary" and Paul "a law
warring against the law of my mind"; and the Savior exhorts "to bind" and "to
seize as of a strong man the vessels", of him who wars against the heavenly
soul; and exhorts "to be rid of it" "on the way, lest we fall into the prison
and the =3.52=.2 punishment"; and similarly also "to be well-disposed" toward
it, not feeding and strengthening it by the license of sins, but from here
already putting it to death and making it vanish by abstinence from
wickedness, in order that in this dissolution it may be dispersed and breathed
away unnoticed, but not taking hold of some subsistence of its own, may have
lasting strength in the =3.53=.1 passage through fire. This is named "tare"
grown together with the soul, the good seed; this also "seed of the Devil", as
consubstantial with him, and "snake" and "heel-striker" and "robber" attacking
a king's head. =3.53=.2 And Adam had without his knowledge the spiritual seed
sown by Sophia into the soul, "having been arranged", he says, "through
Angels, in the hand of a Mediator; but the mediator is not of one; but God is
one". =3.53=.3 "Through Angels" then the male the seeds are ministered, those
put forth into becoming by =3.53=.4 Sophia, insofar as it is possible to come
to be. For as Creator, being moved without his knowledge by Sophia, he thinks
he is self-moved =3.53=.5, similarly also men. First then spiritual seed that
in Adam Sophia put forth, in order that the bone may be, the rational and
heavenly soul, not empty, but full of spiritual marrow. =3.54=.1 And from Adam
three natures are generated; first the irrational, of which was Cain; second
the rational and the just, of which was Abel; third the spiritual, of which
=3.54=.2 was Seth. And the earthy is "according to image"; and the psychic
"according to likeness" of God; and the spiritual according to its own; upon
which three, without the other children of Adam, it is said: "This is the book
of generation =3.54=.3 of men". And that Seth is spiritual, he neither
shepherds, nor farms, but bears a child as fruit, as the spiritual things. And
this one, who "hoped to call upon the Name of the Lord", looking "up", whose
"citizenship is in heaven", this one the world does not contain. =3.55=.1 To
the three incorporeal upon Adam a fourth, the earthy, is put on, the "coats of
skin". =3.55=.2 Neither then from the Spirit, nor then from the inbreathing,
does Adam sow; for both are divine, and through him, but not by him, both are
put forth. =3.55=.3 But his material is active toward seed and generation, as
being mixed with the seed, and unable to stand apart from this =3.56=.1
harmony in life. According to this, our father is Adam, "the first man
=3.56=.2 from earth earthy". And if he had also sown from psychic and from
spiritual, just as from material, all would have become equal and just, and in
all the teaching would have been. Therefore many are the material, but not
many the psychic; and rare the spiritual. =3.56=.3 The spiritual then is saved
by nature; but the psychic, being free-willed, has fitness toward both faith
and incorruption, and toward unbelief and corruption, according to its own
choice; but the material perishes by nature. =3.56=.4 When then the psychic
"are grafted into the good olive" unto faith and incorruption, and share "the
fatness of the olive", and when "the nations enter", then =3.56=.5 "thus all
Israel will be saved". And Israel is allegorized as the spiritual, who will
see God, the genuine son of faithful Abraham "from the free woman", not he
"according to flesh", he from the Egyptian slave-woman. =3.57=.1 There comes
to be then, from the three races, of the one a formation of the spiritual, of
the other a transfer of the psychic from slavery into freedom. After the
kingdom of death then, which had made a great and fair-faced promise, but had
none the less become a ministry of death, every Principality and Divinity
having declined, the great Combatant, Jesus Christ, having taken up in himself
by power the Church, the elect and the called, the one from her who bore the


spiritual, and the other from the economy the psychic, which he saved and
bore up what he took up, and through them also those consubstantial with these
=3.58=.2; "For if the firstfruit is holy, also the lump; if the root is holy,
also the branches." =3.59=.1 A seed then first he put on from her who bore,
not being contained, but containing it by power, which is formed little by
little through knowledge. =3.59=.2 And having come according to the Place,
Jesus found Christ to put on who had been proclaimed beforehand, whom the
Prophets and the Law announced, being an image of the Savior. =3.59=.3 But
this psychic Christ also, whom he put on, was invisible; and it was necessary
that he who arrives into the world, in order to be seen, to be held, to be a
citizen, also =3.59=.4 endure a perceptible body. A body then is woven for him
from the invisible psychic substance, and by power of a divine construction
having arrived into the perceptible world. =3.60=.1 The then: "Holy Spirit
will come upon you", he says of the genesis of the body of the Lord; "And
power of the highest will overshadow you", indicates the formation of God,
which he imprinted on the body in the Virgin. That he himself was other than
what he had taken up, is clear from what he confesses: "I am the Life", "I am
the Truth", =3.61=.2 "I and the Father are one". And the spiritual which he
took up, and the psychic, he thus shows: "And the child grew and advanced in
Wisdom." For of Wisdom the spiritual has need; but of size =3.61=.3 the
psychic. And through the things that flowed from the side, he indicated, by
the outflowings of the passions from those who had passions, that the
substances having become impassible have been saved. =3.61=.4 And when he
says: "The Son of Man must be rejected, insulted, crucified", he appears to
speak as of another, clearly of the passible. =3.61=.5 And: "I will go before
you", he says, "on the third of the days into Galilee"; for he himself goes
before all; and he hinted that he will raise the soul being saved invisibly,
and restore it where he now goes before. =3.61=.6 And he died at the departing
of the Spirit that descended upon him at the Jordan, not having become
separate, but having been drawn together, in order that death also might work;
since how, Life being present in him, did the body die? For thus death would
have prevailed even over the Savior himself, which is absurd. And by guile
death was outgeneraled =3.61=.7; for when the body had died and death had
seized it, the Savior, having drawn back the ray of power that had come upon
it, destroyed death, but the mortal body, having cast off passions, he raised.
=3.61=.8 The psychic then are thus raised and saved again; but the spiritual
having believed are saved above those, having received the souls as "wedding
garments". =3.62=.1 The psychic Christ then sits at the right of the Creator,
according as also David says: "Sit at =3.62=.2 my right", and the rest. And he
sits until the consummation, "in order that they may see him whom they
pierced". And they pierced the appearing, which was flesh of the psychic. "For
a bone of him shall not be broken", it says; just as in the case of Adam the
prophecy allegorized the soul as bone. =3.62=.3 For this very soul of the
