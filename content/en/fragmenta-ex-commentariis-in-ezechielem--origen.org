FROM ORIGEN'S ON EZEKIEL
From the 20th tome of those on Ezekiel.
=13.664= Thus says the Lord Lord: Behold, I will judge between sheep and
sheep, rams and goats. And was it not enough for you that you fed on the
good pasture? And the remainders of the pasture you trampled with your
feet; and you drank the settled water, and the rest you troubled with
your feet, and my sheep fed on the tramplings of your feet, and they
drank the water troubled by your feet. (After setting forth concerning
the sheep and rams, and concerning sheep of goats, that it is customary
for Scripture sometimes to set "sheep" even upon the race of goats, he
adds.) What then is it that he also riddles in these things, come let us
examine according to our power. All good pasture, I think, and all the
settled water, are the whole oracles of the sacred writings. Then since
some approve some of the things written as beneficial, but reject some
as not salvific, these would be those who, after having fed on the good
pasture of the things they selected, and having drunk the settled water
of that which they judged to be better, trample the rest of the pasture,
and trouble the remaining water with their own feet. And such are both
those who approve the New but reject the Old Covenant, and those who say
of the old Writings that some are from a more divine power and the
highest, but some from a lesser. And the shepherd proclaims as his own
sheep those who do not disdain the tramplings of those ones' feet, and
do not set at nought the tramplings of the feet of the blameworthy
sheep, goats and kids; for they did not wish to be sheep of rams worthy
of the right. We therefore, praying to be sheep of the shepherd, let us
never flee from feeding also on those things of the Scriptures that
immediately, so far as the letter, appear unseemly, and because of the
unseemliness of the wording are trampled by those who are neither able
nor willing to use the whole pasture. But even if there be some water
troubled by those ones' feet, who have mixed with the pure word of
Scripture ill-sounding questionings, let us not be turned away, because
of the trouble they have made for the word, from drinking even that
which has been troubled by their feet. And observe carefully that it is
said to those who troubled the water and trampled the pasture as
concerning better ones: And my sheep fed on the tramplings of your feet,
and they drank the water troubled by your =13.665= feet. But also let us
never trample a prophetic pasture, nor trouble a legal water; but when
some also sin concerning the evangelical pasture and the apostolic
water, so as to trample some of the evangelical things, but feed on some
as a good pasture, and of the apostolic things either to reject all or
to approve some and reject some, let us feed on the whole Gospels, and
trample none of them, and drinking all the apostolic things, the settled
water so far as it is in our power, let us keep them and let us trouble
none of the things in them with an unbelief that troubles those who do
not know how to understand the things said.
