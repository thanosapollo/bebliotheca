* On the Holy Martyrs

Of Hesychius, from encomia on martyrs.
1 You then, men brothers and fathers, having gone in eagerly to the many-flowered
garden of the Spirit, harvest from there the virtues, not one but many, rather
not many but all; for virtue needs virtue, since also wing needs wing and wheel
wheel and a step a step and an oar an oar and a rudder a rudder. If you do not
yoke stone to stone, the tower has not been raised into height; in the same way
then, if we do not accomplish law upon law, we do not arrive at heaven; if you
do not join command to command, the course is incomplete. And how can we lay
hold of the crown? Fasting is fair, but without prayer it does not tread upon
demons; "For this," he says, "kind does not come out except in fasting and
prayer." And prayer is fair, but without almsgiving it cannot go up into heaven;
for you have heard the angel saying to Cornelius: "Your prayers and your
alms have gone up into a memorial before God." Therefore the almsgiving carried
the prayer into heaven. Wonderful then is almsgiving, but without faith it does
not work a ransom of sins; "For by alms and faiths sins are cleansed." Zealous
is meekness, but without humility it cannot complete a kingly character; "For
learn from me, he says, that I am meek and humble in heart." Faith is fair as
even the least of it can move mountains, but without love it is vain and extra;
"For even if I have faith," he says, "so as to move mountains, and I do not have
love, I am nothing." Let us be eager then, beloved, to pursue these virtues,
that we may be deemed worthy of the kingdom of the heavens. Do not be, beloved,
earnest concerning the faith, and more sluggish concerning the earnestness, not
industrious concerning the scriptures, and unphilosophical concerning the
manners; for one must be rich in good works, in word, in wisdom, in temperance,
in justice, in courage, in piety, in prudence and holiness; for as vice even
before Gehenna punishes those who pursue it, so virtue prepares those who work
it even before the kingdom to luxuriate.
