Of Saint Epiphanius concerning the twelve stones set in the oracle of the
priest. Sardion, the one called Babylonian, is fiery and bloodlike, and it is
produced in Babylon in the land toward the Assyrians. And it is translucent,
of healing power, which the physicians use, smearing it on, for swellings and
wounds that have come from iron. Topazion is red beyond the anthrax stone, and
it is produced in Topaze, a city of India. And when it is rubbed on a medical
whetstone, it does not give off a juice red according to the color, but milky.
And it fills mixing bowls even many, as many as the one grinding may wish, and
it is diminished neither in weight nor in circumference. And the juice rubbed
from it is useful for affections of the eyes, and for hydromanias when drunk,
and for those driven mad from sea-grape. Smaragdos is green, and in the Indian
mountains the barbarians, digging, cut it. And its power is for mirroring the
faces. Anthrax is sharp-crimson in appearance, and it is produced in Carthage
of Libya, which is called Africa. And they say that it is found not by day but
by night; for from afar sparkling like a torch or a coal, it ceases after an
hour. And those seeking it, having recognized that this is it, go toward its
gleam and find it. And when it is carried, in whatever garments it may be
covered, its gleam shines outside the enclosure. Sapphieros is purplish, and
it is produced in Ethiopia and India. And when it is rubbed, it heals tumors
and pustules, being smeared with milk on the ulcerated places. And the
legislation given on the mountain to Moses is said to have appeared upon a
sapphire stone. Iaspis is emerald-like, and it is found by the mouth of the
river Mermodon, and at Amathus of Cyprus. And it is useful for the epilepsies.
Hyakinthos is somewhat fiery, and it is found in the inner Barbary of Scythia.
And the ancients were accustomed to call Scythia the whole northern clime,
where the Goths and the Danes are. There then within, in the desert of great
Scythia, is a ravine exceedingly deep, and impassable to men, walled about
with mountains of rocks from here and from there, so that someone stooping
from above from the peaks of the mountains, as from walls, cannot look down
upon the floor of the ravine, but from the depth it is darkened as some chaos;
in which those sent from the neigh89.589 boring kings there are condemned men
and compelled. And having sacrificed lambs and flayed them they throw them
from above from the rocks into the chaos of the ravine, and the stones stick
in those meats. Eagles then, dwelling above in the rocks, come down toward the
smell of the meats, and bring up the lambs having the stones. And in the
eating of the meats the stones remain on the ridge of the mountains. And the
condemned, having watched where the meats were brought up by the eagles, go,
and thus they bring the stones. And they have such an energy: for when they
are thrown upon coals of fire, they themselves are not harmed, but they quench
the coals.

Not only this, but also if someone taking the stone and wrapping it in linen,
having placed it upon coals, holding it he himself is burned from the pyre,
but the linen remains unharmed. And it is said also to be useful to women in
childbirth for the sake of easy birth. Achates is somewhat bluish, and this
too is found about those Scythian lands. And when it is rubbed on the
forehead, it turns away the pain of the bite of scorpions and of vipers.
Amethystos is flaming deeply, and it is found in the mountains of Libya toward
the banks of the sea. Chrysolithos is golden, and it is found in the Aphraitid
rock, by the lip of Achaemenid Babylon. For they call Babylon and that well of
the rock Achaemenis. And when it is rubbed, it is medicinal for the stochastic
(and colic) and the abdominal. Beryllion is gray-blue, and it is produced
beside the seeds of the mountain called Taurus. Onychion is yellow, and this
too is found in the same mountain.

On the twelve gems (other fragments in Anastasius of Sinai, Questions and

Of Epiphanius from that to Diodorus.

Somoron then the mountain was named, and the neighboring parts, from Semer,
one son of the Canaanites, before Abraham set foot on the land. And when the
guards came, they were called Samaritans, that is, guar89.597 ds. And they
were guards not only of the land, but also of the law; for they kept the
Pentateuch alone, which they received through Esdras the king, so that from
this the seed of Abraham might be distinguished. But they did not keep the law
perfectly; wherefore Scripture says: "They remained doing the law of God, and
worshipping the idols."

And the law commanding that idols not be worshipped, how will it still be
fulfilled? And the matter has this manner: For the priests of the four
nations, having known that Esdras had come in, abhorring the idols according
to the law of God, having hastened hid the idols in Mount Gerizim in a certain
recess, and they turned the heart of the Samaritans to pray toward the
mountain. Hence, wherever they are, they pray toward the mountain, those from
the east turning toward the west, and from the west toward the east, and from
the north toward the south, attending to the mountain toward the north they
pray, that the Scripture may be fulfilled which says: "They remained doing the
law of God, and worshipping their idols." For even if they themselves do not
know that the idols lie there, still it is not possible for the divine
Scripture to lie.
