ON THE SOUL AGAINST THE ARIANS
10 But what would they say, having looked to the nurture of the infant, or
the advance of age, or the lengthening of the years, or the growth of the
body? And that I may pass over speaking of the wonders fulfilled upon the
earth, let them look at the raisings of the dead, the tokens of the
passion, the traces of the scourges, the bruises of the wounds, the wounded
sides, the marks of the nails, the pouring out of the blood, the signs of
death, and, to speak the whole, the resurrection itself of his own body.
11 And indeed if anyone looks to the birth of the body, he would clearly
find that, born in Bethlehem, he was swaddled, and in Egypt he was reared
for some time because of the plot of the accursed Herod, and in Nazareth,
having become a man, he grew.
12 For the tent of the Word and God is not the same, through which the
blessed Stephen beheld the divine glory.
13 Before the passion, on each occasion he foretold his bodily death,
saying that he would be delivered to those around the high priests, and
announcing the trophy of the cross. But after the passion, having risen on
the third day from the dead, when the disciples were in doubt that he had
been raised, appearing to them in the same body, he confesses that he has
all flesh together with bones, and presenting to their sight the wounded
sides, he also shows them the marks of his nails.
14 And it is possible in a few words to refute their impious slander; for
especially if he were not, for the sake of the salvation of men,
voluntarily delivering his own body to the slaughter of death. First they
attach to him much inability, because he was not able to check the onset
of the enemies.
15 And why do they make much of showing that Christ assumed a soulless
body, fabricating earthly deceits? So that if they should be able to
corrupt some, to define that these things are so, then, having attached
the alterations of the passions to the divine Spirit, they may easily
persuade them that the changeable has not been begotten from the
unchangeable nature.
16 And going through things in agreement with these, the same Jeremiah
introduces, ministering the voices of God, through which he shows that he
does not even receive the intercessions of the righteous, if they should
wish to make request for the sake of the murderers. And the Lord said to
me, saying: If Moses and Samuel should stand before my face, my soul is
not toward them.
17 And if through the appearing of Christ he brings the race of men into
paradise, and when he was crucified he foretold that he would bring the
robber in there on the same day, while the body was still laid in the
tomb, it is established that the soul of Christ, dwelling together in the
proper sense with the Word and God, who encompasses at once the whole
creation of generated things, brought the kindred soul of the man into
paradise. For neither is it written that before the resurrection of the
holy body the body of the robber was raised beforehand, having received
the promise. It is therefore consequent to suppose wisely that the soul,
leading the kindred soul in a friendly way, inasmuch as it also possesses
greater authority, brought the promise to fulfillment on the same day, and
brought him into the exceptional inheritance, opening paradise.
