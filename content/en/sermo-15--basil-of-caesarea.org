Discourse on how the monk ought to be.
You have heard, my brothers beloved by the Lord, the Lord saying: whoever
has left father and mother, brothers or sisters or wife or children, for the
sake of my name, and follows after me, this one will receive a hundredfold
blessing, and will inherit eternal life. And again: whoever has not left
father and mother, brothers and sisters, and has not taken up the cross and
followed me, is not worthy of me. And again: whoever does not hate father
and mother, brothers, wife, children, and his own soul, cannot be my
disciple. And again: he who loves his soul will lose it, and he who has lost
his soul will find it. And again: unless you turn and become as the
children, you will by no means enter into the kingdom of the heavens. And
again, having taken a child, he said: unless you become as this child, you
cannot be saved; for whoever becomes as this child, this one is greater than
you in the kingdom of the heavens. Whoever wishes to be first of you, let
him become your servant and slave of all; and many first will be last, and
the last first. And again he said: many are called, but few chosen, and few
are those who are being saved; many attempt, and few complete; And again he
says: from the forerunner and baptist until today, the kingdom of the
heavens is seized, and the violent seize it.
And again he was speaking his own basa mysteries: I must be spat upon and
buffeted, and struck, and mocked, and on the third day be raised. And Peter,
also first of the apostles, said to him: Lord, be it far from you. And the
Lord says to Peter: Get behind me, Satan, you do not mind the things of God,
but the things of men. Whoever wishes to live piously in Christ will be
persecuted. And when Peter saw the end of the commandment, he says: Lord, a
man ought not to take a wife. And the Lord says to him: not all receive this
word; but there are eunuchs from their mother's womb, and others who have
been made eunuchs by men, and others who are for the kingdom of the heavens;
he who is able to receive it, let him receive it. Therefore, my beloved
brothers and children of the apostles, we were able to receive according to
his longed-for voice; now let us hold the heavenly hope, and let us take
thought for eternal life. Behold, as the Lord commanded us, we hastened,
leaving father and mother, brothers, sisters, wife, sons, fields, vineyards,
houses, and we followed the Savior Christ; we have abandoned all the things
of the world, and having taken up the cross we followed the Savior Christ,
as the apostles; and we wish to please and to serve the Savior Christ.
Brother, if in your absence it happens that you are reviled by someone, and
another comes and reports to you that such-and-such a brother reviled you,
you, as a prudent man, recognize whose plot it is. And do not grow bitter
against your brother. And say also to the one who came and reported it to
you thus: even if my brother happens to have insulted me, nevertheless he
did not do this of himself; the evil one prepared this, that he might cast a
fight between us; but the Lord will bring the evil one to nothing, and will
have mercy on my brother, and may he not abandon us.
