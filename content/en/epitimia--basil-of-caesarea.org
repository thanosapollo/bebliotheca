PENANCES.
12 If anyone should go out of the monastery, not having received a blessing
or been dismissed by the archimandrite with prayer, let him be without
communion. 13 If anyone possesses something in the monastery, or outside the
monastery, let him be without communion. 14 If anyone joins with someone to
withdraw from the monastery for some other evil, let him be without
communion. 15 If any of the choir-leaders finds someone disturbing or
conversing in the choir, and does not cast him out of the choir, let him
himself be without blessing. 16 If anyone is made without blessing for the
sake of discipline, and in contempt does not apologize and receive a
blessing, let him be set apart. 17 If anyone sets something on the tables
either for himself =31.1309= or for another, not having been permitted by
the archimandrite, let him be without blessing. 18 If anyone without a
maphorion does work in any place whatsoever, let him be without blessing.
19 If anyone meets with the coming stranger or with one of his own, not
having been permitted by the archimandrite, except those appointed, let him
be without blessing.
20 If anyone takes something from a brother and hides it, let him be without
blessing. 21 If anyone, not having received a blessing, eats while not being
under discipline, let him be set apart. 22 If anyone does not take a
blessing at his order, let him be without blessing; but if because of need
he did not arrive, let him apologize. 23 If anyone is found at night being
apart with someone, or changing the place where he sleeps, contrary to the
mind of those appointed, let him be set apart. 24 If anyone throws himself
into another work, not having been permitted, let him be without blessing.
25 If anyone exchanges something with someone, or gives a gift contrary to
the mind of the archimandrite, let him be without blessing. 26 If anyone in
the evening after the Our Father is found conversing, let him be set apart.
27 If any of those who are healthy sleeps outside the oratory, let him be
set apart. 28 If anyone at table while eating talks nonsense, let him be
raised to prayer. 29 If anyone is made without blessing, and being grieved
will discipline in return the one disciplining, or contradicting does not
accept it, let him be set apart. But if he should say he is being
disciplined unjustly, let him make the cause known to the archimandrite, and
let him accept what is determined by him.
30 If anyone has a grief toward someone, and in contempt does not reconcile
before the service, or tell the cause to the archimandrite or to the
choir-leader, let him be set apart. 31 If anyone on a day of offering
withholds himself from communion contrary to the mind of the archimandrite,
let him be without blessing. 32 If anyone from the Scriptures argues
contentiously, let him be without blessing; but if having been commanded he
persists, as disobedient and insubordinate let him be set apart. 33 If
anyone washes either another's or his own garment contrary to the mind of
the archimandrite, let him be without blessing. 34 If anyone is absent from
the tables, not having stated the cause, let him remain without food.
=31.1312= 35 If anyone loses something, and, not having found it, does not
report it to the archimandrite, let him be without blessing. 36 If two are
found continually drawing near to one another for the same, and having been
commanded they do not separate, they shall be set apart until they are
corrected. 37 If anyone in the evening does not arrive at the Our Father,
apart from some cause, let him stand in prayer until all have gone to sleep.
38 If anyone, at the time of communion having a grief toward the brother,
does not give him the peace, let him be set apart. 39 If anyone moves his
brother from the order of the service, except those entrusted with the care,
let him be without blessing.
40 If anyone knows a brother wishing secretly to withdraw from the
monastery, and does not report it to the archimandrite or to those set over,
let him be set apart. 41 If anyone rises from the table contrary to the mind
of the archimandrite, let him stand in prayer until the lamp-lighting. 42 If
anyone after the communion goes out of the service, until all receive
blessings, except human necessity, let him eat by himself. 43 If anyone
causes someone to be made without blessing untimely, let him himself be
without blessing. 44 If anyone is found speaking into the cells, except
those dwelling in them, or the hesychasts, let him be without blessing. 45
If anyone speaking jests or idle talk, let him be without blessing. 46 If
anyone is found speaking against someone, or hearing some who speak against,
and not rebuking him, or reporting to the archimandrite, let him be set
apart together with him. 47 If anyone is found in the garden speaking with
someone, or repeating psalms, or sleeping, except those working, or those
appointed, let him be without blessing. 48 and should go out to the
fortress, let him be set apart. 49 If anyone gives something outside, or
receives, contrary to the mind of the archimandrite, let him be set apart.
50 If anyone having met should converse with one of the returning strangers,
except to give the peace, except =31.1313= those appointed, let him be
without blessing. 51 If anyone troubles the archimandrite on the pretext of
the daily duty, except the cellarer or the chief of the week, let him be
without blessing; so that of each daily duty the chief of the week and the
steward may take careful thought concerning every need, lest they fall under
the judgment of negligence. 52 If anyone untimely comes into the cookhouse
or into the cellar except those appointed and those taking thought for good
order, let him be without blessing. 53 If anyone having been woken by
someone is grieved at the time of the service or of the reading, let him be
set apart. 54 If anyone superfluously after the visitation that is after the
morning offices enters into the infirmary, except the hesychasts and those
entrusted with the care, let him be without blessing. 55 If anyone contrary
to the mind of the infirmarian having entered rests in the infirmary, let
him be without blessing; but if he is afflicted or is sick, let him first
make it known to the infirmarian. 56 If any of those who are ailing changes
the place where he sleeps, contrary to the mind of the infirmarian, let him
be set apart. 57 If any of the appointed servants in the infirmary sets
something before a brother contrary to the mind of the infirmarian, let him
be without blessing. 58 If any of the sick is found exchanging contrary to
the mind of the infirmarian, or of one of those serving, let him be without
blessing. 59 If anyone is found, apart from the archimandrite, writing to
someone or receiving letters, let him be set apart.
60 If anyone sends a blessing to someone, or receives anything at any time,
apart from the archimandrite, let him be without blessing.
