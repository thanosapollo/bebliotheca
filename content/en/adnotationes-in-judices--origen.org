ORIGEN'S SELECTIONS ON THE JUDGES.
=17.37= CH. 15. Verse 19. And God broke the wound that was in the jaw, and
water came out from it. Instead of: He broke the earth by the blow of the jaw,
and waters came out. CH. 20. Verse 21. And sons of Benjamin went out. For
whose sake are they defeated, having gone out justly? Or clearly because of
preceding causes. And the psalm says: When I take a time, I will judge
uprightnesses. Then after being defeated both a first and a second time, the
wrath went over upon those who had done wrong, they win a just case. And it is
worthy to wonder at the multitude of the Israelites, that, having gone against
the Benjaminites justly, and having been defeated, they did not think anything
wicked against God, but rather they did not even cease from weeping
beforehand, until they took justice against those who had done wrong. Not
blaming God they said: But this is enough. They entreated to learn a way. The:
Benjamin a snatching wolf; in the morning he will eat, and the rest he will
distribute food, is fulfilled here. For also having conquered earlier, he
became last food of the people. =17.40= CH. 21. Verse 20, 21. Why then did the
sons of Israel command the two hundred from Benjamin to snatch from the
virgins dancing in Shiloh and to marry? All the women of their tribe had been
slaughtered; and they themselves had sworn not to betroth their own daughters
to them. Therefore that they might not, forced by necessity, bring in women of
another tribe, they found a way in ways-not, and they invented the snatching.
