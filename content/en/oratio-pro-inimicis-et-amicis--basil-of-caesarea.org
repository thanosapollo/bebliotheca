ANOTHER PRAYER OF THE SAME.
Long-suffering and everlasting King, who because of the condemnation from a
wood was lifted upon a wood, and having furnished yourself as an example of
long-suffering to those who choose to follow your tracks, who yourself brought
an intercession for those God-fighting who crucified you to the co-without-beginning
Begetter; loving Lord, also to those with whom we are in enmity or who plot, or
who revile, or who despite, or who envy, or who in whatever way from a plot and
despite of the enmity-loving demon hate and turn away, forgive the fault of the
drunkenness toward us, change their judgments from malice into gentleness. Cast
into their hearts a guileless and unhypocritical love; bind them to us in
unbreakable bonds of spiritual goodwill; and of your unmixed life, by words you
know, make them partakers; and those who love us, or also serving for bodily
needs because of your holy name, repay with your rich gifts, and deem them
worthy of the lot of the faithful and prudent stewards. And to those who remember
our weakness in a good disposition, or also praying for us, award your grace
abundantly; to those who have commanded our wretched unworthiness to pray for
them grant the beneficial things, and furnish the requests toward salvation, and
send out your rich mercies and compassions to them. All, compassionate one, who
have trusted upon you have mercy, draw all into your divine love. Stand before
all, and take hold, with us the sinful and useless servants of yours, and work
them heirs of your kingdom. For yours it is to have mercy and to save us, our
God, because yours is the power unto the ages. Amen.
