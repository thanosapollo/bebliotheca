448 Eph 1:17 Gregory among the saints in the discourse On the Son, as I think,
received the two unto Christ: Father of the Word, but God of the assumption,
that from divinity and humanity in one nature he might show Emmanuel. eph 1:22
Or above angels, he says, and archangels he gave the Son himself as head; or
Christ who is above all things seen and thought, or above all the good things
he has done this, the giving of the Son as head. eph 2:2 Or understand
absolutely, that in the now, he says, world you walked in sins. So that
according to this sense, which rather both saint Cyril and saint John receive,
he is also ruler of those who fell with him, but of us he neither rules nor
has ruled, but only works; and it is possible for us not to receive his
working. eph 2:3 As if he said: sinning bodily and psychically. eph 2:5 Now
indeed in power, but later also in energy. 449 eph 2:12 And strangers, he
says, of the covenants, either of the law or of the promise toward them. eph
3:10 Before the times of the incarnation of our Savior the heavenly powers
knew the wisdom of God as simple, accomplished from the powerful alone; for
God, having given being to all things by the impulse of his own will, by
incomprehensible power kept the things created toward continuance, and nothing
was varied, but such as it was beheld, such it was. But now indeed through the
economy toward the church and the human race the wisdom of God has been known
no longer only simple but also manifold, accomplishing the opposites through
the opposites: life through death, glory through dishonor, a trophy through a
cross, the things befitting God through all the cheap things. Through the
economy toward us therefore that came to be in the incarnation, the wisdom and
the power of God has been known as manifold to the heavenly powers. Thus
Gregory among the saints in the eighth homily of the interpretation of the
Song says making it clear. eph 3:21 Or that he prays for the church so as to
keep always the doxology of God and never to be turned aside. And the in
Christ Jesus understand either with Christ Jesus, or you will understand
through Christ Jesus, understanding this to have been said because of the
flesh, which is also to be accepted by us. eph 4:10 For indeed also of old
with naked divinity he filled all things; and having become flesh, that he
might fill all things also with flesh, he descended and ascended. eph 4:13
Fullness of Christ he says the complete knowledge and faith concerning Christ,
such as that he took flesh from a virgin, that from divinity 450 and humanity
he exists into one nature. That having heard perfect man and measure of
stature you might not understand the bodily, he added of the fullness of
Christ, as if he said: until we arrive at the perfection of the complete faith
and knowledge of Christ. eph 4:16 See what I also thought; for I was not able
to understand the things of the blessed John by habit; from whom, Christ, all
the body of the church being fitted together to him and compacted, that is
glued together to him and united as to its own head; for from him is for us
the being glued to him, but glued through every joint, that is sensation, of
the supply according to energy in measure of each one part, that is through
the according-to-energy supply of Christ of the Holy Spirit which comes to be
according to the measure and the power of reception of each one member of
Christ, it makes the increase of the body of Christ, that is of the church.
And increase clearly spiritual; for through the supply of the Holy Spirit the
church increases unto a perfect measure of spiritual stature, whether through
gifts, or through knowledge, or through faith. In love, with which he himself
loves us; for because of loving us, he says, he does these things. eph 4:26 I
heard some interpreting: one, he says, sinless wrath is that toward the demons
and the passions. Or that it hangs from the above, let not the sun, he says,
set upon your provocation, that you may not give place to the devil, through
the thoughts according to the night to kindle you more against the brothers.
eph 4:30 For just as making us manifest that we are a people of God, he sealed
us in baptism; for this is the day of the redemption. 451 eph 5:16 Or thus:
the time is not sure for us; for we are sojourners. Buy it therefore and make
it your own. And how will this be? Through well-pleasing God in it; for then
this becomes one's own, because the good deeds in it follow there glorifying,
just as the bad ones condemning. eph 5:18 For wine has been given toward
gladness, not toward drunkenness. eph 5:21 For through the name of Christ all
things exist for us. For when each would be subjected to each, and not through
a worldly or unfitting matter, but through fear of Christ, what evil will
there be thereafter in the midst? eph 5:24 The whole multitude; for among
those according to each there is also an unsubjected one. eph 5:31 From him
inasmuch as he is our firstfruits of the second fashioning, just as from Adam
because of the first. eph 6:12 Toward the spiritual things, that is demons
that are, either of wickedness such as of the devil for they are ranged under
him, or leaders of wicked deeds. eph 6:14 And you can also understand as
toward Christ, who is truly truth for I am, he says, the truth whom also to
have put on, not only to have been girded, has been granted to men; for as
many as, he says, were baptized into Christ, have put on Christ. 452 eph 6:17
Or that he says the spiritual gifts which are through word and command of God,
or that which is word of God is to be yoked to the below, that it may be thus:
that which is word of God and command, this, he says, do. What kind? praying
through all prayer and supplication that you may be, he says. Or according to
working and cutting the spiritual gifts it is a word of God; for the word of
God is cutting. Thus rather the blessed John. For through word of God the
gifts were accomplished; for whatever you may ask, he says, in my name, he
will give you, says Christ. eph 6:19 Or that I may make defense in boldness or
that I may preach the gospel.
