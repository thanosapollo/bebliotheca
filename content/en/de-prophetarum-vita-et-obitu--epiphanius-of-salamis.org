OF SAINT EPIPHANIUS BISHOP OF CYPRUS CONCERNING THE PROPHETS, HOW THEY FELL
ASLEEP AND WHERE THEY LIE.

1. Nathan the prophet came to be in the days of David from the tribe of Thok.
This man was from Gabath, and he is the one who taught David the law of the
Lord. And Nathan knew that king David transgressed with Bathsheba. And as he
was hastening to come from Gabath to Jerusalem and prevent David from this
lawlessness, Beliar hindered him, because on the way he found a dead man lying
naked; and he remained to bury him there, that he might not be consumed by
wild beasts. And in that night he knew that David had committed the sin, and
he returned mourning to Gabath. And when David killed Uriah the husband of
Bathsheba, God sent Nathan and he rebuked David concerning his sin. And David
feared the Lord, because what he had done was rebuked; and David knew that the
Holy Spirit of God is upon Nathan the prophet; and he honored him as a holy
man of God; and he himself, having grown very old, died; and he was buried in
Gabath in his own land.

2. And Ahijah the Shilonite: this man was from Shiloh of =5= the tribe of
Judah, where the tent of the ark was of old; from the city of Armathaim of Eli
the priest. This prophet said concerning Solomon the son of David, that he
would offend against God; this man also rebuked Jeroboam, that if he should
reign, he would walk deceitfully with the Lord; for he saw a vision of a yoke
of oxen trampling the people and running upon the priests; and he said
beforehand to Solomon that women would make him stand away from the Lord. And
to Jeroboam he said that through your two heifers you will offend against the
Lord, and your race will be in slavery. And Ahijah died and was buried near
the oak that is in Shiloh.

3. Joam the prophet was born in Samaria; this is the one whom the lion struck
on the way and he died, because he rebuked Jeroboam over his golden heifers,
which he made, when he also cursed the sacrifice of Jeroboam, and he stretched
out his hand to harm him, and the hand of Jeroboam was withered. And after
these things he was deceived by the false prophet =6= and he forced him to eat
bread and drink water. And as he went out the lion struck him, and it did not
wrong his donkey. And he was buried in Bether near the false prophet. And
later, as he was dying, the false prophet adjured the priests, that "you shall
bury me with Joam the prophet," because "I deceived him. And in the
resurrection we shall exact justice." And both lie in one tomb.

4. Azariah the prophet: this man was from Symbatha. This is the one who turned
back the captivity of Judah into Israel through prayers. And having prophesied
many things concerning the coming of Christ; he fell asleep in his own
fatherland.

5. Elijah the prophet. This man was from Tishbe [from the land of the Arabs],
of the tribe of Aaron, dwelling in Gilead; because Thesbis was a house given
to the priests; and when his mother bore him, Sobak his father saw a vision:
that men white-appearing were addressing him; and that they were swaddling him
in fire; and they were giving him a flame of fire to eat. And having come to
Jerusalem he announced it to the priests. And the oracle said to him: do not
be afraid; for the dwelling of your child will be light and his word a decree
and he will judge =7= Israel with sword and with fire. This is Elijah who
brought down fire from heaven the third time, and who carried the rain with
his own


tongue and raised the dead and was taken up into heaven in a whirlwind of
fire.

6. Elisha the prophet. This man was from Abelmouth of the land of Reuben. And
upon this man a wonder came to be. That when he was born in Gilgal, the golden
heifer that is in Shiloh cried shrilly, so as to be heard in Jerusalem. And
the priest said through the lights, that a prophet has been born today, who
will pull down the carved things and shatter the molten things. And God did
many wonders through the hand of Elisha; and having died he was buried in
Samaria in Sebastopolis. This man prophesied concerning the presence of the
Lord. And the waters of Jericho that were barren, that is salty, he healed
saying: thus says the Lord. I have healed these waters. And they were healed
until this present day. And he raised the dead and, Naaman the Syrian being a
leper, he cleansed him of the leprosy, and he made Gehazi his servant to
become a leper. And being dead he raised a dead man.

=8= 7. Isaiah the prophet: he was born in Jerusalem of the tribe of Judah; and
he dies under Manasseh the king of Judah, sawn in two, and he was laid under
the oak of Rogel near the crossing of the waters, which Hezekiah the king
destroyed, having blocked them up. And God made Siloam on account of the
prophet, because before dying, being faint, he prayed to drink water; and
immediately living water was sent to him from it. For this reason the name was
called Siloam, which is interpreted Sent. And in the time of Hezekiah the
king, before he made the reservoirs and the pools, at the prayer of the
prophet Isaiah a little water had come out, because the people were in a siege
of the foreigners and that the city might not be destroyed as not having
water. For the enemies were asking from where they drink; for they did not
know. For having the city they were sitting down by Siloam; whenever then the
Jews were coming, water was coming out for them to draw and they were watering
themselves, but the foreigners were not finding it. For the water was fleeing.
Wherefore even until this day it comes out suddenly, that the mystery may be
shown. And since this came about through Isaiah, for the sake of memory the
=9= people also nearby buried him carefully, gloriously, that through his
prayers they might have the enjoyment of the water until the end. For an
oracle also was given them concerning him. And the tomb of Isaiah the prophet
is next to the tomb of the kings behind the tomb of the priests toward the
south side. For Solomon, building Jerusalem, made the tombs of the kings,
David having designed them; and it is toward the east of Zion, which has an
entrance from Gabaoth twenty stades distant from the city. And he made a
winding construction not to be guessed at; and it is even until today unknown
to the many.

8. Jeremiah the prophet was from Anathoth and in Taphnas of Egypt, having been
pelted with stones by the people, he died. And he lies in the place of the
dwelling of Pharaoh, because the Egyptians glorified him, having been
benefited by him. For asps and the beasts of the waters, which the Egyptians
call Nephoth, and the Greeks crocodiles, were putting them to death; and when
the prophet Jeremiah prayed, the wrath of the asps was prevented from that
land and likewise from the river =10= the lurking of the beasts. And as many
as are faithful until this day pray in that place, and taking of the dust they
heal the bite of asps and they put to flight the beasts of the water
themselves. This prophet gave a sign to the priests of Egypt saying, that all
their idols must be shaken and all the handmade things fall; when a virgin
giving birth sets foot in Egypt with a godlike infant, which also came to pass
for them. This prophet before the capture of the temple snatched the ark of
the law and all the things in it and he made them to be swallowed in a rock
and he said to the priests of the people and to the elders standing by: "the
Lord has departed from Sinai to the heavens and he will come again in holy
power. And this will be a sign of his presence, when all the nations shall
worship a tree." And he said


to them, that this ark no one of priests or prophets will still unfold, except
Moses the elect of God, and the =11= tablets in it no one will unfold except
Aaron. And in the resurrection first the ark will arise, and will come out of
the rock, and will be set on Mount Sinai, and all the saints will be gathered
to it, there awaiting the Lord, and fleeing the enemy who wishes to destroy
him. And he sealed the rock with his finger the name of the Lord; and the type
became as an engraving of iron. And a cloud covered the rock and no one
understands that place, nor even <can> read the name until this day and until
the consummation of this age. And the rock is in the wilderness, where the ark
first came to be, between the mountains, in which Moses and Aaron lie; and in
the nights a cloud as fire comes about in that place according to the ancient
type, because the glory of God shall not cease from his law.

9. Ezekiel the prophet: this man was from the land of Sarera of the sons of
the high priests, and he died in the land of the Chaldeans during the
captivity; having prophesied many things concerning the turning back of the
people; and concerning the return of the sons of Israel and concerning the
presence of the Lord [he prophesied]. And the leader of the people killed him
in his sojourning in Babylon, being rebuked by him over the worships of idols.
=12= And they buried him in the field of Maour in the tomb of Shem and
Arphaxad, fathers of Abraham; and the tomb is until this day a double cave;
for Abraham also in Hebron made the tomb of Sarah his wife after its likeness;
it is called double, because it is dug and hidden from ground level and there
is an upper story hanging upon a rock of the earth. This prophet therefore
gave a wonder to the people so as to attend to the river Chebar, that: when
its water fails, to expect the sickle of desolation unto the end of the land;
and when its water floods, it signifies the return of Jerusalem; which also
came to pass. For the holy man also lies in the land of the Assyrians, and
many were gathering to his tomb for prayer and supplication. And once when a
multitude of the Jews had come together there for his memorial, the Chaldeans
fearing lest they rise up; and they were about to come upon them and kill
them; and the prophet made the water of the river stand, that the sons of
Israel might flee, having come to the other side. And those who dared to
pursue the people after them were drowned. This prophet through prayer
spontaneously furnished them abundant food of fishes, because they were being
destroyed by famine; and from the multitude of the fishes they were fed; and
for many who were failing he entreated life to come from God. This =13=
prophet, when the people were being destroyed by the enemies, stood before
their leaders and troubled them. Through wonders and having been frightened
the enemies ceased. Then Ezekiel was saying to the people, that "we have
failed; our hope has perished." And by signs of the bones of the dead he
persuaded them, that there is hope for Israel both here and in the age to
come. This man, being there in the country of the Chaldeans, declared to the
people all the things happening in Jerusalem and in the temple. This prophet
was snatched from the country of the Chaldeans and was brought to Jerusalem
for the rebuke of the unbelieving. This Ezekiel saw according to Moses the
place of the temple of the Lord and the wall broad, as Daniel also said, that
it will be built. This man in Babylon judged the tribe of Dan and Gad, because
they were impious toward the Lord, persecuting those who believe in the law,
and he made a great wonder for them, that the serpents were consuming their
infants and all their cattle. And he foretold, that because of them the people
will not return to Jerusalem, but they will be in their own =14= until the
consummation of their error; for from among them was also the one who killed
him. For he was opposing him until the day of his death.


10. Daniel the prophet. This man was of the tribe of Judah of the race of the
most distinguished of the royal service; but while still an infant he was
brought to Babylon from Judea. And he was born in Bethebor the Upper near
Jerusalem; and he was a temperate man, so that the Jews thought him to be a
eunuch. This man mourned much for Jerusalem and practiced in fasts from every
desirable food and he was a man very dry in appearance; but fair in the grace
of God. And this man prayed much concerning Nebuchadnezzar, because he became
a beast and a cattle, that he might not perish. For his foreparts were as an
ox together with the head and his feet with the hinder parts as a lion. For
these things the rulers have in their youth, the being as an ox coming under
the yoke of Beliar through pleasures, at the end becoming beasts seizing,
destroying, striking and killing. Therefore this =15= holy man knew, that as
an ox he was eating grass and food of human nature was coming to be for him.
For this reason also Nebuchadnezzar after the sending of his scourge, having
come to be in a human heart, was weeping and was asking God every day and
night forty times, entreating the God Beemon, which is the God of Israel. For
a spirit of Satan was coming upon him and it was escaping his notice, that he
had become a man. His tongue was bound from speaking, and a voice of a beast
was being given him; and nodding he immediately wept, and his eyes became as
flesh from weeping. And many rulers and citizens going out from the city were
beholding him. But Daniel alone did not wish to see him; because all the time
of his alteration he was in the prayers concerning him, and he was saying,
that "he will become a man again and will hold his kingdom"; and they did not
believe him, the seven years becoming seven months as he was entreating
concerning him toward the Lord. The mystery of the seven times was completed
upon him, because as a mediator he restored seven. For six years and six
months therefore he was falling down before the Lord God and was confessing
his own impiety. And after the remission of his lawlessness God gave him back
the kingdom again. And he neither ate meats nor =16= drank wine, confessing to
God, because Daniel commanded him to propitiate the Lord with pulse or greens
or soaked things. For this reason also Nebuchadnezzar called Daniel Baltasar
according to the name of his only-begotten son; and he wished to appoint him
joint-heir of his children; but the holy man said: "far be it from me, to
leave the inheritance of my fathers and to be joined to the inheritance of the
uncircumcised." And among the kings of the Persians also he did many wonders,
as many as we have not written in this book; but they are written in the book
of Daniel. This prophet gave a sign in the high mountains of Babylon saying:
that "when you see the mountain from the north smoking, the end of Babylon
will come; but when you see it burning as with fire, the end of all the earth
will come; but if the mountain in the south nods waters, the people will
return in their land; Jerusalem will be built broad and spacious, as also came
to pass through Jesus the high priest and Zerubbabel the son of Salathiel. But
if the mountain flow with blood, there will be a slaughter of Beliar in all
the earth, and the Lord will come upon the earth having become man." There
therefore =17= the holy Daniel fell asleep in Babylon in peace and was buried
in the royal cave gloriously; and his tomb is until this present day known to
all in Babylon.

11. Hosea the prophet: son of Beeri. This man was born in Belomoth of the
tribe of Issachar. This man gave a sign that the Lord would come from heaven
upon the earth; and this the sign of his presence: when the oak that is in
Shiloh is split of itself into twelve parts and there become twelve oaks; thus
also it came to pass. And having prophesied many things concerning the
fornication of his people, he died in peace and was buried in his own land.


12. And Amos the prophet. This man was born in Tekoa of the land of Zebulun.
And he is father of Isaiah the prophet. And Amaziah the priest of Bethel,
having often beaten him with a drum, was railing at him by ambush, and at the
end the son of Amaziah killed him, having struck him with a club upon the
temple, because he was rebuking him concerning the ambush of the two golden
heifers. And still breathing he came into his own land; and he died and was
buried there with his fathers.

13. Micah the prophet. This man was born in Morathi =18= of the tribe of
Ephraim. This man did many things to Ahab king of Judah; and he is killed by
Joram his son, having been thrown from a precipice, because he was rebuking
him for his impieties and those of his fathers which they did. And having died
he was buried in Morathi in his own land quite alone near the common
burial-place; and his tomb is well known until this present day.

14. And Joel the prophet. This man was from the field of Bethom of the land of
Reuben; and he prophesied many things concerning Jerusalem and the end of the
nations. And having seen, he died in peace and was buried at Bethom in his own
land gloriously.

15. Obadiah the prophet. This man was from Shechem from the field of
Bethachamar. This is the third captain of fifty, whom Elijah the Tishbite
spared, and he went down and came to the king. And after these things, leaving
his royal rank, he attached himself to the prophet Elijah and became his
disciple; and having endured many things on his account he was kept safe; and
having died he was buried in the field of Bethachamar with his fathers.

16. Jonah the prophet. This man then was from the land of Kariathmaoum =19=
near Azotus a city of Greeks by the sea. Elijah the prophet was then rebuking
Ahab king of Samaria and having called a great famine upon the land he fled in
the wilderness; and he was fed from the ravens; and he was drinking water from
the brook. And when the brook dried up he hungered. And he came to Sarephtha
of Sidonia to a poor widow woman, who was the mother of Jonah, and he entered
into her house; and she did for him as he commanded her; and he ate and he
blessed her with grain and oil and he remained with her. For he could not
remain with the uncircumcised and her son Jonah having died, God raised him
through Elijah and gave him back living to his mother on account of the
hospitality. And having come of age Jonah was sent to Nineveh to the
Assyrians. And Jonah sought to flee the Lord and was swallowed by the
sea-monster, wishing to go to Tharsis; and having come out from the
sea-monster he preached the destruction of Nineveh. And the Ninevites repented
toward God and were shown mercy. And Jonah was grieved, and having turned back
he did not remain in his own land, but taking his mother he sojourned in Sur
the country of the foreigners. For he was saying, that "thus I will take away
the reproach, because I lied when I prophesied against =20= Nineveh." And
having settled in the land of Saar he died there and was buried in the cave of
Kenezeos the judge. And he prophesied that: when they see in Jerusalem many
nations from the western parts, it shall be made to vanish to the ground.

17. And Nahum the prophet. This man was from Elkesi beyond the Jordan toward
Begabar; of the tribe of Simeon. This man after Jonah gave a sign upon
Nineveh, that it will perish by sweet waters and earthly fire, which also came
to pass. For the lake surrounding it in an earthquake and fire coming from the
desert burned all its higher parts. And Nahum died in peace, and he was buried
in his own land Begabar.

18. Habakkuk the prophet. This man was from the field of Byzzouchar of the
tribe of Simeon. This man saw before the captivity concerning the capture of
Jerusalem, and he mourned exceedingly over the city and the people. And when
Nebuchadnezzar came to Jerusalem


to plunder it, this man fled to Ostracine; and he was there a sojourner into
the land of Ishmael. But when the Chaldeans returned from Jerusalem to their
land, having made it plunder, and the remnant who were in Jerusalem =21= went
down to Egypt. And he himself went up into his own land. He was ministering to
the harvesters of his own field. For he sowed barley. And when he cooked foods
for them, he prophesied to his own saying: "I am going to a far land and I
will return more quickly. But if I delay, carry to the harvesters to eat."
And having come to be in Babylon and having given breakfast to Daniel in the
den of the lions, having returned he stood by the harvesters as they were
eating; and he told no one what had happened. And the prophet understood,
that the people would return more quickly from Babylon to Jerusalem. And he
gave a sign to those in Judea, that they would see in the temple a great
light shining forth. And thus they would see the glory of God.
And concerning the consummation of the temple he foretold, that the plunder of
the temple of Jerusalem would come about by a western nation; then the
spreading of the Debir will be torn into two parts; and the capitals of the
two pillars will be taken away. And no one will know where they will be. And
these will be carried into the wilderness by angels, where in the beginning
the tabernacle of witness was pitched. And in them the Lord will be made known
at the end, because they will give light to those pursued by the serpent as
from the beginning and the Lord will save them from darkness and the shadow of
death and they will be in a holy tent. This prophet prophesied many things
concerning the coming of the Lord; two years before the return =22= of the
people from Babylon he died; and he was buried in his own field gloriously.

19. Zephaniah the prophet: this man then of the tribe of Simeon from the
mountain of Sarabatha; and he prophesied concerning the city of Jerusalem,
that it would be built better into length and breadth, and concerning the end
of the nations and the shame of the ungodly, and concerning the presence of
the Lord. And he died in a revelation of the Lord, and he was buried in his
field quite alone.

20. Haggai the prophet. While still young he returned from Babylon to
Jerusalem and he openly prophesied concerning the return of the people and he
saw the building of the temple of Jerusalem and he himself first sang there
alleluia, which is interpreted: let us praise the living God; amen, which is:
may it be, may it be; there therefore he died and was buried near the priests
gloriously; wherefore we say alleluia, which is a hymn of Haggai and
Zechariah.

21. Zechariah the prophet. This man was son of Barachiah. This man came from
the Chaldeans already advanced in years and being there he prophesied many
things to the people and he gave many signs for proof. This man said to
Josedek, that he will beget a son and in Jerusalem he will serve as priest to
the Lord. This man also blessed Salathiel concerning a son saying: that you
will beget a son and you will call the name Zerubbabel. And in the time of
Cyrus the king =23= of the Persians he gave a sign for victory concerning
Croesus the king of the Lydians and concerning Astyages the king of the Medes
and he prophesied concerning his service, which Cyrus would perform upon
Jerusalem. And he blessed him exceedingly. And the things of his prophecy in
Jerusalem and concerning the end of the nations and concerning the building of
the temple of Jerusalem and the idleness of prophets and priests and the
double judgment he set forth. And he died in Judea in a long old age and was
buried near Haggai the prophet.

22. Malachi the prophet was of the tribe of Zebulun. This man after the return
of the people from Babylon is born in Sopha in the land of Zebulun. And while
still young he had a good life, and he prophesied many things concerning the
sojourning of the Lord and concerning the judgment of the dead; and that the
customs of Moses will be fulfilled and will be changed. And since all the
people honored him as holy and meek, it called him malachi, which is
interpreted angel. For he was also very fair in appearance, but also


as many things as he himself said in prophecy, on that very day an angel
appearing confirmed his prophecy a second time, as it came to pass in the days
of the anarchy, as it is written in Spharphotheim, that is in the book of
Judges. And while young the prophet died and was added to his fathers in his
field.

23. Another Zechariah, prophet and priest. This man was =24= son of Jodae the
priest, and father of John the Baptist. Herod the king killed this man next to
the altar and the house of the Lord. And this man was from Jerusalem from the
house of David, in the midst of the Elam from the house of the Lord. This man
prophesied concerning the generation of Christ. There therefore they buried
him in the house of God next to the temple of the Lord. From then there came
about many wonders in the temple and phantasies and the priests were no longer
able to see a vision of angels; nor to give oracles to the people in the Debir
nor to inquire in the Ephod nor to answer through the lights, as before, until
that day.

24. Simeon the priest: this man was of the tribe of Aaron. This man received
an oracle from the Holy Spirit that he would not see death until he should see
the Christ in flesh. This man carried the Lord into his own arms. And he
glorified God saying: my eyes have seen your salvation. Now you dismiss your
servant, Master, according to your word in peace. And he died and was buried
near the priests, an old man full of days.

<25.> John the Baptist son of Zechariah and Eli =25= zabeth. This man was of
the tribe of Levi. This man declared to us the lamb of God the son of the
Father who took away the sin of the world. This man became an entrance for us
of the kingdom of God. For there is none among those born of women greater
than John the Baptist. And he died, his head having been cut off by Herod,
because of Herodias the wife of Philip his brother. And there were also other
prophets, whose names are written in their generations, in the book of names
of Israel, of whom we have not made mention.
