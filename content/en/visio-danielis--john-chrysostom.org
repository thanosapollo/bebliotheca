* The Vision of Daniel
:PROPERTIES:

:END:

A discourse of our father among the saints John Chrysostom from the vision of
Daniel. Hear, O heaven, with exactness, how the four kingdoms were gathered
with one another: Ethiopians, Macedonians, Greeks, and Romans. These are the
four winds in which Daniel the prophet saw shaking the great sea. Philip, the
father of Alexander, himself married the daughter of Chouseth, king of
Ethiopia, from whom Alexander the Macedonian is born. He himself founded
Alexandria the Great and reigned in it (sic) fifteen years and became master
of many lands and cities, even as far as the sea of the so-called country of
the Sun. And he went in as far as the eastern land and there he found an
untraceable nation, every filthy little beast, snakes, cats, flies, and every
creeping thing. And they did not bury the dead, but rather they ate them, and
the miscarriages of the women, a form of unclean beasts. They did not bury the
dead, but rather they ate them. Alexander, having observed these things being
done by them in the fields, fearing lest they somehow spread upon the earth
and defile it from their filthy practices, earnestly besought the Lord and
gathered them, men with women and children, and, to speak simply, all their
camps, and led them out from the eastern land and pursued after them until
they entered into the parts of the north. And there is no entrance from the
east or from the west. Then again he entreated God and shut them up there with
bronze gates, smearing asynchyton. These filthy and counterfeit nations, then,
have used all the magical evil-arts, and in these things of theirs the nature
of the asynchyton has abolished their filthy and inhuman, or rather even
god-hating, sorcery. And in those 34 times, according to the prophecy of (the)
Ezekiel that says: on the last day of the consummation of the world there will
come out Choth, Gog, Anes, Ageg, Achenaz, Pharbatinos, the Phazenoi, the
Olbians, the Sarmatians, the Thyalaioi, and man-eaters. These twenty-two kings
have been established within the Caspian Gates which Alexander the king
constructed. Byzas then, the king who founded Byzantium, sent into Ethiopia to
Phol king of Ethiopia his general Germanicus, to make peace with him, writing
to him also concerning Chouseth his daughter, that he might take her as wife
and reign with her. The king then, having received the letters from Germanicus
and having seen the honors from him, rejoiced greatly, and he himself rose up
and gathered from all the nations of Ethiopia a multitude of an army. And
taking also his daughter Chouseth he went to Byzantium and gave a great
largesse, having with himself forty thousand Ethiopians. And king Byzas
received them across the sea in Chalcedon with very great gladness, gave many
gifts to those with him, and entered into Byzantium and gave a great largesse
and very great gifts according to the royal custom. And Byzas the king took
Chouseth, daughter of the king of Ethiopia, from whom a daughter is born to
him, whom he also named after the name of his city Byzantia. And her Romulus
the king of Rome, Archelaus, married, and because of her surpassing beauty he
loved her exceedingly. And he himself was exceedingly rich and great-souled,
whence also in the dowries he gifted Rome. And his magnates, having heard,
were indignant against him. And Byzantia also bore three sons, and the first
she named according to his appellation


Archelaus, and the other Urbanus, and the third Claudius. They reigned then
each of them, Archelaus in Rome, and Urbanus in Byzantium, the city of his
mother, Claudius in Alexandria. And the seed of Chouseth, daughter of the king
of Ethiopia, mastered the 35 three, Macedonia, of the Romans and of the
Greeks, that is, of the Romans from the race of the Ethiopians. For holy
David, hinting at this, has said these things: Ethiopia shall stretch out her
hand to God. For the kingdom of the Christians, being constituted from the
race of the Ethiopians, possesses the great and venerable wood of the precious
and life-giving cross, whence this same kingdom of the Christians is strong
above every kingdom, and there is no nation or kingdom under heaven able to
dominate the kingdom of the Christians of the Romans. For as we fittingly said
beforehand, beloved, the all-honorable, life-giving, and strong cross in the
midst of the earth, by which the ends of the inhabited world too are very
clearly marked out according to breadth and length and height and depth. For
what kingdom prevails against the rule of the Romans? For let us hear what the
divine Paul proclaimed beforehand concerning the last day and the kingdom of
the Christians, I mean of course in the letter to the Thessalonians thus. And
I am asked of you and the rest until they are shown that he is God. For the
rest this one who alone restrains, that he be taken out of the midst, and then
the lawless one will be revealed. What then is that which is out of the midst
if not the kingdom of the Christians? For every rule and authority of this
world will be abolished except this one. For it also is warred against and is
not poured out, and all the nations clash with it and will be consumed by it
and they will hold until the last day has come. And this is the "shall stretch
out her hand to God," according to the apostle who says: when every rule and
authority of this world has been abolished, then she herself will hand over to
the king, to God and Father. What kingdom? Clearly that of the Christians, for
there is no kingdom more powerful than it. And because of the multitude of
their sins God will be angered away from it and will call the hunter Ishmael,
and he will move like the sand of the sea, the innumerable, and they will fly
like hawks upon the high mountains, and their horses will leap out as if
flying like eagles, and they will enter into the 36 seven-hilled city in
chariots and horses, and on account of these, of much bloodshed, having made
not a little, and also spoils. What must one say? And Ishmael entered, to the
angel, as far as Attalia. And I Daniel said to the angel, My Lord, why have
these afflictions come upon the Christians? And the angel said to me, Not
because the Lord God loves Ishmael will he give him the authority to master
the land of the Romans, but because of the sins of those dwelling in it. And
the honor of the priests will be taken away, and the sacrifice will fail from
the churches, and wickedness will be filled up upon men, and the people will
become as wild beasts, and priests will be as the common people. In that time,
that is, in the hebdomatic time in which the number of the Ishmaelites and of
their dominion is fulfilled, which they mastered, and they laid waste both
Romania and Pisidia and the remaining islands found near Rome, Calabria and
Sicily, the city called Tyrannis. Then the Ishmaelites, having blasphemed,
will say that the Romans have no deliverance from our hands. Then suddenly
those of that city called Tyrannis will come out and will find through a
revelation of God in the midst of their city a certain man whose name was
lesser in the world. And having seized this man they will lead him as far as
Dine and there they will anoint him as king, whom the men held as if dead and
of no use. And his name is in the thirtieth chapter. This man will go out
against the Ishmaelites in a certain place called Petrinos and they will join
a strong war. And in that place there is a two-mouthed well


and there they will cut one another down so that from the bloods of the Romans
and of the Ishmaelites the well becomes full. And the Lord God will deliver
Ishmael into the hands of the king, and after these things he will send into
their countries and will make a construction of ships and will launch them.
And he will send other messengers of his into the inner parts of Rome and will
tame the fair-haired nations, and together they will pursue Ishmael. And then
the prophecy will be fulfilled that says that a dog and a cub will pursue a
field. And as the 37 king is going to Rome he will enter into a place called
Lombardy, and those of that place will array themselves against him, and
having entreated these he will enter into Rome, and having come to a certain
place where a treasure was locked, he strikes with his scourge upon the zodon,
where the treasure is shut up. And by the command of God they will be opened,
and from it he will make donatives for his people. And he will go out from
Rome with a very great people and will go through the mainland into the
seven-hilled city, and there will be none opposing him, because the Lord God
was with him. And the fear of him will be heard in every city and land, and
the one of the two-hundredth chapter of the thirtieth chapter, having heard,
will flee from the seven-hilled city into the inner part of the east and will
perish badly. Then a young man will arise from the tribe of Dan according to
the prophecy of Jacob the patriarch that says: a serpent sitting upon a road,
biting a horse's heel, and the horseman will fall backward, awaiting the
salvation of the Lord. The horseman is the truth and the piety of the orthodox
Christians, and (the heel) is the last day. And these holy ones in the time
will be bitten by the serpent, that is, the son of perdition, into his
fantasies and into his false-divinations; for he will do signs and wonders
upon the earth, andranē (sic) and faded. The blind will look up and the lame
will walk, the deaf will hear and those with demons will be healed, he will
turn the sun into darkness and the moon into blood, and in these his signs and
fantastical wonders, with deceits, and he will deceive if possible even the
elect, as it is written. For the son of perdition will enter into Jerusalem
and will sit in the temple of the Lord equal to God, being a fleshly man, from
the seed of a man and from the womb of a woman, being from the tribe of Dan.
For Judas Iscariot who betrayed (namely the Lord) was himself also from the
tribe of Dan. For as the affliction of those days is multiplied from the 38
son of perdition, the divine does not bear to look upon the destruction of the
race of men whom the Son and Word of God redeemed with his own blood, but he
sends shortly his own servants, both Enoch and Elijah, for the refutation of
this adversary before all nations, saying that this is the deceiver, the
adversary, and the abomination of desolation. The nations then, seeing him
being refuted by the servants, Enoch and Elijah, will flee from him and will
follow after the saints. The enemy then, seeing himself being terribly refuted
by them and being despised by all, in wrath and anger will kill the saints.
Then the sign of the son of men will appear with heavenly glory, and the Lord
will kill him with the spirit of his mouth according to the voice of the
apostle. Then the righteous will shine as luminaries in the world, holding the
word of life, and the impious will be driven out and will be turned back into
Hades, from which may we be delivered by the grace and love toward man of our
Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory and the might, with the Father and the
Holy Spirit, now and always and unto the ages of ages, amen.
