** ON THE LITTLE INHERITANCE
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:

When after many years he had returned from the palace to his homeland most honored, being already consul, having entered the little villa which his father had left he played in these verses in the Lucilian style:

Hail, little inheritance, kingdoms of my ancestors,
which great-grandfather, which grandfather, which father cultivated,
which already an older man left me by a hastened death:
alas, I had not wished to be able to enjoy it so soon!
It is indeed a just series to succeed a father, but
for the pious a more pleasing order is to be masters together.
Now labor and cares are mine; before, pleasure alone
was in our parts, the rest were the father's.
A small little inheritance, I confess, but no thing was
ever small to the even-minded, add also the like-minded.
I think it fair that the thing stand from the mind, not the mind from the thing.
Croesus desires all things, Diogenes nothing:

Aristippus scatters gold in the midst of the Syrtes,
all golden Lydia is not enough for Midas.
He who has no end of desiring has no end of having:
that is the measure of wealth which you set in the mind.
But learn how great this field of mine is, also that you may
know me and know yourself too, if you can.
Although it is hard to know oneself; know yourself

as quickly as we read it, so quickly we neglect it.
I cultivate twice a hundred iugera of field, a vineyard is cultivated
with a hundred iugera and meadows with half,
wood above double what meadows and vineyard and ploughland are;
a cultivator of the field neither exceeds us nor is wanting,
a spring nearby and a short well, then a pure stream too;
this navigable, ebbing, carries me and draws me back,
fruits are stored for me always for a twin year.
For whom the store is not long, for him also hunger is ready.
This of mine is situated neither far from the city, nor altogether at the city,
that I may not suffer crowds and that I may possess goods.
And as often as disgusts compel me to change place,
I cross and enjoy by turns country or city.
