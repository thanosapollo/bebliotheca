* Prayer for the Possessed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

Prayer of Chrysostom toward those suffering from demons, and every weakness.
=64.1061=
Let us beseech the Lord. Eternal God, who redeemed the race of men from the
captivity of the devil, deliver this your servant from every energy of unclean
spirits, command the wicked and unclean spirits and demons to depart from the
soul and the body of this your servant, and not to remain nor to hide in him.
Let them be put to flight in your holy name, and of your only-begotten Son,
and of your life-giving Spirit, from the fashioning of your hands. That,
having been cleansed from every diabolic despite, he may live holily and
justly and piously, being deemed worthy of the undefiled mysteries of your
only-begotten Son and our God, with whom you are blessed and glorified with
your all-holy and good and life-giving Spirit, now and always, and unto the
ages of ages. Amen.
