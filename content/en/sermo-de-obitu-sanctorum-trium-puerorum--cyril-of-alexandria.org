A DISCOURSE OF CYRIL ARCHBISHOP OF ALEXANDRIA ON THE END OF THE HOLY THREE
CHILDREN AND OF ALL-WISE DANIEL.
A pretext of times has stood by, beloved, to bring a memory of good martyrs;
for this memory imitates the panegyric of the heavenly ranks, upon
intercession and salvation of our souls; as many then as know God, today
stretch forth your hearings here, that you may be deemed worthy of the prize
of the martyrs just now spoken; for these must be praised and wondered at who
have not yet seen an incarnation of Christ, and a death of an immortal, and a
resurrection from the dead, and before the incarnate presence have become
confessors and martyrs of him; concerning these the providence of the
undefiled Trinity through the prophecy just now read, for the sake of the
royal children of the same race, Ananias, Azarias, and Misael; concerning
these I wish to interpret to the Church of God the way of life and advocate
skill and alliance and perfection, how the fair-branch children having played
the man were perfected, etc.
This construction of the good covenant I Cyril, least bishop of the city of
the Alexandrians, obtained in the covenant of Daniel; and I wrote a symbol and
way of life of holy and blameless children, and the perfection into Christ.
And may it be by the grace of Christ, and by the intercession of the
fair-conquering martyrs just now remembered, that the Church of Christ bloom
and advance flourishing in fruit of righteousness; that the fathers and the
mothers sprouting such plants with temperance and piety, the young men and
virgins may be deemed worthy of such a way of life, may grow together of such
a virtue. And the holy children are perfected on the 17th of the month
December.
