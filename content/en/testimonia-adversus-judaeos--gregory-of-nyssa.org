III. Concerning his birth from a Virgin.

undefiled body?) Isaiah, "And they shall wish they had been burned with fire,

XI. Concerning circumcision.
And all the Jews will say, that If you worship the same God, why do you not
circumcise, or offer animals for sacrifice, or keep sabbath, the Scriptures
declaring concerning these things? They shall hear then, that circumcision was
not from the beginning, but came to be in time, as also in time it was to
cease; then, that God having arranged circumcision for the fathers, himself
said, "Behold days are coming says the Lord, and I will make with the house of
Israel a new covenant, not according to the covenant which I made with their
fathers, when I took their hand, to lead them out of the land of Egypt,
because they did not remain in my covenant, and I neglected them, says the
Lord." And again blaming the Jews he says, "All =46.220= the nations are
uncircumcised in flesh, but this people in the heart." And again, "Circumcise
your heart, and not the flesh of your uncircumcision." And again, "Make new
for yourselves a newness, and do not sow upon thorns; but circumcise the
hardness of your heart." Jeremiah, "And circumcise to God the uncircumcision
of your heart." And that circumcision justifies no one, is clear from these
things: Abraham uncircumcised was well-pleasing to God; for first he was seen
well-pleasing, and then he gives him the circumcision. And those in the
wilderness, having been born, in the forty years were uncircumcised; and those
from Adam likewise until Abraham were well-pleasing to God, all were
uncircumcised; for because of the people mixing into the nations, circumcision
was given, and the cause is clear; for the things of circumcision took their
beginning from Abraham, whom God having loved above all those then, proposed
that from him the Christ also according to the flesh should be born. In order
then that what he willed might be able to come to be purely, and
unadulteratedly, for this reason there was need of circumcision, and of
unmixing with the remaining races, so that as some partition, the law might
separate them from the remaining men. Whence also when the one for whom these
things came to be was born, thereafter as superfluous to God the things that
were alien to the ancient constitution have been cast out.

XIII. Concerning keeping the Sabbath.
The Sabbath was given to them, to make cease their money-loving desire. For
the people being poor, having gone out from Egypt, and having nothing, except
what they borrowed from the Egyptians, each made haste through unyielding toil
to procure wealth for himself. Therefore he says, "Six days you shall work;
but on the seventh day you shall not do any work in it." And he adds the
cause, that you may rest, and your wife, and your son, and your daughter, and
your servant, your ox, and your beast of burden. Since for what cause did
Jesus the son of Nave, circling Jericho with trumpets for seven days, not keep
leisure for the Sabbath? And how on the eighth day is a child circumcised? And
why does a woman give birth on a Sabbath day? And how do the priests also do
every sacrifice on a Sabbath? And how do sun, and moon, and stars complete the
appointed course even on the Sabbath?
XIV. Concerning being sealed.

"You have given to those =46.224= who fear you a token, to flee from the face

needed sacrifices, or whole burnt offerings, but since the Levitical tribe did
not have a lot in the land of promise, because God himself is its lot, he gave
them this as a pretext for being fed; for he did not wish them, while serving
as priests, to be fed from toil and business, that is, from huckstering.

buildings of wickedness, and of worthless doctrines, having planted the better
things. David, "Deliver me from the contradiction of a people, you shall set
me for a head of nations. A people, whom I did not know, served me; at the
hearing of the ear it obeyed me; alien sons lied to me; alien sons grew old
and went lame from their paths." Moses from the person of God to Abraham, "And
in you all the ends of the earth shall be blessed." And again, "I will turn my
face away from them, and I will show what shall be for them at the last,
because it is a generation turned aside. Sons in whom there is not faith; they
provoked me to jealousy over a no-God, they angered me in their idols, and I
will provoke them to jealousy over a no-nation; I will anger them over a
nation without understanding." And elsewhere, "He will give," he says, "the
wicked for his burial." Isaiah, "I became manifest to those who do not seek
me, I was found by those who do not inquire of me. (I said, Behold I am
present to a nation, who did not call upon my name.") David, "I became
alienated from my brothers, and a stranger to the sons of my mother." And
concerning the nations he says, "I will declare your name to my brothers, in
the midst of a Church I will hymn you." Concerning Jews, "Because of you my
name is blasphemed continually among the nations." Malachi, "There is not my
will in you, says the Lord almighty." (And concerning Nations, "Because from
the risings of the sun until the settings my name has been glorified among the
Nations, says the Lord almighty;") "and in every place incense is brought to
my name, and great is my name among the Nations, says the Lord almighty." (And
concerning Jews he says,) "But you profane it." David, "Ask of me, and I will
give you nations as your inheritance, and as your possession the ends of the
earth." Moses, "Suffer me to blot out this people, and I will give you a great
nation, and much, =46.228= more than this." Isaiah concerning Jews, "Hear a
word of the Lord, sons of Sodom; give heed to a law of God, people of
Gomorrah." And again, "Your Sabbaths, and the new moons my soul hates; and I
will not accept your prayers; wash, become clean; your hands are defiled with
blood." And that the law clearly declaring that one must not keep festival
outside Jerusalem and the temple according to the, "To you praise is fitting,
O God, in Zion; and to you a vow shall be rendered in Jerusalem," it has been
written, "That they shall worship the Lord each from his place." And again the
Prophet, "Great is his name among the Nations, and in


every place incense is offered to his name, and a pure sacrifice." "But you
the Lord shall take away; and to those who serve me a new name shall be
called, which shall be blessed upon all the earth." And what is newer than the
name of Christians? And David, "They shall announce his righteousness to a
people that shall be born, whom the Lord made" And that David's saying, "God
settles the solitary in a house," signifies the unmixing of those under the
law toward the Nations. Isaiah, "All the earth shall be filled with knowing
the Lord, as much water to cover a sea." David, "All the ends of the earth
shall remember and shall turn toward the Lord, and all the families of the
nations shall worship before him, because the kingdom is the Lord's, and he is
master of the nations." And it is good also to inquire of the Jews, who is the
one who holds now then, whether the Mosaic law, of which there is now
transgression, and not keeping, because there is not even the temple, or the
place, or a prophet announcing beforehand something of the things to come, or
the ark, in which are the tablets, and the Decalogue, in which it was
necessary to fulfill the three feasts, and not elsewhere. And, "That the
kingdom of God shall be taken from the Jews, and shall be given to a nation
producing its fruits." And this would be the nation of Christians, exhibiting
the fitting fruits of the manner of life according to God. David, "Confession
and magnificence is his work, and his righteousness remains unto the age of
the age." And the blameless one confesses to the Father, not his own sins, but
those of the ones who believe upon him; because when David said, "The Lord
said to my Lord: Sit at my right hand," and the rest, Isaiah says more
clearly, "The Lord says to my Christ Cyrus." And they say these things have
been said unto Cyrus the Persian. But it is laughable; for where do the
=46.229= remaining things fit Cyrus, "I have held your right hand; nations
shall hearken before him, and I will break the strength of kings; I will open
gates before him, and cities shall not be shut; I will go before you, and I
will make mountains level; I will crush bronze doors, and I will break iron
bars, and I will give you dark treasures?" How then will these things fit
Cyrus, who a little later was shamefully slain among the Massagetae and flayed
into a wineskin? and after a little the same prophet interprets them, saying
from the person of God, "I the Lord God who called you in righteousness; I
held your hand, and I will strengthen you, and I will give you for a covenant
of a race, for a light of nations, to open eyes of the blind, and to lead out
those bound from bonds, and those sitting in darkness from a house of guard."
And again the same Isaiah, "And he said to me, This is a great thing for you,
to be called my child, and to set up the tribes of Jacob, and to turn the
dispersion of Israel; and I have set you for a covenant of a race, for a light
of nations, that you may be for salvation unto the end of the earth."
XVII. That before the second presence of the Lord Elijah shall come.
Malachi the prophet says, "Behold I send you Elijah the Tishbite, before the
great and manifest day of the Lord comes, who shall restore hearts of fathers
toward sons, and a heart of a man toward his neighbor, lest coming I strike
the earth utterly."
XVIII. That we shall be called Christians.
Isaiah says, "Nations shall see your glory, and all the kings your
righteousness, and he shall call you the new name, which the Lord shall name
it." And again, "And to those who serve me a new name shall be called, which
shall be blessed upon the earth." Hosea, "And it shall be at the last his name
shall be manifest in all the earth, and by the


name of him many peoples shall be called, and having walked according to his
ways, they shall live in them." =46.232=
XIX. That Herod shall be troubled, and all those with him.
Jeremiah says, "In that day, says the Lord, the heart of the king shall
perish, and the heart of the rulers, and the priests shall be beside
themselves, and the prophets shall wonder."
XX. Concerning Baptism.
Ezekiel, "And the Lord said to me: This water that goes forth into Galilee,
shall sanctify the waters. And it shall be, for every soul, upon which this
water shall come, it shall live and be healed;" Jeremiah, "Behold as a lion he
shall come up from the midst of the Jordan."
XXI. Concerning the descent of the Lord into Egypt.
Isaiah says, "Behold the Lord sits upon a light cloud, and he shall come into
Egypt, and all the handmade things of Egypt shall be shaken." And again, "The
Lord shall be known to the Egyptians, and they shall fear the Lord in that
day." XXII. Concerning the Holy Spirit. Isaiah, "And now the Lord has sent me,
and his Spirit." From Exodus, "And he said to Moses: Bring up to me seventy
elders, and I will take from the Spirit that is upon you, and I will pour out
upon them, and I will take you up." David, "Create a clean heart in me, O God,
and renew a right Spirit in my inward parts, and do not take your Holy Spirit
away from me, and establish me with a governing Spirit." And again, "Where
shall I go from your Spirit?" Job, "A divine Spirit is that which made me."
And again, "There is a Spirit in mortals, and the breath of the Almighty is
that which teaches me." And again, "The Lord lives, who has thus judged me,
and the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter. And a divine Spirit that which
remains for me in the nostrils." Zechariah, "I am among you, and my Spirit has
stood in the midst of you." Solomon, "The Spirit of the Lord has filled the
inhabited world." =46.233= Isaiah: "They provoked the Holy Spirit, and it was
turned for them into enmity. The house of Jacob provoked the Spirit of the
Lord." Joel: "And it shall be in the last days, I will pour out from my Spirit
upon all flesh," that is, the believing, "and upon your sons, and upon your
daughters," and the rest. Isaiah: "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, for
which cause he has anointed me," and, "Seven spirits shall rest upon him."
And, "The Spirit of the Lord came down, and guided them." And, "A Spirit of
knowledge filled Bezalel the architect of the tent; a Spirit being provoked,
and a Spirit that took up Elijah in a chariot, and was sought from Elisha
double." And, "A Spirit shall go forth from me, and every breath I made." And,
"Behold my child the chosen, my beloved, in whom my soul was well pleased, I
will put my Spirit upon him." And, "Woe, apostate children, thus says the
Lord: You made a counsel not
