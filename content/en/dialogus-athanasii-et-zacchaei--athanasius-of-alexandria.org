* Dialogue of Athanasius and Zacchaeus

Discourse of Athanasius, archbishop of Alexandria, to Zacchaeus, teacher of
the law of the Jews.

1 Zacchaeus said: You Christians go astray, first in that you suppose there
are also other gods besides the one and only God, Scripture everywhere saying
that God is one: Hear, it says, O Israel. The Lord your God is one; and again:
I am God first and I am after these things, and besides me there is no God.
And again: Hear my people and I will speak to you, Israel, and I will testify
to you; Israel, if you hear me, there is in you no recent god, nor shall you
worship a strange god. For I am the Lord your God, who brought you up from the
land of Egypt. And a myriad of other such things. And second, that you also
call Christ God, and him passible and from a woman, and hearing it you are not
ashamed.

2 Athanasius said: Do you then want me first to show you that Christ too is
written as God in Scripture, and they are not two gods. And thus, that Christ
is also passible, and from a woman. And that it is no shame to those who call
upon him? Zacchaeus said: Show that Christ too is God and they are not two
gods. Athanasius said: First be taught that Christ is God, and then you will
learn that there are not two gods. Zacchaeus: Speak.

3 Athanasius said: I want to lead you from the first book, and so in order
bring you on to the others. Zacchaeus said: You cannot show it. Athanasius
said: Even if Moses says that God has said, Let us make man according to our
image and likeness. To whom do you say God said this?

4 Zacchaeus said: Was Christ then already there, the one born under Caesar
Augustus? Athanasius said: He was always, being God the Word; but he was
called Christ when united to the flesh. Zacchaeus said: From where is it clear
that he was? Athanasius said: First let us know to whom he was speaking, and
thus it is found whether the one made flesh is the same, or not.

5 Zacchaeus said: I say that it is not Christ to whom he was speaking.
Athanasius said: Did God speak to anyone at all, Let us make man according to
our image and according to likeness? Zacchaeus said: He was speaking to
himself. Athanasius said: But he did not say I will make man, but Let us make.

6 Zacchaeus said: He was speaking to the angels. Athanasius said: And do you
call the angels fellow-workers of God? Zacchaeus said: And what is absurd in
that? Athanasius said: So then he no longer made all things in wisdom?

7 Zacchaeus said: And he did make all things in wisdom. Athanasius said: It is
clear then that he said to him, Let us make man according to our image and
likeness. Zacchaeus said: What then, is the wisdom of God Christ? Athanasius
said: Do not hurry, but with meekness and fear, having a good conscience, let
us inquire. Zacchaeus said: How can you show that the wisdom is Christ?
Athanasius said: Do you agree at all that God made all things in wisdom?
Zacchaeus said: So it is written.

8 Athanasius said: And this wisdom itself is also called his Word, as the
prophet says: By the word of the Lord the heavens were established. Zacchaeus
said: Yes, but he did not say the Christ. Athanasius said: For now confess
that the wisdom is the Word to whom he said Let us make man according to our
image and likeness, as being an image of the archetype. Zacchaeus said: Yes.

9 Athanasius said: And by saying our, he showed also the hypostasis of the one
hearing; for he did not say according to my image and likeness, but according
to our image and likeness. Zacchaeus said: Do you want to say that the wisdom
of God is another God? Athanasius said: There is no other God outside of God,
just as neither is the radiance of the light another light; but the light is
light, and the radiance is light, yet not another and another light. So also
the wisdom of God is God, yet not another and another God; for it is written:
I am God

and there is no other; and again: Lord our God, besides you we know no other.

10 Zacchaeus said: So then Christ is a goddess. Athanasius said: Say God, and
do not think as a Jew that upon the incorporeal the feminine names and the
masculine are spoken alike, as they are upon bodies; since even your soul is
called by a feminine name, soul, and there is not a female soul and a male
soul.

11 Zacchaeus said: Show me that the wisdom of God too is called God in its own
person. Athanasius said: For now it has been shown that there is some power
according to Scripture to whom God says, Let us make man according to our
image and likeness, and that this power is the wisdom of God the prophet said,
saying, You made all things in wisdom; and he named this same wisdom Word,
saying, By the word of the Lord the heavens were established, and by the
spirit of his mouth all their host. And that it is neither a word sent up
through vocal organs, nor a spirit through the respiratory ones, is clear from
God's being incorporeal.

12 Zacchaeus said: Let there be some power, even the wisdom to whom he says,
Let us make man according to our image and likeness; is the power also God?
Athanasius said: To this very power God says, Behold, Adam has become as one
of us. Zacchaeus said: For if he said, Behold, Adam has become as one of us,
God?

13 Athanasius said: Confess for now that there is some power with whom God
customarily converses, and it is she who says through Solomon: When he was
preparing the heaven, I was present with him, and I was beside him arranging,
and this: I was she in whom he rejoiced. Zacchaeus said: Did he say that I was
God? Athanasius said: Let the word advance in stature and spiritual wisdom.
Zacchaeus said: Show that there is also another who is God. Athanasius said:
If one has not learned the letters, he cannot read syllables. You must
therefore be schooled in the elements and so understand what is said and
signified through the letters.

14 Zacchaeus said: See, I have learned that there is some power in whom God
rejoiced; have you shown that she is also God? Athanasius said: If Scripture
says, And the Lord God appeared to Abraham. And he said, I will not hide from
Abraham my servant what I am doing. And Abraham shall become a great and
numerous nation. And in shall be blessed in him all the nations of the earth;
for he knew that he will charge his sons and his house after him, and they
will keep the way of the Lord God, to do alms and judgment, that the Lord God
may bring upon Abraham what he spoke to him. And the Lord God said, The cry of
Sodom and Gomorrah has been multiplied toward me, and their sins are very
great. I will go down therefore and see whether they are accomplishing
according to their cry that is coming to me. And if not, that I may know. And
the men turned away from there and came to Sodom. But Abraham was still
standing before the Lord. And Abraham blessed him and said, Will you destroy a
righteous man with an ungodly, and the righteous be as the ungodly? If there
are fifty righteous in the city, will you destroy them? Will you not spare the
whole place for the sake of the fifty righteous, if they are in it? By no
means shall you do this thing, to kill a righteous man with an ungodly, and
the righteous be as the ungodly. By no means shall you, who judge all the
earth, not do judgment. And the Lord God said, If I find in Sodom fifty
righteous in the city, I will spare the whole place for their sake. And so,
taking away five, and again five, he came as far as ten. Whom then do you say
spoke these things to Abraham, God himself, or his power? Zacchaeus said: God
himself.

15 Athanasius said: When Scripture says, And the Lord God rained upon Sodom
and Gomorrah brimstone and fire from the Lord out of heaven. And he overthrew
these cities and all the country round about. From which Lord then did the
Lord God rain brimstone and fire upon Sodom and Gomorrah? Zacchaeus said: It
does not say the Lord God rained, but the Lord rained. Athanasius: And most of
the copies have, The Lord God. Let us grant

that God is not set down; is it not clear that the one who rained is the one
who said to Lot, Behold, I have admired your face also in this matter, of not
overthrowing the city concerning which you spoke.

16 Zacchaeus said: No; but the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrah from the
Lord fire and brimstone. That is, an angel rained from the Lord God.
Athanasius: And you call the angel of the Lord Lord, but you do not call his
power Lord? Zacchaeus said: I said that he called the angel Lord, raining from
the Lord God. For did he call the power Lord?

17 Athanasius said: We said at the beginning of the discourse that it is
written, He made all things in wisdom; clearly, if all things, this too; for
this also is one of all things, the raining of fire and brimstone upon Sodom
and Gomorrah. Zacchaeus said: So the Lord God rained from the Lord of wisdom?
Athanasius said: No; but the wisdom herself, being Lord and God, rained fire
and brimstone from the Lord God. Zacchaeus said: But he did not say from the
Lord God, but from the Lord; is the wisdom then called the Lord God, and God
called Lord only?

18 Athanasius said: Since it is confessed by all that the cause and begetter
of the wisdom is Lord and God, for this reason he said of him Lord only, but
of the wisdom he added God as well. For he knew those who disobey him.

19 Zacchaeus said: Two gods then? Athanasius said: God forbid we should say
that. Zacchaeus said: If God is God and begetter, and the wisdom is God, two
gods. Athanasius said: There are not two gods, since the nature is the same,
and one will, and one accord. And just as a king is so called, and the image
of the king, and they are not two kings, so God, and the image and wisdom of
God, and not two gods. And in the case of man the image is by imitation, but
in the case of God by nature.

20 Zacchaeus said: If God is God, and the image is God, two gods. Athanasius
said: There are two gods when the prototype has one will and the image
another; but when it is the same will, the same knowledge, the same mind, the
same nature, one God, because of the invariable sameness of the substance.
Indeed Moses himself, showing the invariable sameness of mind and of will and
of substance of the man and the woman, said one flesh, saying, For this cause
a man shall leave father and mother, and the two shall be one flesh. And in
the ode he says, Horse and rider he threw into the sea, because of the
sameness of nature; and if he spoke of the horses and the riders, though they
are bodies, in the singular, he would not have called the incorporeal God, and
his Word who is God, in the plural.

21 Zacchaeus said: Let us grant that the wisdom too is God; is Christ also
from a woman? Athanasius said: This wisdom herself, being alone and truly God
from God, when men were perishing, willed to appear on earth and to live with
men, that through herself she might save, by the Father's will, the race of
men. And having sanctified the virgin Mary, she took a body from her, that
having lived with men bodily as a man, through the like she might save the
race of men.

22 Zacchaeus said: You blaspheme, man, saying that the wisdom of God has
entered a human womb. Athanasius said: Do not be troubled: I persuade you from
the Scriptures also, that not in Mary's womb alone did the divine fashion a
temple for itself, but in every woman's womb the wisdom fashions and
sanctifies human beings. Indeed it said to Jeremiah, Before I fashioned you
from the belly, I know you. And before you came out of the womb, I sanctified
you.

23 Zacchaeus said: Yes, it fashions and creates; but does it also enter a
womb? Athanasius said: First, do you not say that it fashioned the womb of the
woman itself, and every member of the body? Zacchaeus said: Yes. Athanasius
said: If then it deigned to take clay from the earth and through its own hands
to fashion a womb, and every other member it shapes, why do you loathe it when
you hear that it deigned to fashion a temple for itself from a womb, and from
the beginning to work these things through itself? And if it fashions, it
fashions being within;

for the divine, being everywhere, has the inward and the outward alike. Then
this sun that gives the day, even when it comes to be in dead bodies and
stretches over uncleannesses, is not wronged, is not defiled, but rather helps
the dead and foul things, drying them and doing away the stench. And do you
think the divine is defiled, and do you not rather believe that it sanctifies
the womb, opens the gates of the belly, and the craftsman orders all things,
keeps them safe, and brings them to completion?

24 Zacchaeus said: And did the wisdom of God appear on earth? Athanasius said:
And what is strange in that? You hear Jeremiah saying, This is our God;
another shall not be reckoned beside him. He found out every way of knowledge.
After these things he appeared on earth, and lived with men.

25 Zacchaeus said: It is not Scripture in Jeremiah. Athanasius said: Read the
letters of Baruch, that you may know and be persuaded that it is written.
Zacchaeus said: I know that it is written in that letter itself, but not in
Jeremiah. Athanasius: Jeremiah together with Baruch and the Lamentations and
the letter is written as one book; and Jeremiah these four books are named as
one.

26 Zacchaeus said: You have not yet shown that the wisdom is also Christ.
Athanasius: I want to lead you on to read that God promised to live with men,
and so, led from Scripture, to come to understanding.

27 Zacchaeus said: God appeared on earth and lived with men when in the holy
temple he dealt with the prophets and the patriarchs? Athanasius said: After
these things it is said he appeared on earth and lived with men; clearly after
the law. And still likewise it is said in the prophet Isaiah: My people, they
who call you blessed lead you astray, and they trouble the paths of your ways;
but now the Lord will stand for judgment, and he will set the people for
judgment; the Lord himself will come to judgment with the elders of the people
and with his rulers; but you, why have you burned my vineyard?

28 Zacchaeus said: Show that he was born from a woman. Athanasius: Do you
believe Isaiah the prophet? Zacchaeus said: Yes. Athanasius: Hear him saying,
And they will wish they had been burned with fire; for a child was born to us,
a son, and he was given to us, whose government is upon his shoulder. And his
name is called Messenger of great counsel, Wonderful Counsellor, Mighty God,
Potentate, Prince of peace, Father of the age to come.

29 Zacchaeus said: As you say then, did the wisdom of God die? Athanasius: If
I do not read to you that he died willingly, condemn me as unlearned; but if
you do read it, fear to mock, lest you be found in the day of judgment and
say, This is he whom we had for a laughingstock. Zacchaeus said: Show it from
the Scriptures and I am persuaded.

30 Athanasius: For now confess that God was born on earth, and this is the
Emmanuel of whom the prophet Isaiah said: Behold, the virgin shall have in the
womb and shall bear a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which is
interpreted God with us; that when you have advanced to this I may persuade
you that he died willingly for us all.

31 Zacchaeus said: For every righteous man is not God. Athanasius: But no one
is Emmanuel, no one from a virgin. Zacchaeus said: Can you persuade me that
Mary, being a virgin, gave birth? Athanasius said: The prophet said, Behold,
the virgin shall have in the womb and shall bear a son, and they shall call
his name Emmanuel, which is interpreted God with us.

32 Zacchaeus said: Behold the young woman is what was said, and not the
virgin. Athanasius: And what sort of sign is it that a young woman, not a
virgin, conceiving from a man should bear, when God says, Ask a sign toward
the depth or toward the height, and the prophet has said, I will not ask, nor
will I tempt the Lord, and therefore he added, Is it a small thing for you to
give a contest to men? And how do you give a contest to the Lord? Therefore
the Lord himself will give you a sign: Behold, the virgin shall have in the
womb and shall bear a son, and they shall call his name

Emmanuel. Was this the great sign which the Lord himself furnished instead of
all, that a younger woman conceiving [not] from a man should bear a son, one
of those humans according to custom?

33 Zacchaeus said: The sign was this, that before the child knows to call
father or mother, he took the power of Damascus and the spoils of Samaria.
Athanasius: What child, before he knows to call father or mother, refused evil
so as to choose the good, and took the power of Damascus and the spoils of
Samaria, if not Christ alone? For while he lay in the manger, the magi came,
sent by Herod to search out and make it known to him. And this they did not do
which Herod sought; but having worshipped him they gave gold, frankincense,
and myrrh, that what was said might be fulfilled: Before the child knows to
call father or mother, he shall take the power of Damascus and the spoils of
Samaria.

34 Zacchaeus said: Were the magi then Damascenes? Athanasius: In Scripture
those who think such things as the Egyptians are called Egyptians, and those
who think such things as the Canaanites are called Canaanites, and those who
think such things as the Amorites are called Amorites; indeed the prophet
cries against your nation, Your father was an Amorite, your land a Hittite. So
also of the magi, who think the same things as Damascenes and Samaritans, he
called them Damascenes and Samaritans.

35 Zacchaeus said: Why, if the Christ you speak of was going to come, did the
prophets not say openly that such and such will be, but you bring me
testimonies scatteringly, now from this prophet, now from another, and now
from yet another? Could not one of them say openly that the wisdom of God is
going to be made flesh, and to suffer this and to do that? Athanasius: When so
many have spoken, you were not persuaded; and if one were speaking, how would
you have been persuaded? Zacchaeus said: And why did they not all say it
openly, that no one might be scandalized? Athanasius: And how was what was
preached still a mystery, if it was preached openly? Besides, God also knew
that you are a wicked nation and a people full of sins; therefore he said
these things through parables, lest hearing that Christ is going to be born,
and the nations are going to become God's inheritance, you burn the books, so
that the nations, not having read, might not be saved. For this is also why
you crucified him, since he said, The vineyard will be taken from you and
given to a nation producing the fruits. How would you not also have burned the
books, you who crucified the Master and stoned those who preached? That if
they had also preached openly you would have done the same things is not
doubtful; for if after the open signs in Egypt, and the wonders in the Red
Sea, and the marvels after these, you turned to idolatry saying to Aaron, Make
us gods, how is it not confessed that, being of such a choice, you did the
same things?

36 Zacchaeus said: Are you not ashamed, speaking of a cross of Master and God
and power and wisdom? Athanasius: The prophets were not ashamed to speak; how
should I be ashamed? Zacchaeus said: And can you show that the prophets have
said he would be crucified? Athanasius: First Moses says, And you shall see
your life hanging before your eyes, and you shall not believe in your life; in
the morning you shall say, Would that it were evening, and in the evening you
shall say, Would that it were morning. For from the sixth hour darkness came
over all the earth until the ninth hour. And from the ninth the light appeared
again, so that those who saw said, How did evening come, which is darkness,
and again how did morning come, which is light.

37 Zacchaeus said: Persuade me from the old covenant that he must be
crucified. Athanasius: You shall see your life hanging before your eyes, and
you shall not believe in your life, is of the old covenant; and in the morning
you shall say, How did evening come, and in the evening, How did morning come,
likewise is of the old.

38 Zacchaeus said: But this is not a cross, nor death. Athanasius: If Isaiah
says, Lord, who has believed

our report? And to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed? We announced
before him as a child, as a root in a thirsty land; he has no form nor glory.
And we saw him and he had no form nor beauty, but his form was without honor,
failing beside all men; and if David also says, They dug my hands and my feet.
They divided my garments among themselves, and upon my clothing they cast a
lot, how do you not understand the cross?

39 Zacchaeus said: Not even so have you mentioned death. Athanasius: A man
being in a blow and knowing how to bear infirmity, because he turned away his
face; he was dishonored and not reckoned. He bears our sins, and for our sins
he is in pain; and we reckoned him to be in toil and in a blow and in
ill-treatment; but he was wounded for our sins; the chastisement of our peace
is upon him, by his bruise we were healed. All we like sheep have gone astray;
a man has gone astray in his way; and the Lord delivered him up for our sins;
and he, because he has been ill-treated, does not open his mouth; as a sheep
he was led to slaughter, and as a lamb before him who shears it is voiceless,
so he does not open his mouth. In his humiliation his judgment was taken away;
and who shall declare his generation? For his life is taken from the earth;
from the lawlessness of the people he was led to death; and I will give the
wicked for his burial, and the rich for his death. Because he did no
lawlessness, nor was deceit found in his mouth; and the Lord wills to cleanse
him from the blow. If you give concerning sin, your soul shall see long-lived
seed; and the Lord wills to take away from the pain of his soul, to show him
light, and to fashion him with understanding, to justify a righteous man
serving many well; and he himself will send away their sins. Therefore he
himself shall inherit many, and shall divide spoils of the strong; because his
soul was delivered up to death, and he was reckoned among the lawless; and he
himself bore the sins of many, and for their sins he was delivered up.

40 Zacchaeus said: All that you have said, you have said the prophet spoke
concerning a man. Athanasius: Concerning the arm of the Lord that came to be:
when you hear arm of God, understand power of God; for the divine is not
composed of members. Zacchaeus said: I heard him saying, A man being in honor,
and knowing how to bear infirmity. Athanasius: You seem to me not to attend to
what was said. You heard him saying, Lord, who has believed our report, and to
whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed? We announced before him as a
child.

41 Zacchaeus said: Did the arm of the Lord, the power you speak of, change and
become a child? Athanasius: It did not change, God forbid; but taking flesh,
and the power being as in a temple in the flesh, it became man; being man
according to flesh, but being God according to spirit. And as man he was led
to slaughter; but as God he was taking the sin of the world. For this is also
why he himself shall inherit many, and of the strong demons he divided the
spoils he took, whom they had long been spoiling as men. Because his soul was
delivered up to death, and he was reckoned among the lawless, because he was
hung between two robbers and reckoned as if impious, the law saying, Cursed is
everyone who hangs upon a tree.

42 Zacchaeus said: See then, he is cursed. Athanasius: For this reason the
wicked shall be given for his death, and the rich for his burial, because they
reckoned him with the lawless.

43 Zacchaeus said: As the prophet says, Who shall declare his generation? But
I tell the generation of Jesus; for he was son of Joseph, and his brothers
James and Joses and Judas and Simon, and his mother Mary.

44 Athanasius: For this reason the prophet said, Who shall declare his
generation? Unless one is wise, he cannot know that he has been born of the
Holy Spirit and Mary the virgin.

45 Zacchaeus said: Did the Holy Spirit, in place of a man, mix with Mary?
Athanasius: You see that

a wise man is sought, who can understand that the Holy Spirit has no bodily
mixing. Zacchaeus said: You said he has been born of the Holy Spirit and Mary
the virgin. Athanasius: But not according to mixing, but according to
creation. For as the first man was from God and from the earth, not according
to mixing but according to creation, so hear Christ also from the Holy Spirit
and Mary the virgin.

46 Zacchaeus said: Why then do you also call him Christ, who neither reigned
nor was anointed by any of the prophets? Athanasius: You have been taught from
the prophets that he is called power and Word and wisdom and arm and child and
man; and one may also learn that he is ruler and king and Christ and priest
and prophet; for he became all things to all, that he might save all,
remaining God, not having changed his nature. Zacchaeus said: How can you show
me?

47 Athanasius: First, that he is ruler and leader, hear Moses writing the
blessings of Jacob and saying, A ruler shall not fail from Judah, and a leader
from his thighs, until he comes for whom it is laid up, and he is the
expectation of nations. And see what the prophecy was: that Jacob himself,
dying, called his sons saying, Gather together, that I may announce to you
what will meet you at the last of the days; gather together that I may
announce to you. Sons of Jacob, hear Israel your father. And when they were
gathered, he blesses Judah with the blessing already said. And Joseph, though
reigning, he makes a servant of Judah, saying to him, And the sons of your
father shall worship you. Because a ruler shall not fail from Judah, and a
leader from his thighs, until he comes for whom it is laid up. And he is the
expectation of nations.

48 Zacchaeus: He said it concerning David, that he was going to be born from
Judah after generations. Athanasius: And yet from David the rulers increased.
But the prophecy says that the rulers of the Jewish nation are to be until he
comes for whom it is laid up, and he is the expectation of nations. For your
David became an expectation of the Jews, and not of us the nations.

49 Zacchaeus said: And do you say that the nations too are called into life,
whom God forbade? Athanasius: You heard in the second psalm David saying
prophetically, The Lord said to me, You are my son; today I have begotten you.
Ask of me and I will give you nations as your inheritance, and as your
possession the ends of the earth.

50 Zacchaeus said: David was speaking concerning himself, The Lord said to me,
You are my son; today I have begotten you. Not concerning Christ, was he?
Athanasius: And when did David inherit the nations? Zacchaeus said: The
nations means the twelve tribes of Israel. Athanasius: And the ends of the
earth, when did David take them?

51 Zacchaeus said: Has Christ then taken the ends of the earth? Athanasius:
Learn that in all Egypt, and in Palestine, and in Phoenicia, in Syria, in
Cilicia, in Cappadocia, in Pontus, in Armenia, in Scythia, and in a word in
the whole inhabited world we inscribe Christ and call upon him as king.

52 Zacchaeus said: And in Egypt do you call Christ king, among those disowned
by God? And how can Christ be from God, and, as you say, son of God, when God
blamed our fathers because they went in the way of Egypt? Or did you not hear
the prophet himself saying, What have you to do with the way of Egypt, to
drink the water of Geon?

53 Athanasius: He does not want us to imitate the Egyptians; but he wills them
to be saved and to come to piety, since he wills every nation also. Indeed,
hear the prophet saying, And it shall be, as many as are left of all the
nations that came against Jerusalem, they shall also go up year by year to
worship the Lord Almighty and to keep the feast of tabernacles. And it shall
be, as many as do not go up of all the tribes of the earth to Jerusalem to
worship the King, the Lord Almighty, they themselves too shall be added; but
if the tribe of Egypt does not go up nor come, upon these also shall be the
fall with which the Lord will strike all the nations that do not go up to

keep the feast of tabernacles.

54 Zacchaeus said: This shall be the sin of Egypt, and the sin of all the
nations, as many as do not go up to keep the feast of tabernacles.
[Athanasius:] And the Egyptians themselves he wills to be saved and to go up
to Jerusalem, yet not to go about in Egypt. Athanasius said: You cannot show
me that he wants all to meet year by year in the sensible Jerusalem. For how
can this come to be, so that even those from the ocean come year by year to
Jerusalem? And even if this too be granted you, that it is possible, with
people attending to nothing else, or having leisure for this alone.

55 [Athanasius]: Hear Isaiah the prophet, prophesying expressly that God is
worshipped in Egypt itself: And the Lord shall be known to the Egyptians, and
the Egyptians shall fear the Lord in that day, and they shall offer
sacrifices, and they shall vow vows to the Lord and pay them.

56 Zacchaeus said: He did not say in Egypt, but the Egyptians shall fear the
Lord, and they shall offer sacrifices and vow vows to the Lord and pay them,
not in Egypt but in Jerusalem. Athanasius: You cannot show even this, that he
says in Jerusalem; but that I may not make you contentious, hear the prophet
himself saying openly: In that day there shall be five cities in Egypt
speaking the Canaanite tongue and swearing by the name of the Lord. One city
shall be called city of Asedec; in that day there shall be an altar in it to
the Lord. And it shall be a sign forever to the Lord in the land of Egypt.
Because they shall cry to the Lord because of those who afflict them, and the
Lord shall send them a man who shall save them. And the Lord shall be known to
the Egyptians, and the Egyptians shall fear the Lord. And the Egyptians shall
know the holy God in that day, and they shall make sacrifices, and they shall
vow to the Lord and pay. And the Lord shall strike the Egyptians with a great
blow, shaking their handmade things. And he shall heal them with healing, and
they shall turn to the Lord, and he shall hear them and heal them.

57 Zacchaeus said: Against these things I have nothing to say. Athanasius: May
it be yours also in the greater matters to let the same voice go, that you too
may be called by the new name given upon the earth. Zacchaeus said: Is there
then a greater name than the one we have, being called Israelites? Athanasius:
Hear God saying again through the prophet Isaiah: But to those who serve me a
new name shall be called, which shall be blessed upon the earth. Zacchaeus
said: And what is the name? Athanasius: If you are counted worthy to be called
a Christian, you will know the new name.

58 Zacchaeus said: You promised to show that he is also Christ, and king, and
priest. Athanasius: If Christ is anointed, it is not with earthly oil, but
with the Spirit of God, according to the prophet David who says, Therefore
God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness beyond your fellows.

59 Zacchaeus said: The oil of gladness is the chrism prepared by Moses.
Athanasius: For this reason beyond your fellows is said, and it does not have
according to your fellows; for his fellows were anointed with earthly oil; but
he with the Holy Spirit, as it is written, The Spirit of the Lord is upon me,
because he has anointed me; he has sent me to preach good news to the poor.

60 Zacchaeus said: Isaiah is the one saying these things; not your Christ, is
he? Athanasius: Is not the one anointed in the Holy Spirit called Christ?
Zacchaeus said: Yes. Athanasius: Go then to Jerusalem, and learn there by
asking where the Holy Spirit came down and upon whom; and then that you may
hear that it was in the Jordan upon the one born of Mary under Caesar
Augustus.

64 Zacchaeus said: And am I to believe your gospels? Athanasius: For this
reason both the place has been told you and the time, that going to the place
you may receive the proof of the facts, and may know the prophecy fulfilled
upon all who believe in him, seeing eternal gladness upon

their head, and saying, The light of your face has been signed upon us, O
Lord; you have given gladness into my heart.

65 Zacchaeus: But this was not said concerning you Christians, but it was said
concerning us Israelites. Athanasius: Hear what follows in the prophecy, that
you may know it was said concerning us. Zacchaeus said: Speak. Athanasius: It
says thus: And I will make an eternal covenant with them, and their seed shall
be known among the nations.

66 Zacchaeus said: The seed of the Jews shall be known among the nations.
Athanasius: You speak truly: for the holy apostles, being seed of Abraham,
became known to us the nations, and their offspring in the midst of peoples,
holy martyrs; for everyone who sees them shall recognize them, that these are
seed blessed by God; and they shall rejoice with joy in the Lord.

67 Zacchaeus said: We have rejoiced in the Lord. Athanasius: And again I say:
what eternal gladness of yours is there, your land being made desert, and your
cities having become burned with fire, and you always owing to suffer for
this? Yes or no? Speak.

68 Zacchaeus said: But I said that these things are going to come about and
the city is going to be built. Athanasius said: Let us see from what follows
whether this has not come about; it says thus: Let my soul rejoice in the
Lord; for he has clothed me with a garment of salvation, and has put about me
a tunic of gladness. And if you knew that as many of us as were baptized into
Christ have put on Christ, and a tunic of gladness, we received the grace of
the Spirit, and as bridegrooms we have the sign of the cross as a mitre. And
as a bride we were adorned with adornment by our deeds; and as earth
increasing its flower, the church year by year blooms with those being
illumined; and as a garden its seeds come up, the catechumens, so the Lord
will make righteousness spring up; because it is a day of salvation and of
exultation; and I mean the resurrection before all the nations.

69 Zacchaeus: You understand for yourself as you wish; and as the saying goes,
you proclaim new moons. Athanasius: Since you still disobey, hear what
follows, and in fear speak the truth: For Zion I will not be silent, and for
Jerusalem I will not rest, until her righteousness go forth as light, and my
salvation shall burn as a lamp; and nations shall see your righteousness, and
all the kings your glory; and he shall call my new name, which the Lord shall
name it. And you shall be a crown of beauty in the hand of the Lord, and a
diadem of kingship in the hand of your God.

70 Zacchaeus: Concerning whom do you say these things are said? Athanasius:
Concerning Jerusalem; for it is no longer a city of Jews, but a city of
Christians, called by the new name. If you go there you will see this city
belonging to Christ, and a dwelling of monks, and the resurrection of Christ,
and all the kings bringing their glory, and the nations with all the peoples
announcing her righteousness with which she was justified. Zacchaeus said: So
it is no longer burned with fire and without glory? Athanasius said: It was
overthrown and dishonored for Jews; but it was built and glorified for
Christians. Zacchaeus said: The prophecy says, And strangers shall come
shepherding your sheep, and aliens ploughmen and vinedressers; but you shall
be called priests of the Lord, ministers of our God; you shall eat the
strength of nations, and in their wealth you shall be wondered at. You see
that he promises the promise to us the Jews.

71 Athanasius: These things you say truly; for so also the promises of God are
fulfilled, which he promised swearing by himself to Abraham: Surely blessing I
will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply your seed, as the stars of
heaven, and as the sand by the lip of the sea. And he swore truth to David and
did not set him aside; but his seed shall sit unto the age upon his throne.
For it is plain that from Judah the same our Lord Jesus Christ has sprung, and
in him all the promises are fulfilled; and the strangers shepherd his rational
sheep, and

aliens are ploughmen farming his church; but he with his disciples has the
priesthood, eating the strength of the nations; for I, he says in the gospels,
have food to eat which you do not know, and in the wealth of the nations he is
wondered at; for you see the wise, the rhetors, and the poets bringing him the
wealth of their words, and bidding farewell to idolatry, but offering
themselves to the word of grace; and you, seeing dead men raised, did not
believe, but were made dead; but the nations rose together with the dead.

72 Zacchaeus said: And what is great about raising the dead? Did not magi
raise the dead? And they did signs so as even to stand against Moses.
Athanasius: So then, because magi have done signs and stood against Moses, did
Moses do nothing great?

73 Zacchaeus said: Moses did great signs; but he is not believed to be God
from the signs, but from the truth. Athanasius: And what is greater than the
truth of Christ? For what magus before being born was able to do signs and
wonders?

74 Zacchaeus said: What sign then did Christ do before being born? Athanasius:
He made the prophets speak concerning him: Behold, the virgin shall have in
the womb; and a myriad of other such things; and while he was in the womb
itself he made John leap in gladness.

75 Zacchaeus said: I do not believe your gospels. Athanasius said: Then you do
not believe even that he was Mary's son? Zacchaeus: He was born in Jerusalem
itself, and I know that he is Mary's son. Athanasius: And in Jerusalem itself
Elizabeth was carrying John when Christ made him leap in gladness; and
immediately when he was born he drew magi from the east to worship him; but no
magus once born made a star appear in heaven, and other magi come and worship
him.

76 Zacchaeus said: Can you then persuade me that magi came when he was born
and worshipped him? Athanasius: Be persuaded by the facts themselves; for ask
why Herod killed children in Bethlehem, and learning the cause you will know
that it was because of Christ, the magi having come and said, Where is he who
has been born king of the Jews? For we have seen his star in the east, and we
have come to worship him; and the memorials of the children are plain to this
day.

77 Zacchaeus said: We do not believe that it happened so. Was your Christ then
somehow unable to help the children, that they might not die? Athanasius: If
then you mock, set against it the children killed by Pharaoh at the time when
Moses was born, and say, Could God not rescue the children of the Hebrews? But
if, being able, he did not rescue them, Christ too, being able, did not rescue
them; for he himself both then and now is long-suffering.

78 Zacchaeus said: But in the case of Moses God avenged the death of the
children through the plagues which the Lord brought upon the Egyptians.
Athanasius: If Jews are not without sense, they have suffered more than
Egyptians in the consumption of Jerusalem; and Egyptians suffered in one year
or two; but Jews from Christ until now are punished, and they will be punished
also in what comes after, if they do not repent.

79 Zacchaeus said: Even if I believe in Christ, I believe in him as priest,
not as God; for you said that with his disciples he has the priesthood.
Athanasius: But I said that he himself is both priest and Lord and God and
shepherd.

80 Zacchaeus: God is admittedly called shepherd, since he is shepherd of the
people, as the prophet says: You who shepherd Israel, give heed, who lead
Joseph like sheep. Athanasius: The same prophet also called him Lord and God
and priest.

81 Zacchaeus said: Read me where it is written. Athanasius: In the 109th psalm
it is said thus: The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand, until I put
your enemies a footstool of your feet; the Lord shall send forth

a rod of power to you from Zion; and rule in the midst of your enemies; with
you is the rule in the day of your power, in the splendors of your holy ones.
From the womb before the morning star I begot you; the Lord has sworn and will
not repent, You are a priest unto the age according to the order of
Melchizedek.

82 Zacchaeus said: And when did he become priest? It was said concerning
Solomon according to the order of Melchizedek; and these things unto the age;
for so it was said: The Lord has sworn and will not repent; You are a priest
unto the age according to the order of Melchizedek. Athanasius: Solomon
neither became a priest, nor in his whole life was he well-pleasing to God;
but having also done the evil before the Lord he died; how then does he remain
a priest unto the age?

83 Zacchaeus said: So he said to Christ, Sit at my right hand, until I put the
enemies a footstool of your feet. Athanasius: David said, The Lord said, that
is God, to my Lord, to Christ: Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies a
footstool of your feet.

84 Zacchaeus said: And does Christ sit at the right hand of God? Athanasius:
This is what the prophet says.

85 Zacchaeus said: So then Christ is greater than the one being blessed?
Athanasius: God forbid. Zacchaeus said: How then does he sit at his right
hand? Athanasius: As only-begotten son of his own Father.

86 Zacchaeus said: And how is he still priest, if he sits at his right hand?
Athanasius: Because he is not only priest but also God; for this reason, being
God by nature, he took flesh from Mary, that becoming man, and taking up the
sacrifice on our behalf, he might become priest according to the order of
Melchizedek; who became priest not according to a law of a fleshly
commandment, but having been anointed with the Holy Spirit, he is priest unto
the age.

87 Zacchaeus said: And how can the one who died under Pontius Pilate be priest
unto the age? Athanasius: Those dead in mind look around at his death. But the
living see his resurrection.

88 Zacchaeus said: And who can persuade me that he rose? Athanasius: The
prophet David saying in a psalm that you will not abandon my soul to Hades,
nor will you give your holy one to see corruption.

89 Zacchaeus said: Was he not then a man of soul and body? Athanasius: He was
man according to flesh, but God according to spirit. Indeed, hear what God
says through the prophet Isaiah: Egypt has toiled and the merchandise of the
Ethiopians, and the Seboeim, tall men, shall pass over to you ... and all who
oppose him shall be put to shame, and they shall go in shame. Be renewed
toward me, islands. Israel is saved with an eternal salvation; they shall not
be ashamed nor put to shame unto the age ... Do you think the prophet is
speaking falsely, Israel is saved by the Lord with an eternal salvation? If
you want to say that we were neither ashamed nor put to shame, having lost
all things and become slaves of the Romans, dare to say that our fathers
acquired false prophets.
Zacchaeus said: God forbid we should say the prophets are false.

91 Athanasius: Therefore Israel has been saved with an eternal salvation; and
they shall not be ashamed, nor shall they be put to shame unto the age, the
holy apostles, being Hebrews by nature, both according to flesh and according
to spirit; for he is not a Jew who is circumcised in the open, but he who in
secret has the circumcision of the heart. God therefore blames those who have
the circumcision only in the open and not in the heart, saying thus: All the
nations are uncircumcised in flesh, but this my people in the heart.

92 Zacchaeus said: Did God, having commanded Abraham, saying, Every male of
yours shall be circumcised on the eighth day, turn away from the circumcision?

93 Athanasius: You shall be taught the account concerning circumcision; but
first tell me whether you have understood to whom he was speaking through the
prophet: Egypt has toiled and the merchandise of the Ethiopians, and the
Seboeim, tall men, shall pass over to you and they shall be slaves to you and
they shall follow after you bound in handcuffs, and they shall worship you,
and in you they shall pray, because God is in you and there is no God besides
you; for you are God, and we did not know it, God of

Israel, salvation.

94 Zacchaeus said: He was speaking to Jerusalem, Egypt has toiled, and the
merchandise of the Ethiopians, and the Seboeim, tall men, shall pass over to
you, and they shall be slaves to you, and they shall follow you bound in
handcuffs: ...

96 Athanasius: Is this Jerusalem then also God? For he says that they shall
worship you, and in you they shall pray, because God is in you, and there is
no God besides you; for you are God and we did not know it, God of Israel,
Savior. Zacchaeus said: To whom else then does he speak?

97 Athanasius: To no one else, except Christ. Zacchaeus said: How? Athanasius:
Egypt has toiled and the merchandise of the Ethiopians, when Christ was born
and idolatry was being loosed and magic being done away. And the Seboeim, tall
men, passed over to him, and became his slaves, and they shall follow after
him, bound in handcuffs, all the tall of the earth; and in him they shall
pray, and they shall worship him, and they shall confess that God is in him,
and there is no God besides him; for he himself is God and they did not know
it, God of Israel, Savior.

98 Zacchaeus said: How is God both in him and there is no God besides him?
Athanasius: Because of the invariable sameness of the substance; because of
the identity of the glory; because of the unmediated character of the power;
for this reason the Lord God is one Lord, because the will is one, one
authority, one might; for the Father does not will one thing and the Son
another. And if you want still more mysteries, read the seventy-first psalm of
David with fear of God and truth and a good conscience; for perhaps in your
synagogue, instead of songs that delight the ear, you read these things.

99 Zacchaeus said: What then does the psalm say concerning your Christ?
Athanasius: Read and you will find. Zacchaeus said: I have read and I found
that it speaks concerning Solomon.

100 Athanasius: O God, give your judgment to the king: who is speaking?
Zacchaeus said: David asks the Lord that he give judging well to his son, that
is to Solomon. Athanasius: And your righteousness to the son of the king?
Zacchaeus said: To the son of the king, he says, to my son; for the kingship
was David's when he was praying to God, that God himself give his
righteousness to Solomon.

101 Athanasius: Follow him with fear of God. Let the mountains take up peace
for the people and the hills righteousness; he shall judge the poor of the
people, and shall save the sons of the needy, and shall humble a slanderer.

102 Zacchaeus says: All these things he says concerning Solomon. Athanasius:
Let us see then also what follows. Zacchaeus said: Speak.

103 Athanasius: And he shall continue with the sun, and before the moon
generations of generations; will Solomon then continue with the sun, and
before the moon generations of generations? Zacchaeus said: His name shall
continue in the sun. See then: even you Christians in all the earth remember
his name as wise; and unto generations the glory of the name continues with
him.

104 Athanasius: And before the moon, generations of generations, his name was.
Zacchaeus said: And God knew his name even before the moon.

105 Athanasius: Hear then what follows. Zacchaeus said: Speak. Athanasius: He
shall come down as rain upon a fleece, and as drops dripping upon the earth;
in his days righteousness shall spring up, and abundance of peace, until the
moon be taken away. And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from
rivers to the ends of the inhabited world. Ethiopians shall fall down before
him, and his enemies shall lick dust. Kings of Tharsis and islands shall bring
gifts, kings of Arabians and Saba shall lead gifts; and all the kings of the
earth shall worship him, all nations shall serve him; because he delivered a
poor man from a mighty one, and a needy man who had no helper; he shall spare
a poor man and a needy, and shall save souls of needy men. From usury and from
injustice he shall redeem their souls, and his name is honorable before him.
And he shall live and there shall be given him of the

gold of Arabia, and they shall pray concerning him always, they shall bless
him all the day; there shall be a support in the earth upon the tops of the
mountains; their fruit shall be lifted above Lebanon, and they shall blossom
from a city as grass of the earth. His name shall be blessed unto the ages,
before the sun his name remains. And in him all the tribes of the earth shall
be blessed. All nations shall call him blessed; blessed is the Lord God of
Israel, who alone does wonders. And blessed is the name of his glory, the holy
one, unto the age and unto the age of the age; and all the earth shall be
filled with his glory; so be it, so be it.

106 Zacchaeus said: These things are admittedly said concerning the Christ;
but he has not yet come for now. Athanasius: What of what was said has not
been fulfilled? Zacchaeus said: All of it.

107 Athanasius: Hear then the prophet saying, And I took the thirty pieces of
silver, the price of the one priced, whom they priced from the sons of Israel,
and they gave them for the potter's field, as the Lord commanded me. You see
that the betrayer also, taking the thirty pieces of silver from the chief
priests, gave them back; and the field was bought; and the prophecy was
fulfilled; and how do you say that the Christ has not yet come?

108 Zacchaeus said: I know that the prophet says these things, but not
concerning the Christ. Athanasius: He has had dominion from sea to sea, and
from rivers to the ends of the inhabited world; Ethiopians shall fall before
him. And his enemies, you: you eat dust; and all the kings of the earth shall
worship him; all nations shall serve him; and in him all the tribes of the
earth shall be blessed.

110 Zacchaeus said: Shall those who crucified him worship him? Athanasius: The
enemies were written down, and the passion, and the death, and the honor, and
the glory, and the resurrection; you hear then David saying, Why did nations
neigh, and peoples meditate empty things? The kings of the earth stood by, and
the rulers were gathered together against the Lord and against his Christ.

111 Zacchaeus said: How then both against him and for him? Athanasius: Because
God knew both the good things and the others as they would be, he has made all
things be written; and this is what was said by God through the prophet:
Behold, I lay in Zion a stone of stumbling and a rock of scandal; and he who
believes on him shall not be put to shame.

112 Zacchaeus said: And why did he lay him as a stone of stumbling, and not
rather make him be confessed openly? Athanasius: If he did not foretell he
shall not be put to shame, blame the one who laid him; but if it has been
foretold to you, blame yourself for not having believed. For he himself is the
stone set at nought by us who were building, concerning whom David says, The
stone which the builders rejected, this has become the head of the corner.

113 Zacchaeus said: Did the wisdom of God become a stone? Athanasius: The
wisdom of God was called a stone as to the flesh; for since she took flesh
from the virgin, that as in that flesh she might appear to us, for this reason
she was called a stone; and this is the stone which Daniel interpreted, cut
from a mountain without hands, and making fine the image of idolatry.

114 Zacchaeus said: And from what mountain was a stone cut? Athanasius: He
calls the mountain the virgin Mary; for this is also why without hands,
because without a man's handicraft she bore the stone, that is the body. And
the prophecy says: Nebuchadnezzar saw a dream, and the dream went from him,
that is he forgot it; and calling the enchanters and the magi and the wise men
of Babylon, he said to them that the dream has departed from me. Now tell it
to me, and the interpretation of the dream itself. And when the magi and the
enchanters said that there is no man on earth who will be able to tell the
king's matter, but only gods, whose dwelling is not on earth. Nebuchadnezzar
commanded all the enchanters and the magi and the

wise men to perish; and Daniel, learning this matter of the king, asked that
a term of the three days be given him. And receiving it from the king, going
into his house he begged the Lord, and the dream which the king saw was
revealed to him, and its interpretation. And the dream was this. An image,
whose appearance was extraordinary; its sight fearful; the head of pure gold,
the hands and the breast and its arms silver; the belly and the thighs
bronze; the shins iron; the feet part iron, part clay; he was watching until
a stone was cut from a mountain without hands, and it struck the image on the
feet of iron and clay, and it made them fine to the end; then at once the
clay, the iron, and the bronze, the silver and the gold were made fine; and
they became as dust from a summer threshing-floor; and the abundance of the
wind took them up, and no place was found for them; and the stone that struck
the image became a great mountain, and it filled all the earth. This then was
the king's dream; and Daniel interprets the dream; and he was speaking of a
differing image as differing kingships, and the stone as a kingship which
shall not be corrupted unto the ages. And this is the kingship of Christ,
likened to a stone, because your fathers stumbled in it.

115 Zacchaeus: Now I hear that your Christ is likened to a stone. Athanasius:
Always the unseen and invisible things are read through open parables; or did
you not hear God saying through the prophet, And I will be as a panther to
Ephraim and as a leopard to the house of Judah; and in another, And I will
meet them as a bear in want. Is the divine then insulted when compared to a
bear and the other animals?

116 Zacchaeus said: Did God truly become a bear? He says it because of the
fearful. Athanasius: So, because of the many stumbling, according to a notion
toward Christ, he was called a stone; but hear also another prophecy toward
Christ from the same prophet: I was watching, he says, and four great beasts
were coming up from the sea, differing from one another; the first as a
lioness, having wings, and its wings as of an eagle; I was watching until its
wings were plucked, and it was lifted from the earth, and it stood on feet of
a man, and a heart of a man was given it. And behold a second beast like a
bear, and it stood to one side, and three ribs in its mouth between its teeth;
and so they said to it, Arise and eat much flesh. Behind this I was watching
and behold, a beast as a leopard, and it had four wings of a bird above it,
and four heads to the beast, and authority was given it. Behind this I was
watching, and behold a fourth beast, fearful and astounding, and exceedingly
strong; [its teeth iron; eating; making fine, and it was treading the remnants
with its feet, and it itself exceedingly strong] beyond all the beasts before
it; and ten horns to it; I was considering its horns, and behold another
little horn came up in the midst of them, and three horns of it from those
before it were rooted out from before it; and behold eyes as the eye of a man
in this horn, and a mouth speaking great things; I was watching until thrones
were set, and an ancient of days sat; and his clothing white as snow, and the
hair of his head as pure wool; his throne a flame of fire, his wheels burning
fire; a river of fire was drawing before him; a thousand thousands were
ministering to him, and ten thousand ten thousands were standing by him; a
court sat and books were opened. I was watching in a vision of the night and
behold with the clouds of heaven one as a son of man coming, and he came as
far as the ancient of days and was brought to him; and to him was given the
rule and the honor and the kingship; and all the peoples, tribes, tongues,
shall serve him; his authority is an eternal authority, which

shall not pass away; and his kingship shall not be corrupted. My spirit
shuddered, I Daniel, and the vision of my head was troubling me; and I came to
one of those standing, and I was seeking to learn the exact account from him
concerning all these things; and he told me the exact account, and he made
known to me a reasonable interpretation: these four beasts, four kingships
shall arise upon the earth, which shall be taken away, and holy ones of the
Most High shall receive the kingship, and they shall hold it unto an age of
ages.

117 Zacchaeus said: And who are the four kingships, and who are the holy ones?
Athanasius: Who is the ancient of days? If you know, speak. Zacchaeus said: I
say God; for to whom else do a thousand thousands minister, and ten thousand
ten thousands stand by, if not to God?

118 Athanasius: You have spoken well; and he who came with the clouds of
heaven having a likeness of a son of man, who was he? Zacchaeus said: The
Christ.

119 Athanasius: And the holy ones clearly are those who shall hold the
kingship, his potentates; and the four kings, the kings of the four parts of
the inhabited world, even praying to be slaves of those holy ones.

120 Zacchaeus said: But I did not mean the Christ you speak of, but the one we
expect. Athanasius: And again I say: if the one you expect comes, he will find
all things already taken beforehand by the true one; for the years of the
times have also arrived, and we have been taught by the prophet himself, and
the nations have been saved, idolatry having been loosed, and all that was
prophesied has arrived.

121 Zacchaeus said: Where is the time written? Athanasius: Daniel himself
again says: And while I was still speaking and praying and confessing the sins
of my people Israel, and casting my mercy before God concerning the mountain
of the Lord God the holy one; and while I was still speaking in the prayer,
and behold the man Gabriel whom I saw in the first vision flying, touched me
as at the hour of the evening sacrifice, and he set me up, and he spoke with
me and said: Daniel, I have now come forth to bring you to understanding; at
the beginning of your supplication the word went forth, and I came to announce
to you, because you are a man of desires; and consider in your word, and
understand in the vision; seventy weeks have been cut short upon your people
and upon the holy city, for sin to be finished, and to seal sins, and to wipe
out injustices, and to bring in eternal righteousness, and to seal vision and
prophet, and to anoint a holy of holies; and you shall know and understand
from the going forth of a word to answer, and to build Jerusalem, until Christ
the leader, seven weeks and sixty-two weeks; and it shall return and a street
and a wall shall be built, and the times shall be renewed; and after the
sixty-two weeks, anointing shall be utterly destroyed, and there is no
judgment in it; and he shall destroy the city and the holy place. You have
heard openly that until Christ the leader, seven weeks of years, and sixty-two
weeks, which is sixty-nine weeks, that is years 483. Read therefore the
Chronographer and you will find from Darius son of Arshaurus as far as Christ
there are years CCCCLXXXIII. For in the first year of Darius the prophet says
he saw the revelation; and that when the wonderful one has arrived, anointing
and judgment shall be utterly destroyed, that is king and judge; for no longer
is there among you either a king or a judge; but the city also and the holy
place have been destroyed.

122 Zacchaeus said: You promised to tell me the cause why you do not accept
the circumcision from the fathers. Athanasius: Because God, having appointed
the circumcision to the fathers, himself said: Behold, days are coming, says
the Lord, and I will make with the house of Judah a new covenant, not
according to the covenant which I made with their fathers, in the day when I
took their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, giving my laws upon
their hearts; for no longer is there a law in the foreskin, that their glory
may not be in the shame but in the heart.

123 Zacchaeus said: And did God, who commanded our

fathers saying that every male who is not circumcised on the eighth day shall
be utterly destroyed from the people, no longer wants the circumcision?
Athanasius: He knows what is expedient; and then he was requiring the shadow,
but now the truth; for this was not what he was requiring in the first place,
that the flesh of the foreskin be circumcised; but wishing thereby to signify
the truth. Indeed he blames them saying, All the nations are uncircumcised in
flesh, but this people in the heart. And that the circumcision justifies no
one is clear from this, that Abraham uncircumcised was well-pleasing to God;
for first he appeared to him, and then he gives him the commandment of
circumcision; and those born in the wilderness in the forty years were
uncircumcised.

124 Zacchaeus said: You cannot show it. Athanasius: I know that when he was
about to enter the land of the promise, then according to the commandment of
God Joshua the son of Nun, taking stones of rock, and in those very stones,
circumcises the people. Zacchaeus: If then you know that God cares for the
sake of circumcision, how do you say, circumcision is nothing?

125 That it is a command of God is plain, which is why it was commanded; for
since he wished to make a marvel between the Egyptians and the sons of Israel
themselves, before going away into Egypt he gives them the circumcision, that
the race might be plain to all men; and when they were in the wilderness again
he was not requiring the circumcision, for they were alone, and there was
no one mixing with them; but when they were about to be mixed with other
nations, he wills them again to be circumcised, that the nation might be plain
to their own. And then he tells Joshua to take knives of rock and circumcise
them; this not idly, but symbolically; for the rock is Christ, in whom we are
circumcised, not the flesh of the foreskin. And if these things were not done
symbolically, why were they not circumcised with iron, but with rocks?

126 Zacchaeus said: That the mighty works of God might be shown, because they
were able to be circumcised even with rocks. Athanasius: It would have been
shown still more, if they had been circumcised by a bare word without any
matter; but that you may know that circumcision does not justify: Noah was a
righteous man and godly without circumcision; Enoch was translated, as it is
written, being uncircumcised.

127 Zacchaeus said: From where can you show me that they were uncircumcised?
Athanasius: Because circumcision began from Abraham; for to him first God
commanded to be circumcised, and to his seed.

128 Zacchaeus said: Persuade me why you refuse to make the sacrifices, when
the holy ones first sacrificed to God; or will you say this too has taken its
beginning from Abraham? Though Scripture says that from Adam they sacrificed,
and their sacrifices were received, being from animals.

129 Athanasius: We too know that all the holy ones before the coming of Christ
were sacrificing; but after Christ the sacrifice through irrational animals
has ceased, and that through the Spirit has increased, God crying expressly, I
will not eat flesh of bulls or drink blood of goats; sacrifice to God
sacrifices of praise.
