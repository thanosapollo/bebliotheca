* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also called Theophorus, to her who is blessed in the
grace of God the Father in Christ Jesus our Savior, in whom I greet the
church which is in Magnesia on the Maeander, and I pray in God the
Father and in Jesus Christ that she may have abundant joy.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
Having learned of the well-ordered character of your love according to
God, I rejoiced and resolved to speak to you in the faith of Jesus
Christ.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
For having been counted worthy of a name most befitting God, in the
bonds which I carry about I sing the churches, in which I pray for a
union of flesh and spirit that belongs to Jesus Christ, who is our life
always, and of faith and love, than which nothing is preferred, and,
what is more important still, of Jesus and the Father; in whom, if we
endure all the despite of the ruler of this age and escape it, we shall
attain to God.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
Since then I was permitted to see you in the persons of Damas, your
bishop worthy of God, and of the worthy presbyters Bassus and
Apollonius, and of my fellow-servant the deacon Zotion, of whom may I
have joy, because he is subject to the bishop as to the grace of God,
and to the presbytery as to the law of Jesus Christ.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
And it is fitting for you also not to take advantage of the bishop's
youth, but according to the power of God the Father to render him all
reverence, as I have learned that the holy presbyters also have not
presumed upon his apparent youthful rank, but as men prudent in God
yield to him, yet not to him, but to the Father of Jesus Christ, the
bishop of all.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
For the honor, then, of him who has willed us, it is fitting to obey
without any hypocrisy. For a man does not deceive this bishop who is
seen, but he cheats the one who is unseen. And such a matter is not
with the flesh, but with God, who knows the hidden things.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
It is fitting, then, not only to be called Christians, but also to be
so; just as some also call a man bishop, yet do everything apart from
him. Such people do not seem to me to have a good conscience, because
they do not assemble securely according to the commandment.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
Since then all things have an end, and the two lie before us together,
death and life, and each is about to go to his own place;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
for just as there are two coinages, the one of God and the other of the
world, and each of them has its own stamp laid upon it, the unbelievers
bear the stamp of this world, but the faithful in love bear the stamp of
God the Father through Jesus Christ, through whom, unless we willingly
choose to die unto his passion, his life is not in us.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
Since then in the persons named above I have seen your whole multitude
in faith and have loved them, I exhort you: be eager to do all things in
the concord of God, with the bishop presiding in the place of God, and
the presbyters in the place of the council of the apostles, and the
deacons, most dear to me, entrusted with the ministry of Jesus Christ,
who was with the Father before the ages and has appeared at the end.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
All of you, then, having received a like mind of God, reverence one
another, and let no one look upon his neighbor according to the flesh,
but in Jesus Christ love one another always. Let there be nothing among
you that can divide you, but be united with the bishop and with those
who preside, as a type and teaching of incorruption.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
As then the Lord did nothing without the Father, being united with him,
neither by himself nor through the apostles, so neither do you do
anything without the bishop and the presbyters. Do not try to make
anything appear reasonable privately among yourselves, but let there be
in common one prayer, one petition, one mind, one hope in love, in the
joy that is without blame, which is Jesus Christ, than whom nothing is
better.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
All of you run together as to one temple of God, as to one altar, to one
Jesus Christ, who came forth from one Father and is with one and has
returned to him.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
Do not be led astray by strange doctrines or by old fables which are
unprofitable. For if we still live according to Judaism, we confess that
we have not received grace.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
For the most divine prophets lived according to Christ Jesus. For this
reason they were also persecuted, being inspired by his grace, so that
the disobedient might be fully convinced that there is one God, who has
manifested himself through Jesus Christ his Son, who is his Word that
came forth from silence, who in all things was well-pleasing to him who
sent him.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
If then those who walked in ancient practices have come to the newness
of hope, no longer keeping the sabbath, but living according to the
Lord's day, on which also our life sprang up through him and through his
death, which some deny, through which mystery we received the faith, and
for this reason we endure, that we may be found disciples of Jesus
Christ, our only teacher;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
how shall we be able to live without him, of whom even the prophets,
being his disciples in the Spirit, expected him as their teacher? And
for this reason he whom they rightly awaited, when he came, raised them
from the dead.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
Let us not then be insensible of his kindness. For if he should imitate
us according to what we do, we are no more. For this reason, having
become his disciples, let us learn to live according to Christianity.
For whoever is called by another name besides this is not of God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h2
:END:
Put away then the evil leaven, which has grown old and sour, and be
changed into new leaven, which is Jesus Christ. Be salted in him, so
that none among you may be corrupted, since you will be convicted by
your smell.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h3
:END:
It is absurd to speak of Jesus Christ and to judaize. For Christianity
did not believe in Judaism, but Judaism in Christianity, in which
every tongue that has believed has been gathered to God.

* Chapter 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h1
:END:
These things, my beloved, I write not because I have learned that any of
you are so disposed, but as one less than you I wish you to be on guard,
lest you fall into the hooks of vain glory, but that you may be fully
convinced of the birth and the passion and the resurrection which took
place in the time of the governorship of Pontius Pilate; things truly
and surely done by Jesus Christ, our hope, from which may none of you be
turned aside.

* Chapter 12
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h1
:END:
May I have joy of you in all things, if I am worthy. For even if I am
bound, I am not to be compared to one of you who are free. I know that
you are not puffed up; for you have Jesus Christ in yourselves. And
still more, when I praise you, I know that you are ashamed, as it is
written, that the righteous man is his own accuser.

* Chapter 13
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h1
:END:
Be eager then to be confirmed in the teachings of the Lord and of the
apostles, so that in all that you do you may prosper in flesh and
spirit, in faith and love, in Son and Father and in Spirit, in beginning
and in end, with your most worthy bishop and the worthily woven
spiritual crown of your presbytery and the deacons according to God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h2
:END:
Be subject to the bishop and to one another, as Jesus Christ was to the
Father, and the apostles to Christ and to the Father, that there may be
a union both of flesh and of spirit.

* Chapter 14
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h15-h1
:END:
Knowing that you are full of God, I have exhorted you briefly. Remember
me in your prayers, that I may attain to God, and remember the church in
Syria, of which I am not worthy to be called; for I have need of your
united prayer in God and of your love, that the church in Syria may be
counted worthy to be refreshed through your church.

* Chapter 15
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h16-h1
:END:
The Ephesians greet you from Smyrna, from which I also write to you,
being present to the glory of God, just as you also who have refreshed
me in every way, together with Polycarp, bishop of the Smyrnaeans. And
the rest of the churches also greet you in the honor of Jesus Christ.
Farewell in the concord of God, possessing an undivided spirit, which is
Jesus Christ.
