91 Concerning the impatient and those who do not have a foundation of faith,
that their choices are such; if (he says) I do not partake of the grace of the
spirit, I am unable to remain, 92 but if I receive, then I will remain. This
one does not remain even having received, because he cannot bear afflictions
nobly, not having thus prepared himself, it being necessary to judge that it
is owed by me to my master to serve with all goodwill 93 and, whether he gives
me [grace] or not, I do not leave the course, reasoning this, that I am not
worthy that even his name be called upon me. And if he will also hand me over
to Gehenna to be punished, I do not leave him. 94 As also Job: if you kill me,
I will not leave you and I will not depart from my innocence. Such a one will
justly inherit life.
