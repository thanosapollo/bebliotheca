* Fragments on Matthew
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

1 Mt 21:23 To those coming deceitfully not in order to learn it is untimely to
unfold the deeper things.
2 Mt 21:28-32 A certain man had two sons and the first is the nations, whom
God the common father called first; for having sown the taught and natural law
in the thoughts he gave the things seen as heralds, but the good order of
their soul was confused upon woods and stones; "they said," for, "to the wood:
you are my father." Wherefore they did not endure being hindered; for they
said we do not know the Lord. These then, having later changed their mind,
went away into the vineyard. And when the first had declined, the father came
to the second, that is to Israel, who being called through Moses said: "all
things whatever God spoke we will both do and hear." Who neither did nor heard
nor later changed their mind. Whom the harlots and the tax-collectors
condemned, that is the nations.
3 Mt 21:28-32 This the parable wishes to say, that the tax-collectors and the
harlots, having repented and done the things pleasing to God, are justified,
having been persuaded by John, even if at the beginning they were disobedient
to the law, rather to God. And the Pharisees who say they keep the law in only
bare words are rejected beside God; "for not in words is the kingdom of the
heavens, but in works"; "for not the hearer of the law, but the doer" is just.
And the rest beside God, these are like those who promised to go away into the
vineyard in order to work and did not go away.
