* Inscription
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h1-h1
:END:
Ignatius, who is also Theophorus, to the church of God the Father and of the
beloved Jesus Christ, shown mercy in every gift, filled with faith and love,
lacking in no gift, most worthy of God and bearing holiness, which is in
Smyrna of Asia, in a blameless spirit and in the word of God: abundant
greeting.

* Chapter 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h1
:END:
I glorify Jesus Christ the God who has so made you wise; for I perceived that
you are fitted together in unshaken faith, as if nailed to the cross of the
Lord Jesus Christ in flesh and in spirit, and established in love in the
blood of Christ, fully assured unto our Lord, who is truly of the race of
David according to the flesh, Son of God according to the will and power of
God, truly born of a virgin, baptized by John that all righteousness might
be fulfilled by him;

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h2-h2
:END:
truly nailed in the flesh for us under Pontius Pilate and Herod the tetrarch,
from whose fruit we are, from his God-blessed passion, that he might raise
an ensign unto the ages through the resurrection for his saints and
faithful, whether among Jews or among Gentiles, in the one body of his
church.

* Chapter 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h3-h1
:END:
For all these things he suffered for us, that we might be saved; and he truly
suffered, as also he truly raised himself, not as some unbelievers say, that
he suffered in appearance, they themselves being appearance; and according
as they think, so shall it happen to them, being bodiless and demonic.

* Chapter 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h1
:END:
For I know and believe that he was in the flesh even after the resurrection.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h2
:END:
And when he came to those about Peter, he said to them: Take, handle me and
see that I am not a bodiless demon. And immediately they touched him and
believed, being mixed with his flesh and spirit. For this reason they also
despised death, and were found above death.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h4-h3
:END:
And after the resurrection he ate and drank with them as one in the flesh,
though spiritually united with the Father.

* Chapter 4
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h1
:END:
These things I exhort you, beloved, knowing that you also hold thus. But I
guard you in advance from the beasts in human form, whom you must not only
not receive, but if possible not even meet; only pray for them, if somehow
they may repent, which is difficult; yet Jesus Christ, our true life, has
authority over this.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h5-h2
:END:
For if these things were done by our Lord in appearance, then I too am bound
in appearance. And why have I given myself over to death, to fire, to sword,
to beasts? But near the sword, near to God; among the beasts, among God.
Only in the name of Jesus Christ I endure all things, that I may suffer with
him, he who is the perfect man strengthening me.

* Chapter 5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h1
:END:
Whom some, not knowing, deny; rather they have been denied by him, being
advocates of death rather than of the truth; whom neither the prophecies nor
the law of Moses have persuaded, nor even until now the gospel, nor our own
sufferings of each man.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h2
:END:
For they think the same concerning us also. For what does it profit me if
anyone praises me, but blasphemes my Lord, not confessing him as bearing
flesh? And he who says this has denied him utterly, being a bearer of a
corpse.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h6-h3
:END:
Their names, being unbelieving, it has not seemed good to me to write. But
may it not even be mine to remember them, until they repent unto the
passion, which is our resurrection.

* Chapter 6
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h1
:END:
Let no one be deceived: even the heavenly things and the glory of the angels
and the rulers, visible and invisible, if they do not believe in the blood
of Christ, for them too there is judgment. He who can receive it, let him
receive it. Let no one's place puff him up; for the whole is faith and love,
than which nothing is preferred.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h7-h2
:END:
But observe those who hold strange opinions concerning the grace of Jesus
Christ that has come to us, how they are contrary to the mind of God. They
have no care for love, none for the widow, none for the orphan, none for the
afflicted, none for the bound or the free, none for the hungry or the
thirsty.

* Chapter 7
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h1
:END:
They abstain from eucharist and prayer, because they do not confess that the
eucharist is the flesh of our Savior Jesus Christ, which suffered for our
sins, which the Father in his kindness raised. Those then who contradict the
gift of God die while they dispute. It would have been better for them to
love, that they also might rise.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h8-h2
:END:
It is fitting to keep away from such people and to speak of them neither
privately nor in common, but to give heed to the prophets, and especially to
the gospel, in which the passion has been shown to us and the resurrection
has been perfected. Flee divisions as the beginning of evils.

* Chapter 8
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h1
:END:
Follow the bishop, all of you, as Jesus Christ follows the Father, and the
presbytery as the apostles. And reverence the deacons as the commandment of
God. Let no one do anything pertaining to the church apart from the bishop.
Let that eucharist be considered valid which is under the bishop, or under
one to whom he himself has entrusted it.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h9-h2
:END:
Wherever the bishop appears, there let the multitude be, just as wherever
Jesus Christ is, there is the catholic church. It is not permitted apart
from the bishop either to baptize or to hold a love-feast; but whatever he
approves, this also is well-pleasing to God, that everything you do may be
secure and valid.

* Chapter 9
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h1
:END:
It is reasonable, then, that we should come to our senses, while we still
have time to repent unto God. It is well to know God and the bishop. He who
honors the bishop is honored by God; he who does anything without the
bishop's knowledge serves the devil.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h10-h2
:END:
Let all things therefore abound for you in grace; for you are worthy. In all
things you have refreshed me, and Jesus Christ you. You have loved me, both
absent and present. God will be your recompense; through him, enduring all
things, you shall attain unto him.

* Chapter 10
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h1
:END:
Philo and Rheus Agathopous, who followed me for the word of God, you did well
in receiving as deacons of God; they also give thanks to the Lord for you,
because you refreshed them in every way. Nothing shall be lost to you.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h11-h2
:END:
My spirit is a ransom for you, and my bonds, which you did not despise nor
were ashamed of. Nor shall the perfect hope, Jesus Christ, be ashamed of
you.

* Chapter 11
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h1
:END:
Your prayer went forth to the church in Antioch of Syria, from which, bound
with most God-worthy bonds, I greet all, not being worthy to be from there,
being the last of them; yet according to the will I was counted worthy, not
from conscience but from the grace of God, which I pray may be given me in
fullness, that through your prayer I may attain unto God.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h2
:END:
That therefore your work may be perfect both on earth and in heaven, it is
fitting for the honor of God that your church appoint a God-ambassador, to
go to Syria and rejoice with them that they are at peace and have received
their own greatness and that their own corporate body has been restored to
them.

** Section 3
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h12-h3
:END:
It seemed to me therefore a thing worthy of God to send one of your people
with a letter, that he might join in glorifying the calm that has come to
them according to God, and that they had already reached harbor by your
prayer. Being perfect, think also perfect things. For God is ready to grant
to you who wish to do well.

* Chapter 12
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h1
:END:
The love of the brothers who are in Troas greets you, from which place I
write to you through Burrhus, whom you sent with me together with the
Ephesians, your brothers, who has refreshed me in every way. Would that all
imitated him, being an exemplar of the ministry of God. Grace will repay him
in all things.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h13-h2
:END:
I greet the God-worthy bishop and the God-befitting presbytery and my
fellow-servants the deacons, and each man and all in common, in the name of
Jesus Christ and in his flesh and blood, in his passion and resurrection,
both fleshly and spiritual, in the unity of God and of you. Grace, mercy,
peace, endurance be with you always.

* Chapter 13
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14
:END:

** Section 1
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h1
:END:
I greet the households of my brothers with their wives and children, and the
virgins who are called widows. Farewell in the power of the Father. Philo,
who is with me, greets you.

** Section 2
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h14-h2
:END:
I greet the household of Tavia, whom I pray to be established in faith and
love, both fleshly and spiritual. I greet Alce, a name dear to me, and
Daphnus the incomparable, and Eutecnus, and all by name. Farewell in the
grace of God.
