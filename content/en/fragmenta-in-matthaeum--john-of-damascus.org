others, or also for the glory of God. But the complete abandonment is
when God has done all things for salvation, and the man remains

destruction, as Judas. "And they set over his head his accusation
written: This is Jesus the King of the Jews." The writing of the tablet
which Pilate fixed before the Lord's head fulfilled the Lord's voice,
saying, When I am lifted up, I will draw all to myself. The reading
from there, then, made clear that the saving passion had come to pass
not for Judea only, but also for every Hellen[ic] and barbarian. "And
from the sixth hour darkness came upon all the earth, until the ninth
hour." The sun is eclipsed when the body of the moon has become as a

be transmitted to us. As much, then, as the moon is found to hide the
sun, so much also does the eclipse come to be. But the eclipse of the

* Fragment μεθ'

:END:

am with you all the days, until the consummation of the age. Amen." He
did not say he would be with those only, but also with all who believe
after them. For the apostles were not going to remain until the
consummation of the age; but he speaks with the faithful as with one
body. Do not tell me, he says, the difficulty of the matters. For I am
with you, making all things easy. This he also said continually to the
prophets in the Old [Testament], both to Jeremiah putting forward his
youth, and to Moses, and to Ezekiel drawing back, I am with you. So
also here, to these. But consider for me here too their difference.
For those, being sent to one nation, many =96.1413= times declined; but
these uttered nothing of the kind, being sent into the inhabited world.
And the "until," in many places of Scripture, signifies the appointed
term of a defined time; yet it does not deny what is after these
things. It has been said now concerning the Ever-Virgin Theotokos that
Joseph did not know her until she bore her firstborn son; and it is not
to be supposed that after these things she accepted union with a man.
The Lord therefore promised here also to be with us until the
consummation, not as one who will be separated after the consummation
of the age. The divine Apostle, at any rate, says that after the common
resurrection We shall be caught up in clouds to a meeting of the Lord
in the air, and so we shall always be with the Lord.

And he also reminds them of the consummation, that he may rather draw
them away, and that they may not look only at the present things that
are unpleasant, but also at the future goods that are unbounded; for
the painful things, he says, which you will undergo in the present
life, are dissolved together with it; seeing that even this age itself
will come to a consummation; but the good things of which you will
partake remain immortal. Having thus anointed and raised their minds,
he also sent them out by the memory of that day. For that day is longed
for by those who live in good works, just as it is fearful to those in
sins, as to condemned men; but let us not only fear and shudder, but
also be changed while there is time, being altered with the good
alteration, that we may partake of eternal life in Christ Jesus our
Lord, to whom be the glory and the might with the unoriginate Father,
and the life-giving Spirit, now and always, and unto the ages of ages.
Amen.
