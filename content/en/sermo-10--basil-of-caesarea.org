OF OUR FATHER AMONG THE SAINTS BASIL, ARCHBISHOP
OF CAESAREA IN CAPPADOCIA, ASCETICA
Of our father among the saints Basil, Archbishop of Caesarea
in Cappadocia, ASCETIC PRELIMINARY SKETCH.
The king's ordinances toward his subjects are good; greater still, and more
kingly, are the commands given to soldiers. Since, then, military commands
are being proclaimed, let him who longs for the high and great rank pay
heed, he who wishes to stand always at Christ's side, he who hears that
great voice: If anyone serve me, let him follow me. And where I am, there
also let my servant be. Where is Christ the King? In heaven, plainly.
There, soldier, you ought to run your course. Forget every rest upon the
earth. No soldier builds a house, nor surrounds himself with holdings of
fields, nor mixes himself in various trades that gather money. No one on
campaign is entangled in the affairs of life, that he may please the one
who enlisted him. A soldier has the king's provisions; he need not furnish
food for himself, nor occupy himself with this. A house is opened to him
everywhere among the subjects by the king's command. He need not toil over
a dwelling; the tent is in the open streets, food according to need, water
to drink, and sleep only so much as nature =31.621= has given.
But there are journeys and many vigils; endurance toward heat, toward cold;
contests against enemies; dangers even to the last; death, if it so happen,
often; yet a glorious death, and honors, and royal gifts. Life is toilsome
in wars, splendid in peace. The prize of valor, and the crown for one who
has lived well in achievements, is to be entrusted with command, to be
called a friend of the king, to stand near him, to receive a welcome, to be
honored from the king's hand, to be set over the subjects, and to intercede
for outside friends, as they may wish. Come then, soldier of Christ: taking
small examples from human affairs, take thought of the eternal goods. Let a
houseless, cityless, possessionless life be set before you. Be free, loosed
from all worldly cares; let desire for a wife not bind you, nor care for a
child. For this is impossible in the divine campaign. For the weapons of
our warfare are not fleshly, but powerful for God. The nature of the body
does not conquer you, nor choke you against your will; it does not make you
a prisoner instead of a free man. Do not seek to leave children on earth,
but to lead them up into heaven; not to cling to bodily marriages, but to
long for spiritual ones, to rule souls, and to beget children spiritually.
Imitate the heavenly Bridegroom; overthrow the uprisings of unseen enemies;
war against principalities and powers, driving them out first from your own
soul, that they may have no lot in you, then from those who flee to you and
set you forward as leader and champion of those guarded through your word.
Overthrow reasonings that rise up against the faith of Christ; war through
the word of piety against the impious and wicked reasoning.
For, he says, overthrowing reasonings, and every height raised against the
knowledge of God. And first of all take courage in the hand of the great
King, which frightens and turns the adversaries to flight as soon as it
merely appears; but when he also wishes you to become good through dangers,
and wishes his own army to engage the opposing army, then be unconquered in
preparation toward every toil, and unmoved in soul toward danger, and
migrate eagerly from land to land, and from sea to sea. For when they
persecute you, he says, flee from city to city. And when you must be
summoned =31.624= to a court, and stand before rulers, and bear the
uprisings of crowds, and see the terrible gaze of the executioner, and hear
a harsh voice, and endure the hard sight of instruments of punishment, and
be tried by tortures, and contend even to death, do not refuse all these
things, having before your eyes Christ who was in these things for you,
knowing that you must come to be in these things for Christ's sake, and you
will conquer in them. For you follow a conquering King, who wishes you to
become a sharer of his victory. For not even by dying have you been
defeated, but then indeed you have conquered most perfectly, having kept
the truth unmoved for yourself until the end, and having held unshaken the
boldness on behalf of the truth.
And from death you will journey toward eternal life, and from dishonor
among men to the glory that is from God, and from the afflictions and
punishments in the world to the eternal rest with the angels. Earth did not
receive you as a citizen, but heaven will receive you; the world persecuted
you, but angels will bear you to present you to Christ; and you will be
called a friend, and you will hear the most longed-for praise: Well done,
good and faithful servant, noble soldier, and imitator of the Master,
follower of the King; I will repay you with my own gifts; I will hear your
words, since you also heard mine. You will ask salvation for brothers who
are worn down, and for the partners of the faith and the initiates of
sacred love you will receive from the King a share in the goods; and you
will dance the eternal dances, and among the angels you will wear the
crown, reigning over creation under the King, and sharing the ages
blessedly with the choir of blessedness. But if he should still wish to
leave you in the world after the contests, that you may also contend
through more and various contests, and save many from the unseen and seen
wars, great also will be your glory on earth, and you will be honored among
friends, who have found you a protector and helper and good intercessor.
These will nourish you as a good soldier; these will honor you as a noble
champion; these will welcome you and receive you with joy as receiving an
angel of God, as Paul says, as Christ Jesus. These and such as these are
the examples of the divine campaign. And the word is not addressed to men
only. For the female also serves as a soldier with Christ, being enrolled
in the campaign by courage of soul, and not rejected because of the
weakness of the body =31.625=, and many women have excelled no less than
men.
And there are some who have even been more highly approved. From these are
those who fill the choir of virginity. From these are those who shine in
the contests of confession and the victories of martyrdom. And they
followed the Lord himself at his coming, not men only, but also women, and
through both the ministry of the Savior was accomplished. Since such and so
glorious things are laid up for the campaign according to Christ, let
fathers of sons desire it, and mothers of daughters. Let them bring forward
their own offspring, rejoicing in the eternal hopes of which their children
will share with them, desiring to have protectors with Christ and good
intercessors. And let us not be small-souled concerning the children, nor
fear if they will toil, but let us rejoice, because they will be glorified.
Let us present to the Lord what was given by him, that we also may become
sharers of the children's good standing, bringing ourselves forward with
them and standing beside them. To those then so eager and so contending,
one might well say from the Psalmist: Blessed are you by the Lord who made
heaven and earth. And according to Moses, he will pray
concerning them: Bless, Lord, their works; bring down the brow of those who
have stood against them. Be men as the noble, run the course nobly toward
the eternal crowns, in Christ Jesus our Lord, to whom is the glory unto the
ages. Amen.
