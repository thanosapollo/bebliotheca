Exhortation to a virgin.
1 Love the Lord and he will love you, and serve him and he will enlighten your
heart.
2 Honor your mother as the mother of Christ, and do not provoke the gray hair
of her who bore you.
3 Love your sisters as daughters of your mother, and do not abandon the way of
peace.
4 Let the rising sun see the book in your hands, and after the second hour
your work.
5 Pray without ceasing, and remember Christ who gave you birth. 6 Refuse
meetings with men, lest idols arise in your soul and become a stumbling-block
for you at the time of prayer. 7 You have Christ as your beloved; cast all men
away from you and you will not live a shameful life.
8 Put anger and wrath far from you, and let resentment not lodge in you.
9 Do not say: today I will eat and tomorrow I will not eat, for you do not do
this with prudence. For it will harm your body and pain your stomach.
10 It is not good to eat meat, nor good to drink wine, but these should be
offered to those who are sick.
11 A bold virgin will not be saved, and one who lives in luxury will not see
her bridegroom.
12 Do not say that the servant-girl has grieved me and I will repay her,
because there is no slavery among daughters of God.
13 Do not give your ear to vain words, and flee the tales of old women who go
about.
14 Do not look upon the feasts of drunkards, and do not enter the weddings of
strangers. For every virgin who does these things is unclean before the Lord.
15 Open your mouth with the word of God, and keep your tongue from much
speaking.
16 Humble yourself before the Lord, and his right hand will exalt you.
17 Do not turn away a poor person in a time of affliction, and oil will not
fail in your lamp.
18 Do all things for the Lord's sake, and do not seek glory from men, for the
glory of men is like the flower of grass, but the glory of the Lord remains
forever.
19 The Lord loves a meek virgin, but an irascible virgin will be hated.
20 An obedient virgin will be shown mercy, but she who contradicts strongly is
a fool.
21 The Lord will destroy a murmuring virgin, but he will deliver the thankful
one from death.
22 Laughter is shameful and shamelessness is disgraceful, and every foolish
woman is entangled with such things.
23 She who beautifies her garments will be without chastity.
24 Do not dwell together with worldly women, lest they twist your heart and
make just counsels void.
25 With tears in the night call upon the Lord, and let no one perceive you
praying, and you will find grace.

26 Desire for walks and longing for others' houses overturns the state of the
soul and corrupts its eagerness.
27 A faithful virgin will not be cowardly, but the unfaithful one will flee
even her own shadow.
28 Envy melts the soul, and jealousy eats it up.
29 She who despises the sister who is sick will also be far from Christ.
30 Do not say: this is mine and this is yours. For in Christ Jesus all things
are common.
31 Do not pry into another's life, and do not rejoice at the fall of your
sister.
32 Help virgins who are in need, and do not exalt yourself over your noble
birth.
33 Do not speak a word in the church of the Lord, and do not lift up your
eyes. For the Lord knows your heart, and he watches all your thoughts.
34 Drive every evil desire from you, and your enemies will not grieve you.
35 Sing psalms from your heart, and do not merely move your tongue in your
mouth.
36 A foolish virgin will love silver, but the prudent virgin will give even
her morsel.
37 As an impulse of fire is hard to restrain, so a virgin's soul that has been
wounded is hard to heal.
38 Do not give your soul to wicked thoughts, lest they defile your heart and
drive pure prayer far from you.
39 Grief is heavy and acedia is unbearable, but tears toward God are stronger
than both.
40 Hunger and thirst wither evil desires, and good vigil purifies the mind.
41 Love turns away wrath and anger, but gifts overturn resentment.
42 She who secretly slanders her sister will be cast out of the bridal
chamber, and she will cry at its doors and there will be none to hear.
43 The lamp of a merciless virgin will be quenched, and she will not see her
bridegroom coming forth.
44 Glass striking against a stone will be shattered, and a virgin who touches
a man will not be held innocent.
45 A meek woman is better than an irascible and wrathful virgin.
46 She who draws out a man's words with laughter is like one who puts a noose
around her own neck.
47 As a pearl in a golden setting, so is a virgin covered by modesty.
48 Songs of demons and pipes slacken the soul and destroy its vigor. Guard
that vigor always, lest you become disgraceful.
49 Do not take pleasure in laughable things, and do not rejoice with those who
mock, for the Lord has abandoned them.
50 Do not despise your sister who is eating, and do not be lifted up over your
self-control. For you do not know what the Lord has purposed, or who will
stand before him.
51 She who pities her eyes growing pale and her flesh wasting away will not
rejoice in passionlessness of soul.
52 Self-control is heavy and chastity is hard to accomplish, but nothing is
sweeter than the heavenly bridegroom.
53 Souls of virgins will be enlightened, but souls of the unclean will see
darkness.

54 I have seen men corrupting virgins in doctrines and making their virginity
vain. But you, child, hear the doctrines of the church of the Lord, and let no
stranger persuade you otherwise. For the righteous will inherit light, but the
impious will dwell in darkness.
55 Virgin eyes will see the Lord. The ears of virgins will hear his words. The
mouth of virgins will kiss its bridegroom. The sense of smell of virgins will
run to his myrrhs. Virgin hands will touch the Lord, and chastity of flesh
will be acceptable. A virgin soul will be crowned, and with her bridegroom she
will live always. A spiritual garment will be given to her, and with angels in
the heavens she will keep festival. She will kindle an unquenchable lamp, and
oil will not fail in her vessels. She will receive eternal wealth, and she
will inherit the kingdom of God.
56 My words, child, have been spoken to you, and let your heart keep my
sayings. Remember Christ who guards you, and do not forget the adorable
Trinity.
