OF THE SAME, INSTRUCTION TO PRIESTS 3 Concerning the divine grace
Attend to yourself, O priest, and to those whom you are about to teach, and
see the ministry which you have received, that you may fulfill it; for you
were not entrusted with an earthly ministry, but a heavenly; not of men, but
of angels. Be eager to present yourself to the Lord a workman unashamed,
cutting right the word of truth. Never stand at a synaxis having enmity
against anyone, lest you put to flight 188 the Paraclete on a day of synaxis.
Do not judge, nor contend at all, but hiding yourself, remaining in the
church, pray and read until the hour in which you must complete the divine
mystagogy; and thus stand in compunction at the holy altar, not looking around
hither and thither, but standing with shuddering and fear before the heavenly
king. Do not for human favor hasten the prayers or cut them short, not
entreating "take a face of a man," but look toward the king set forth and the
powers standing around in a circle. Make yourself worthy of the sacred
canons; do not liturgize together with those whom the canon rejects. See then
before whom you have stood, how you priest and to whom you give a share.
See 189 lest you forget the master's command and that of the holy apostles;
"For do not give, he says, the holy things to the dogs, and do not throw the
pearls before the swine"; "Beware of the dogs" and the rest. See then lest,
being led by human fear, you be frightened of a man unto your fall; do not
hand over the Son of God into hands of unworthy ones. See then lest you be
shamed by the glorious of the earth, nor even of the one wearing the diadem be
frightened in that hour when you stand to priest. See those wishing to receive
freely in the houses, how you give; I have no affair, you will see. But to
those worthy of the divine communion give a share freely, as you also
received; to those whom the divine canons 190 do not permit, do not give a
share, for they were reckoned as Gentiles, and until they turn, woe to those
who give a share to them. See then lest a mouse or anything else touch the
divine mysteries; lest from your carelessness they be moistened or smoked or
handled by the unholy. [See how you arrange the holy things after the
fulfillment of the divine liturgy; not hastening lest a pearl fall away, see
that you do not leave the holy cup having a stream and it be dusty, but having
arranged both, go thus. See, if holy gifts remain it is not permitted to
communicate them except to the priests and them alone; but if not, and there
are sufficient sober children, bring them also and let them communicate, and
let them remain fasting until the fifth hour. See lest flies fall into the
holy cup or sit upon the 191 bread; see lest anything else touch the divine
mysteries]. Keeping these and such things you will save both yourself and
those who hear you.
